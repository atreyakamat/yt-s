## Fact check

- Tennis ball covers are felt, typically a wool and nylon blend, applied as two dumbbell-shaped pieces.
- The nap increases aerodynamic drag compared with a smooth ball, slowing it.
- The felt helps the ball grip the strings, allowing players to impart spin; topspin creates a downward Magnus force so the ball dips.
- As felt wears, drag drops and the ball plays faster; pro events change balls after the first 7 games, then every 9 games.
- Source: Wimbledon/ITF ball-change schedule: first change after 7 games, then every 9 (https://www.aol.com/news/youre-looking-lack-fluff-why-120005486.html).
