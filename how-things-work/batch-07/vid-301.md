## Fact check

- Many stem-style true wireless earbuds place beamforming microphones in the stem, closer to the mouth, to improve call pickup ('in many earbuds').
- Using two or more microphones lets software separate voice from ambient noise (beamforming).
- The human head absorbs 2.4 GHz radio; placing the antenna lower and outward in the stem helps the link. Stems also house battery space and touch/force sensors on some models.
