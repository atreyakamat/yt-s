## Fact check

- Sealing liquid cartridge → many waterless urinals use a cartridge with a low-density oil-based sealant floating on the trapped liquid.
- Densities → sealant oils are less dense than water (around 0.8-0.9 g/ml); urine is about 1.0-1.03 g/ml.
- Urine passes through, smells are blocked → the sealant acts as a liquid trap seal.
- Water savings → a flushing urinal can use tens of thousands of litres a year; 'thousands' is conservative.
