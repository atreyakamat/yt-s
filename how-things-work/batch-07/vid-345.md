## Fact check

- Fluorescent tubes contain low-pressure argon (or similar) plus a small amount of mercury; the discharge excites mercury atoms, which emit mainly ultraviolet light (253.7 nm).
- A phosphor coating on the inside of the glass absorbs the UV and emits visible light; the phosphor blend sets the colour temperature.
- Because they contain mercury, fluorescent tubes are classed as hazardous waste and should be recycled.
