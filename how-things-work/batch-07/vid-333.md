## Fact check

- Crash pulses in car impacts last roughly 0.1 s (about 70-150 ms).
- Impulse: for the same change in momentum, a longer stopping time means a lower average force (F = Δp/Δt).
- Crumple zones absorb energy by controlled deformation, while a rigid safety cell protects occupants.
- Béla Barényi patented the crumple zone / rigid cabin concept in 1952; the first production cars designed with it appeared in 1959.
- Crumple zone patent 1952 (Barenyi), first used in 1959 Mercedes W111: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crumple_zone
