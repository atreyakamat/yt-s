## Fact check

- Video doorbells contain a wide-angle camera, microphone, speaker, Wi-Fi radio and motion detection.
- Battery models save power by keeping the camera and Wi-Fi in low-power states until triggered; many use a passive infrared (PIR) sensor, which detects body heat, hence 'on many models'.
- Wired models are typically powered by the existing low-voltage doorbell transformer.
