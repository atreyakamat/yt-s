## Fact check

- Common oxygen absorber sachets contain finely divided iron powder plus salt and a moisture source.
- In a sealed pack the iron oxidises (rusts), consuming oxygen; the reaction is exothermic so sachets can feel warm.
- Absorbers can bring residual oxygen down to around 0.1% or lower, versus about 21% in air.
- Low oxygen slows fat oxidation (rancidity) and the growth of aerobic moulds.
- Sachets are labelled 'do not eat'.
- Oxygen absorbers bring oxygen down to about 0.1% or less in sealed packs: OK. Source: https://www.humipak.com.my/products/food-packaging/oxygen-absorber/
