## Fact check

- Many mainline railways (e.g. much of Europe, UK, India, high-speed lines) use 25 kV AC overhead lines; others use lower DC voltages, hence 'many main lines'.
- Pantographs are raised against the contact wire by springs or air pressure.
- The contact strip is usually carbon (graphite) which slides along the wire.
- Traction return current flows through the wheels and running rails.
- Overhead contact wire is staggered side to side so the pantograph strip wears evenly.
