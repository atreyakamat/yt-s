## Fact check

- Heating coil and fan → hair dryers blow air over a resistance-wire heating element.
- Blocked intake causes overheating → less airflow carries away less heat; manufacturers say to keep the filter clear.
- Bimetal snap-disc cut-out → a common self-resetting thermal protector; the domed disc snaps over abruptly at its set temperature.
- Backup thermal fuse → most hair dryers also have a one-shot thermal fuse that opens permanently if the cut-out fails.
