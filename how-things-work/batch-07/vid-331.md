## Fact check

- Many laptops with hard disk drives include an accelerometer-based free-fall sensor (HDD active protection) that parks the heads on a load/unload ramp.
- HDD heads fly on an air bearing nanometres above the platter, far smaller than dust particles.
- In free fall an accelerometer reads close to zero g, which is what the sensor detects.
- A fall from about 75 cm takes about 0.39 s (t = sqrt(2h/g)); head parking is quoted at a few hundred milliseconds or less, so 'roughly 0.2 s' is an approximation.
