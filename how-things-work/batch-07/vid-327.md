## Fact check

- Gun puffing: grains are heated in a sealed rotating pressure vessel; the water inside them gets hotter than 100 °C without boiling because of the pressure.
- Sudden release of the lid drops the pressure; the superheated water flashes to steam, expanding the grains several-fold.
- Alexander P. Anderson developed the puffing process in the early 1900s and it was publicised with cannons firing puffed grains (St Louis World's Fair, 1904).
- Anderson's puffing guns shown at 1904 St. Louis fair, so '1900s' is OK. Source: https://www.history.com/news/1904-st-louis-worlds-fair-new-american-foods
