## Fact check

- Ratchet belts have a toothed track on the back of the strap and a buckle with a spring-loaded pawl.
- The ridges are angled, so the strap slides in one direction and locks in the other; a lever lifts the pawl to release: standard ratchet design.
- Ratchet steps are typically around 6 mm (about a quarter inch); traditional belt holes are usually about 2.5 cm (one inch) apart.
