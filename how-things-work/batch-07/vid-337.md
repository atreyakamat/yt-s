## Fact check

- The speed of light is about 299,792 km/s, i.e. about 30 cm per nanosecond.
- Round trip to a wall 5 m away is 10 m, which takes about 33 ns; 1 m away about 6.7 ns; 10 m away about 67 ns.
- Handheld laser distance meters commonly use phase-shift measurement of a modulated beam (some use pulse time-of-flight).
- Typical stated accuracy for consumer models is about plus or minus 1.5 to 3 mm.
- Light travels ~30 cm per nanosecond; 5 m round trip ~33 ns: arithmetic from c, OK.
