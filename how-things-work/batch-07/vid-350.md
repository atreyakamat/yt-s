## Fact check

- Common domestic meters are positive-displacement (nutating disc or rotary piston) or multi-jet types: flow turns an element, and each rotation corresponds to a set volume.
- Many meters use a magnetic coupling through a sealed wall so the register (dials) stays dry.
- Many meters have a low-flow or leak indicator (a small star, triangle or dial) that turns at very small flows; if it moves with all water off, there is a leak.
