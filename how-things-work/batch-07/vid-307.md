## Fact check

- A twist drill cuts with two lips at the point; the helical flutes evacuate chips (and let coolant in), acting like an auger.
- Straight-fluted drills exist for special uses (brittle materials, short chips) but don't lift chips well in deep holes.
- 118° is the standard general-purpose point angle for twist drills (135° is also common).
- Periodically withdrawing the bit ('peck drilling') clears chips in deep holes.
