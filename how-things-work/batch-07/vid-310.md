## Fact check

- The running man pictogram was designed by Yukio Ota after a Japanese design competition in the 1970s that followed serious department store fires.
- Japan adopted it in 1982, and it became part of the ISO safety sign standards in the 1980s (later ISO 7010).
- In the ISO safety colour system, green indicates safe condition/escape, red prohibition or fire equipment, yellow warning.
- Exit signs are typically illuminated with battery backup or photoluminescent so they stay visible in a power cut. Some countries, such as the US, still widely use red word signs.
