## Fact check

- Air-activated hand warmers contain iron powder, water, salt, activated carbon and vermiculite in a porous pouch, sealed in an airtight outer wrapper.
- Oxidation of iron (rusting) is exothermic; fine powder and salt accelerate it enough to produce noticeable heat.
- Vermiculite holds water and insulates; activated carbon helps distribute heat and retain water. Many last several hours.
