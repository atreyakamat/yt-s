## Fact check

- Fisher-Yates shuffle produces an unbiased random permutation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fisher%E2%80%93Yates_shuffle
- People expect random sequences to look evenly spread (clustering illusion). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clustering_illusion
- Some players spread songs by the same artist apart to feel more random; Spotify described changing its shuffle for this reason. Source: https://rnd.atspotify.com/how-to-shuffle-songs/
