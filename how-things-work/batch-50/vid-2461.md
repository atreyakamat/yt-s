## Fact check

- Auto-darkening filters use an LCD shutter that darkens when sensors detect the welding arc; introduced in 1981 by Hornell. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welding_helmet
- Standards require UV and IR protection even in the light state. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welding_helmet
- Switching time is not quoted; script says 'in a blink' and 'split second' only.
