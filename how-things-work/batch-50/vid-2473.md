## Fact check

- ISPM 15 requires wood packaging material to be heat treated (minimum 56 °C core temperature for 30 minutes) or fumigated, and marked. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISPM_15
- The mark includes the IPPC symbol, country code and treatment code such as HT. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISPM_15
- Rule purpose is to prevent spread of wood pests. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISPM_15
- Verified: heat treatment needs the wood core at 56 C for at least 30 minutes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISPM_15
