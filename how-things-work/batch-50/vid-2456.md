## Fact check

- Plot foiled 9 August 2006 with 24 arrests in London, Birmingham and High Wycombe; peroxide-based liquid explosives disguised as soft drinks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_transatlantic_aircraft_plot
- US restrictions from 13 August 2006; eventually carry-on liquids limited to 100 ml each in one clear bag; UK allowed limited liquids again on 6 November 2006. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_transatlantic_aircraft_plot
- As of 2026 the 100 ml limit still applies at most airports, with some relaxing as CT scanners are installed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_transatlantic_aircraft_plot
