## Fact check

- A theodolite has a telescope rotating about horizontal and vertical axes, each with a graduated circle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodolite
- The word first appeared in Leonard Digges's 1571 book; Jonathan Sisson built the first modern-style instrument in 1725; Jesse Ramsden's 1787 instrument made it accurate. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodolite
- By 1977, Wild, Kern and Hewlett-Packard offered total stations. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodolite
