## Fact check

- GSM phones can place emergency calls such as 112 without a SIM card; networks accept them from any device. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/112_(emergency_telephone_number)
- Emergency calls can be placed on any available network. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_telephone_number
- Rules vary by country and device (script says so). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_telephone_number
