## Fact check

- An arc lamp produces light from an electric arc between carbon electrodes, which wear away and need regulating. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arc_lamp
- The Yablochkov candle was used for street lighting in Paris in the late 1870s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yablochkov_candle
- Edison demonstrated a practical incandescent lamp in 1879. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incandescent_light_bulb
- Arc lamps were generally too bright for domestic use. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arc_lamp
- Verified: Yablochkov candle invented 1876, used in Paris from 1877 (Louvre) and 1878 (Avenue de l'Opera). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yablochkov_candle
