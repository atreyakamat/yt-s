## Fact check

- Average blink rate about 17 per minute in a lab, gaps of roughly 2 to 10 seconds; blink duration typically 100 to 150 ms (UCL researcher). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blinking
- Reading or long focus drops the rate to about 4 to 5 per minute, a major reason eyes dry out and tire; screens link to computer vision syndrome. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blinking
- Infants blink about 1 to 2 times per minute; adult levels by adolescence; blinking spreads tears and removes irritants. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blinking
