## Fact check

- A tubular pin tumbler lock arranges its pins in a circle and is opened by a hollow, cylindrical key with notches; used in bike locks and other small locks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tubular_pin_tumbler_lock
- Pins must sit at the shear line for the plug to turn: standard pin tumbler principle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pin_tumbler_lock
