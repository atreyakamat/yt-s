## Fact check

- Developed by British engineer Denys Fisher between 1962 and 1964 using Meccano pieces; first sold 1965, shown at 1965 Nuremberg International Toy Fair; US launch by Kenner in 1966; Toy of the Year 1967. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirograph
- Mathematics: a small circle rolls inside a fixed circle without slipping, pen point at a distance from the small circle's centre (hypotrochoid). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirograph
