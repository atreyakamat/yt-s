- Low cabin humidity dries the nose, weakening smell; tests cited found perception of saltiness and sweetness drops about 30% at altitude; pressurisation can make food taste dry and flavorless. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airline_meal
- In 1973 UTA chef Raymond Oliver increased salt, sugar and oil in recipes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airline_meal
- Stronger-tasting dishes were developed to compensate. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airline_meal
