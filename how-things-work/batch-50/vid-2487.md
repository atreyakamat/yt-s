## Fact check

- Weather radar sends microwave pulses about one microsecond long, listens about a millisecond; delay gives distance; Doppler measures phase shift to find motion toward or away from the radar and can reveal rotation and wind shear; antenna sweeps 360 degrees then tilts to next elevation; full volume scan typically 5 to 10 minutes; NEXRAD colours: green 20 dBZ light, yellow 35 moderate, red 50 heavy (about 2 inches per hour), magenta 65 extremely heavy likely hail. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_radar
