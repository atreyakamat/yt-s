## Fact check

- The soufflé originated in France in the early 18th century; earliest mention attributed to chef Francois Massialot; Carême helped popularise it in the early 19th century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Souffl%C3%A9
- A soufflé generally deflates about 5 to 10 minutes after cooking; the name is the past participle of souffler, meaning to blow, breathe, inflate or puff; beaten egg whites provide the lift. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Souffl%C3%A9
- Trapped air expanding on heating and shrinking on cooling is ordinary gas behaviour (reasoning, no number).
