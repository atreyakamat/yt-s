## Fact check

- Four main ingredients: pigment, gum arabic binder, additives (glycerin, ox gall, honey, preservatives), water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watercolor_painting
- The gum binder is mostly absorbed by the paper, leaving pigment particles on the surface; transparency is caused by the paper being visible between the particles; Chinese white makes it opaque. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watercolor_painting
- Paper often cotton, cold-pressed (textured) or hot-pressed (smoother); cotton reduces distortion when wet. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watercolor_painting
- Albrecht Dürer (1471-1528) generally considered among the earliest notable watercolorists; Reeves Silver Palette 1781; Society of Painters in Water Colours 1804. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watercolor_painting
- Unpainted paper stays white: follows from the transparency explanation.
