## Fact check

- Classification (hump) yards use gravity: wagons are pushed over a hump and roll down to sorted tracks, with switches and retarders controlling speed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classification_yard
- Modern yards use computers to set switches. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classification_yard
- 'Hundreds' is qualitative; big yards classify many cars per day. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classification_yard
