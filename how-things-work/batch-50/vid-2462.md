## Fact check

- Mechanical time switches use a small synchronous motor driven from the mains. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_switch
- Synchronous clock motors keep time using the mains frequency. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synchronous_motor
- Plug-in timers use segments or pegs on a dial to trigger a switch. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_switch
