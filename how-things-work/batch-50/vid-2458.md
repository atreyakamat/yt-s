## Fact check

- Shortcrust recipes usually use about 2 parts flour to 1 part fat by weight. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shortcrust_pastry
- Cold ingredients keep fat in distinct pieces which release steam in the oven to form flaky pockets; water is added only after fat and flour are combined so flour is coated and less likely to develop gluten; overworking stretches gluten and makes the crust tough. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shortcrust_pastry
