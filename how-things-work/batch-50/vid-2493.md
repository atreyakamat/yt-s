## Fact check

- The Cutler mail chute was patented by James G. Cutler in 1883. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mail_chute
- The chutes have glass fronts so jams can be seen and cleared. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mail_chute
- Mail chutes in US buildings are treated as federal mail receptacles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mail_chute
- Gauge value is illustrative.
- Verified: James Goold Cutler, Rochester architect, received US patent 284,951 on 11 September 1883. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mail_chute
