## Fact check

- Lenticular clouds are stationary lens- or saucer-shaped clouds; form when moist stable air flows over mountains making standing waves; condensation at crest when temperature drops below dew point; can reach up to about 12,000 m; mistaken for UFOs. Cloud fading at the sinking side and staying put because the wave is tied to terrain is the standing-wave mechanism (the article implies it; stated as inference). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenticular_cloud
