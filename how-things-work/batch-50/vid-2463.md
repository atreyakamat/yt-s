## Fact check

- Pallet jacks use a hydraulic pump with a release valve to raise and lower forks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pallet_jack
- Hydraulic fluid is practically incompressible, so force is multiplied by piston area ratio (Pascal's principle). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydraulics
- Gauge value is illustrative.
