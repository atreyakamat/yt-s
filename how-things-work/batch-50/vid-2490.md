## Fact check

- The foot has longitudinal (medial and lateral) and transverse arches. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arches_of_the_foot
- Elastic soft tissue, especially the plantar aponeurosis running heel to toes, spreads ground-contact forces over a longer time and stores and returns energy at the next step; the plantar calcaneonavicular (spring) ligament is elastic. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arches_of_the_foot
- Flat-foot prevalence in one cited study was 19.0%. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arches_of_the_foot
