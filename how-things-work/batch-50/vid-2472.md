## Fact check

- Fixed solar panels are tilted and oriented toward the equator to maximise yearly energy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photovoltaic_system
- A common rule of thumb sets tilt near the site's latitude. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_panel#Positioning
- Sun elevation varies seasonally. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_zenith_angle
- Tilt also helps shed rain, dust and snow. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photovoltaic_system
