## Fact check

- Cacao domesticated about 3300 BC in southeast Ecuador (Mayo-Chinchipe); Mokaya drank cacao by 1900 BC; early Maya chocolate about 600 BC. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_chocolate
- Early drinks were bitter, frothy and watery; poured from a height for a brown foam; dried ground chilli was the most popular addition. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_chocolate
- 1828 van Houten patent for Dutch cocoa; 1847 Fry's eating chocolate in Great Britain; 1875 Daniel Peter milk chocolate. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_chocolate
