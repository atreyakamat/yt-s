## Fact check

- English crumpets have a flat top with many small pores; batter contains yeast and bicarbonate of soda was usually added from the 19th century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crumpet
- Scottish crumpets: bubbles rise to the surface during cooking leaving holes; a shallow ring restrains the batter; the spongy texture lets butter soak in. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crumpet
- Elizabeth Raffald published the first recognisable crumpet-type recipe in 1769. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crumpet
