## Fact check

- Alphonse Bertillon introduced the system in 1883; it used 10 body measurements including height, arm span, torso length, head dimensions and the lengths of the right ear, left foot, middle finger and forearm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertillon_system
- British India dropped the measurements in 1897 in favour of fingerprinting, and England followed around 1900. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertillon_system
- Adult bone lengths are stable: general anatomy, reasoning only.
