## Fact check

- Crepuscular rays are visible shafts of sunlight when the Sun is just above or below a cloud layer; almost parallel, spreading look is perspective like parallel lines meeting in the distance; name from Latin crepusculum, twilight; anticrepuscular rays appear on the opposite side of the sky; warm colour from Rayleigh scattering, longer path at dawn/dusk. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crepuscular_rays
