## Fact check

- Tank trucks commonly use internal baffles to reduce liquid surge during acceleration and braking. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank_truck
- Cross sections are often round or elliptical for strength; elliptical keeps centre of gravity low. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tank_truck
- Bar values are illustrative relative centre-of-gravity heights, not measurements.
