## Fact check

- Pneumatic jackhammers: compressed air from a compressor drives a piston that strikes the chisel; a valve alternates the air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackhammer
- Concrete is weaker in tension than compression. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
