## Fact check

- Stomach rumbles (borborygmi) are sounds from gas and fluid moving through the intestines, driven by peristalsis; hunger makes them louder because the empty stomach amplifies the sound. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borborygmus
- About two hours after the stomach empties the migrating motor complex restarts peristalsis, recurring about every hour. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borborygmus
- Swallowed air and carbonated drinks add gas; carbohydrate-rich foods can produce excess gas. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borborygmus
