## Fact check

- Belching (burping) is the audible release of gas from the oesophagus and stomach through the mouth; most air swallowed while eating and drinking cannot be absorbed and is vented by belching. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belching
- Swallowed air rises from the stomach into the oesophagus through a relaxed lower oesophageal sphincter; the distension triggers the belch reflex, which opens the upper oesophageal sphincter. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belching
- The gas is mainly oxygen and nitrogen; with beer and carbonated drinks it is mainly carbon dioxide. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belching
- Chewing gum, sucking hard candy, talking while eating or drinking can cause more air to be swallowed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belching
- In microgravity, burping is frequently associated with regurgitation ('wet burping'). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belching
- An infant accumulates gas when feeding and this needs to be vented by burping. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belching
