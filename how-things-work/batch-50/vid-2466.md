## Fact check

- Microfiber is made of threads finer than one denier with diameters under ten micrometers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microfiber
- Most microfiber is polyester, polyamide (such as nylon), or a blend. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microfiber
- Bicomponent filaments are often split into finer strands; fabrics move moisture away. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microfiber
- Washing synthetic fabrics releases microfibers, a form of microplastic. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microfiber
- Fast drying follows from thin, low-volume fabric: reasoning, no number.
