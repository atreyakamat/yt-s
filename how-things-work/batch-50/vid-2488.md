## Fact check

- Raw spinach is 91.4 g water per 100 g, about 97 kJ (23 kcal) per 100 g. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinach
- Spinach may have originated about 2,000 years ago in parts of Iran and the Indian subcontinent. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinach
- Heat makes leaf cells lose water and collapse, so the volume shrinks. This is reasoning from the water content, not a quoted figure.
