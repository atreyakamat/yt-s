## Fact check

- Most glassblowing between 870 and 1,040 C; soda-lime glass workable at about 730 C; furnace working temperature about 1,090 C. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glassblowing
- Pipe tip dipped into molten glass and collects like honey on a dipper; marvered; glory hole reheats; lehr (annealer) cools glass over hours to days to prevent cracking. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glassblowing
- Glassblowing dated to the 1st century BC, developed by Phoenician and Syrian craftsmen. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glassblowing
