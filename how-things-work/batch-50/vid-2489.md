## Fact check

- Barrel rotates on slowly turning rails; rocks slide past each other with abrasive grit and water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_tumbler
- Coarse about 60-90 mesh to shape; fine 120-220 mesh, then 400-600 mesh; optional pre-polish about 1200; final polish powder such as cerium oxide with plastic pellets. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_tumbler
- A full tumble typically takes 3-5 weeks in at least 3 steps; vibratory tumblers work about twice as fast. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_tumbler
