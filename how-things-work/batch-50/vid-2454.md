## Fact check

- Privacy filters use microlouvre film: a layer of tiny opaque slats that limits the viewing angle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microlouver
- Light at an angle is absorbed by the slats, so the picture is visible only close to head-on; brightness is somewhat reduced. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microlouver
