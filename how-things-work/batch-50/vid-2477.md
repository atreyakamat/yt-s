## Fact check

- Calendar blue moon is the second full moon in a calendar month; seasonal definition is third of four in a season; calendar blue moons occur about every 2.7 years; term appeared in the 1937 Maine Farmers' Almanac, misread by James Hugh Pruett in Sky & Telescope in 1946; atmospheric blue moon needs smoke/ash particles about 1 micrometre wide; 1883 Krakatoa eruption produced months of blue and green Sun and Moon. Synodic month 29.5 days. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_moon
- Mean synodic month 29.53 days. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_phase
