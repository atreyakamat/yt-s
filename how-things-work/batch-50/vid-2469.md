## Fact check

- Earliest evidence is a depiction in the tomb of Petosiris in Egypt, 4th century BC; tenuous evidence of a turned artifact at a Mycenaean Greek site, 13th or 14th century BC. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lathe
- Dead-centre lathes were rotated by a bow, typically horsehair, wrapped around the workpiece. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lathe
- Workpiece rotates about an axis, giving symmetry; tool rest on woodturning lathes; tailstock supports the far end; slide-rest shown in 1717 and 1772 Encyclopedie editions; Verbruggen's 1772 cannon-boring lathe. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lathe
