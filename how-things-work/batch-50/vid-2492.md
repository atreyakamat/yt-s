## Fact check

- Monocrystalline cells are cut from cylindrical ingots, giving pseudo-square wafers with clipped corners to reduce silicon waste. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monocrystalline_silicon
- Anti-reflective coatings (often silicon nitride) give cells their blue colour. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_cell
- Metal fingers and busbars on the front collect current while blocking some light. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_cell
- Not all panels are monocrystalline; polycrystalline cells are square. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polycrystalline_silicon
