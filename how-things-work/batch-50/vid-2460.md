## Fact check

- Itch is an unpleasant skin sensation that creates a strong urge to scratch; histamine-driven itch is carried by unmyelinated C fibres; itch and pain share a nerve bundle but use two distinct systems. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itch
- Itch can be inhibited by other painful stimuli such as rubbing or scratching; scratching relief is often temporary and can create an itch-scratch cycle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itch
- About 280 million people worldwide (around 4%) have difficulty with itchiness. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itch
