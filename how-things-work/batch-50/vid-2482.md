## Fact check

- Solar trackers orient panels toward the sun through the day using motors; single-axis and dual-axis types exist. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_tracker
- Trackers increase energy yield versus fixed mounts, at higher cost and maintenance. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_tracker
- Control uses sun-position calculations and/or light sensors. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_tracker
- Gauge is illustrative, not measured.
