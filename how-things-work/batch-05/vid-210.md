## Fact check

- Piezo igniters use a spring-loaded hammer that, at the end of its travel, strikes a piezoelectric ceramic (commonly lead zirconate titanate).
- The piezoelectric effect: certain crystals produce a voltage when mechanically stressed.
- A single strike produces a pulse of several thousand volts, enough to spark across a few millimetres of air.
- Piezo ignition is used in many gas lighters, gas hobs and barbecue igniters with no battery.
