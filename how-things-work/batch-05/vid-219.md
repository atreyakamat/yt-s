## Fact check

- Driver-ball contact time is commonly measured at about 0.4-0.5 milliseconds.
- High-speed footage shows the ball deforming noticeably at impact and rebounding.
- Tour average clubhead speed with a driver is roughly 110-115 mph and ball speed roughly 165-170 mph; a 'smash factor' near 1.5 means the ball leaves faster than the club.
- Typical tour driver backspin is around 2,500 rpm.
- Ball speed, launch and spin are set during contact; nothing after the ball leaves the face can change them.
- Contact time: SOFTEN to 'about half a millisecond'; a source only says the impact lasts less than a millisecond (0.5 ms is the commonly quoted figure). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golf_ball
- Pro driver ~110 mph clubhead, ~165 mph ball: OK, TrackMan PGA Tour average 113 mph club, 167 mph ball (smash factor 1.48; the legal limit is 1.5). Source: https://support.trackmangolf.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/7349802113051
- Driver backspin about 2,500 rpm: OK within 'around', PGA Tour averages are about 2,600-2,700 rpm, amateurs 2,000-2,800. Source: https://www.golfmonthly.com/features/decoding-the-launch-monitor-data-smash-factor-launch-angle-spin-rate-and-more-explained
