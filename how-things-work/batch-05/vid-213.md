## Fact check

- Pyrotechnic seatbelt pretensioners use a small gas generator, triggered by the airbag control unit, to retract webbing (by piston/cable or by rotating the spool) and remove slack.
- Activation takes on the order of 10-20 milliseconds; a blink takes roughly 100-150 ms. Both bars are labelled 'roughly'.
- Load limiters (torsion bars or tear webbing) allow controlled payout to limit belt force on the chest.
- Pyrotechnic pretensioners are single-use and are replaced after deployment ('most' because some reversible electric pretensioners exist).
- Pyrotechnic pretensioners fire once; electric ones can reset: OK, 'most' is right. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seat_belt
