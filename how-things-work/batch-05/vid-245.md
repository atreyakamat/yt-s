## Fact check

- Link boys carrying torches ('links') were hired to light pedestrians' way in London before street lighting.
- Pall Mall was lit by gas in 1807 in a public demonstration by Frederick Winsor, one of the first gas-lit streets.
- Coal gas was piped to lamps; lamplighters lit them at dusk with a pole and extinguished them at dawn.
- Around 1,500 gas street lamps still operate in London, mostly in Westminster and the City (widely reported figure, hence 'around').
