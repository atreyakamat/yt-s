## Fact check

- Most walk-through metal detectors use pulse induction: coils send short magnetic pulses.
- A changing magnetic field induces eddy currents in conductive metal.
- Eddy currents create their own decaying magnetic field, which receiver coils detect after the pulse.
- Multi-zone detectors use several coil zones to indicate the approximate height of the metal on the body.
