## Fact check

- QR codes encode bits as dark/light modules.
- They use Reed-Solomon error correction; level H recovers about 30% of codewords (levels L/M/Q/H ≈ 7/15/25/30%).
- Logos placed over a QR code rely on this error correction.
- QR code was invented in 1994 by Denso Wave (Japan) to track automotive parts.
- QR code created in 1994 by Masahiro Hara at Denso Wave to track car parts: OK. Source: https://www.aist.go.jp/pdf/aist_e/synthesiology_e/vol12_no1/vol12_01_p19_p28.pdf
- Error-correction level H restores about 30% of codewords: OK (7% L, 15% M, 25% Q, 30% H); it is codewords, not exactly 30% of the visible area. Source: https://www.tec-it.com/en/support/knowbase/symbologies/qrcode
