## Fact check

- Classic denim uses indigo-dyed warp (lengthwise) yarns and undyed white weft yarns.
- Standard denim is a 3/1 twill: each warp yarn floats over three weft yarns and under one, offset one step per row, creating diagonal lines.
- Because the warp dominates the face, the front looks blue and the back looks pale.
- Twill is a dense, durable weave and its texture hides soiling: why it suited workwear.
