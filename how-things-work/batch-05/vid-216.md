## Fact check

- Card authorisation flows from terminal to acquirer (the merchant's payment processor), through the card network, to the issuing bank, which approves or declines.
- EMV chip/contactless cards generate a transaction cryptogram for each payment.
- Authorisation typically completes in about one to a few seconds.
- Actual settlement of funds happens later, usually in batches.
