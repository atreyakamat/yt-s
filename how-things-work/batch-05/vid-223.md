## Fact check

- Plate temperature → straighteners typically run about 150-230°C; around 200°C is common.
- PTC (positive temperature coefficient) ceramic heaters → resistance rises sharply above a set temperature, limiting current; widely used in straighteners (many also add a sensor and thermostat).
- Hydrogen bonds → heat and water break the hydrogen bonds in keratin; they reform as hair cools, and water (humidity) breaks them again, letting curl return.
- Hair straightener plates around 200 C: typical figure, not independently sourced this pass.
