## Fact check

- Hand signals were the original way to signal turns and are still taught in many driving codes.
- Trafficators (semaphore arms that swung out and lit) were common on European cars from the 1920s into the 1950s.
- Actress Florence Lawrence is widely credited with designing an 'auto signaling arm' for her car around 1914 (she did not patent it).
- Flashing electric turn signals were introduced by a US carmaker in the late 1930s (1939 model year).
- Regulations set flash rates of about 60-120 flashes per minute (UN R48: 90 ± 30 per minute), i.e. once or twice a second.
