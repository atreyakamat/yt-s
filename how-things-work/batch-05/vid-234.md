## Fact check

- The Magic 8 Ball contains a 20-sided die (icosahedron) floating in dark blue liquid, read through a window.
- Each face carries one answer in raised letters; the die floats up so one face presses against the window.
- The standard answers are 10 affirmative, 5 non-committal and 5 negative.
- So, assuming fair faces, about half of all readings are some form of yes.
- Magic 8 Ball has 20 answers on an icosahedron die: 10 affirmative, 5 non-committal, 5 negative: OK. Source: https://www.wikipedia.com/wiki/Magic_8_ball
