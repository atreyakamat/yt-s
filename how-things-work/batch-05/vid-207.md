## Fact check

- Sparkling wine bottles hold roughly 5-6 bar of pressure, about six times atmospheric (often quoted as about three times a car tyre's gauge pressure; car tyres run about 2-2.5 bar).
- High-speed filming (Liger-Belair and others, published 2019) showed the cork leaves in about a millisecond and the escaping CO2 jet is briefly supersonic.
- Rapid (adiabatic) expansion cools the gas sharply, condensing a small fog/cloud at the bottle neck.
- Bubbling follows because CO2 comes out of solution once the pressure is released.
