## Fact check

- Elisha Otis developed his safety brake in 1852-53 and demonstrated it at the New York Crystal Palace exhibition in 1854, having the hoisting rope cut while he stood on the platform.
- His device used a spring that, when rope tension was lost, forced pawls into ratchet teeth on the guide rails.
- Before safe passenger elevators, buildings were limited mostly to about five or six storeys. 
- Modern elevators have safety gear triggered by an overspeed governor.
