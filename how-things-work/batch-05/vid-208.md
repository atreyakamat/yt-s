## Fact check

- Electromagnetic drive → sonic toothbrushes use a resonant electromagnetic drive (magnet and coil) rather than a rotating motor.
- About 260 Hz → a widely used sonic toothbrush design runs at about 260 Hz; 'many', since models vary.
- Over 30,000 strokes a minute → 260 × 2 directions × 60 s ≈ 31,200, matching makers' 'about 31,000 brush strokes per minute'.
- Middle C ≈ 261.6 Hz → standard tuning; 260 Hz is audible as a hum near that pitch.
- About 2 ms per stroke → 1 / (260 × 2) ≈ 1.9 ms.
