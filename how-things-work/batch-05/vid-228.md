## Fact check

- Optical rain sensors (mounted behind the windscreen near the mirror) send infrared light into the glass at an angle so it totally internally reflects back to a photodiode when the glass is dry.
- Water on the outer surface changes the refractive boundary so some light escapes; the drop in returned light triggers the wipers and sets their speed.
- Many sensor modules combine rain and ambient-light sensing for automatic headlights.
