## Fact check

- Permanent markers contain a colourant, a resin binder, and a fast-evaporating solvent such as alcohol (older types used xylene or toluene).
- After evaporation, the water-insoluble resin binds the colour to the surface.
- Alcohol-based solvents can redissolve the resin, which is why rubbing alcohol helps remove it.
- The odour is the evaporating solvent; uncapped markers dry out.
