## Fact check

- Dual-clutch transmissions have two input shafts/clutches: one for odd gears, one for even gears (and usually reverse).
- The control unit pre-selects the next gear on the idle shaft; shifting swaps clutches, keeping torque interruption minimal.
- DCT shift times are commonly quoted at around 100 ms or less; a manual shift by a person typically takes several hundred ms. Both labelled 'roughly'.
