## Fact check

- Manual gearboxes use selector rails (rods), each with a fork that engages one of two gears depending on direction (e.g. 1-2, 3-4, 5-6/reverse).
- Lateral stick movement selects the rail; fore/aft movement shifts it; the resulting gate traces an H (a double H for 5-6 speeds).
- Neutral is the central cross-plane; an interlock prevents two rails moving at once so two gears can't engage.
