## Fact check

- Keyboard switches are arranged in a row/column matrix scanned by a microcontroller.
- Mechanical contacts bounce for a few milliseconds; firmware debounces before registering a press.
- USB keyboards are commonly polled at 125 Hz by default; many gaming keyboards use 1,000 Hz.
- USB keyboards poll at 125 Hz by default, 1000 Hz for gaming: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_keyboard
