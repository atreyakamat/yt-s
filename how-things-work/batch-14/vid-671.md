## Fact check

- The tagine is a traditional North African (especially Moroccan) clay cooking pot with a shallow base and conical lid; it is known for cooking with little added liquid.
- The lid's tip is farthest from the heat and coolest, so steam condenses on the inner walls and runs back into the dish, limiting moisture loss.
- The wide, shallow base holds food in a single layer.
