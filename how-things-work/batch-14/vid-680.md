## Fact check

- Overhead transmission conductors are normally bare; air provides insulation, with clearance kept by height and insulator strings.
- The common conductor is ACSR: aluminium strands around a steel core; aluminium (about 2.7 g/cm³) is far lighter than copper (about 8.9 g/cm³) and conducts well, and steel adds tensile strength.
- Conductors heat with current and ambient temperature, expand and sag more.
- A bird on a single wire is safe because there is no path to a different potential.
