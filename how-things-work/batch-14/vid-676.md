## Fact check

- A SIM is a smart card with a microprocessor and memory storing the IMSI (subscriber identity) and secret key Ki, also held by the operator.
- Authentication is challenge-response: the network sends a random number (RAND); the SIM computes a response with Ki and returns it. Ki never leaves the SIM.
- First SIMs (1991) were full credit-card size; mini-SIM followed in the mid-1990s; nano-SIM in 2012; eSIMs are embedded chips.
