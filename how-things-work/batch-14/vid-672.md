## Fact check

- UHT (ultra-high temperature) treatment heats milk to about 135-150 °C (commonly around 140 °C) for a few seconds (typically 2-5 s).
- Normal HTST pasteurisation is about 72 °C for 15 s.
- UHT sterilises the milk, including spores; it's then aseptically packaged in sterile cartons and keeps for months unopened at room temperature.
- The heat causes slight Maillard/cooked flavours, often described as a caramel note.
- Once opened it must be refrigerated like normal milk.
- UHT: above 140 C for about 2-5 seconds; flash pasteurisation 72 C: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra-high-temperature_processing
