## Fact check

- Reusable heat packs contain a supersaturated solution of sodium acetate (trihydrate) that stays liquid below its melting point (about 58 °C).
- Flexing the metal disc creates nucleation sites; crystallisation then propagates through the pack in seconds.
- Crystallisation releases the latent heat of fusion; packs typically reach around 50-54 °C.
- Heating the pack in boiling water redissolves the crystals so it can be reused.
- Reusable sodium acetate packs get warm to roughly 50-54 C after the disc is clicked; softened visual to about 50. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hand_warmer (mechanism); temperature from common product guidance, not independently fetched.
