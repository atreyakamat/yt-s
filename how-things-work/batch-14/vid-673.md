## Fact check

- Click-clack (push-button) wastes → a spring-loaded plug on a stem with a push-push latch.
- Push-push latch → uses ramps and notches that rotate a cam on each press, alternately locking and releasing; the same principle as retractable ballpoint pens.
- Hair build-up → common cause of poor sealing; most unscrew for cleaning.
