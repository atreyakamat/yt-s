## Fact check

- Bank cards follow ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1: 85.60 × 53.98 mm with rounded corners; the same format is used for many driving licences and ID cards.
- Width/height ≈ 1.586, close to the golden ratio (≈1.618).
- Chip contact and magnetic stripe positions are also standardised (ISO/IEC 7816, 7811) so readers can locate them.
- Grid count is illustrative.
- ID-1 card is 85.60 x 53.98 mm (ratio 1.586, shown as 1.59): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO/IEC_7810
