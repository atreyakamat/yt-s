## Fact check

- Contactless payments use NFC at 13.56 MHz, with a practical range of about 4 cm.
- Passive contactless cards are powered by the reader's field via an antenna coil (inductive coupling).
- EMV contactless transactions generate a unique cryptogram per transaction; phone wallets also use a token instead of the real card number.
- Magnetic stripes carry static data that can be copied.
- NFC works at 13.56 MHz over a few centimetres (about 4-10 cm): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Near-field_communication
