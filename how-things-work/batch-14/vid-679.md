## Fact check

- Active fin stabilisers are fitted to many cruise ships, ferries and yachts; they extend from the hull below the waterline.
- Gyroscopic/motion sensors detect roll; a control system adjusts the fins' angle to create counteracting lift.
- Fins generate force from water flowing past them, so they work best when the ship is moving (zero-speed fins exist on some yachts).
- They can greatly reduce roll; many retract into the hull in port.
