## Fact check

- Internal-frame packs use stays or a framesheet to transfer load to the hip belt.
- Outdoor retailers commonly advise that a well-fitted hip belt carries about 80% of the load, with the rest on the shoulders.
- Correct fit centres the belt over the iliac crest (top of the hip bones), not the soft waist; fitting order is hip belt first, then shoulder straps.
- No medical claims are made.
- Hip belt can carry most of the load, 'up to about 90%' per encyclopedia, so about 80% is within range: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backpack
