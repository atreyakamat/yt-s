## Fact check

- Infrared emitter and detector → automatic taps use an active IR sensor: an LED sends pulses and a photodiode detects reflections.
- Solenoid valve → an electromagnet-operated valve opens and closes the water flow.
- Shuts when hands leave → the controller closes the valve when the reflection disappears (often after a short delay).
- Touch-free and can't be left running → the main reasons they're used in public washrooms.
