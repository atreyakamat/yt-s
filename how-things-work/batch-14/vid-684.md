## Fact check

- Cylinder music boxes have a spring-driven, pinned cylinder that plucks the tuned teeth of a steel comb.
- Longer, heavier teeth sound lower; bass teeth often have weights added underneath.
- A small fan-shaped air brake (governor) regulates the speed so the tune plays at a steady tempo.
- Placing the box on a resonant surface such as a wooden table makes it louder, as the surface vibrates with it.
