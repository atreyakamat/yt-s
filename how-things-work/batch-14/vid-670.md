## Fact check

- High-visibility garments combine fluorescent background material (for daylight) and retroreflective tape (for headlights at night), as in EN ISO 20471 / ANSI 107.
- Fluorescent pigments absorb shorter wavelengths, including UV, and re-emit visible light, appearing brighter than ordinary colours.
- Studies on 'biological motion' or 'biomotion' show retroreflective markings on moving joints (ankles, knees, wrists, elbows) help drivers recognise pedestrians sooner. Bars are illustrative.
