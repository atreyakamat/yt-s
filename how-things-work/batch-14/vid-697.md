## Fact check

- A chalk line is a reel of string in a case filled with powdered chalk; the string is coated as it is pulled out.
- A string under tension between two points lies along the straight line between them (ignoring small sag over long spans).
- Lifting and releasing the taut string makes it strike the surface and transfer chalk, marking a straight line almost instantly.
- Chalk lines are commonly tens of metres (e.g. 30 m / 100 ft) long.
