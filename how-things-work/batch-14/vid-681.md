## Fact check

- Mechanical pencils use a clutch: split jaws held closed by a ring; pressing the button pushes the jaws forward out of the ring so they open, and a spring returns them.
- Many have a rubber lead retainer at the tip that holds the lead between clicks.
- Common lead diameters are 0.3, 0.5, 0.7 and 0.9 mm.
- Leads are made to a fixed diameter, so they don't need sharpening.
