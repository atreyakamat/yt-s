## Fact check

- The cylinder on some cables is a ferrite bead/choke: a ring of ferrite ceramic around the cable.
- Ferrite adds impedance at high frequencies, dissipating common-mode RF noise as heat while passing DC and low-frequency signals.
- Cables can radiate and pick up electromagnetic interference; electronics must meet EMC emission limits (e.g., FCC/CE rules).
- Many modern devices put filtering components on the PCB instead.
