## Fact check

- Onion cells store sulfur-containing compounds (sulfoxides) separately from the enzyme alliinase.
- Cutting ruptures the cells; enzymes rapidly convert the compounds, and lachrymatory-factor synthase forms syn-propanethial-S-oxide, a volatile irritant gas.
- The gas stimulates sensory nerves in the eye, triggering tears that dilute and flush it.
- This system is generally considered a chemical defence against animals.
- Chilling reduces volatility, and a sharp knife crushes fewer cells, so less gas is released.
