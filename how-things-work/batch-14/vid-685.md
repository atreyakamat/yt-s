## Fact check

- Foam earplugs use slow-recovery polyurethane or PVC foam, which is compressed, inserted and expands to fill the ear canal.
- Ear canals are irregular and curved, so soft expanding foam seals better than a rigid plug.
- Rated noise reductions for foam plugs are often around 29-33 dB (NRR); real-world reduction depends on fit.
- Decibels are logarithmic: a 30 dB reduction is a factor of 1,000 in sound intensity.
