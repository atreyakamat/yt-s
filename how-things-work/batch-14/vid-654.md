## Fact check

- Many quality playing cards are made of two layers of paper laminated with a dark (black, blue or grey) adhesive core, often called black-core or blue-core stock.
- The dark core makes the card opaque so the face can't be seen through the back against light.
- Many decks have an embossed 'air-cushion' style finish with tiny dimples that reduce friction and help cards slide and fan.
- Rounded corners reduce dog-earing; corner indices let players read cards held in a fan.
