## Fact check

- Most plug-in surge protectors use metal oxide varistors (MOVs) connected across live/neutral/earth.
- MOVs have high resistance at normal voltage and conduct sharply above their clamping voltage, diverting surge energy; response times are in the nanosecond range.
- MOVs degrade with each significant surge; many surge strips have a 'protected' indicator that goes out when protection is lost.
- Surges come from lightning and from switching of large loads on the grid.
