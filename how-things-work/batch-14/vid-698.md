## Fact check

- Side-release buckles have a male part with two flexible prongs and a centre guide, and a female housing with side openings.
- The prongs flex inward on insertion and snap out into the openings, latching against their edges; the click is the snap.
- Releasing requires pressing both exposed tabs inward at once in the standard design.
