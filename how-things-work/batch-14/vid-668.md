## Fact check

- Raised heels on footwear are linked to horse riding: a heel stops the foot sliding through the stirrup (a dragging risk in falls).
- Historians (e.g. the Bata Shoe Museum) trace heels to Persian cavalry around the 10th century; they spread to European fashion around 1600.
- Cowboy boots keep a tall, angled ('underslung') riding heel for stirrup grip.
- Persian riders' heeled boots, 10th century; reached Europe in the early 1600s: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-heeled_shoe
