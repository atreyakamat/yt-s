## Fact check

- Ridged ('crinkle-cut') crisps are cut with a wavy/corrugated blade.
- Corrugation increases bending stiffness, the same principle used in corrugated cardboard, so ridged crisps break less when dipping.
- Ridges increase surface area, giving more area for seasoning.
- Ridged crisps are commonly sliced thicker than standard thin crisps ('often').
