## Fact check

- Perforations → a line of cuts separated by uncut 'ties' or bridges; the standard way toilet paper sheets are separated.
- Stress concentrates at the perforation → less material along the line means it fails there first.
- 1857 flat sheets → Joseph Gayetty sold packaged flat toilet paper in the US from 1857.
- Perforated roll → Seth Wheeler's patents for perforated rolled paper date from 1871 (wrapping paper), 1883 (mentions toilet use), 1890 and 1891 (toilet-paper roll); sources disagree on which was first, so the script says 'late 1800s'. Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/US272369A and https://patents.google.com/patent/US422866
- Stamps and tickets → also separated by perforations.
