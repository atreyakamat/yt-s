## Fact check

- Trigger sprayers are small piston pumps: the trigger drives a piston into a chamber, and a return spring draws it back.
- Two check valves (often a ball valve at the inlet and a flap or ball at the outlet) keep flow one-way; liquid refills via the dip tube.
- A swirl chamber in the nozzle imparts rotation so the liquid exits as a hollow cone and breaks into droplets; adjustable nozzles switch between swirl (spray) and direct (stream).
