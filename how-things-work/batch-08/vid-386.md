## Fact check

- Espresso is conventionally brewed at about 9 bar, roughly nine times atmospheric pressure (about 1 bar), using an electric pump (vibratory or rotary) in most machines.
- Recommended brew water temperature is about 90-96 °C, just below boiling.
- A typical shot takes about 25-30 seconds. Crema is an emulsion of coffee oils and CO2 bubbles formed under pressure.
- Drip coffee makers rely on gravity to pass water through the grounds.
