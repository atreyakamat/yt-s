## Fact check

- US currency paper is 75% cotton and 25% linen (US Bureau of Engraving and Printing).
- The BEP states a note can withstand about 4,000 double folds before tearing.
- US currency paper contains small red and blue security fibres.
- Cotton fibres are longer than typical wood pulp fibres, giving greater strength and wet resistance.
