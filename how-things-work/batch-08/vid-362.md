## Fact check

- Ray Tomlinson sent the first networked email on ARPANET in 1971 and chose @ to separate user and host.
- @ was used commercially to mean 'at the rate of'; a 1536 letter by a Florentine merchant is an early known use.
- @ appeared on some typewriters in the late 1800s; it was rarely used in the 1960s.
- Italian 'chiocciola' (snail); Dutch 'apenstaartje' (monkey's tail).
- The gauge value is illustrative.
- 1536 Florentine merchant letter and Tomlinson 1971: OK. Typewriter date softened to '1800s'. Source: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/arts-culture/the-accidental-history-of-the-symbol-18054936/
