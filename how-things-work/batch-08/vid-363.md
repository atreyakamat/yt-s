## Fact check

- Seat belts were used in early aircraft; most cars had no belts until the 1950s-60s, when lap belts started appearing.
- Lap-only belts restrain the pelvis but let the upper body jackknife forward.
- Nils Bohlin, an engineer at Volvo, developed the three-point belt introduced in 1959; Volvo made the patent available to other manufacturers free of charge.
- Compulsory belt laws began in the 1970s (Victoria, Australia, 1970 was first) and spread worldwide over the following decades.
- Bohlin three-point belt 1959, Volvo let other carmakers use it free: OK. Source: https://www.hagerty.co.uk/articles/automotive-history/sixty-years-of-volvos-seatbelt-patent
