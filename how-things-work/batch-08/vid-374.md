## Fact check

- Overhead hydraulic door closers contain a spring compressed when the door opens and a piston in an oil-filled cylinder.
- Closing speed is controlled by oil flowing through small adjustable valves; separate valves usually set sweep speed and latch speed.
- Without the hydraulic damping the spring would slam the door.
