## Fact check

- Edge thinner than a hair → razor edges taper to a tip radius well under a micrometre; human hair is roughly 20-180 µm thick.
- Coatings → modern blades use hard chromium-based coatings and a PTFE (non-stick polymer) layer to reduce friction.
- 50 times softer and the 2020 study → Roscioli, Taheri-Mousavi and Tasan (MIT), Science, 2020: hair, about 50 times softer than steel, caused chipping where the steel microstructure was heterogeneous and hair met the edge at an angle.
- MIT 2020: hair about 50x softer than steel yet chips razor edges: OK. Source: https://news.mit.edu/2020/why-shaving-dulls-razors-0806
