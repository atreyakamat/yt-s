## Fact check

- Instant coffee is made from brewed coffee extract, concentrated, then dried by spray drying or freeze drying.
- Spray drying atomises concentrate into hot air in a tall tower; droplets dry into powder.
- Freeze drying freezes the extract (around -40 °C is typical), grinds it into granules, then sublimates the ice under vacuum.
- Sublimation means ice turns to vapour without melting.
