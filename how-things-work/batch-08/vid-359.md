## Fact check

- Before piped supply, households drew water from wells, rivers and public pumps or fountains.
- 1 litre of water weighs 1 kg, so a 10 L bucket is 10 kg.
- Water carriers sold water door to door in many historic cities (e.g. London's water-bearers, Paris porteurs d'eau).
- In 1854 John Snow linked a Soho cholera outbreak to the Broad Street pump.
- Roman aqueducts fed public fountains; indoor taps became normal in most Western homes during the 1900s.
