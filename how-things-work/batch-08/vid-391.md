## Fact check

- In-ear headphones rely on an acoustic seal for low-frequency response; leaks cause significant bass loss (well documented in headphone acoustics).
- Active noise cancellation performance also depends on passive seal; manufacturers recommend choosing the right tip size.
- Some earbud companion apps include an ear-tip fit test that plays a sound and measures it with the inner microphone.
