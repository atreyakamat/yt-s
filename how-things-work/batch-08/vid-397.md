## Fact check

- The 4-to-1 rule (base 1 unit out per 4 units of working height) is standard guidance from safety bodies such as OSHA and the UK HSE; it gives an angle of about 75 degrees.
- The elbow/arm test (toes at the feet, arms extended horizontally reach a rung) is a widely taught check for this angle.
- Ladders used for access to an upper level should extend about 1 m (HSE) or 3 ft (OSHA) above the landing.
- Keeping the belt buckle within the stiles and maintaining three points of contact are standard ladder-safety advice.
