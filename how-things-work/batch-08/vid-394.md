## Fact check

- Elevator controllers latch a hall call on the first press; the lit button indicates the registered call. Repeat presses don't change priority.
- Group controllers assign cars based on direction of travel, position and pending calls.
- Pressing both up and down registers two calls, so a car going the wrong way may stop needlessly.
- Unnecessary stops lengthen trips for other passengers. The gauge is qualitative.
