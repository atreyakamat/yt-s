## Fact check

- Two-part epoxy adhesives consist of an epoxy resin and a hardener (curing agent, e.g. amines); mixing starts a polymerisation that forms a cross-linked thermoset network.
- The curing reaction is exothermic; large masses can heat up noticeably.
- Many epoxies reach handling strength in minutes to hours and full cure in about 24 hours (varies by product).
- Because curing is chemical rather than solvent evaporation, epoxy fills gaps, shrinks little, and cures in enclosed joints.
