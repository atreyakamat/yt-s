## Fact check

- Otto Rohwedder, a jeweller from Iowa, began working on a bread slicer around 1912; his machine first went into commercial use in July 1928 at the Chillicothe Baking Company in Missouri.
- Bakers had worried that pre-sliced bread would go stale quickly; Rohwedder's machine also wrapped the sliced loaf.
- Sliced bread spread rapidly in the US in the 1930s.
- Rohwedder's slicer used by Chillicothe Baking Co., Missouri, first sliced loaf 7 July 1928; started work 1912: OK. Source: https://asbe.org/?p=1662
