## Fact check

- Harry Coover found cyanoacrylates in 1942 at Eastman Kodak while searching for clear plastics for gun sights; they were rejected as too sticky.
- In 1951 Coover and Fred Joyner rediscovered them (Joyner stuck a pair of refractometer prisms together), and Coover recognised their use as an adhesive.
- The first product, Eastman 910, went on sale in 1958.
- Cyanoacrylate polymerises rapidly when it contacts traces of water (hydroxide ions) on surfaces, so it bonds in seconds; moisture in air sets glue left in the nozzle.
- Coover 1942 gunsights, rediscovered 1951, sold 1958: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Coover
