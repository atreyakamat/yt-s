## Fact check

- Before the stethoscope, doctors used immediate auscultation, placing the ear on the chest.
- René Laennec invented the stethoscope in 1816 in Paris, first by rolling paper into a tube while examining a young woman; he published his work in 1819.
- His early stethoscopes were monaural wooden tubes; the name comes from Greek 'stethos' (chest) and 'skopein' (to look/examine).
- Binaural stethoscopes appeared in the 1850s; the diaphragm chest piece came later.
- Laennec 1816, treatise 1819: OK. Source: https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/rene-laennec
