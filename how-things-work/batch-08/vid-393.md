## Fact check

- The recirculation setting closes the fresh-air intake flap so the HVAC fan recirculates cabin air; useful for outside fumes/dust and for faster cooling with A/C.
- Occupants' breath adds water vapour; with no fresh air exchange, humidity rises and condenses on cold glass (fogging).
- Studies of car cabins show CO2 concentration rises substantially with recirculation on, especially with several occupants over long drives.
- No health advice is given; the script says to use it in short bursts, matching common manufacturer guidance.
