## Fact check

- Steven Sasson built the first self-contained digital camera at Eastman Kodak in 1975.
- It weighed about 8 pounds (3.6 kg), captured 100 x 100 pixel (0.01 MP) black-and-white images, and took about 23 seconds to record an image to a cassette tape; images were viewed on a TV.
- Modern phone cameras have 12-200 MP, i.e. thousands of times more pixels.
- Sasson 1975 camera: about 8 lb (3.6 kg), 0.01 MP, 23 s to tape, black and white: OK. Source: https://spectrum.ieee.org/first-digital-camera-history
