## Fact check

- Speech-enabled ('talking') ATMs offer audio instructions through a standard 3.5 mm headphone jack for blind and visually impaired users; the audio is private.
- Some ATMs offer a screen-blanking option in audio mode (script says 'some').
- Keypads follow the telephone layout with a raised dot on the 5 key for orientation.
- The 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design (section 707) require ATMs to be speech enabled with private listening; they apply to new and altered machines.
