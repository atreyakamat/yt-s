## Fact check

- Automatic inflatable life jackets contain a CO2 cartridge and a firing mechanism with a water-soluble element (bobbin or pellet) restraining a spring-loaded pin.
- When the element dissolves, the spring drives the pin to pierce the cartridge, inflating the bladder within seconds ('about 5 s' is a typical figure).
- Hydrostatic versions trigger from water pressure at a small depth, reducing accidental firing from rain or spray.
- They also have a manual pull cord and an oral inflation tube.
