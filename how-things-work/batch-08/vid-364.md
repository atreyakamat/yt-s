## Fact check

- Local mean time shifts 4 minutes per degree of longitude (360° in 24 hours).
- Bristol, about 2.6° west of Greenwich, kept local time about 10 minutes behind London.
- The Great Western Railway adopted London time in 1840; most British railways had followed by 1847-48 ('Railway time'); Britain made GMT legal time in 1880.
- US and Canadian railroads introduced standard time zones on 18 November 1883.
- The station grid is illustrative.
