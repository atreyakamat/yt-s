## Fact check

- Concentrator nozzle → the standard name for the flat slotted nozzle supplied with most hair dryers.
- It narrows the airflow → a smaller outlet turns the same airflow into a narrower, faster, directed stream.
- Aim down the hair to dry smoother → common stylist advice: airflow from root to tip lays the cuticle flat.
- Keep a gap → manufacturers' instructions advise not to hold the outlet against hair, as heat concentrates in one spot.
