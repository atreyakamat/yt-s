## Fact check

- Garments often come with a small bag holding spare buttons and a fabric swatch.
- The swatch is intended for testing cleaning products, detergents and wash temperatures for colourfastness and shrinkage before treating the garment: common retailer and care-guide advice.
- Dry cleaners can test solvents on it: same purpose.
