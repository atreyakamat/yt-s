## Fact check

- Cargo ships take on ballast water when lightly loaded to maintain draft, stability, propeller and rudder immersion.
- Without ballast, a light ship rides high, has more windage and can be less stable and harder to control.
- Ballast tanks are typically in the double bottom and wing (side) tanks.
- Ballast is discharged when cargo is loaded; international rules now require treating it to stop spreading marine species.
