## Fact check

- A piano key is a lever; pressing it drives the action that throws a felt-covered hammer at the string(s).
- The escapement releases the hammer just before impact so it rebounds and the string can sustain.
- Each key lifts a damper while held; releasing the key lets the damper silence the string.
- A modern grand has 88 keys and around 220-240 strings, since most treble and middle notes use 2-3 strings each.
- Total string tension is commonly cited at about 18-20 tons, carried by a cast-iron plate.
- About 230 strings, about 20 tons of tension, 88 keys: OK, varies by model. Source: https://www.yamaha.com/en/musical_instrument_guide/piano/manufacturing/manufacturing003.html
