## Fact check

- Common refrigerator ice makers: a solenoid water valve fills a mould for a few seconds; a thermostat waits until the mould is well below freezing.
- A heater under the mould then warms it briefly to free the cubes, and a motor turns ejector arms that push the crescent (half-moon) cubes into the bin.
- A wire shut-off arm (or an optical sensor in some models) stops production when the bin is full.
