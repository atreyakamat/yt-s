## Fact check

- Kingsford was established in 1920 by Henry Ford (Orin Stafford later patented the briquette method),  using waste such as stumps, branches and sawdust from his sawmill and plants. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingsford_(charcoal)
- Edward G. Kingsford, a Michigan timber cruiser, was enlisted by Ford to locate wood. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingsford_(charcoal)
- Briquettes may use starch as a binder. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charcoal_briquette
