## Fact check

- A greenhouse works largely by trapping warm air so it can't mix with cooler air outside (reduced convection). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenhouse
- Tomatoes are buzz pollinated; bumblebees are used commercially in greenhouses. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buzz_pollination
