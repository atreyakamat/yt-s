## Fact check

- Chimney (stack) effect: hot air rises, drawing fresh air in at the base and increasing combustion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stack_effect
- Chimney starters light charcoal using paper beneath a steel cylinder (qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charcoal
