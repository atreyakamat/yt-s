## Fact check

- Auto-belays use a retractable strap/rope on a spool with a speed-sensing brake that slows a falling climber; spring retracts slack. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belaying
- Climbing gyms commonly use auto-belay devices. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climbing_gym
