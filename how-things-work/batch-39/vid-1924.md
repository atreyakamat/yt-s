## Fact check

- Body-fat scales use bioelectrical impedance analysis: a small current is passed through the body and resistance measured. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioelectrical_impedance_analysis
- Estimates use height, age and sex formulas; hydration affects readings. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioelectrical_impedance_analysis
