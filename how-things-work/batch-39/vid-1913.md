## Fact check

- Real greenhouses mainly retain heat by blocking the movement of air; the atmospheric greenhouse effect works differently, by limiting radiative heat loss. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenhouse_effect
- Glass admits sunlight which warms surfaces inside; vents release hot air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenhouse
