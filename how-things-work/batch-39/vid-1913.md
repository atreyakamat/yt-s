## Fact check

- Photosynthesis uses the PAR range, 400 to 700 nm; blue 400-500, green 500-600, red 600-700 nm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_light
- Red and blue together are the most cost-efficient combination, but plants grow better when green is added. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_light
- Individual LEDs emit narrow colour ranges so manufacturers mix them in set proportions. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_light
- High-pressure sodium lamps emit more red and yellow and plants tend to elongate because they lack blue and UV. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_light
