## Fact check

- Most retail goods once had no fixed price; Quakers objected to different prices for different customers; John Wanamaker opened his Philadelphia store in 1874 with the Quaker honest price; Macy's in the 1870s used price tags, ending haggling and shortening clerk training. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_tag
- Piggly Wiggly 1916 as first self-service store. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_tag
