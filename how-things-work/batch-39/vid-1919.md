## Fact check

- A lever balances when the torques about the fulcrum are equal; torque is force times perpendicular distance. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torque
- Seesaws are levers with a central fulcrum. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seesaw
