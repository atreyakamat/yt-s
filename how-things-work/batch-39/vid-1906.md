## Fact check

- A quadcopter has four rotors, two spinning clockwise and two anticlockwise so that their torques cancel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadcopter
- Movement comes from varying individual rotor speeds: tilting for translation, speeding a diagonal pair for yaw. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadcopter
- A flight controller uses gyroscopes and accelerometers and updates control loops at hundreds or thousands of times per second (typical; script says 'hundreds'). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flight_controller
