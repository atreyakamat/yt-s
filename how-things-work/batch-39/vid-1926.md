## Fact check

- Containers have standardised corner fittings (corner castings) used for lifting and securing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermodal_container
- Spreaders lock onto the corner castings with twistlocks that rotate (about a quarter turn) to lock. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spreader_(crane) ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twistlock
- Ship-to-shore container cranes often have the operator cab on the trolley so the driver can look down at the load. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Container_crane
