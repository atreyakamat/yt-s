## Fact check

- Each channel strip has gain/trim, equalisation, a fader and pan; gain is usually set as high as possible before clipping. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixing_console
- Channel counts: about six on a small coffeehouse stage, about 24 on a nightclub rock stage, up to 96 in a professional studio. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixing_console
