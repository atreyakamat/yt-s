## Fact check

- Portland cement clinker is produced in a rotary kiln at about 1,450 C from limestone and clay, then ground with gypsum. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_cement
- Lava erupts at roughly 700 to 1,200 C; the bar uses about 1,100 as a round figure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lava
- Cement hardens by hydration, a chemical reaction with water, not by drying. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_cement
