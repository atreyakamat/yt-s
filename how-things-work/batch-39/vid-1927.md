## Fact check

- Tubes need a cathode heater, a control grid between cathode and plate, and a plate (anode) collecting current. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacuum-tube_amplifier
- Tubes run at higher voltage and lower current than transistors; an output transformer matches their high output impedance to a low-impedance speaker. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacuum-tube_amplifier
- Clipping is less abrupt than in solid-state amps; tube amps remain a standard for many professional guitarists. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacuum-tube_amplifier
