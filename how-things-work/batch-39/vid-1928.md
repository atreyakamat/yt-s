## Fact check

- Centre-pivot irrigation uses motorised towers moving around a central pivot, producing circular crop areas visible from the air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center-pivot_irrigation
- Outer towers move faster to keep the line straight. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center-pivot_irrigation
