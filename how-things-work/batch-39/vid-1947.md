## Fact check

- In a counterweight fly system, battens hang from lift lines connected to an arbor loaded with weights matched to the load; usually one flyman operates it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fly_system
- Raising the arbor lowers the batten and vice versa; older hemp systems used manila rope and sandbags, and the terminology borrows from ship rigging because early stagehands were often sailors. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fly_system
