## Fact check

- Slip casting pours a liquid clay slip into a porous plaster mould that absorbs water; excess slip is poured out to leave a hollow cast. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slip_casting
- It is widely used for sanitary ware such as toilets and for pottery. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slip_casting
