## Fact check

- Meteors are the light from meteoroids entering the atmosphere; many meteoroids are about the size of a grain of sand or small pebble. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteoroid
- Entry speeds are roughly 11 to 72 km/s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteor
- Meteors typically appear around 75-100 km altitude and the glow is produced by ablation and heated air; they last a second or so. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meteor
- Airliners cruise at about 10-12 km. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cruising_altitude
