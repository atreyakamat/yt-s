## Fact check

- A keel provides lateral resistance to prevent leeway (sideways drift) and ballast to counter heeling. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keel
- Sailboats can sail at an angle to the wind (upwind) because the keel resists sideways motion and converts sail force into forward motion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing_into_the_wind
