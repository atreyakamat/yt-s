## Fact check

- Woofers reproduce low frequencies and tweeters high frequencies; a crossover circuit splits the signal between drivers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audio_crossover
- Multi-way speakers add a midrange driver for the frequencies between woofer and tweeter. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loudspeaker
