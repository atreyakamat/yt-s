## Fact check

- Agricultural tractors commonly have larger rear drive wheels; tyre size spreads load and improves traction on soft ground. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tractor
- Agricultural tyre tread is in a lugged (chevron) pattern for traction and self-cleaning. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tractor
