## Fact check

- Eli Whitney's cotton gin was patented in 1794 (built 1793); it uses a toothed drum and a grate to separate fibre from seeds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_gin
- Hand-cleaning: about ten hours of labour to separate one pound of fibre, so roughly a pound per working day; Whitney applied Oct 1793, patent granted 14 Mar 1794; the gin expanded cotton production and slavery in the US South. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_gin
