## Fact check

- Soft serve is made by freezing a liquid mix in a barrel with a scraper (dasher) while incorporating air, and is served at a higher temperature than hard ice cream. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_serve
- Air content (overrun) makes ice cream lighter. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_cream
