## Fact check

- Natural chalk is a soft white limestone made of calcium carbonate from microscopic marine organisms (coccoliths). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalk
- Blackboard "chalk" sticks are commonly made of gypsum (calcium sulfate). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackboard_chalk
