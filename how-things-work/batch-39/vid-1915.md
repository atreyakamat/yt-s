## Fact check

- Robert Gair, a Brooklyn paper-bag maker, is credited with inventing the pre-cut folding carton after a metal rule used for creasing cut a bag by accident. (Wikipedia articles disagree on the year, 1879 on Robert_Gair vs 1890 on Cardboard_box, so the script says "1800s".) Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Gair and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardboard_box
- Before cartons, goods were shipped in wooden boxes, crates and barrels. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardboard_box
- Flat-packed boxes take far less space than assembled ones; bar chart is illustrative only. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carton
