## Fact check

- Daisuke Inoue claimed in a 1996 interview to have built the first karaoke-style machine in Kobe; he began loaning machines in 1971. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karaoke
- His "8 Juke" machines had an amplifier, microphone, coin box and car stereo playing specially made 8-track tapes; they used 100 yen coins for several minutes of singing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karaoke
- Karaoke = kara (empty) + clipped ōkesutora (orchestra). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karaoke
- Inoue never patented his machine. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karaoke
