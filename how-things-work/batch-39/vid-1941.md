## Fact check

- Conventional produce PLUs are four-digit codes in the 3000 and 4000 ranges; organic produce may be flagged by prefixing the four-digit code with a 9. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_look-up_code
- The International Federation for Produce Standards (IFPS), formed 2001, administers the codes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price_look-up_code
