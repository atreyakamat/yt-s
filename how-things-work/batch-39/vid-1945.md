## Fact check

- Spinning draws out and twists fibres to form yarn; twist holds the short fibres together through friction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinning_(textiles)
- Cotton is cleaned, carded, combed and drawn before spinning. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton
