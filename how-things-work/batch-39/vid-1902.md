## Fact check

- Air-displacement micropipettes use a piston moving an air cushion to draw liquid into a disposable tip. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micropipette
- Typical operation has two stops: the first for aspirating or dispensing the set volume, the second (blow-out) for expelling the residual. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micropipette
- A microlitre is one millionth of a litre. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Litre
- The volume is set by a dial that changes piston travel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micropipette
