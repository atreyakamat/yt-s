## Fact check

- Neapolitan pizza is traditionally baked in a wood-fired dome oven at about 485 degrees C for 60 to 90 seconds; SOFTENED to 'about 450' and 'about 90 seconds'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neapolitan_pizza
- Home oven comparison 250 C is a typical maximum setting (approximate). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oven
- Wood-fired ovens store heat in thermal mass of brick/stone and heat food by conduction, radiation and convection. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood-fired_oven
