## Fact check

- Dental air-turbine handpieces are driven by compressed air and rotate at very high speeds, commonly a few hundred thousand rpm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_drill
- Water spray coolant is used to reduce heat from friction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_drill
- Air turbines have high speed but low torque. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_drill
- Script's roughly 300,000 rpm is a mid-range figure within the 'few hundred thousand' range. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_drill
