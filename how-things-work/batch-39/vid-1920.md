## Fact check

- Conveyor belt sushi was invented by Yoshiaki Shiraishi, who opened the first such restaurant in Osaka in 1958, inspired by a beer bottling plant conveyor. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conveyor_belt_sushi
- The first-restaurant claim and staffing motive are as described in the article ('said to be'). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conveyor_belt_sushi
