## Fact check

- The Erlenmeyer flask was invented by German chemist Emil Erlenmeyer in 1860. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlenmeyer_flask
- It has a flat base, conical body and cylindrical neck; the shape allows swirling without spillage and fits a stopper. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlenmeyer_flask
- The conical shape reduces loss of vapour/splashing during heating. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlenmeyer_flask
