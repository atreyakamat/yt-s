## Fact check

- A planisphere has a star chart disc and an overlying mask with an oval window that rotates to show the visible sky for a given date and time. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planisphere
- Planispheres are designed for a specific latitude range; they are usable only near the latitude they are made for. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planisphere
- The stars appear to move westward over the night because the Earth rotates. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diurnal_motion
