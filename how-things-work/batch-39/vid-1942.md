## Fact check

- Ultrasound imaging uses high-frequency sound waves and their echoes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_ultrasound
- Acoustic coupling gel removes air between the transducer and the skin, as air reflects ultrasound strongly. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_ultrasound
- The gel is water-based. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrasound_gel
