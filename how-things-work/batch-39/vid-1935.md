## Fact check

- Stacking crisps are formed from a dough of dried potato and other ingredients, shaped, and fried. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pringles
- Their shape is a hyperbolic paraboloid (saddle) designed for stacking. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pringles
- Curved shells are stronger than flat plates; bar chart is illustrative, no number claimed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_paraboloid
