## Fact check

- When a cash sale completes the register sends an electrical impulse to a solenoid that releases the drawer's latch; only the register or keys held by managers open it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash_register
- Spring ejecting the drawer is the common design; no timing number claimed.
