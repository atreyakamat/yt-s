## Fact check

- Cartridges hold liquefied gas, typically butane, propane or a mixture of hydrocarbons; the gas vaporises as it leaves the bottle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portable_stove
- Butane has poor performance in cold weather; propane is better. Boiling points: propane -42.2 C, butane -1 C (the page lists these). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portable_stove
- "Already boiling" is shorthand: at normal temperatures both fuels are above their boiling points, so they stay liquid only because of the pressure. This follows from the boiling points above.
