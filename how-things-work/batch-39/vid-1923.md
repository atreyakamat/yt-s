## Fact check

- Piezo igniters use a spring-loaded hammer striking a piezoelectric crystal, which produces a high voltage that creates a spark across a gap. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piezo_ignition
- Piezoelectric effect: some crystals generate voltage when mechanically stressed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piezoelectricity
