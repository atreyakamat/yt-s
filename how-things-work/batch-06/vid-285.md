## Fact check

- Sticky tape uses a pressure-sensitive adhesive (PSA): a viscoelastic polymer that bonds on pressure without solvent evaporation, heat or curing.
- PSAs wet the surface by flowing into its microscopic roughness; adhesion comes mainly from van der Waals forces.
- Tape backings (cellulose acetate or polypropylene) carry a release coating on the back so the roll unwinds.
- Bond strength rises with applied pressure because it increases real contact area.
