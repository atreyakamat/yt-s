## Fact check

- Tactile paving (tactile ground surface indicators) helps blind and partially sighted people detect hazards underfoot or with a cane.
- UK guidance: blister paving (rows of flat-topped domes) marks pedestrian crossing points; corduroy (rounded bars across the direction of travel) warns of hazards such as steps; offset blister paving marks off-street railway platform edges.
- Tactile blocks were invented by Seiichi Miyake in Japan in 1965 and first installed in Okayama in 1967.
