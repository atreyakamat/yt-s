## Fact check

- Domestic water meters are usually positive-displacement (nutating disc or rotary piston) or turbine/multi-jet types: water moves a wheel or disc in proportion to volume.
- Many use a magnetic coupling so the register (dials) stays dry and sealed from the water.
- Many meters have a small fast-turning leak indicator (star, triangle or dial); if it moves with every tap off, water is flowing somewhere. Standard utility advice.
- One cubic metre = 1,000 litres (SI definition); a full bath is about 150-200 litres, so about 5-7 baths: SOFTEN from 'about seven' to 'roughly six' (I could not find a sourced bath volume). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubic_metre
