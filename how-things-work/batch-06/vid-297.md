## Fact check

- Carbonated drinks in sealed PET bottles are under internal pressure of a few bar.
- Pressure vessels favour rounded shapes; flat panels deform.
- Carbonated PET bottles use a 'petaloid' base, usually with five feet, which combines pressure-resistant curved sections with a stable footing.
- Still water bottles don't need to resist carbonation pressure and often use flatter bases (bar shows 0 petaloid feet as a simplification).
- Five-petal base on carbonated PET bottles: SOFTEN to 'most'; I found no source giving the count, and designs vary (4 to 6 petals), so the claim is now hedged. Petaloid base patented by Domas Adomaitis, 1971. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic_bottle
