## Fact check

- Low temperatures increase electrolyte viscosity and slow electrode reactions, raising a lithium-ion cell's internal resistance.
- Higher resistance causes voltage sag under load; phones shut down when voltage drops below a cutoff, even with charge remaining.
- Capacity loss from cold is largely temporary; it recovers when the battery warms (phone makers note this in their temperature guidance).
