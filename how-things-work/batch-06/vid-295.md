## Fact check

- Instant cold packs contain water in an inner pouch and a solid such as ammonium nitrate or urea in the outer bag.
- Dissolving these solids in water is endothermic: it absorbs heat, so the mixture cools quickly.
- Typical instant packs stay cold for roughly 15-20 minutes and cannot be reused.
