## Fact check

- Hygiene reasons → the gap removes the front area most exposed to urine splashes and lets users wipe without touching the seat; these are the commonly given rationales.
- Home toilets → the codes apply to public facilities, so closed seats are normal at home.
- Open-front seats in public restrooms: OK for the Uniform Plumbing Code section 409.2.2 ('all water closet seats, except those within dwelling units or for private use, shall be of the open front type'); IPC not confirmed, so wording softened to 'many US plumbing codes'.
