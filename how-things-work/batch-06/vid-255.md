## Fact check

- A typical 7-9 kg load still holds roughly half its dry weight in water after spinning, i.e. several litres.
- Vented dryers blow humid air outside; condenser dryers cool it in a heat exchanger and collect water in a tank or drain; heat pump dryers use a refrigerant circuit to dehumidify and reheat the air.
- Heat pump dryers use substantially less energy than vented or condenser models (often around half or less).
- Sensor drying: metal strips in the drum measure the conductivity of damp clothes touching them and end the cycle when they are dry.
