## Fact check

- Bicycles commonly use Schrader (car-style) and Presta (thin) valves.
- Presta valves have a small knurled lock nut on the valve stem that must be unscrewed before inflating, and re-tightened after.
- Pressing the unscrewed tip releases a little air and frees a stuck valve, a standard tip from bike mechanics.
- Presta valves have no internal spring; air pressure inside the tyre holds the valve closed.
