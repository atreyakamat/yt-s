## Fact check

- Tankless (instantaneous) water heaters heat water as it flows and are switched on by a flow sensor.
- Power = flow x specific heat x temperature rise: 5 L/min = 0.083 kg/s; 0.083 x 4186 x 30 = about 10.5 kW.
- Kettles are about 2-3 kW (3 kW in the UK), so about three kettles.
- At fixed power, faster flow means a smaller temperature rise, so the water comes out cooler. Large current draw needs heavy cables.
