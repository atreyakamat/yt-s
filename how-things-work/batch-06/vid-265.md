## Fact check

- UVB is the main cause of sunburn; UVA penetrates deeper into skin and contributes to skin ageing.
- Organic (chemical) UV filters absorb UV and dissipate the energy mostly as heat.
- Mineral filters (zinc oxide, titanium dioxide) mainly absorb UV and also scatter some of it.
- SPF measures protection against UVB-caused burning; 'broad spectrum' labelling indicates tested UVA protection.
