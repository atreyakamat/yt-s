## Fact check

- Watermarks are made by varying the thickness of paper during forming, using raised/sunken patterns on the mould or a dandy roll; they are not printed.
- Thinner areas transmit more light and look brighter; thicker areas look darker.
- Paper watermarks first appeared in Italy (Fabriano) in the late 13th century.
- Bar values are illustrative, not measurements.
