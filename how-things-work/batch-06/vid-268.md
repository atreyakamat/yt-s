## Fact check

- 1891 patent showing 'over' → Seth Wheeler's US patent 459,516 (1891) for perforated wrapping/toilet paper rolls shows the paper hanging over the top.
- Over puts the end facing you → simple geometry of a roll on a wall holder.
- Under is harder for pets to unroll → widely given reason for 'under'; pawing at an under roll spins it against the wall.
- Hotel fold → housekeeping commonly folds the end into a point to signal the bathroom has been serviced.
