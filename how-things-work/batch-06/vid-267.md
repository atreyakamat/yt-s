## Fact check

- The classic folded 'oyster pail' takeaway box was patented in 1894 (Frederick Weeks Wilcox) and is folded from a single piece of paperboard, held with a wire handle.
- The folds create leak-resistant corners without glue.
- Removing the wire handle and unfolding lets the box open out into a flat, plate-like sheet.
