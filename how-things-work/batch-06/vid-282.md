## Fact check

- Induction cap sealing: a laminated liner (foil plus heat-seal polymer, often with card/wax backing) sits inside the cap; the closed container passes under an induction coil.
- The alternating magnetic field induces eddy currents in the aluminium foil, heating it without contact; non-metal parts aren't heated directly.
- The heat melts the polymer layer, bonding the foil to the container rim; sealing takes on the order of a second or two.
- The seal is airtight/leak-resistant and tamper-evident.
