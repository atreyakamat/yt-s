## Fact check

- Each half of a binocular is a small refracting telescope: objective lens, prism assembly, eyepiece.
- A converging objective lens forms an inverted, left-right reversed image.
- Prisms (Porro or roof) use internal reflections to re-invert the image and fold the optical path, shortening the instrument.
- Porro prism binoculars have the offset, wide 'zigzag' shape; roof prism binoculars have straight barrels.
- In a spec like 8x42, 8 is the magnification and 42 is the objective lens diameter in millimeters.
- 8x42 = 8x magnification, 42 mm objective lenses: definition.
