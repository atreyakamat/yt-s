## Fact check

- Early bicycles and track bikes are fixed-gear: the pedals turn whenever the rear wheel does.
- Freewheels and freehubs use spring-loaded pawls engaging a ratchet ring (some use other systems like star ratchets).
- Pedalling forward makes pawls engage the ratchet teeth; when coasting, the pawls ride over the teeth and snap back, producing the clicking sound.
- Click rate rises with wheel speed because more teeth pass the pawls each second.
