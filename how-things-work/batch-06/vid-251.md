## Fact check

- Josephine Cochrane of Shelbyville, Illinois, received US patent 355,139 in 1886 for a dishwashing machine; she is reported to have wanted to stop her servants chipping her heirloom china.
- Her design used wire compartments measured to fit plates, cups and saucers, placed on a wheel inside a copper boiler while hot soapy water was pumped and sprayed over them.
- Joel Houghton patented a hand-turned wooden dishwasher in 1850, which was not very effective.
- Her machine was shown at the 1893 World's Columbian Exposition in Chicago; hotels and restaurants were the main early buyers.
- Home dishwashers spread slowly, gaining popularity from the 1950s onward as homes got reliable hot water.
