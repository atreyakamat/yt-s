## Fact check

- The end hook is riveted through elongated holes so it can slide; the travel equals the hook's thickness, compensating for it on inside (push) and outside (hook) measurements.
- Hook thickness on common tapes is around 1 mm (about 1/32 inch), so 'often about a millimetre'.
- Bending the hook or the rivets changes the compensation and makes readings inaccurate.
- Many tape hooks have a slot or hole to catch a nail or screw head for one-person measuring.
