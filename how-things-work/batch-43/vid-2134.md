## Fact check

- Épée uses an electrical tip switch: a hit registers when the tip is pressed with enough force. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89p%C3%A9e
- Épée double-touch window is about 40 ms (1/25 s). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89p%C3%A9e
- Electric scoring replaced judges who watched for hits. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fencing
- Epee double-touch window 40 ms: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89p%C3%A9e
- Electric scoring: epee 1933, foil 1956, sabre 1988: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fencing
