## Fact check

- Haemodialysis passes blood through a dialyser containing semipermeable hollow-fibre membranes, with dialysate flowing around. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemodialysis
- Blood and dialysate flow in opposite directions (counter-current) to improve exchange. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemodialysis
- Waste solutes pass through by diffusion; blood cells and large proteins are retained. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dialysis
