## Fact check

- Ergonomic snow shovels use a bent or curved shaft to reduce bending and back strain (design rationale from manufacturers and occupational-health bodies). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shovel
- Wet snow is heavy; 'several kilograms' per shovelful is a hedge, no exact figure is claimed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snow
