## Fact check

- Buried-wire pet fences: a transmitter sends a radio signal through a boundary wire; the collar receiver sounds a warning tone near the wire and may then give a static correction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pet_fence
- The system only works if the collar batteries work; training with flags is standard. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pet_fence
