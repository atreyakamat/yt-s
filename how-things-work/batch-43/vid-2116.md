## Fact check

- Telstar 1 was launched on 10 July 1962 and relayed the first live transatlantic television pictures. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telstar
- Before communications satellites, transatlantic TV could not be sent live; undersea telephone cables (TAT-1, 1956) carried calls only, as TV needs far more bandwidth. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transatlantic_telephone_cable
- Telstar was in a low elliptical orbit and could relay only during short visibility windows each orbit. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telstar
- Television broadcast signals travel line-of-sight and are blocked by the Earth's curvature. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Line-of-sight_propagation
- Telstar 1 launched 10 July 1962; first live transatlantic TV 23 July 1962; medium elliptical orbit, about 30 min of each 2.5 h orbit over Atlantic: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telstar
