## Fact check

- Concrete hardens through hydration of cement, a chemical reaction with water, not by drying. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
- Curing keeps concrete moist so hydration continues; rapid drying reduces strength and causes cracking. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
- Concrete strength is conventionally tested at 28 days and keeps developing afterwards. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
