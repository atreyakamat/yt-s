## Fact check

- Swirling wine increases the surface area and releases volatile aroma compounds, a standard step in wine tasting. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wine_tasting
- Wine glass shape (narrower rim) concentrates aromas. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stemware
