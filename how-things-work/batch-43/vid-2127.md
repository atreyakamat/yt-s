## Fact check

- Each marimba bar has a tube below it, usually aluminium, that amplifies the sound; tube length varies with pitch. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marimba
- Rosewood bars give the best sound; lowering a bar's pitch means removing wood from the middle of its underside; the marimba has a lower range than the xylophone and doesn't transpose. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marimba
