## Fact check

- Casters trail behind their pivot axis so they self-align with the direction of travel; some can flutter or shimmy at speed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caster
- Many strollers include a front-wheel swivel lock. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_transport
