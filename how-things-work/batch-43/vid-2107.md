## Fact check

- Pressurised air (wind) from bellows or blowers passes through pipes chosen from a keyboard; each pipe produces one pitch set by its length; a rank is pipes of one timbre; each stop typically controls one rank. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pipe_organ
- The largest organs can have over 33,000 pipes; the Salt Lake Tabernacle organ has 11,623 pipes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pipe_organ
