## Fact check

- 1948: Bernard Silver overheard a food chain president ask a dean for an automatic checkout system; he and Norman Woodland began work. Early UV ink faded and was expensive; Woodland drew inspiration from Morse code. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcode
- Patent filed 20 October 1949; US patent 2,612,994 issued 7 October 1952. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcode
- 3 April 1973: IBM-developed UPC selected as the standard; linear codes chosen over RCA bullseye partly because smeared ink made bars taller but still readable. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcode
- 26 June 1974: a 10-pack of Wrigley's Juicy Fruit scanned at Marsh Supermarket, Troy, Ohio, the first commercial UPC use; first successful system at Sainsbury's UK in 1972. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcode
