## Fact check

- Sharps containers are puncture-resistant, leak-proof containers for used needles and blades. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharps_waste
- They have a restricted opening and are sealed and disposed of whole when filled to the fill line. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharps_waste
- Many designs use a one-way entry. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharps_waste
