## Fact check

- Slime/flubber can be made by cross-linking polyvinyl alcohol with borax; making it from PVA-based glues is a common elementary science demonstration. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borax
- Borax ingestion can cause nausea, vomiting and abdominal pain; large amounts can be fatal. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borax
- Slime toy introduced by Mattel in February 1976; placed in the non-Newtonian fluids category. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slime_(toy)
