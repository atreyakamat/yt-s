## Fact check

- Play-Doh began in the 1930s as a non-staining modelling compound developed by Noah McVicker to clean coal residue from wallpaper. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play-Doh
- Kay Zufall, a nursery school teacher, had pupils use it in the mid-1950s; it was demonstrated at a convention in 1956. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play-Doh
- The maker says it is mainly water, salt and flour; in 1957 a chemist reduced the salt so models dried without losing colour. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play-Doh
- Kutol wallpaper cleaner 1930s; toy mid-1950s, 1956; salt cut 1957: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Play-Doh
