## Fact check

- Trampolines use a bed (fabric mat) connected to a frame by springs; energy is stored in springs. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trampoline
- Bounce height can be increased by timing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trampolining
- Some energy is lost as heat each bounce (damping). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damping
