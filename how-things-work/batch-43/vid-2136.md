## Fact check

- Apollo capsules re-entered at about 11 km/s on return from the Moon. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_command_and_service_module
- A blunt body creates a detached bow shock so most heat is carried away in the airflow rather than absorbed by the vehicle (blunt body theory, H. Julian Allen). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_entry
- Capsules use ablative heat shields that char and erode, carrying heat away. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_entry#Ablative
