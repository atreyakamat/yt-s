## Fact check

- Meat slicers cut meats and cheeses; compared with a knife they take less effort and keep texture intact; thickness is easily adjustable; older models crank, newer use an electric motor. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meat_slicer
- Thickness plate behind the blade and sliding tray are the standard design of commercial slicers.
