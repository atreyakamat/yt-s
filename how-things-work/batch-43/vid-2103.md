## Fact check

- Reinforced garden hoses are made with an inner tube, a reinforcing layer (such as textile braid or mesh) and an outer cover. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garden_hose
- The reinforcement resists the internal water pressure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hose
