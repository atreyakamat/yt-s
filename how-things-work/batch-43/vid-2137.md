## Fact check

- A leafcutter bee filmed at 6,000 fps and played back at 30 fps is a 200x slow-motion ratio (6,000 / 30 = 200; 200 seconds is about 3.3 minutes). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-speed_photography
- A high-speed camera is one that can record above 250 frames per second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-speed_photography
- Eadweard Muybridge's 1878 gallop sequence investigated whether a horse's feet all leave the ground at once. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-speed_photography
- Standard film frame rate is 24 per second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frame_rate
