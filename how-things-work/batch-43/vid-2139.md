## Fact check

- On a direct-drive tricycle the pedals are attached to the front wheel, so distance per pedal turn equals the wheel's circumference. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tricycle
- The same direct-drive idea explains the penny-farthing's big front wheel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penny-farthing
