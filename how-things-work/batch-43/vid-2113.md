- Ball-lock quick-connect fittings use spring-loaded balls retained by a sliding sleeve that engage a groove, with an elastomer seal. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quick_connect_(fluid_coupling)
- Release speed ('under a second') is a loose description; no figure is claimed.
