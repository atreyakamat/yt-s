## Fact check

- Polymer dispersed liquid crystal (PDLC) smart glass: liquid crystal droplets in a polymer film between conductive layers; scatters light when off (opaque/frosted), aligns under an electric field when on (transparent). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_glass
- Many PDLC films are opaque when unpowered, transparent when powered. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_glass
