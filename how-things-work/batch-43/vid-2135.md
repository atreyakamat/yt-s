## Fact check

- Spray drying atomises a liquid into hot gas so droplets dry into powder very quickly; used for milk powder, instant coffee and detergents. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spray_drying
- Droplet drying takes seconds, short enough to avoid heat damage. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spray_drying
- Milk is concentrated by evaporation before spraying. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powdered_milk
