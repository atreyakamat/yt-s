## Fact check

- F-holes let the soundboards vibrate more freely and let air inside the instrument travel out; a 2015 MIT study found acoustic conductance is proportional to the hole's perimeter, not its area. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F-hole
- F-holes let a luthier reach in to adjust the sound post; used on the violin family, archtop guitars and many hollow-body electric guitars. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F-hole
- MIT 2015 study: sound output grows with f-hole perimeter, not area: OK. Source: https://news.mit.edu/2015/violin-acoustic-power-0210
