## Fact check

- Indirect-vent goggles have covered vents designed to prevent liquid splash entry while allowing airflow to reduce fogging. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goggles
- Direct-vent goggles let liquid in; non-vented goggles fog more. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goggles
- Chemical splash goggles seal around the eyes, unlike ordinary safety glasses. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safety_glasses
