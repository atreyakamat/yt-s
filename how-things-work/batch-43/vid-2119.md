## Fact check

- A drawing compass draws a circle because one leg is fixed at the centre and the other keeps a constant distance from it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compass_(drawing_tool)
- Holding technique (knob at the top) is standard practice for avoiding changes to the radius. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compass_(drawing_tool)
