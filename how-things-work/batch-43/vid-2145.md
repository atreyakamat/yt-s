## Fact check

- Chain-link fencing is made by weaving or screwing helical zigzag wires through each other to form diamond openings. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chain-link_fencing
- Wire is galvanised (zinc-coated) or vinyl-coated against corrosion; ends are twisted or knuckled. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chain-link_fencing
