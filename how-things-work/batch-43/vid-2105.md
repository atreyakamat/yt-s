## Fact check

- Sugar is extracted from cane or beet juice, concentrated, and crystallised in vacuum pans, then centrifuges separate crystals from molasses. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_refinery
- Boiling under vacuum lowers the boiling point, which avoids caramelising the sugar. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_refinery
- Brown sugar is commonly made by adding molasses to refined white sugar. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_sugar
