## Fact check

- Charm pricing/left-digit effect: people judge numbers mainly by leftmost digits (Thomas and Morwitz coined the term, 2005). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_pricing
- A 1997 Marketing Bulletin study found about 60% of advertised prices ended in 9. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_pricing
- Nine endings were rare until the 1880s and widespread only in the 1920s; theory is controversial and results mixed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_pricing
