## Fact check

- Training (stabiliser) wheels are small wheels mounted on either side of the rear wheel to prevent the bicycle falling over. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Training_wheels
- Mounting height and the amount of lean they allow vary; the script says "set low, so both touch" as a general case. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Training_wheels
