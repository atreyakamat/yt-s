## Fact check

- A gyrocompass uses a fast-spinning gyroscope and Earth's rotation to find true north, and is not affected by magnetic fields. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyrocompass
- The gyroscope's axis aligns with the Earth's rotation axis (meridian) through gyroscopic precession and a damping or pendulous mechanism. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyrocompass
- True north differs from magnetic north; ship hulls disturb magnetic compasses. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_declination
