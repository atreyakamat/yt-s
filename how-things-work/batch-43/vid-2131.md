## Fact check

- A heat lamp is an incandescent bulb designed mainly to produce heat; a lower filament temperature shifts the emission to longer infrared wavelengths; many have an internal reflector and a red filter that reduces visible light; restaurants use them to keep food warm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_lamp
- Food drying out under a lamp is general food-service knowledge.
