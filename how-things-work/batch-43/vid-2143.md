## Fact check

- Before the telegraph, weather reports moved roughly 60-120 km per day; the telegraph, invented in 1835, began 'the modern age of weather forecasting'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_forecasting
- FitzRoy was appointed in 1854 to a Board of Trade department; after the Royal Charter storm of October 1859 he developed charts, a gale warning service began in February 1861, and the first daily forecasts appeared in The Times in 1861. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_forecasting
- FitzRoy coined 'weather forecast' around 1859-1861. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_forecasting
