## Fact check

- Ethylene is a plant hormone that triggers fruit ripening. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethylene
- Climacteric fruit such as avocado, banana and apple produce ethylene as they ripen. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ripening
