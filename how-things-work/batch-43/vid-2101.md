## Fact check

- Sten Gustaf Thulin designed the one-piece plastic bag in the early 1960s for Celloplast of Norrkoping, Sweden, which patented it worldwide in 1965; plastic bags gradually displaced paper (Kroger switched in 1982); formed by folding, welding and die-cutting a flat plastic tube. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic_shopping_bag
