## Fact check

- Jethro Tull refined the seed drill in 1701 (Wikipedia: 'further refined'); earlier drills existed (China, 2nd century BCE; Babylon); early drills were small enough for one horse. Script says 'refined'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed_drill
- Earlier sowing was by broadcasting seed by hand, which was uneven and wasteful. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed_drill
