## Fact check

- A swashplate transfers the pilot's control inputs from the non-rotating to the rotating frame, changing the pitch of the rotor blades. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swashplate_(aeronautics)
- Collective input raises or lowers the swashplate, changing all blades' pitch together; cyclic input tilts it, so blade pitch varies through each rotation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helicopter_flight_controls
- Tilting the rotor disc produces horizontal movement. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helicopter_flight_controls
