## Fact check

- Surgeons historically operated in street clothes before aseptic technique introduced special gowns. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrubs_(clothing)
- White gowns were replaced by green and later blue in the early twentieth century; green is often explained as reducing eye strain and after-images from red. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrubs_(clothing)
- Green is opposite red on the colour wheel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_wheel
- Modern scrubs are simple, launderable garments. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrubs_(clothing)
- Early green over white by an early 20th-century doctor; the date for white gowns is not sourced, so timeline now says 'Later'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrubs_(clothing)
