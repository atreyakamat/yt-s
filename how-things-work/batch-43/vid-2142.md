## Fact check

- Pneumatic tube systems send capsules through tubes using air pressure or vacuum; widely used in hospitals. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumatic_tube
- Diverters route carriers to destination stations. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumatic_tube
- The first pneumatic mail systems appeared in the 1850s (London 1853 for telegrams). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumatic_tube
