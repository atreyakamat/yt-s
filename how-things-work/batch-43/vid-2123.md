## Fact check

- Submersible pond pumps use a centrifugal impeller; many have a magnetic drive in which a magnet coupling transmits torque through a sealed wall. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_drive_pump
- Centrifugal pumps throw fluid outward using an impeller. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrifugal_pump
