## Fact check

- A wireless microphone has a transmitter that sends the audio signal by radio to a receiver tuned to the same frequency, which converts it back to an audio signal. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wireless_microphone
- Receivers commonly use diversity reception (two antennas) to avoid dropouts. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diversity_scheme
