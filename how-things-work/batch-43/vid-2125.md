## Fact check

- Modern marshmallows are a whipped foam of sugar, corn syrup and gelatin, with air trapped in a gelatin network. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshmallow
- Commercial marshmallows are extruded as a rope, dusted with starch and cut. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshmallow
- Gelatin melts with heat, so toasted marshmallows soften. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelatin
