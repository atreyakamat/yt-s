## Fact check

- Chest-strap heart rate monitors detect the heart's electrical activity via electrodes (ECG-type signal) and transmit wirelessly. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_rate_monitor
- Moisture/electrode gel improves skin-electrode contact. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrocardiography
