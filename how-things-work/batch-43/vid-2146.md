## Fact check

- A marine propeller's blades are twisted airfoils that accelerate water astern, producing forward thrust. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propeller
- Blade tips move faster than the root, so blade pitch/twist varies along the radius. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propeller
- Cavitation forms vapour bubbles in low-pressure regions; collapsing bubbles can pit and erode propeller surfaces. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavitation
- Large, slow-turning propellers are generally more efficient for big ships. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_propulsion
