## Fact check

- Toughening process → glass is heated to about 620°C and rapidly cooled with air jets (quenching).
- Surface compression, core tension → the surface solidifies first and the contracting core puts it into compression.
- About 4x stronger → toughened glass is commonly quoted as 4-5 times stronger than annealed glass of the same thickness.
- Whole pane fractures almost instantly → once the compressive layer is breached, the stored stress releases and cracks propagate through the whole sheet.
- Small blunt pieces → toughened (tempered) safety glass breaks into small granular pieces, which is why it's required for shower screens.
- Toughening: heated to about 620 C; about 4x stronger than annealed: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempered_glass
