## Fact check

- File systems keep metadata (directory entries, allocation tables) pointing to file data.
- Deleting (including emptying the recycle bin) normally removes the entry and marks blocks as free; data remains until overwritten, which is why recovery tools work on hard drives.
- SSDs with TRIM let the drive erase freed blocks, making recovery much less likely.
- Factory resets with full erasure or encryption are the recommended way to clear a device before selling.
- Gauge value is illustrative.
