## Fact check

- Rooftop PV panels are arrays of silicon cells under toughened glass, with a backsheet and aluminium frame.
- A cell has n-type (extra electrons) and p-type (electron-deficient) layers; the p-n junction creates a built-in electric field.
- Absorbed photons free electrons; the field separates charges, driving current through an external circuit.
- A silicon cell produces roughly 0.5-0.6 V, so panels wire 60-ish cells in series; an inverter converts DC to AC.
