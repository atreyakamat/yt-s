## Fact check

- Since 1982 the US cent has been 97.5% zinc and 2.5% copper (copper-plated zinc); before that it was 95% copper.
- The switch was made because rising copper prices pushed the metal value toward or above face value.
- US Mint reports showed each cent costing several cents to produce (about 3.7 cents in fiscal 2024).
- The US Treasury ended production of pennies for circulation in 2025; existing pennies remain legal tender.
