## Fact check

- Airliner main tyres are commonly inflated to about 200 psi (e.g. roughly 200-220 psi), versus about 30-35 psi for car tyres.
- Aircraft brakes and tyres can become very hot after landing or a rejected take-off.
- Regulators (e.g. FAA) require dry nitrogen inflation for many transport aircraft tyres to reduce the risk of tyre fires/explosions from oxidation.
- Dry nitrogen avoids moisture that could freeze or vaporise, giving more stable pressure.
- Airliner tyres run up to about 200 psi; filled with dry nitrogen: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_tire
