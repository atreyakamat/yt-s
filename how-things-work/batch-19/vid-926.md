## Fact check

- Pitcher filter cartridges typically combine granular activated carbon with ion-exchange resin beads.
- Activated carbon has a very large internal surface area, commonly 500-1,500 m² per gram; chlorine and many organic taste/odour compounds adsorb onto it.
- Ion-exchange resin swaps calcium (and magnesium) ions for hydrogen or sodium ions, reducing carbonate hardness and limescale.
- Both have finite capacity, so cartridges must be replaced on schedule.
- Activated carbon over 500 m2 per gram: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Activated_carbon
