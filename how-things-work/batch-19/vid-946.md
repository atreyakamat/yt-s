## Fact check

- Most phones use lithium-ion polymer pouch cells sealed in an aluminium laminate foil pouch.
- Electrolyte decomposition (accelerated by heat, overcharge, age or damage) produces gas, causing pouch swelling.
- Common signs are a lifting display or bulging back. Manufacturers and safety guidance advise stopping use and charging, not puncturing or compressing it, and having it replaced professionally. This is device-safety guidance, not medical advice.
