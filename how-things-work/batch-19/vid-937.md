## Fact check

- Jigsaws convert rotary motor motion to reciprocating blade motion using an eccentric pin or crank on a gear driving a slotted plunger (a scotch-yoke type mechanism).
- Typical maximum stroke rates are about 3,000-3,200 strokes per minute; 3,000 per minute = 50 per second.
- Standard jigsaw blades have upward-pointing teeth and cut on the upstroke, so tear-out appears on the top face (down-cut blades exist).
- Orbital (pendulum) action moves the blade forward on the upstroke for faster, rougher cutting.
