## Fact check

- Foaming pumps have two concentric pistons/chambers, one for liquid and a larger one for air, which are compressed together into a mixing chamber.
- The mixture passes through one or more fine mesh screens that generate a uniform foam.
- Foaming soaps are dilute, low-viscosity formulas; thick soap does not foam properly and can clog the mesh.
- Foam dispensers deliver less liquid soap per dose than standard pumps.
