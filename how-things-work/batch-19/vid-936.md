## Fact check

- Office shredders use an optical (or mechanical) sensor at the throat to start the motor automatically.
- Cutting heads are two counter-rotating shafts of interlocking cutting discs; cross-cut discs have hooks that also cut across the strip.
- A strip-cut A4 page at ~6 mm strips gives about 35-40 strips; a cross-cut at about 4 × 40 mm gives several hundred pieces. Values are approximate.
- Most shredders have thermal cut-offs to prevent motor overheating.
