## Fact check

- UK road 'grit' is mainly crushed rock salt; councils pre-treat routes ahead of forecast frost.
- Salt dissolves in surface moisture to form brine, which has a lower freezing point than water (saturated brine around -21 °C).
- Traffic helps crush and distribute salt; it becomes much less effective in very low temperatures, roughly below -7 to -10 °C.
- Minor roads are often not gritted and carry less traffic, so they ice up first.
