## Fact check

- Ethylene (C2H4) is a plant hormone that triggers ripening in climacteric fruits such as bananas.
- Ripening fruit produces more ethylene, which accelerates ripening (autocatalytic).
- During ripening, starch converts to sugars, chlorophyll breaks down (green to yellow), and cell walls soften.
- Ethylene from ripe fruit can speed ripening of nearby fruit; a paper bag concentrates it.
- Bananas are shipped green and commercially ripened in rooms with controlled ethylene.
