## Fact check

- Contrast-detect autofocus iterates (hunts) by moving the lens and measuring sharpness.
- Phase-detection autofocus (PDAF) uses masked or split (dual) pixels that see light from opposite sides of the lens aperture.
- The offset (phase difference) between the two images indicates both direction and amount of defocus, so the lens can move directly to focus, typically within a fraction of a second.
