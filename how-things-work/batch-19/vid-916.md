## Fact check

- Alkaline cells: zinc powder gel anode in the centre with a brass current collector (negative), manganese dioxide cathode lining the steel can (positive), separated by a paper/polymer separator, with potassium hydroxide electrolyte.
- Zinc is oxidised (gives electrons) and MnO2 is reduced (accepts electrons); electrons flow through the external circuit.
- Nominal voltage is 1.5 V regardless of size (AAA, AA, C, D); size changes capacity, not voltage.
