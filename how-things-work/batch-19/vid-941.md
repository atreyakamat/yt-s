## Fact check

- Most fridges use a spring-loaded plunger switch on the cabinet frame that the closed door presses in, opening the lamp circuit; opening the door releases it and the lamp lights.
- Some models use a reed switch or Hall sensor with a magnet in the door.
- On many fan-cooled (frost-free) fridges the door switch also stops the evaporator fan while the door is open.
