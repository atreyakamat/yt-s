## Fact check

- Most clothes irons use a bimetallic strip thermostat: two bonded metals with different expansion rates bend when heated, opening contacts and cutting the element.
- On cooling, the strip returns and contacts close, so the iron cycles around the set temperature; the indicator light follows the element.
- The temperature dial adjusts the contact position (how far the strip must bend); the contacts snap open in a fraction of a second, producing the click.
