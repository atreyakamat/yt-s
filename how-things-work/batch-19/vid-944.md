## Fact check

- Septic tanks serve properties not connected to mains sewers.
- Inside, solids settle as sludge, fats/oils float as scum, and clarified effluent sits in the middle.
- Anaerobic bacteria partially digest the sludge.
- Effluent flows to a drainage field (soakaway) where soil provides further treatment; tanks are typically desludged every one to a few years.
