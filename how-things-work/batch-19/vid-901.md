## Fact check

- Smartwatch optical heart-rate sensors (PPG) sit under the rear crystal; ambient light and poor contact are major noise sources.
- Many smartwatches use a convex/domed sensor window to improve skin contact and reduce ambient light leakage.
- Optical barriers separate LEDs from photodiodes to stop light travelling directly between them without passing through tissue.
