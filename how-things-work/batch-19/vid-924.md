## Fact check

- Racing lane lines consist of floats/discs threaded on a steel cable; wave-quelling designs have finned discs that rotate.
- Rotating finned discs disrupt and dissipate surface waves, reducing turbulence passing between lanes.
- Competition pools use overflow (deck-level) gutters that absorb waves rather than reflecting them off the walls.
- World Aquatics (FINA) rules require the floats within 5 m of each end wall to be a distinct color.
