## Fact check

- Hydrocolloid dressings contain gel-forming agents such as carboxymethylcellulose, pectin or gelatin in an adhesive matrix, backed by a thin polyurethane film.
- They absorb wound fluid and form a gel, which appears as a white, swollen area, and maintain a moist environment.
- The film backing is water- and bacteria-resistant but vapour-permeable. Educational only, not medical advice.
