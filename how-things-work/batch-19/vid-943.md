## Fact check

- UK wiring rules (BS 7671) → standard sockets are not allowed within 3 m of the bath/shower zones; shaver supply units to BS EN 61558-2-5 are the exception.
- Isolating transformer → separate primary and secondary windings; the secondary is not referenced to earth, so touching one conductor gives no earth return path.
- 20 to 50 VA: BS EN 61558-2-5 sets 20 VA minimum, 50 VA maximum (was "about 20 W"): SOFTEN. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AC_power_plugs_and_sockets:_British_and_related_types
- Shaver supply units: 20 VA minimum, 50 VA maximum under BS EN 61558-2-5. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AC_power_plugs_and_sockets:_British_and_related_types
