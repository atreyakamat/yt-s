## Fact check

- In myopia (short sight), distant light focuses in front of the retina; diverging (concave) lenses correct it and are thinner at the centre, thicker at the edges.
- In hyperopia (long sight), light focuses behind the retina; converging (convex) lenses are thicker at the centre.
- Higher prescriptions need more curvature, increasing thickness; high-index materials refract more strongly, allowing thinner lenses. Bars illustrative.
- Minus lenses make eyes appear smaller to observers.
