## Fact check

- Rear-view cameras output a horizontally mirrored image so it corresponds with the view in the car's mirrors.
- They use wide-angle (fisheye) lenses, commonly around 130-170 degrees, causing barrel distortion at the edges.
- Many systems overlay distance guide lines (often red/yellow/green), and dynamic lines follow steering angle.
- The 1956 Buick Centurion concept car had a rear-facing camera; US FMVSS 111 required rear visibility cameras on new light vehicles from May 2018.
