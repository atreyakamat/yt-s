## Fact check

- Water expands about 9% in volume when it freezes.
- Potholes form when water enters cracks, freeze-thaw cycles widen them and weaken the base, and traffic loads break out the surface.
- Pothole numbers peak in late winter and early spring after repeated freeze-thaw cycles.
- Ice about 9% more volume than water: standard physical property (not separately fetched).
