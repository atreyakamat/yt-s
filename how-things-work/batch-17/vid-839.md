## Fact check

- Medieval open halls had a central open hearth; smoke escaped through a louvre or vent in the roof.
- Surviving medieval roof timbers in English hall houses are often smoke-blackened, a key dating clue.
- Inserting chimneys (16th-17th centuries, England's 'Great Rebuilding') allowed halls to be floored over, creating upstairs rooms.
