## Fact check

- Digital kitchen scales use a load cell: an aluminium beam with bonded foil strain gauges.
- Load makes the beam flex by a tiny amount (micrometres); the foil gauges stretch or compress, changing their resistance.
- A Wheatstone bridge and analogue-to-digital converter measure the change, and the microcontroller converts it to grams (250 g is just an example reading).
- Tare stores the current reading as the zero offset.
