## Fact check

- Bluetooth is named after Harald 'Bluetooth' Gormsson, a 10th-century king of Denmark (and Norway) known for uniting Danish tribes.
- Intel engineer Jim Kardach proposed it as a code name in 1997; the Bluetooth Special Interest Group formed in 1998, and spec 1.0 was released in 1999. The name stuck when other candidates fell through.
- The logo combines the Younger Futhark runes Hagall (H) and Bjarkan (B).
- The 'bad tooth' in the title is a popular explanation of the nickname (possibly a dead blue-grey tooth); historians aren't certain, so the voiceover doesn't claim it.
