## Fact check

- Mirror coatings are thin metal or metal-oxide films, typically tens to hundreds of nanometres, far thinner than a hair (~70,000 nm).
- They partially reflect and partially transmit light.
- The 'one-way' effect depends on the brighter side seeing its own reflection, as with one-way mirrors.
- Coloured mirror finishes use multilayer thin films whose interference reflects certain wavelengths more strongly.
- Reflecting extra light reduces glare, so mirrored lenses are common for snow and water sports.
