## Fact check

- Scraping vs evaporating → blade/jet hand dryers remove water mainly by shearing it off with high-velocity air rather than evaporating it.
- Thin slit, powerful motor → these dryers push air through narrow apertures (well under a few mm).
- Several hundred km/h → manufacturers quote air speeds of roughly 400-700 km/h; worded as 'several hundred'.
- About 10-15 s vs 30-45 s → typical quoted drying times; bars show rounded examples (12 s, 40 s).
