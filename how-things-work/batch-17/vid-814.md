## Fact check

- Time of useful consciousness at around 35,000-40,000 ft is roughly 15-60 seconds, so '~30 s' is a fair rounded figure.
- Passenger masks deploy automatically when cabin altitude exceeds about 14,000 ft.
- Most airliners use chemical oxygen generators (sodium chlorate with iron powder); pulling the mask releases a firing pin.
- The reaction is exothermic: canisters get very hot and may produce a burning smell; they typically supply oxygen for about 12-20 minutes.
- This gives pilots time to descend to about 10,000 ft where outside air is breathable.
