## Fact check

- Iron-on patches have a thermoplastic hot-melt adhesive backing (often polyamide or polyester based).
- Hot-melt adhesives are solid at room temperature, melt when heated and resolidify on cooling, like glue-gun sticks.
- Melted adhesive penetrates between fibres and mechanically locks on cooling.
- High heat in washing or drying can soften the adhesive; sewing the edges is common advice for durability.
