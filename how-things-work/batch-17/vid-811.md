## Fact check

- Lithium-ion packs include a protection circuit module (PCM/PCB) with a protection IC and MOSFET switches, commonly mounted on top of phone cells.
- It guards against overcharge, over-discharge, overcurrent and short circuit by opening the MOSFETs.
- Short-circuit detection delays are typically in the microsecond-to-millisecond range, far quicker than a human blink (roughly 100-400 ms).
