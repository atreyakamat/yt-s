## Fact check

- Self-drilling (often called Tek) screws have a drill-bit point with flutes that drills a hole in sheet metal before the threads engage.
- The threads then form or cut mating threads in the drilled hole.
- Manufacturers specify that the drill point (unthreaded flute length) must exceed the material thickness, or the threads engage before drilling is complete and the screw binds or snaps.
- Roofing versions use bonded EPDM rubber washers to seal against water.
