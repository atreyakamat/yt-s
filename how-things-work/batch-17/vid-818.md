## Fact check

- Slap bracelets contain a thin spring-steel band with a transverse curve, covered in fabric or plastic.
- The transverse curvature stiffens the band lengthwise, as with a tape measure blade.
- Impact flattens the cross-curve, the band loses its stiffness and rapidly coils, releasing stored elastic energy.
- The band is bistable: stable both straight and coiled.
