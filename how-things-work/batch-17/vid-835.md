## Fact check

- Lens tint (darkness) and UV blocking are independent; even clear lenses can block UV.
- Pupils dilate in dimmer light, so dark lenses without UV protection can increase UV reaching the inside of the eye. Eye health organisations warn about this.
- UV400 means the lens blocks wavelengths up to 400 nm, covering UVA and UVB.
