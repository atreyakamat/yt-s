## Fact check

- Protective earth conductors (green/yellow) bond exposed metal parts of Class I appliances to earth.
- In normal operation the earth carries little or no current.
- If a live conductor contacts an earthed case, the low-impedance earth path causes a large fault current that blows the fuse or trips the breaker (and RCD), disconnecting supply.
- Without earthing, the case can remain live and a person touching it may provide the path to earth. Double-insulated (Class II) appliances don't need an earth.
