## Fact check

- Calculator and keyboard keys are wired as a row/column matrix; the controller drives rows in turn and reads columns to locate a press.
- Rubber-dome keypads use conductive carbon pads to bridge PCB traces.
- Mechanical contacts bounce for a few milliseconds; firmware 'debounces' by waiting for a stable reading.
- Scan rates of hundreds of times per second are typical; the grid is illustrative.
