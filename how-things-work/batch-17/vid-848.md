## Fact check

- Before synthetic dyes, colours came from plants (indigo, weld), insects (cochineal, kermes) and molluscs (Tyrian purple).
- Tyrian purple required roughly 10,000 murex snails per gram (figure widely cited, e.g. from experimental archaeology), making purple a royal colour.
- In 1856 William Henry Perkin, aged 18, attempted to synthesise quinine (an anti-malarial) from coal-tar chemicals and instead obtained mauveine.
- Mauve became a fashion craze around 1858-1862 and launched the synthetic dye industry.
