## Fact check

- Centrifugal juicers use a fast-spinning flat cutting/grating disc at the base of a fine mesh basket; typical speeds are several thousand to over 10,000 rpm.
- Centrifugal effect throws pulp against the mesh; juice passes through the holes while fibre is retained, like a washing machine spin cycle.
- Many have a tapered basket so pulp climbs up and is ejected into a pulp container, and juice leaves through a spout.
