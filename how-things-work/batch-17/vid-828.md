## Fact check

- Exhaust noise comes from pressure pulses as each cylinder's exhaust valve opens.
- Reactive mufflers use chambers, baffles and perforated tubes so reflected waves interfere destructively with incoming ones.
- Absorptive (dissipative) sections use fibre packing (e.g. glass or mineral wool) to convert sound energy into heat.
- Performance exhausts use less restrictive designs and produce more noise deliberately.
