## Fact check

- A hanging Slinky stretches under its own weight; at the bottom, spring tension balances gravity.
- When the top is released, the collapse travels down the spring as a wave; the bottom stays stationary until it arrives, since nothing has changed the forces on it.
- For a standard metal Slinky the bottom hovers for roughly 0.3 seconds (widely demonstrated in slow-motion experiments).
- The center of mass still accelerates downward at g throughout.
- Slinky is the toy's common name.
