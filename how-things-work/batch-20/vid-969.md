## Fact check

- The Frisbie Pie Company of Bridgeport, Connecticut (founded 1871) sold pies in metal tins that college students tossed for fun.
- Walter Frederick Morrison developed a plastic flying disc in 1948 (later the Pluto Platter).
- Wham-O named it Frisbee in 1957 (after the pie company, altered spelling): FIX from 1958. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frisbee
- Spin gives gyroscopic stability; the cambered disc generates lift like a wing.
- Ultimate was created in the late 1960s.
