## Fact check

- Coal gas ('town gas') was made by heating coal in closed retorts, stored in gasholders and piped to street lamps and homes.
- Pall Mall in London was lit by gas in a public demonstration in 1807, among the first gas-lit streets.
- Lamplighters lit and extinguished street gas lamps by hand using poles.
- The incandescent gas mantle (Carl Auer von Welsbach, 1880s) gave much brighter light than a bare flame.
- Patrick Hamilton's 1938 play 'Gas Light', in which a husband dims the gas lights and denies it, is the origin of the term 'gaslighting'.
