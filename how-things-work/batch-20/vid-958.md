## Fact check

- Air, fire and sun → traditional ways to dry hair.
- Late 1880s: gas-stove bonnet dryer: Godefroy's salon hair dryer dates to 1888 (some sources say 1890), so the script says late 1880s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hair_dryer
- Handheld around 1920, about 100 W early, up to about 2,000 W today: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hair_dryer
- Vacuum cleaner ads → early 1900s vacuum cleaner ads suggested attaching the hose to the exhaust to dry hair.
- About 100 W around 1920 → early handheld dryers (1920s) produced roughly 100 W and often overheated.
- About 2,000 W today → typical modern hair dryers are around 1,800-2,200 W, roughly 20 times more.
- Handheld dryers ~1920 at only 100 W; modern up to ~2,000 W: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hair_dryer
