## Fact check

- Vacuum tubes (valves) amplify signals using a heated cathode; they were bulky, hot, power-hungry and needed warm-up time.
- The point-contact transistor was invented at Bell Labs in December 1947 (Bardeen, Brattain, Shockley).
- The first commercial transistor radio, the Regency TR-1, went on sale in late 1954; pocket transistor radios became hugely popular in the late 1950s-1960s.
