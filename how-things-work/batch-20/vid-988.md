## Fact check

- Vapour condenses on cold surfaces → basic dew-point physics; why mirrors and windows mist up.
- Damp encourages mould → mould needs persistent moisture; standard guidance from housing and health bodies.
- Overrun timers → common feature of bathroom extractor fans, wired to the light switch.
- 15 minutes → England's Approved Document F requires intermittent extract fans in rooms without an openable window to have a 15-minute overrun.
