## Fact check

- Bow drills (a cord wrapped round a spindle driven by a bow) are ancient; the carpenter's brace (a cranked handle with a chest pad) was used from the Middle Ages onward.
- Arthur James Arnot patented an electric drill in 1889 (Melbourne); Wilhelm and Carl Fein made the first portable handheld electric drill in 1895.
- A pistol-grip drill with trigger switch was patented in 1917; cordless drills appeared in the early 1960s.
- A brace is turned roughly once per second (about 60 rpm); cordless drill high gear is commonly around 1,500-2,000 rpm (about 25-33 turns per second).
- Electric drill patent 1889, Fein handheld 1895, Black & Decker pistol grip 1917: OK. Cordless 1960s: not on fetched page, left as decade. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drill
