## Fact check

- Before nylon, fine stockings were mostly silk (expensive, prone to snags and sagging); cotton, wool and rayon were the alternatives.
- Nylon was developed by Wallace Carothers' team at DuPont, first synthesised in 1935; it is a polyamide of long-chain molecules.
- Nylon stockings went on general sale in the US on 15 May 1940; millions of pairs sold within days (often cited as about 4 million in four days).
- During the Second World War nylon was diverted to parachutes and other military uses; some women drew seams on their legs with makeup or eyebrow pencil.
- Nylon 1935, stockings on sale 1940, nylon diverted to parachutes 1942: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nylon
