## Fact check

- Shop credit recorded in ledgers ('on account', 'on tick') was common before cards.
- Department stores issued metal charge plates (charga-plates) from the late 1920s/1930s.
- In 1950 the first multi-merchant charge card for restaurants was introduced in the US (Diners Club).
- In 1958 Bank of America mass-mailed BankAmericard credit cards in Fresno, California.
