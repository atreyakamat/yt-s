## Fact check

- Disinfectant labels specify a contact (dwell) time during which the surface must stay visibly wet to achieve the claimed kill; this ranges from under a minute to around 10 minutes depending on product and organism.
- Organic soil (grease, food) reduces disinfectant efficacy, so public health guidance is to clean first, then disinfect.
- Kill claims such as 99.9% come from standardised lab tests run at the labelled contact time. This is general cleaning guidance, not medical advice.
