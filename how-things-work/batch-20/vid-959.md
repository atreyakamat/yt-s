## Fact check

- Overloaded early wiring was a fire risk.
- Edison patented a fuse in 1890 (US 438305): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuse_(electrical)
- Fuses melt when current exceeds their rating, opening the circuit before the cables overheat.
- Rewireable fuses (ceramic carrier with replaceable fuse wire) were common in UK homes for decades; using oversized wire or nails defeated them, a well-known hazard.
- Miniature circuit breaker patented 1924 (Stotz / Brown Boveri): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_breaker
