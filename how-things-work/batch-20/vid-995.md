## Fact check

- Zipper (late) merging: drivers use both lanes until the merge point, then alternate. Minnesota DOT says it reduces the overall length of a backup by as much as 40 percent and evens out lane speeds.
- Merging early leaves one lane empty, so the same number of cars occupies a single lane and the queue is longer.
- Many agencies promote it with signs such as 'Use both lanes' and 'Merge in turn' (UK Highway Code rule 134 says merge in turn).
- MnDOT: zipper merge reduces backup length by as much as 40 percent: OK. Source: https://www.dot.state.mn.us/zippermerge/
