## Fact check

- Tetraethyl lead was identified as an anti-knock additive in 1921 and sold in petrol from 1923.
- Lead from exhaust contaminated air, soil and dust; lead exposure is linked to health harm, particularly neurological effects in children (WHO).
- Lead poisons catalytic converters; the US required unleaded petrol for new cars with converters from the 1975 model year.
- UNEP announced the end of leaded petrol for cars worldwide in 2021, when Algeria stopped selling it.
