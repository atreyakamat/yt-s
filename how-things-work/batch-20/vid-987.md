## Fact check

- Many jars are hot-filled or heat-processed, creating a partial vacuum as contents cool.
- Lids with a 'safety button' (vacuum indicator) have a flexible centre held down by the vacuum.
- When the seal is broken (by opening or damage), pressure equalises and the button pops up, making a click.
- Packaging commonly says not to use the product if the button is raised before opening.
