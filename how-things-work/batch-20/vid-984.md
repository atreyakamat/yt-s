## Fact check

- Most modern surfboards have a polyurethane or EPS foam core, a wooden stringer down the center, and a fiberglass-cloth-and-resin shell.
- The stringer adds stiffness and strength and controls flex.
- Fins provide lateral grip ('hold') so the board can turn and carve.
- Surf wax on the deck provides traction for the feet.
- Traditional Hawaiian olo boards were solid wood and could weigh well over 50 kg.
