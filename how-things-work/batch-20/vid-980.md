## Fact check

- Automatic rising bollards control access to bus lanes and pedestrian zones; they are raised and lowered by hydraulic or electromechanical drives in a pit.
- Access is granted by transponders, permit cards, intercom or ANPR.
- Safety loops detect a vehicle over the bollard and hold it down; it rises after the authorised vehicle passes, so tailgating vehicles can be struck.
- Crash-rated versions are designed to stop heavy vehicles.
