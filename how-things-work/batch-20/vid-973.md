## Fact check

- UVA and UVB → the two UV bands relevant to sunscreen; UVA has longer wavelengths.
- Mineral (zinc oxide, titanium dioxide) and organic filters → the two filter types.
- Mostly absorption → organic filters absorb UV and dissipate it as heat; studies (e.g. Cole et al., 2016) show mineral filters also work mainly by absorption, scattering only a few percent.
- SPF figures → SPF 15 ≈ 93%, SPF 30 ≈ 97%, SPF 50 ≈ 98% of UVB blocked under test conditions.
- Reapply → product labels instruct reapplication; filters can photodegrade and rub or wash off. No medical advice given.
- SPF 15/30/50 = 93/97/98% UVB blocked (1/SPF transmitted): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunscreen
