## Fact check

- Router antennas radiate broadly in all directions; placing the router centrally and elevated improves coverage.
- Water strongly absorbs 2.4/5 GHz signals; metal reflects; walls attenuate, especially brick and concrete.
- Manufacturers and ISPs commonly advise keeping routers out of cupboards, off the floor, and away from fish tanks, mirrors and metal.
- Bar values are illustrative of relative signal loss, not measurements.
