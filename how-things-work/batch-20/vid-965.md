## Fact check

- Night watchmen patrolled towns with lanterns and called the hour before modern policing and cameras.
- In 1942 German engineer Walter Bruch designed a closed-circuit television system to observe V-2 rocket launches at Peenemünde from a safe distance.
- 'Closed circuit' means the signal is sent over a private link to specific monitors rather than broadcast.
- Street CCTV appeared in the late 1960s (e.g. Olean, New York, 1968).
