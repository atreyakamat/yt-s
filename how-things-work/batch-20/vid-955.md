## Fact check

- Adhesive plasters consist of a backing (fabric or plastic), a pressure-sensitive adhesive, an absorbent pad, a perforated low-adherent film over the pad, and release papers.
- Pressure-sensitive adhesives bond with light pressure without needing solvents, water or heat.
- The perforated non-adherent film lets fluid pass to the pad while reducing adherence to the wound.
