## Fact check

- Echo sounders use a hull transducer to send acoustic pulses and receive echoes.
- The speed of sound in seawater is about 1,500 m/s (varies with temperature and salinity).
- Depth = speed x time / 2; 0.1 s gives 150 m round trip, 75 m depth.
- Fish finders work on the same principle, showing echoes from fish and vegetation.
