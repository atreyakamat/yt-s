## Fact check

- Ring binder mechanisms mount half-rings on two hinged plates held under a spring-steel cover.
- The plates act as a toggle (over-centre mechanism): pushing the hinge past the midpoint lets the spring snap rings fully open or closed.
- There is no stable halfway position. Gauge is illustrative.
