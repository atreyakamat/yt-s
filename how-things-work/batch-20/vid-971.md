## Fact check

- 18th-century cake recipes (for example pound cakes) instructed cooks to beat batter or eggs for an hour or more.
- Before mechanical beaters, cooks used forks, spoons, and whisks made from bundles of twigs.
- Many rotary egg beater patents were filed in the US from the 1850s to the 1870s (e.g. the Dover egg beater, 1870s), using a crank and gears to spin two interlocking beaters in opposite directions.
- Electric hand and stand mixers followed in the early 20th century.
