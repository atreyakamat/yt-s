## Fact check

- Security holograms (diffractive optically variable devices) are embossed microstructures, metallised with a thin aluminium layer and applied as foils by hot stamping.
- Grating spacings are on the order of a micrometre, i.e. hundreds to thousands of lines per millimetre.
- Diffraction splits white light into colours; varying grating patterns create images that change with viewing angle.
- Copiers and scanners cannot reproduce diffractive effects.
