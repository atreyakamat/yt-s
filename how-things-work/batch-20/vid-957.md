## Fact check

- Penny licks were small, thick-glass dishes used by 19th-century British street vendors; customers licked them clean and returned them for reuse.
- They were linked to the spread of disease and were banned in London around the turn of the century (commonly dated 1899).
- Edible cones appear in European cookery from the 19th century (e.g. Agnes Marshall's 1888 cookbook).
- Ice cream cones were popularised at the 1904 St Louis World's Fair.
