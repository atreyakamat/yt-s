## Fact check

- Car brakes are hydraulic: the pedal (a lever) drives the master cylinder piston, pressurising brake fluid that acts on caliper/wheel-cylinder pistons at each wheel.
- Liquids are nearly incompressible, so pressure is transmitted equally (Pascal's principle); larger output pistons give force multiplication.
- A vacuum or electric brake booster adds assistance.
- Air in the lines compresses, causing a spongy pedal; brakes are 'bled' to remove it.
