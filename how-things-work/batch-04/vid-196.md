## Fact check

- Qi-style wireless chargers use inductive coupling between a transmitter coil in the pad and a receiver coil in the phone, like a transformer with an air gap.
- Qi operates at roughly 80-200 kHz, i.e. the current alternates over a hundred thousand times a second.
- Faraday's law of induction: a changing magnetic flux induces a voltage in a nearby coil.
- Coupling (and efficiency) drops quickly as the coils move apart or out of alignment. (Gauge is illustrative.)
- Qi wireless charging runs at about 110-205 kHz (over 100,000 flips a second): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qi_(standard)
