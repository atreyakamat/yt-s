## Fact check

- Most binoculars have a diopter adjustment, usually a ring on one eyepiece, to compensate for focus differences between a user's eyes.
- Standard setup: close the eye on the diopter side, focus with the center wheel, then switch eyes and adjust only the diopter ring.
- Adjusting the hinge to match the distance between your eyes merges the two images into one circle.
- It's common for a person's two eyes to differ slightly in focus.
