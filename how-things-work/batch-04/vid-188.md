## Fact check

- Classic baseball caps are built from six panels (five- and other panel caps exist; the script says 'most').
- The panels meet at the crown, covered by a fabric-covered button, sometimes nicknamed a 'squatchee'.
- The small stitched holes are eyelets for ventilation: standard description by cap makers.
- The brim was added to shade players' eyes from the sun: standard history of the baseball cap.
