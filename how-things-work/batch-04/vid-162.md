## Fact check

- Microwaves heat food mainly through water molecules; dry kernels heat comparatively slowly.
- Microwave popcorn bags contain a susceptor: a thin metallised (usually aluminium) film laminated between paper layers that absorbs microwave energy and gets hot.
- Susceptors typically reach roughly 200 °C or more, enough to melt fat and pop corn (which pops around 180 °C). Bars are rounded illustrations.
- Bags are printed 'this side down' so the susceptor sits under the kernels.
- Popcorn pops around 190 C (380 F) and the bag reaches about 230 C (450 F): FIXED bars from 180/200 to 190/230. Source: https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/6137098
