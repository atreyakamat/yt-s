## Fact check

- Steam irons drip water from a tank into a heated steam chamber in the soleplate, where it vaporises and exits through holes.
- Cotton is cellulose; creases are set by hydrogen bonds between cellulose chains. Heat and moisture break and allow them to reform in the new (flat) shape as the fabric dries.
- Care-label iron dots: one dot up to about 110 °C, two dots about 150 °C, three dots about 200 °C.
