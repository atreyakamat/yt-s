## Fact check

- Many running shoes have an offset extra top eyelet intended for a 'heel lock' / 'runner's loop' / 'lace lock'.
- The technique: thread each lace into the top eyelet on the same side to make a loop, cross the laces through the opposite loops, pull down and tie.
- It tightens the collar around the ankle and reduces heel slip: widely recommended by running shoe retailers.
- No medical claims are made.
