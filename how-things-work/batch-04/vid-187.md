## Fact check

- Cross-head (Phillips) recesses have tapered walls, so under torque the driver is pushed axially out of the recess (cam-out).
- The six-lobe star drive (widely known by the trademark Torx) was developed in 1967; its near-vertical lobe walls transmit torque with little axial push, reducing cam-out and allowing higher torque.
- Rounded lobes spread contact stress, reducing bit and recess wear.
- It is common in cars, electronics and decking screws.
