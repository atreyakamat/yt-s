## Fact check

- The indentation in a wine bottle base is called a punt.
- In hand-blown glass, the pontil (rod) left a rough scar on the base; pushing the base up kept the scar from scratching surfaces and let the bottle stand on a ring.
- A domed base resists internal pressure better; champagne/sparkling wine bottles hold roughly 5-6 atmospheres.
- Modern machine-made bottles don't strictly need punts; reasons given today include strength, stability and tradition. A bottle's volume is legally labelled, so the punt doesn't reduce the stated contents.
