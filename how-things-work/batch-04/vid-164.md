## Fact check

- Smart thermostats contain a temperature sensor (usually a thermistor, whose resistance changes with temperature), a microcontroller, a wireless radio and an output to the boiler.
- Many control the boiler with a simple relay (volt-free contact) that closes to call for heat; some use digital protocols (e.g. OpenTherm) instead, hence 'can' and 'still a switch' kept general.
- Geofencing (using the phone's location) and learning/optimum start (learning warm-up time) are common smart-thermostat features.
