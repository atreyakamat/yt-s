## Fact check

- The standard HDMI Type A connector is a trapezoid-like shape with bevelled corners on one side, so it is keyed to one orientation.
- Type A has 19 pins arranged in two staggered rows.
- Pins carry TMDS data pairs, a clock pair, DDC (display info), CEC, hot-plug detect, +5 V and grounds.
- HDMI carries digital video and audio in one cable; the HDMI 1.0 specification was released in December 2002.
- HDMI Type A has 19 pins; HDMI 1.0 released December 2002: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HDMI
