## Fact check

- Clothing doesn't generate heat; it reduces heat loss from the body.
- Still air is a poor conductor of heat (about 0.026 W/m·K); trapped air is what insulates in most clothing.
- Water conducts heat around 23-25 times better than air (about 0.6 W/m·K), so 'about 25 times' is fair.
- Cotton absorbs and holds water; wool and polyester base layers move moisture away or stay warm when damp: standard outdoor guidance.
- Waffle-knit and brushed fabrics create air pockets: true.
