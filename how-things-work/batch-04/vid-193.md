## Fact check

- Float-operated fill valve (ballcock) → a float on an arm (or a float cup) opens the inlet valve when the level drops and closes it when the level rises.
- Refill tube → a small tube sends some water into the overflow pipe to refill the bowl's trap seal.
- Cattle troughs → use the same float (ball) valve principle.
