## Fact check

- Traditional Chinese wok stoves have a round opening the wok sits into, so the flame wraps the curved base. Flat-bottomed woks exist for flat Western hobs.
- Heat concentrates at the bottom of the curve; the sloping sides are cooler, so cooks move food up the sides to slow it down.
- The curved walls let tossed food slide back to the hot centre, and a small amount of oil pools at the bottom.
- Traditional woks are thin carbon steel (or thin cast iron), which responds quickly to heat changes.
