## Fact check

- Many UK pelican, puffin and toucan crossings have a tactile rotating cone on the underside of the push-button unit, which turns during the green man period for blind and deafblind pedestrians.
- UK guidance is that audible signals are not used where crossings are staggered or close together, because a pedestrian might hear the wrong crossing; the cone still works there.
