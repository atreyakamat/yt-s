## Fact check

- Optical fibre: a glass core and cladding of slightly lower refractive index; the standard cladding diameter is 125 µm, similar to a human hair.
- Light stays in the core by total internal reflection at shallow angles.
- Data is sent as modulated laser light at gigabit rates (billions of symbols per second).
- Light in glass travels at roughly two-thirds of c, about 200,000 km/s.
- Copper phone lines suffer much higher attenuation at broadband frequencies than fibre.
