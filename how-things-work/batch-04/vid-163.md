## Fact check

- Mostly water → typical shampoos are roughly 70-80% water with about 10-20% surfactants (rounded figures shown).
- Surfactants have a hydrophilic head and hydrophobic tail → basic surfactant chemistry.
- Micelles → surfactants surround oil droplets, which are then rinsed away.
- Foam isn't the cleaner → cosmetic chemists note foam level doesn't equal cleaning power; low-foam cleansers still clean. Foam is kept largely because consumers expect it.
- Shampoo is mostly water (about 50-90%) with roughly 10-25% surfactants: OK. Source: https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/8298553
