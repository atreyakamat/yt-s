## Fact check

- Iron soleplates taper to a point to reach collars, around buttons and into pleats; the broad rear covers larger areas.
- Irons have a flat heel rest so they can be stood upright between strokes, keeping the hot plate off fabric.
- Steam irons have many steam holes across the soleplate to spread steam.
- Many soleplates have a 'button groove' around the tip edge.
