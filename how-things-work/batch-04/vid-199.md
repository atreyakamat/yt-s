## Fact check

- Most airport full-body scanners in use (e.g. in the US and EU) are millimetre-wave systems, which use non-ionising radio-frequency energy.
- Rotating antenna arrays scan the traveller in a few seconds while they hold a pose with arms raised.
- Millimetre waves pass through clothing and reflect off the skin and concealed objects.
- Automatic target recognition software displays a generic figure with highlighted boxes rather than an image of the person.
- TSA-style airport scanners use millimetre waves and show a generic outline, not X-ray: OK. Source: https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20121018234817/http://www.tsa.gov/approach/tech/ait/how_it_works.shtm
