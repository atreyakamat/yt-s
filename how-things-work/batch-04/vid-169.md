## Fact check

- Checkpoint X-ray machines pass X-rays through bags on a conveyor to detector arrays.
- Denser, higher atomic number materials absorb more X-rays; the gauge is a qualitative illustration.
- Dual-energy X-ray systems compare absorption at two energies to estimate effective atomic number.
- The common colour convention: orange for organic materials, green for mixed/inorganic, blue for metals.
- Dense items like laptops can hide what is behind them, which is why some checkpoints ask for them to be removed.
