## Fact check

- Most digital clinical thermometers use a thermistor in the tip; NTC thermistors' resistance falls as temperature rises.
- The circuit passes a small current and converts the measured resistance to temperature.
- The beep signals the reading has stabilised (or a predictive estimate is complete), depending on the model. Bar values are illustrative.
