## Fact check

- Most disposable paper hot cups are paperboard with a thin polyethylene lining (typically tens of micrometres) to make them liquid-tight.
- Wax coatings were used for cold cups but soften/melt at hot drink temperatures (paraffin melts roughly 46-68 °C; 60 is a rounded illustration).
- Seams and bases are heat-sealed using the plastic layer.
- The bonded plastic makes cups hard to recycle in standard paper mills; specialist facilities separate the fibre.
- Alternatives with bio-based or aqueous (water-based) coatings exist.
