## Fact check

- The Metropolitan Railway in London opened on 10 January 1863, the world's first underground railway, using steam locomotives.
- It was built mostly by 'cut and cover' beneath streets.
- Locomotives were designed to condense steam, but tunnels were still smoky.
- The City and South London Railway (1890) was the first deep-level electric tube railway.
- The street grid is illustrative.
- Metropolitan Railway opened 10 January 1863, steam-hauled, the first underground railway. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_Railway
