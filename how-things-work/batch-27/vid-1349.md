## Fact check

- uPVC (and many aluminium) window frames use a drained-and-ventilated design: water that passes the outer seal collects in the frame's outer chamber.
- Drainage slots, often with hooded covers, let this water escape outside.
- Blocked drainage slots are a common cause of water ingress; manufacturers advise keeping them clear.
