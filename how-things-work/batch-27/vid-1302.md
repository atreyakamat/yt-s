## Fact check

- Drinking through a straw works by reducing pressure inside the straw/mouth; atmospheric pressure on the liquid surface pushes the liquid up.
- Atmospheric pressure can support a water column of about 10.3 m with a perfect vacuum, so ~10 m is the theoretical limit for lifting water by suction.
- Without an atmosphere (e.g. on the Moon), there is no outside pressure to push liquid up.
- A hole in the straw lets air in and equalises pressure, so it stops working.
