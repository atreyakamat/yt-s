## Fact check

- Wet shaving routine → heating water, lathering with a brush and a razor was the norm.
- Water softens hair → hydrated hair absorbs water, swells and needs much less force to cut; the reason for pre-shave wetting.
- 1930/1931 → Jacob Schick patented his electric dry shaver in 1930 and it went on sale in 1931.
- 1939 → the first rotary-head electric shaver was introduced in the Netherlands in 1939.
- Dry shavers use cutters behind a slotted or perforated guard.
- Schick patented his electric razor in May 1930 and was a former Army officer (Lt. Colonel) known as Col. Schick; 'first successful' is contested (Bruecker 1915), so wording softened and the patent year corrected to 1930. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Schick and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_razor
