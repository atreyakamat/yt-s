## Fact check

- Suspended timber ground floors sit on joists over a void (common in pre-1950s UK houses).
- Ground moisture raises humidity in the void; damp timber is prone to fungal decay.
- Air bricks in external walls provide cross-ventilation of the void.
- Blocking air bricks (raised ground, planting, patios) is a well-known cause of damp and rot.
