## Fact check

- Pencils were sharpened with knives before mechanical sharpeners.
- 'Penknife' originally referred to a small knife for trimming quill pens.
- Bernard Lassimonne of France received the first pencil sharpener patent in 1828; crank sharpeners became common in the late 1800s and electric ones in the 1900s.
- Hand sharpeners hold a blade at a fixed angle beside a conical hole. Gauge is illustrative.
- Bernard Lassimonne applied for the first pencil sharpener patent (French patent 2444) in 1828. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pencil_sharpener
