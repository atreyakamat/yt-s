## Fact check

- Before metal foil, food was wrapped in leaves, cloth, waxed/oiled paper or covered with dishes.
- Tin foil was used commercially in the 19th century; it could impart a slight tinny taste.
- Aluminium was a costly precious metal in the 1850s; the Hall-Héroult electrolytic process (1886) made it cheap.
- In 1910 the first aluminium foil rolling plant opened in Kreuzlingen, Switzerland (Dr. Lauber, Neher & Cie); aluminium foil then displaced tin foil, but 'tin foil' remains a common name.
- Aluminium foil replaced tin foil from 1910 in Switzerland. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminium_foil
- Hall-Heroult process (1886) made aluminium far more available. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminium
