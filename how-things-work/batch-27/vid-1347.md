## Fact check

- Salt (sodium chloride) absorbs moisture from humid air (strongly above about 75% relative humidity), and crystals cake together as surface moisture dries.
- Uncooked rice absorbs some moisture, helping keep salt in a shaker drier; grains are larger than shaker holes.
- Many table salts contain anti-caking agents.
- This is a common household tip, which is why the reel frames it as the reason people add rice.
