## Fact check

- In coal-heated 19th-century Britain, household refuse was dominated by ash and cinders ('dust'), hence 'dustbin' and 'dustman'.
- Victorian dust contractors ran dust yards where waste was sorted; fine ash ('breeze') was sold to brickmakers, and rags, bones and metal were resold.
- Plastic bin bags appeared from 1950; wheeled bins spread in the UK mainly in the 1980s.
