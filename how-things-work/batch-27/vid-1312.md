## Fact check

- Standard retractable utility knives use a slider and blade carrier with a spring-loaded catch engaging notches to set blade extension.
- Many have a two-part handle joined by a screw with storage for spare blades inside.
- Standard utility blades are trapezoidal with two notches on the back edge and are symmetrical, so they can be reversed to use the second corner.
- Safety guidance: dull blades require more force and are more likely to slip, so sharp blades are safer.
