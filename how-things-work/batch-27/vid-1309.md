## Fact check

- Tubeless bicycle tyres seal directly to a tubeless-compatible rim with no inner tube.
- Liquid sealant (typically latex-based with particles/fibres) is added inside.
- Escaping air carries sealant into a puncture, where particles clog it and latex coagulates to seal it.
- Sealant works on small punctures (a few mm) and dries out over a few months, so it needs replenishing.
