## Fact check

- Refrigerant (compressor) dehumidifiers pass air over a cold evaporator below the dew point, condensing water into a tank.
- The air then passes over the hot condenser, which reheats it.
- Compressor and fan energy, plus released latent heat, make the outlet air slightly warmer than the inlet. (Desiccant types work differently; the script describes the common compressor type.)
