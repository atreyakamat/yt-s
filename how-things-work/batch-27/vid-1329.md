## Fact check

- Harpsichords pluck strings with quill (or plastic) plectra, so key force barely changes loudness.
- Bartolomeo Cristofori built the first piano, with a hammer action, in Florence around 1700.
- With hammers, faster key strokes produce louder notes, so players can control dynamics by touch.
- The instrument was called 'gravicembalo col piano e forte' (harpsichord with soft and loud), later pianoforte, shortened to piano.
- Cast-iron frames became common in the 19th century, and 88 keys became standard in the late 1800s.
