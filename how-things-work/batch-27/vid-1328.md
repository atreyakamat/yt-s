## Fact check

- Jacob Davis, a tailor in Reno, Nevada, began riveting pocket corners of work trousers around 1871, reportedly inspired by rivets on horse blankets.
- Lacking money to patent it, he partnered with his cloth supplier Levi Strauss; US patent granted 20 May 1873.
- Rivets reinforce stress points by clamping fabric layers.
- Back-pocket rivets were covered in the 1930s (complaints of scratched saddles and furniture) and later removed.
