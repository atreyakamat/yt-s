## Fact check

- Lever grease guns use a lever-driven small-bore plunger; a spring-loaded follower in the barrel feeds grease to the pump.
- Pressure = force / area, so the small plunger generates high pressure; hand grease guns are commonly rated about 6,000-10,000 psi maximum.
- Car tyres run about 30-35 psi, so 6,000 psi is roughly 200 times more.
- Grease fittings (nipples) contain a spring-loaded check ball that lets grease in and keeps dirt out.
- Standard practice is to pump until fresh grease purges old grease from the joint (where the design allows).
