## Fact check

- On typical North American distribution poles the primary (high-voltage, thousands of volts) conductors are at the top, then transformers and secondary (household voltage) lines.
- Safety codes require a communication worker safety zone (clear space) between power and communication attachments.
- Telephone, cable TV and fibre lines are attached lowest so communications workers stay away from power conductors.
