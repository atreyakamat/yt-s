## Fact check

- Self-closing (non-concussive) taps → push-button taps with a hydraulic timing mechanism, common in public washrooms.
- Timing → pressing displaces water from a chamber; it refills through a small bleed orifice, and the piston/valve closes as it does.
- Orifice size sets run time → manufacturers offer adjustable or fixed timings; scale or debris can alter it.
