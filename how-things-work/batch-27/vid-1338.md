## Fact check

- Most modern fuel-injected cars use an electric fuel pump mounted in the tank (often in a module with the fuel level sender).
- Fuel flowing through and around the pump cools and lubricates it; the vapour inside is far too rich and lacks oxygen to ignite.
- Many cars run the pump briefly at key-on to prime/pressurise the fuel system, producing an audible whirr.
- Running very low on fuel reduces the pump's cooling and can shorten its life, a common mechanic's advice.
