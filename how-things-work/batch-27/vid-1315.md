## Fact check

- Passive earmuffs use rigid, dense cups (mass blocks transmitted sound), absorbent foam inside the cup, and cushions that seal against the head; a headband spring provides clamping force.
- Leaks, such as from glasses arms breaking the seal, can significantly reduce protection. The gauge is illustrative.
- Passive earmuffs attenuate high frequencies much more than low frequencies; bar values are typical rough attenuations, not a specific product's data.
