## Fact check

- Water heating accounts for the large majority (often around 80-90%) of a washing machine's energy use.
- Eco programmes run at lower temperatures for longer (often around 3 hours) to achieve rated cleaning performance with less energy; EU energy labels are based on the eco 40-60 programme.
- Sinner's circle: cleaning depends on temperature, chemistry, mechanical action and time; reducing one requires increasing others.
- Modern detergents, with enzymes, are formulated to clean at low temperatures.
