## Fact check

- 'Plumber' derives from Latin 'plumbum' (lead); Roman water distribution used lead pipes (fistulae).
- Lead is soft, malleable and corrosion-resistant, and joints could be made by melting (wiped joints).
- Lead is toxic and dissolves more readily into soft (low-mineral, acidic) water.
- Lead pipes were banned for new water supply installations in the UK in 1970; copper and later plastic replaced them.
