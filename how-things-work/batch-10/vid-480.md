## Fact check

- Rug beating: carpets were hung outdoors and beaten with rattan/cane carpet beaters to drive dust out.
- Sprinkling damp tea leaves on carpets before sweeping to catch dust is a documented Victorian household practice.
- Mechanical carpet sweepers with rotating brushes were patented and sold from the 1870s; powered vacuum cleaners appeared in the early 1900s.
- Upright vacuums' rotating brush rolls are often called 'beater bars' because they agitate the carpet.
