## Fact check

- Concealed cisterns → the cistern sits in a frame behind a wall panel.
- Pneumatic actuation → many concealed cistern flush plates use bellows and small air tubes to operate the flush valve.
- Piston/actuator lifts the valve → the air pulse drives an actuator on the drop valve.
- Dual flush → pneumatic dual flush plates typically use one tube per button.
