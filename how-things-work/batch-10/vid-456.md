## Fact check

- Postal sorting machines image envelopes and use OCR to read printed and handwritten addresses, then print/spray a barcode (e.g. USPS uses fluorescent or ink barcodes; Royal Mail uses phosphor/ink codes).
- Letter sorting machines such as USPS delivery bar code sorters handle on the order of 30,000-40,000 letters per hour (about 10 per second).
- Unreadable addresses are sent to remote video encoding operators who key them from the image.
- Grid ratio is illustrative.
