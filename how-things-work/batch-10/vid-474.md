## Fact check

- Modern climbing ropes are kernmantle construction: a core ('kern') of nylon strands inside a woven sheath ('mantle').
- The core carries most of the load; the sheath protects it from abrasion, dirt and UV.
- Dynamic ropes are designed to stretch substantially in a fall, lengthening the stopping time and lowering peak force on the climber.
- Static (low-stretch) ropes are used for rescue, hauling and rappelling, not lead falls.
- Stretch and internal friction dissipate fall energy, partly as heat.
