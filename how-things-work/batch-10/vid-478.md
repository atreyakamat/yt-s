## Fact check

- Tooth powders → historical recipes used abrasives such as crushed shells, chalk, salt and ash/charcoal.
- Paste in jars → 1800s toothpastes and tooth powders were sold in jars and pots.
- 1841 → American painter John Goffe Rand patented the collapsible metal paint tube.
- 1892 → Connecticut dentist Washington Sheffield is credited with the first toothpaste in a collapsible tube.
- Plastic laminate tubes replaced metal in the second half of the 1900s.
