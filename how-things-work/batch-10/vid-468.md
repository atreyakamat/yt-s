## Fact check

- Conventional (torque-converter) automatics use a fluid coupling with an impeller (engine side), turbine (gearbox side) and stator; the 'two fans' analogy is the standard explanation.
- At idle in Drive the converter transmits a little torque, causing 'creep'.
- Ratios come from planetary gearsets (sun, planets, ring) selected by hydraulically applied clutches and bands; modern units are controlled by a transmission computer using speed and throttle position.
- Some automatics are CVTs or dual-clutch boxes, hence 'a classic automatic'.
