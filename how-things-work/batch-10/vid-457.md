## Fact check

- Manual staple guns (tackers) work by compressing a spring with the handle; a catch (sear) releases the driver blade at the end of the stroke, and the spring drives it down.
- Because the energy is stored then released at once, impact doesn't depend on how fast you squeeze.
- A feed spring in the magazine pushes the next staple under the driver.
