## Fact check

- Data centres house servers in racks; nearly all electrical power used by IT equipment ends up as heat.
- Hot-aisle/cold-aisle layout is standard practice.
- Uninterruptible power supplies (batteries) bridge the gap until diesel generators start.
- IEA estimates data centres used about 1-2% of global electricity in recent years (around 1.5% in 2024).
