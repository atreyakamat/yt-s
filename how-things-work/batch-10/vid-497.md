## Fact check

- Most laptop trackpads are capacitive: a grid of row and column electrodes measures changes in capacitance.
- A conductive finger alters the field at nearby electrode crossings; non-conductive objects like a plastic pen cap don't register.
- The controller scans the grid continuously (commonly around 100 times a second or more).
- Palm rejection ignores large contact areas while typing.
- Some premium trackpads add force sensors for pressure, but finger tracking itself is capacitive.
