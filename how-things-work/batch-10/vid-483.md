## Fact check

- Early cars were started with a hand crank; drivers had to set spark advance and choke/throttle first.
- Engine kickback could spin the crank backwards, causing broken thumbs, wrists and arms ('chauffeur's fracture').
- Charles Kettering's electric self-starter was introduced on the 1912 Cadillac; electric starters became standard through the 1910s-1920s.
