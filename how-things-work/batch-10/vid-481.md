## Fact check

- Nathaniel Baldwin built headphones in his kitchen in Utah around 1910 and sold them to the US Navy for radio use (widely documented history).
- Earlier telephone/radio operators used single earpieces or heavy headsets.
- Early headphones were moving-iron designs: a magnet and coil vibrated a thin iron diaphragm. Modern earbuds still use a magnet and voice coil to move a diaphragm.
- Stereo headphones for hi-fi appeared in the late 1950s; portable cassette players arrived in 1979.
- Nathaniel Baldwin built headsets in his Utah kitchen around 1910 and sold them to the US Navy: OK. 'First headphones' is disputed (earlier telephone headsets existed), so the title now says 'first modern headphones'. Source: https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathaniel_Baldwin
