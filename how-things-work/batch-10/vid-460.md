## Fact check

- AEDs analyse the heart rhythm through the pads and only advise a shock for shockable rhythms such as ventricular fibrillation.
- The device charges a capacitor (a few seconds) and delivers a biphasic shock lasting a few to around 10-20 milliseconds; a blink takes roughly 100-400 ms. Bars are approximate.
- Defibrillation stops the chaotic electrical activity, allowing the heart's natural pacemaker to re-establish a rhythm; it does not 'restart' a flatlined heart.
- Public AEDs give spoken instructions. This is educational only, not first-aid training.
