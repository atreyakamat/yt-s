## Fact check

- Aircraft wheels are not driven and are generally stationary before touchdown.
- Airliner touchdown speeds are typically about 230-280 km/h, so '~250 km/h' is a fair round figure.
- Tyres spin up within a fraction of a second; during that skid rubber is abraded and heated, creating the visible smoke.
- Rubber deposits build up in touchdown zones and are periodically removed to restore runway friction.
- Wheel pre-rotation has been proposed and tested many times but not adopted, largely due to weight, cost and complexity.
