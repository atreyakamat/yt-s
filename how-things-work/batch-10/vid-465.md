## Fact check

- 'Magic' erasers are melamine-formaldehyde resin foam; its open-cell microstructure is made of very hard, thin strands, so it acts as a very fine abrasive.
- The foam abrades away as it cleans, which is why it shrinks and crumbles.
- Because it is abrasive it can dull glossy finishes such as car paint and polished plastics; makers advise testing an inconspicuous area.
- Melamine foam is also used for acoustic and thermal insulation panels.
