## Fact check

- Hydraulic power steering uses an engine-driven pump and a rotary valve that directs pressurised fluid to assist the rack.
- Electric power steering (EPS), now used on most new cars, places a motor on the column or rack and uses a torque sensor (torsion bar) to measure driver effort.
- Speed-sensitive assistance reduces help at higher speeds for stability.
