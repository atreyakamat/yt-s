## Fact check

- Honey is roughly 80% sugars and about 17-18% water.
- High sugar concentration (low water activity) draws water out of microbes by osmosis.
- Honey is acidic, typically pH around 3.5-4, similar to orange juice.
- Bees add glucose oxidase, which produces small amounts of hydrogen peroxide when honey is diluted.
- Crystallisation is normal; if honey absorbs water it can ferment, hence 'keep the lid on'.
