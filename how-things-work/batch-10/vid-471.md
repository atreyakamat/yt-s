## Fact check

- Thermal paper is coated with a leuco dye and a developer (an acidic compound) that react when melted by heat, producing a dark image.
- Thermal printers use a printhead with a row of small resistive heating elements; no ink or toner is used.
- Heat darkens unprinted thermal paper; light, heat and some chemicals cause printed images to fade over time.
