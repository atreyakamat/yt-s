## Fact check

- The abacus has been used for thousands of years.
- The slide rule was invented in the 1620s (William Oughtred); engineers used slide rules widely until the 1970s, including in the Apollo program.
- 'Computer' was a job title for people who performed calculations; many were women (e.g. at observatories, NACA/NASA).
- Printed mathematical tables (logarithms, trig) were standard tools.
- Electronic pocket calculators became widely available in the early 1970s.
