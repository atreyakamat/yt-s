## Fact check

- Driver feedback ('your speed') signs use a low-power Doppler radar to measure approaching vehicles' speed and show it on an LED display; some show smiley or sad faces.
- They carry no enforcement, but studies show they reduce average speeds in many locations.
- Many units log vehicle counts and speeds for road authorities, and many are solar powered.
