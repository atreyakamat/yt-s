## Fact check

- The first parking meter was installed in Oklahoma City on 16 July 1935; it was devised by Carl Magee, a newspaper publisher, and cost a nickel per hour.
- Downtown spaces were being taken all day by workers, hurting shops; enforcing time limits by watching cars was impractical.
- Early meters were coin-operated clockwork timers that showed a red 'expired' flag when time ran out.
- London's first parking meters were installed in Mayfair in 1958.
