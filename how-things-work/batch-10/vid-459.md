## Fact check

- Reusable click hand warmers contain a supersaturated solution of sodium acetate (as the trihydrate) in water.
- Flexing the metal disc releases tiny seed crystals that nucleate crystallization; it spreads through the pack within seconds.
- Crystallization releases latent heat; the pack sits near the trihydrate's melting point, around 54-58 °C.
- Boiling the pack redissolves the crystals so it can be reused.
