## Fact check

- Mesopotamian cuneiform was written on clay tablets; Egyptians made papyrus from laminated strips of papyrus reed; parchment is prepared animal skin.
- Paper originated in China; Cai Lun is credited with improving and reporting papermaking to the emperor in 105 AD (earlier paper fragments exist).
- Papermaking spread through the Islamic world from the 8th century and reached Europe (Spain) by the 1100s.
