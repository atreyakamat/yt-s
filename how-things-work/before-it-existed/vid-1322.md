## Fact check

- Joseph Marie Jacquard's loom (demonstrated 1804) used punched cards to control weaving patterns.
- Herman Hollerith's punched-card tabulators processed the 1890 US census far faster than the hand-counted 1880 census.
- Programs and data were commonly punched one line per card into the 80-column card format, common from the 1920s-1970s.
- 80-column cards influenced 80-character terminal widths.
- Programmers marked deck edges with a diagonal line to make re-sorting easier.
