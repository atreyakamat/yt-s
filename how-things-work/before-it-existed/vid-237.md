## Fact check

- Sumerians around 3000 BC drank beer through long tubes; a gold-and-lapis straw was found in a royal tomb at Ur.
- Hollow rye grass stalks were used as straws in the 1800s; they tended to go soft and add a grassy flavour.
- Marvin Stone patented the wound paper drinking straw in 1888 (his prototype was paper wound around a pencil) and coated it with paraffin wax.
- The bendy straw was patented by Joseph Friedman in 1937 ('1930s' on screen).
