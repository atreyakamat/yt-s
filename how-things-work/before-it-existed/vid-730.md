## Fact check

- Adolf Eugen Fick produced glass scleral contact lenses in 1888; they covered much of the eye's surface and could only be tolerated for limited periods.
- PMMA plastic lenses appeared in the 1930s-40s, and corneal lenses (Kevin Tuohy, 1948) sat on the cornea only.
- Otto Wichterle developed hydrogel soft lenses; in 1961 he made the first ones at home using a spin-casting device built from a Merkur children's construction set.
