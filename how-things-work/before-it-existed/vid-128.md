## Fact check

- George de Mestral, a Swiss engineer, got the idea after a 1941 walk when burdock burrs stuck to his clothes and dog.
- He examined burrs under a microscope and saw tiny hooks catching loops in fabric and fur.
- The working version used nylon hooks and loops after years of development; the patent was granted in 1955 (Swiss patent filed 1951).
- Name: French 'velours' (velvet) and 'crochet' (hook).
- Hook and loop fasteners were used by NASA on Apollo missions and are common on shoes and bags.
