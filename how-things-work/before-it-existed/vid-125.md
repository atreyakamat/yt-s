## Fact check

- The first traffic signal was installed outside the Houses of Parliament in London in December 1868, designed by railway engineer John Peake Knight, with semaphore arms by day and red/green gas lamps at night, operated by a policeman.
- At night red meant stop and green meant caution.
- In January 1869 it exploded due to a gas leak, injuring the policeman operating it.
- The first electric traffic signal (red and green) was installed in Cleveland, Ohio in 1914; William Potts' three-colour signal with amber appeared in Detroit around 1920.
