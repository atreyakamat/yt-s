## Fact check

- Before the metronome, tempo was indicated with Italian terms such as allegro, andante and adagio, which are imprecise.
- Johann Nepomuk Maelzel patented the mechanical metronome in 1815 (building on Dietrich Nikolaus Winkel's design).
- It uses a double-weighted pendulum; moving the sliding weight up slows it and moving it down speeds it up.
- Metronome markings give tempo in beats per minute.
- Beethoven was among the first composers to add metronome markings, publishing them for his symphonies in 1817.
