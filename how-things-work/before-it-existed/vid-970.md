## Fact check

- Cork is the bark of the cork oak; it consists of sealed, gas-filled cells and has a density of roughly 0.12-0.24 g/cm³ (shown as about 0.2).
- The RNLI introduced cork lifejackets for lifeboat crews in the 1850s (Captain John Ross Ward's design, 1854).
- Titanic (1912) carried cork lifejackets; they could injure wearers who jumped from height.
- Kapok-filled jackets were used in the 20th century; closed-cell foam became standard later.
