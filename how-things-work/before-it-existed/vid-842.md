## Fact check

- 1990s PCs had PS/2 keyboard and mouse ports, a 25-pin parallel printer port (DB-25), serial ports and a game port; serial/parallel plugs used thumbscrews.
- Adding devices often required drivers and reboots.
- USB (Universal Serial Bus) 1.0 was released in 1996; legacy ports faded in the 2000s; USB-C followed in 2014.
- USB supplies power and supports hot-plugging.
