## Fact check

- In November 1902, on a hunting trip in Mississippi, President Theodore Roosevelt refused to shoot a bear that had been tied to a tree, considering it unsportsmanlike.
- Clifford Berryman's Washington Post cartoon of the incident was widely reprinted.
- Brooklyn shopkeeper Morris Michtom displayed a stuffed bear labeled 'Teddy's bear' and went on to found a toy company.
- Independently, the German firm Steiff produced a jointed bear in 1902-1903 that sold in large numbers.
- Teddy bears became a craze within a couple of years and remain popular over a century later.
