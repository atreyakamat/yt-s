## Fact check

- Wilhelm Conrad Röntgen discovered X-rays in November 1895 while experimenting with a cathode-ray tube, noticing a fluorescent screen glowing despite the tube being covered.
- He named them X-rays because their nature was unknown. On 22 December 1895 he X-rayed his wife Anna Bertha's hand, showing bones and her ring.
- Medical use began in 1896; Röntgen received the first Nobel Prize in Physics in 1901.
- Shoe-fitting fluoroscopes were used in shops from the 1920s to the 1950s. Bars are illustrative absorption.
