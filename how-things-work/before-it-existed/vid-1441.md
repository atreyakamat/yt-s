## Fact check

- SMS was designed to carry short messages over GSM signalling channels.
- SMS messages are limited to 160 7-bit characters (140 bytes).
- On 3 December 1992 engineer Neil Papworth sent 'Merry Christmas' from a computer to Vodafone's Richard Jarvis's handset in the UK; phones then couldn't type texts, so he couldn't reply.
- Handsets able to send SMS appeared from 1993 (Nokia); global SMS volumes reached billions per day in the 2000s.
