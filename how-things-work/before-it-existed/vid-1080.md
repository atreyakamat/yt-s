## Fact check

- Traditional home soap-making leached water through hardwood ash to produce lye (potassium hydroxide solution), then boiled it with saved animal fats to make soft soap.
- Lye is caustic and burns skin.
- Babylonian records from around 2800 BC describe soap-like material made from fats and ashes.
- Industrial soap production expanded in the 19th century; modern soap uses the same saponification reaction with measured, purified ingredients.
