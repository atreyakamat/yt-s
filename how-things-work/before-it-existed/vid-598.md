## Fact check

- Flint, shells, bronze → early razors were flint blades, sharpened shells were also used, and bronze razors appear in the Bronze Age.
- Straight razor nickname 'cut-throat' → common British name.
- Barbers → before safety razors, many men were shaved by barbers.
- 1880 → the Kampfe brothers patented a safety razor with a guard in the US in 1880.
- 1904 → patent granted for a razor using thin disposable double-edged blades (King Camp Gillette's, filed 1901).
- 1970s → twin-blade cartridge razors launched in 1971.
