## Fact check

- Birch bark tar lumps with human tooth impressions found in Scandinavia date back several thousand years (some around 9,000-10,000 years; a 5,700-year-old piece from Denmark yielded human DNA in 2019), so 'at least 5,000 years' is conservative.
- The Maya and Aztecs chewed chicle, latex from the sapodilla tree.
- Thomas Adams experimented with chicle as a rubber substitute in the 1870s; when that failed he sold it as chewing gum.
- Modern gum bases are mostly synthetic food-grade polymers (e.g. polyvinyl acetate, polyisobutylene).
