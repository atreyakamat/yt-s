## Fact check

- Phone keypads (ITU E.161) put letters on keys 2-9 (8 of 12 keys): three letters each, except 7 (PQRS) and 9 (WXYZ) with four.
- Multi-tap: H = 4 pressed twice, I = 4 pressed three times, so 'hi' = 5 presses (plus a pause, since both letters share a key); S = 7 pressed four times.
- T9 (Text on 9 keys) predictive text, introduced in the late 1990s, needed one press per letter ('hi' = 4, 4) and used a dictionary to pick the word; a key cycled through alternatives with the same key sequence.
