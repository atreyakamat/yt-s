## Fact check

- The widely told story: Frank Epperson, aged 11, left a cup of powdered soda drink with a stirring stick outside on a cold night in 1905 (San Francisco area); it froze on the stick. The script says 'the story goes' because it rests on Epperson's account.
- He began selling frozen treats on sticks around 1923 and received a patent in 1924.
- Earlier frozen desserts (water ices, ice cream) were served in cups or dishes.
