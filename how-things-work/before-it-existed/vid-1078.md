## Fact check

- Soap scum in hard water → soap reacts with calcium and magnesium ions to form insoluble scum that dulls hair.
- Etymology → 'shampoo' comes from Hindi 'champo' (imperative of chāmpnā, to press/knead).
- 1814 → Sake Dean Mahomed opened his shampooing vapour bath in Brighton.
- 1903 → Hans Schwarzkopf sold a shampoo powder in Berlin.
- 1930s → synthetic-detergent shampoos appeared; detergents don't form scum in hard water.
