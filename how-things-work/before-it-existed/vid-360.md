## Fact check

- 'Sad iron' (or flat iron): 'sad' is an old English word meaning solid or heavy. They were solid cast iron, often weighing a few kilograms, heated on a stove or fire.
- Irons were used in rotation: one in use while others reheated.
- Mary Florence Potts patented a sad iron with a detachable handle in 1870-71.
- Henry W. Seely patented the electric flatiron in 1882; electric irons spread in the early 20th century as homes were wired.
- Testing heat by sprinkling water and watching it sizzle is a traditional method.
