## Fact check

- Before cable ties, aircraft wire harnesses were laced with waxed cord tied by hand.
- Maurus C. Logan of Thomas & Betts devised the cable tie after seeing harness lacing (often told as workers with cut fingers); the patent was filed in 1958 (granted 1962).
- Cable ties use a pawl (tooth) in the head that engages ridges on the strap, letting it tighten but not loosen.
