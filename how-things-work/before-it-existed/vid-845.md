## Fact check

- Edward N. Hines of the Wayne County Road Commission, Michigan, is credited with the painted centre line, first used in 1911; the story that a leaking milk wagon inspired him is widely told, hence 'reportedly'.
- Dr June McCarroll painted a line on a road near Indio, California around 1917 and campaigned for them; California adopted centre lines statewide in 1924.
- Early roads were unmarked and shared by cars, carts and horses.
