## Fact check

- Safe-conduct letters issued by monarchs are a long-standing precursor of passports.
- In parts of 19th-century Europe passport requirements were relaxed and many travellers crossed borders without them.
- Passport requirements spread widely during and after the First World War.
- The 1920 League of Nations Paris Conference on Passports agreed guidelines for a standard booklet format with a photograph.
- Machine-readable passports followed in the 1980s; biometric e-passports with an embedded chip are now common.
