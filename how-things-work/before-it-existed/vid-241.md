## Fact check

- Lead-acid battery invented by Gaston Planté in 1859; nickel-cadmium by Waldemar Jungner in 1899.
- Early handheld mobile phones of the 1980s offered roughly 30 minutes of talk time on bulky batteries ('about half an hour').
- Lithium is the lightest metal and has very low electrode potential, giving high energy density.
- Lithium metal anodes had dendrite/fire risks; lithium-ion uses intercalation hosts instead. Commercialised by Sony in 1991.
- The 2019 Nobel Prize in Chemistry went to John Goodenough, M. Stanley Whittingham and Akira Yoshino for lithium-ion batteries.
