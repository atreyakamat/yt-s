## Fact check

- The oldest known stone tools (Lomekwi 3, Kenya) are about 3.3 million years old and were made by striking stones with hammerstones.
- Hafting (binding stone heads onto handles) appears much later in the Stone Age (evidence from several hundred thousand years ago).
- Kinetic energy = 1/2 m v^2, so doubling head speed quadruples energy.
- Iron claw hammers are known from the Roman period.
