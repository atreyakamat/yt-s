## Fact check

- Britain recorded 7,343 road deaths in 1934, with far fewer vehicles than today.
- In 1934 Transport Minister Leslie Hore-Belisha introduced crossings marked by metal studs and orange globes on black-and-white poles, later called Belisha beacons.
- The studded crossings were hard to see; after trials, the black-and-white striped zebra crossing was set in law by the Pedestrian Crossings Regulations 1951.
