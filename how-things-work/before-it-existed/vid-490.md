## Fact check

- Reading stones (magnifying domes of glass or rock crystal) were used in medieval Europe from around the 11th century.
- Spectacles are generally dated to around 1286 in northern Italy; a 1306 sermon by Giordano da Pisa mentions their invention about 20 years earlier.
- Early spectacles were convex lenses for close work, without temple arms; concave lenses for short sight appear in the 15th century; side arms became common in the 1700s (often credited to Edward Scarlett, c. 1727).
- Bars show approximate ages: about 740 years for glasses, about 300 for arms.
