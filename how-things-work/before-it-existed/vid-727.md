## Fact check

- Clay sealings stamped with seals were used in Mesopotamia and Egypt on doors and containers to reveal tampering rather than prevent entry.
- Padlocks were used in the Roman world; many Roman padlocks survive archaeologically.
- Barbed-spring padlocks (a bolt with flared spring leaves that catch inside the case, compressed by the key to withdraw) were used in Roman, Chinese and later medieval contexts.
- Padlocks were mass-produced in factories in the 1800s.
