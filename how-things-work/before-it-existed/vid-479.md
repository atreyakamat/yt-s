## Fact check

- Candles, oil lamps and rushlights were the main domestic lighting before gas.
- Gas lighting spread in British cities from the 1810s and into homes during the 1800s; lamps had a tap and were lit with a match or taper.
- Gas lighting produced soot, heat and fumes.
- Practical incandescent bulbs arrived around 1879-80; wall switches followed in the 1880s (e.g. J. H. Holmes's quick-break switch, 1884); home electrification spread through the 1900s.
