## Fact check

- Carl Flügge showed in the 1890s that droplets expelled during speech can carry bacteria.
- Johann von Mikulicz-Radecki is credited with using a gauze surgical mask in 1897.
- Gauze masks were reusable; cloth masks were widely used during the 1910-11 Manchurian plague and the 1918 flu.
- Disposable non-woven masks became common from the 1960s.
