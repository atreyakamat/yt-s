## Fact check

- Denis Papin, a French physicist, demonstrated his 'steam digester' to the Royal Society in London in 1679; it could soften bones.
- Papin is credited with an early weighted steam safety valve, the principle still used on pressure cookers and boilers.
- Pressure retorts were used in the canning industry in the 19th century to sterilise cans at above 100 °C.
- Domestic stovetop pressure cookers became popular from the late 1930s and 1940s (e.g. shown at the 1939 New York World's Fair). 1679 to the late 1930s is about 250-260 years.
