## Fact check

- Early cars had no mirrors; racing cars carried riding mechanics who, among other jobs, watched for traffic behind.
- Dorothy Levitt's 1909 book 'The Woman and the Car' advised women drivers to carry a small hand mirror to look behind.
- Ray Harroun won the first Indianapolis 500 (1911) driving alone with a rear-view mirror mounted on his car.
- Rear-view mirrors became common on production cars by the 1920s.
