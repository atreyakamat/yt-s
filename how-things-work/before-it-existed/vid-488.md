## Fact check

- Before machines, all garments were hand sewn; skilled hand sewing runs at a few dozen stitches per minute (30 is a fair estimate).
- Barthélemy Thimonnier patented a chain-stitch machine in France in 1830; tailors fearing for their livelihoods destroyed his workshop in 1831.
- Elias Howe patented a lockstitch machine in 1846 using two threads and an eye-pointed needle; early demonstrations were around 250 stitches a minute.
- In the 1850s machines were sold to households, including on instalment plans, and ready-made clothing became much cheaper.
- Modern domestic machines run around 750-1,100 stitches a minute.
