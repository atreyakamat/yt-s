## Fact check

- 8-inch floppy introduced 1971, 5.25-inch in 1976, 3.5-inch in the early 1980s with a rigid case and metal shutter.
- The disc inside is a flexible Mylar film with a magnetic coating; floppy drive heads contact the media.
- The common 3.5-inch HD floppy held 1.44 MB; phone photos are typically around 2-5 MB.
- 1 TB ÷ 1.44 MB ≈ 694,000, so about 700,000.
- On 3.5-inch disks, an open write-protect hole means the disk is protected.
