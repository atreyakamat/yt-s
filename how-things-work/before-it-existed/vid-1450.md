## Fact check

- Earle Dickson, a cotton buyer at a US medical supply company, created a ready-made adhesive bandage in 1920 for his wife Josephine, who often cut herself in the kitchen.
- He placed gauze pads on surgical tape and covered them with crinoline; his employer commercialised it.
- Early versions were handmade; machine production began in the mid-1920s and sterilised versions followed in the 1930s.
