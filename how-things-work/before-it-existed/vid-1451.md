## Fact check

- Wafer irons, two hinged iron plates on long handles used over a fire, were in use in medieval Europe; plates were often engraved with patterns, coats of arms or religious images.
- Deeper grid patterns producing pocketed waffles developed in the Low Countries in later centuries.
- Cornelius Swartwout of Troy, New York, received a US patent for a stovetop waffle iron on 24 August 1869.
- Electric waffle irons appeared in the early 20th century (e.g. General Electric developments in the 1910s); modern ones use thermostats and ready lights.
