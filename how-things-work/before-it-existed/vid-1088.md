## Fact check

- 'Straights' (shoes made on a single symmetrical last, wearable on either foot) were common in Europe and America into the early 19th century.
- Lasts are the foot-shaped forms shoes are built on; one last instead of a mirrored pair saved cost.
- Shoes shaped for left and right feet existed earlier (e.g. some Roman footwear).
- Copying lathes in the 1800s (e.g. Blanchard's 1818 lathe) made mirrored lasts easy, and left/right pairs became standard by the later 1800s.
