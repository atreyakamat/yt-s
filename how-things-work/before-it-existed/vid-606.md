## Fact check

- Businesses employed copy clerks and kept letter-copy books.
- James Watt patented a letter copying press in 1780, using special ink and damp tissue paper.
- Carbon paper came into use in the early 1800s and was common in offices into the late 20th century.
- Stencil duplicators (mimeographs) were widely used from the 1880s.
- Chester Carlson made the first xerographic copy in 1938; the first automatic plain-paper office copier launched in 1959. Grid is illustrative.
