## Fact check

- Chew sticks → used for thousands of years (Babylonian, Egyptian, Chinese and other records).
- Miswak → twigs of the arak tree (Salvadora persica), still widely used, especially in the Middle East, Africa and South Asia.
- 1498 → the bristle toothbrush with hog hair set in bone or bamboo is recorded in China in 1498.
- William Addis → made a toothbrush while in prison around 1770 and founded a toothbrush business around 1780.
- Nylon bristles 1938 → first nylon-bristle toothbrushes went on sale in the US in 1938; natural bristles stay damp longer.
