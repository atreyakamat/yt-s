## Fact check

- 19th-century city milk was often produced in poor conditions and transported without refrigeration; milk-borne diseases (e.g. tuberculosis, typhoid) were a real problem.
- Louis Pasteur developed gentle heating to prevent wine spoilage in the 1860s; the process bears his name.
- Milk pasteurisation was developed and adopted from the late 1800s into the early 1900s.
- Standard HTST pasteurisation: at least 72 °C for 15 seconds, then rapid cooling.
