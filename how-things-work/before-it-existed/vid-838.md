## Fact check

- Basin and jug washing → standard in homes without plumbing; the washstand with basin and ewer.
- Greek showers → Greek vase paintings (c. 4th century BC) show athletes showering under spouts fed by piped water.
- 1767 → William Feetham, a London stove maker, patented a hand-pumped shower that recirculated the water.
- Regency shower → tall English showers around 1810.
- Showers at home spread with indoor plumbing and hot running water in the 1900s.
