## Fact check

- On 3 May 1978, Gary Thuerk of Digital Equipment Corporation sent an advert for a product presentation to about 400 of the roughly 2,600 ARPANET users; it drew complaints and a rebuke from network administrators.
- The term 'spam' derives from Monty Python's 1970 'Spam' sketch, where the word is repeated endlessly; it was applied to online junk messages in the 1980s-1990s.
- Spam accounts for roughly 45-50% of global email traffic, with most filtered automatically.
