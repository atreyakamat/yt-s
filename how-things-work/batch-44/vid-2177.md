## Fact check

- Cinema and theatre floors are raked (stadium-style seating) so each row sits higher than the one in front, improving sightlines over the heads ahead (general design principle). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadium_seating
- Sightline planning and staggered seating are standard seating design techniques (no numbers used). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movie_theater
