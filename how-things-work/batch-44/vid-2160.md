## Fact check

- Strip curtains are used in doorways of cold storage and freezer rooms to reduce air exchange while allowing passage by people and vehicles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strip_curtain
- Cold air is denser than warm air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Density_of_air
