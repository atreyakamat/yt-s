## Fact check

- A centrifugal separator spins milk: denser skim milk moves toward the bowl wall and lighter cream gathers toward the centre; gravity separation is slow. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cream_separator
- Skimmed milk has most of the fat removed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skimmed_milk
