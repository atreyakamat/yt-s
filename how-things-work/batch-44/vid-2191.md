## Fact check

- Georges Claude showed neon tube lighting in Paris in 1910; a large neon sign lit Paris by 1913. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neon_lighting
- Pure neon produces an orange glow; other colours use other gases or phosphor coatings; an electrode at each end of a sealed tube and several thousand volts ionise the low-pressure gas. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neon_lighting
