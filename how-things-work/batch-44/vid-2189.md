## Fact check

- Earth's axial tilt is about 23.44 degrees, which causes the seasons. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axial_tilt
- Seasons are caused by the axial tilt, not by changes in distance from the Sun. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Season
