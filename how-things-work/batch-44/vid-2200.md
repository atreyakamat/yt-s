## Fact check

- Inverted bottle water coolers: water leaves and air enters the bottle in bubbles to replace the volume, equalising pressure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_cooler
- Air pressure holds water in an inverted container with small opening until air can enter (the principle of the chicken feeder/bottle dispenser). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_pressure
