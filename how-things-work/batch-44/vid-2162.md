## Fact check

- Electrosurgery uses high-frequency alternating current (above ~100-200 kHz) which does not stimulate nerves and muscles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrosurgery
- Heating is concentrated at the small active electrode; cut and coagulation modes use different waveforms. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrosurgery
- The large return (dispersive) electrode spreads the current to avoid burns. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrosurgery
