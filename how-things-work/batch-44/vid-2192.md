## Fact check

- Before general anaesthesia, surgery was done quickly, with patients restrained; alcohol and opium were used. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_general_anesthesia
- On 16 October 1846 William T. G. Morton demonstrated ether anaesthesia at Massachusetts General Hospital (the Ether Dome). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ether_Dome
- Ether was first synthesised in 1540 (Valerius Cordus). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diethyl_ether
- Ether's effects had been noticed earlier, including recreational use in the 1840s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diethyl_ether
- News spread internationally within months. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_general_anesthesia
