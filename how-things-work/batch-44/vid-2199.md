## Fact check

- Pop-up tents use flexible spring-steel wire sewn into the fabric; folding twists the wire into coils, and releasing lets it return to its open shape. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tent
- Spring steel returns to its original shape after bending (elastic energy). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elastic_energy
