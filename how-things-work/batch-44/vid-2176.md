## Fact check

- Vortex generators create vortices that mix high-energy air into the slow boundary layer, delaying flow separation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vortex_generator
- Delaying separation helps avoid stalls and allows flight at lower speeds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vortex_generator
- A stall is a reduction in lift when airflow separates from the wing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stall_(fluid_dynamics)
