## Fact check

- Chocolate bars are moulded with grooves that act as thin lines where the bar breaks; thinner sections break under less force (stress concentrates at the thin section). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chocolate_bar
- Bar chart is illustrative; no saving is claimed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chocolate_bar
- Chocolate is poured into moulds and sets after tempering. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chocolate
