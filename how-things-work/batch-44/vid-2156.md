## Fact check

- A skydiver reaches about 99% of terminal speed after about 15 seconds (90% after 8 s). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminal_velocity
- Belly-to-earth terminal velocity is about 200 km/h (120 mph). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parachuting
- Head-down speeds: about 90 m/s (about 320 km/h) with limbs pulled in; script bar uses about 320. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminal_velocity
- Felix Baumgartner reached about 1,357 km/h in a 2012 jump from about 39 km. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Baumgartner
