## Fact check

- The 1900 Galveston hurricane killed an estimated 6,000 to 12,000 people, with little warning. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1900_Galveston_hurricane
- TIROS-1, launched 1 April 1960, was the first operational weather satellite and returned television pictures of clouds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TIROS-1
- Hurricane reconnaissance flights began in the 1940s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurricane_hunters
- Satellites provide imagery of whole storm systems, including over open ocean. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_satellite
