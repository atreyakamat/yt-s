## Fact check

- Machine-made glass marbles are produced by cutting a stream of molten glass into gobs that roll between grooved rollers; Martin Christensen patented a marble machine in the early 1900s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marble_(toy)
- Colour streaks are added to the glass stream. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marble_(toy)
- Surface tension pulls molten glass toward a sphere. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_tension
