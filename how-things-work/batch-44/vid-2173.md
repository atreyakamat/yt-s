## Fact check

- A light (often UV) attracts flying insects to interleaved bare wire grids carrying a high voltage, typically 2 kilovolts or more; current through the insect kills it, and the grid is designed so current is too low to endanger a person. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bug_zapper
- A University of Delaware study of six devices over 15 summer nights killed 13,789 insects, only 31 of them biting insects (about 0.2%). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bug_zapper
- Mosquitoes are drawn to carbon dioxide and water vapour from breath rather than UV light. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bug_zapper
- Grid icon shows 1 highlighted in 40 as a visual stand-in only (31 of 13,789 is far less than 1 in 40).
