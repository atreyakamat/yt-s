## Fact check

- Foley is named for Jack Donovan Foley, who began at Universal Studios in 1914; early microphones captured mostly dialogue so other sounds were added later. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foley_(filmmaking)
- Horse hooves are made with coconut shell halves stuffed with padding; bones breaking with items such as frozen romaine lettuce; footsteps are done in Foley pits. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foley_(filmmaking)
- The Jazz Singer (1927) was the first commercially successful sound film. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silent_film
