## Fact check

- Test tubes are typically round-bottomed (also flat-bottomed variants exist) and held in racks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_tube
- Round bottoms are easier to clean and spread stress and heat more evenly than sharp corners. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_tube
- Round-bottomed tubes are used in centrifuges. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrifuge_tube
