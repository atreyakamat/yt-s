## Fact check

- An electrocardiogram records the heart's electrical activity via electrodes on the skin. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrocardiography
- Standard ECG paper speed is 25 mm/s, so a 1 mm small square is 0.04 s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrocardiography
- ECG signals are of the order of a millivolt. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrocardiography
