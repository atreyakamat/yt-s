## Fact check

- Honey bee wings beat in the range of roughly 200-230 times per second, and the buzz pitch reflects that rate. Source: https://www.sciencefocus.com/nature/why-do-bees-buzz
- Bees generate heat by shivering flight muscles; bee flight muscles are asynchronous (stretch-activated). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insect_flight
