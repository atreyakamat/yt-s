## Fact check

- The penny system labels nail sizes with a d, from denarius; one explanation is that it once reflected the price per hundred nails. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penny_(unit)
- A 16d nail is 3.5 inches (89 mm) long. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nail_(fastener)
- In the system, a larger number means a longer nail. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nail_(fastener)
