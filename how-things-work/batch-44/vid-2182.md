## Fact check

- Dental composite resins are light-cured: a photoinitiator such as camphorquinone absorbs blue light (around 450-470 nm) and triggers polymerisation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_composite
- Curing lights are commonly LED units emitting blue light. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_curing_light
- Composite is placed and cured in thin increments because light penetration is limited. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_composite
- Orange shields filter blue light to protect the eyes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dental_curing_light
