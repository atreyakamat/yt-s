## Fact check

- Édouard Bénédictus's 1903 invention was inspired by a lab accident: a flask coated with cellulose nitrate shattered but its pieces did not separate. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laminated_glass
- Laminated glass bonds glass layers with a plastic interlayer (PVB came later, 1927) so fragments stay attached; windscreens are its most common use today. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laminated_glass
