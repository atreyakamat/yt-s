## Fact check

- A disco ball is covered in hundreds or thousands of mirrored facets on a lightweight core; each reflects the spotlight, and a motor turns it at typically 0.5 to 3 rpm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disco_ball
- Records of a mirrored ball date to 1897; they were widespread in nightclubs in the 1920s and standard in discotheques in the 1960s to 1980s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disco_ball
