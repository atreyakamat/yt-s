## Fact check

- Electronic cart systems fit each cart with a locking wheel clamp ("boot"); a wire around the car park perimeter locks the boot when the cart leaves; staff unlock with a handheld remote; a cattle-grid-style floor can stop carts at exits; the article notes they are expensive and not foolproof.
