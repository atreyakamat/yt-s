## Fact check

- 19th-century workers used woven baskets or a handkerchief, then tinplate boxes and recycled biscuit tins; fitted boxes appeared in the 1850s and patents in the 1860s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunchbox
- American Thermos produced the first metal lunch box for kids in 1920; the first licensed character lunch box (Mickey Mouse) came in 1935. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunchbox
