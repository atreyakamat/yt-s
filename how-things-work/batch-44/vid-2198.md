## Fact check

- Olive oil extraction: crushing to a paste, malaxation for about 20-30 minutes, then separation by centrifugation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_oil
- The maximum temperature permitted for cold-pressed oil is 27 °C. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olive_oil
