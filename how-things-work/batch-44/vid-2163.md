## Fact check

- Watering cans use a spout and often a perforated 'rose' to break the flow into a spray. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watering_can
- Rose position (up or down) changes the spray strength (qualitative, common design). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watering_can
