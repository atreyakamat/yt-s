## Fact check

- Contact and rubbing between materials transfers charge (the triboelectric effect). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triboelectric_effect
- Like charges repel, which makes charged hair strands spread apart; dry air limits leakage so charge builds up until it discharges as a spark. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Static_electricity
