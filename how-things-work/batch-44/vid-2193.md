## Fact check

- A recoil starter has a rope coiled on a reel under spring tension; pulling it tensions the spring and engages a ratcheting (freewheel) clutch that turns the crankshaft; once the engine runs the clutch disengages and the spring retracts the rope. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recoil_starter
