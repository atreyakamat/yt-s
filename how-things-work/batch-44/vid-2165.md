## Fact check

- Hard candy is made by boiling sugar and glucose syrup to the hard crack stage, about 149 to 154 C (script: about 150). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_candy
- Hard candy is an amorphous solid, a sugar glass, with no crystalline structure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_candy
- Hard candy absorbs moisture from humid air and becomes sticky. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_candy
