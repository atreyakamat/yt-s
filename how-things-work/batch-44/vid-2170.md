## Fact check

- The Metropolitan Free Drinking Fountain Association's first London fountain opened 21 April 1859 on Holborn Hill (St Sepulchre-without-Newgate), paid for by Samuel Gurney; the article does not say it was the city's first public fountain; water from private companies was 'often contaminated'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drinking_fountain
- Private water companies were often inadequate and contaminated at that time. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drinking_fountain
