## Fact check

- A gobo is a stencil or template placed in or near a light source to control the shape of the projected light; the name is commonly said to come from "goes before optics". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gobo_(lighting)
- Gobos are made of metal or glass; glass gobos can carry colour and fine detail (general description). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gobo_(lighting)
