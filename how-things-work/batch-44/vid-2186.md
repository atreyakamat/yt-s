## Fact check

- A pressure altimeter is an aneroid barometer calibrated to read altitude, because air pressure falls with height. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altimeter
- Atmospheric pressure at about 5.5 km is roughly half of the sea-level value. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_pressure
- Aneroid capsules expand and contract with pressure and move the needle through linkages. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aneroid_barometer
- Pilots set the altimeter to local pressure (QNH) because weather changes pressure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altimeter#Pressure_altimeter
