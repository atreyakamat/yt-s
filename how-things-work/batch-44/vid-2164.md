## Fact check

- 1908 London Olympic marathon covered 26 miles 385 yards (about 42.195 km) from Windsor Castle to the stadium; made official by the IAAF in 1921. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathon
- First modern Olympic marathon was in 1896; distances varied before 1921. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marathon
