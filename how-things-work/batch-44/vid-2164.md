## Fact check

- The torch relay was introduced by Carl Diem at the 1936 Berlin Games and "had no ancient precedent". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_torch
- First lighting ceremony at Olympia on 20 July 1936; the flame travelled 3,187 km carried by 3,331 runners in twelve days and eleven nights. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_torch
- Torches made by Krupp; the mirror used to light the flame made by Zeiss Optics; a sacred fire burned on the altar of Hestia at the ancient Olympics. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_torch
