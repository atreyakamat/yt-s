## Fact check

- Candling checks eggs; in US grading air cell size and quality determine AA, A or B. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg_as_food
- In the EU, egg codes begin with 0 organic, 1 free-range, 2 barn, 3 cages. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg_as_food
