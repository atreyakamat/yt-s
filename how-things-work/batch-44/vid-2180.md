## Fact check

- Some ice cream scoops have a thermally conductive liquid in the handle that transfers hand heat and helps keep ice cream from freezing to the metal. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_cream_scoop
- Heat flows from warmer to cooler objects. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_transfer
