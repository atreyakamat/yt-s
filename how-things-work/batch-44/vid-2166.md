## Fact check

- A basic periscope uses two mirrors (or prisms) at 45 degrees at each end of a tube so light is redirected down the tube to the viewer's eye. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Periscope
- Submarine periscopes may include lenses for magnification. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Periscope
- Some modern submarines use photonics masts with cameras instead of optical periscopes that penetrate the hull. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Submarine_periscope
