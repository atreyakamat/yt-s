## Fact check

- The chain brake activator sits forward of the upper handle and is triggered by a kickback event; it tensions a band around the clutch drum, stopping the chain within milliseconds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chainsaw
- Kickback occurs when a chain tooth at the tip of the guide bar catches on wood without cutting through it, swinging the bar upward toward the operator; the clutch releases immediately. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chainsaw
