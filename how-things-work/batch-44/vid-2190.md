## Fact check

- Manual call points are switches in a fire alarm system operated by pressing or breaking a frangible element; the signal goes to the fire alarm panel which sounds the alarms. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manual_fire_alarm_activation
- 'Blink' is a SOFTENED qualitative claim: the switch closes instantly when the element gives way. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manual_fire_alarm_activation
