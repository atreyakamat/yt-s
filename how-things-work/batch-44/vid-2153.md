## Fact check

- Hedge trimmers use reciprocating blades, where a moving toothed blade slides against a fixed one, producing a shearing cut. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedge_trimmer
- Teeth have guards or wide gaps to feed branches and protect the user. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedge_trimmer
