## Fact check

- The horn emits a constant frequency; observers on the train hear no change.
- A moving source compresses waves ahead of it and stretches those behind (Doppler effect), raising the received frequency on approach and lowering it on recession.
- The shift switches from high to low as the source passes the listener.
- Christian Doppler proposed the effect in 1842; Buys Ballot tested it in 1845 in the Netherlands using musicians on a train. Police radar uses the Doppler effect.
