## Fact check

- Driving the main rotor creates an equal and opposite torque on the fuselage (Newton's third law).
- A tail rotor produces sideways thrust at the end of a long boom to counter that torque.
- Anti-torque pedals change the tail rotor's blade pitch, controlling yaw.
- Tandem and coaxial helicopters use counter-rotating rotors so torques cancel and need no tail rotor.
