## Fact check

- Home carbonators use a cylinder of liquefied CO2; a valve releases gas through a tube into a sealed bottle of water.
- Gas solubility rises with pressure (Henry's law) and is higher in cold water.
- Some dissolved CO2 forms carbonic acid, giving a sharp taste.
- A pressure-relief valve vents excess pressure, producing the characteristic buzzing sound.
- Opening the bottle lowers pressure, so dissolved gas comes out as bubbles.
