## Fact check

- Uncoated glass or plastic reflects roughly 4% of light at each surface (more for high-index plastics).
- Anti-reflective coatings are multilayer thin films, each layer around a quarter of a wavelength thick (tens to ~100 nm).
- Reflections from the layer boundaries interfere destructively, reducing reflection to well under 1%.
- Energy not reflected is transmitted, improving light through the lens; a faint residual (often green) reflection remains.
- A bare glass lens reflects about 4% per surface: standard optics figure (Fresnel reflection at n of 1.5). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-reflective_coating
