## Fact check

- Water expands about 9% in volume when it freezes.
- Research (e.g. University of Illinois, for the insulation industry) showed pipes burst not at the ice blockage but where water is trapped between the ice and a closed fixture, as pressure rises.
- Liquid water is nearly incompressible, so pressure rises sharply.
- Leaks often appear on thawing, when the ice no longer blocks the split. Pipe lagging reduces freezing risk.
