## Fact check

- A mechanical watch is regulated by a balance wheel oscillating on a hairspring; the spring makes each oscillation take the same time (isochronism).
- Each swing operates the escapement (pallet fork), releasing the escape wheel one tooth at a time; the escapement also gives the balance a small push.
- 28,800 vibrations per hour (4 Hz) is a very common beat rate: 8 beats per second.
- The seconds hand therefore advances in small steps, appearing to sweep, unlike most quartz watches which step once per second.
- 4 Hz x 2 beats x 3600 = 28,800 beats per hour: arithmetic.
