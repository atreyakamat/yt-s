## Fact check

- Curved fingernail clipper jaws → standard fingernail clippers have concave curved cutting edges.
- Edges meet and pinch → nail clipper blades are end-cutting: both edges close against each other.
- Toenail clippers often straight, toenails cut straight across → common design; health services (e.g. NHS) advise cutting toenails straight across.
- Toenail clippers bigger → toenails are thicker, so toenail clippers have larger jaw openings and longer levers.
