## Fact check

- USB ports supply 5 V by default (vSafe5V).
- USB Power Delivery negotiation runs over the USB-C CC (configuration channel) line: the source advertises Power Data Objects (e.g., 5 V, 9 V, 15 V, 20 V), the sink requests one, the source accepts and transitions. This takes well under a second.
- Other fast-charge protocols (e.g., Qualcomm QC) also negotiate before raising voltage.
- Charging power is reduced as the battery approaches full (constant-voltage phase), with renegotiation as needed.
