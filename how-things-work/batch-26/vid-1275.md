## Fact check

- Dry cleaning uses non-water solvents, historically and still commonly perchloroethylene ('perc'), as well as hydrocarbon and other solvents.
- Water swells hydrophilic fibres like wool and silk, causing shrinkage and distortion; solvents don't, and they dissolve oils and grease well.
- Machines wash, extract and dry in a closed drum; solvent is filtered and distilled for reuse.
- Water-based stains are pre-treated ('spotting') before the solvent wash; finishing uses steam and pressing.
