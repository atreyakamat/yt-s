## Fact check

- Snap-action bimetallic disc thermostats/cut-outs are widely used as overheat protection in heaters, boilers and appliances.
- The disc is a bimetal (two metals with different expansion) formed into a dome; at the calibrated temperature it snaps to the opposite curvature, opening contacts quickly.
- Auto-reset types snap back on cooling; manual-reset types need a button, hence 'on many'.
