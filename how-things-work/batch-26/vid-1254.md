## Fact check

- Air hockey tables have a playing surface perforated with many small holes, fed by a blower in a plenum beneath.
- Air jets through the holes create a thin cushion that lifts the light plastic puck slightly, reducing friction to near zero.
- With the blower off, the puck rests on the surface and friction stops it quickly.
- Hovercraft use the same principle of riding on a cushion of air.
