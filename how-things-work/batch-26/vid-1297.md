## Fact check

- Typical 115/125 mm angle grinders run around 10,000-12,000 rpm no-load.
- A bevel gear pair turns the drive 90° from the motor axis to the spindle, giving the tool its name.
- Rim speed for a 125 mm disc at 11,000 rpm: pi x 0.125 m x 183 rev/s = about 72 m/s = about 260 km/h.
- Grinding sparks are hot steel particles that oxidise (burn) in air.
- Discs are marked with a maximum operating speed (rpm and m/s, commonly 80 m/s) which must exceed the tool's rated speed; the guard is a required safety feature.
- 125 mm disc at 11,000 rpm: pi x 0.125 m x 11,000/60 = about 72 m/s = about 260 km/h: arithmetic.
