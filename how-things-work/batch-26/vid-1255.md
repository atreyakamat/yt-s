## Fact check

- Reusable gel packs contain water with gelling agents (e.g. cellulose derivatives or sodium polyacrylate) and often a freezing-point depressant such as propylene glycol, so they stay flexible.
- Home freezers run at about -18 °C; a freezing-point depressant keeps the gel from setting completely solid. The gauge is illustrative.
- Gel packs absorb heat as they warm; melting ice crystals absorb latent heat, adding cooling.
