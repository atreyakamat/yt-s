## Fact check

- Ferromagnetic steel contains magnetic domains; when randomly oriented their fields cancel, when aligned the steel is magnetised.
- Stroking steel in one direction with a strong magnet (or using a magnetiser block) aligns domains and magnetises it.
- Many screwdrivers and bit holders use a permanent magnet in the holder to magnetise the bit.
- Impacts and heating (towards the Curie temperature) disorder domains and reduce magnetisation; demagnetiser tools do this with a reversing field.
