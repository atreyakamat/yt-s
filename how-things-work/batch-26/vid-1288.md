## Fact check

- Bubble structure → a soap film is a thin water layer stabilised by surfactant molecules on both surfaces.
- Drainage thins the top → gravity and evaporation thin the film, especially at the top, where bubbles usually burst.
- Rainbow colours → thin-film interference between reflections from the two surfaces; colours change with thickness.
- Hole edge retracts at metres per second → film rupture speeds (Taylor-Culick) are typically several to tens of m/s, so a bath-sized bubble disappears in a few milliseconds.
- Blink → a blink takes roughly 100-400 ms, far longer.
