## Fact check

- Webcams commonly capture around 30 fps.
- Video codecs use inter-frame compression: mostly sending differences from previous frames.
- Real-time media is sent in small packets, usually over UDP (e.g. RTP/WebRTC); late or lost packets are concealed or skipped rather than retransmitted where waiting would add delay.
- Acoustic echo cancellation removes the far-end audio from the local microphone signal.
- ITU-T G.114 recommends one-way delay under about 150 ms for natural conversation; 400 ms is commonly cited as the upper acceptable limit.
