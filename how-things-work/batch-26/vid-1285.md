## Fact check

- Emergency stop devices (IEC 60947-5-5, ISO 13850) are red actuators on a yellow background, typically mushroom-headed.
- They use normally closed contacts with positive (direct) opening, so pressing mechanically forces contacts apart even if welded; an open circuit, such as a broken wire, also stops the machine.
- They latch when pressed and must be manually reset (twist or pull); resetting must not restart the machine by itself.
