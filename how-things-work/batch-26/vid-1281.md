## Fact check

- Recovered paper is repulped in water; screens and cleaners remove contaminants such as staples and plastics.
- Flotation de-inking uses surfactants and injected air; hydrophobic ink particles attach to bubbles and are removed as froth.
- Fibres shorten with each cycle; paper fibre is commonly said to be recyclable about 5-7 times.
- Virgin fibre is often blended in to maintain strength.
