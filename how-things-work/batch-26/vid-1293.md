## Fact check

- Stop-start systems shut the engine off when stationary and restart it when the brake is released or the clutch pressed, subject to battery charge, engine temperature and climate-control conditions.
- They use strengthened starters or belt-driven starter-generators and enhanced (EFB/AGM) batteries rated for many more start cycles.
- Restarts typically take around 0.3-0.4 s.
- Fuel savings are largest in urban stop-start driving (often quoted at several percent) and negligible on the open road.
