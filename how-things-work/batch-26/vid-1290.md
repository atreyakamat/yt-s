## Fact check

- Some cordless stick vacuums use brushless DC motors rated at around 100,000-125,000 rpm; 100,000 rpm is about 1,667 revolutions per second.
- Current Formula 1 engines are limited to 15,000 rpm; a car engine cruises at roughly 2,000-3,000 rpm.
- The fan expels air, lowering pressure at the inlet so outside air (and dirt) flows in.
- Brushless motors use electronic commutation (a controller switching electromagnet coils), allowing higher speeds and smaller size than brushed universal motors.
- Dyson digital motors cited at 104,000 rpm (about 1,700 per second); Formula 1 engines are limited to 15,000 rpm: OK for "some" motors. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyson_(company)
