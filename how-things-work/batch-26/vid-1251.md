## Fact check

- Correction tape dispensers contain a supply spool of carrier ribbon coated with a dry white film and adhesive, a pressing tip, and a gear-driven take-up spool.
- Pressure transfers the white film to paper; the empty carrier is wound back.
- The film is dry, so it can be written on immediately; misuse can jam or snap the ribbon.
