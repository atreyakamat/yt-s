## Fact check

- Lane departure warning and lane keeping assist use a forward camera (usually mounted behind the windscreen near the mirror) and image processing to detect lane markings.
- The system estimates lateral position continuously; it warns if the car drifts across a line without the indicator, and lane keeping applies corrective torque via the electric power steering.
- Worn markings, snow, rain and glare degrade performance; these are driver-assistance features, not self-driving.
