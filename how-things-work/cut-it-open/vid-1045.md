## Fact check

- Most thermal cameras detect long-wave infrared (about 8-14 µm), emitted by objects near room and body temperature.
- Ordinary glass is opaque at these wavelengths; lenses use germanium, chalcogenide glass or similar.
- Uncooled thermal sensors are microbolometer arrays: thermally isolated suspended membranes whose resistance changes with temperature.
- Long-wave infrared penetrates smoke far better than visible light, so firefighters use thermal imagers. Bars are illustrative.
