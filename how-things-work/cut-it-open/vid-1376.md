## Fact check

- Electric egg cookers steam eggs using a measured volume of water on a heating plate; the cup is marked for soft, medium and hard.
- While water remains the plate stays near 100 °C; once it boils dry the temperature rises sharply and a thermostat switches the heater off and triggers a buzzer, so the water volume sets the cooking time.
- Many measuring cups include a pin for piercing the shell, which lets air escape and reduces cracking.
