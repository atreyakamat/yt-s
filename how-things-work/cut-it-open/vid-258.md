## Fact check

- A typical fuel sender is a float on a pivoting arm that moves a wiper over a variable resistor (rheostat); the gauge responds to the resistance.
- Gauges are damped (mechanically or electronically) so sloshing doesn't swing the needle.
- Irregular tank shapes and float travel limits make many gauges non-linear; a needle that lingers near full is a common, well-known effect.
