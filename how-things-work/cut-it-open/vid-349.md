## Fact check

- Track circuits divide the line into electrically insulated blocks with a low-voltage supply at one end and a relay at the other.
- With the block clear, the relay is energised and the signal can show proceed.
- A train's wheelsets connect the rails, shunting the current away from the relay so it drops, setting the signal to danger.
- The design is fail-safe: a broken rail or lost power also de-energises the relay. Many lines now also use axle counters.
