## Fact check

- Phone micro-speakers are moving-coil (dynamic) drivers: a voice coil attached to a diaphragm sits in a permanent magnet's gap.
- Alternating audio current makes the coil's magnetic field reverse, so it is pushed and pulled by the permanent magnet (Lorentz force), moving the diaphragm.
- Frequency of motion sets pitch; low frequencies need large volume displacement, which tiny drivers can't provide, so phone speakers have weak bass.
