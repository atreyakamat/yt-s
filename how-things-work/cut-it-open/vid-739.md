## Fact check

- Turbofan thrust reversers don't reverse engine rotation; they redirect airflow.
- Cascade-type reversers on high-bypass engines slide the cowl back; blocker doors block the bypass duct and cascade vanes direct the air forward and outward.
- Reverse thrust supplements wheel brakes and spoilers, reducing brake wear and stopping distance, especially on wet runways.
- Reversers are most effective at high speed and are typically stowed at lower speeds to avoid ingesting debris.
