## Fact check

- Locking pliers use an over-centre toggle linkage: as the link passes slightly beyond the straight (dead-centre) position, the reaction force from the jaws holds it locked.
- Mechanical advantage of a toggle rises sharply as the links approach straight, giving very high clamping force.
- The screw in the end of the handle adjusts the jaw opening; the release lever pushes the link back over centre.
