## Fact check

- W-beam guardrail uses a corrugated steel rail whose profile gives beam strength; on impact the rail deflects in tension and posts yield, absorbing energy.
- Barriers are designed to contain and redirect vehicles at a shallow angle rather than stop them abruptly.
- Blunt barrier ends could penetrate vehicles; modern installations bury or flare ends or use energy-absorbing terminals that flatten the rail as the head is pushed along it.
