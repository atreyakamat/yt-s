## Fact check

- Cotton is cellulose; neighbouring chains are held by hydrogen bonds.
- Wrinkling occurs when hydrogen bonds break under bending/moisture and re-form in the deformed shape; heat and moisture from ironing let them re-form flat.
- Wrinkle-resistant finishes use cross-linking resins (e.g. DMDHEU-type) cured at high temperature to form covalent links between cellulose chains.
- Cross-linking reduces tear/abrasion strength and softness: a known trade-off.
