## Fact check

- Railway points (switches) have two movable tapered switch rails (blades) between fixed stock rails.
- One blade is closed against its stock rail and the other open; the wheel flange is guided by the closed blade.
- Point machines move and lock the blades; interlocking ensures signals only clear when points are detected and locked in position.
- At the frog (crossing), check rails (guard rails) guide the opposite wheel to keep flanges on the right path.
