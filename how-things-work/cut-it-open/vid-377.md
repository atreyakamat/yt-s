## Fact check

- LCD pixels are made of red, green and blue subpixels with colour filters.
- LCDs use a backlight (usually LEDs) that stays lit; liquid crystals between polarisers control how much light passes. (Some monitors dim backlight zones, but the principle stands.)
- Additive mixing: red + green light is seen as yellow; all three as white.
- 1920 × 1080 × 3 = 6,220,800 subpixels.
- A water droplet magnifies the subpixels: a well-known demonstration.
