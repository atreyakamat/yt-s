## Fact check

- Adaptive bitrate streaming (HLS, DASH) splits video into segments of a few seconds, each encoded at multiple bitrates/resolutions.
- Players maintain a buffer of downloaded segments.
- The player switches to lower-bitrate segments when throughput drops, to avoid stalling (rebuffering), and climbs back when it improves.
