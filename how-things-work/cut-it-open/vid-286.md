## Fact check

- Wrist heart-rate sensors use photoplethysmography (PPG): LEDs illuminate the skin and photodiodes measure reflected light.
- Green light is strongly absorbed by blood (haemoglobin), so changes in blood volume with each pulse change the reflected signal.
- Motion and ambient light leaking under a loose band are known sources of PPG error, so manufacturers recommend a snug fit. This is a description of how the device works, not health advice.
