## Fact check

- Rattan reeds contain many fine longitudinal channels (vascular tubes) that draw thin fragrance oil up by capillary action; fibre reeds work similarly.
- The oil evaporates from the exposed reed surface; diffuser oils are thin (often solvent-based) to wick well.
- Using more reeds increases evaporating area and scent strength; flipping reeds re-exposes saturated ends when tips clog.
