## Fact check

- Wetsuits are made of foamed neoprene (polychloroprene) containing closed gas cells (often nitrogen).
- The gas cells give low thermal conductivity, insulating the body.
- Wetsuits allow a thin layer of water in, which the body warms; a snug fit limits 'flushing' of cold water.
- Water pressure compresses the gas cells at depth, reducing thickness and insulation.
