## Fact check

- Cocoa butter in well-tempered chocolate (form V crystals) melts at about 33-34 °C, below body temperature (about 37 °C).
- Cocoa butter is polymorphic with six crystal forms (I-VI); form V gives gloss and snap.
- Tempering melts, cools and gently rewarms chocolate to favour form V crystals.
- Poorly tempered chocolate is dull, soft and crumbly and melts more easily.
