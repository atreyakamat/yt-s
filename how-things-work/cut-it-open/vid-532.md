## Fact check

- Line lasers pass a laser diode's beam through a cylindrical (rod) lens, which spreads it in one plane into a fan, projecting a line.
- Common self-levelling line lasers suspend the optics on a pendulum; eddy-current (magnetic) damping stops it swinging quickly.
- The self-levelling range is limited (typically a few degrees); beyond it the laser flashes or a warning shows.
