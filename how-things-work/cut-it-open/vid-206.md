## Fact check

- Dishwashers hold only a few litres in the sump at a time; modern efficient machines use roughly 10-15 litres for a whole cycle.
- The water is heated, pumped up through rotating spray arms, falls back, passes through a filter and is recirculated.
- Dishwasher detergents commonly contain enzymes (amylase for starch, protease for protein) and other agents for fats.
- Studies (e.g. University of Bonn) found hand washing typically uses much more water than an efficient full dishwasher, hence 'can use less'.
