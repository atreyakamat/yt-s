## Fact check

- Some phones (e.g., certain models from 2016) and many modern laptop trackpads use solid-state, non-moving buttons with force/pressure sensing.
- A linear vibration actuator produces a short haptic pulse that users perceive as a mechanical click.
- With the device powered off, these buttons give no click, demonstrating the effect.
- No moving switch improves durability and helps water resistance.
