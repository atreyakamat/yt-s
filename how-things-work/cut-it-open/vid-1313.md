## Fact check

- Tie-dye is a resist-dyeing technique: binding compresses fabric so dye can't penetrate those areas.
- Dye penetration falls off from outer to inner folds, giving gradients and white areas.
- Home tie-dye on cotton typically uses fibre-reactive dyes with soda ash (sodium carbonate) to form a covalent bond with cellulose.
- Twisting gives spirals, folding gives stripes, pinching gives rings.
- Related traditional methods include shibori (Japan) and bandhani (India), centuries old.
