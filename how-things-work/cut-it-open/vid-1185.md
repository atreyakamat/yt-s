## Fact check

- Pilling: abrasion works fibre ends loose; they entangle into balls that stay anchored by a few fibres.
- Fabric shavers use a battery motor driving rotating blades behind a perforated guard; pills protrude through the holes and are cut, while the flat fabric surface stays below.
- Pressing hard pushes fabric into the holes and can cut it, so makers advise light pressure.
