## Fact check

- Automatic monitors use the oscillometric method: the cuff inflates to occlude the artery, then deflates while a pressure sensor detects pulse-induced oscillations.
- Oscillation amplitude rises to a maximum (near mean arterial pressure) and falls; algorithms estimate systolic and diastolic from this envelope. Bars are illustrative.
- Systolic is pressure during the heartbeat; diastolic between beats.
- Blood pressure is given in mmHg because early sphygmomanometers used a mercury column. Educational only, not medical advice.
