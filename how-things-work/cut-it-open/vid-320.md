## Fact check

- Electronic electricity meters sample voltage and current waveforms thousands of times per second and multiply them to compute power, then integrate over time to get energy (kWh).
- UK smart meters record consumption in half-hour periods and send readings over a wireless network, removing manual reads and estimated bills.
- Typical values: a UK kettle is about 3 kW; a TV is around 100 W.
