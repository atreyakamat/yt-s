## Fact check

- Many office papers contain optical brightening agents that fluoresce blue under UV.
- Banknote substrates are made without brighteners, so they appear dull under UV, while UV-fluorescent inks and fibres are added as security features (e.g. euro notes).
- UV detector lamps are a common retail counterfeit check; counterfeits on ordinary paper often fluoresce.
- The gauge is illustrative.
