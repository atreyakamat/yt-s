## Fact check

- A turn-lock (twist lock) has a slotted plate on the flap and a rotating post on the bag body.
- The post head is an elongated bar that passes through the slot only when aligned; turning it 90 degrees blocks the flap.
- Turn locks hold position by friction or a small spring detent depending on the design (hence 'a tight fit or a small spring').
- 'Your bag' refers to bags that use this clasp type.
