## Fact check

- Smart speakers use microphone arrays (commonly 2-8 mics) and beamforming to locate and enhance the speaker's voice.
- Wake-word detection runs on-device on a short rolling audio buffer; audio is streamed to the cloud only after the wake word is detected (indicated by a light).
- Similar-sounding words can trigger false wakes; manufacturers acknowledge this. (Grid is an illustration, not a measured rate.)
