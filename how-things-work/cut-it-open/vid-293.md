## Fact check

- Down is the soft undercoat plumage of ducks and geese; clusters have no quill, unlike feathers.
- Down insulates by trapping still air in its lofted clusters.
- Baffles are sewn compartments that keep down evenly distributed.
- Fill power is the volume in cubic inches one ounce of down occupies; roughly 550 is basic, 700 good, 800+ premium. Higher fill power gives more warmth for the weight.
- Wet down clumps and loses most of its loft and insulation.
