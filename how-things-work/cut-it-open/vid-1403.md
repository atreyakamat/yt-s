## Fact check

- Contactless (NFC/RFID, 13.56 MHz) cards contain a chip and a coil antenna and have no battery; the reader's field powers them by induction at a few centimetres.
- RFID-blocking wallets use a conductive foil or mesh layer that shields the card from the field (Faraday cage principle).
- Cards must be removed to pay contactlessly.
- No claim is made about how likely skimming is.
