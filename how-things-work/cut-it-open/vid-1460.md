## Fact check

- Solar compactor bins use a lid-mounted solar panel and battery to power a fill-level sensor and a motor-driven compaction plate.
- Manufacturers claim around five times (some up to eight times) the capacity of a standard bin, so 'several times' and '~5x'.
- They report fill level wirelessly so collections are scheduled when needed; enclosed hoppers keep out birds and vermin.
