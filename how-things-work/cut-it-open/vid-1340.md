## Fact check

- Pneumatic road tubes are rubber tubes laid across the carriageway; a tyre crossing sends an air pressure pulse to a switch in a roadside counter that timestamps it.
- Pairs of hits from the same tube indicate axles; with two tubes at a known spacing, the time difference gives speed.
- Axle count and spacing are used to classify vehicles; the data informs road planning and speed limits.
