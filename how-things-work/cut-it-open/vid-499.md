## Fact check

- Airliners use vacuum toilets to save weight: each flush uses a small amount of water (often around 0.2 litre or less) versus several litres at home.
- The bowl connects via a flush valve and piping to a waste tank.
- Pressure difference is provided by a vacuum generator on the ground/low altitude and by the low outside air pressure at altitude.
- When the valve opens, the pressure difference pulls waste and air into the pipe; a rinse is sprayed and the valve closes.
