## Fact check

- Diesel engines use compression ignition: compression ratios of roughly 15-22:1 versus about 9-12:1 for petrol, heating air to several hundred °C so injected fuel self-ignites.
- Glow plugs heat the combustion chamber for cold starts; they are not spark plugs.
- Higher compression contributes to diesel efficiency; rapid combustion produces the characteristic knock/clatter.
