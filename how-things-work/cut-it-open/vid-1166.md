## Fact check

- Sous vide ('under vacuum') cooking seals food in plastic bags and cooks it in a temperature-controlled water bath.
- Immersion circulators contain a heater, a pump to circulate water and a temperature sensor with a controller; they typically hold temperature to within about 0.1-0.5 °C.
- Food cannot exceed the bath temperature, so it cooks evenly to the set doneness; a brief high-heat sear afterwards provides Maillard browning.
