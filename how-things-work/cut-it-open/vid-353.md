## Fact check

- Watch cases are sealed with gaskets (O-rings) at the caseback, crystal and crown.
- Pressure increases by about 1 bar (one atmosphere) per 10 m of water.
- Water resistance ratings are based on static pressure tests; makers and ISO 2281 guidance treat 30 m (3 bar) as splash/rain resistant only.
- Common maker guidance: 50 m light swimming, 100 m swimming and snorkelling, 200 m diving.
- Gaskets degrade with age, which is why services include pressure re-testing.
