## Fact check

- Inductive charging → electric toothbrush chargers use a primary coil in the base and a secondary coil in the handle; no exposed contacts.
- Alternating current creates a changing magnetic field that induces current in the second coil (Faraday's law).
- Same principle as transformers and Qi wireless phone charging.
- Sealed handle → induction lets the brush be watertight.
