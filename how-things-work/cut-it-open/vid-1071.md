## Fact check

- Remoistenable envelope gums are commonly dextrin (heat-treated starch, e.g. from corn or potato), sometimes blended with other water-soluble adhesives.
- The gum is applied wet and dried; moistening reactivates it, and it re-dries bonded to the paper fibres.
- Any water source reactivates it. Gauge is illustrative.
