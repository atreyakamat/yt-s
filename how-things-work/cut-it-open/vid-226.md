## Fact check

- Active noise cancellation uses microphones (feedforward outside, feedback inside the cup) and processing to generate an inverted-phase signal.
- Destructive interference: a wave and its inverted copy sum to near zero.
- ANC is most effective at low frequencies (roughly below 1 kHz) and steady noise; higher, sudden sounds are mostly reduced by passive isolation (cushions/tips).
