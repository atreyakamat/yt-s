## Fact check

- Capsaicin activates the TRPV1 receptor, which normally responds to painful heat (around 43 °C and above), producing a burning sensation without a real temperature rise.
- Capsaicin is fat-soluble and poorly soluble in water; milk (casein and fat) is commonly found more effective than water at reducing the burn.
- Birds' TRPV1 receptors are insensitive to capsaicin, so birds eat chillies and disperse the seeds.
- Pungency (Scoville rating) depends on capsaicinoid content. Bars are illustrative.
