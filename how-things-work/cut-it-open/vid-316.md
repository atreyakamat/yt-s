## Fact check

- Pedometers in phones and wearables use MEMS accelerometers measuring three axes.
- Step detection algorithms look for periodic peaks in acceleration magnitude in a typical walking cadence range (roughly 1-2.5 steps per second) and use regularity checks to reject random motion.
- Repetitive arm movements can produce false steps on wrist-worn trackers; this is a well-known limitation.
