## Fact check

- Frost-free (auto-defrost) freezers use a fan and hidden evaporator coils; frost forms on the coils from moisture in air that enters when the door opens.
- Frost insulates the coils and reduces cooling efficiency.
- A defrost timer or control board periodically runs an electric defrost heater (typically one to a few times a day) for a short period while the fan is off.
- Melt water drains through a tube to a drip pan near the compressor/condenser, where warmth evaporates it.
