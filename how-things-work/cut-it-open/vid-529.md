## Fact check

- At about 35,000-40,000 ft, the time of useful consciousness without oxygen is roughly 30 seconds to a minute or two.
- Most airliners pressurise with engine bleed air; the Boeing 787 uses electric compressors.
- Cabin pressure is controlled by outflow valves (usually near the rear) that regulate how much air leaves.
- Cabin altitude is typically held at or below 8,000 ft (about 2,400 m); cruise altitude is around 11,000 m.
- Pressure changes during climb and descent cause ears to pop and sealed bottles to swell or crumple.
