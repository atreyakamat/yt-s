## Fact check

- Leaks in pressurised pipes generate noise that travels along the pipe wall and water.
- Acoustic loggers attached to valves and hydrants record at night when background noise is lowest.
- Leak noise correlators use two sensors: the difference in arrival time, with known pipe length and sound speed, locates the leak, often to within about a metre.
- Ground microphones and listening sticks confirm the spot before excavation.
