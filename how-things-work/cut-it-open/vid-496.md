## Fact check

- Phone gyroscopes are MEMS vibratory gyroscopes: a proof mass is driven to oscillate (typically at kilohertz frequencies), and rotation produces a Coriolis force perpendicular to the drive motion.
- Capacitive comb electrodes sense the Coriolis displacement, which is proportional to angular rate.
- Three-axis gyros measure pitch, roll and yaw rates, used in games, image stabilisation and navigation.
