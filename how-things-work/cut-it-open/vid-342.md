## Fact check

- Press-to-close (zipper) bags use an interlocking profile: a male rib with an enlarged head and a female channel that flexes to capture it.
- Forces from inside the bag tend to tighten the interlock; the outer flanges are angled so pulling them apart opens it more easily.
- Some brands colour the two tracks (e.g. yellow and blue) so they appear green when closed.
