## Fact check

- Door letter plates have a top-hinged flap closed by a spring.
- An open slot lets cold air through; letterbox draught excluders use two rows of brush bristles meeting in the middle, often with an inner flap.
- Post passes between the brushes, which then close to reduce draughts.
