## Fact check

- Emergency (space) blankets are thin PET film coated with vapour-deposited aluminium and typically weigh around 50-60 g.
- Shiny aluminium reflects most thermal infrared radiation; the blanket also reduces convective (wind) and evaporative losses. Bars are illustrative of heat-loss routes, not measured shares.
- Metallised film was developed for NASA spacecraft insulation in the 1960s.
