## Fact check

- Puffin (Pedestrian User-Friendly Intelligent) crossings use kerbside detectors to confirm a pedestrian is still waiting (cancelling the demand if they leave) and on-crossing detectors to extend the all-red to traffic while pedestrians are crossing.
- This gives slower pedestrians more time and avoids unnecessary delay to traffic.
- The red/green figures are shown in a nearside unit at eye level, so pedestrians look toward oncoming traffic.
