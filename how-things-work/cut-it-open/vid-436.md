## Fact check

- Most analog thumbsticks use two potentiometers mounted at 90 degrees to measure X and Y deflection; a spring recentres the stick.
- A potentiometer is a wiper sliding on a resistive (often carbon) track, the same principle as a volume knob.
- Wear and debris on the resistive track are a common cause of 'stick drift'.
- Hall-effect thumbsticks sense a magnet's position without contact, avoiding that wear.
