## Fact check

- Many mouse wheels use an optical encoder: a slotted disc interrupts a light beam to a sensor (others use mechanical encoders).
- Two offset sensors (quadrature) give direction from which signal changes first.
- Detents ('notches') come from a spring pressing on a toothed ring.
- Pressing the wheel acts as the middle button; middle-clicking a link opens it in a new tab in common browsers.
