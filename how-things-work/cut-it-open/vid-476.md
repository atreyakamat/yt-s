## Fact check

- Most household ovens use on/off (bang-bang) control: the element runs at full power until the sensed temperature passes the set point, then switches off until it falls below.
- This produces a temperature swing around the set point; swings of 10 °C or more are commonly measured in home ovens.
- Electronic ovens use a resistance temperature probe (RTD/thermistor); many mechanical thermostats use a liquid-filled capillary bulb whose expansion operates a switch.
