## Fact check

- Water's density falls from about 999.7 kg/m3 at 10 °C to about 971.8 kg/m3 at 80 °C, roughly 3% expansion: 'a few percent'.
- Sealed (pressurised) heating systems use an expansion vessel: a steel tank divided by a rubber diaphragm, with pressurised air/nitrogen on one side and system water on the other.
- Heating expansion vessels are commonly red (potable water ones are often blue or white).
- Without expansion space, pressure rises until the pressure relief valve opens.
