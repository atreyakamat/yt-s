## Fact check

- Water-resistant phones use adhesive gaskets bonding the display and back to the frame, rubber seals on buttons/SIM tray, and hydrophobic acoustic meshes/membranes over speaker and mic openings.
- IP68 (IEC 60529) means dust-tight and tested for continuous immersion beyond 1 m under manufacturer-specified conditions, typically still fresh water.
- Phone makers state water resistance is not permanent and may decrease as a result of normal wear; liquid damage is usually not covered by warranty.
