## Fact check

- Oxygen bleach powders typically contain sodium percarbonate, an adduct of sodium carbonate and hydrogen peroxide; in water it releases hydrogen peroxide.
- Hydrogen peroxide decomposes, releasing oxygen (visible fizzing), and oxidises chromophores (light-absorbing conjugated bonds) in stains, decolourising them.
- Oxygen bleach is generally colour-safe compared with chlorine bleach, though labels should be checked.
