## Fact check

- Pedal bins use a pivoting lever: pressing the pedal raises the other end, which pushes a rod (or rotates a linkage) that opens the hinged lid.
- The geometry turns a short pedal stroke into a large lid movement.
- Soft-close lids use a damper that forces air or oil through a small orifice to slow closing.
