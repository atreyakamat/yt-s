## Fact check

- Hot air balloons use propane burners to heat air inside a nylon (or polyester) envelope.
- Warmer air is less dense than cooler air, creating buoyant lift.
- Envelope air is typically heated to around 100 °C (maximum often around 120 °C).
- A parachute vent at the top releases hot air to descend.
- Balloons cannot steer horizontally; pilots change altitude to use winds blowing in different directions (the layers are an example).
