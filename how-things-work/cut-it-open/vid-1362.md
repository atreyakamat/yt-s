## Fact check

- Gelatine is a protein derived from collagen (from animal skin, bones and connective tissue).
- In hot liquid, gelatine strands are dissolved; on cooling they partly re-form helices and tangle into a gel network that traps the syrup.
- Gummies are commonly deposited into moulds made in trays of starch powder ('starch mogul' process) and then dried/stoved, often for a day or more.
- Vegetarian gummies use pectin, starch or agar instead.
