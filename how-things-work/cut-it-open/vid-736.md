## Fact check

- Bluetooth trackers contain a coin cell, a Bluetooth Low Energy radio and usually a buzzer/speaker; they have no GPS.
- They broadcast BLE advertisements periodically with rotating identifiers for privacy.
- Crowd-sourced finding networks have nearby participating phones upload the tag's location end-to-end encrypted, readable only by the owner.
- Typical coin-cell battery life is about a year.
