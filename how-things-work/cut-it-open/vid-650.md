## Fact check

- Accessible pedestrian signals (US MUTCD) use a locator tone repeating about once a second to help find the push button, and a distinct walk indication (rapid tick or tone, or a speech message, which may name the street).
- They include a tactile raised arrow on the button that points in the crossing direction and vibrates during the walk interval.
- APS volume automatically adjusts to ambient noise via a microphone.
