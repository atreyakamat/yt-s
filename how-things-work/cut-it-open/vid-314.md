## Fact check

- Electrical power = voltage x current (for AC, using instantaneous values, which is what sampling meters do).
- Electronic/smart meters sample voltage and current waveforms at kilohertz rates and integrate power over time to get energy in kWh.
- 1 kWh = 1 kW for 1 hour.
- Older electromechanical (Ferraris) meters used a rotating aluminium disc driven by eddy currents, its speed proportional to power.
