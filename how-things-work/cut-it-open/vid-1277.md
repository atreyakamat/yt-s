## Fact check

- Without a VPN, ISPs can see destination IP addresses and often domain names of traffic.
- A VPN encrypts traffic between the device and the VPN server; the ISP sees only encrypted traffic to that server.
- Destination websites see the VPN server's IP address.
- The VPN provider can observe the traffic metadata the ISP previously could, so trust shifts to it.
- Accounts, cookies and browser fingerprinting can still identify users.
