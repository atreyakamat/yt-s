## Fact check

- Antivirus uses signature detection (hashes/byte patterns of known malware), with databases covering millions of samples.
- Independent labs such as AV-TEST register several hundred thousand new malware samples per day (over 400,000 in recent years).
- Behavioural/heuristic detection flags suspicious actions such as mass file encryption (ransomware).
- Detected files are quarantined; signature updates are frequent, often multiple times daily.
