## Fact check

- A standard 10-hole diatonic harmonica has two reed plates with 10 reeds each (20 total), mounted either side of a comb, under covers.
- Reeds are thin strips, usually brass, riveted at one end over a matching slot.
- Airflow makes a reed vibrate through its slot; the vibration frequency (hundreds of Hz) sets the note.
- Blow reeds sit on the top plate and draw reeds on the bottom, so each hole gives a blow note and a draw note.
- Longer, heavier reeds vibrate at lower frequencies.
