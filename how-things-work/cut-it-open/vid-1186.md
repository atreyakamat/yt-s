## Fact check

- In low light, photon shot noise dominates: few photons per pixel produce grainy images.
- Night modes capture multiple frames (often with varying exposures) over roughly one to several seconds.
- Frames are aligned (registered) to compensate for hand motion, then merged; averaging N frames reduces random noise by about the square root of N while keeping static detail.
- Tone mapping then lifts shadows and adjusts colour.
