## Fact check

- Phones use a fuel gauge IC that combines coulomb counting (integrating current through a sense resistor) with voltage-based models to estimate state of charge.
- Coulomb counting drifts over time, so voltage readings at rest are used to correct it.
- Load and low temperature cause voltage sag, which can lead to sudden percentage drops or unexpected shutdowns, especially in aged batteries. (Bars show an example of a sudden drop, not measured data.)
- Gauges track capacity fade, so 100% on an old battery holds less energy.
