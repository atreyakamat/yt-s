## Fact check

- Fluorescent pigments absorb shorter wavelengths (UV and blue) and re-emit longer visible wavelengths, so they can return more visible light than ordinary colours. Bars are illustrative.
- Human daytime (photopic) vision is most sensitive around 555 nm, yellow-green.
- Fluorescence works in daylight; at night, high-visibility clothing relies on retroreflective tape.
