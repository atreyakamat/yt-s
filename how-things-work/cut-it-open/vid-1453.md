## Fact check

- Sponges are animals (phylum Porifera) → they lack nerves, muscles and organs such as a brain or heart.
- Filter feeders → they pump water through pores and channels, capturing food particles.
- Bath sponges → the spongin fibre skeleton of species such as Spongia, cleaned and dried.
- Synthetic sponges → usually polyurethane or cellulose foam.
- Loofah → the dried fibrous fruit of Luffa plants, in the gourd family (Cucurbitaceae) with cucumbers.
