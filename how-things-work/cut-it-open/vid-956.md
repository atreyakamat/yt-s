## Fact check

- Dishwasher tablets typically contain alkaline builders (e.g. carbonates, citrates), enzymes (protease, amylase), oxygen bleach (percarbonate) with activators, and surfactants, often in separate layers or compartments.
- Builders bind hardness minerals (softening water) and raise alkalinity to loosen fats.
- Amylase breaks down starch and protease breaks down proteins such as egg.
- Many tablets have water-soluble PVA film wrappers.
- Detergent enzymes are designed to work best in warm water (roughly 40-60 °C).
