## Fact check

- Soft contact lenses are made from hydrogels or silicone hydrogels, cross-linked polymers that absorb water; water content varies by material, which is why the hook says 'partly'.
- The cornea is avascular and gets most of its oxygen from the air via the tear film.
- Conventional hydrogels transmit oxygen mainly through their water; silicone hydrogels transmit much more oxygen through the silicone.
- Soft lens centre thickness is typically around 0.05-0.15 mm, so 'around a tenth of a millimetre' is fair.
- Soft lenses sit on the tear film and move slightly with each blink.
