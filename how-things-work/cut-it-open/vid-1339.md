## Fact check

- Wire spokes are slender and buckle under compression, so wheels are built with all spokes pre-tensioned.
- Spoke tension is adjusted at the nipples where spokes meet the rim.
- Under load, spokes at the bottom lose some tension while others change little, so the hub is effectively supported by the remaining tension ('hangs'). The gauge is qualitative.
- Uneven tension after a broken or loose spoke makes the rim go out of true (wobble).
