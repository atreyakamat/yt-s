## Fact check

- Electrophoretic (E Ink) displays use microcapsules (or microcups) roughly the diameter of a human hair containing positively and negatively charged white and black pigment particles in a clear fluid.
- An electric field moves particles to the top or bottom; the image is bistable and persists with no power.
- Power is used only when the image changes, and the display reflects ambient light rather than emitting it, so e-readers typically last weeks per charge.
