## Fact check

- Water conducts → wet skin lowers resistance, raising shock risk.
- Pull-cord switches → the switch body is mounted on the ceiling, operated by an insulating cord.
- Ratchet → each pull rotates a cam/ratchet mechanism that alternately makes and breaks the contacts.
- UK zones → BS 7671 restricts switches in bathroom zones; ordinary wall switches must be outside zone 2 (or used via pull-cord).
