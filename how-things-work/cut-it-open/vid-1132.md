## Fact check

- Common digital calipers use a capacitive linear encoder: a patterned electrode strip (scale) along the beam and electrodes on the slider; the electronics measure capacitive coupling to determine position.
- They resolve coarse position by counting pattern periods and fine position by phase within a period, displaying 0.01 mm resolution.
- The zero button sets the reference anywhere (for comparisons); calipers measure outside, inside and depth with one slider.
