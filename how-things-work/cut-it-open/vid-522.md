## Fact check

- Microbes need available water to grow (water activity); salt lowers water activity.
- Salt draws water out of food (and microbial cells) by osmosis.
- Salt-curing of fish and meat is ancient and was a major preservation method for millennia (e.g. salt cod, ham).
- Salt was historically valuable largely because of its use in preserving food.
