## Fact check

- Escalator steps form a continuous loop driven by step chains from a motor and drive gear, usually at the top.
- Each step has two step-chain (front) wheels and two trailer (rear) wheels running on separate tracks.
- On the incline the tracks are offset so steps form stairs; near the landings they converge so steps flatten.
- The handrail is a rubber belt driven by the same drive system to move at about step speed.
