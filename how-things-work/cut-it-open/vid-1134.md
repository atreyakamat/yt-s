## Fact check

- Squeaky toys are hollow, flexible rubber or vinyl bodies with a small squeaker (a reed whistle) fitted in a hole.
- Squeezing forces air out through the squeaker; the reed vibrates rapidly, producing the squeak.
- When released, the elastic body springs back and draws air in again.
- Many squeakers are designed to sound mainly in one direction, so release is quieter.
