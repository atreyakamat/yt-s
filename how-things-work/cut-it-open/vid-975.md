## Fact check

- Steam mops have a water tank, a small pump (or gravity feed) and a heating element/block that vaporises water into steam delivered through a pad.
- Heat and moisture soften and loosen grime, so plain water is used; microfibre pads collect the loosened dirt.
- Makers warn against steam on unsealed wood and some laminates, as moisture and heat can cause swelling or damage.
