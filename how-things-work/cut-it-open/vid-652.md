## Fact check

- Bolt cutters use a compound lever (toggle) linkage: the handles act on a second pair of levers that drive the short jaws, so the mechanical advantages multiply.
- The 5 x 5 = 25 figures are an illustrative example of how two stages multiply; real tools vary.
- Work in = work out: the large handle movement becomes a small jaw movement.
- Large bolt cutters come in lengths up to about 36-42 inches (roughly 0.9-1.07 m).
