## Fact check

- Hop-derived iso-alpha acids break down under blue/UV light (with riboflavin as a photosensitiser), forming 3-methyl-2-butene-1-thiol, a compound very similar to skunk thiols. This is called 'light-struck' beer.
- In direct sunlight, light-struck flavour can develop within minutes.
- Brown glass blocks most light in the damaging wavelengths; green and clear glass block much less. Bars are approximate illustrations of relative protection, not exact measurements.
- Cans are opaque.
