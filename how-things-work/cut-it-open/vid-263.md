## Fact check

- Photochromic lenses contain molecules (organic dyes such as naphthopyrans in plastic lenses, silver halide in glass) that change structure under UV and absorb visible light.
- Without UV they revert thermally, so lenses clear indoors (fading takes longer than darkening).
- Windscreen glass blocks most UV, so standard photochromic lenses barely darken in cars: widely noted by lens makers.
- The reverse reaction is faster when warm, so they darken less on hot days and more in the cold.
