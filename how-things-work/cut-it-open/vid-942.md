## Fact check

- Enzymatic browning: polyphenol oxidase (PPO) and phenolic compounds are separated in intact cells; cutting mixes them with oxygen.
- PPO oxidises phenols to quinones, which polymerise into brown melanin pigments (melanins are also the pigments in human skin and freckles).
- Browning is thought to help defend damaged plant tissue against microbes. It isn't rotting.
- Lemon juice slows browning: low pH inhibits PPO and ascorbic acid (vitamin C) reverses quinone formation.
