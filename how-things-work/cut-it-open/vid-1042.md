## Fact check

- Many metric measuring wheels have a 1 m circumference; diameter = 1 / pi = about 0.318 m (31.8 cm). (Imperial models use other sizes, e.g. 1 ft or 4 ft.)
- A mechanical counter is advanced by the axle each revolution, so distance = turns x circumference.
- Rough ground makes the wheel travel a longer path than the straight-line distance, so readings run long.
