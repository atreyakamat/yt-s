## Fact check

- Phone proximity sensors pair an infrared emitter (LED or VCSEL) with a photodetector near the earpiece.
- Reflected IR intensity rises as an object approaches; above a threshold the phone turns off the display and disables touch during calls.
- This prevents accidental face touches and saves display power.
