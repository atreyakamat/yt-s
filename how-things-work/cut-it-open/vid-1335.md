## Fact check

- Synthetic (e.g. polypropylene/polyester) dusters and ostrich feathers build triboelectric charge when rubbed or shaken, attracting light dust particles.
- Fine, branched fibres create many spaces that physically trap dust.
- Lambswool dusters contain natural lanolin, which helps dust cling, plus static attraction.
