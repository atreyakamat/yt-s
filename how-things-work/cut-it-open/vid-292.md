## Fact check

- Most handheld stud finders are capacitive: sensor plates form a capacitor with the wall, and denser material (a stud) behind the board changes the measured capacitance.
- They calibrate to the wall when switched on, so they must be started over a hollow section; starting over a stud makes them miss it (standard manufacturer instruction).
- Studs are spaced at regular intervals (common standards are 400 mm or 600 mm, or 16 inches), so the script avoids giving one number.
