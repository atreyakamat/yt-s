## Fact check

- Laser printers use a photoconductive drum that is uniformly charged; a laser discharges areas to form an electrostatic image.
- Toner is a fine powder of plastic (polymer) particles with pigment, attracted to the image areas and transferred to paper by charge.
- A fuser unit melts the toner into the paper; fuser temperatures are roughly 180-220 °C.
- Pages leave warm because of the fuser.
