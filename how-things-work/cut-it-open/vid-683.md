## Fact check

- Outdoor jackets use a durable water repellent (DWR) finish on the face fabric.
- DWR lowers surface energy so water's cohesion beats adhesion, forming beads with a high contact angle that roll off.
- When DWR wears or is contaminated, the face fabric 'wets out', reducing breathability and feeling clammy.
- Makers advise washing with technical cleaner and, where the label allows, low tumble-dry heat to reactivate DWR, or re-proofing sprays.
