## Fact check

- Capacitive touchscreens detect a conductive object (your finger) changing the local electric field/capacitance.
- Ordinary glove fabrics are insulating and block this.
- Touchscreen gloves use conductive yarns (commonly silver-coated nylon, or copper/carbon-loaded fibres) in the fingertips.
- The yarn connects the skin to the screen surface, so the screen registers a touch.
