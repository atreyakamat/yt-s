## Fact check

- 'Modem' = modulator-demodulator.
- Broadband arrives over phone copper (DSL), coax (cable, DOCSIS) or fibre (with an optical network terminal).
- DSL uses frequencies above the voice band, so phone and internet share the line.
- DSL uses discrete multitone modulation: hundreds to thousands of subcarriers, each carrying a few bits.
- VDSL speeds fall with line length; bar values are typical examples, not guarantees.
- Many home units combine modem and router.
