## Fact check

- Disposable surgical masks typically have three polypropylene layers: spunbond outer and inner layers and a meltblown middle filter layer.
- Meltblown fibres are a few micrometres or less across, much thinner than a human hair (about 50-100 µm).
- Meltblown filter media are commonly electrostatically charged (electret), which captures particles more efficiently without increasing breathing resistance much. Bars are illustrative.
- Moisture and wear can reduce electrostatic charge.
