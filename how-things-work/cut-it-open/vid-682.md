## Fact check

- One-part (RTV) silicone sealants cure by reacting with atmospheric moisture; the common acetoxy type releases acetic acid, giving the vinegar smell (neutral-cure types release other compounds).
- Curing proceeds from the surface inward: a skin forms in minutes, and full cure progresses roughly 2-3 mm per 24 hours.
- Cured silicone is an elastic rubber that accommodates joint movement.
- Cured silicone has very low surface energy, so paint does not adhere to it.
