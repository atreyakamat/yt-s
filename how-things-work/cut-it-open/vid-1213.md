## Fact check

- Shaving foam aerosols → soap/surfactant cream with dissolved liquefied hydrocarbon propellant (e.g. butane, isobutane, propane).
- Foaming on release → the propellant vaporises at atmospheric pressure, expanding into bubbles.
- Post-foaming gels → use isopentane/isobutane blends; isopentane boils around 28°C, so the gel foams when warmed and rubbed on skin.
