## Fact check

- PTFE (polytetrafluoroethylene) has one of the lowest coefficients of friction of any solid; it is the classic non-stick coating.
- Structure: a carbon backbone fully surrounded by fluorine atoms; the strong, tightly held C-F bonds give it very low surface energy, so little sticks to it.
- Application: pans are grit-blasted or etched, coated with a primer and top coats, then baked (sintered) so the coating keys mechanically into the rough surface.
