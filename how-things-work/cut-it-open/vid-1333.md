## Fact check

- Fine-mist pump → perfume atomisers are crimped piston pumps with a dip tube, spring and check valve.
- Swirl chamber → the actuator insert gives the liquid a rotational component before the orifice, forming a hollow cone that breaks into fine droplets.
- Refill → the spring returns the piston and the inlet valve lets liquid in.
- Trigger sprayers → use a similar pump-and-nozzle principle.
