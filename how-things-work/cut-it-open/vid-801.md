## Fact check

- Bimetallic coins are made from an outer ring and a centre pill of different alloys, placed together and struck simultaneously; the strike expands the centre to lock it, often aided by a groove.
- The €1 and €2 coins, the UK £2 and the 12-sided £1 (2017) are bimetallic.
- Two-alloy construction is harder to counterfeit and gives a distinctive signature for coin validators. Grid is illustrative.
