## Fact check

- Warm water is less dense than cold, so storage cylinders stratify with the hottest water at the top.
- Hot water is drawn from the top of the cylinder; cold feed enters at the bottom (often through a diffuser) to avoid mixing.
- Indirect cylinders have a copper coil heated by the boiler's primary water, which doesn't mix with tap water; most also have an immersion heater as backup.
- Modern cylinders are insulated with factory-applied foam.
