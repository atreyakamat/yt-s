## Fact check

- Telephoto lenses need long focal lengths; a 5x phone telephoto typically needs a focal length/track length in the range of 15-20+ mm, longer than phone thickness (~7-9 mm). Values are rounded ('about', 'roughly').
- Periscope (folded) cameras use a prism or mirror to redirect light 90 degrees so the lens elements lie along the phone's length.
- This enables 5x and higher optical zoom in thin phones.
