## Fact check

- Hard hats consist of a rigid shell and an internal suspension (harness) that keeps a clearance gap between the shell and the head.
- The shell distributes impact over a larger area; the suspension and shell deflection extend the stopping time, reducing peak force (impulse = force × time).
- Bar values are illustrative, not test data.
