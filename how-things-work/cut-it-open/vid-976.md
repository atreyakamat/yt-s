## Fact check

- Bone conduction headphones use transducers (magnet + coil) that vibrate pads resting on the cheekbones/temporal bone.
- Vibrations are conducted through the skull to the cochlea, bypassing the eardrum and middle ear.
- Leaving the ear canal open preserves awareness of surroundings.
- You hear your own voice partly by bone conduction, which emphasises lower frequencies, which is why recordings of your voice sound higher/different.
