## Fact check

- Push-pull sports caps have a fixed central pin/post and a sliding spout with an orifice.
- When pushed down, the post seals the spout orifice; when pulled up, the orifice clears the post and liquid flows around it.
- The design allows one-handed (or teeth) operation without removing a cap.
