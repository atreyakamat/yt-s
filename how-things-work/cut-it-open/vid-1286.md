## Fact check

- Boiling water taps are fed from an insulated tank (typically a few litres) under the sink, often with a filter.
- Some systems (pressurised tanks) store water at around 105-110 °C; because it is under pressure it doesn't boil until released, when it flashes to boiling and sputters steam. Others keep water just below 100 °C, hence 'in some systems'.
- Heavy insulation keeps standby energy use low.
- Most have child-safety handles requiring push-and-turn actions.
