## Fact check

- Golden Gate Bridge: each of the two main cables contains 27,572 galvanised steel wires about 0.192 in (about 5 mm) thick (bridge district figures).
- Total length of wire in both main cables is about 80,000 miles (about 129,000 km); Earth's equator is about 40,075 km, so about three times round.
- The cables were made by aerial spinning: spinning wheels carried wire back and forth across the span, then the wires were compacted into a round cable and wrapped.
- Main cables are anchored in massive concrete anchorages at each end.
