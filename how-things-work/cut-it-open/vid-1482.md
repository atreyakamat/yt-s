## Fact check

- Air contains water vapour; the maximum amount it can hold rises with temperature (bars are a relative illustration).
- A cold glass cools adjacent air below its dew point, so vapour condenses into droplets on the surface.
- Dew and cloud formation are the same process.
- Higher humidity means more vapour, so more condensation forms.
