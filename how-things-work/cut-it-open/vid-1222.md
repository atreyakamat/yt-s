## Fact check

- Reciprocating air compressors use a motor-driven piston with inlet and outlet (reed/check) valves to pump air into a receiver tank.
- A pressure switch stops the motor at the cut-out pressure and restarts it at a lower cut-in pressure; common DIY units cut out around 8 bar (about 115-120 psi).
- Compressing air raises its temperature, heating the pump head.
- Moisture in the compressed air condenses in the tank as it cools; manufacturers advise draining it regularly.
