## Fact check

- CPUs have a few to a few dozen complex cores; GPUs have thousands of simpler shader cores designed for parallel work.
- Rendering involves similar calculations per pixel/vertex, which suits parallel processing.
- 1920 × 1080 × 60 = 124,416,000 pixels per second.
- Graphics cards have dedicated VRAM and large coolers.
- Core counts in the bars are typical examples (8-core CPU, ~5,000-core GPU).
- Neural network training relies on massively parallel matrix maths, which is why GPUs are used for AI.
