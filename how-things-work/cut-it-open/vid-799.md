## Fact check

- TCAS (Traffic alert and Collision Avoidance System) interrogates nearby aircraft transponders, which reply with altitude; range is from reply timing.
- It issues traffic advisories ('Traffic, traffic') and resolution advisories.
- When both aircraft have TCAS II, they coordinate complementary manoeuvres (one climbs, one descends).
- After the 2002 Überlingen collision, guidance is clear that pilots must follow a resolution advisory even against an ATC instruction.
