## Fact check

- Wastewater treatment: preliminary screening and grit removal, primary settlement (sludge), then secondary treatment where aerated tanks let bacteria consume dissolved organic matter (activated sludge).
- The bacterial flocs settle in final tanks; part is returned to the aeration tanks as 'return activated sludge'.
- Treated effluent is discharged to rivers or the sea; sludge is often anaerobically digested to produce biogas used to generate electricity on site.
