## Fact check

- Vapour-compression cycle: compressor, condenser (at the front of the car), expansion valve/orifice, evaporator (behind the dashboard).
- R-134a boils at about -26 °C at atmospheric pressure (R-1234yf at about -29 °C), so 'about -26 °C' and 'far below freezing'.
- Compression raises gas temperature; the condenser rejects heat; the pressure drop at the expansion device chills the refrigerant; it evaporates in the evaporator, absorbing cabin heat.
- Water vapour condenses on the cold evaporator and drains under the car, which is normal.
