## Fact check

- Most motorcycles use a multi-plate clutch: friction plates keyed to the clutch basket (engine side) alternate with steel plates keyed to the inner hub (gearbox side).
- Coil or diaphragm springs clamp the plate stack via a pressure plate.
- The lever (cable or hydraulic) lifts the pressure plate against the springs, letting the plates slip.
- Most motorcycle clutches are 'wet', running in engine/gearbox oil for cooling and smooth engagement.
