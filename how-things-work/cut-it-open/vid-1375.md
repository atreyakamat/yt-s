## Fact check

- Liquid crystal thermometers use thermochromic (cholesteric/chiral nematic) liquid crystals arranged in a helical structure.
- The helix selectively reflects light of a wavelength set by its pitch; temperature changes the pitch, shifting the reflected colour (typically red at the cooler end to blue at the warmer end of the range).
- Each segment uses a formulation calibrated to a different temperature; a black backing absorbs other light to improve contrast. Bars and grid are illustrative.
