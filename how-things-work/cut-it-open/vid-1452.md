## Fact check

- Ice cream is frozen while being agitated, incorporating air; the volume increase is called overrun.
- Overrun ranges from about 20-30% in dense premium ice creams to around 100% in some economy products (100% overrun means the product is half air by volume). Bars show approximate air share by volume.
- Partially coalesced fat globules surround and stabilise air bubbles.
- Agitation during freezing keeps ice crystals small, giving a smooth texture.
- Ice cream is commonly sold by volume, so weight shows how much product (versus air) you get.
