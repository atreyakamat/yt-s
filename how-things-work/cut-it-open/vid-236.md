## Fact check

- Typical basic drip coffee makers have no pump: water flows by gravity into a heated (often aluminium) tube, boils, and the steam bubbles push slugs of water up a riser tube (a bubble/geyser pump, like a percolator).
- A one-way check valve between the tank and the heating tube stops water flowing back.
- Water exits over a shower head onto the grounds and drips through the filter by gravity. The gurgling sound is the boiling bubbles.
- Espresso and pod machines are different: they use electric pumps.
