## Fact check

- Common hand-held can openers have a sharp circular cutting wheel above the lid and a serrated (toothed) feed wheel below the rim; turning the key rotates the feed wheel, which drags the can past the cutter.
- The cutting wheel presses and rolls rather than sawing, shearing the thin steel as it goes.
- Side-cutting ('safety') openers cut through the side of the rim seam, so the lid comes off with smooth edges.
