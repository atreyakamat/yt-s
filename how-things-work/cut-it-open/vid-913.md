## Fact check

- Foaming soap is diluted → foaming dispensers use a low-viscosity, diluted soap.
- Two chambers → foam pumps have a liquid piston and a larger air piston that inject both into a mixing chamber.
- Mesh screens → fine nylon meshes in the nozzle shear the mix into uniform foam.
- Less soap per wash → the foam is mostly air.
