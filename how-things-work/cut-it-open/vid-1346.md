## Fact check

- A knife edge is the apex where two bevels meet; dulling rounds, rolls or chips the apex.
- Pull-through sharpeners hold abrasive rods, wheels or carbide blades crossed at a preset angle, so both bevels are ground at once.
- Two-stage models have a coarse stage to reshape and a fine (often ceramic) stage to refine/polish.
