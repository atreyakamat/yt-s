## Fact check

- Indigo is insoluble in water; dyers reduce it to leuco-indigo, which is soluble and yellow-green.
- On exposure to air the leuco form oxidises back to blue indigo within minutes: the classic green-to-blue change.
- Denim warp yarn is dipped repeatedly to build colour; indigo penetrates only the outer layer ('ring dyeing'), leaving a white core.
- Abrasion removes the outer dyed layer, which is why jeans fade where they rub.
