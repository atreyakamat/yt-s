## Fact check

- Contactless travel cards are passive NFC/RFID cards: an antenna coil picks up energy from the reader's 13.56 MHz field to power the chip, so there's no battery.
- The card-reader transaction takes a fraction of a second.
- Magnetic-stripe paper tickets are transported by rollers past read/write heads in the gate and returned.
- Gates use arrays of infrared sensors to track people passing, prevent the paddles closing on a passenger, and flag tailgating.
