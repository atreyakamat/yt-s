## Fact check

- Zip normally uses DEFLATE: LZ77 replaces repeated strings with (distance, length) back-references, then Huffman coding gives frequent symbols shorter codes.
- It is lossless: decompression restores the exact original bytes.
- English text often compresses to roughly a quarter to a third of its size; the gauge is illustrative.
- JPEG photos and compressed video are already compressed, so zipping gains little.
- Bit lengths in the bars are illustrative of Huffman coding, not exact.
