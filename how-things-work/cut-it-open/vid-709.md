## Fact check

- Augustin-Jean Fresnel developed the stepped (Fresnel) lens; the first was installed in the Cordouan lighthouse, France, in 1823.
- A Fresnel lens keeps the refracting curved surfaces of a large lens as concentric rings, greatly reducing thickness and weight.
- Concentric prisms collimate light from a central lamp into a beam; lighthouses have distinctive flash patterns (characteristics) to identify them.
- Fresnel lens principles are used in vehicle lamps and other lights.
