## Fact check

- 'Passively safe' or breakaway supports for signs and lighting are used on many high-speed roads so vehicles aren't brought to an abrupt stop.
- Slip-base designs use plates clamped by bolts in slotted holes that slide apart on impact; other columns are designed to deform and absorb energy.
- Lower deceleration reduces injury to occupants.
