## Fact check

- Rising butt hinges have helical (spiral) bearing faces on the knuckle; opening the door lifts it.
- The door's weight makes it tend to swing back closed (a gentle self-closing action).
- The lift helps the door clear carpets; the top of the door edge is often eased to allow the movement.
