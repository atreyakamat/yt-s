## Fact check

- Sebum → the scalp's oil, which makes hair look greasy as it spreads along strands.
- Ingredients → dry shampoos use absorbent powders such as rice/corn/tapioca starch, clays or silica, usually in an aerosol.
- Absorption, not removal → the powder holds oil until the hair is washed or brushed.
- Powder on grease stains → talc or starch is a common laundry trick for absorbing grease.
