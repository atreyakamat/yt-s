## Fact check

- Cruise-optimised wings produce limited lift at low speeds.
- Trailing-edge flaps and leading-edge slats extend for take-off and landing.
- They increase wing area and camber, raising maximum lift so the aircraft can fly slower.
- Lower approach speeds shorten landing distance; flaps also add drag, allowing a steeper descent.
