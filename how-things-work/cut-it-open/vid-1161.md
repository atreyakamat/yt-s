## Fact check

- Carbonless copy paper (NCR) uses a 'CB' (coated back) sheet with microencapsulated colourless (leuco) dye and a 'CF' (coated front) sheet with an acidic developer (e.g. clay or resin).
- Pressure ruptures the microcapsules; the dye reacts with the developer to form a coloured mark.
- Middle sheets are 'CFB', coated on both sides, allowing multi-part forms.
