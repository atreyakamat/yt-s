## Fact check

- Flexible fridge magnets are ferrite powder in a rubber/plastic binder.
- They are magnetised with a pattern of alternating poles in stripes; this arrangement (related to a Halbach array) concentrates the field on one face, so the other face holds weakly.
- Multipole stripe patterns give a strong field at contact that falls off quickly with distance.
- Sliding two such magnets back to back gives a bumpy, ratcheting feel in one direction as the stripes pass each other.
