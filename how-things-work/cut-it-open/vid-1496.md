## Fact check

- Yogurt is made by fermenting milk with lactic acid bacteria (e.g. Streptococcus thermophilus and Lactobacillus bulgaricus); live yogurt contains very large numbers of them.
- Typical incubation is about 40-45 °C (43 °C is common) for several hours; yogurt makers simply hold this temperature.
- Bacteria convert lactose to lactic acid; falling pH makes casein proteins coagulate into a gel.
- Temperatures well above about 50-55 °C kill the cultures; cold slows them, so chilling stops fermentation.
