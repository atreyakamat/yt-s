## Fact check

- Structural firefighting turnout gear (e.g. NFPA 1971 / EN 469) has three layers: a flame-resistant outer shell (often aramid or PBI blends), a moisture barrier, and a thermal liner.
- The moisture barrier blocks liquid water and many chemicals while being somewhat breathable.
- Thermal liners insulate largely by trapping still air; gear provides limited time in extreme events such as flashover. The gauge is illustrative.
- Retroreflective and fluorescent trim improves visibility.
