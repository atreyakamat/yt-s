## Fact check

- Tap water contains dissolved gases and minerals.
- In an ice tray, water freezes from the outer surfaces inward; ice crystals exclude dissolved gas and minerals, concentrating them in the remaining liquid.
- The last part to freeze (the centre) traps them as tiny bubbles and inclusions, which scatter light and look white.
- Directional freezing (from one side) lets the gas be pushed out ahead of the freezing front, producing clear ice.
