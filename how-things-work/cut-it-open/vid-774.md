## Fact check

- Inflatable life jackets contain a folded bladder and a compressed CO2 cartridge.
- Automatic models use a water-sensitive element (a dissolving tablet or bobbin) that, when wet, releases a spring-loaded pin to pierce the cartridge; some use a hydrostatic trigger instead.
- CO2 inflates the bladder within seconds.
- They also have a manual pull cord and an oral inflation tube; after firing they must be rearmed with a new cartridge and trigger.
- No safety advice is given beyond describing how the device works.
