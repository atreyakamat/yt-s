## Fact check

- Decaffeination is done on green (unroasted) coffee beans; they're moistened/steamed first so caffeine can be extracted.
- Main methods: water process with activated charcoal filters (e.g. 'Swiss Water'), solvents (ethyl acetate or methylene chloride), and supercritical carbon dioxide.
- In the water process, caffeine-free 'flavour-charged' water extracts caffeine while leaving flavour compounds.
- US decaf must have at least 97% of caffeine removed; decaf still contains a small amount.
- Ludwig Roselius's first commercial process (patented around 1905-1906) used benzene, no longer used.
