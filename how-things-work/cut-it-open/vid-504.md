## Fact check

- Modern performance bowling balls have a coverstock, a filler, and a dense inner core ('weight block'), in symmetric or asymmetric shapes.
- Core shape sets the ball's mass distribution (radius of gyration, differential), which affects how it rotates and how much it hooks.
- Lanes are oiled over the front portion, so balls skid there and hook when they reach the drier back end.
- Reactive resin and urethane covers grip the lane more than polyester (plastic) covers.
- Pro shops lay out finger holes relative to the core's pin to tune ball reaction.
