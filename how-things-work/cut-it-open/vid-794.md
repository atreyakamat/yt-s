## Fact check

- Socket outlets use spring contacts (typically brass or phosphor bronze) that grip the plug pins.
- Terminals behind the faceplate connect live, neutral and earth; brown/blue/green-and-yellow are the UK/EU colours.
- A poor contact has higher resistance, so it heats (P = I^2 R); warm or scorched sockets are a recognised warning sign needing an electrician.
