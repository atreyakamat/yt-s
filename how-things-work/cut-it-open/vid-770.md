## Fact check

- Many road tunnels use longitudinal ventilation with ceiling-mounted jet fans that push air along the tunnel.
- Sensors monitor carbon monoxide, nitrogen dioxide and visibility (haze) to control the fans.
- In a fire, the fans control smoke movement, pushing it in one direction so people can evacuate on the smoke-free side, often via emergency cross-passages.
- Jet fans are typically reversible.
