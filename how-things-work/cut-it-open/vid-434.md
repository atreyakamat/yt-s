## Fact check

- Many water networks use service reservoirs on high ground or water towers so gravity provides distribution pressure; pumps fill them, often off-peak.
- Hydrostatic pressure: 10 m of water = about 0.98 bar, so 'about one bar'.
- Pressure depends on the height difference between the stored water level and the outlet, so properties close to the reservoir level get lower pressure.
