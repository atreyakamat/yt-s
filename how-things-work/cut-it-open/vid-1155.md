## Fact check

- Garment steamers heat water in a boiler/element and deliver steam through a hose or head.
- Steam (heat plus moisture) loosens hydrogen bonds in fibres; on a hanging garment, the fabric's weight pulls creases out without pressing.
- Without a hot soleplate, steamers avoid shine and scorching on delicate fabrics.
- Gurgling/spitting comes from steam condensing in the cooler hose.
