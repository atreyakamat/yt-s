## Fact check

- The soil and vent stack carries waste from WCs and sinks; its top usually terminates above the roof as a vent.
- Traps (U-bends) hold a water seal that blocks sewer gases.
- Water rushing down the stack causes negative pressure that can siphon traps; venting admits air to equalise pressure.
- Vents discharge sewer gases at high level away from openings; gurgling fixtures can indicate poor venting.
