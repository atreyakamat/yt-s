## Fact check

- Air-activated hand warmers contain iron powder, salt, water, activated carbon and vermiculite in a porous pouch, sealed in an airtight outer wrapper.
- When exposed to air, iron oxidizes (rusts), an exothermic reaction that releases heat.
- Fine powder has a huge surface area, so it oxidizes far faster than a solid piece of iron.
- Salt acts as a catalyst/electrolyte, carbon helps disperse heat, vermiculite retains moisture and insulates.
- Typical warmers last several hours, ending when the iron is used up.
