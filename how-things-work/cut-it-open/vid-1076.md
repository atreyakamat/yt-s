## Fact check

- Espresso machines with steam wands have a boiler (or thermoblock) producing steam under pressure, released through a valve and a multi-hole tip.
- Holding the tip just below the surface draws air in ('stretching'); lowering it creates a whirlpool that heats and breaks large bubbles into microfoam.
- Milk proteins (caseins and whey) stabilise foam bubbles.
- Baristas typically steam to about 55-65 °C; much hotter gives a cooked flavour.
