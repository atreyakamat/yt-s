## Fact check

- Dry-barrel hydrants, used where winters freeze, have the main valve at the base below the frost line; the barrel above stays empty until opened.
- The operating nut on top turns a long stem down to the main valve.
- When the valve closes, drain holes at the base open so water left in the barrel drains into the soil, preventing freezing.
- Wet-barrel hydrants, full of water up to each outlet valve, are used in mild climates such as much of California.
