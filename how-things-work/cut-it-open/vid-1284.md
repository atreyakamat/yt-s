## Fact check

- Official table tennis balls are 40 mm in diameter and weigh 2.7 g; they are hollow and typically made of two glued halves.
- Their low mass makes them highly sensitive to spin, producing strong curve and kick.
- The ITTF increased ball size from 38 mm to 40 mm in 2000 to slow the game and make it easier to watch.
- Balls were traditionally celluloid, which is highly flammable; the ITTF moved to non-celluloid plastic balls in 2014.
