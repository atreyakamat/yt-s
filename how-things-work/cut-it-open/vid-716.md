## Fact check

- Air fryers are compact convection ovens: a heating element with a high-speed fan above the cooking chamber.
- Perforated or mesh baskets let air circulate under the food.
- Rapid hot, dry airflow dries the surface so Maillard browning and crisping occur; little or no oil is required compared with deep frying.
- The small chamber close to the element heats quickly, so cooking is often faster than in a full-size oven.
