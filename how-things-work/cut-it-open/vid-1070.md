## Fact check

- Recycled glass is crushed into cullet; magnets remove ferrous lids, air knives remove labels, and optical sorters remove ceramics, stones and porcelain.
- Ceramics have a higher melting point and leave inclusions/defects in new glass, so a small amount can spoil a batch.
- Glass is colour-sorted because colourants can't practically be removed.
- Glass furnaces run at about 1,500 °C; cullet melts at a lower temperature than raw materials, saving energy. Glass can be recycled repeatedly without loss of quality.
