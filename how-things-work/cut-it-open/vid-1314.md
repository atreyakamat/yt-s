## Fact check

- A whoopee cushion is a rubber bladder with a flattened neck at one end.
- When air is forced out, the flat neck walls repeatedly slap open and shut, producing the sound (a self-excited flutter, like buzzing lips).
- Brass players produce sound by buzzing their lips in a similar way; a balloon squeals by the same mechanism when its neck is stretched.
- Pressure and flow affect the vibration rate, changing pitch.
- The modern whoopee cushion was developed around 1930 by a Toronto rubber company.
