## Fact check

- Pull-back toy cars contain a small motor unit with a coiled (spiral) spring and a gear train connected to one axle.
- Rolling the car backwards winds the spring through the gears, storing elastic potential energy.
- On release the spring unwinds and drives the wheels forward; the gearing converts slow spring rotation into faster wheel rotation.
- Winding further (up to the spring's limit) stores more energy and gives a longer run.
