## Fact check

- Many aerosols use a liquefied gas propellant (e.g. propane/butane blends) that sits as a liquid with vapour above it, typically several bar above atmospheric pressure.
- A dip tube runs from the valve to the bottom; opening the valve lets vapour pressure push liquid up and out.
- On release the propellant flash-evaporates, breaking the product into droplets; evaporation absorbs heat, cooling the can.
- Cans carry warnings not to pierce or expose to heat because pressure rises with temperature.
