## Fact check

- Tape uses pressure-sensitive adhesives (PSAs), which are viscoelastic: they flow under pressure to wet the surface and resist separation like a solid when pulled.
- Adhesion comes largely from van der Waals forces over the real contact area, which grows with pressure and time.
- Tape backings have a release coating so the roll can unwind.
- Grid is illustrative.
