## Fact check

- Phone accelerometers are MEMS (micro-electro-mechanical systems) devices etched from silicon, packaged in chips a few millimetres across.
- A capacitive MEMS accelerometer has a proof mass on silicon springs with interdigitated comb fingers; acceleration shifts the mass and changes the capacitance between fingers.
- Three-axis sensing measures gravity's direction, which the phone uses for screen rotation.
