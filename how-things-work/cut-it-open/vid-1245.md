## Fact check

- Mothballs are made of naphthalene or paradichlorobenzene, both of which sublime (solid to vapour) at room temperature.
- Clothes moth larvae, not adults, damage fabric by eating keratin in wool and similar fibres; they favour soiled fabric.
- Mothballs work by vapour concentration in sealed containers; labels direct use in airtight containers. The vapours are toxic, so they must be kept from children and pets.
