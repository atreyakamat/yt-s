## Fact check

- Security threads are embedded in banknote paper during manufacture; windowed threads weave to the surface in places.
- Many threads carry microprinted text (e.g. 'USA 20' on US notes).
- On current US notes, the thread glows under UV: $5 blue, $10 orange, $20 green, $50 yellow, $100 pink.
- Bar heights simply show the note value.
