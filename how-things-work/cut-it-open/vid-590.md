## Fact check

- Roads are built with a camber or crossfall so water drains to the edges.
- Road gullies have a grating over a gully pot that traps silt (often with a water seal) before the outlet pipe; councils empty them with suction tankers.
- Where drainage is separate from foul sewers, surface water usually flows to rivers or streams without treatment, so pollutants poured into drains harm wildlife. Older combined systems send it to treatment, hence 'in many towns'.
