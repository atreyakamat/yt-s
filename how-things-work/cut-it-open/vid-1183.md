## Fact check

- Mechanism → swivel lipstick cases use a cup with pegs that run in straight slots (stopping rotation) and a helical groove (converting twist to lift).
- Formula → lipsticks are mainly waxes and oils with pigments; waxes give firmness, oils soften at skin temperature.
- Glue sticks → use a twist mechanism (usually a screw) to raise the stick, a similar idea.
