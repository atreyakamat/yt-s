## Fact check

- Four load cells → most digital bathroom scales have a load cell in each corner/foot.
- Strain gauge → a foil grid bonded to the beam; stretching lengthens and thins the conductor, raising resistance (measured with a bridge circuit).
- Tiny bend → load cell deflection is a fraction of a millimetre.
- Sum of corners → the total is the same whichever way you lean; example bars total 70 kg.
