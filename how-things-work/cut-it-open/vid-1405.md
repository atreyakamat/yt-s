## Fact check

- Presbyopia, the age-related loss of near focusing, is caused by the eye's crystalline lens becoming less flexible.
- Bifocals (often credited to Benjamin Franklin) have a visible dividing line; progressive (varifocal) lenses change power gradually from distance at the top to near at the bottom with an intermediate corridor.
- The surface curvature changes progressively; unavoidable peripheral astigmatism creates blurry side zones, so wearers learn to turn their heads. Bars are illustrative.
