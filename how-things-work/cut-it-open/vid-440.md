## Fact check

- Modern sign sheeting is often microprismatic: arrays of tiny cube-corner prisms; older grades use glass beads. Both are retroreflective.
- A cube corner (three mutually perpendicular faces) returns light parallel to its incoming direction.
- Because a driver's eyes are close to the headlights, they see the strong returned light.
- Apollo 11 (1969) left a cube-corner retroreflector array on the Moon that is still used for laser ranging.
