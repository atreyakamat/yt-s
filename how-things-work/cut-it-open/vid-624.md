## Fact check

- Claw machines use a gantry on rails driven by motors; the claw is usually closed by an electromagnet (solenoid) whose strength depends on the power supplied.
- Operator manuals for many machines include settings for claw strength and a target payout rate.
- With payout control on, the machine gives a weak grip on most plays and full strength only occasionally to meet the set rate.
- Player skill still affects positioning; the script says machines 'can' do this because settings vary by machine and local rules.
