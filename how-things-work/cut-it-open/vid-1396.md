## Fact check

- Integral instant film (e.g., the classic square-format type) contains light-sensitive silver halide/dye layers and a reagent pod in the wide bottom border.
- Exiting through rollers ruptures the pod and spreads reagent between layers.
- An opacifier temporarily shields the developing layers from light so development can happen outside the camera; dyes migrate to the image-receiving layer and the opacifier clears.
- The film's manufacturer has stated shaking doesn't help and can damage/blotch the image; it advises keeping it face down and flat while developing.
