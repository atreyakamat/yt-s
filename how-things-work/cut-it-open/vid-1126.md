## Fact check

- Red laser pointers use a direct semiconductor laser diode (around 635-670 nm) with a collimating lens.
- Laser light is monochromatic and coherent, giving a narrow beam.
- Classic green pointers are diode-pumped solid-state lasers: an 808 nm infrared diode pumps a Nd:YVO4 crystal lasing at 1064 nm; a KTP crystal frequency-doubles it to 532 nm green. An IR filter blocks residual infrared.
- Human eyes are most sensitive around 555 nm, so green appears much brighter than red at equal power.
- Aiming lasers at aircraft is illegal in many countries and can injure eyes. This is a general safety statement, not medical advice.
