## Fact check

- Domestic oven doors typically use two to four panes of heat-resistant glass separated by air gaps; pyrolytic ovens usually have three or four.
- Air is a poor conductor of heat, so the gaps insulate.
- Inner panes often carry a low-emissivity (thin metallic oxide) coating that reflects infrared heat back into the cavity.
- Many ovens use a cooling fan that draws air through the door and out of a vent, keeping the outer pane cool.
