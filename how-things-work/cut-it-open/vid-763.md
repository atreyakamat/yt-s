## Fact check

- Composition → hairsprays are film-forming polymers (e.g. PVP/VA copolymers, acrylates) dissolved in ethanol, with a propellant in aerosols.
- Alcohol evaporates fast → leaving the polymer behind.
- Spot welds → the polymer collects at hair crossing points, bonding fibres; cosmetic chemists describe these as 'spot welds'.
- Water-soluble polymers → washing removes the film; brushing breaks the bonds.
