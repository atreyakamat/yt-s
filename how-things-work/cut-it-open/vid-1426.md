## Fact check

- Walkie-talkies are half-duplex: they use a single shared channel and either transmit or receive, never both at once.
- Push-to-talk switches the unit from receive to transmit mode; simultaneous transmissions interfere ('doubling').
- Many handheld radios use the loudspeaker as a microphone when transmitting.
- Procedure words like 'over' signal the end of a transmission so the other party can respond.
