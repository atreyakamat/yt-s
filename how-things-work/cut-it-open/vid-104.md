## Fact check

- Combination (combi) boilers heat domestic hot water on demand and have no storage cylinder.
- A flow sensor/switch detects the hot tap opening; a diverter valve sends the primary (boiler) water to a plate heat exchanger instead of the radiators.
- Plate heat exchangers transfer heat between the two circuits through thin plates without mixing them.
- When demand stops, the diverter returns to central heating mode.
