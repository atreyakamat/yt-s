## Fact check

- Spring-powered foam dart blasters use a spring-loaded plunger in an air cylinder (a springer).
- Priming compresses the spring and a catch holds the plunger; the trigger releases it.
- The plunger rapidly compresses air in the cylinder, and that air pressure propels the dart down the barrel; the spring does not contact the dart.
- Motorized blasters instead use two counter-rotating flywheels that grip and fling the dart.
