## Fact check

- PET bottles are made by stretch blow moulding from injection-moulded preforms, which already have the finished screw thread and neck ring.
- Preforms are compact, so they're often shipped to bottlers and blown on site.
- The preform is reheated above its softening point (not melted), stretched with a rod and blown with high-pressure air against a chilled mould.
- The blowing step takes on the order of a second or less ('around a second').
- Biaxial stretching orients PET molecules, increasing strength and allowing thin walls.
