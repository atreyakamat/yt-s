## Fact check

- Carpet cleaners (extractors) have a clean solution tank and a separate recovery tank; a pump sprays solution, brushes agitate, and a vacuum motor extracts the dirty water.
- 'Steam cleaning' of carpets usually refers to hot water extraction rather than true steam.
