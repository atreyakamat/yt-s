## Fact check

- Automatic level crossings are triggered by track circuits, treadles or axle counters some distance before the crossing.
- The strike-in distance is set so the fastest permitted train allows sufficient warning time.
- Lights and audible alarms start before barriers lower.
- Many barrier designs are counterweighted or arranged to fall to the lowered position under gravity on power loss (fail-safe); designs vary, hence 'many'.
- Barriers rise once the train clears the crossing section.
