## Fact check

- Peanuts are roughly 45-50% fat (oil) by weight.
- In natural peanut butter, the oil is less dense than the ground solids and separates to the top over time.
- Many commercial peanut butters add a small amount of fat that is solid at room temperature (e.g. hydrogenated vegetable oil or palm oil) as a stabiliser; its crystals form a network that holds the oil in place.
