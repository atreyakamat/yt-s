## Fact check

- EU pedelecs provide assistance only while the rider pedals (throttle-only bikes are classed differently).
- Cadence sensors detect pedalling using magnets and a sensor; torque sensors measure force/strain in the drivetrain.
- Assistance is proportional to rider input with torque sensors, with user-selectable modes.
- In the EU, pedelec assistance must cut off at 25 km/h (and nominal motor power is limited to 250 W).
