## Fact check

- Three-jaw drill chucks hold jaws in bores angled toward the axis; a threaded nut (ring) inside the sleeve engages threads on the jaws and drives them forward and inward together.
- Because the jaws move equally, the chuck is self-centring.
- The thread provides mechanical advantage; many keyless chucks have a ratcheting lock that clicks.
- Reversing the sleeve retracts and opens the jaws.
