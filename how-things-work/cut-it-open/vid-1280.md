## Fact check

- Many US hydrants use a pentagonal operating nut (the common standard size is 1-1/2 inch point-to-flat), which needs a matching hydrant wrench.
- A regular pentagon has no parallel faces, so open-ended or adjustable wrenches can't grip it well, deterring tampering.
- Unauthorised hydrant opening lowers system pressure available for firefighting; some cities also fit locking caps.
- Hydrants are opened and closed slowly to avoid water hammer (pressure surges).
