## Fact check

- Transparency / ambient / hear-through modes use the external microphones (also used for ANC) to capture ambient sound and play it through the drivers.
- Processing compensates for the passive attenuation of the ear tip (stronger at higher frequencies) to sound natural.
- Latency is kept very low (well under a few milliseconds in good implementations) to avoid echo-like artefacts.
