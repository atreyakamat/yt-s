## Fact check

- A ceiling rose is the ceiling fitting a pendant light hangs from; it contains terminal blocks.
- UK lighting commonly uses the loop-in method: the supply cable arrives, a cable continues to the next rose, and a cable runs to the switch and returns as the switched live.
- The pendant flex is hooked over anchorages in the rose so its weight isn't borne by the terminals.
- Work on fixed wiring should be done with the power isolated, ideally by an electrician.
