## Fact check

- Remote keyless entry fobs commonly use rolling (hopping) codes: fob and car share a secret key and a synchronised counter; each press produces a new encrypted code.
- The receiver accepts codes within a window of upcoming counter values (so a few presses out of range still work) and rejects already-used codes, defeating simple record-and-replay.
- Fobs are typically powered by a coin cell battery.
