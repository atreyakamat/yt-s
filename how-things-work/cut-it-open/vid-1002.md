## Fact check

- Menthol activates TRPM8, the main cold-sensing receptor in sensory nerves, producing a cooling sensation without lowering temperature.
- Menthol sensitises TRPM8 so it fires at warmer temperatures, which is why cool water feels much colder after mint.
- Capsaicin (chilli) similarly activates the heat receptor TRPV1. Bars are illustrative.
