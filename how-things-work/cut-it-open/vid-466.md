## Fact check

- Modern phone chargers are switch-mode power supplies: they rectify AC mains to DC, switch it at high frequency, step it down through a small transformer, then rectify and regulate the output.
- Switching frequencies are typically tens to hundreds of kHz (title's '100,000 times a second' is within that range); higher frequency allows a much smaller transformer than 50/60 Hz linear designs.
- A feedback controller adjusts switching to regulate output voltage.
