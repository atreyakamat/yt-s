## Fact check

- Adult webbing clothes moths (Tineola bisselliella) have reduced mouthparts and do not feed.
- Larvae feed on animal fibres (wool, silk, fur, feathers), digesting keratin, which few animals can.
- Larvae favour dark, undisturbed places and soiled fabric (sweat, food residues).
- Standard prevention: clean before storage, sealed storage, regular disturbance and inspection.
