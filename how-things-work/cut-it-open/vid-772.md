## Fact check

- Manual key duplicators clamp the original and the blank side by side on a carriage; a stylus (tracer) follows the original's bitting while a rotating cutter cuts the blank in the same position.
- Keys are aligned by the shoulder (or tip) in the clamps; misalignment produces a faulty copy.
- A wire brush or deburring wheel removes burrs after cutting.
- Each copy adds small errors, so copying from a copy degrades accuracy; locksmiths prefer cutting from originals or by code.
