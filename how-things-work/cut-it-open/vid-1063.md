## Fact check

- Hollow bars → towel rails are hollow tube ladders, like radiators.
- Plumbed rails → connected to central heating circuits.
- Electric rails → 'wet' electric rails are filled with water-glycol or oil and heated by an element (dry-element types also exist).
- Convection → heated fluid rises and cooler fluid sinks, circulating heat.
- Warm air holds more moisture → speeds drying.
