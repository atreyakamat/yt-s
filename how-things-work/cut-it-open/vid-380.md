## Fact check

- Asphalt concrete is roughly 95% aggregate (stone, sand) and 5% bitumen binder by weight.
- Flexible pavements are layered: surface course, binder course, base, sub-base of crushed rock, on the natural subgrade.
- The layers spread wheel loads so the stress reaching the subgrade is low enough to avoid deformation.
- Resurfacing typically replaces only the top layer(s); well-built lower layers can last decades. Roman roads were also built in layers.
