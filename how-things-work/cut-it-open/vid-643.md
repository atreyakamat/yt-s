## Fact check

- Infrared emission → all warm objects emit infrared radiation in proportion to temperature.
- Eardrum → the tympanic membrane shares blood supply with the hypothalamus, the brain's temperature-control centre, which is why it's used.
- Thermopile → many thermocouple junctions in series that generate a voltage when warmed by absorbed infrared.
- Speed → tympanic thermometers give readings in about 1-3 s; 37°C is the typical body temperature figure. No medical guidance given.
