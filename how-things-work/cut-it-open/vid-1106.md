## Fact check

- Typical hand mixers use a worm on the motor shaft driving two worm wheels on either side, giving speed reduction, more torque and opposite rotation.
- Because both wheels are driven by the same worm, they are phase-locked, so the interlocking beaters never collide.
- Many models have beaters with different stems (e.g. one with a collar) that fit only one socket each.
