## Fact check

- Parking guidance systems commonly use an ultrasonic sensor above each bay: echo time is shorter when a vehicle is present.
- Each bay's indicator shows green (free) or red (occupied), and a central controller totals free spaces for level and entrance signs (37 is an illustrative number).
- Some systems use camera-based sensors instead.
