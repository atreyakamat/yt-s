## Fact check

- Coin production: metal strip is rolled to thickness, blanks are punched, annealed and cleaned, then upset (rimmed) before striking.
- Blanks are struck between obverse and reverse dies under tens of tonnes of force, causing the metal to flow into the design.
- US Mint high-speed presses strike up to about 750 coins per minute. Grid and gauge are illustrative.
