## Fact check

- Cooking releases water vapour, smoke, odours and aerosolised fat droplets.
- Cooker hood grease filters are layered aluminium mesh or stainless baffles; droplets have too much inertia to follow the sharp turns of the airflow and impact on the metal.
- Ducted hoods vent outdoors; recirculating hoods pass air through activated charcoal filters to absorb odours and return it to the kitchen.
- Many manufacturers say metal grease filters are dishwasher-safe and should be cleaned regularly.
