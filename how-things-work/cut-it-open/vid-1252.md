## Fact check

- Manual wire strippers have graded notches marked with conductor sizes (AWG or mm²); each notch closes to the conductor's diameter so only the insulation is cut.
- Using too small a notch nicks or cuts strands, creating weak points that can break or overheat.
- Usual technique: close on the wire, rotate slightly to score the insulation, then pull it off.
- Automatic/self-adjusting strippers grip, cut and remove insulation in one squeeze.
