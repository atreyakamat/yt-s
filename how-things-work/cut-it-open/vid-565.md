## Fact check

- Pressurised metered-dose inhalers contain drug suspended or dissolved in a liquefied propellant under pressure.
- A metering valve fills a small fixed-volume chamber; actuation seals it from the canister and releases that dose through the nozzle.
- The propellant flash-evaporates to form an aerosol; particles of roughly 1-5 micrometres are most likely to reach the lower airways. Bars are illustrative.
- Many inhalers include dose counters. Educational only, not medical advice.
