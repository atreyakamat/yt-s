## Fact check

- Cloud storage runs on servers and drives in data centres.
- Large providers split objects into chunks and replicate or erasure-code them across disks, racks and often separate facilities.
- At data-centre scale, drive failures are routine and redundancy rebuilds lost data.
- Many sync clients upload only changed blocks (delta sync).
- 'Times three' in the title reflects the common practice of keeping about three copies; exact schemes vary.
