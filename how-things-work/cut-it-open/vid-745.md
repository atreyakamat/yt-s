## Fact check

- Digital bathroom scales typically use four load cells, one per foot, each with strain gauges.
- A strain gauge is a fine zigzag metal foil; stretching it increases its resistance, measured with a bridge circuit.
- The deflection is microscopic; the electronics sum the four cells and convert to weight. Uneven or soft floors can affect readings.
