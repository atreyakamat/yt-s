## Fact check

- Lint rollers are rolls of adhesive paper wound with the adhesive facing outward on a core with a handle.
- The adhesive is low-to-medium tack so it picks up loose fibres and hair without lifting the fabric.
- Sheets are perforated or spiral/diagonal-cut so a used layer can be peeled off.
- The adhesive lint roller was invented in 1956 (Nicholas McKay), so 'the 1950s'.
