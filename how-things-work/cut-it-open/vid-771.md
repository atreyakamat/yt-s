## Fact check

- Rubber consists of long polymer chains (polyisoprene) that are randomly coiled; stretching aligns them.
- Rubber elasticity is mainly entropic: thermal motion drives chains back toward disordered, coiled states.
- Vulcanisation adds sulphur cross-links so chains don't slide past each other permanently (Goodyear, 1839).
- Rubber warms when stretched quickly (thermoelastic effect); UV and ozone degrade rubber over time. Gauge is illustrative.
