## Fact check

- A shank is a supportive insert in the waist of a shoe sole, between heel and ball.
- Shanks are made of steel, fibreglass, plastic (e.g. TPU/nylon) or wood.
- In heeled shoes and boots the waist of the sole is raised off the ground, so it needs stiffening to avoid collapse.
- Shanks are important in high heels and in work boots used on ladders.
