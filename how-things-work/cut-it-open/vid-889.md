## Fact check

- Most aircraft main gear use oleo-pneumatic struts containing hydraulic oil and compressed nitrogen.
- Compressed nitrogen acts as the spring; oil metered through orifices provides damping.
- Retractable gear is raised by hydraulics after take-off to reduce drag.
- Airliners have an alternate extension (gravity free-fall) system that releases uplocks so the gear falls and locks down.
