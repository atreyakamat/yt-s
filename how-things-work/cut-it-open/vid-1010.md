## Fact check

- Signal coordination ('green wave') sets offsets between adjacent signals so a platoon travelling at the design speed meets successive greens.
- Arriving faster than the design speed means reaching the next signal before it turns green.
- Copenhagen operates green waves for cyclists timed at about 20 km/h on several routes.
- Two-way coordination is constrained by signal spacing, so it usually favours one direction at peak times.
