## Fact check

- Many dishwasher detergent dispensers use a thermal 'wax motor' actuator: a small heater warms a wax pellet that expands markedly on melting and drives a plunger to release the spring-loaded lid. Some designs use solenoids or bimetal actuators instead, hence 'many' and 'often'.
- The controller energises it at the start of the main wash, so detergent isn't lost in the pre-rinse.
