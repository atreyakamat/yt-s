## Fact check

- Air conditioners are heat pumps: they move heat from indoors to outdoors via the vapour-compression refrigeration cycle.
- Split systems have an indoor evaporator unit and an outdoor unit with compressor and condenser, linked by copper refrigerant lines.
- Common refrigerants (e.g. R32, R410A) boil far below 0 °C at atmospheric pressure.
- Compression raises the gas temperature above outdoor air so heat can be rejected; an expansion valve drops pressure and temperature.
- The evaporator coil is below the dew point, so moisture condenses and is drained away: the drip.
