## Fact check

- A dynamic (moving-coil) microphone has a diaphragm attached to a voice coil suspended in the gap of a permanent magnet.
- Sound moves the diaphragm and coil; motion in the magnetic field induces a small voltage (electromagnetic induction).
- A loudspeaker uses the same parts in reverse: current in the coil moves the cone to make sound.
- Dynamic mics need no phantom power or battery, and are known for being rugged.
- Handheld vocal mics commonly have a mesh grille with foam behind it as a windscreen.
