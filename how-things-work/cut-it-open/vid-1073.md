## Fact check

- Hide is mostly collagen, which decomposes through bacterial action if untreated; dried untreated hide becomes hard (rawhide).
- Tanning cross-links collagen, stabilising it against microbes, heat and water.
- Chrome tanning (chromium(III) salts), commercialised in the 1880s, accounts for the large majority of leather today and takes roughly a day.
- Vegetable tanning with bark tannins takes weeks to months (about 40 days is a typical middle figure).
