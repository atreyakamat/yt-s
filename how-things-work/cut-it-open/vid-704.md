## Fact check

- Traditional box sash windows have two vertically sliding sashes in a frame with hollow boxes either side.
- Each sash hangs on cords running over pulleys to cast iron (or lead) weights in the boxes, weighted to counterbalance the sash.
- A balanced sash stays in any position; a broken cord makes the sash drop, a common repair.
