## Fact check

- Audio induction loops carry an amplified audio current through a wire loop, producing a varying magnetic field.
- Hearing aids and cochlear implants with a telecoil (T setting) pick up this field by electromagnetic induction, giving the speaker's voice with far less background noise.
- The hearing loop symbol shows an ear, often with a letter T. Grid is illustrative.
