## Fact check

- Hub dynamos are generators built into the front hub: permanent magnets rotate with the hub shell around stationary coils on the axle.
- Electromagnetic induction makes a voltage that rises with speed (output is AC).
- Common standard output is 6 V, 3 W (a few watts), enough for LED lights.
- Unlike bottle dynamos they do not rub the tyre; drag is a few watts while lighting and low when lights are off.
