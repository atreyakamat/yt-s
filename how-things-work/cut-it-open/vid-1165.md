## Fact check

- Natural gas is mainly methane, which is colourless and odourless.
- Gas suppliers add odorants such as mercaptans (thiols) and tetrahydrothiophene, sulphur compounds with a rotten-egg/cabbage smell.
- These odorants are detectable by the human nose at parts-per-billion levels.
- Regulations require gas to be odorised so it is detectable well below its lower flammable limit (commonly at one-fifth of it).
