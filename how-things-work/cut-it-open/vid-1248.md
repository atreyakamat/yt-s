## Fact check

- EV traction motors (permanent-magnet synchronous or induction) have a stationary stator with copper windings and a rotating rotor; the rotor is the main moving part besides bearings.
- An inverter switches three-phase current through the windings to create a rotating magnetic field that the rotor follows.
- Electric motors deliver maximum torque from zero rpm and operate over a wide speed range, so most EVs use a single-speed reduction gear.
