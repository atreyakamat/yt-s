## Fact check

- Commercial canning fills and seals (double-seams) the can first, then heat-processes it in a retort.
- Retorts are pressurised, reaching about 121 °C, hotter than boiling water at normal pressure.
- This temperature is needed to destroy heat-resistant bacterial spores that survive 100 °C.
- The hermetic seal prevents recontamination; canned food remains safe for years if the can is undamaged, while quality slowly declines.
- Bulging cans are a recognised warning sign of spoilage (this is general information, not advice).
