## Fact check

- Many stand mixers use 'planetary' mixing: the attachment rotates on its own shaft while the shaft travels around the bowl, so it covers the whole bowl (some other designs rotate the bowl instead).
- This is done with a planetary gear set: a planet gear meshes with a stationary internal ring gear while the carrier is driven by the motor.
- Dough hooks use the same motion to knead.
