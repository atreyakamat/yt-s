## Fact check

- Concrete's tensile strength is roughly a tenth of its compressive strength.
- In a bending beam the bottom is in tension, so reinforcement is placed near the tension face.
- Steel and concrete have very similar coefficients of thermal expansion (about 10-12 x 10^-6 per °C), which is why they work together.
- Concrete's alkalinity passivates steel; corrosion products occupy more volume than steel, causing cracking and spalling.
