## Fact check

- Airliner wing boxes (spars, ribs, skins) are sealed to form integral fuel tanks; many also have a centre tank.
- Long-haul jets carry well over 100,000 litres (e.g. 777-300ER about 181,000 L; A350-900 about 141,000 L).
- Fuel weight in the wings counteracts the upward bending from lift (wing bending relief), reducing root bending moment.
- Fuel management typically uses centre tank fuel first to keep weight in the wings longer.
