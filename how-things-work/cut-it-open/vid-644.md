## Fact check

- Stack effect: hot flue gas is less dense than outside air, so it rises and creates lower pressure at the base, drawing combustion air through the fire.
- More air raises combustion rate and temperature, strengthening the draught.
- Draught increases with chimney height and the temperature difference between flue gas and outside air.
- A cold flue gives weak or reversed draught at first, so smoke can spill into the room until the flue warms.
