## Fact check

- 'Security tint' patterns are printed on the inside of envelopes to make them opaque so contents (cheques, statements) can't be read through the paper.
- Busy, dark, repetitive patterns mask text by adding visual noise and blocking transmitted light.
- Some organisations use custom security tint designs. Grid and gauge are illustrative.
