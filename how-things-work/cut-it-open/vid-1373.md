## Fact check

- Domestic silkworms (Bombyx mori caterpillars) spin cocoons from a single continuous filament, typically about 300-900 metres long.
- The filament is fibroin protein coated with sericin gum.
- Cocoons are softened in hot water and several filaments are reeled together into a thread.
- Silk fibres have a roughly triangular cross-section that refracts light, giving shimmer.
- Silk production in China dates back roughly 5,000 years; Silk Road trade flourished from around the 2nd century BC.
