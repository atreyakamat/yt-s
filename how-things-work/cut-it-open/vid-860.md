## Fact check

- Signal cabinets house the controller, detector amplifier cards for loops and other sensors, a conflict monitor / malfunction management unit, and sometimes battery backup.
- Actuated controllers cycle through phases and extend green while detectors show approaching traffic.
- In the US, a conflict monitor that detects conflicting greens puts the intersection into flashing operation until serviced; practice varies elsewhere (UK signals typically switch off), hence 'in many places'.
- Many controllers are linked to central urban traffic control systems.
