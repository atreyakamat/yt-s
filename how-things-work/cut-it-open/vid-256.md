## Fact check

- True wireless earbuds have small batteries (typically a few hours of playback); the case contains a larger lithium-ion battery that recharges them several times.
- Cases use magnets to seat and hold the buds and spring-loaded (pogo) pins contacting metal pads on the buds to charge them and exchange status.
- Many cases use a magnet and a Hall-effect sensor to detect lid opening, which triggers wake-up and pairing.
