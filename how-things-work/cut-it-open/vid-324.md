## Fact check

- Skateboard trucks consist of a baseplate, kingpin, bushings, pivot cup and hanger with axle.
- Tilting the deck compresses the bushings and rotates the hanger about the pivot axis, steering the wheels ('lean steering').
- Front and rear trucks are mirror images, so they steer in opposite directions, tightening the turn.
- Tightening the kingpin nut preloads the bushings and stiffens turning; loosening makes it more responsive.
- Softer bushings turn more easily; harder bushings resist and feel more stable at speed.
