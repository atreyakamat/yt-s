## Fact check

- Rear cassettes range from small to large cogs; tooth counts like 11 and 34 are common on modern bikes.
- A rear derailleur has a parallelogram arm and a cage with two jockey (pulley) wheels guiding the chain.
- On most rear derailleurs, cable tension moves the chain toward larger cogs and a return spring moves it toward smaller cogs.
- The spring-loaded cage takes up chain slack as gear size changes. The chain must be moving (pedalling) for it to shift.
