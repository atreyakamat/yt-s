## Fact check

- Typical petrol engine energy balance: roughly 25-30 % useful work, about 30 % rejected to coolant and about 30-40 % lost in exhaust heat; all bars labelled 'roughly'.
- Liquid cooling: a water pump circulates coolant (water + antifreeze, usually ethylene glycol) through engine passages to the radiator, with a thermostat controlling flow.
- Radiators use many thin tubes with fins to increase surface area; ram air cools at speed and an electric or engine-driven fan pulls air through at low speed.
