## Fact check

- Many spin mops have a helical (spiral) rod mechanism in the handle that converts a linear push into rotation of the mop head, like a push-spinning top; others use a foot pedal and gears.
- The bucket's spin basket lets the head rotate; centrifugal action flings water out through the basket.
- Several pumps reduce the mop from soaking to damp without hand wringing.
