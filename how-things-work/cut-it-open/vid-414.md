## Fact check

- A snare drum has a set of coiled metal wires (the snares) stretched across the thin resonant (bottom) head.
- Striking the batter head pushes air inside the shell, moving the bottom head against the snares, which rattle and add the bright buzz.
- A throw-off lever disengages the snares, giving a tom-like sound.
- Snares commonly buzz sympathetically when other instruments play nearby.
- Drum heads are tuned with a drum key by adjusting tension rods evenly.
