## Fact check

- Hot-melt glue sticks are thermoplastics (commonly EVA-based blends) that melt when heated and solidify on cooling, with no curing reaction, so they can be remelted.
- Low-temperature guns run around 120 °C and high-temperature guns around 190-200 °C.
- The trigger mechanism grips and pushes the stick into the heated chamber.
- Hot glue sets within seconds to about a minute; cold smooth substrates quench it before it wets the surface, weakening the bond.
