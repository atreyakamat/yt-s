## Fact check

- A current in a conductor produces a magnetic field around it (Ampere's law).
- AC clamp meters work as a current transformer: the iron jaws concentrate the field through a secondary coil, inducing a proportional current.
- Clamping a two-core cable gives roughly zero because the outgoing and returning currents produce opposite fields that cancel.
- AC/DC clamp meters use a Hall-effect sensor in a gap in the core to measure steady (DC) fields.
