## Fact check

- CAPTCHA = Completely Automated Public Turing test to tell Computers and Humans Apart, coined around 2000-2003 at Carnegie Mellon.
- reCAPTCHA (from 2007) showed a control word and an unknown word from scanned books/newspapers; answers to the unknown word were used to digitise text once enough users agreed.
- It was used by millions of users daily and helped digitise newspaper and book archives.
- Later versions use behaviour signals (checkbox, risk analysis) and image challenges because machine OCR improved.
