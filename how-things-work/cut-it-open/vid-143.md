## Fact check

- Self-winding watches use a weighted rotor that pivots with wrist movement and winds the mainspring through reduction gears.
- The mainspring powers the gear train, regulated by the balance wheel and escapement; no battery.
- Automatic mainsprings use a slipping bridle so a full spring slips instead of overwinding.
- Typical power reserve is roughly 38-50 hours, i.e. around two days; 40 hours shown as typical.
