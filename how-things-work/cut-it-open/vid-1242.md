## Fact check

- The wire cage on sparkling wine is called a muselet; it has a metal cap (plaque) on top of the cork and legs anchored under the bottle lip.
- Sparkling wine bottles hold about 5-6 atmospheres of pressure.
- The cage is commonly opened with six half-turns of the twisted loop (widely cited standard).
- Adolphe Jacquesson patented the muselet in 1844; earlier corks were tied down with string.
