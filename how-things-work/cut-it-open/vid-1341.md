## Fact check

- Many banknotes use intaglio printing: ink held in engraved recesses of a plate is transferred under very high pressure, leaving raised, tactile ink.
- Intaglio's raised texture is a key 'feel' security feature that standard printers cannot reproduce.
- Several currencies add tactile marks for blind and partially sighted users (e.g. raised dots on Canadian and UK polymer notes).
