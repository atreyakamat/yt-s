## Fact check

- Multi-way extension leads use continuous metal (usually brass) bus strips for live, neutral and earth; every socket's contacts are part of or clip onto these strips.
- The whole lead is limited by its cable and plug rating (e.g. 13 A in the UK), not per socket.
- A kettle (about 3 kW) plus a heater can exceed a 13 A (about 3 kW at 230 V) limit.
- UK BS 1363 plugs contain a cartridge fuse that melts on sustained overcurrent.
