## Fact check

- Resin identification codes (1-7 inside a chasing-arrows triangle) were introduced in 1988 by the US Society of the Plastics Industry to identify plastic type, not to guarantee recyclability.
- 1 = PET (drinks bottles), 2 = HDPE (milk jugs), 4 = LDPE (bags, films), 5 = PP (tubs, caps), 6 = PS (polystyrene, foam), 7 = other.
- Plastics must be sorted by type for mechanical recycling because they have different melting behaviour.
- PET (1) and HDPE (2) are the most widely collected; acceptance varies by local scheme.
