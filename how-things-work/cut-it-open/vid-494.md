## Fact check

- Air at -5 °C still holds thermal energy (it's far above absolute zero).
- Air source heat pumps use a refrigerant evaporating below outdoor temperature so heat flows into it, then compress it to a higher temperature to heat water; an expansion valve completes the cycle.
- Typical seasonal coefficients of performance are around 3 (about 3 kWh heat per kWh electricity), hence 'about three'.
- Same vapour-compression cycle as a fridge, reversed in purpose.
