## Fact check

- Chocolate bloom is a whitish/greyish surface; two types are fat bloom and sugar bloom.
- Fat bloom: cocoa butter migrates to the surface (often after warming) and recrystallises in larger, light-scattering crystals.
- Sugar bloom: surface moisture (e.g. condensation after refrigeration) dissolves sugar, which recrystallises as it dries.
- Bloom is caused mostly by temperature fluctuations and poor storage. It affects appearance and texture, not the ingredients; it is not mould.
