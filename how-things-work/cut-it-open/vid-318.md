## Fact check

- EV packs are made of many lithium-ion cells (hundreds of large pouch/prismatic cells, or thousands of small cylindrical cells) grouped into modules.
- Lithium-ion cells move lithium ions between a graphite anode and a metal-oxide cathode through an electrolyte, with a separator between them.
- Single Li-ion cells are about 3.6-3.7 V; packs are commonly around 400 V (some 800 V). Car starter batteries are 12 V.
- Many EV packs use liquid thermal management; a battery management system monitors cell voltages and temperatures and balances cells.
