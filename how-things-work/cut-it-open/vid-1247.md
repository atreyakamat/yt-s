## Fact check

- WPA2-Personal uses a 4-way handshake: router and client exchange nonces and derive session keys from the pre-shared key; the passphrase itself is not transmitted.
- Matching message integrity codes prove both sides hold the same key.
- Each client gets its own pairwise transient key.
- Traditional open networks have no link-layer encryption; WPA3's Enhanced Open (OWE) can encrypt open networks, hence 'often' and 'older open networks'. HTTPS still protects website traffic on open Wi-Fi.
