## Fact check

- Solar calculators commonly use amorphous silicon thin-film cells, which perform relatively well under indoor fluorescent/LED light.
- Photovoltaic effect: photons free electrons; the p-n (or p-i-n) junction's built-in field drives them as current.
- Calculators with reflective LCDs and low-power CMOS chips draw microwatts, so indoor light suffices.
- Many models are dual-powered with a backup button cell.
