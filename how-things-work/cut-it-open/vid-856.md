## Fact check

- Tempered glass screen protectors consist of an oleophobic coating, a thin tempered (often chemically or thermally strengthened) glass layer, and a silicone adhesive layer.
- Thermal tempering heats glass and rapidly cools the surface, leaving it in compression, which raises strength.
- The protector is a sacrificial layer: it can crack while absorbing and spreading impact energy that might otherwise damage the display glass (it reduces, not eliminates, risk).
- Oleophobic coatings repel skin oils, reducing visible fingerprints.
