## Fact check

- Direct TPMS: a battery-powered sensor mounted on the inside of the valve stem measures pressure and transmits by radio; sealed batteries typically last around 5-10 years.
- Indirect TPMS uses ABS wheel-speed sensors: an under-inflated tyre has a smaller rolling radius and turns faster than the others.
- Both systems light a dashboard warning when pressure drops significantly (e.g. US rules: 25 % below recommended).
