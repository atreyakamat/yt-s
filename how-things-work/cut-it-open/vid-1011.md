## Fact check

- Standard crayons are paraffin wax with pigments, the same wax family used in many candles.
- Crayons are made by pouring molten coloured wax into moulds.
- Paraffin softens and melts at modest temperatures (roughly 50-60 °C), achievable in a closed car in sun.
- Wax repels water, the basis of 'wax resist' art. Gauge is illustrative.
