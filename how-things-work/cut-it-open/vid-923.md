## Fact check

- Fleece is made from polyester (PET), the same polymer as most drinks bottles; much fleece uses recycled bottles.
- Recycling shreds PET into flakes, melts it and extrudes it through spinnerets into fibres.
- Fleece fabric is knitted and then napped (brushed with wire rollers) to raise a pile.
- The pile traps air; polyester absorbs little water so it dries fast.
- Synthetic fabrics shed microfibres in washing that can reach waterways: documented by multiple studies.
