## Fact check

- Emergency locking retractors use two triggers: webbing-sensitive (a centrifugal/inertia mechanism that locks when the belt pays out quickly) and vehicle-sensitive (a pendulum or ball that moves under hard braking, impact or tilt).
- A rewind spring keeps the webbing taut.
- Belts commonly lock when a car is parked on a steep slope because the vehicle-sensitive sensor is tilted; letting the belt retract slightly and pulling slowly releases it.
