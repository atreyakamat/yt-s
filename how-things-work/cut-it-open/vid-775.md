## Fact check

- Lateral flow tests have a sample pad, a conjugate pad with antibodies attached to coloured particles (commonly colloidal gold), a nitrocellulose membrane with test and control lines, and a wicking pad.
- Liquid moves along the strip by capillary action.
- At the test line, immobilised antibodies capture the target bound to gold-labelled antibodies; the control line captures the labelled antibodies regardless, confirming flow.
- Gold nanoparticles appear red because of how they absorb and scatter light.
