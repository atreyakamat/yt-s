## Fact check

- Widgets are hollow plastic capsules with a small hole, used in some canned stouts and ales.
- Liquid nitrogen is dosed just before sealing; it vaporises and pressurises the can, forcing gas and beer into the widget.
- On opening, the can pressure drops and the widget jets its contents through the hole, nucleating many small bubbles that form a creamy head.
- The canned-stout widget was introduced in the late 1980s (patented 1985, launched 1989).
