## Fact check

- Dishwasher spray arms are hollow and fed by the circulation pump; they are typically not motor-driven.
- Angled nozzles produce a reaction force (Newton's third law) that rotates the arm, the same principle as a rotary lawn sprinkler.
- Manufacturers advise checking that tall items don't block the spray arms, as a stalled arm cleans poorly.
