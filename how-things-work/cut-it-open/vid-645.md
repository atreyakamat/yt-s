## Fact check

- Optical brightening agents (fluorescent whitening agents) in many detergents absorb UV (about 340-370 nm) and emit blue light (about 420-470 nm).
- Blue emission offsets yellowing and adds reflected light, so fabrics appear whiter; brighteners stay on fabric after rinsing.
- Brightener-treated fabrics fluoresce under black (UV) lights.
- Laundry bluing (a blue dye/pigment such as Prussian blue) was used from the 19th century to counter yellowing.
