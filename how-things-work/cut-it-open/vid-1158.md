## Fact check

- Engine thermostats are wax-pellet valves: a sealed wax charge melts and expands with temperature, pushing a pin to open the valve against a spring.
- Typical opening temperatures are about 82-95 °C ('around ninety').
- Closed thermostats keep coolant out of the radiator for faster warm-up (cold engines have higher wear and emissions); a stuck-open unit causes slow warm-up, a stuck-closed one overheating.
