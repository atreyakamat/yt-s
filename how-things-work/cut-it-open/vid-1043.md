## Fact check

- Disposable air-activated hand warmers contain iron powder, water, salt, activated carbon and vermiculite (a fluffy mineral that holds water).
- They're sealed airtight; opening lets oxygen reach the iron.
- Iron oxidation (rusting) is exothermic; the fine powder's large surface area and the salt catalyst make it fast enough to feel.
- They typically stay warm for several hours (often quoted up to around 8-10).
