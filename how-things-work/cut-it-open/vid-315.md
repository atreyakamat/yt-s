## Fact check

- Biological detergents contain enzymes: proteases (protein stains like blood, egg, grass), amylases (starch), lipases (fats), and others.
- Enzymes are catalysts: they are not consumed and can act on many substrate molecules.
- Detergent enzymes work well in roughly the 30-40 °C range; high heat denatures many enzymes.
- Industrial enzymes are produced by fermentation with microorganisms and supplied as encapsulated granules to reduce dust.
