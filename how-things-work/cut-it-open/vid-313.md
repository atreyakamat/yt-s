## Fact check

- Main ingredients → sodium bicarbonate and citric acid, often with corn starch, oils, fragrance and colour.
- Reaction needs water → the solids only react once dissolved.
- Products → the acid-base reaction releases carbon dioxide (plus water and sodium citrate).
- Baking powder → also combines sodium bicarbonate with an acid to release CO2.
- Starch → used as a filler/binder that slows dissolving.
