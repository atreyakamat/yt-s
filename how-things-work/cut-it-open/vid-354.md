## Fact check

- The Etch A Sketch screen is coated on the inside with aluminum powder mixed with small plastic beads that help it flow.
- A stylus behind the glass is moved by a system of strings and pulleys connected to the two knobs, one horizontal and one vertical.
- The stylus scrapes powder off the glass, leaving a dark line where you see into the unlit interior.
- Shaking re-coats the glass with powder, erasing the picture.
- Etch A Sketch is the toy's common name, so the brand is the topic.
