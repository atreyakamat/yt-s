## Fact check

- Hard hats have a rigid shell and a suspension harness that holds the shell away from the head, leaving a clearance gap (a few centimetres).
- The shell distributes impact load; the suspension stretches, increasing stopping distance and reducing the force reaching the head.
- Shells often have a ridge for stiffness and a brim to shed debris and rain.
- Manufacturers and safety regulators advise replacing a hard hat after any significant impact, even without visible damage.
