## Fact check

- Skateboard and inline skate wheels typically hold two ball bearings each, with a spacer between them.
- A ball bearing has inner and outer races (rings), a set of steel balls, a cage/retainer keeping them spaced, and usually shields.
- Rolling contact has far lower friction than a sliding bushing.
- The standard '608' bearing used in most skates and skateboards is 22 mm outer diameter, 8 mm bore, 7 mm wide.
- Dirt and moisture cause grinding and corrosion, slowing bearings.
