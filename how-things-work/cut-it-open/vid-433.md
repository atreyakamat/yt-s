## Fact check

- Thousands of sweat glands per armpit → eccrine and apocrine glands open via ducts onto the skin.
- Aluminium salts (e.g. aluminium chlorohydrate) → the active ingredients in antiperspirants.
- Gel plug → the salts dissolve in sweat and precipitate as a gel that temporarily blocks the upper duct.
- Temporary → plugs are cleared by skin renewal and washing.
- Deodorants target odour, not sweat volume.
