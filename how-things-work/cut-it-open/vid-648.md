## Fact check

- Suspension springs carry the car's weight and absorb bumps; an undamped spring oscillates for a long time.
- Shock absorbers (dampers) are hydraulic: a piston with small orifices/valves forces oil to flow, converting motion energy into heat.
- Worn dampers allow excess body movement and reduce tyre contact on rough roads, lowering grip.
