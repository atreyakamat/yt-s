## Fact check

- Flow restrictors → small discs or regulators in the inlet limit flow rate.
- Flow rates → older/unrestricted shower heads often deliver 15 L/min or more; water-saving heads commonly 6-8 L/min (7 shown).
- Venturi effect → fast-moving water has lower pressure (Bernoulli), drawing air in through side ports in aerating heads.
- Less hot water → less energy to heat it.
