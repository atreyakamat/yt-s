## Fact check

- Odometers infer distance from rotations of the wheels or the gearbox output shaft.
- A typical car tyre is about 0.6-0.7 m in diameter, so one revolution is about 2 m.
- Mechanical odometers were driven by a flexible cable from the gearbox through worm gears to a stack of number drums; a carry pin advances the next drum after 9.
- Electronic odometers count pulses from a speed sensor and store totals in non-volatile memory.
- Larger-circumference tyres travel further per revolution, so the odometer under-reads unless recalibrated.
