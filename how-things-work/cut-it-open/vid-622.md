## Fact check

- Blowtorch canisters hold propane, butane or a mix as a liquid under pressure; opening the valve lets it vaporise and exit through a small jet.
- The jet entrains air through side ports (venturi/Bunsen principle) so gas and air premix before combustion.
- Piezo igniters use a spring-loaded hammer striking a piezoelectric crystal, generating a high voltage spark.
- Propane-air flames reach roughly 1,900-2,000 °C at their hottest; solder melts around 183-220 °C.
- A blue flame indicates good air mixing (complete combustion); a yellow, sooty flame indicates too little air.
