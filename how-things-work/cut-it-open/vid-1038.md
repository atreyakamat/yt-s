## Fact check

- Power windows use an electric motor driving a window regulator (cable-and-pulley or scissor/arm type).
- Anti-pinch systems monitor motor current and/or speed (often via Hall sensors); a sudden rise in load during closing triggers reversal.
- Regulations such as US FMVSS 118 and UN ECE R21 require automatic reversal for power windows that close automatically (one-touch up).
