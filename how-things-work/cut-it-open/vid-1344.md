## Fact check

- A kazoo is a tube with a hole covered by a thin membrane (plastic film or waxed paper) under a small turret.
- It is played by humming or vocalizing into it; simply blowing produces little or no sound.
- The voice vibrates the membrane, which adds a buzzing timbre (a mirliton effect).
- The pitch comes entirely from the player's voice.
