## Fact check

- Nylon-insert lock nuts (often called Nyloc) have an unthreaded nylon collar with an inner diameter slightly smaller than the bolt's major diameter.
- The bolt threads deform the nylon, which grips them elastically and adds prevailing torque, resisting vibration loosening; they feel stiff throughout turning once engaged.
- Nylon inserts are typically limited to about 120 °C; all-metal prevailing-torque nuts are used for higher temperatures.
- Locking performance decreases with reuse; many guidelines recommend replacing them.
