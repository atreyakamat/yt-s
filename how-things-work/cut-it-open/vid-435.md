## Fact check

- Plug-in liquid air fresheners use a porous wick that draws fragrance oil up by capillary action; a small resistive heater warms the wick tip to speed evaporation.
- On many common models the intensity setting raises or lowers the wick relative to the heater rather than changing the heater's power.
- Basic plug-ins have no fan or motor.
