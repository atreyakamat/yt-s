## Fact check

- Spiral (helix) vending machines hold products between the coils of a metal spiral; a motor rotates the spiral one full turn per vend, advancing items one pitch so the front one drops.
- Spirals come in different pitches (gap sizes) to suit product sizes.
- A snack that catches on the end of the coil after one turn is the classic stuck-item case.
