## Fact check

- Film coatings are thin polymer layers (often with pigments) applied to tablets to mask taste, protect from moisture/light, ease swallowing and aid identification.
- Enteric coatings use pH-dependent polymers that resist dissolving in the acidic stomach (pH roughly 1.5-3.5) and dissolve at the higher pH of the small intestine (around 6 and above). Bars show relative acidity, illustrative.
- They protect the stomach from irritating drugs or protect acid-sensitive drugs; crushing defeats the coating, which is why some labels say not to crush. Educational only, not medical advice.
