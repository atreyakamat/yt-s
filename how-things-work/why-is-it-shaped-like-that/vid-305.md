## Fact check

- A round cone presents the same silhouette from any direction.
- A wide base and tapered top give a low centre of mass; many cones have weighted rubber bases.
- Hollow tapered cones nest for compact stacking and transport.
- Geometry: a cone lying on its side rolls in a circle around its apex rather than in a straight line.
- Cones are made of flexible PVC or rubber so vehicles can strike them without serious damage.
