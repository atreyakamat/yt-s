## Fact check

- On a slope, the component of gravity along the ramp grows with steepness, so the push needed at each moment rises. Bars are illustrative.
- Accessibility standards such as the US ADA set a maximum running slope of 1:12 for ramps; UK guidance uses similar or gentler gradients depending on length.
- At 1:12, a 15 cm rise needs 180 cm of ramp.
- Standards require level landings at intervals and at turns, and handrails and edge protection on longer ramps.
