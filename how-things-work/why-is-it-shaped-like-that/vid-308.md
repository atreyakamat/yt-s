## Fact check

- The foot meets the leg at roughly a right angle at the ankle.
- Tube socks have no heel and tend to rotate and bunch: commonly observed.
- Heeled socks are knitted with short rows (partial rows that shorten then lengthen) to form a heel pocket, the same method hand knitters use for a 'short-row heel'.
- The heel pocket anchors the sock's orientation on the foot.
