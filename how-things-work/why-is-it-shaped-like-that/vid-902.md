## Fact check

- Convective heat transfer to air is proportional to surface area, so fins increase heat dissipation.
- Fin spacing trades surface area against airflow resistance; too-dense fins choke airflow.
- Thermal conductivity: copper ≈ 400 W/m·K, aluminium ≈ 205 W/m·K; aluminium is lighter and cheaper; many coolers use copper bases/heat pipes with aluminium fins.
