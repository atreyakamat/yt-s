## Fact check

- Speakers radiate sound by moving air; grilles must provide enough open area for acoustic output.
- Micro-speaker diaphragms are very thin; perforated grilles protect them mechanically while multiple small holes keep total open area.
- Phones place acoustic mesh (often hydrophobic) behind grilles to resist dust and water.
- Phone makers suggest cleaning openings with a soft, dry brush.
