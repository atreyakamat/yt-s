## Fact check

- Angled brooms are cut so the bristle edge lies flat when the broom is held at the natural sweeping angle, and the point reaches corners and edges.
- 'Flagged' bristles have split tips that increase surface area and pick up fine dust; unflagged stiff bristles suit coarse debris and outdoor use.
