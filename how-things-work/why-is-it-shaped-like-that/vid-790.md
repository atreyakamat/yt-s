## Fact check

- Skin continuously loses water (transepidermal water loss); occlusive backings trap moisture, which can macerate skin and weaken adhesion.
- Many plastic plasters are perforated so moisture vapour and air can pass through.
- Adhesive peel starts at edges; sharp corners concentrate peel force, so rounded corners resist lifting. Bars are illustrative.
