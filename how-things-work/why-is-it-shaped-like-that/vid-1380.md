## Fact check

- Safety matches put red phosphorus on the striking surface and potassium chlorate/sulfur on the head; neither half ignites easily alone.
- Matchboxes have striking strips on the outer sides and a sliding inner tray, keeping heads away from the strip.
- Early friction matches (1826) and later strike-anywhere matches carried all reactive chemicals on the head and could light on rough surfaces; safety matches spread from the 1850s.
