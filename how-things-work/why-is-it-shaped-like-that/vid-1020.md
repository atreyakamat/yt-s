## Fact check

- Scrub sponges laminate an absorbent foam (polyurethane or cellulose) to a non-woven scouring pad of nylon/polyester fibres bonded with resin containing abrasive mineral particles (e.g. aluminium oxide).
- Many brands colour-code abrasiveness, commonly green for heavy-duty and blue/white for non-scratch, but codes vary by brand, hence 'often'.
- Abrasive pads can scratch non-stick coatings, which is why cookware makers advise non-scratch sponges.
