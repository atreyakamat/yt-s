## Fact check

- Ring lifebuoys can be grabbed from any side and slipped over the head and arms to support a person in the water.
- They are typically filled with closed-cell foam and carry an outside grab line, with a throwing line attached at stations; SOLAS-type buoys use bright orange and retroreflective tape.
- Removing the centre reduces weight for throwing. Bars are illustrative.
