## Fact check

- Cold air is denser than warm air and sinks; opening a front door lets cold air spill out at the bottom, while an open-top chest retains it.
- Less air exchange means less moisture entering (less frost) and less energy to recover; chest freezers are generally more energy efficient than uprights.
- Chest freezers generally hold temperature longer during power cuts, especially when kept closed and full.
