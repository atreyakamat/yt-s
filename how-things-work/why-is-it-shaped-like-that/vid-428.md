## Fact check

- Dry friction for rubber depends strongly on real contact area with the surface.
- On wet surfaces a smooth sole can ride on a water film (hydroplaning), the same principle as tyre aquaplaning; grooves channel water away.
- Herringbone (zigzag) outsoles are standard on basketball and court shoes for multi-directional grip.
- Trail running shoes use deep lugs for traction in mud and soft ground.
