## Fact check

- Carpenter's pencils have a flat (rectangular or oval) body so they don't roll off sloped or uneven surfaces.
- The lead is a flat rectangular strip, so it can draw a wide or narrow line depending on orientation.
- The thick lead is strong and resists breaking on rough surfaces.
- They don't fit standard sharpeners and are usually sharpened with a knife, producing a chisel point.
