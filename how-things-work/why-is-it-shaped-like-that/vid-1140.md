## Fact check

- Tea lights are small enough that the whole wax charge liquefies while burning, so they are sold in thin aluminium or plastic cups.
- The wick is crimped into a metal sustainer tab that holds it upright in liquid wax and stops the flame when the wax is used up.
- They are named after their use in teapot warmers; typical burn time is around 4 hours.
