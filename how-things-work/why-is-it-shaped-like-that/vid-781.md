## Fact check

- Mains is about 230 V in many countries (120 V in North America); laptops commonly take around 19-20 V DC input (USB-C PD up to 20 V or more).
- Laptop adapters are switch-mode supplies with transformers, capacitors and controllers; conversion losses produce heat.
- Placing the supply outside the laptop keeps heat, size and mains voltage out of the chassis; an in-line brick keeps the wall plug compact.
