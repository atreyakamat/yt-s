## Fact check

- A spring latch bolt has a bevelled face on the closing side; the strike plate pushes it in as the door closes and the spring extends it into the strike hole.
- The flat face then bears on the strike, so pulling the door doesn't retract it; the handle retracts it via the spindle and cam (follower).
- A deadbolt is square-ended and spring-free in that sense, thrown and withdrawn only by key or thumb-turn, so it can't be pushed back.
