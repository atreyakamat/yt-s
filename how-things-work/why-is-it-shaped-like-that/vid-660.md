## Fact check

- Dustpans have a thin, tapered leading edge (often a flexible rubber lip) so they sit flush and debris doesn't pass under the edge.
- The pan floor rises toward a hooded back, and raised sides and hood reduce spilling.
- Many long-handled (lobby) dustpans pivot so the pan tips back when lifted.
