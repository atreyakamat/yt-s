## Fact check

- Visors (hoods) shade signal lenses from direct sun, which can otherwise cause 'sun phantom' where an unlit lens appears lit, and restrict viewing angles so adjacent approaches don't see the wrong signal.
- Black backplates improve contrast; retroreflective borders on backplates (promoted by the US FHWA) keep signals visible at night when power fails.
- Vertical signals put red on top in a fixed order, which helps colour-blind drivers read them by position.
