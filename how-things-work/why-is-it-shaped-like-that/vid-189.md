## Fact check

- The classic ball is a truncated icosahedron: 12 pentagons and 20 hexagons, 32 panels in all.
- Regular hexagons tile a flat plane; a closed shape needs pentagons to create curvature.
- By Euler's formula, any closed polyhedron made only of pentagons and hexagons with three faces at each corner has exactly 12 pentagons.
- Inflation pushes the flat panels outward so the ball becomes close to spherical.
- The black-and-white 32-panel design was popularized by the 1970 World Cup ball, chosen to be visible on black-and-white television.
- The C60 'buckyball' molecule (buckminsterfullerene) has the same truncated icosahedron shape.
