## Fact check

- Salad spinners spin a perforated basket inside a solid bowl, usually via a crank, pull cord or pump with gearing.
- By inertia, objects tend to move in straight lines; the basket wall supplies the inward force that keeps leaves turning, while water passes through the slots and is flung to the outer bowl.
- Dressing adheres better to dry leaves because water dilutes and repels oil-based dressings.
