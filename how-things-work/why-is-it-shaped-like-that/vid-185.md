## Fact check

- Structural steel expands about 12 millionths of its length per °C; over a 40 °C seasonal swing a 100 m span changes by about 4.8 cm, so 'about five centimetres'.
- Finger (comb) expansion joints use interlocking steel teeth so the gap can open and close while wheels stay supported across it.
- Daily temperature changes make bridges expand and contract every day.
