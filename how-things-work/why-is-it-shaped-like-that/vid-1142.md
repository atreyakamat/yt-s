## Fact check

- Many keycaps have a cylindrical or spherical dish to cradle and centre the fingertip.
- Sculpted keycap profiles give each row a different height and angle to follow finger reach; some profiles are uniform instead (hence 'many keyboards').
- Space bars are often flat or convex since thumbs strike them differently.
- Space is the most frequently typed character in typical text.
