## Fact check

- BS 1363 (UK) plugs: the earth pin is longer than the live and neutral pins.
- BS 1363 sockets have shutters over the live and neutral apertures that are opened by the earth pin pushing on a mechanism as it enters.
- Because it is longer, the earth makes contact before and breaks contact after the live/neutral pins.
