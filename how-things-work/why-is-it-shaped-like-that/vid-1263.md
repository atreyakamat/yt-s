## Fact check

- Many wheel nuts and bolts use a conical (commonly 60°) seat matching tapered holes in the wheel; tightening centres the wheel on the hub and creates a firm friction lock.
- Other designs use spherical (ball) or flat seats; mismatched seat types don't seat correctly and can loosen.
- Tightening in a star/criss-cross pattern pulls the wheel down evenly.
