## Fact check

- Convex mirrors give a wider field of view at the cost of reduced image size, which makes objects seem further away.
- US rules require passenger-side convex mirrors to carry the warning 'Objects in mirror are closer than they appear'.
- Aspheric mirrors, common in Europe, have an outer zone with stronger curvature to reduce the blind spot.
- Which mirrors are convex varies by country and car, hence 'many' and 'some'.
