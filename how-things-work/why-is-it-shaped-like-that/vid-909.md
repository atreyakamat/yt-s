## Fact check

- American football developed from rugby; early balls were inflated pig bladders in leather, giving a rounded egg shape.
- The forward pass was legalized in 1906; the ball was made slimmer in later rule changes (notably 1912 and 1934) to make passing easier.
- A spiral spins about the long axis; gyroscopic stability keeps the nose pointed and stops tumbling.
- A ball moving nose-first presents a small cross-section and lower drag than a tumbling one.
