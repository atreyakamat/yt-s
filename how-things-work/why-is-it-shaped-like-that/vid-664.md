## Fact check

- Ballasted track consists of rails on sleepers (ties) laid in a bed of crushed stone ballast.
- Ballast distributes loads to the subgrade, holds sleepers against lateral and longitudinal movement, and provides drainage.
- Angular crushed stone interlocks; rounded stones would roll and shift under load.
- Ballast discourages vegetation growth (it is also often treated to control weeds).
