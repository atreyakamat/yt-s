## Fact check

- 'Toe spring' is the footwear term for the upward curve of the sole at the toe.
- Walking gait rolls from heel strike to toe-off, bending at the metatarsophalangeal joints (ball of the foot).
- Stiff soles resist bending; toe spring provides a rocker so the shoe rolls through toe-off: standard footwear design rationale.
- The raised toe also adds clearance during swing, reducing scuffing.
