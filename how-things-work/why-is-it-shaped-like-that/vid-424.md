## Fact check

- Typical airliner cruise is around Mach 0.78-0.85.
- Airflow accelerates over the wing's upper surface and can become locally supersonic, forming shock waves that sharply increase drag (wave drag) and can cause buffet.
- Sweeping the wing reduces the component of airflow perpendicular to the leading edge, delaying shock formation to a higher flight speed.
- The 'air over the wing' bar shows the local flow reaching about Mach 1, a simplification.
