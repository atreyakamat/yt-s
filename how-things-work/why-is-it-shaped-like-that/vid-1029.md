## Fact check

- The standard 'clock' dartboard order is 20, 1, 18, 4, 13, 6, 10, 15, 2, 17, 3, 19, 7, 16, 8, 11, 14, 9, 12, 5, so 20 sits between 5 and 1.
- High and low numbers alternate around the board to penalize inaccuracy.
- Treble 20 scores 60, the highest single-dart score; three gives 180. The inner bull is 50, so three bulls make 150.
- The layout is commonly attributed to Brian Gamlin in 1896, but the attribution is disputed.
