## Fact check

- Internal pressure loads a vessel's walls; in a cylinder or sphere the wall is in fairly even tension, while flat walls bend and corners concentrate stress.
- Spheres are the most efficient pressure-vessel shape, but cylinders with domed ends are practical to make, store and carry.
- Aerosol cans, gas cylinders and scuba tanks all use cylindrical bodies with curved ends for this reason. Bars are illustrative.
