## Fact check

- Lattice towers are triangulated steel frameworks; triangles are rigid shapes that can't deform without changing a side length.
- An open lattice presents far less area to the wind and uses less steel than a solid structure of the same strength.
- Lattice towers are delivered as bolted steel members and assembled on site, which suits remote terrain.
- Cross-arms and insulators keep conductors separated from each other and from the tower; many pylons carry an earth wire at the top for lightning protection.
