## Fact check

- Joseph Friedman invented the bendy straw after watching his young daughter struggle with a straight straw at a soda fountain; he inserted a screw, wrapped dental floss into the threads to press corrugations into the paper, and patented it in 1937.
- The corrugations let the tube bend without kinking shut, like an accordion.
- Hospitals were early major customers (for patients drinking while lying down); his company sold to hospitals in the late 1940s-1950s.
