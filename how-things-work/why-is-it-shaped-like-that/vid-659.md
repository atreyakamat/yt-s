## Fact check

- François Blondel's Cours d'architecture (1670s-80s) gave the rule 2 x rise + going = about 24 French inches (about 63-65 cm), based on a person's stride.
- Modern codes still use it: UK Approved Document K requires 2R + G between 550 and 700 mm; about 60 cm is a typical target.
- Building codes require uniform risers and goings within a flight because people adapt their gait after the first steps and trip on irregular ones.
