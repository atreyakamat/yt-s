## Fact check

- In cup noodles the dried noodle block is held partway down a tapered cup rather than sitting on the bottom (the cup was introduced in 1971).
- The suspended block reinforces the cup and protects the noodles in transit; water fills the space below so noodles rehydrate evenly.
- The packing method places the inverted cup over the noodle block, then flips it, so the block wedges in place.
