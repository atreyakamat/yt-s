## Fact check

- More grout lines = more slip resistance → common tiling guidance for wet floors; grout lines add texture.
- Shower floors slope to the drain → required for drainage; centre drains create a shallow funnel shape.
- Small tiles follow multi-way slopes → large rigid tiles can't conform to a funnel slope without cutting, so they leave lips (lippage) or rock.
