## Fact check

- Most highlighters use a chisel-shaped fibre tip that gives a broad stroke on the flat face and a fine line on the edge.
- Typical line widths are roughly 1 mm (edge) to about 5 mm (face); exact widths vary by product.
- Fibre (felt) tips wick ink from the reservoir by capillary action.
