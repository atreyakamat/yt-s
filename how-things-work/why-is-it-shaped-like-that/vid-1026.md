## Fact check

- Plastic films resist tearing when intact but tear easily once a notch is present, because stress concentrates at the notch tip.
- Dispenser teeth are serrated points that initiate many small tears which link into one cut.
- The same principle is used for tear notches on packets and serrated edges on foil and film boxes.
