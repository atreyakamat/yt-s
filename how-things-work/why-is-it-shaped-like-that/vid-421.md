## Fact check

- 3.5 mm audio plugs use the TRS (tip-ring-sleeve) layout: tip = left, ring = right, sleeve = ground/common.
- Insulating plastic bands separate the conductive sections.
- TRRS plugs add another ring for a microphone (shown as three insulating bands).
- The jack has separate spring contacts aligned with each section.
