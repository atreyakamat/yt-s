## Fact check

- Human horizontal visual field is close to 180 degrees (roughly 180-200 degrees with both eyes).
- Flat-front frames leave side gaps where peripheral light and UV can enter; wrap styles reduce this.
- Wrap lenses shield eyes from wind, dust and debris, which is why cyclists and skiers use them.
- Highly curved lenses can introduce optical distortion unless they are designed/decentred to compensate.
