## Fact check

- Escalator steps have longitudinal cleats (grooves); comb plates at the landings have teeth that mesh with them.
- The meshing comb guides feet and objects off the step and minimises the gap where the step goes under the landing.
- Comb plate impact devices / safety switches stop the escalator if an object jams the comb.
