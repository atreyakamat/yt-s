## Fact check

- Steam from hot pizza softens the corrugated lid, which can sag onto the toppings.
- The three-legged plastic 'package saver' (pizza saver) was patented in 1985 by Carmela Vitale of Dix Hills, New York (filed 1983).
- A tripod is the minimum number of legs for a stable stand on any surface.
