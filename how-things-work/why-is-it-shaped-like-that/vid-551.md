## Fact check

- Serrated edges concentrate force on the tips of the teeth, which lets them pierce a hard crust or skin with little downward pressure.
- The scalloped edges between points cut in a sawing motion, so soft bread is not compressed.
- Serrated knives are commonly recommended for tomatoes and other foods with a tough skin and soft interior.
