## Fact check

- A crowbar is a first-class lever; the curved (gooseneck) end acts as the fulcrum against the surface, and rolls so the fulcrum point shifts as it lifts.
- Force multiplication = distance from fulcrum to hand / distance from fulcrum to load; the 10x figure is an illustrative example.
- The flattened, split tip acts as a wedge and as a nail claw.
- Wrecking/pry bars come in lengths up to about 60 inches (about 1.5 m).
