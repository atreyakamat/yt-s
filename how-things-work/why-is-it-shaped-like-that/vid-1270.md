## Fact check

- ISO 7010 / ISO 3864 safety signs use: yellow triangle with black border for warning; red circle with diagonal bar for prohibition; blue circle for mandatory action; green square/rectangle for safe condition; red square/rectangle for fire equipment.
- Shape and colour coding lets signs be recognised quickly and across languages. Bars are illustrative.
