## Fact check

- When a funnel spout seals a bottle neck, air displaced by incoming liquid cannot escape easily, so it bubbles up through the spout and the flow stalls ('glugging') or overflows.
- Many funnels have ribs or grooves on the outside of the spout to keep an air gap against the neck, venting the displaced air.
- Lifting the funnel slightly does the same job.
