## Fact check

- Load-bearing brickwork is laid in a bond with staggered perpendicular joints; continuous vertical joints (stack bond) are structurally weak and only used decoratively or with reinforcement, hence 'never' for normal load-bearing walls.
- Stretcher bond offsets each course by half a brick, distributing loads to bricks below.
- In solid walls, headers (bricks laid end-out) tie the leaves together; Flemish bond alternates headers and stretchers in each course.
