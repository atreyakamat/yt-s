## Fact check

- Railway wheelsets have both wheels rigidly fixed to one axle, so they turn at the same rate.
- On a curve the outer rail is longer than the inner rail.
- Wheel treads are coned (tapered), larger in diameter toward the flange.
- When the wheelset shifts outward on a curve, the outer wheel runs on a larger rolling radius, so it covers more distance per turn and the wheelset steers itself. Flanges act as a last line of guidance.
