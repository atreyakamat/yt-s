## Fact check

- Bottle makers have 'lightweighted' PET water bottles substantially over recent decades to use less plastic (bars are an illustration of relative wall thickness, not measurements).
- Thin cylindrical walls dent and buckle easily; circumferential ribs add hoop stiffness, like hoops on a barrel, and corrugation stiffens thin sheet (as in corrugated iron).
- Ribs and texture also improve grip.
