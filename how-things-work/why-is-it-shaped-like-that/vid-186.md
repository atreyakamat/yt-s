## Fact check

- A4 is 210 × 297 mm; A-series sheets have sides in the ratio 1:√2 (≈1.414).
- Halving a 1:√2 rectangle gives another 1:√2 rectangle; A0 has an area of 1 m², and A4 is A0 halved four times.
- Georg Christoph Lichtenberg described the advantages of the √2 ratio in 1786; Germany standardised it as DIN 476 in 1922; it became international standard ISO 216 in 1975.
