## Fact check

- At highway speeds aerodynamic drag is the largest resistive force on a typical car, exceeding rolling resistance.
- Aerodynamic drag force scales with the square of speed (doubling speed gives 4x the force).
- A teardrop/streamlined body minimises flow separation; Wunibald Kamm showed that abruptly truncating the tail (Kamm tail) keeps most of the benefit.
- Drag coefficients: a brick-like shape about 1.0, boxy 1970s cars around 0.45-0.5, modern cars about 0.25-0.30.
