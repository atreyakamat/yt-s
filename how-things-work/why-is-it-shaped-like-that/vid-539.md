## Fact check

- Windows, especially single glazing, have the lowest surface temperature in most rooms.
- Air cooled by the glass becomes denser and falls, creating a cold downdraught across the floor.
- A radiator below the window heats that air as it falls, counteracting the draught and driving a convection loop round the room; this is the classic reason for under-window placement.
- Wall space under windows is otherwise hard to use for furniture.
