## Fact check

- Overflow channel → most bathroom basins have a built-in overflow passage leading to the waste outlet.
- Prevents overflowing → it drains water once the level reaches the hole (if the flow is not more than it can take).
- Air path speeds draining → the overflow vents air into the waste so the draining water doesn't create a partial vacuum and glug.
- Collects grime → the channel is hidden and gets soap scum and biofilm; rinsing is a common cleaning tip.
