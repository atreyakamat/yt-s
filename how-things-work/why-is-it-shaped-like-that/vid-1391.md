## Fact check

- A rolling blade contacts the food at a small area, concentrating force (high pressure) to cut through the crust.
- Because the wheel rolls rather than slides, there is little sideways drag on cheese and toppings, unlike a sawing knife.
- A circular blade presents continuous edge for long cuts; most cutters have a guard over the wheel.
