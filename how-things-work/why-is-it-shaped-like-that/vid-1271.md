## Fact check

- Western chef's knives have a curved 'belly' rising to the tip, enabling a rocking chop with the tip kept on the board.
- The tall blade gives knuckle clearance above the cutting board.
- The heel, nearest the handle, is the thickest and strongest section and is used for heavy cuts; the tip for fine work.
- Blades taper from spine to edge, acting as a wedge.
