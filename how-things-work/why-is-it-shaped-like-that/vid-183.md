## Fact check

- The black border is a ceramic 'frit' printed on and fused to the glass during manufacture; it provides a bonding surface for the urethane adhesive.
- The frit hides the adhesive and protects it from UV light, which degrades urethane.
- The halftone dot gradient softens the transition, reducing uneven heating/thermal stress at the band edge (and optical distortion), and looks neater. These are the standard explanations given by glass makers.
