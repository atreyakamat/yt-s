## Fact check

- The crown is used to wind mechanical watches and set the time/date.
- About 10% of people are left-handed; most people wear a watch on the non-dominant (usually left) wrist.
- A crown at 3 o'clock is reachable by the right hand when worn on the left wrist and stays clear of the back of the hand when the wrist bends.
- 'Destro' or left-handed watches put the crown at 9 o'clock for wearing on the right wrist.
