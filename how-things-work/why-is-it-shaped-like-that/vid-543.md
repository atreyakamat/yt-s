## Fact check

- Older brake pad compounds could outgas at high temperature, forming a boundary layer that reduced friction; cross-drilling and slotting were introduced to vent gas, water and debris.
- Slots continually wipe/deglaze the pad surface; drilling also reduces mass and adds cooling edges.
- Cross-drilled discs are prone to stress cracks originating at the holes under repeated heavy heat cycles, so many motorsport discs use slots (or plain vented discs) instead.
