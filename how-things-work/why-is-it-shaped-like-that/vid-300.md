## Fact check

- Ironing boards taper so garments (shirt shoulders, trouser waists, sleeves) can be pulled over the narrow end.
- Most board tops are perforated metal mesh, letting steam pass through rather than condensing on the board.
- A padded cover cushions seams and buttons so they don't imprint through the fabric.
- Many boards have a heat-resistant iron rest at the wide end.
