## Fact check

- Water pressure increases with depth; your chest is squeezed while the air in a snorkel stays at surface pressure.
- Breathing muscles can only overcome a small pressure difference; breathing through a tube becomes very difficult or impossible at around a meter down.
- A snorkel adds 'dead space': exhaled air left in the tube is re-inhaled, and a longer tube means more of it.
- Snorkels are therefore kept short, just long enough to clear the surface.
