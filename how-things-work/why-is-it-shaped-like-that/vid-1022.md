## Fact check

- QR codes have three finder patterns at three corners with a 1:1:3:1:1 dark-light-dark-light-dark ratio, detectable from any scan direction.
- Using three rather than four finder patterns lets the decoder determine orientation.
- Timing patterns (alternating modules between finder patterns) give module size and grid coordinates.
- Together these allow decoding at any rotation and at a tilt.
