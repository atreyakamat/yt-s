## Fact check

- Heat moves into cake batter from the pan walls; in a wide tin the centre is last to set, so dense batters risk a raw middle and dry edges.
- A tube pan's central column conducts heat into the middle of the cake, making the batter a ring that sits close to hot metal everywhere.
- Fluting increases the metal surface in contact with batter and gives the decorative shape.
- Ring/tube cakes such as the Gugelhupf (kugelhopf) are traditional in Central Europe; the 'Bundt' pan was made by Nordic Ware in Minneapolis in 1950.
