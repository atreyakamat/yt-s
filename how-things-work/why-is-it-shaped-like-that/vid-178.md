## Fact check

- Hollow flushing rim with holes → traditional toilet bowls have a hollow rim fed by the cistern, with holes or slots that release water around the bowl.
- Siphon jet → many (especially North American siphonic) bowls have a jet near the bottom that helps start the siphon; 'many', not all (washdown toilets don't).
- Rimless toilets → a newer design that uses a shaped spreader instead of a hollow rim, marketed as easier to clean.
