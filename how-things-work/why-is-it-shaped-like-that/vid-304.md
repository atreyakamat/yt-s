## Fact check

- Lift involves lower pressure above the wing and higher pressure below it.
- At the wingtip, air flows from the high-pressure underside to the low-pressure top, forming wingtip vortices that cause induced drag.
- Winglets reduce the strength of these vortices and so reduce induced drag.
- Airlines and manufacturers commonly cite fuel savings of around 3-5% from winglets, so 'a few percent' is fair.
