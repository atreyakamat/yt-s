## Fact check

- At depth, ground pressure acts all round a tunnel; a circular lining carries it mainly as compression (hoop thrust), like an arch closed into a ring, avoiding stress concentrations at corners.
- Tunnel boring machines have a rotating cutterhead, so they excavate a circular bore; precast concrete segments are bolted into rings behind the shield as the machine advances.
- Shallow cut-and-cover tunnels are often rectangular, which is why the script says deep tunnels.
