## Fact check

- Terry cloth loops → bath towels are terry weave, with extra warp yarn forming uncut loops (pile) on both sides.
- Loops add surface area → more fibre exposed to water than a flat weave.
- Capillary action → water wicks into the small spaces between cotton fibres.
- Cotton holds many times its weight → cotton is highly absorbent; no exact figure given.
- Fabric softener reduces towel absorbency → softeners leave a waxy/cationic coating; towel makers commonly advise using little or none.
