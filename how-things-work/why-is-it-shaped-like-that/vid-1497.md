## Fact check

- Food can bodies are made of thin tinplate or steel; the circumferential ribs are called beads.
- Cans are heat-processed after sealing and then cooled, which creates internal vacuum; external pressure can cause thin smooth walls to panel or buckle.
- Beading increases hoop stiffness, resisting implosion and denting without extra metal.
- Pressurised carbonated drink cans are held rigid by internal gas pressure so don't need beads.
