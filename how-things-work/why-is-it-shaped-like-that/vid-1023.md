## Fact check

- Brake pedals are typically larger than accelerator pedals so they are easy to find quickly and can take high, sustained force; on automatics the wide pad is reachable with either foot.
- The narrower (often floor-hinged) accelerator lets the driver rest the heel and pivot for fine control.
- Distinct pedal sizes and positions help the driver identify pedals by feel; these are standard ergonomic design reasons.
