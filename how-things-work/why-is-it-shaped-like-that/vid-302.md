## Fact check

- 8P8C ('RJ45') plugs have a flexible latch tab that locks into the jack and is pressed to release.
- The plug body is usually clear polycarbonate, letting installers check wire order.
- Ethernet cable has 8 conductors in 4 twisted pairs (orange, green, blue, brown); twisting reduces interference and crosstalk.
- Snagless boots protect the latch from catching and breaking.
