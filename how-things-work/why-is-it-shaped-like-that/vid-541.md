## Fact check

- Punch-hole (hole-punch) displays have a cut-out through the OLED stack for the front camera.
- Display layers (pixels, transistors, wiring, polariser) block and scatter light, which degrades image quality for a camera behind them.
- Phones moved from bezels to notches (popular around 2017-2018) to punch holes; under-display cameras exist but generally produce softer images.
