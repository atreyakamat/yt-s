## Fact check

- Pliers are a pair of first-class levers joined at a pivot; force at the jaws = hand force x (handle distance / jaw distance from the pivot).
- The bar values are an example: a point 5 times closer to the pivot than the hand gets 5 times the force; real ratios vary by design.
- Combination pliers place the wire cutters right beside the joint where leverage is greatest, with gripping jaws and a rounded section further out.
- Serrated jaws increase grip on smooth or round objects.
