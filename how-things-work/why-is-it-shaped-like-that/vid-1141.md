## Fact check

- Most remote controls use a single moulded silicone rubber keypad with collapsible domes.
- Each dome has a conductive carbon pill on its underside that bridges interdigitated contacts on the PCB when pressed.
- Carbon pills/contacts wear and get contaminated over time, causing unresponsive buttons; rubber keypads are cheap, quiet and fairly sealed.
