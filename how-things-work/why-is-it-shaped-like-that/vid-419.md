## Fact check

- A frog is the indentation in one or both bed faces of a pressed or moulded brick.
- It reduces the clay needed and the brick's weight, and helps more even drying and firing.
- Laid frog up and filled with mortar, it forms a key that improves bond and strength (common UK guidance).
- Brickmakers commonly impressed their name or initials in the frog.
