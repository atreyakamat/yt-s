## Fact check

- Euro banknote lengths: €5 120 mm, €10 127 mm, €20 133 mm, €50 140 mm, €100 147 mm (the €200 is 153 mm); sizes and colours differ to help visually impaired users.
- US banknotes are all the same size; this has been the subject of accessibility complaints and litigation.
- Many banknotes use raised intaglio print that can be felt.
