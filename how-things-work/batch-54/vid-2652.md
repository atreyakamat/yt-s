## Fact check

- Hot water is less dense than cold and stratifies above it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermal_stratification
- Tall, narrow storage tanks promote stratification, so hot water is drawn from the top. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_water_storage_tank
- Inlets are designed to limit mixing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_water_storage_tank
- Bar values are illustrative, not measured.
