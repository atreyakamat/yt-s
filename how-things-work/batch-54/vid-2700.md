## Fact check

- Afterimages occur because photochemical activity in the retina continues after the stimulus ends; negative afterimages are shaped by neural adaptation and show complementary colours; a green image gives a magenta afterimage. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afterimage
- Opponent-process theory describes four channels: red/cyan, green/magenta, blue/yellow, black/white. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afterimage
- Afterimages are likelier with intense, large images viewed with a very steady eye because microsaccades can't move the image onto unadapted retina; staring for 5-60 seconds produces a negative afterimage. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afterimage
