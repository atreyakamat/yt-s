## Fact check

- A coil carries alternating current, creating an alternating magnetic field; nearby conductive metal produces eddy currents that generate a magnetic field of their own; a second coil senses the change and the control box signals with a tone, number, light or needle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_detector
- Tunable induction detectors can distinguish metals by phase response; some metals (tinfoil and gold) have similar responses and discrimination has limits. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_detector
