## Fact check

- An autorecloser is a circuit breaker that automatically closes again after a trip, since most overhead faults are temporary. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autorecloser
- Recloser dead times are often a fraction of a second to a few seconds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autorecloser
- After a set number of failed attempts the recloser locks out. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autorecloser
- Grid icon highlight (17 of 20) is illustrative of 'most', not a statistic.
