## Fact check

- Earthshine is sunlight reflected off Earth's surface and clouds that lights the Moon's night side; Leonardo da Vinci sketched and remarked on it in the 16th century and thought oceans reflected it, but clouds contribute more; best seen a few days before or after new moon at dusk or dawn; waxing crescent in the western evening sky, waning crescent before dawn. Full Earth far brighter than a full Moon: earthlight maximum magnitude -17.7 seen from the Moon vs the full Moon about -12.7 (standard figure not on this page; script says 'many times brighter'). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earthshine
