## Fact check

- The blind spot is where the optic nerve leaves the retina, with no photoreceptor cells; roughly 7.5 degrees high and 5.5 degrees wide; about 12-15 degrees temporally. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blind_spot_(vision)
- The brain interpolates the gap from surrounding detail and input from the other eye; first documented by Edme Mariotte around 1668. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blind_spot_(vision)
