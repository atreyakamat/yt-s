## Fact check

- Gravity roller conveyors use free-spinning rollers on a slight decline so loads move by gravity, and need loads with a firm flat base. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conveyor_system
- Brakes or speed controllers can be fitted to control gravity conveyors. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conveyor_system
- Gauge value is illustrative.
