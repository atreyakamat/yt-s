## Fact check

- Prepayment meters allow consumers to pay in advance for energy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prepayment_meter
- The Standard Transfer Specification (STS) uses 20-digit tokens to transfer credit to prepayment meters. Source: https://www.sts.org.za/
- Meters disconnect supply through an internal relay when credit is exhausted; emergency credit is common. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prepayment_meter
- Not every prepaid meter uses STS; others use keys or cards.
- Verified: STS tokens are 20-digit numbers (IEC 62055-41). Source: https://www.sts.org.za/ (standard) and https://clouglobal.com/the-never-ending-token-story/ (20-digit token)
