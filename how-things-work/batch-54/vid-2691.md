## Fact check

- Rack-and-pinion construction hoists: electric-driven pinions in the cage mesh with a toothed rack on a mast attached to the building; mast sections are added as the building rises. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rack_and_pinion
- Rack railways (mountain trains) use the same rack-and-pinion principle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rack_railway
