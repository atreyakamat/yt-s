## Fact check

- UPF 40-50+ is 'Excellent', blocking 97.5-98+% of UV; UPF 15-24 'Good' blocks 93.3-95.9%; UPF 25-39 blocks 96.0-97.4%. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultraviolet_protection_factor
- Factors: weave, colour, weight, stretch, wetness; wet fabric can lose up to half its protection (except silk and viscose); darker fabrics generally protect better. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultraviolet_protection_factor
