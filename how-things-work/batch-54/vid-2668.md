## Fact check

- The active molecule is miraculin, a glycoprotein; after eating the fleshy fruit it binds the tongue's taste buds so sour foods taste sweet; the effect lasts up to about 30 minutes until saliva washes it away; at neutral pH miraculin binds and blocks receptors, at low pH it can activate the sweet receptors; native to West Africa. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synsepalum_dulcificum
- 1725: Chevalier des Marchais, on a West African excursion, noticed locals chewing the berry before meals. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synsepalum_dulcificum
