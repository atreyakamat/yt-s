## Fact check

- The child-resistant locking closure was invented in 1967 by Dr Henri Breault; the US Poison Prevention Packaging Act of 1970 gave the Consumer Product Safety Commission authority to regulate it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child-resistant_packaging
- Many closures require two dissimilar motions to open; there is strong evidence such lids reduced child poisonings in the US, though they are not perfect. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child-resistant_packaging
- Push-and-turn mechanism (outer shell and inner cap that mesh when pushed): standard design description.
