## Fact check

- The base sits in a groove around the bottom of the walls; a latch on the outside tightens the wall belt. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springform_pan
- Easy release suits delicate bases like graham cracker crusts; cheesecakes and tortes are the classic uses. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springform_pan
- Most common pan is round and 9 inches (23 cm) across. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springform_pan
- Not reliably waterproof: as the latch loosens and the coating wears the seal weakens, so many bakers wrap the pan in foil. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springform_pan
- Springform invented in Germany by the company CHG, founded in 1882. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springform_pan
