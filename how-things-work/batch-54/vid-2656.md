## Fact check

- Gilmore Schjeldahl created the plastic-lined airsickness bag for Northwest Orient Airlines in 1949; he first designed it for food storage. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airsickness_bag
- Earlier bags were waxed paper or card; modern bags are mostly plastic-lined paper, a significant share fully plastic; a clip closes used bags; bags carry labelling and diagrams. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airsickness_bag
- Dutch collector Niek Vermeulen held the Guinness record with 6,016 different bags as of January 2010. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airsickness_bag
