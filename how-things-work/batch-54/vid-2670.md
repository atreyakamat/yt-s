## Fact check

- The average human tongue has about 2,000 to 8,000 taste buds; taste buds last about 10 days. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taste_bud
- The tongue map is a myth: tastes can be detected by any area of the tongue; five basic tastes are salty, sour, bitter, sweet and savoury (umami). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taste_bud
- Four types of lingual papillae: fungiform, foliate and circumvallate contain taste buds; filiform are the most numerous and have none. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taste_bud
