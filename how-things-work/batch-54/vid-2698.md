## Fact check

- The meat surface must exceed 150 C for a brown crust; water boils around 100 C so the surface must be free of water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Searing
- That moisture released by crowded food stays as steam and keeps the surface near 100 C is reasoning from those two numbers (the page does not discuss crowding).
