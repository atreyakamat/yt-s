## Fact check

- Microwave motion sensors use Doppler radar principles: a continuous microwave signal is emitted, and motion shifts the phase of the reflected waves, producing a low audio-frequency signal the electronics read as motion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_detector
- Microwave sensors are often paired with passive infrared sensors, and both must trigger to reduce false alarms. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_detector
