## Fact check

- A hypocaust is a Roman system of underfloor heating where a furnace's hot gases circulate under a raised floor. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypocaust
- The raised floor stood on short pillars (pilae); flues in walls carried gases up. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypocaust
- Hypocausts were used in baths and villas across the Roman Empire including Britain. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypocaust
- Hypocaust means 'heat from below' in Greek. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypocaust
- Furnace tenders were commonly slaves, per sources on Roman baths. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_bathing
- Verified: hypocaust invention attributed to Sergius Orata c. 80 BC; remains found across Europe including Britain. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypocaust
