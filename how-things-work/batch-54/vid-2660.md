## Fact check

- A hypnic jerk is a brief involuntary muscle contraction (myoclonus) as a person falls asleep, during non-REM sleep, often with a falling feeling or vivid dream. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypnic_jerk
- About 70% of people have one at least once in their lives and about 10% have them daily. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypnic_jerk
- Cause unclear; one idea is a reflex to the drop in blood pressure and muscle relaxation, another is the brain misreading relaxation as falling; risk factors include irregular sleep, anxiety, stress, caffeine or nicotine and fatigue. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypnic_jerk
