## Fact check

- Cyanotype announced by John Herschel in 1842; uses ferric ammonium citrate and potassium ferricyanide; UV reduces iron(III) to iron(II) forming insoluble Prussian blue. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyanotype
- Exposure a few seconds in strong direct sunlight or 10-20 minutes on a dull day; development by rinsing in cold running water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyanotype
- Anna Atkins's cyanotype albums (from 1843) effectively the world's first photographically illustrated books. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyanotype
