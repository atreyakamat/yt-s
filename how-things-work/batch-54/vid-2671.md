## Fact check

- Mobile crane booms are telescopic: nested sections extended by hydraulic cylinders and locked with pins; capacity falls as boom length and radius increase (load charts); outriggers and counterweights add stability. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crane_(machine)
