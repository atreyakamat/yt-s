## Fact check

- Moon's geometric albedo about 0.136 (about 14%), comparable to asphalt; mean synodic month 29.53 days; orbital period 27.32 days; the Moon rises about 50 minutes later each day (50.47 minutes average, from Harvest Moon article). That the Moon is visible in daylight around first and last quarter is standard observation: the Sun-lit half faces us at those phases; a new Moon is in the sky in daytime but unlit from Earth. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon
- Average rise delay of about 50 minutes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvest_moon
