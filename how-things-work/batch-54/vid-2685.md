## Fact check

- Time-lapse photography captures frames at a much lower rate than the playback rate. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-lapse_photography
- Standard video is commonly recorded at about 24 to 30 frames per second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frame_rate
- Speed-up factor equals the capture interval divided by the playback frame interval; bar values are worked examples (a ten-second gap played at thirty frames per second is 300x; two seconds is 60x).
