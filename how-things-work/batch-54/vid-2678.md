## Fact check

- Sodium bicarbonate reacts with acids to release carbon dioxide; with acetic acid it forms sodium acetate, water and CO2. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodium_bicarbonate
- It begins to decompose at about 80-100 C into sodium carbonate, water and CO2; baking soda is a leavening agent. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodium_bicarbonate
- In 1846 John Dwight and Austin Church set up the first US factory making baking soda. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodium_bicarbonate
