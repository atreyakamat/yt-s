## Fact check

- A gabion is a wire-mesh cage filled with rock or other material; the name comes from Italian gabbione, 'big cage'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabion
- Uses include erosion control, retaining walls, flood walls and noise barriers; gabions are highly deformable. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabion
- Leonardo da Vinci designed an early type for the foundations of the Sforza Castle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabion
- Water drains through the gaps between stones: physical reasoning, no number used.
