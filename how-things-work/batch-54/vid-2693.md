## Fact check

- Curtain-sided trailers use sliding fabric curtains on roof rails to allow side loading. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-trailer
- Curtains protect from weather but are not a substitute for load securing (general road-safety rule; qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-trailer
