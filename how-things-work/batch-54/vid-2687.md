## Fact check

- Hydrostatic equilibrium: gravity balanced by pressure-gradient force, causing objects in space to be spherical; Mimas (icy, 396 km) smallest body that appears to have an equilibrium shape; Pallas and Vesta (rocky, about 520 km) largest rocky bodies with an obviously non-equilibrium shape; Ceres (945 km) smallest body confirmed in hydrostatic equilibrium; IAU 2006 definition requires gravity sufficient to overcome rigidity and assume hydrostatic equilibrium. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrostatic_equilibrium
