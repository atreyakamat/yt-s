## Fact check

- A returning boomerang is a rotating wing with two or more airfoil arms; each blade moves faster through the air on one half of its rotation so it makes more lift; thrown nearly upright, each blade makes more lift at the top; gyroscopic precession tilts the plane of rotation 90 degrees from the direction of flight, making it turn. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boomerang
- Held tilted outward from nearly vertical to about 20-30 degrees; a right-handed boomerang is aimed to the right of the oncoming wind. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boomerang
- Wyrie Swamp, South Australia, oldest surviving Aboriginal boomerang, dated 10,000 BC. David Schummy threw 427.2 m on 15 March 2005 (Guinness World Record for longest throw of any object by a human). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boomerang
