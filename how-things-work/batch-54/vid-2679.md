## Fact check

- Glass coloured by adding metallic oxides or finely divided metals while molten; copper gives green, cobalt deep blue; gold about 0.001% makes ruby gold glass. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stained_glass
- Pieces held by H-sectioned lead cames with soldered joints. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stained_glass
- Grisaille: powdered glass, iron or rust filings, clay and a binder painted on and fired. Earliest documented reference 675 AD (Monkwearmouth); York Minster Tree of Jesse c. 1170 oldest stained-glass window in England; earliest dated silver-stain window 1313 at Le Mesnil-Villeman. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stained_glass
