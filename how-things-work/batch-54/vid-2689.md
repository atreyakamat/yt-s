## Fact check

- Steps: wax model, shell of silica slurry and stucco, kiln burnout melts wax out, molten metal poured, shell broken away and sprues cut off. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost-wax_casting
- Oldest examples: Varna Necropolis, Bulgaria gold objects dated 4550-4450 BC (about 6,500 years old); Mehrgarh copper amulet about 4000 BC; Mesopotamia c. 3500-2750 BC. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost-wax_casting
- Each casting breaks its own shell so the mould is single-use for the indirect method: inference from the steps (article does not state it directly).
