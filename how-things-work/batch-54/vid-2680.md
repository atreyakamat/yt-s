## Fact check

- Blue eyes contain no blue pigment; low stromal melanin lets short wavelengths scatter (Tyndall/Rayleigh scattering) while longer ones are absorbed by dark pigment behind. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_eye_color
- Brown is about 79% of the world's population; blue about 8% to 10%. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_eye_color
- A 2008 study hypothesised the OCA2 blue-eye mutation arose in one individual 6,000 to 10,000 years ago; later ancient DNA research suggests roughly 50,000 years ago. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_eye_color
- The same kind of scattering (Rayleigh) makes the sky look blue. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_eye_color
