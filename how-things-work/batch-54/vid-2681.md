## Fact check

- Pneumatic caisson: bottomless box sealed at the top and filled with compressed air to keep water and mud out; workers enter through an airlock and the chamber is filled with concrete to form the pier. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caisson_(engineering)
- Caisson workers were the first to suffer what was called caisson disease (decompression sickness). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caisson_(engineering)
- Royal Albert Bridge (1859), Eads Bridge (1874), Brooklyn Bridge (completed 1883). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caisson_(engineering)
