## Fact check

- Traveller's cheques first issued 1 January 1772 by the London Credit Exchange Company for use in 90 European cities. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traveler%27s_cheque
- In 1874 Thomas Cook issued 'circular notes'; American Express built a large-scale system in 1891. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traveler%27s_cheque
- Purchaser signs one panel on receipt and a second in front of the payee; payment guaranteed when signatures matched. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traveler%27s_cheque
- American Express's stored-value Travelers Cheque Card was discontinued in October 2007; issued cheques don't expire. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traveler%27s_cheque
- Replacement of lost cheques: standard issuer feature (Wikipedia notes issuers guaranteed payment).
