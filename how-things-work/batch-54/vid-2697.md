## Fact check

- Moonbow: moonlight much weaker than sunlight so much fainter; light usually too dim for cone cells so most people see white; long-exposure photographs reveal colours; needs full or nearly full Moon below about 42 degrees elevation, very dark sky, and rain or spray in the part of the sky opposite the Moon; notable locations Victoria Falls, Cumberland Falls, Lower Yosemite Fall. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonbow
