## Fact check

- Noctilucent clouds at about 76 to 85 km, higher than any other clouds; tiny water-ice crystals up to about 100 nm; need temperatures below about -120 C; best seen when the Sun is 6 to 16 degrees below the horizon; first observed in 1885, two years after the 1883 Krakatoa eruption; seen mostly in summer from roughly 50 to 65 degrees latitude. Airliner altitude about 11 km and weather clouds up to about 12 km are rounded comparison bars. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noctilucent_cloud
