## Fact check

- Iris recognition photographs the iris with near-infrared illumination; melanin absorbs little at these wavelengths, so even dark eyes show detailed structure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_recognition
- Daugman's method produces a 2048-bit template and compares two codes by Hamming distance (counting differing bits). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iris_recognition
