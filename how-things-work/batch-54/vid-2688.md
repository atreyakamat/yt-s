## Fact check

- Rice starch components (amylose and amylopectin) give different textures; short-grain japonica is sticky and keeps its shape, long-grain indica stays intact, dry and fluffy, medium-grain is moist and tends to stick together. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice
- Rice is the staple food of over half of the world's population; world production was 800 million tonnes in 2023; Asian rice was domesticated in China about 13,500 to 8,200 years ago. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice
