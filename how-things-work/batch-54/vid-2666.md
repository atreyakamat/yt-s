## Fact check

- Water and the cornea have nearly the same refractive index, so the eye can't focus underwater; a mask keeps an air space in front of the eyes and includes the nose. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diving_mask
- Exhaling through the nose into the mask equalises the pressure; the skirt must allow pinching the nostrils to clear the ears; goggles that don't cover the nose are unsuitable for diving; mask squeeze is barotrauma from the pressure difference on descent. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diving_mask
- Masks may have a single tempered glass faceplate or two lenses. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diving_mask
