## Fact check

- Temperature and pressure relief valves on water heaters open at set pressure or temperature limits (commonly around 99 C and 10 bar in North America). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relief_valve
- Water flashes to steam, expanding by over a thousand times in volume (about 1,600 times at atmospheric pressure). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boiling_liquid_expanding_vapor_explosion
- Discharge pipes must not be blocked or capped. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relief_valve
- Gauge is illustrative.
