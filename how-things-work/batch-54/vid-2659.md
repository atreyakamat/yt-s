## Fact check

- Pinhole camera is a light-tight box with a tiny aperture; projects an inverted image on the opposite side. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinhole_camera
- Young (1971): for 550 nm light, optimal d about 0.0366 sqrt(f) mm; for f = 50 mm about 0.259 mm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinhole_camera
- Exposures on modern film range from about 5 seconds to several hours; Mozi c. 500 BCE earliest description; Ibn al-Haytham 965-1039; Brewster 1856 first known description of pinhole photography. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinhole_camera
