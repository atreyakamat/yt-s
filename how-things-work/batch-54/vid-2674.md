## Fact check

- Jeremiah Chubb of Portsmouth patented the detector lock in 1818, developed after a burglary at Portsmouth Dockyard; if tampered with it jammed until a special regulator key reset it (Charles Chubb's 1824 patent removed that need). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chubb_detector_lock
- Jeremiah Chubb claimed the 100 pound government prize; separately he challenged a convict locksmith, who tried for two or three months before giving up (sources differ on the order, so the script says 'later' and 'months'). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chubb_detector_lock
- Robert Barron invented the double-acting lever tumbler lock in 1778. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lock_and_key
