## Fact check

- Standard atmospheric pressure is about 101 kPa, which is roughly 10 tonnes of force per square metre. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_pressure
- Vacuum lifters use a pump to evacuate a sealed pad so atmospheric pressure holds the load. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suction_cup
- Lifting force scales with pad area. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_pressure
- Bar values are illustrative relative lifting capacity.
