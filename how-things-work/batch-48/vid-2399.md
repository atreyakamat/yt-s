## Fact check

- A refractor's magnification is the objective's focal length divided by the eyepiece's focal length. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refracting_telescope
- Lippershey, c. 1608, tried to patent a refracting telescope; Galileo in May 1609 built his own; his most powerful instrument magnified about 30 times. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refracting_telescope
- Reflecting telescopes can be made in far larger apertures than refractors. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refracting_telescope
- Bigger aperture collects more light: standard optics reasoning (no number used).
