## Fact check

- Earliest documented windmills were in Persia, vertical-axis, around the 9th century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windmill
- Windmills appeared in Europe in the 12th century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windmill
- Post mills could be rotated into the wind; windmills were used for grinding grain and pumping water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windmill
- Verified: one of the earliest recorded windmill designs dates to about 700-900 AD in Persia, with horizontal sails; vertical windmills reached Europe in the 12th century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windmill
