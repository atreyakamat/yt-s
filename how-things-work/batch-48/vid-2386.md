## Fact check

- A taximeter calculates fares from a combination of distance travelled and waiting time. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taximeter
- The car generates a signal sent to the taximeter; the k constant is the number of pulses per kilometre needed to indicate one kilometre; tariff values and travel data are multiplied to give the fare. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taximeter
- Work cycle stages: Free (For Hire), Occupied (Hired), To Pay (Stopped). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taximeter
- Taximeters are often visually sealed by a municipal weights and scales authority after initial calibration; fraud control. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taximeter
- Modern taximeter invented by Friedrich Wilhelm Gustav Bruhn in 1891; the Daimler Victoria, the first meter-equipped taxicab, was built by Gottlieb Daimler in 1897. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taximeter
