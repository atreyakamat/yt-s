## Fact check

- Fog is a low cloud that cuts visibility below 1 km; mist less; radiation fog forms under clear calm skies as ground cools; advection fog forms when moist air blows over a cooler surface; sea fog forms when vapour condenses on salt particles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fog
