## Fact check

- A potter's wheel from the Cucuteni-Trypillia culture in western Ukraine, dated to the middle of the 5th millennium BC, is the oldest found; script says over 6,000 years (about 6,500). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potter%27s_wheel
- Stone potter's wheel at Ur dated to about 3129 BC; tournette/slow wheel used around 3500 BC in the Near East, turned slowly by hand or foot while a pot was coiled. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potter%27s_wheel
- Fast wheel developed in mid-to-late 3rd millennium BC using the stored rotational energy of a heavy stone wheel; Iron Age flywheel at ground level kicked by the potter's foot. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potter%27s_wheel
