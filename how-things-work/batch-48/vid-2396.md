## Fact check

- Motion sickness arises from a mismatch between inner ear motion sense and visual input. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_sickness
- Acupressure bands stimulate the median nerve at the inner wrist; effectiveness 'may be nothing more than a placebo'; evidence mixed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_sickness
- Behavioural measures include keeping the head still and focusing on the horizon; habituation is the most effective approach but must be repeated at least weekly to keep working. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_sickness
