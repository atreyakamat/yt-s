## Fact check

- Phase-detection autofocus uses paired pixels or sensors to measure offset and so the direction and amount of lens movement. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autofocus
- Contrast-detection autofocus hunts back and forth around the sharpest point. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autofocus
- Many phone sensors include on-sensor phase-detection pixels (dual pixel). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autofocus
