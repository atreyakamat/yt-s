## Fact check

- Boiling point elevation: dT = Kb x molality x i; water Kb 0.512 C/molal; one molal NaCl raises the boiling point about 1 C (about 0.97 to 1.02). One mole of NaCl is about 58 g. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boiling-point_elevation
- Salt amount does not affect pasta cooking speed; adding before or after boiling makes no difference; Italian pasta is cooked unsalted with salt added to the water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta
