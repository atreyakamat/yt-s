## Fact check

- Large rotors turn at slow speeds (typically about 10 to 20 rpm) and gearboxes step this up to generator speed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_turbine
- Generators typically need roughly 750 to 3,600 rpm (about 1,800 typical), far above rotor speed, so a gearbox steps it up (reworded from "about fifteen hundred"). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_turbine_design
- Yaw drives turn the nacelle to face the wind; blade pitch systems feather blades in high winds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nacelle_(wind_turbine)
- Direct-drive turbines have no gearbox. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct-drive_mechanism
