## Fact check

- Luke Howard proposed his cloud nomenclature in an 1802 presentation to the Askesian Society; his essay On the Modification of Clouds was published in 1803; he named the main types cumulus, stratus and cirrus plus intermediate forms such as cirrostratus and cirrocumulus; pharmacist by training and manufacturing chemist; Latin naming aided adoption; Goethe admired it and wrote poems praising Howard. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Howard_(meteorologist)
- Meanings of the Latin words (curl of hair, heap, layer) are standard Latin: cirrus, cumulus, stratus.
