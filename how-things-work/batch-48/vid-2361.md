## Fact check

- Tube-and-coupler scaffolds are built from steel tubes joined by couplers (clamps), including right-angle couplers, tightened by a bolt. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scaffolding
- Diagonal bracing makes frames rigid because triangles do not change shape under load. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle
