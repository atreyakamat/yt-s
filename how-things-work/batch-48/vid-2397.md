## Fact check

- PoP is meaningless without a time interval, 12-hour periods common; NWS measurable threshold 0.01 inch (0.25 mm), Met Office 0.1 mm, Environment Canada 0.2 mm; ensemble forecasts express probability as the share of simulations showing rain; 'scattered' is a descriptive term for 30-50% areal coverage. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_of_precipitation
