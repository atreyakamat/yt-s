## Fact check

- Modern spring mattress cores (innersprings) are made of steel coils, with upholstery layers; insulator pads sit over the coils so upholstery does not cup down. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattress
- Bonnell coils are the oldest and most common, first adapted from 19th-century carriage seat springs; knotted, round-top, hourglass-shaped wire coil. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattress
- Coils are mostly connected by interconnecting wires (cross wire helicals); encased coils are not connected but the fabric encasement helps preserve the shape. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattress
- Lower gauge number means thicker spring; 12.5 gauge (1.94 mm) is the thickest typically available and feels firm; 14 gauge is 1.63 mm and 14 to 15.5 gauge give more easily under pressure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattress
- In Europe, polyurethane foam cores and latex cores have long been popular; in North America the typical mattress is an innerspring. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mattress
