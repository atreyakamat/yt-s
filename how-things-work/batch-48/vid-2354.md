## Fact check

- A people counter measures how many people pass an entrance; types include infrared beams, thermal imaging, Wi-Fi trackers, floor technologies and video counters. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_counter
- How a beam counter works (each person breaking the beam adds one) is the general principle of an infrared beam counter; the page lists infrared beams as a method. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_counter
- Footfall is the number of people who enter a shop in a period; conversion rate is the percentage of visitors who buy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_counter
- Retailers use counts for staffing allocation; shopping malls use them to see where people gather, and areas where people gather are often charged higher rent. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_counter
- On buses and trains they are generally called Automated Passenger Counters (APC) and help operators allocate resources to routes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People_counter
