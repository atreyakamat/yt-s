## Fact check

- A truss is a structure of members connected at joints, typically arranged in triangles, with members mainly in tension or compression. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truss
- A triangle is rigid: its shape is fixed by its side lengths. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle
