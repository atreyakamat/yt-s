## Fact check

- Undo erases the last change; redo reverses the undo; the history buffer and an undo/redo manager are the common components. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undo
- Typical shortcuts: undo Ctrl+Z (Command+Z on macOS); redo Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z (Command+Shift+Z on macOS). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undo
- Some actions are not stored and cannot be undone, e.g. saving a file; scrolling or selection are usually not stored. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undo
- The File Retrieval and Editing System (FRESS), developed from 1968 at Brown University, is reported to be the first computer-based system with an undo feature; the Xerox PARC Bravo editor had Undo in 1974. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undo
- Xerox PARC programmers assigned the Ctrl-Z shortcut to undo, which became a crucial feature in the personal computer era. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undo
