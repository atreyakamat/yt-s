## Fact check

- The Nokia tune is based on a passage from Gran Vals by Francisco Tarrega, composed in 1902. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nokia_tune
- Nokia tune was used as a default ringtone on Nokia phones from the 1990s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nokia_tune
- Tune was adapted in 1993 by Nokia execs from bars 13-16 of Gran Vals and first shipped on the Nokia 2110 in 1994; Wikipedia calls it the first identifiable musical ringtone and does not rank it 'most famous', so the script says 'world-famous'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nokia_tune
- Simple tones carry on small speakers. Reasoning claim, no number given.
