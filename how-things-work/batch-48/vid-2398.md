## Fact check

- Barm from beer fermentation could be used in bread making; in the 19th century bakers often got yeast from brewers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baker%27s_yeast
- In 1846 brewers shifted to bottom-fermenting yeast, creating a yeast shortage for bakers, so the Vienna Process was developed that year. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baker%27s_yeast
- Compressed (cake) yeast became standard in the early 20th century; active dry yeast was developed by Fleischmann's during World War II for the US armed forces. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baker%27s_yeast
- Baker's yeast is Saccharomyces cerevisiae and produces carbon dioxide. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baker%27s_yeast
