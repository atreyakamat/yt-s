## Fact check

- Earwax is made by sebaceous and modified sweat glands, with shed skin, about 60% keratin, 12-20% fatty acids, 6-9% cholesterol; pH about 6.1 with antimicrobial action. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earwax
- Skin cells migrate outward like a conveyor belt and jaw movement helps loosen debris. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earwax
- A single base change in ABCC11 decides wet (about 50% lipid) vs dry (about 20% lipid); dry is more common in East Asian, Southeast Asian and Native American populations. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earwax
- Nails and earwax both contain keratin (alpha-keratin in nails). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nail_(anatomy)
