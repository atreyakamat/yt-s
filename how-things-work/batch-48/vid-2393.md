## Fact check

- Curved roller conveyors use tapered rollers so outer and inner edges of a bend travel at different speeds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conveyor_system
- Train wheels are coned so the two wheels can cover different distances on a curve. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheelset_(rail_transport)
- Speed values on the bars are illustrative relative figures (proportional to roller radius), not measurements.
