## Fact check

- Freezing-point depression: solutes dilute the solvent so fewer solvent molecules are free to freeze; it keeps ice cream soft below 0 C and is why salt melts road ice. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freezing-point_depression
- Sodium chloride can lower the freezing point of water to about -21 C (-6 F); below that road salt stops being effective. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freezing-point_depression
