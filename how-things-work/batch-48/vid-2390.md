## Fact check

- A sneeze can produce 40,000 droplets, typically 0.5 to 5 micrometres; plumes once observed to 0.6 m, and up to 8 m in 2020 recordings. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sneeze
- Reflex path: sensory neurons in the nose via the trigeminal nerve to the brainstem, then motor nerves trigger muscle contractions. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sneeze
- The idea that you cannot sneeze with eyes open is inaccurate. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sneeze
- Photic sneeze reflex affects 18-35% of people. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sneeze
- A computer simulation estimated a held-in sneeze pressure of 39 kPa, about 24 times that of a normal sneeze. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sneeze
