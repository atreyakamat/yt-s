## Fact check

- Dried pasta is semolina and water; during cooking, starch granules absorb water and swell and viscosity rises. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta
- Italian pasta is often cooked al dente because it finishes softening in the sauce. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta
- That some starch leaks into the cooking water (cloudy) and helps sauces cling is common culinary knowledge and follows from starch gelatinisation; no numbers are used. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starch
