## Fact check

- Seasoned firewood is commonly stated as around 20% moisture content or less. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firewood
- Wet wood burns cooler and produces more smoke and creosote because energy goes into evaporating water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firewood
- Creosote deposits form in flues from incomplete combustion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creosote
- Seasoning times vary (often six months to two years depending on species and conditions). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firewood
- Verified: seasoned wood typically has 20% or less moisture; air drying takes one to three years. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Firewood
