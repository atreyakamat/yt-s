## Fact check

- Control signal is a PWM pulse sent every 20 ms (50 Hz), nominally 1.0 to 2.0 ms, 1.5 ms is neutral. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Servo_(radio_control)
- Potentiometer on the output shaft; controller compares measured with commanded position; error drives the motor until zero; if the shaft is pushed away the error reappears and the motor corrects it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Servo_(radio_control)
- Parts: small motor, reduction gears, potentiometer, simple electronics. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Servo_(radio_control)
