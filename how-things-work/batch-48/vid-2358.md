## Fact check

- Flambé: alcohol is added to a hot pan and ignited; the liquid is below its flash point at room temperature, heating raises vapour pressure so it can be ignited. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flamb%C3%A9
- Liquors of about 40% alcohol (80 proof) are ideal; wines and beers have too little. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flamb%C3%A9
- Flame temperature can exceed 500 °C but the pan stays below Maillard/caramelisation temperatures; one experimental model had about 35% of the alcohol boiled off. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flamb%C3%A9
- Safety: do not add alcohol to a pan on a burner; ignite with a long fireplace match. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flamb%C3%A9
- Typical strength: beer about 4-6%, wine about 12.5-14.5% (shown as 5 and 12 for round examples), whisky and vodka commonly 40%. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcohol_by_volume
