## Fact check

- Refrigerated trucks have insulated bodies and a refrigeration unit, often powered by a separate diesel engine. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refrigerated_truck
- Refrigeration units hold temperature of pre-cooled cargo rather than cooling warm cargo; air circulates under loads via floor channels. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refrigerated_truck
