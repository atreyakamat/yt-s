## Fact check

- A glaze is a glassy coating on ceramics, making the item impermeable to liquids; seals naturally porous earthenware. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceramic_glaze
- Silica is the main glass former; flux oxides lower the melting temperature; alumina stiffens the molten glaze so it does not run. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceramic_glaze
- Colourants include iron oxide, copper carbonate, cobalt carbonate; cobalt gives the blue of blue-and-white porcelain. Glazes first appeared on stone in the 4th millennium BC; glazing pottery followed invention of glass around 1500 BC. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceramic_glaze
