## Fact check

- Dimensional (volumetric) weight is calculated from a package's dimensions divided by a divisor, and carriers charge the greater of actual and dimensional weight. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimensional_weight
- Vehicles and aircraft often fill in volume before reaching a weight limit (explanation given in the same article). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimensional_weight
- Bar chart values are illustrative cost units, not real prices.
