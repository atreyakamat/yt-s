## Fact check

- Falling raindrops are not tear-shaped; small ones nearly spherical; drops larger than about 2 mm flattened by air pressure and break up; typical 0.5 to 4 mm; largest recorded 8.8 mm; a 3 mm drop falls about 8 m/s. Surface tension making small drops spherical is standard physics (script wording). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raindrop
