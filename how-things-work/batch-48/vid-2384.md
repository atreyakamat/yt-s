## Fact check

- Security holograms are embossed: a master pattern is stamped into thin film, which allows mass production on cards. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_hologram
- Holograms work by diffraction of light, with the appearance changing with viewing angle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hologram
- The original is made with lasers using interference; the equipment to originate one is specialised, so ordinary printing can't reproduce it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_hologram
