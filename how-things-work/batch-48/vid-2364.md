## Fact check

- Master key systems use extra pins/wafers (spacers) so a lock can be opened by two different keys, the change key and the master key. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_keying
- More shear lines mean more keys can open the lock, a recognised trade-off of master keying. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_keying
- Wikipedia: master-keyed pin tumbler locks often have two shear points per pin position (change key and master key), which adds valid keys and generally reduces security; the script's 'spacer' wording is the common trade term for the extra pin. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_keying
