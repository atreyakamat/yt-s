## Fact check

- Small ceramic pyramids placed in the kiln, usually sets of three; bend from 0 to 90 degrees; give temperature equivalent; depend on heatwork (temperature and time). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrometric_cone
- Orton cone values at 60 C/hr: cone 022 590 C, 04 1063 C, 6 1222 C, 10 1285 C. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrometric_cone
- Hermann Seger modern form first used in 1886 at the Royal Porcelain Factory, Berlin; Wedgwood published a pyrometer in 1782. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrometric_cone
