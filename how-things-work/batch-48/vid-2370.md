## Fact check

- Friction ridges begin forming around the 13th week of pregnancy and their unique features are set by about the 15th week. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fingerprint
- Identical twins' prints are very similar but not identical. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fingerprint
- Loops about 60-65%, whorls about 30-35%, arches about 5% (Henry system). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fingerprint
- Ridges amplify vibrations so fine textures can be sensed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fingerprint
- Henry Faulds, a Scottish surgeon in Tokyo, published on fingerprint identification in 1880; Galton's Finger Prints came out in 1892; Scotland Yard began identifying criminals by fingerprints in 1901. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fingerprint
