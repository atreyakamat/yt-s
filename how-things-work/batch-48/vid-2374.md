## Fact check

- Graphite is a layered form of carbon whose sheets slide easily over each other, used as a dry lubricant. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphite
- Oils attract dust and can gum up fine mechanisms; dry lubricants (graphite, PTFE) are preferred in locks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_lubricant
