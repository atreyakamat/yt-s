## Fact check

- Parcel lockers are self-service machines with multiple compartments, unlocked by a code after courier deposit; they come in various compartment sizes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parcel_locker
- Compartments are assigned by a networked system and opened with a code. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parcel_locker
- Bar values are relative door sizes, not measurements.
