## Fact check

- Upper lashes typically 7 to 8 mm; eye width typically 24.2 mm; lashes typically grow to about one third of the eye's width (bars show about 24 and 8). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyelash
- Length is an adaptation to reduce tear film evaporation and dust deposition; lashes longer or shorter than one third of eye width show reduced efficacy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyelash
- Four functions: protect from dust and debris, trigger the blink reflex, regulate tear film evaporation, protect from excess light and UV. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyelash
- The lash roots are surrounded by many nerve endings, so touch triggers the blink reflex. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyelash
- Upper eyelid typically 90 to 160 lashes, lower 75 to 80; number cannot be increased after birth as all follicles develop in the embryo. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyelash
- Eyelashes take about seven to eight weeks to grow back if pulled out. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyelash
