## Fact check

- Ground temperature a few metres down is relatively stable year-round. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geothermal_heat_pump
- Ground loops circulate water or water-antifreeze mixtures. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geothermal_heat_pump
- Heat pumps deliver several units of heat per unit of electricity (coefficient of performance typically about 3 to 5). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coefficient_of_performance
- Bar values are illustrative of a typical ratio, not a measurement.
- Verified: heat pump COP typically 3-6 ("roughly 4" is within range). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geothermal_heat_pump
