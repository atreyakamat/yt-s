## Fact check

- Thong sandals appear in ancient Egyptian murals; a surviving pair made of papyrus found in Europe is about 1,500 years old. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flip-flop_(footwear)
- Soldiers returning from WWII brought Japanese zori to the US, an antecedent of the modern flip-flop. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flip-flop_(footwear)
- 1950s: American makers redesigned them in bright colors. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flip-flop_(footwear)
- Havaianas marketed in Brazil in 1962; more than 150 million pairs produced yearly by 2010. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flip-flop_(footwear)
- Y-shaped strap; name 'may be an imitation of the sound'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flip-flop_(footwear)
