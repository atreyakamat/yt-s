## Fact check

- One-time passwords sent by SMS are generated by the service, expire after a short time and are single-use. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-time_password
- SMS codes can be intercepted or targeted via SIM swapping, so app-based codes are considered stronger. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-factor_authentication
