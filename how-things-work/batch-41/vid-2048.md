## Fact check

- Cheese-making: coagulation of milk with rennet or acid into curds, cutting, draining whey, pressing, salting and ageing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheese
- Rennet contains chymosin, which clots casein. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rennet
