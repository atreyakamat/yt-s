## Fact check

- An energiser sends a pulse approximately once per second along the fence wire. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_fence
- Pulsed fences use short pulses so contact is startling but brief; they rely on a return path through the earth. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_fence
