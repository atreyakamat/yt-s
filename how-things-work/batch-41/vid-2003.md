## Fact check

- Solar garden lights combine a photovoltaic cell, a rechargeable battery, an LED and a circuit that switches on when the panel's output falls at dusk. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar-powered_lamp
- LEDs are efficient low-power light sources. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light-emitting_diode
