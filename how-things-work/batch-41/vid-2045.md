## Fact check

- Glass wool is made by forcing molten glass through a spinner with small holes (rotary process), with a binder applied and cured in an oven. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_wool
- Glass wool insulates by trapping air; script gives about 98 percent air as a round, illustrative figure (insulation batts are commonly well above 90 percent air by volume). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_wool
- Glass wool is also called fibreglass insulation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_wool
