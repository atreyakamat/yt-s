## Fact check

- The stack effect: in buildings warm indoor air rises and escapes at the top while outside air is drawn in at the bottom; stronger in tall buildings and in cold weather. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stack_effect
- Lift shafts and stairwells increase the effect; revolving doors, vestibules and air locks mitigate; it can reverse in hot climates. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stack_effect
