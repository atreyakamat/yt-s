## Fact check

- On a paper machine, fibres align mostly in the machine direction, giving paper a grain. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper
- Folding or bending across the grain tends to crack the surface and is stiffer to bend against; book pages are generally grain parallel to the spine. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper
