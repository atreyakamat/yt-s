## Fact check

- Stephane Tarnier introduced an incubator for premature infants at the Paris Maternité in 1880, inspired by poultry incubators at the Paris Zoo. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infant_incubator
- Martin Couney displayed premature infants in incubators at fairs and on Coney Island in the early 1900s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_A._Couney
- Premature babies have trouble keeping their body temperature. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infant_incubator
- Modern incubators control temperature, humidity and oxygen. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infant_incubator
- Tarnier developed incubator 1880 in Paris, based on chick-hatching devices: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incubator_(neonatal)
