## Fact check

- Ejection seats use explosive charges and/or rocket motors to propel the occupant out of an aircraft; the canopy is jettisoned or fractured first. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ejection_seat
- Zero-zero ejection seats can save a pilot at zero altitude and zero airspeed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ejection_seat
- A drogue parachute stabilises the seat and a main parachute is then deployed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ejection_seat
- Martin-Baker alone reports more than 7,000 lives saved by its seats (script says thousands). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin-Baker
