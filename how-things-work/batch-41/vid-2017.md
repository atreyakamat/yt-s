## Fact check

- A stroboscope uses repeated brief flashes to make periodic motion appear slowed or stopped; mismatched timing makes wheels seem to move slowly or backwards (stroboscopic / wagon-wheel effect). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stroboscope
- Harold Edgerton is generally credited with pioneering the stroboscope to freeze fast motion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-speed_photography
