## Fact check

- Sharp corners concentrate stress; rounded fillets reduce stress concentration. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stress_concentration
- Glass is brittle and fails by cracking from stress concentrations. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass
- Container glass is blown into moulds, and wall thickness depends on the shape. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_container_industry
