## Fact check

- Blown film extrusion extrudes melted plastic through a ring die, inflates the tube into a bubble, cools it with air, and flattens it with nip rollers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blown_film_extrusion
- Bags are then sealed and cut from the flattened tube. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plastic_bag
