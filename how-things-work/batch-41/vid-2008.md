## Fact check

- Tea types differ mainly in oxidation level; green tea heating (kill-green) stops oxidation, black tea is fully oxidised, oolong partly. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea
- All come from Camellia sinensis. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camellia_sinensis
