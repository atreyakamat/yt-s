## Fact check

- Water's density drops by about 9% when it freezes, so it expands by roughly that amount. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Properties_of_water
- Ice expansion can burst pipes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freezing#Pipe_bursting
- Advice to shut off and drain outdoor taps is standard winter guidance (no figure claimed).
- Water expands about 9% on freezing: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Properties_of_water
