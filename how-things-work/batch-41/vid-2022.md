## Fact check

- Peristaltic pumps move fluid by compressing a flexible tube with rollers or fingers; the fluid contacts only the tube. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peristaltic_pump
- Infusion pumps commonly use peristaltic mechanisms and have alarms for air or occlusion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infusion_pump
- Peristalsis is the muscle wave in the digestive tract. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peristalsis
