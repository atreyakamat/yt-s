## Fact check

- Automatic flushers use an infrared proximity sensor and a solenoid valve; typically battery-powered. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toilet#Flushing_mechanism
- Infrared sensors detect reflected infrared light to sense presence. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proximity_sensor
