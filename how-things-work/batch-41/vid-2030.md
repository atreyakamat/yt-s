## Fact check

- Josiah Latimer Clark installed a pneumatic tube system in London in the 1850s (dated 1853 or 1854 by different accounts; patent 1854) linking the Stock Exchange and the Electric Telegraph Company's offices. Softened to 1850s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumatic_tube
- Pneumatic tubes move carriers with compressed air or partial vacuum; used by banks (drive-throughs) and hospitals (specimens). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumatic_tube
- Early London pneumatic line linked Stock Exchange and Electric Telegraph Company, 1850s (1853/54): softened to 1850s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumatic_tube
