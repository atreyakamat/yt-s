## Fact check

- Geostationary weather satellites orbit about 35,786 km above the equator and stay over a fixed point; they provide frequent images of the same disc. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_satellite
- Polar-orbiting weather satellites fly at low altitude (under about 1,000 km) and cover the whole Earth as it rotates beneath them. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_satellite
- Weather satellites use visible and infrared imagery; infrared works at night and shows cloud-top temperature. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_satellite
- Geostationary about 35,800 km; polar orbiters about 800-850 km: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_satellite
