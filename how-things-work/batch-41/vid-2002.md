## Fact check

- A fume hood is a ventilated enclosure that draws air in at the front and exhausts it out of the building. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fume_hood
- The sash is a movable window; lowering it increases protection and air velocity at the opening. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fume_hood
- Baffles at the back help guide even airflow. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fume_hood
