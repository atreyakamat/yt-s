- Green is the common backdrop for TV and electronic cinematography partly because weather presenters often wore blue suits; the green channel carried the strongest signal in analog video. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chroma_key
- Red overlaps skin tones and is avoided; blue screens were developed at RKO in the 1930s; Petro Vlahos received an Academy Award in 1964; early 1970s networks switched backdrops from blue to green. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chroma_key
