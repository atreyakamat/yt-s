## Fact check

- Clumping litter uses bentonite clay that absorbs moisture and clumps. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat_litter
- Edward Lowe began selling clay cat litter in 1947; clumping calcium bentonite litter appeared in the UK in the 1950s, and Thomas Nelson developed clumping bentonite litter in the US in 1984. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat_litter
- Bentonite swells as water enters between its platelets. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bentonite
- Edward Lowe sold clay litter from 1947; clumping clay in UK in 1950s, bentonite clumping in US 1984: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat_litter
