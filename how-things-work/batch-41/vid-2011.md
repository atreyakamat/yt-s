## Fact check

- Standing period recommended so temperatures inside the food equalise; use a food thermometer because uneven heating can leave regions below safe temperatures. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microwave_oven
- At 2.45 GHz microwaves penetrate only about 1 cm into most foods; the interior of thicker foods heats mainly by conduction; "inside out" is called a misconception. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microwave_oven
- Uneven heating: uneven energy distribution in the oven and different absorption by the food; hot and dead spots exist. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microwave_oven
