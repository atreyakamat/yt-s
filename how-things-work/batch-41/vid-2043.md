## Fact check

- Tube pitch depends on length: longer tubes give lower notes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_chime
- Chimes are often tuned to a musical scale. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_chime
- Tubes are suspended near a vibration node so they can ring freely (qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tubular_bells
- Bar values (cm) are illustrative, not measurements.
