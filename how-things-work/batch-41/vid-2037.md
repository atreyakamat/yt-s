## Fact check

- Homer Dudley developed the vocoder at Bell Labs starting in 1928; splits speech into typically 8 to 20 bands and sends envelope levels, needing less bandwidth. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocoder
- Only band envelopes are transmitted, none of the original signal, which suited secure voice; SIGSALY (1943) used it for encrypted wartime calls. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocoder
- The Voder was shown at the 1939-1940 New York World's Fair, played with keys, a wrist bar and a foot pedal. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocoder
- Dudley began vocoder 1928 at Bell Labs; Voder at 1939 World's Fair; SIGSALY 1943: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vocoder
