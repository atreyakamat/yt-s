## Fact check

- The Stevenson screen is painted white to reflect solar radiation; double-louvred walls keep out precipitation and direct heat while letting air circulate. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevenson_screen
- WMO standard places thermometers between 1.25 and 2 m above ground. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevenson_screen
- Designed by Thomas Stevenson (1818-1887), a Scottish civil engineer who also designed many lighthouses. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevenson_screen
- WMO standard height 1.25-2 m, so about 1.5 m is within range: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevenson_screen
