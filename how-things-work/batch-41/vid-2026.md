## Fact check

- A kill cord stops a vessel's engine if the operator leaves the controls, preventing a runaway vessel that could endanger other vessels or swimmers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kill_switch
- The cord connects the kill switch to the operator, usually via life jacket or clothing; if thrown overboard the cord pulls the switch; it lets the operator swim back and climb aboard without being struck by the propeller; it is a type of dead man's switch. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kill_switch
