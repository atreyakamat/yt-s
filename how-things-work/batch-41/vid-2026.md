## Fact check

- Vessels' sidelights are red to port (left) and green to starboard (right). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navigation_light
- Sidelights are shielded so each is visible only through an arc from dead ahead to just abaft the beam on its own side (112.5 degrees); a stern light is white. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navigation_light
- Seeing both red and green indicates a vessel approaching head on; seeing only one shows which side faces you. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navigation_light
