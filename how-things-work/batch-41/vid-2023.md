## Fact check

- Energisers send a brief high-voltage pulse approximately once per second along the wire. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_fence
- The circuit is completed when an animal touches both the wire and earth; fences rely on animals learning to avoid the shock. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_fence
- Pulsed delivery lets a person or animal pull away between pulses (design rationale, qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_fence
