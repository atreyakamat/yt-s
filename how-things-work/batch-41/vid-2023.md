## Fact check

- A cattle grid is a pit across a road covered by a grid of bars; animals are reluctant to walk on the grates, wheeled vehicles and people pass easily. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle_grid
- Flint Hills, Kansas study: spaces between bars 44 to 203 mm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle_grid
- Painted stripes mimic the grid; cattle can sometimes learn to cross once one animal does. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle_grid
- Modern cattle grid independently invented several times on the US Great Plains around 1905-1915; a patent was issued in 1915. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cattle_grid
