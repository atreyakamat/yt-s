## Fact check

- Lenticular printing uses a sheet of lenticular lenses over interlaced image strips so different images are seen from different angles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenticular_printing
- Left and right eyes see different strips, giving a 3D impression; several frames can give animation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenticular_printing
