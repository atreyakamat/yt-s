## Fact check

- Croydon Airport had the world's first airport control tower with radio communication to aircraft, in 1920. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_traffic_control
- Archie League is regarded as the first air traffic controller, using flags at Lambert Field in St Louis in 1929. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archie_League
- The 1956 Grand Canyon mid-air collision killed all 128 people aboard both airliners and prompted the creation of the Federal Aviation Agency (Federal Aviation Act, 1958). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1956_Grand_Canyon_mid-air_collision
- Croydon opened first control tower 1920; Grand Canyon collision 1956 killed all 128; FAA 1958: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_traffic_control
- Archie League hired 1929 at St. Louis as first US controller, used flags: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archie_League
