## Fact check

- Most CGMs insert a sensor under the skin into subcutaneous tissue and measure interstitial fluid; readings lag blood glucose by about 5 to 10 minutes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_glucose_monitor
- Most are electrochemical systems using an enzymatic reaction with glucose, producing a nanoampere-scale current proportional to glucose concentration. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_glucose_monitor
- Electrochemical systems typically last 14 to 16 days; the FDA approved the first CGM system in 1999. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_glucose_monitor
- General explainer only; no medical advice is given.
