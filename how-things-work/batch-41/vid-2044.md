## Fact check

- Optical heart-rate sensors use photoplethysmography: LEDs and a photodiode detect blood volume changes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photoplethysmogram
- Green light is strongly absorbed by blood (haemoglobin), common in wrist sensors. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photoplethysmogram
- Motion and fit affect accuracy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photoplethysmogram
