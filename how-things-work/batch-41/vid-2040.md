## Fact check

- Barcode scanners use light (laser or camera) and a photodetector to read the pattern of bars; white reflects light, black absorbs it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcode_reader
- The first product scanned with a UPC barcode in a shop was a pack of chewing gum, June 1974, in Troy, Ohio. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Product_Code
- First scanned item was a pack of Wrigley's gum, 26 June 1974, Troy, Ohio: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Product_Code
