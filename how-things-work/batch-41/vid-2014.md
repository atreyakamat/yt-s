## Fact check

- Hawk-Eye uses multiple high-speed cameras and triangulation to track the ball in 3D. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawk-Eye
- Average error in tennis reported about 3.6 mm (rounded to about four millimetres). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawk-Eye
- Ball fuzz length is roughly a millimetre or two (illustrative figure; not claimed in voiceover). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_ball
- Hawk-Eye advertises average tennis error of 3.6 mm, up to ten cameras triangulate in 3D: OK (the 4 mm is a rounding of the maker's claim). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawk-Eye
