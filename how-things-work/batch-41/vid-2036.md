## Fact check

- A hovercraft is supported by a cushion of air held beneath the hull by a flexible skirt; fans produce the cushion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hovercraft
- Separate propellers (often ducted air propellers) with rudders provide thrust and steering. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hovercraft
- Hovercraft can travel over water, mud, ice and land because they do not contact the surface. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hovercraft
