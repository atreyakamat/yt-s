## Fact check

- Post-mix fountain dispensers keep syrup and carbonated water separate and combine them at the nozzle when dispensed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountain_soda
- Carbonators dissolve carbon dioxide into chilled water under pressure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbonation
