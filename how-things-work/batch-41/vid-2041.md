## Fact check

- James Ritty, a Dayton, Ohio saloon owner, patented the first cash register in 1879, suspecting employees of pocketing cash; reportedly inspired by a steamship's propeller-revolution counter. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash_register
- 1884: John H. Patterson bought the company, renamed it National Cash Register and added a paper roll creating the receipt; early registers rang a bell when the drawer opened. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash_register
