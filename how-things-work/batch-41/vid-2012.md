## Fact check

- A femtosecond is 10^-15 second, a quadrillionth of a second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Femtosecond
- A blink lasts roughly 100 to 400 milliseconds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blinking
- One second is about 31.7 million years measured in femtoseconds (10^15 s). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Femtosecond
- Femtosecond lasers are used in refractive eye surgery to create a corneal flap by forming many tiny cavitation bubbles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LASIK
- Ultrashort pulses limit heat spread into nearby tissue. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Femtosecond_laser
