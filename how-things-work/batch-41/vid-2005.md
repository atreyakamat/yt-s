## Fact check

- The Hall-Heroult process, discovered independently by Charles Hall and Paul Heroult in 1886, dissolves alumina in molten cryolite and electrolyses it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hall%E2%80%93H%C3%A9roult_process
- The bath operates at roughly 940 to 980 C, far below the 2,000 C-plus melting point of alumina (script says about 950). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hall%E2%80%93H%C3%A9roult_process
- Alumina is refined from bauxite; smelting needs very large amounts of electricity and smelters are often sited near cheap hydroelectric power. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminium_smelting
- Hall and Heroult discovered process in 1886; bath about 940-980 C, alumina melts at 2072 C: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hall%E2%80%93H%C3%A9roult_process
