## Fact check

- Round balers roll grass using belts or rollers, then wrap with net or twine and discharge the bale from the back. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baler
- Round bales have a thatched-roof effect that sheds weather; the first modern round baler (Vermeer 605) came in 1972. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baler
