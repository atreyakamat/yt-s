## Fact check (verify before posting)

A typical cumulus cloud (~1 km³) contains ~500,000 kg (about 500 tonnes) of water; droplets ~10-20 µm with terminal fall speeds of ~1 cm/s; updrafts support them. Good (USGS/NOAA often cite ~1 million pounds).
