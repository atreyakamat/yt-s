## Fact check (verify before posting)

Arctic amplification: ice-albedo feedback plus other factors; Rantanen et al. (2022, Communications Earth & Environment) found warming within the Arctic Circle (above 66N) nearly four times the global average since 1979 (via Wikipedia 'Polar amplification'; wider Arctic above 60N ~3x for 1971-2019).

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. The Arctic warms faster than anywhere.
2. Bright ice reflects most sunlight.
3. As ice melts, dark ocean absorbs more heat.
4. That melts more ice in a loop.
5. Called ice-albedo feedback.
6. The Arctic has warmed about four times faster than average.
