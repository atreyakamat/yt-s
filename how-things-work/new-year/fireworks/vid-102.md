## Fact check (verify before posting)

Copper(I) chloride emits blue but decomposes at high temperature; narrow stable range. Source: chemistry of pyrotechnic colours (ACS, Compound Interest).

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Blue is the hardest colour in fireworks.
2. Blue comes from copper compounds, and they are fragile.
3. Too much heat and the blue-emitting molecule breaks apart.
4. Too cool and it just fizzles out dim.
5. So makers must hit a narrow temperature window.
6. That's why a crisp blue shell is the one pros brag about.
