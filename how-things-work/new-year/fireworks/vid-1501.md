# vid-1501 · Why every firework colour is a different metal

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Every firework colour is a different metal."
- Backup 1: "The sky is a chemistry lab at midnight."
- Backup 2: "Red firework? That's strontium."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Strontium=red, barium=green, sodium=yellow, copper=blue. Standard pyrotechnic chemistry (see Compound Interest / ACS fireworks chemistry pages).

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Every firework colour is a different metal.
2. Pyrotechnicians pack metal salts into little pellets called stars.
3. Heat excites the metal's electrons, and they release that energy as light.
4. Strontium burns red. Barium burns green. Sodium burns yellow.
5. Each element gives off its own fixed colours of light.
6. So the sky show is chemistry you can see from a mile away.
