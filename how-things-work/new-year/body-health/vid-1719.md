## Fact check (verify before posting)

Water is polar and forms hydrogen bonds; non-polar oil is excluded; emulsifiers (soap, lecithin) have both polar and non-polar ends. Basic chemistry. Good.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Oil and water refuse to mix.
2. Water molecules stick strongly to each other.
3. Oil molecules can't join in that bonding.
4. So water pushes oil away into its own layer.
5. Oil is less dense, so it sits on top.
6. Soap and egg yolk help them mix.
