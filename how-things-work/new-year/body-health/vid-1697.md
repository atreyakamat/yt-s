## Fact check (verify before posting)

Lips (vermilion) lack sebaceous glands and have a thin stratum corneum; evaporation from dry air dries them; licking leads to more drying (lip-licking cheilitis). Persistent cracking merits medical advice.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Lips chap because they lack protection.
2. Lip skin is thin and has no oil glands.
3. Cold, dry air pulls water out quickly.
4. Licking makes it worse as saliva evaporates.
5. Balm seals in moisture.
6. Drink water and avoid peeling skin.
