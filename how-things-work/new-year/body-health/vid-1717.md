## Fact check (verify before posting)

Enzymatic browning via polyphenol oxidase; ascorbic/citric acid inhibits; fruit remains edible unless mouldy. Peel browning also from ripening (chlorophyll breakdown, melanin formation).

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Bananas brown when exposed to air.
2. Cut cells release an enzyme called polyphenol oxidase.
3. It reacts with oxygen and plant chemicals.
4. The reaction makes brown pigments.
5. Lemon juice slows it down with acid.
6. Browned fruit is safe to eat, but may taste softer.
