## Fact check (verify before posting)

General brewing guidance: black/herbal ~95-100 °C, green/white ~70-85 °C; hotter water extracts more catechins/tannins (bitterness/astringency). Preferences vary; times vary by tea.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Different teas need different water temperatures.
2. Black tea likes water near boiling.
3. Green tea is better a bit cooler, around 80 degrees.
4. Hot water can make green tea taste bitter.
5. Cooler water extracts less of the bitter compounds.
6. Steep for the time the packet suggests.
