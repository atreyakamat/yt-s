# vid-1660 · How to run a quick year review

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Review last year in twenty minutes."
- Backup 1: "A tiny retrospective for your year."
- Backup 2: "Look back before you leap."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Retrospective practice borrowed from agile 'retros' and journaling; reflection improves learning (Di Stefano et al. 2014, learning by thinking). The structure is a suggested routine, not a validated protocol.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Review last year in twenty minutes.
2. Write what went well and what didn't.
3. Note three things you're proud of.
4. List one lesson from each setback.
5. Choose one thing to keep and one to drop.
6. Looking back first makes goals more realistic.
