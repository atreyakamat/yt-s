## Fact check (verify before posting)

Egyptian decans/hours (12 night, 12 day hours of variable length) influenced the 24-hour day; Hellenistic astronomers (Hipparchus) fixed equal hours; base-60 from Mesopotamia; 60 has 12 divisors. 'Twelve from finger joints' is a folk explanation, omitted.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Why do we split days into twenty-four hours?
2. Ancient Egyptians divided daylight into twelve parts.
3. They also split night into twelve.
4. That made twenty-four parts in total.
5. Babylonians favoured sixty, giving minutes and seconds.
6. Sixty divides evenly by many numbers.
