## Hook

## Fact check (verify before posting)

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Give your year a theme, not a list.
2. A single word is easier to remember than ten goals.
3. Examples are focus, health, build or calm.
4. A theme helps you say yes or no quickly.
5. Check it against choices once a month.
6. Themes bend; strict goals can break you.
