## Fact check (verify before posting)

'Cheer' from Old French 'chiere' (face, expression), via Latin cara/Greek kara 'head'; sense shifted to mood, then encouragement; 'cheers' as a toast attested in the 20th century (OED earliest toast uses ~1900s). Reasonable; adjust date if needed.

6. Later it became a toast across many countries.
