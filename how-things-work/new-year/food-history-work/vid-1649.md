# vid-1649 · Why spicy food burns

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Spicy food tricks your nerves into feeling heat."
- Backup 1: "Your tongue thinks chillies are hot."
- Backup 2: "Why milk beats water for spice."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Capsaicin activates the TRPV1 receptor (activated by heat above ~43 °C); casein in milk binds capsaicin; capsaicin is fat-soluble, so water doesn't help. Well established (David Julius, Nobel 2021).

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Spicy food tricks your nerves into feeling heat.
2. Chillies contain a chemical called capsaicin.
3. It activates a heat sensor in your nerves.
4. That sensor normally responds to hot temperatures.
5. So your brain reads the chilli as burning.
6. Milk helps because the casein binds capsaicin.
