## Fact check (verify before posting)

In the pre-Julian Roman calendar February was the final month and intercalary days were inserted after it; Caesar's reform placed the leap day by doubling the sixth day before March Kalends (24 Feb). Today's 29 Feb dates from that tradition. Simplified.

3. Extra days were tacked onto the end of the year.
4. Later the year start moved to January.
5. February kept the odd day by tradition.
6. Leap day lands on February 29 every fourth year.
