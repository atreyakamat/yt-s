## Fact check (verify before posting)

Seven-day cycles appear in Babylonian/Jewish traditions; the planetary week (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) from Hellenistic astrology; lunar phase quarter ≈ 7.4 days. Origin is debated; the week doesn't map to an astronomical period.
