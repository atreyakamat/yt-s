# vid-1538 · How caffeine keeps you awake

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Caffeine doesn't give energy. It blocks tiredness."
- Backup 1: "Why afternoon coffee wrecks your night."
- Backup 2: "The chemical behind your tiredness."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Caffeine is an adenosine receptor antagonist; average half-life ~5 h (range ~3-7 h; varies by person, pregnancy, smoking, medications).

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Caffeine doesn't give energy. It blocks tiredness.
2. A chemical called adenosine builds up while you're awake.
3. It binds to receptors and makes you feel sleepy.
4. Caffeine fits those receptors and blocks the signal.
5. But it lasts: its half-life is about five hours.
6. So a late coffee can still hit at bedtime.
