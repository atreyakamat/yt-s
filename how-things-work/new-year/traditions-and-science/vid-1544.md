# vid-1544 · Why your battery wears out

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Your phone battery ages even if unused."
- Backup 1: "Why old phones die fast."
- Backup 2: "A charging habit that saves your battery."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Li-ion degradation: SEI growth, lithium plating, heat accelerates aging; high state-of-charge and deep discharge both accelerate wear (Battery University). Modern phones often include optimised charging.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Your phone battery ages even if unused.
2. A lithium-ion battery shuttles lithium ions back and forth.
3. Each cycle slowly damages its internal structure.
4. Heat speeds the damage up a lot.
5. Sitting at one hundred percent or zero also stresses it.
6. Keep it cool and between twenty and eighty.
