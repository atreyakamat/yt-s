# vid-1539 · Why darkness makes you sleepy

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Your brain makes a sleep signal from darkness."
- Backup 1: "Why screens at night mess with sleep."
- Backup 2: "Your body clock runs on light."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Pineal melatonin secretion rises in darkness and is suppressed by light, mainly via intrinsically photosensitive retinal ganglion cells. Morning light advances the circadian phase.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Your brain makes a sleep signal from darkness.
2. A tiny gland releases melatonin as evening light fades.
3. It tells your body that night has started.
4. Bright light at night can delay that release.
5. Morning daylight helps reset your body clock.
6. So dim lights at night, and get daylight early.
