## Fact check (verify before posting)

Delayed-onset muscle soreness typically peaks 24-72 h after exercise and resolves within ~5-7 days; lactic acid is NOT the cause (myth). Light movement may ease symptoms.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Your sore muscles peak two days later.
2. Hard or unfamiliar exercise causes tiny damage in muscle fibres.
3. Your body sends repair signals, and inflammation follows.
4. That takes time, so soreness builds over a day or two.
5. It usually fades within three to five days.
6. Light movement can help it feel better.
