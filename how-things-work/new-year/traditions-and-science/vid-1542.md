# vid-1542 · Why passkeys beat passwords

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Passkeys can't be phished like passwords."
- Backup 1: "A two-minute resolution: use passkeys."
- Backup 2: "Why your next login won't need a password."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Passkeys use WebAuthn/FIDO2 public-key credentials bound to the site's origin, which makes them resistant to phishing; biometrics/PIN unlock the local private key.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Passkeys can't be phished like passwords.
2. A passkey is a pair of cryptographic keys.
3. Your device keeps the private key secret.
4. The site only stores the public key.
5. A fake site can't ask you to type a secret.
6. A fingerprint or PIN unlocks it on your device.
