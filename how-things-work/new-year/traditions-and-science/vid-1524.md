## Fact check (verify before posting)

Champagne bottles are typically ~5-6 bar (about 5-6 atm) at cellar temperature; corks can exit around 40-50 km/h (Liger-Belair). Hedge as 'about'. Safety tip: hold cork, twist bottle.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. A champagne bottle holds tyre-level pressure.
2. Inside, dissolved gas pushes outward at about six atmospheres.
3. That's roughly the pressure in a bus tyre.
4. Pop the cork and it can leave at around fifty kilometres an hour.
5. Which is why you point it away from faces.
6. Hold the cork and twist the bottle, not the cork.
