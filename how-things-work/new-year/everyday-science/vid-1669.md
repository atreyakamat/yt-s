## Fact check (verify before posting)

Folk belief; smoke-carries-prayers idea is cited in popular histories of birthday candles (e.g. Greeks/Germans) but evidence is thin. Present as tradition, not fact.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Blowing out candles carries an old wish.
2. Many cultures saw smoke as carrying prayers upward.
3. Candles on cakes came from that idea.
4. You wish silently before the flame goes out.
5. One breath for all candles is said to guarantee it.
6. The wish is said to stay secret to come true.
