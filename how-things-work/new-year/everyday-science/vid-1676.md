## Fact check (verify before posting)

Borborygmi: sounds from gas/fluid in the GI tract; migrating motor complex produces contractions ~every 90-120 min between meals; empty stomach makes it louder. Good.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Your stomach growls even when it's full.
2. Muscles squeeze food and gas along the gut.
3. The wave of squeezing is called peristalsis.
4. Gas and fluid moving makes the noise.
5. Hunger waves between meals make it louder.
6. An empty stomach has nothing to muffle it.
