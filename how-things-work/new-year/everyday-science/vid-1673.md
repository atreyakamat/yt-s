## Fact check (verify before posting)

Self-heard voice combines air- and bone-conducted sound (bone conduction boosts low frequencies); a recording lacks the bone component; listeners hear the air-conducted voice (a microphone differs from ears but is closer).

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. You hate your voice on recordings.
2. You hear yourself through air and bone.
3. Bone conduction adds low, rich tones.
4. Recordings capture only the air-borne sound.
5. Others hear the recording version of you.
6. So the recorded voice is the real one.
