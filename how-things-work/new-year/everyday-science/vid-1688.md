# vid-1688 · Why camels have humps

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "A camel's hump isn't full of water."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Camel humps store fat (up to ~36 kg); metabolism of fat also yields metabolic water but the net water benefit is small; camels tolerate ~25-30% loss of body weight in water, and can drink ~100-150 L within minutes. Good.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. A camel's hump isn't full of water.
2. The hump stores fat, not water.
3. Fat can be turned into energy when food is scarce.
4. Storing fat in one spot helps the rest stay cool.
5. Camels survive by tolerating dehydration.
6. They can drink around a hundred litres in minutes.
