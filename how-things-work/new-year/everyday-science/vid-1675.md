## Fact check (verify before posting)

Hiccup = involuntary diaphragm/intercostal contraction followed by glottis closure (~35 ms). Common triggers: gulping, fizzy drinks, alcohol, sudden temperature change. Most episodes resolve; persistent hiccups (>48h) warrant medical attention. Not medical advice.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Hiccups are a tiny spasm in your chest.
2. Your diaphragm suddenly contracts.
3. Air rushes in and the vocal cords snap shut.
4. That snap makes the hic sound.
5. Eating fast or fizzy drinks can trigger them.
6. Most pass within minutes.
