# vid-1556 · Why paying off high-interest debt first saves money

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Pay off the costliest debt first."
- Backup 1: "Which debt should you pay first?"
- Backup 2: "The math behind avalanche vs snowball."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Avalanche (highest APR first) minimises total interest; snowball (smallest balance first) can improve motivation (Gal & McShane 2012 found progress on closing accounts predicts success). Not financial advice.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Pay off the costliest debt first.
2. Debt grows by a percentage of what you still owe.
3. A higher rate means faster growth.
4. So extra money kills the most expensive debt first.
5. This is called the avalanche method.
6. Paying the smallest first is the snowball method, which can feel better.
