# vid-1550 · Why salt melts ice on roads

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Salt doesn't melt ice. It lowers freezing."
- Backup 1: "Why gritters spread salt."
- Backup 2: "The chemistry on every icy road."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Freezing-point depression: NaCl brine eutectic is -21 °C but in practice rock salt works poorly below about -9 to -12 °C; other de-icers (CaCl2, MgCl2) work colder.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Salt doesn't melt ice. It lowers freezing.
2. Pure water freezes at zero degrees Celsius.
3. Dissolved salt gets in the way of freezing.
4. That lowers the freezing point of the salty water.
5. Road salt works down to roughly minus nine degrees.
6. Colder than that, it needs other chemicals.
