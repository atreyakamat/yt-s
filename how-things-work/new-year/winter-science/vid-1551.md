## Hook

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Mittens have lower surface-area-to-volume ratio and let fingers share heat, so they're generally warmer than gloves of similar insulation; basic heat-transfer reasoning.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Mittens keep your hands warmer than gloves.
2. In mittens, your fingers share one warm space.
3. Less surface area is exposed to the cold.
4. Heat escapes from surface, so less surface loses less.
5. Gloves give you finger dexterity, though.
6. Wear liner gloves under mittens for both.
