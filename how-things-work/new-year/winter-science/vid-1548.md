# vid-1548 · Why snowflakes have six sides

## Hook

- Backup 2: "Why snowflakes aren't triangles."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Ice Ih has hexagonal symmetry; branching growth depends on temperature and supersaturation (Libbrecht). Not all snow crystals are six-armed stars (plates, columns, needles exist).

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Every snowflake starts with six sides.
2. Water molecules lock into a hexagonal pattern when they freeze.
3. That pattern is the crystal's basic shape.
4. Each arm grows as vapour sticks to the corners.
5. Temperature and humidity shape the details.
6. That's why the arms match but flakes differ.
