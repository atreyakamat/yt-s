# vid-1554 · Does cold weather give you a cold

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Cold weather doesn't give you a cold."
- Backup 1: "Why colds peak in winter."
- Backup 2: "Your mother was partly wrong about coats."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Colds are viral (rhinoviruses etc). Foxman et al. 2015 (PNAS): rhinovirus replicated better at 33 °C than 37 °C in mouse airway cells (a lab finding, not proof in people). Winter indoor crowding and low humidity also increase spread.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Cold weather doesn't give you a cold.
2. Colds are caused by viruses, not temperature.
3. In winter, people spend more time indoors together.
4. Dry air also helps some viruses spread.
5. One study found a cold-causing virus copies better at nose-cold temperatures.
6. So wash your hands and keep your nose warm.
