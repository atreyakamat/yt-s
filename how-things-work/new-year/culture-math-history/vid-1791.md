## Fact check (verify before posting)

Only one near-complete Viking helmet survives (Gjermundbu, Norway) and has no horns; horned imagery popularised by Swedish artist Gustav Malmström and Carl Emil Doepler's costumes for the 1876 Bayreuth Ring cycle. Good.
