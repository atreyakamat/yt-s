# vid-1512 · The Year 2038 problem

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Computers have a second Y2K coming."
- Backup 1: "Your old router might break in 2038."
- Backup 2: "The day 32-bit time runs out."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Signed 32-bit time_t overflows at 03:14:07 UTC on 19 Jan 2038 and wraps to 13 Dec 1901. 64-bit time_t lasts ~292 billion years.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Computers have a second Y2K coming.
2. Many systems store time as seconds since 1 January 1970.
3. On older 32-bit systems, that number has a limit.
4. It runs out on 19 January 2038.
5. After that, the clock wraps around to 1901.
6. The fix is simple: 64-bit time, which lasts billions of years.
