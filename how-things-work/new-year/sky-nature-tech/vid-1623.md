# vid-1623 · How to find the North Star

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Find Polaris using the Big Dipper."
- Backup 1: "Two stars point to north."
- Backup 2: "The star that doesn't move."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Polaris sits within about 0.7° of the north celestial pole; the pointer stars Dubhe and Merak point to it at roughly 5x their separation. Visible only from the northern hemisphere. It's not the brightest star in the sky.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Find Polaris using the Big Dipper.
2. Look for the Big Dipper's two outer stars.
3. Draw a line through them away from the bowl.
4. Follow it about five times their gap.
5. The first bright star you meet is Polaris.
6. It sits almost exactly above Earth's north pole.
