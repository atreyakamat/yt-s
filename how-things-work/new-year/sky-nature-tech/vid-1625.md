# vid-1625 · Why the Northern Lights glow green

## Hook

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Green (557.7 nm) emission from atomic oxygen at ~100-150 km; red (630 nm) from oxygen above ~200 km; nitrogen adds blue/purple at lower altitudes. Solar wind funnelled by Earth's magnetosphere.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Auroras glow green for a reason.
2. Charged particles from the Sun hit our atmosphere.
3. Earth's magnetic field funnels them to the poles.
4. Oxygen atoms about 100 km up give off green light.
5. Higher up, oxygen can glow red.
6. Nitrogen can add blue and purple.
