## Fact check (verify before posting)

The Moon illusion is a well-known perceptual illusion; explanations (apparent distance, size-contrast, etc.) remain debated; the Moon's angular size is about 0.5 degrees and in fact slightly smaller at the horizon because of the extra distance from the observer.
