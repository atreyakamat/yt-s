# vid-1638 · Why you should clear your phone's storage

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "A full phone slows down."
- Backup 1: "Why a full phone feels slow."
- Backup 2: "Back up, then clear space."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

SSD/flash performance and write amplification worsen when nearly full; the 10-20% free guideline is common advice not a hard standard. Always back up before deleting.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. A full phone slows down.
2. Flash storage needs free space to work well.
3. Apps need room for cache and updates.
4. Photos and videos are the usual space hogs.
5. Back them up, then delete originals.
6. Aim for at least ten to twenty percent free.
