# vid-1600 · Why a rest day helps you get stronger

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Muscles grow while you rest."
- Backup 1: "Gains happen while you recover."
- Backup 2: "Why rest days aren't lazy."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Muscle protein synthesis stays elevated ~24-48 h after resistance training; rest days are a standard recommendation. Many guidelines suggest 1-2 rest days/week, individual variation. 'Tiny tears' is the common simplification (microtrauma hypothesis is debated).

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Muscles grow while you rest.
2. Training makes tiny tears in muscle fibres.
3. Rest lets your body repair and thicken them.
4. Sleep and protein support the repair.
5. Too little rest can stall progress and raise injury risk.
6. Most plans include one or two rest days a week.
