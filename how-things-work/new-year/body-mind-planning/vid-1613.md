## Fact check (verify before posting)

Oppezzo & Schwartz (2014, Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition): walking increased divergent thinking (about 60% average increase on the Guilford alternate uses test) vs sitting; effects on convergent thinking were mixed. Study sizes were modest.
