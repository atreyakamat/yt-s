# vid-1614 · Why cold showers are overhyped

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Cold showers wake you, not slim you."
- Backup 1: "What a cold shower really does."
- Backup 2: "Awake, yes. Magic, no."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

A 2016 Dutch RCT (Buijze et al., PLOS ONE) found fewer sickness-absence days with cold-ending showers but no change in illness days; evidence quality low. Cold-water immersion triggers a sympathetic response/'cold shock'. Cardiac risk exists for some. Not medical advice.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Cold showers wake you, not slim you.
2. Cold water triggers a stress response and alertness.
3. Your heart rate and breathing jump for a moment.
4. Some studies find fewer sick days, but evidence is weak.
5. It won't burn meaningful fat on its own.
6. If you have heart issues, check with a doctor.
