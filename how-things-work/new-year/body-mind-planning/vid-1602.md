# vid-1602 · Why drinking water doesn't need eight glasses

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "The eight glasses rule has no strong evidence."
- Backup 1: "The eight-glass rule is a myth."
- Backup 2: "Your thirst is smarter than a rule."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

No evidence base for a universal 8x8 oz rule; US National Academies suggest total water ~3.7 L/day for men and ~2.7 L/day for women including food and drinks (adequate intake, not requirement). Needs vary; consult guidance for medical conditions.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. The eight glasses rule has no strong evidence.
2. Your water needs depend on size, weather and activity.
3. Food and other drinks also count toward it.
4. Thirst is a reliable guide for most healthy people.
5. Pale yellow urine is a handy check.
6. More is needed when it's hot or you exercise.
