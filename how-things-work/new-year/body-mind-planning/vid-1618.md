# vid-1618 · How the 50/30/20 budget works

## Hook

## Fact check (verify before posting)

50/30/20 rule popularised by Elizabeth Warren and Amelia Warren Tyagi in 'All Your Worth' (2005). It's a rule of thumb; high-cost areas may make 50% for needs unrealistic. Not financial advice.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Split your take-home pay three ways.
2. Fifty percent goes to needs like rent and food.
3. Thirty percent goes to wants like fun and travel.
4. Twenty percent goes to savings and debt repayment.
5. Elizabeth Warren popularised the rule in a 2005 book.
6. Adjust the split to fit your own income.
