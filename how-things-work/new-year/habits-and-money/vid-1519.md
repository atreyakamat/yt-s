## Fact check (verify before posting)

Rule of 72 approximation: years ≈ 72 / rate%. Accurate to within a few percent for typical rates (6-10%). Not investment advice.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. A trick to double money in your head.
2. Take the number 72.
3. Divide it by your yearly return rate.
4. The answer is roughly the years needed to double.
5. At 6 percent, money doubles in about 12 years.
6. At 9 percent it takes about 8.
