# vid-116 · How long a habit really takes to form

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Forget 21 days. Habits take longer."
- Backup 1: "The 21 day habit rule is a myth."
- Backup 2: "The real number is 66, but it varies."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Lally et al. 2009 (Eur J Social Psychology): median 66 days to automaticity, range 18-254. Missing one opportunity did not materially affect habit formation. The 21-day idea is often traced to Maxwell Maltz's 1960 book.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Forget 21 days. Habits take longer.
2. The famous 21-day rule never came from a real study.
3. A University College London study tracked people building new habits.
4. On average, it took 66 days to feel automatic.
5. But the range was huge, from 18 days to 254.
6. And missing one day didn't ruin it.
