## Fact check (verify before posting)

Greek custom: hanging a sea squill (kremmida/skilokremmydo) on the door for growth/rebirth; some parents tap children's heads on New Year's Day. Regional variations.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Greeks hang an onion on the door.
2. It's called a kremmida, a sea onion.
3. Onions keep sprouting, even when hung up.
4. So they stand for growth and rebirth.
5. On New Year's Day, parents tap kids' heads with it.
6. It's a wish for a healthy, growing year.
