## Fact check (verify before posting)

Joya no Kane: Buddhist temples ring 108 times; 107 usually before midnight and the last after. 108 explained as bonno (earthly desires); interpretations vary by sect.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Japan rings temple bells one hundred eight times.
2. It's called Joya no Kane, held on New Year's Eve.
3. Many Buddhists link 108 to human worldly desires.
4. Each ring is said to clear one of them.
5. The last ring usually sounds after midnight.
6. A fresh start with a clean slate.
