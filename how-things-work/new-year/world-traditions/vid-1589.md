# vid-1589 · Why Ethiopia celebrates New Year in September

## Hook

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Ethiopian calendar: 13 months (12 x 30 days + Pagume with 5 or 6 days); new year (Enkutatash) usually 11 Sept (12 Sept in Gregorian leap-year-following years); about 7-8 years behind the Gregorian calendar due to a different Annunciation calculation.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Ethiopia is seven years behind the calendar.
2. Its calendar has thirteen months.
3. Twelve months have thirty days each.
4. The thirteenth month is just five or six days.
5. New Year, Enkutatash, falls around September 11.
6. That's why Ethiopia says it's seven to eight years back.
