## Fact check (verify before posting)

Italian New Year's tradition: lenticchie (coin-shaped, swell when cooked) with cotechino or zampone for prosperity. Folk belief.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Italians eat lentils for luck at midnight.
2. Lentils look like tiny coins.
3. They swell when cooked, like growing wealth.
4. Usually served with pork sausage called cotechino.
5. Many Italians eat them just after midnight.
6. More lentils is said to mean more money.
