## Fact check

- Glue sticks typically use sodium stearate (a soap) as a gelling agent, with water and an adhesive polymer such as PVP.
- The gel is solid at rest and shears into a smearable film when rubbed.
- Disappearing-colour glue sticks use an indicator dye that loses its colour as the glue dries.
- Without the cap, water evaporates and the stick hardens.
