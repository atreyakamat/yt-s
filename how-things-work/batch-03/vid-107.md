## Fact check

- SSDs use NAND flash and have no moving parts: definition of a solid-state drive.
- Flash cells store data as charge on a floating gate or charge-trap layer surrounded by insulation; charge is pushed in by tunnelling under high voltage.
- Flash is non-volatile, so data is kept with power off.
- MLC/TLC cells store 2/3 bits using 4/8 charge levels; the bars show bits per cell (1, 2, 3).
- Flash cells have limited program/erase cycles; controllers use wear levelling to spread writes.
