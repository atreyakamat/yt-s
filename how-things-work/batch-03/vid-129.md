## Fact check

- James Naismith invented basketball in December 1891 at the YMCA training school in Springfield, Massachusetts, to keep a class active indoors in winter.
- He used peach baskets fixed to the gym's balcony about 10 feet high; the regulation rim height is still 10 feet.
- The baskets had bottoms, so the ball was retrieved after each score (by ladder or a person on the balcony).
- The first game used a soccer ball and is reported to have ended 1-0.
- Backboards were introduced in the 1890s, partly to stop balcony spectators interfering with shots.
- Open-bottomed nets came into use in the early 1900s.
