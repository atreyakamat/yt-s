## Fact check

- France offered a prize (1795) for a method of preserving food; Nicolas Appert's sealed-and-boiled glass jar method won it (around 1809-1810). Microbes weren't understood yet.
- Peter Durand patented the tin can (tin-plated iron) in Britain in 1810; Bryan Donkin and John Hall began a cannery around 1812-1813.
- Early cans were heavy and opened with a hammer and chisel.
- The first can opener patent was Ezra Warner's, USA, 1858. 1858 - 1810 = 48 years.
