## Fact check

- Before the 1840 reform, British postage was charged by distance and number of sheets and was usually paid by the recipient.
- Recipients sometimes refused letters after reading coded marks on the cover, a known abuse cited by Rowland Hill.
- Rowland Hill proposed uniform prepaid penny postage; the Penny Black (1840) was the first adhesive postage stamp.
- UK stamps carry no country name, a privilege of being first. Bar values are illustrative.
