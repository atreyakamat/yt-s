## Fact check

- Bernard Sadow filed a patent for 'rolling luggage' in 1970 (granted 1972), so the timeline says "patent filed": a case with four wheels pulled by a strap.
- Sadow said department stores were initially reluctant, partly over the idea that men would not want to pull a bag on wheels.
- Northwest Airlines pilot Robert Plath created the upright two-wheel case with a telescoping handle in 1987, first sold to flight crews.
- Apollo 11 landed in July 1969, before the rolling suitcase patent. The grid is illustrative ('most'), not a statistic.
