## Fact check

- Stored-pressure dry powder extinguishers hold powder (often monoammonium phosphate) pressurised with nitrogen; a dip tube carries powder from the bottom to the valve.
- Fire needs heat, fuel and oxygen plus a chemical chain reaction (the fire tetrahedron); dry chemical agents smother and interrupt that chain reaction.
- Stored-pressure extinguishers have a gauge showing whether they are charged.
