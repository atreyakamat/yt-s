## Fact check

- Robert William Thomson patented a pneumatic tyre in 1845 (Britain); the idea did not catch on.
- John Boyd Dunlop, a Scottish-born veterinary surgeon in Belfast, developed a pneumatic tyre in 1888 for his son's tricycle.
- In 1895 André and Édouard Michelin entered a car on pneumatic tyres in the Paris-Bordeaux-Paris race; it suffered many punctures.
- Before pneumatic tyres, wheels used iron-shod wood and later solid rubber; air tyres absorb bumps because the air acts as a spring.
- Dunlop 1888, Thomson air tyre patent 1845, Michelin Eclair 1895: OK. Source: https://www.scotsman.com/arts-and-culture/scottish-fact-of-the-day-two-inventors-and-the-story-of-the-pneumatic-tyre-1598097
