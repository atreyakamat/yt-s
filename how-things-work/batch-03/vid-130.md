## Fact check

- Bucket brigades were the main firefighting method for centuries; hand-pumped fire engines were in use by the 1600s-1700s.
- George William Manby is credited with inventing the portable pressurised fire extinguisher in 1818: a copper vessel of potassium carbonate solution under compressed air.
- Glass 'fire grenades' filled with salt water or other liquids were sold in the later 1800s to be thrown at fires; some were mounted in wall brackets.
- Manby's portable pressurised extinguisher, 1818: OK. Source: https://labc.co.uk/news/fire-extinguisher-200-years-and-counting
- Glass fire grenades '1870s': SOFTENED to 'Late 1800s' as production dates vary. Source: https://thehenryford.org/artifact/237258
