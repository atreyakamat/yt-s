## Fact check

- A standard 3x3 cube has 6 center pieces, 12 edges and 8 corners.
- The centers are attached to a central core (spider) with screws and springs; their relative positions never change.
- Edge and corner pieces have internal 'feet' that interlock under neighboring pieces, holding the cube together.
- The 3x3 cube has about 43 quintillion (43,252,003,274,489,856,000) reachable positions.
- In 2010 it was proven that every position can be solved in 20 moves or fewer (half-turn metric).
