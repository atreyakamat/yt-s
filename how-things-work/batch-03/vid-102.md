## Fact check

- Roasted coffee releases carbon dioxide for days after roasting (degassing).
- Without a vent, sealed bags of fresh coffee can puff up and burst; exposure to oxygen makes coffee stale.
- One-way degassing valves (developed in Italy in the 1960s) use a flexible disc/diaphragm, often with a thin layer of oil or silicone liquid, to seal.
- Internal pressure opens the valve; it closes again when inside pressure falls, so outside air can't enter.
