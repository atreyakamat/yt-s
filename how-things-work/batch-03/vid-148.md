## Fact check

- Small button = shorter flush → dual flush valves either lift less or close earlier on the half flush.
- About 6 L full and 3-4 L half → UK rules since 2001 cap a full flush at 6 L; common dual flush ratings are 6/4 or 6/3 L. Older UK single-flush toilets used about 9 L (US ones often more).
- Nearly 3,000 L a year → 4 flushes × about 2 L saved × 365 days = 2,920 L.
- Australia around 1980 → dual flush is credited to Bruce Thompson's design for an Australian maker, launched about 1980, prompted by water shortages.
