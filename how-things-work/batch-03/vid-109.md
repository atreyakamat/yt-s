## Fact check

- Traction elevators hang the car and a counterweight on opposite ends of the hoist ropes over a drive sheave.
- The counterweight is typically the car's weight plus about 40-50% of rated load ('roughly half').
- The motor only has to supply the imbalance, so it can be much smaller and uses less energy.
- The counterweight runs on its own guide rails in the shaft and passes the car at mid-travel when the ropes are the same length on both sides. Bar values are illustrative proportions.
