## Fact check

- Disposable lighters hold butane (often mixed with isobutane/propane) as a liquid under its own vapour pressure.
- n-Butane boils at about -0.5 °C at normal pressure, so it is a gas at room temperature unless pressurised.
- The 'flint' is ferrocerium; scraping it with a steel wheel throws hot sparks.
- Pressing the lever opens a valve; released liquid vaporises at the jet and is lit by the sparks. Releasing it closes the valve.
- Butane boils at about -1 C: OK (-0.5 C). Source: https://www.engineeringtoolbox.com/amp/butane-d_1415.html
