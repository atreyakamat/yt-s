## Fact check

- Repositionable sticky note adhesive is made of acrylic microspheres; only part of the sphere surface contacts the substrate, giving low tack.
- Spencer Silver developed the microsphere adhesive in 1968 while trying to make a strong adhesive; Art Fry used it for hymnbook bookmarks around 1974; the notes launched nationally in the US in 1980.
- Contamination such as dust and oils reduces contact area and tack, so reused notes lose grip.
- Grid counts are illustrative, not measured.
