## Fact check

- Most trainers have a rubber outsole and a foam midsole (EVA or similar), under the upper.
- EVA and similar foams are closed-cell foams: gas trapped in many tiny sealed cells; a midsole contains millions of them.
- Peak ground reaction force in running is roughly 2-3 times body weight; walking about 1.1-1.3 times.
- Foam midsoles lose cushioning over use; common guidance is replacing running shoes after several hundred kilometres (often quoted 500-800 km).
