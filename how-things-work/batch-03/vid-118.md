## Fact check

- Chamber pots and privies → the norm for most households before flushing toilets and sewers.
- Edinburgh 'gardyloo' → from French 'gardez l'eau'; residents of Edinburgh's tall tenements notoriously emptied waste into the street with this warning.
- Garderobes → medieval castle latrines, often projecting from the wall, dropping into a pit or moat.
- 1596 → Sir John Harington built a flushing water closet; Elizabeth I is said to have had one installed at Richmond Palace.
- 1775 S-bend; 1858 Great Stink; London's main sewers built c.1859-1875 → standard history of sanitation.
- Harington's flushing toilet 1596, S-bend patent 1775 (Cumming), Great Stink 1858: OK. Source: https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/a-flushing-story/
