## Fact check

- Expansion wall plugs are plastic sleeves, usually split, with fins or ribs and a collar; a screw driven in expands the sleeve against the hole wall.
- Fins stop the plug rotating as the screw turns; the collar stops it being pushed too deep.
- The holding force comes from friction and keying of the expanded plug against the hole along its length.
- A screw alone in plaster or masonry dust strips easily because the material crumbles; matching drill size to plug size is standard guidance.
