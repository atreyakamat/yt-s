## Fact check

- Before machines, household laundry ('wash day', traditionally Monday) commonly took most of a day: hauling and heating water, scrubbing, rinsing, wringing and drying.
- Washboards: a frame holding a ridged (often corrugated metal) surface used to scrub clothes.
- Electric washing machines appeared in the early 20th century (e.g. US models around 1908-1910) and spread into homes over following decades.
- Washboards are used as percussion in zydeco, skiffle and jug band music, often played with thimbles or spoons.
- Electric washing machines arrived in the early 1900s (Thor, about 1907-1908): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thor_washing_machine
