## Fact check

- A column of water about 10.2 m high exerts about 1 bar (100 kPa) of pressure, so 'every ten metres adds about one bar' is right.
- Utilities commonly fill towers during low-demand hours, often at night, and let the stored water meet daytime peaks so pumps can be sized for average demand.
- Elevated storage keeps supplying by gravity during pump or power failures for a limited time, and serves as fire reserve.
- 10 m of water is about 1 bar: OK (10.2 m exactly). Source: https://www.convertunits.com/from/meter+of+head/to/Bar
