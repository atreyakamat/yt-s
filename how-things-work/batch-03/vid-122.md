## Fact check

- Douglas Engelbart and Bill English built the first mouse prototype at SRI in 1964 with a wooden shell and two perpendicular wheels.
- It was shown publicly at the 1968 'Mother of All Demos'; the patent ('X-Y position indicator') was granted in 1970.
- The name came from the cord resembling a tail.
- Earlier input: keyboards/teletypes, punched cards, and light pens (1950s onward).
- Ball mice followed in the 1970s; optical mice became common around 2000. 'Billions sold' is a safe round claim for decades of PC use.
