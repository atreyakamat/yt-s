## Fact check

- A typical car clutch: friction disc between the engine flywheel and a pressure plate, clamped by a diaphragm spring; pressing the pedal moves a release bearing against the spring, releasing the clamp.
- Biting point: the pedal position where the disc starts to transmit torque while slipping.
- The friction disc is a wear item; prolonged slipping wears its lining faster.
