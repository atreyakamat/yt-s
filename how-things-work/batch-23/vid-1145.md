## Fact check

- Concrete shrinks as it cures and dries and moves with temperature, so it inevitably cracks.
- Control (contraction) joints are grooves tooled or sawn to about a quarter of the slab depth, creating a weak plane so the crack forms beneath the groove, in a straight, hidden line.
- Separate expansion (isolation) joints filled with compressible material let slabs expand without buckling.
