## Fact check

- Corrugating increases a sheet's second moment of area (effective depth), and bending stiffness rises steeply with depth, so thin sheet spans between purlins.
- The paper zigzag demonstration is a standard illustration of the same effect.
- Corrugation troughs channel rainwater down the roof slope.
- Henry Robinson Palmer patented corrugated iron in London in 1829; it was exported worldwide in the 1800s.
