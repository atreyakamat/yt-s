## Fact check

- A timing belt (toothed rubber) or chain drives the camshaft(s) from the crankshaft at half crank speed in four-stroke engines.
- In 'interference' engines, valve and piston paths overlap in space; if timing is lost, pistons can strike open valves, causing major damage.
- Makers specify belt replacement intervals, commonly somewhere around 60,000-100,000 miles or by age.
