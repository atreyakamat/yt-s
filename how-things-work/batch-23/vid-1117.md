## Fact check

- Cross-head recesses have tapered walls that generate an axial force pushing the driver out under torque (cam-out), so firm axial pressure is needed.
- Using an undersized tip concentrates load and strips the recess; matching tip size is standard advice.
- PH2 (size 2) is the most common cross-head size for household screws.
- Keeping the driver in line with the screw axis keeps full tip engagement.
- Many household cross screws take a size 2 tip: Wikipedia lists #2 for wood screws #5-9 and M3.5-M5; "most" softened to "many". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phillips_screw_drive
