## Fact check

- Spring-loaded spindle → common toilet roll holders use two telescoping tubes with a coil spring between them.
- Compressing the spring shortens the spindle so it fits between the holder's end cups; released, it extends and presses into them.
- The roll turns on the spindle while spring pressure keeps the spindle seated.
