## Fact check

- Glass cutters use a small hardened steel or tungsten carbide wheel; many have an oil reservoir.
- Scoring creates a shallow fissure (vent) along the line; applying bending stress propagates the crack through the glass along the score.
- Standard advice: score once with steady pressure; going over a score again damages the edge and the wheel and gives poor breaks.
- A good score typically produces a steady fine hissing or 'zipping' sound; lubrication improves the score and wheel life.
