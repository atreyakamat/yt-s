## Fact check

- Wide teeth for detangling, fine teeth for smoothing/parting → standard hairdressing use of a dual-tooth comb.
- Wet hair stretches more → hair is more elastic when wet (it can stretch noticeably further before breaking).
- Fine teeth snag more knots → smaller gaps catch tangles rather than letting them pass.
