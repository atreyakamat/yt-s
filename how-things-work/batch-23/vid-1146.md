## Fact check

- ISO 838 specifies two holes of about 6 mm diameter with centres 80 mm apart, used widely outside North America.
- US three-hole punch spacing is 4.25 inches (about 108 mm) between centres.
- Four-hole systems (e.g. 80 mm spacing repeated) are used in some countries such as Sweden and parts of Europe.
- Gauge is illustrative.
- ISO 838: two holes 80 mm apart, 6 mm diameter; US letter three-hole 4.25 in (108 mm); four-hole systems exist: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hole_punch
