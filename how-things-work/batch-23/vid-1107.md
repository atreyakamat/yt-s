## Fact check

- Ketchup is a shear-thinning (non-Newtonian) fluid with a yield stress: thick at rest, runnier when force is applied.
- The common advice is to tilt the bottle and tap firmly near the neck so the ketchup at the opening is sheared and starts flowing; hitting the base moves the whole mass without shearing it near the opening.
- Once flowing, it can thin suddenly and come out quickly.
