## Fact check

- Sports ball valves are typically butyl or silicone plugs with a self-sealing slit.
- Manufacturers advise moistening the inflation needle (water or a recommended valve lubricant) before inserting it to avoid damaging the valve.
- A damaged valve can cause slow leaks.
- Inserting and removing the needle straight avoids tearing; many balls print the recommended pressure near the valve.
