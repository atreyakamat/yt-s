## Fact check

- Detachable chairlifts and gondolas run a continuously moving haul rope around bullwheels at each terminal.
- Carriers attach with spring-loaded grips that clamp the rope.
- In terminals, the grip is opened by a cam/rail, and the carrier detaches and is moved by tyre conveyors (rotating tyres).
- Carriers are slowed for loading and accelerated to rope speed before re-gripping. Rope speeds are typically around 5 m/s versus about 1 m/s or less for boarding; the bars show m/s.
- Detachable chairlifts: cable about 5 m/s, station speed about 1 m/s: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detachable_chairlift
