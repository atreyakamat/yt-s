## Fact check

- Hard hats commonly have a moulded 'date clock' inside showing month and year of manufacture.
- UV exposure degrades polymers such as HDPE, making shells more brittle over time.
- Manufacturers specify service lives; common guidance is replacing suspensions about yearly and shells after around 2-5 years, varying by maker and use. Bars use typical figures.
