## Fact check

- Flatbed scanners use a moving carriage with a light source and a linear image sensor (CCD with mirrors and lens, or a contact image sensor).
- Colour is captured as red, green and blue channels via filters or switched LEDs.
- A stepper motor advances the carriage line by line; an A4 page (297 mm ≈ 11.7 in) at 300 dpi is about 3,500 lines.
- Movement of the original during scanning causes distortion.
- A4 is 297 mm tall = 11.69 in x 300 dpi = about 3,500 lines: arithmetic, OK.
