## Fact check

- High-speed trains entering tunnels generate compression waves that can emerge at the exit as a 'tunnel boom' (micro-pressure wave).
- JR West engineer Eiji Nakatsu, a birdwatcher, drew on the kingfisher's beak for the 500 Series Shinkansen nose (1997).
- A long, gradually changing cross-section reduces the compression wave strength.
- The redesign was reported to be quieter and use around 15% less electricity while running faster.
