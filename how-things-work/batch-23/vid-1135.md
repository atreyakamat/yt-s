## Fact check

- Radiation protection aprons contain lead (or lead-equivalent composites) dispersed in flexible rubber/vinyl, commonly 0.25-0.5 mm lead equivalent.
- High atomic number, dense materials absorb X-rays strongly (photoelectric absorption scales steeply with atomic number); lead has atomic number 82. Bars are illustrative.
- Aprons are stored hung or flat because folding can crack the shielding. Lighter lead-free composites use metals such as bismuth, tungsten or antimony.
