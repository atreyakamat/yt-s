## Fact check

- The diamond-shaped two-slot patch on backpacks is a lash tab (lash point), nicknamed the 'pig snout' or 'pig nose'.
- Cord or straps threaded through the slots secure gear to the outside of the pack.
- It comes from climbing and hiking packs, where it held gear such as ice axes and mats.
- A sewn patch distributes load over more stitching than a single narrow loop.
