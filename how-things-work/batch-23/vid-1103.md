## Fact check

- High-visibility garments combine fluorescent background material with retroreflective tape (standards such as EN ISO 20471).
- Fluorescent dyes absorb UV and short wavelengths and re-emit visible light, so they look brighter than ordinary colours in daylight.
- Retroreflective tape uses glass microbeads backed by a reflective layer, or microprisms, to return light toward its source.
- Because a driver sits close to their headlights, the returned light reaches their eyes.
