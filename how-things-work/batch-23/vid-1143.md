## Fact check

- A parabolic mirror reflects rays from its focus into a parallel beam; headlamp reflectors are based on this.
- Satellite dishes use the same geometry in reverse, concentrating incoming parallel waves at the focus.
- Modern 'free-form' multi-facet reflectors are computer-designed to shape the beam and produce the sharp cut-off of dipped beams.
