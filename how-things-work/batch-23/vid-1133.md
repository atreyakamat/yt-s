## Fact check

- Sapphire is crystalline aluminium oxide (corundum); colourless synthetic sapphire is used for watch crystals.
- Aluminium oxide melts at about 2,050 °C; synthetic crystals (boules) are grown from the melt.
- Boules are sliced and shaped with diamond tools, then polished.
- Sapphire is 9 on the Mohs scale; diamond is 10; mineral glass is around 5.5; acrylic is far softer.
- Sapphire is very scratch resistant but brittle, so it can chip or crack under sharp impact.
- Sapphire is Mohs 9, diamond 10; melting point about 2,030-2,050 C ("over 2,000" OK). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapphire
