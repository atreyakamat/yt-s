## Fact check

- Stop at about 38°C → thermostatic shower valves commonly have a safety stop around 38°C that needs a button press to override.
- Body temperature about 37°C → standard figure.
- 60°C scalds in seconds → widely cited burn data: water at 60°C can cause a full-thickness burn in about 5 s for adults.
- Wax capsule thermostat → most thermostatic mixers use a wax element that expands with temperature to move the mixing shuttle.
- Fail-safe → thermostatic valves are designed to shut off (or nearly) the hot flow if the cold supply fails; 'most' used because designs vary.
