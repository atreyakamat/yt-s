## Fact check

- Standard box graters have coarse and medium/fine shredding holes, a rasp-style side with small sharp burrs for zest, hard cheese, nutmeg or ginger, and a single blade slot for slicing.
- The cutting holes have raised edges facing one way, so they cut on one stroke direction only.
- Laying the grater on its side to grate is a widely shared tip that keeps gratings contained.
