# vid-2803 · Why bread goes stale

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Stale bread isn't just dried out."
- Backup 1: "Why bread goes hard without drying out."
- Backup 2: "The starch change behind stale bread."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Bread staling is mainly starch retrogradation plus moisture redistribution; it is fastest at fridge temperatures and slowed by freezing; reheating reverses part of it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staling

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Stale bread isn't just dried out.
2. Starch in bread slowly re-crystallises as it ages.
3. That process is called retrogradation.
4. Water drifts from the crumb to the crust, which goes soft.
5. A fridge speeds staling up, but a freezer slows it.
6. Warming stale bread reverses part of the change.
