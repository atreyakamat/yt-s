## Fact check (verify before posting)

Alliinase converts alliin to allicin when garlic cells are damaged; more cell damage generally gives a stronger flavour. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allicin

4. The result is allicin, the sharp smell.
5. Finely chopped garlic makes more than a whole clove.
