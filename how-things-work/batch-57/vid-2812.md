# vid-2812 · Why copper pans heat evenly

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Copper heats food more evenly than steel."
- Backup 1: "Why chefs love copper pans."
- Backup 2: "The metal that spreads heat."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Copper thermal conductivity is about 400 W/m-K versus roughly 15-25 for stainless steel; clad cookware uses copper or aluminium cores. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cookware_and_bakeware

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Copper heats food more evenly than steel.
2. Copper conducts heat very well.
3. It carries heat sideways, not only upwards.
4. Stainless steel conducts far less.
5. That's why many steel pans have a copper or aluminium core.
6. Copper reacts with acids, so many pans are lined.
