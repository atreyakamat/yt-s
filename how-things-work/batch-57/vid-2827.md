# vid-2827 · Why you rinse rice

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Rinsing rice changes how it cooks."
- Backup 1: "Fluffy or sticky: the rinse decides."
- Backup 2: "The cloudy water from rice."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Surface starch on milled rice gelatinises and makes grains clump; rinsing reduces it. Sushi and risotto styles may need starch. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Rinsing rice changes how it cooks.
2. Rice grains have loose starch on the surface.
3. That starch turns sticky in hot water.
4. Rinsing washes it off, for separate grains.
5. For sticky dishes like risotto, skip the rinse.
6. Rinse until the water runs mostly clear.
