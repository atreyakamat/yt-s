# vid-2822 · Why salt pulls water out of vegetables

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Salt makes vegetables sweat."
- Backup 1: "Why salted cucumber turns watery."
- Backup 2: "The osmosis trick in the kitchen."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Salt creates an osmotic gradient that draws water out of plant cells, used to remove moisture before cooking and in curing and pickling. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osmosis

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Salt makes vegetables sweat.
2. Water moves toward the saltier side of a cell wall.
3. That movement is called osmosis.
4. Salted aubergine or cucumber weeps liquid within minutes.
5. It's also how salt cures meat and makes pickles.
6. Pat the pieces dry and they cook crisper, not soggy.
