# vid-2804 · Baking soda vs baking powder

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Baking soda and powder are not the same."
- Backup 1: "Don't swap these two."
- Backup 2: "The acid that makes cakes rise."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Baking soda = sodium bicarbonate (needs acid); baking powder = bicarbonate plus acid salts (and starch), so it reacts with moisture and heat. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baking_powder

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Baking soda and powder are not the same.
2. Baking soda is pure sodium bicarbonate, a base.
3. It needs an acid in the recipe to make gas.
4. Baking powder already contains its own acid.
5. Swapping them without adjusting can taste soapy or flat.
