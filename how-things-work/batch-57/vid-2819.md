# vid-2819 · Why salt makes ice cream freeze

## Hook

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Salt causes freezing-point depression, so ice-salt brine can reach about -21 C (eutectic for NaCl), cold enough to freeze ice cream. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freezing-point_depression

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Salt helps freeze ice cream.
2. Salt lowers the melting point of ice.
3. So ice melts below its normal zero degrees.
4. Melting takes heat from the cream's container.
5. The mix gets cold enough to freeze while you churn.
