## Fact check (verify before posting)

Searing does not seal in juices (tested by food scientists); it drives the Maillard reaction for flavour; moisture loss depends on internal temperature. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Searing

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Searing a steak doesn't seal in juices.
2. The crust is not waterproof, so moisture still escapes.
3. What searing really adds is flavour.
4. The browning reaction creates hundreds of tasty compounds.
5. So sear for taste, and watch the inside temperature for juiciness.
