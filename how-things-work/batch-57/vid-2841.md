# vid-2841 · Why tomatoes lose flavour in the fridge

## Hook

- Backup 1: "Why cold tomatoes taste of nothing."
- Backup 2: "The fridge mistake for tomatoes."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Chilling ripe tomatoes below about 12 C reduces volatile flavour compounds (Zhang et al., 2016 PNAS); texture also suffers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomato

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. The fridge strips flavour from tomatoes.
2. Tomato flavour comes from delicate aroma compounds.
3. Chilling cuts the genes that make those compounds.
4. The change can't be undone by warming up later.
5. Keep them on the counter until they're ripe.
