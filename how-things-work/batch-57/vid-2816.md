# vid-2816 · Why espresso has crema

## Hook

- Backup 1: "What the golden layer on espresso is."
- Backup 2: "Why fresh coffee has more crema."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Crema is a foam of CO2 bubbles stabilised by emulsified coffee oils, formed when pressurised water releases dissolved gas; fresher beans give more crema. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crema_(coffee)

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Espresso wears a golden foam.
2. Hot water is forced through coffee at high pressure.
3. It carries tiny coffee oils and dissolves lots of carbon dioxide.
4. When the pressure drops, the gas forms bubbles.
5. The oils stabilise them as a foam on top.
