# vid-2848 · Why egg whites turn white when cooked

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Clear egg white turns solid white."
- Backup 1: "Why heat changes an egg's colour."
- Backup 2: "What cooking does to egg white."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Heat denatures albumen proteins (ovalbumin sets near 80 C; white starts setting around 62 C); the aggregated network scatters light, making the white opaque. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg_white

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Clear egg white turns solid white.
2. Egg white is mostly water and folded proteins.
3. Heat unfolds the proteins.
4. They tangle together into a network.
5. That network scatters light, so it looks white.
6. Cook it longer and the network tightens, turning rubbery.
