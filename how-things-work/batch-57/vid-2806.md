## Fact check (verify before posting)

Typical culinary salt amounts raise water's boiling point by a fraction of a degree (boiling point elevation); the main effect is seasoning. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boiling-point_elevation

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Pasta water needs salt for flavour.
2. Salt seasons the pasta from the inside as it soaks.
3. It does raise the boiling point, but only slightly.
4. You'd need a lot more salt to cook noticeably faster.
5. Add it once the water boils, then taste the water.
