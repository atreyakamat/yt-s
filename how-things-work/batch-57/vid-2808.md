# vid-2808 · Why boiled eggs get a green ring

## Hook

- Backup 1: "The ring on an overcooked yolk."
- Backup 2: "Why long-boiled eggs turn green."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

The green-grey ring is ferrous sulfide formed from hydrogen sulfide in the white and iron in the yolk; it is harmless; cooling eggs promptly reduces it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg_as_food

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Overcooked eggs grow a green ring.
2. Egg whites release sulfur as they cook.
3. Yolks contain iron.
4. With long heat they meet at the yolk's edge.
5. The result is harmless iron sulfide.
6. Cool eggs in cold water right after cooking.
