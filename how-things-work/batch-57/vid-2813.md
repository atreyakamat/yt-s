# vid-2813 · Why cast iron stays hot

## Hook

- Backup 1: "A pan that remembers its heat."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Cast iron has high volumetric heat capacity relative to thin pans, so it resists temperature drops, but conducts heat less than aluminium or copper. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cast_iron_cookware

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Cast iron holds heat like a battery.
2. It's thick, so it stores a lot of heat.
3. Dropping in cold food barely cools the pan.
4. But it heats slowly and unevenly at first.
5. Preheat it gently so the whole pan evens out.
