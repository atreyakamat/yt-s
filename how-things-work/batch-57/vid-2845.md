# vid-2845 · Why sparkling water tastes sharp

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Sparkling water bites your tongue."
- Backup 1: "The sting in sparkling water."
- Backup 2: "Why fizzy water is sharp."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Carbonation is detected via carbonic anhydrase enzyme activity converting CO2 to acid on sour-sensing taste cells, plus somatosensory (bubble) effects. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbonated_water

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Sparkling water bites your tongue.
2. Dissolved carbon dioxide makes carbonic acid.
3. Nerve cells in your mouth sense that acid.
4. The bubbles add touch and a prickling feel.
5. So the sharpness is partly taste, partly feel.
6. Flat water loses that bite as the gas escapes.
