## Fact check (verify before posting)

Honing realigns a rolled or bent edge; sharpening removes material on a whetstone or grinder to form a new edge. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honing_steel

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. A honing steel doesn't really sharpen.
2. A used knife edge bends over, like a bent wire.
3. Honing pushes that edge back into line.
4. It removes almost no metal.
5. When the edge is worn away, you need a stone to sharpen.
