# vid-2838 · Why brine makes meat juicier

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Brining lets muscle proteins retain more water and reduces cooking loss; it seasons the meat throughout. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brining

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Soaking meat in brine keeps it juicy.
2. Salt loosens muscle proteins.
3. Loosened proteins can hold more water.
4. Less moisture leaks out during cooking.
5. Cooked brined meat is more tender and seasoned inside.
6. Too long in brine makes meat salty and spongy.
