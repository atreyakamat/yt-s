# vid-2840 · Why potatoes turn sweet in the fridge

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Cold storage turns potatoes sweeter."
- Backup 1: "Don't refrigerate your potatoes."
- Backup 2: "Why cold-stored fries go dark."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Cold-induced sweetening converts starch to reducing sugars below about 6-8 C; these raise acrylamide formation when fried or baked. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acrylamide

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Cold storage turns potatoes sweeter.
2. Cold makes potatoes turn starch into sugar.
3. Frying that sugar can darken fries too much.
4. It can also form more acrylamide, a harmful compound.
5. Store potatoes somewhere cool and dark, not the fridge.
