# vid-2833 · Why yogurt thickens

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Lactic acid bacteria ferment lactose to lactic acid; falling pH destabilises casein micelles, forming a gel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogurt

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Yogurt is milk changed by bacteria.
2. Bacteria eat lactose, the milk sugar.
3. They make lactic acid as waste.
4. The acid makes milk proteins clump into a gel.
5. That gel is the thick, tangy texture.
6. Warmth speeds the bacteria up, and cold slows them.
