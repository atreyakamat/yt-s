# vid-2837 · Why tempura batter uses ice-cold water

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Cold water and minimal mixing limit gluten development, giving a lighter, crisper fried coating. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempura

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Tempura batter is made with iced water.
2. Cold water slows gluten from forming.
3. Less gluten means a light, brittle crust.
4. The big temperature gap also drives off moisture quickly.
5. Lumpy batter is fine: don't overmix.
6. Fry at a hot, steady temperature so it stays light.
