# vid-2817 · Why tea turns bitter if you steep it too long

## Hook

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Longer steeping extracts more polyphenols (tannins) that are astringent and bitter; recommended steep times vary by tea type. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Steep tea too long and it turns bitter.
2. Hot water first pulls out flavour and caffeine.
3. Over time, it extracts more tannins too.
4. Tannins taste bitter and dry out your mouth.
5. So time the steep: a few minutes, not ten.
