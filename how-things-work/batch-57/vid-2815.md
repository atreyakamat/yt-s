## Fact check (verify before posting)

Cold brew extracts differently from hot brewing (lower extraction rate of some acids and bitter compounds; typically 12-24 hour steep). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_brew

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Cold brew tastes less sharp than hot.
2. Cold water extracts compounds more slowly.
3. Some bitter and acidic compounds dissolve less.
4. It steeps for many hours instead of minutes.
5. You end up with a different flavour, not just a weaker one.
