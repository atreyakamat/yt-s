## Fact check (verify before posting)

Heating milk denatures casein and whey proteins; evaporation at the surface concentrates them into a film (skin) that traps fat. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milk_skin

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Hot milk grows its own skin.
2. Heat unfolds proteins at the surface.
3. Water evaporates there, which concentrates them.
4. The proteins link into a thin film with fat.
5. Stir, or cover the pan, and the film can't form.
