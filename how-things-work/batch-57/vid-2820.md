## Fact check (verify before posting)

Beer head forms from CO2 bubbles stabilised by proteins and hop-derived iso-alpha acids; lipids and detergent residue destabilise it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_(beer)

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Beer foam comes from gas and protein.
2. Beer holds dissolved carbon dioxide.
3. Pouring makes it escape as bubbles.
4. Proteins and hop compounds stabilise the bubbles.
5. Grease from lips or a dirty glass kills the head.
6. Pouring at an angle first keeps the foam manageable.
