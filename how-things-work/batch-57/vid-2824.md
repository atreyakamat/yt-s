## Fact check (verify before posting)

Bromelain is a protease that digests gelatin; heating (canning) denatures it so cooked pineapple does not prevent gelling. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromelain

## Voiceover (read-through)

3. Fresh pineapple holds an enzyme, bromelain.
4. It chops proteins into small pieces.
5. Cooked or canned pineapple is fine, since heat destroys it.
6. The same enzyme is used in meat tenderisers.
