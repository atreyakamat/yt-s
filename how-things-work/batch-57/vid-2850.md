## Fact check (verify before posting)

Condensation of steam releases latent heat (about 2,260 kJ/kg), several times the heat released by cooling the same mass of water from 100 C to skin temperature. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enthalpy_of_vaporization

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Steam burns worse than boiling water.
2. Steam at 100 degrees carries hidden heat.
3. It releases that heat when it turns back to water.
4. That adds to the heat from the temperature itself.
5. So steam delivers much more energy to your skin.
