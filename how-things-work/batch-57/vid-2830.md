## Fact check (verify before posting)

Caramelisation of sucrose begins at roughly 160-170 C; it differs from the Maillard reaction since no amino acids are needed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caramelization

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Caramel is sugar being broken down.
2. Heated sugar first melts into a clear liquid.
3. Past about 170 degrees, molecules break apart.
4. They recombine into new brown, bitter-sweet compounds.
5. Too long and it becomes bitter and burnt.
