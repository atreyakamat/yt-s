# vid-2811 · Why knives go dull

## Hook

- Backup 1: "Why a kitchen knife loses its edge."
- Backup 2: "Your cutting board is dulling your knife."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Edge dulling comes from deformation and microchipping; harder cutting surfaces (glass, stone) cause faster wear than wood or plastic. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knife_sharpening

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Knives dull because the edge deforms.
2. A sharp edge is only a few microns wide.
3. Hard foods and boards chip and roll it over.
4. Glass and stone boards wear it fastest.
5. Wood or plastic boards are kinder to the blade.
