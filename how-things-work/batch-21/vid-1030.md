## Fact check

- Shorter wavelengths carry more energy per photon: UV > blue > red. Bars are illustrative.
- Many drugs are photosensitive and can degrade on exposure to UV and visible light.
- Amber glass absorbs UV and much of the blue end of the visible spectrum (below roughly 450-500 nm), protecting light-sensitive contents; pharmacopoeias specify light-resistant containers.
- Light reacting with hop compounds in beer causes a 'skunky' off-flavour; brown bottles reduce this.
