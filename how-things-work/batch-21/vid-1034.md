## Fact check

- Spring roller blinds contain a torsion spring in the roller, wound as the blind is pulled down.
- A pawl-and-ratchet at the end locks the roller when it's stationary or slow.
- A quick pull and release spins the roller fast enough that the pawls are held clear (centrifugally), so the spring retracts the blind.
