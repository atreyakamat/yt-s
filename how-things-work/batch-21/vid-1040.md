## Fact check

- Real-time passenger information systems use automatic vehicle location: on-board GPS units report position over mobile data at intervals from seconds to about a minute.
- Arrival predictions combine remaining distance with typical or historical running speeds for that time of day.
- Predictions are displayed on stop screens and in apps, and can change when traffic conditions change.
