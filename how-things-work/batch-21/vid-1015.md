## Fact check

- An uncoated lens reflects about 4% of light at each surface (more for higher-index plastics), around 8% in total; good AR coatings cut this to around 1% or less.
- AR coatings are thin-film stacks with thicknesses on the order of a quarter wavelength (about 100 nm), thinner than a soap film.
- Reflections from layer boundaries interfere destructively when out of phase by half a wavelength.
- Cancellation is imperfect across all wavelengths, so a faint residual colour (often green or purple) remains.
