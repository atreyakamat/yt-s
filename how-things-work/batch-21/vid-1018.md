## Fact check

- Limescale is mostly calcium carbonate → deposited when hard water evaporates.
- Rubber/silicone 'easy-clean' nozzles → many shower heads have flexible nozzles that makers say can be wiped or rubbed to remove scale.
- Rigid scale cracks when its base flexes → brittle mineral crust can't follow the bending rubber.
- Heavier scale may still need descaling → hence 'often no soaking needed'.
