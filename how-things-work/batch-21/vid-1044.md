## Fact check

- A B-flat trumpet's tubing is about 1.4-1.5 m (roughly 4.5-4.9 ft) long when unwound.
- The player's buzzing lips set the vibration; a fixed tube supports only certain notes (the harmonic series), with gaps between them.
- Piston valves route air through extra tubing loops, lengthening the tube and lowering pitch.
- Valve 2 lowers by a semitone, valve 1 by a whole tone, valve 3 by three semitones.
- Three valves give 2^3 = 8 combinations (including none), filling in the chromatic gaps.
