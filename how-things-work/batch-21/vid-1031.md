## Fact check

- Cone drippers concentrate the coffee grounds into a deeper bed at the narrow end, so water flows through more coffee; flat-bottom baskets give a wider, shallower bed.
- Many cone drippers have internal ribs that keep the paper filter off the wall, letting water drain and air escape through the sides as well as the tip.
- A deeper or finer bed adds resistance and slows drawdown.
