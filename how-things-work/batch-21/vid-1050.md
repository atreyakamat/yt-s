## Fact check

- Tungsten's resistance rises steeply with temperature; a filament's cold resistance is roughly a tenth to a fifteenth of its hot resistance, so inrush current is roughly 10x normal.
- A filament reaches operating temperature in roughly tens of milliseconds to around 0.1 s, after which current settles.
- Evaporation over life thins parts of the filament; thin sections heat fastest during inrush and are where it typically breaks, which is why bulbs commonly fail at switch-on.
