## Fact check

- Ship hulls are divided by transverse watertight bulkheads into compartments, with watertight doors.
- Flooding is contained to damaged compartments, preserving buoyancy.
- Subdivision rules (SOLAS) require ships to survive specified flooding scenarios, which vary by ship type and size.
- Titanic could stay afloat with its first four compartments flooded; the iceberg breached the first five or six, and water spilled over bulkheads that did not reach high enough.
