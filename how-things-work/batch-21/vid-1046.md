## Fact check

- Blade grinders use a spinning blade that shatters beans randomly, giving a wide range of particle sizes (fines and boulders).
- Burr grinders grind beans between two burrs (flat discs or conical); particles exit only when small enough to pass the set gap, giving more uniform size.
- Adjusting burr spacing sets grind size: fine for espresso, coarse for French press (cafetière).
- Uneven particle sizes extract unevenly: fines over-extract (bitter), boulders under-extract (sour).
