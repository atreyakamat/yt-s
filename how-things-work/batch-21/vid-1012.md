## Fact check

- A bench vice uses a lead screw; one turn advances the jaw by the thread lead, typically a few millimetres.
- Example figures: a handle radius of about 15 cm gives about 940 mm of hand travel per turn; with about 5 mm per turn the ideal mechanical advantage is about 190, so 'well over 100x on paper' holds. Friction reduces the real figure.
- Screw threads of this kind are self-locking, so the vice does not back off under load.
- Vice jaws are hardened and serrated; soft jaw covers (aluminium, plastic, wood) protect delicate work.
