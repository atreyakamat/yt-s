## Fact check

- Deep frying cooks food from the outside in; a thick solid disc risks an overdone exterior before the centre is cooked.
- A ring shape means no part of the dough is far from the oil, so it cooks evenly and quickly (typically about a minute or two per side).
- Filled doughnuts are made without a hole from smaller, lighter dough pieces and filled after frying.
- Cut-out centres are commonly fried and sold as 'doughnut holes'.
