## Fact check

- FHWA: a four-leg intersection has 32 vehicle-to-vehicle conflict points; a single-lane roundabout has 8.
- Roundabout geometry forces low entry speeds and turns right-angle collisions into low-angle ones.
- IIHS studies of US intersections converted to roundabouts found about a 75% drop in injury crashes and about 90% drop in fatal crashes.
- The UK made the 'give way to traffic on the roundabout' priority rule general in 1966.
- 8 conflict points, about 75 percent fewer injury crashes, 90 percent fewer deadly: OK, a US study found 76 percent fewer injuries and 90 percent fewer serious injuries and deaths; UK priority rule 1966. The 32 figure fits a plain unsignalised crossroads (signalised is 32 to 64). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roundabout
