## Fact check

- Many smartphones include a MEMS barometric pressure sensor with a thin membrane over a sealed reference cavity (piezoresistive or capacitive).
- Air pressure drops by about 12 Pa per metre near sea level; a floor of roughly 3 m changes pressure by about 36 Pa, within the resolution of phone barometers (a few Pa).
- Phones and fitness apps use it for floors climbed and to assist altitude/vertical location (e.g., emergency-call floor estimation).
