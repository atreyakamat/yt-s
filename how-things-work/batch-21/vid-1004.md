## Fact check

- Sectional garage doors commonly weigh roughly 70-150 kg, about two adults.
- Torsion springs on a shaft above the door are wound as the door closes via cables on drums, storing energy that counterbalances the door when opening.
- A properly balanced door can be lifted by hand; the opener mainly moves and guides it.
- Manufacturers warn that torsion spring adjustment is dangerous and should be done by trained technicians.
