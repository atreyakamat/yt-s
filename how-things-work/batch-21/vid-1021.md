## Fact check

- Conductive enclosures shield radio frequencies (Faraday cage effect).
- Metal-framed phones use plastic-filled 'antenna bands' or slots to divide the frame into segments that act as radiating antenna elements for cellular, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth and GPS.
- An antenna's resonant frequency depends on its electrical length (roughly a quarter or half wavelength), so segment length tunes the band.
