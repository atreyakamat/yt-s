## Fact check

- Aerosol whipped cream cans contain liquid cream with nitrous oxide dissolved under pressure.
- Nitrous oxide is fat-soluble and doesn't make the cream acidic.
- When dispensed, pressure drops and the dissolved gas forms bubbles, foaming the cream instantly; it can expand to around four times its volume.
- CO2 is not used because it forms carbonic acid, giving a sour, fizzy taste.
