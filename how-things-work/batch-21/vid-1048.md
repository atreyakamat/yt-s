## Fact check

- Perforated foil → foil shavers use a thin perforated metal foil (well under 0.1 mm, thinner than typical paper).
- Oscillating cutter → a block of blades moves side to side under the foil, shearing hairs against the foil's hole edges.
- Thousands of times a minute → foil shaver cutters typically oscillate several thousand to over ten thousand times per minute depending on the model.
- Foil separates cutter from skin → the core safety idea of foil shavers.
