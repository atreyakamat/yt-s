## Fact check

- Household rubber gloves are made by dip moulding: hand-shaped formers are dipped in a coagulant and then in latex (natural rubber, or synthetic nitrile), then dried and cured.
- Natural latex comes from the sap of the rubber tree (Hevea brasiliensis); vulcanisation with sulfur cross-links the rubber, making it elastic.
- Many household gloves are flock-lined with cotton fibres for comfort and easy donning; textured palms improve wet grip; a beaded (rolled) cuff reinforces the opening against tearing.
