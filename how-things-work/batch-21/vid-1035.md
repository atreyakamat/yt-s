## Fact check

- Hard water contains dissolved calcium and magnesium (largely as bicarbonates) from chalk and limestone.
- Heating drives off CO2 and precipitates calcium carbonate, the main component of limescale and of limestone/chalk.
- Acid descalers (citric, acetic, sulfamic acid) react with calcium carbonate to form soluble salts, water and carbon dioxide (the fizz).
- Scale is a poor heat conductor, so heavy scaling reduces heating efficiency.
