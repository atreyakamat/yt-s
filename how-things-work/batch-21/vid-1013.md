## Fact check

- A heel counter is a stiffener (thermoplastic or leatherboard) built into the back of a shoe around the heel.
- It stabilises the rearfoot and helps the shoe keep its shape: standard footwear construction.
- Squeezing the heel is a common test of counter stiffness.
- Crushing the heel by stepping on it can break down or permanently fold the counter.
