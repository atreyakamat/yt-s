## Fact check

- Canals change level using locks, forming steps.
- A pound lock has gates at each end and a chamber between.
- Paddles/sluices let water flow in from the upper level or out to the lower level by gravity; no pumps are needed for operation (some canals pump water to resupply summits).
- Mitre gates are pressed shut by the higher water's pressure.
