## Fact check

- Residential front doors usually open inward, keeping hinges (and removable hinge pins) on the protected side and allowing the door to be braced.
- Crowds pressing on an inward-opening door can hold it shut.
- 1883 Victoria Hall disaster (Sunderland): 183 children died at an inward-opening, bolted door, prompting laws for outward-opening exits and panic bars.
- 1903 Iroquois Theatre fire (Chicago): exits included inward-opening and locked doors; codes changed afterwards.
- Building codes now generally require exit doors from assembly spaces to open in the direction of escape.
- Iroquois Theatre fire 1903, inward-opening doors, rule changes: OK, some exits opened inward, and the fire drove outward-opening door and panic bar rules. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iroquois_Theatre_fire
