## Fact check

- Immobiliser transponders are passive RFID chips (often glass capsules a few mm long, rice-grain sized) powered by the inductive field of an antenna coil around the ignition/start area.
- Modern systems use cryptographic challenge-response; without a valid response the ECU disables fuel injection and/or ignition.
- Immobilisers became widespread in the 1990s (mandatory on new cars in the EU from 1998); research in the UK and elsewhere links them to large falls in car theft.
