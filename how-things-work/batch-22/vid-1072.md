## Fact check

- Tube/pipe cutters have a hardened cutting wheel opposite guide rollers; the wheel is fed in by a screw and the tool is rotated around the pipe, deepening a groove until it parts.
- Standard instructions: tighten a small amount each revolution; overtightening can deform thin-wall pipe.
- The process leaves an internal burr that should be removed with a reamer/deburrer.
- Cuts are clean and square, without swarf.
