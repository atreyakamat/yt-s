## Fact check

- Most bicycle helmets use expanded polystyrene (EPS) foam beneath a thin polycarbonate shell.
- EPS absorbs impact energy by crushing, lengthening the deceleration time over a few milliseconds and lowering peak force (impulse = force x time).
- EPS does not rebound, so manufacturers advise replacing a helmet after any significant impact, even without visible damage.
