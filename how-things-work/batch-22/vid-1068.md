## Fact check

- Central locking uses door lock actuators (small DC motors with reduction gears, or solenoids) driving linkage rods on each latch, commanded by the body control module.
- Many cars have speed-sensing auto-lock and automatically unlock doors when a crash is detected (airbag deployment signal).
