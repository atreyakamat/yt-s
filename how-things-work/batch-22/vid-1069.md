## Fact check

- Bicycle roller chains have alternating inner and outer link plates joined by pins, with rollers on bushings/pins.
- A typical adult bike chain has about 106-120 links/rollers, so '100+' is fair.
- Rollers engage sprocket teeth with rolling contact, reducing friction.
- Lab tests show clean, lubricated bicycle chain drives can be about 95-98% efficient.
- 'Chain stretch' is actually wear at the pins and bushings, which elongates the chain and accelerates sprocket wear.
