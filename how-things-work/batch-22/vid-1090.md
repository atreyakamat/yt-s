## Fact check

- E.D. Bullard Company created the 'Hard Boiled Hat' in 1919 from steamed canvas, glue and black paint, inspired by WWI steel helmets.
- Hard hats were required on major 1930s projects such as the Golden Gate Bridge.
- Materials moved to aluminium (1930s), fibreglass (1940s) and thermoplastics (1950s onwards). Bars show rough relative protection, illustrative only.
- 1919 hard boiled hat, Golden Gate Bridge 1933 required, fibreglass 1940s, aluminium about 1938: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_hat
