## Fact check

- VR headsets place displays a few centimetres from the eyes; humans cannot comfortably focus that close.
- Lenses (Fresnel, aspheric or pancake designs) create a virtual image at a comfortable distance (often around 1-2 m).
- Stereoscopic rendering provides each eye a view from a slightly offset camera (matching interpupillary distance), producing depth perception.
- Head tracking (IMU and/or cameras) updates the view as the head moves.
