## Fact check

- Before 1800, electricity experiments relied on electrostatic generators and Leyden jars, producing brief discharges.
- Luigi Galvani reported frog-leg twitching with dissimilar metals in the 1780s-1791 ('animal electricity').
- Alessandro Volta announced the voltaic pile in 1800: alternating zinc and copper (or silver) discs separated by brine-soaked cloth or card, giving the first continuous current.
- The volt was named in his honour, adopted in 1881.
