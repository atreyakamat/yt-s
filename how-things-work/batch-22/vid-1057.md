## Fact check

- Many cordless drills use dynamic (electric) braking: on trigger release the switch disconnects the battery and short-circuits the motor terminals (brushed motors) or the controller brakes electronically (brushless).
- A spinning permanent-magnet DC motor generates a back-EMF; shorting it drives current whose magnetic force opposes rotation, and the braking torque is proportional to speed.
- Braked drills stop in a fraction of a second, compared with coasting for a few seconds without a brake.
