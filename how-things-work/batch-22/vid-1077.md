## Fact check

- Before granulated and cube sugar were common, refined sugar was sold in tall hard cones (sugarloaves) and broken up with sugar nips/nippers.
- Jakub Kryštof Rad, director of a sugar refinery in Dačice (Moravia, now Czech Republic), reportedly invented the sugar cube after his wife cut her finger cutting sugar; he received a patent in 1843. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jakub_Kry%C5%A1tof_Rad (Moravia, 23 Jan 1843 patent). The finger detail is the traditional telling; the source says she cut herself.
- Sugar cubes are made by pressing moist sugar into moulds and drying.
