## Fact check

- PC motherboards use a coin cell (commonly CR2032) to power the real-time clock and keep firmware settings (historically in CMOS RAM) when unplugged.
- RTCs use a 32,768 Hz quartz crystal = 2^15, so 15 halvings give 1 Hz.
- Many modern boards store settings in flash, but the clock and some settings still rely on the cell.
- Cells last years (often 3-10); a dead cell causes clock resets and default settings.
