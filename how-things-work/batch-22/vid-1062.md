## Fact check

- Freezer burn is caused by sublimation of ice from the surface of frozen food, leaving dehydrated, discoloured, tough areas; oxidation also affects flavour.
- The water vapour re-deposits as frost on colder surfaces such as the packaging or freezer walls.
- Air contact and temperature fluctuations speed sublimation; airtight wrapping reduces it.
- Domestic freezers typically run at about -18 °C.
