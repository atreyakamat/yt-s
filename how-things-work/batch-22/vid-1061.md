## Fact check

- Pop-up timers contain a spring-loaded plunger held in by a fusible metal alloy (or similar compound) at the tip.
- The alloy is formulated to soften/melt at a set temperature (often around 80-85 °C / 175-185 °F); when it does, the spring pushes the indicator up almost instantly.
- Food-safety agencies such as the USDA advise also checking with a food thermometer, because the timer measures only one spot.
