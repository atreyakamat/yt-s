## Fact check

- Most bike helmets use expanded polystyrene (EPS) foam liners, which are mostly air by volume, under a thin plastic shell.
- Helmet impacts last roughly 5-15 milliseconds; the foam crushing lengthens stopping time and reduces peak acceleration. Bars are illustrative orders of magnitude.
- The smooth shell helps the helmet slide and spreads load across the liner.
- EPS crush is permanent, so manufacturers advise replacing helmets after a significant impact.
