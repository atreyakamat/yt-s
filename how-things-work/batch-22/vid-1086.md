## Fact check

- Quill pens, usually goose feathers, were the main writing tool in Europe from the Middle Ages into the 1800s; they needed regular re-cutting with a small knife, hence 'penknife'.
- Mass-produced steel nibs replaced quills during the 1800s.
- László Bíró, a Hungarian journalist, with his brother György, patented the ballpoint in 1938, inspired by quick-drying newspaper ink.
- Ballpoints spread worldwide in the 1940s-50s.
