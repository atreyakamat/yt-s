## Fact check

- On 9 September 1947, operators of the Harvard Mark II found a moth trapped in a relay and taped it into the logbook with the note 'First actual case of bug being found.'
- Grace Hopper popularised the story; the log is held by the Smithsonian's National Museum of American History.
- 'Bug' for a technical fault predates this; Thomas Edison used it in the 1870s.
- The Mark II was an electromechanical relay computer.
- Moth in Harvard Mark II relay c.1947, logbook note, Edison used bug in 1870s: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_bug
