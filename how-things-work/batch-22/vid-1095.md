## Fact check

- Glass cleaners are typically mostly water with a volatile solvent (isopropanol, ethanol or glycol ethers), small amounts of surfactant, and in some formulas ammonia.
- Streaks are residues (surfactant, minerals from hard water, oils) left as liquid evaporates; volatile components and low surfactant levels minimise residue.
- Ammonia-containing cleaners must never be mixed with bleach.
