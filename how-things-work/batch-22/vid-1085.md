## Fact check

- Manual card imprinters ('knuckle busters', 'zip-zap machines') pressed a card's embossed (raised) characters onto multi-part carbon sales slips.
- Merchants consulted printed warning bulletins listing lost or stolen card numbers, and phoned for authorisation above a floor limit.
- Embossed numbers existed for imprinting; many newer cards are flat-printed because imprinters are obsolete.
