## Fact check

- A moving sidewalk ran along the pier at the 1893 World's Columbian Exposition in Chicago; it had a slower step-on platform and a faster platform with seats (the faster roughly double the speed).
- The 1900 Paris Exposition featured a moving pavement (trottoir roulant).
- Airport moving walkways appeared in the late 1950s (e.g. Dallas Love Field, 1958).
- Modern walkways use pallets or a belt over rollers with a powered handrail, similar to escalators.
- 1893 Chicago walkway, 1900 Paris, first airport walkway: OK, 1893 Columbian Exposition, Paris 1900, Love Field Dallas 1958. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moving_walkway
