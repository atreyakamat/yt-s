## Fact check

- Commonly cited figures for US cars around 1900: about 40 % steam, 38 % electric, 22 % petrol (labelled 'roughly').
- Early EVs were quiet, clean and needed no hand crank, but lead-acid batteries limited range and speed.
- The mass-produced Model T (1908), Kettering's electric starter (1912) and cheap oil made petrol cars dominant; EVs had largely disappeared by the 1930s.
- Lithium-ion batteries enabled the modern EV revival in the 2000s-2010s.
