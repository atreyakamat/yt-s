## Fact check

- Design → rotary nose trimmers have a cutter spinning inside a slotted or perforated guard tube.
- Shearing → hairs entering the slots are cut between the rotating blade and the guard's edges.
- Guard keeps skin out → slot widths admit hairs but not skin, the same principle as foil shavers.
- Trimming leaves the root → it cuts hair rather than pulling it out.
