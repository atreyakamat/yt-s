## Fact check

- Ping/latency is round-trip time in milliseconds.
- Light in optical fibre travels about 200,000 km/s, i.e. 200 km per ms.
- London-New York great-circle distance is about 5,570 km, so a round trip is over 11,000 km; minimum fibre RTT is about 56 ms; real routes are typically ~70 ms.
- Games use regional servers to reduce latency. Bar values for city/country are typical examples.
- Simple visual reaction time is around 200-250 ms.
