## Fact check

- Central heating circulators use a motor-driven impeller (centrifugal pump), usually a wet-rotor design.
- In a closed, filled loop the static head on the rising and falling legs cancels, so the pump only overcomes friction/flow resistance.
- Modern high-efficiency circulators typically draw about 5-45 W; older fixed-speed pumps 60-100 W. A traditional bulb was 60 W.
