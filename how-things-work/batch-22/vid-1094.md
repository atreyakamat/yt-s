## Fact check

- Mechanical segment timers have a 24-hour dial with captive tappets/segments, commonly in 15-minute steps (96 per day).
- Pushed segments actuate a microswitch as they pass, switching the heating circuit.
- The dial is driven by a small synchronous motor whose speed is locked to mains frequency (50 Hz in the UK), so it loses time during power cuts.
