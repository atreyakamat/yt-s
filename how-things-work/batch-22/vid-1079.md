## Fact check

- Before piped hot water, water was heated in pots or kettles over fires or ranges.
- Tin baths in front of the fire, with family members sharing the water in turn, were common in working-class British homes into the 1900s.
- A 'copper' was a brick-set boiler with a fire beneath, used for laundry.
- Victorian kitchen ranges often had back boilers supplying hot water.
- Benjamin Waddy Maughan invented the gas 'geyser' water heater in 1868.
