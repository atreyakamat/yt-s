## Fact check

- Corrugated board consists of a fluted (wavy) medium glued between flat linerboards.
- The flutes act like arches/columns, giving stiffness and stacking strength; the liners hold the flutes in shape.
- Air in the flutes keeps it light and adds cushioning.
- Corrugated paper was patented in England in 1856 (Healey and Allen) as a lining for tall hats; Albert Jones (US, 1871) used it to wrap bottles; corrugated boxes came into use around 1900 (Gair paperboard boxes 1890; corrugated applied early 20th century). 1856, 1871 confirmed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corrugated_fiberboard
