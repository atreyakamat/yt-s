## Fact check

- Standard concert pitch A4 is 440 Hz, and A tuning forks vibrate at 440 Hz.
- The prongs vibrate symmetrically in opposite directions, so the stem moves very little and energy is lost slowly; the fork rings long.
- Tuning forks produce a nearly pure tone, dominated by the fundamental after the initial strike.
- Pressing the stem on a table couples vibration into the larger surface, which radiates sound more efficiently.
- Frequency rises with shorter or stiffer prongs; 256 Hz and 512 Hz C forks are common.
- Concert A is 440 Hz (ISO 16); a C fork at 256 Hz exists as scientific pitch: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concert_pitch
