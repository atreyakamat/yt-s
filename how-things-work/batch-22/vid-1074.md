## Fact check

- Each side of a vinyl record has one continuous spiral groove running from the outer edge inward.
- An LP side's groove is several hundred meters long (about 20 minutes at 33 1/3 rpm is roughly 650+ revolutions).
- The groove's lateral and vertical modulations are an analog of the audio waveform.
- A diamond stylus tracks the groove; in a moving-magnet cartridge its motion moves a magnet relative to coils, inducing a signal (moving-coil types reverse this).
- In stereo records, each 45-degree wall of the V-groove carries one channel.
- LP albums play at 33 1/3 rpm.
