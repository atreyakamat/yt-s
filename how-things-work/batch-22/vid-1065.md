## Fact check

- Common household drain gels contain sodium hydroxide (caustic soda), often with sodium hypochlorite; they are formulated denser than water so they sink through standing water.
- Strong alkali saponifies fats into soap and hydrolyses proteins such as hair keratin; dissolving/reacting releases heat.
- Labels warn to wear gloves and never mix with other drain products or cleaners.
