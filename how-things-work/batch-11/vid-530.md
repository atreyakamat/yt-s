## Fact check

- Surface water treatment typically uses coagulation (adding aluminium or iron salts so particles clump into floc), sedimentation, then filtration through sand and gravel beds.
- Disinfection uses chlorine, chloramine, UV or ozone; a small chlorine residual is kept to protect water in the distribution pipes.
- Natural groundwater is filtered as it percolates through sand and rock layers.
