## Fact check

- Xerography: a charged photoconductive drum is exposed to light reflected from the original; illuminated areas discharge and dark areas keep charge, which attracts toner.
- Classic copiers used moving mirrors and a lens; modern digital copiers scan the page and print it like a laser printer.
- Chester Carlson made the first xerographic copy in 1938; the first automatic plain-paper office copier launched in 1959.
- 'Xerography' comes from Greek for 'dry writing'.
