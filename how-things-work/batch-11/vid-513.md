## Fact check

- Tread wear indicators are raised bars moulded into the main grooves at 1.6 mm (2/32 inch in the US).
- 1.6 mm is the legal minimum tread depth in the UK and EU; US states commonly use 2/32 inch (about 1.6 mm), hence 'a common legal limit'.
- New car tyres typically have about 7-9 mm of tread ('about 8').
- Sidewall markers such as 'TWI' or a small triangle indicate where the bars are.
- Tests by tyre makers and safety groups show wet braking distances increase significantly as tread wears, before the legal limit.
