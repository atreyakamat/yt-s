## Fact check

- Laser printers use a photoconductive drum that is charged uniformly; a laser discharges areas to form an electrostatic image.
- Toner is a fine powder of plastic resin, pigment and charge-control additives.
- A transfer charge on the paper pulls toner from the drum; a fuser then melts it at roughly 180-220 °C.
- Freshly fused pages are warm.
