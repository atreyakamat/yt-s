## Fact check

- Laptop hinges are friction (torque) hinges: a shaft clamped by spring steel bands or stacked washers creates friction that holds position.
- Torque is tuned to support the display at any angle without lifting the base; the 'open with one finger' goal is a common design target.
- Friction surfaces wear over many cycles, so hinges can loosen with age.
