## Fact check

- Fire doors are rated for fire resistance, commonly 30 or 60 minutes (e.g. FD30/FD60 in the UK).
- They have solid, dense cores (e.g. particleboard or mineral cores) rather than hollow construction.
- Intumescent strips in the frame or door edge expand greatly when heated, sealing the gap; smoke seals (brush or blade) restrict cold smoke.
- Fire doors are fitted with self-closing devices because they only work when closed.
- FD30 / FD60 = 30 or 60 minutes fire resistance: OK. Source: https://www.safelincs.co.uk/fire-door-ratings-fd30-or-fd60/
