## Fact check

- Pyrolytic self-clean cycles heat the oven cavity to roughly 400-500 °C (often quoted around 470-500 °C), decomposing food residue to ash.
- The door locks automatically during the cycle and until the oven cools.
- Pyrolytic ovens have extra insulation and multi-pane doors; many have a catalytic converter in the exhaust path to reduce smoke and odour.
- Cycles commonly last about 1.5-3 hours, after which the ash is wiped out.
- Pyrolytic clean heats to about 420-500 C, so "over 400": OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-cleaning_oven
