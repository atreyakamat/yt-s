## Fact check

- In Western music an octave has 12 semitones; a piano octave has 7 white and 5 black keys.
- White keys give the C major scale (C D E F G A B).
- Most adjacent white keys are a whole tone apart, with a black key (sharp/flat) in between; E-F and B-C are only a semitone apart, so there's no black key there.
- This splits the black keys into groups of 2 (C#, D#) and 3 (F#, G#, A#).
- C is the white key just left of each group of two black keys; F is just left of each group of three.
