## Fact check

- Early paper bags were envelope-style with a V or pointed base and couldn't stand on their own.
- Margaret E. Knight built a machine in 1868 that cut, folded and glued flat-bottomed paper bags; she was granted a patent in 1871.
- Charles Stilwell patented the pleated-side bag that folds flat ('self-opening sack') in 1883.
- A flat rectangular base lets the bag stand open and makes use of the full volume (bars are a relative illustration).
