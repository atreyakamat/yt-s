## Fact check

- The common 'Gem' paper clip is a single steel wire bent into a double loop; it grips by elastic (spring) force, without piercing the paper.
- Bending beyond the elastic limit leaves the wire permanently deformed.
- The Gem-style clip was manufactured in Britain by the 1890s; its basic shape is unchanged.
