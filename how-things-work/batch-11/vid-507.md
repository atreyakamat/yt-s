## Fact check

- EU Single-Use Plastics Directive (2019/904) requires that caps and lids of plastic beverage containers up to 3 litres remain attached during use, from 3 July 2024.
- Caps and lids are among the most commonly found beach litter items in EU surveys, which is why the rule was introduced. The grid is an illustration, not a statistic.
- Tethered caps keep the cap with the bottle for collection and recycling.
- Many tethered designs have a hinge that lets the cap be flipped back and held in place.
- Tethered caps from 3 July 2024, containers up to 3 litres, Directive 2019/904 (agreed 2019): OK. Source: https://www.esmmagazine.com/index.php/packaging-design/all-plastic-caps-to-be-tethered-to-bottles-in-the-eu-from-july-2024-267495
- Bottle caps among most common beach litter: OK. Source: https://commission.europa.eu/news/less-plastic-waste-means-cleaner-beaches-2024-08-14_en
