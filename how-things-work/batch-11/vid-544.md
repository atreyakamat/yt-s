## Fact check

- Wings sag on the ground under their own and fuel weight and bend upward under lift in flight.
- Composite-wing airliners like the 787 show large in-flight flex; tip deflection of a few metres in cruise is commonly reported.
- Flexibility absorbs gust loads; a stiffer wing would need more structure (weight).
- Certification requires the structure to carry ultimate load = 1.5 x limit load (the most expected in service); wing bend tests demonstrate this.
- Wings tested to 150 percent of limit load (ultimate load, factor 1.5): OK. Source: https://www.aviationpros.com/tools-equipment/insurance-finance/aircraft/press-release/12221715/gulfstream-aerospace-corporation-gulfstream-completes-ultimate-load-testing-for-g500
