## Fact check

- Many mechanical fridge thermostats are numbered 1-5 or 1-7 where higher numbers mean more cooling (colder); manufacturer manuals state this. Some models differ, hence 'many'.
- The thermostat setting changes when the compressor cuts in and out; a colder setting means it runs longer.
- Too cold a setting can freeze produce near the cooling plate and uses more energy.
- UK Food Standards Agency advises 5 °C or below (US FDA: 40 °F / 4 °C or below), so 'about 5 degrees or colder' is fair.
