## Fact check

- Contactless cards are powered by the reader's radio field, so every card in range can respond.
- 'Card clash': with two or more contactless cards together, a reader may reject the tap or take payment from a card you didn't intend (Transport for London warns about this).
- TfL says touching in and out with different cards can leave two incomplete journeys, each potentially charged a maximum fare.
