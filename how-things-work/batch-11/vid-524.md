## Fact check

- Wet underfloor heating uses pipe loops laid in screed (or plates) over insulation.
- Typical flow temperatures are about 35-45 °C (up to ~55 °C), versus roughly 60-75 °C for conventional radiators; a hot bath is about 40 °C.
- The large emitting area lets a low water temperature deliver enough heat; heat is spread more evenly from the floor up.
- Screed systems have high thermal mass and respond slowly, so steady operation is usually recommended.
- Underfloor flow about 35-45 C vs radiators about 60-75 C: OK. Source: https://www.nu-heat.co.uk/blog/boost-boiler-performance-ufh/
