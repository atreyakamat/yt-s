## Fact check

- Fogging → water vapour condenses on surfaces colder than the air's dew point, forming droplets that scatter light.
- Demister pads → thin electric heating pads bonded to the back of the mirror.
- Wired to the lighting circuit → common installation so the pad runs while the light is on.
- Car rear window demisters → heating wires keep the glass above the dew point.
