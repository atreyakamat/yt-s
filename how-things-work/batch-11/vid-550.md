## Fact check

- Ribs and corrugations increase the bending stiffness of thin shells, the same principle as folded card or corrugated sheet.
- A curved dome tends to deflect falling objects rather than take them square on.
- Brims or peaks shade the face and shed rain. Bars show relative weight for similar stiffness, illustrative only.
