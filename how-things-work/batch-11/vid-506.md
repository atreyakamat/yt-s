## Fact check

- A boundary layer of slower, cooler air forms around food in still air, slowing heat transfer.
- Fan (convection) ovens circulate hot air, often heated by a ring element around the fan, thinning that layer and increasing the heat transfer rate (forced convection).
- Recipe and manufacturer guidance commonly reduces the temperature by about 20 °C for fan ovens (e.g. 200 °C conventional = 180 °C fan).
- Circulating air also evens out temperature differences between shelves.
