## Fact check

- Cool shot cuts the heater, keeps the fan → that is what the button does on hair dryers that have it.
- Hydrogen bonds in keratin break with heat and water and reform on cooling/drying → the basis of temporary heat styling ('set' of hair).
- Cool air helps a style hold → cooling hair in its new shape lets those bonds reform in that shape; standard stylist advice.
