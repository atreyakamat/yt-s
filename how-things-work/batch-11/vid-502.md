## Fact check

- Non-contact voltage testers detect the alternating electric field around an energised AC conductor via capacitive coupling; the user's body provides the path to ground.
- Mains AC alternates at 50 or 60 Hz depending on country.
- The coupled current is tiny (far below the threshold of sensation, about 1 mA) and is amplified by the tester's electronics.
- They cannot detect DC, and shielded or armoured cables can block the field.
- Proving the tester on a known live source before and after testing is standard electrical safety practice.
