## Fact check

- SPF is the ratio of UV dose needed to cause minimal redness with sunscreen versus without, measured in a lab at 2 mg/cm².
- Transmitted UVB is roughly 1/SPF: SPF 15 blocks about 93%, SPF 30 about 97%, SPF 50 about 98%.
- Studies show people typically apply roughly a quarter to a half of the tested amount, reducing real-world protection. The gauge is illustrative.
- SPF measures UVB (burning) protection; 'broad spectrum' indicates UVA protection. Educational only, not medical advice.
