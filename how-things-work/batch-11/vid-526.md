## Fact check

- Phones contain 3-axis magnetometers (Hall-effect or magnetoresistive sensors) that measure the geomagnetic field vector.
- Earth's field at the surface is about 25-65 microtesla (50 shown); a small permanent magnet can produce millitesla fields close up, i.e. many times stronger. Bars in microtesla: 50 for Earth, 5000 (5 mT) as a conservative near-field value for a small magnet.
- Hall-effect sensors work because a magnetic field deflects moving charge carriers, producing a measurable voltage.
- Figure-eight motion lets the phone calibrate out hard- and soft-iron distortion from nearby components.
