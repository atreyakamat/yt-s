## Fact check

- Suspension insulator strings (glass, porcelain or polymer) hold conductors away from grounded towers.
- Insulator sheds/ribs lengthen the creepage (surface leakage) distance and leave sheltered undersides that stay drier, reducing flashover in wet or polluted conditions.
- String length scales with voltage: roughly a handful of discs for lower voltages and around 20 or more for 400 kV class lines, so counting discs gives a rough voltage guess. The bar values are illustrative.
- Disc count rises with voltage (about 10-12 kV per disc; 220 kV 10-12, 400 kV 18-21+ discs); "dozens" SOFTENED to "twenty or more". Source: https://fingrid.fi/en/grid/safety/safety-instructions/identifying-the-voltage-level
