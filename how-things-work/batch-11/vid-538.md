## Fact check

- Pivoting heads → most modern cartridge razors have a spring-loaded pivot so the head follows facial contours.
- Rigid heads contact along one edge on a curve → geometry of a flat blade on a curved surface.
- Fixed blade angle → cartridge blades are set at a fixed angle relative to the guard and cap, unlike a straight razor.
