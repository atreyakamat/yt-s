## Fact check

- Sealed (combi/system) boilers show system pressure in bar on a gauge.
- Manufacturers commonly recommend roughly 1-1.5 bar when cold (it rises when hot); the VO tells viewers to check their manual.
- Low-pressure sensors make many boilers lock out below a threshold.
- Pressure is topped up via a filling loop (or built-in key); pressure relief valves are typically set at 3 bar and discharge outside.
- Cold reading about 1-1.5 bar, up to about 2 bar when hot: OK. Source: https://britishgas.co.uk/heating/guides/boiler-pressure.html
