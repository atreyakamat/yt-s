## Fact check

- Stitching perforates waterproof fabric, and thread can wick water through the holes.
- Waterproof garments seal seams with seam tape on the inside.
- Seam-taping machines use hot air to activate the tape's adhesive while rollers press it on.
- 'Critically taped' garments seal only exposed seams such as shoulders and hood; 'fully taped' garments seal every seam.
