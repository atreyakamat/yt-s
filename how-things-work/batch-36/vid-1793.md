# vid-1793 · Why Napoleon wasn't short

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Napoleon wasn't actually short."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Napoleon was 5 ft 2 in in French units = ~1.686 m (5 ft 6.5 in) in modern units; average French male height then ~1.65 m; British caricatures (James Gillray) spread the 'Little Boney' image. Good.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Napoleon wasn't actually short.
2. He stood about five foot six in modern units.
3. That was average or above for a Frenchman then.
4. French inches were longer than British ones.
5. British cartoons drew him tiny.
6. His guards were chosen to be tall, which made him look small.
