# vid-1756 · Why hurricanes spin

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Hurricanes spin because Earth spins."
- Backup 1: "The Coriolis effect at work."
- Backup 2: "Warm water powers them."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Tropical cyclones rotate counter-clockwise in the Northern Hemisphere and clockwise in the Southern due to the Coriolis effect; they need sea surface ≥ ~26.5 °C and don't form on the equator. Good.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Hurricanes spin because Earth spins.
2. Warm ocean water feeds the storm.
3. Air rushes in toward the low-pressure centre.
4. Earth's rotation deflects it, called the Coriolis effect.
5. That sets up counter-clockwise spin in the north.
6. South of the equator, they spin the other way.
