## Fact check (verify before posting)

Water's absorption coefficient is higher at red wavelengths; blue is least absorbed and backscattered; sky reflection contributes slightly; chlorophyll and CDOM shift colour toward green/brown. Good.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Water absorbs red light first.
2. Red light fades in the first few metres.
3. Blue travels deepest and scatters back.
4. Pure water is faintly blue by itself.
5. Plankton and sediment make it green.
6. It isn't just reflecting the sky.
