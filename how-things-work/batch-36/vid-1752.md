## Voiceover (read-through)

2. It's called petrichor.
3. Plants and soil release oils during dry spells.
4. Soil bacteria release a compound called geosmin.
5. Raindrops hitting soil puff the aroma into the air.
6. Humans can smell geosmin at tiny levels.
