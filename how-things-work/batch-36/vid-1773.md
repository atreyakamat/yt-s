# vid-1773 · Why we can't hear sound in space

## Hook

- Backup 2: "Radio works where sound can't."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Sound is a mechanical wave requiring a medium; radio waves are electromagnetic and travel through vacuum. Interstellar gas is very thin but supports extremely low-frequency waves; for practical purposes silent. Good.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. In space, no one can hear you.
2. Sound needs matter to travel through.
3. Air, water or solids carry the vibrations.
4. Space is almost a perfect vacuum.
5. So there's nothing to vibrate.
6. Astronauts talk by radio instead.
