## Hook

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Glacial erosion (plucking and abrasion) creates U-shaped valleys, cirques and fjords; river valleys are V-shaped. Glaciers move from centimetres to metres a day.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Glaciers are slow rivers of ice.
2. Snow piles up and compresses into ice.
3. The ice flows downhill under its own weight.
4. It drags rocks and scrapes the valley.
5. Valleys become U-shaped, not V-shaped.
6. Melted glaciers leave lakes and fjords.
