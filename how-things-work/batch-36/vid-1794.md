## Fact check (verify before posting)

Library of Alexandria (founded c. 285-246 BCE under Ptolemies); Caesar's fire (48 BCE) may have destroyed warehouses/books; decline through lost patronage, 2nd c. BCE purge of scholars (Ptolemy VIII), Aurelian's destruction of the Brucheion (c. 272 CE); no single event, historians agree on gradual decline.
