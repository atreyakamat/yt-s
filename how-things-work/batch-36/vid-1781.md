## Voiceover (read-through)

1. A Rubik's Cube has billions of states.
2. It has about forty-three quintillion positions.
3. Yet any state can be solved in twenty moves or fewer.
4. That limit is called God's number.
5. It was proven in 2010 with computers.
6. Erno Rubik invented it in 1974.
