## Fact check (verify before posting)

2. Rain slowly dissolves minerals from rocks.
3. Rivers carry them to the sea.
4. Water evaporates but salt stays behind.
5. Seafloor vents add minerals too.
6. Seawater is about three and a half percent salt.
