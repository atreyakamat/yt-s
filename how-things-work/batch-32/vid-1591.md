# vid-1591 · Why not every fourth year is a leap year

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Why isn't every fourth year a leap year?"
- Backup 1: "The leap year rule most people don't know."
- Backup 2: "Why 1900 had no leap day but 2000 did."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Gregorian rule: leap if divisible by 4, except century years not divisible by 400 (1900 no; 2000 yes; 2100 no). Mean tropical year ~365.2422 days. 2028 is a leap year; 2025 is not. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leap_year

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Why isn't every fourth year a leap year?
2. A year is about 365 days and nearly six hours.
3. Every four years, those hours make an extra day.
4. But the extra time is slightly less than six hours.
5. So years divisible by 100 skip it, unless also divisible by 400.
6. That keeps calendar and seasons in step.
