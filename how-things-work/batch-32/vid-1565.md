## Fact check (verify before posting)

Charles's law / ideal gas: at constant pressure, volume is proportional to absolute temperature; a balloon taken from 20 °C to 0 °C loses ~7% of its volume. Latex also becomes stiffer in the cold.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Balloons shrink when it gets cold.
2. Gas molecules move more slowly when cooled.
3. They hit the balloon walls less hard.
4. So the gas takes up less space.
5. Bring it back inside and it re-inflates.
6. The gas hasn't leaked; it only contracted.
