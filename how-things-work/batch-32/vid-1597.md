## Fact check (verify before posting)

Gail Matthews (Dominican University, 2015) reported higher goal achievement when goals were written and shared with weekly updates, but it was a small unpublished study; later work on goal-setting (Locke & Latham) supports specific goals. Don't overstate; 'a nudge, not a guarantee'.
