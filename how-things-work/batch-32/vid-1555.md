## Hook

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Cold reduces usable battery capacity and increases cabin heating and air-density drag; studies (e.g. AAA, Recurrent) report range losses of roughly 10-40% depending on temperature and heating method. Give no single number.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Cold weather cuts electric car range.
2. Battery chemistry slows down when it's cold.
3. Less energy can be drawn out at once.
4. The car also spends energy heating the cabin.
5. Dense, cold air adds more drag.
6. Pre-warming while plugged in helps a lot.
