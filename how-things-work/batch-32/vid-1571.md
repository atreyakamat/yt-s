## Fact check (verify before posting)

Hogmanay first-footing: traditional lucky first-footer is a tall dark-haired man bearing coal, shortbread/black bun, salt, whisky. Regional variations; the 'dark-haired' belief is often linked to avoiding fair-haired Viking raiders (folklore, unproven).
