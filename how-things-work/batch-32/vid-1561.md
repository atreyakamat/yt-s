## Fact check (verify before posting)

QR codes use Reed-Solomon error correction with four levels (L ~7%, M ~15%, Q ~25%, H ~30% of codewords recoverable); three finder patterns indicate orientation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QR_code

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. A QR code works even when it's scratched.
2. Three big squares show the scanner which way is up.
3. The code stores your data as small black and white squares.
4. It also adds error-correcting extra data.
5. That lets a scanner rebuild missing parts.
6. At the highest level it can lose up to about thirty percent.
