# vid-1557 · Why inflation quietly shrinks your savings

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Cash in a drawer slowly shrinks."
- Backup 1: "The silent cost of keeping cash."
- Backup 2: "Prices double faster than you think."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

72/3 = 24 years. Rule of 72 is an approximation; inflation rates vary by country and year. Not investment advice. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rule_of_72

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Cash in a drawer slowly shrinks.
2. Inflation means prices rise a little every year.
3. The same money buys less each year.
4. Use the rule of 72 again: divide 72 by the rate.
5. At three percent inflation, prices double in about twenty-four years.
6. So savings usually need to earn more than inflation.
