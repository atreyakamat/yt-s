# vid-1593 · What gives fireworks their colours

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Standard pyrotechnic colour chemistry: strontium (red), sodium (yellow), copper (blue), barium (green), calcium (orange). Blue is widely described as the hardest colour to produce, since copper compounds break down at high temperature. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrotechnic_colorant

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Fireworks get their colour from metal salts.
2. Strontium burns red.
3. Sodium burns yellow-orange.
4. Copper compounds burn blue.
5. Barium makes green and calcium makes orange.
6. Blue is the hardest colour to get right.
