## Fact check (verify before posting)

Often-cited statistics (e.g. ~8-9% success, ~80% abandon by mid-February) come from popular surveys and Strava/US news analyses; not a single rigorous study. Present as 'often found' and not a measured law.. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Year%27s_resolution

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Many resolutions don't last until February.
2. Studies often find only a minority keep them all year.
3. Big vague goals give your brain no clear first step.
4. Small, specific actions are far easier to start.
5. Tracking a habit makes progress visible.
6. Aim for tiny wins you can repeat.
