# vid-1568 · Why LED bulbs run cooler than old bulbs

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Old bulbs wasted energy as heat."
- Backup 1: "Why your old bulb burned your fingers."
- Backup 2: "The light bulb that wastes less."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Incandescent bulbs convert only about 5% of input power to visible light (~90% heat); LEDs ~30-50% or more; LEDs still produce some heat that needs sinking.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Old bulbs wasted energy as heat.
2. A filament bulb heats a wire until it glows.
3. Most of that energy becomes heat, not light.
4. An LED makes light directly in a tiny semiconductor chip.
5. It needs far less energy for the same brightness.
6. So LED lights stay cooler and last much longer.
