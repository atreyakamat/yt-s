# vid-1595 · How long it takes to build a habit

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Habits take longer than twenty-one days."
- Backup 1: "The real number is closer to 66."
- Backup 2: "The 21 day habit rule is a myth."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Lally et al. (2009, European Journal of Social Psychology): median 66 days to automaticity, range 18-254; a single missed day did not materially affect habit formation. Small sample (96 people), one specific behaviour each. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habit

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Habits take longer than twenty-one days.
2. The 21-day rule has no strong science behind it.
3. A 2009 study tracked people forming a daily habit.
4. It took about two months on average.
5. But it ranged from 18 to 254 days.
6. Missing one day didn't ruin the habit.
