# vid-1558 · Why paying yourself first works

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Save first, spend what's left."
- Backup 1: "The easiest money habit to start."
- Backup 2: "Set it up once and forget it."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

'Pay yourself first' is a standard budgeting heuristic; automatic enrolment/defaults raise saving (Thaler & Benartzi, Save More Tomorrow). Not personalised advice.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Save first, spend what's left.
2. Most people spend, then save what remains.
3. Flip it: move savings out the day you're paid.
4. Automatic transfers remove the daily decision.
5. You adapt to living on the smaller amount.
6. Start with any amount, even one percent.
