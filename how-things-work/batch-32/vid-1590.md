# vid-1590 · Why Julian and Gregorian calendars disagree

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Some Christians celebrate Christmas on January seventh."
- Backup 1: "Why Orthodox Christmas is on January 7."
- Backup 2: "The calendar that drifts 13 days."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Julian year 365.25 d vs mean tropical year ~365.2422 d → ~1 day per 128 years; gap is 13 days 1900-2100. Some Orthodox churches (Russian, Serbian, Georgian, Ethiopian/Coptic variations) observe Dec 25 Julian = Jan 7 Gregorian. Not all Orthodox churches (many use Revised Julian). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_calendar
