# vid-1564 · Why helium balloons float

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Helium balloons float because they're lighter than air."
- Backup 1: "How a gas floats a balloon."
- Backup 2: "Why a balloon can't lift a gift."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Helium ~0.17 kg/m3 vs air ~1.2 kg/m3 at room temperature: net lift ~1.0 g per litre. Balloon skin weight reduces it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helium

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Helium balloons float because they're lighter than air.
2. Helium is far less dense than the air around it.
3. Air pushes up on the balloon with buoyant force.
4. If that push beats its weight, it rises.
5. Each litre of helium lifts about one gram.
6. That's why a party balloon can lift almost nothing.
