# vid-1599 · Why the planning fallacy makes plans fail

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "We always underestimate how long things take."
- Backup 1: "Why your to-do list never fits the day."
- Backup 2: "Plan with last year's data, not hope."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Planning fallacy coined by Kahneman & Tversky (1979); reference class forecasting later advocated by Kahneman and Lovallo; popular 'Hofstadter's law' is a joke law. Good. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planning_fallacy

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. We always underestimate how long things take.
2. It's called the planning fallacy.
3. Kahneman and Tversky described it in 1979.
4. We imagine the best case and ignore delays.
5. Look at how long similar tasks really took.
6. Then add a buffer on top.
