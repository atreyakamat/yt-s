## Fact check (verify before posting)

Southern US custom: black-eyed peas (Hoppin' John), collard greens (paper money), cornbread (gold) eaten on New Year's Day for luck/prosperity. Folk tradition, origins debated.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Southern Americans eat peas on New Year's Day.
2. The dish is called Hoppin' John.
3. Peas are said to stand for coins.
4. Greens are served alongside, said to mean paper money.
5. Cornbread stands for gold.
6. Many families say you must eat them before noon.
