## Fact check (verify before posting)

Active noise cancellation uses mics, DSP and an anti-phase signal (destructive interference); most effective on low-frequency, steady noise (engines, HVAC) and less on sudden/high sounds.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Noise-cancelling headphones add sound to remove sound.
2. A tiny microphone listens to the noise around you.
3. A chip flips the sound wave upside down.
4. The speaker plays that inverted wave.
5. The waves cancel each other out, like peaks meeting valleys.
6. It works best on steady low sounds, like engines.
