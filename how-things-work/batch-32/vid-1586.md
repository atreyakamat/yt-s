## Fact check (verify before posting)

Diwali falls in Oct/Nov depending on the Hindu lunisolar calendar; in Gujarat and some other regions the new year (Vikram Samvat, Bestu Varas) begins the day after Diwali. Chopda Pujan opens new account books for many traders.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Diwali is a New Year for many Indians.
2. In some regions, the new year starts the day after Diwali.
3. Diwali often falls in October or November.
4. Homes are lit with small oil lamps called diyas.
5. Lamps stand for light winning over darkness.
6. Businesses open new account books at this time.
