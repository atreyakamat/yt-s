## Fact check (verify before posting)

Hongbao / ya sui qian: red envelopes of money given at Lunar New Year; legend of demon Sui; even amounts preferred; 4 avoided (homophone with 'death' in Mandarin/Cantonese). Legend is folklore. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_envelope

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Red envelopes carry luck, not just cash.
2. Red stands for luck and chases away bad spirits.
3. Adults give them to children and unmarried juniors.
4. One legend says they protect kids from a demon called Sui.
5. Even-numbered amounts are usually preferred.
6. The number four is avoided as it sounds like death.
