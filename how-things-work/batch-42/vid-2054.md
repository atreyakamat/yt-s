## Fact check

- Pouring water on sauna stones creates steam (löyly), raising humidity. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauna
- Humidity reduces evaporative cooling from sweat, making heat feel stronger; steam condensing on skin releases heat. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_index
- Air temperature changes only a little compared with the perceived heat (script wording: barely moves). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauna
