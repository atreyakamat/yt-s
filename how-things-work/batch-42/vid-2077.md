## Fact check

- A clapperboard syncs picture and sound and marks each scene and take; film stock reacts to light, not sound, so audio is recorded separately. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clapperboard
- Slates show production title, director, director of photography, date, scene and take; digital slates show SMPTE timecode but the sticks are still snapped as a manual backup. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clapperboard
