## Fact check

- Aquarium heaters use a heating element in a sealed glass tube with a thermostat; tropical aquaria are kept warm, commonly in the mid-20s °C. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquarium
- Thermostats switch the element on and off to maintain a set temperature. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermostat
