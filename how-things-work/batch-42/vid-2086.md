## Fact check

- The first tide-predicting machine was built by William Thomson (later Lord Kelvin) in 1872-73. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tide-predicting_machine
- The machine uses pulleys and a wire to sum sinusoidal components, one per tidal constituent, and a pen records the result. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tide-predicting_machine
- Tides come from the combined gravitational pull of the Moon and Sun. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tide
- Such machines were used to produce tide tables much faster than by hand calculation (general claim, no figure used). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tide-predicting_machine
- Kelvin's first machine 1872; wire over pulleys sums components, cranks driven by gears: reworded 'geared wheel' to 'wheel'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tide-predicting_machine
