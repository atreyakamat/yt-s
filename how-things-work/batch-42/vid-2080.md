## Fact check

- Turnstiles (tripod type) have arms on a rotating shaft with a ratchet/pawl mechanism, released by a solenoid when a valid fare is presented. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turnstile
- Three arms means one third of a turn per person (geometry of a tripod). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turnstile
