## Fact check

- Clément Ader demonstrated an early two-channel system in Paris in 1881: telephone transmitters carried the Paris Opera stage to the Electrical Exhibition, one receiver per ear. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereophonic_sound
- Alan Blumlein filed UK patent 394,325 on 14 December 1931, covering two channels in one groove, the two groove walls at right angles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereophonic_sound
- The first stereo discs sold to the public in March 1958 (1931 to 1958 = 27 years). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereophonic_sound
