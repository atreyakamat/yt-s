## Fact check

- Terracotta is unglazed fired clay and is porous. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terracotta
- Evaporation of water from a surface cools it (evaporative cooling). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evaporative_cooler
- Porous clay pots dry soil faster than non-porous plastic ones (qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flowerpot
