## Fact check

- Scalpels are commonly a handle with a replaceable disposable blade. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalpel
- Surgical blades are numbered by shape, for example No. 10 (curved cutting edge) and No. 11 (pointed). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalpel
- Disposable blades are supplied sterile. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalpel
- Sharper edges cut with less force. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalpel
