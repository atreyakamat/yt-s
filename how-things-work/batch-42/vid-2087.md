## Fact check

- Léon Theremin invented the instrument in October 1920 and patented it in the US in 1928. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theremin
- Two antennas act as position sensors, each forming half a capacitor with the performer's hand; two radio-frequency oscillators are mixed and the audible difference is amplified. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theremin
- Pitch hand: closer to the pitch antenna raises the note; volume hand: moving away from the volume antenna makes it louder. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theremin
- Theremin invented 1920, patented in the US 1928: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theremin
