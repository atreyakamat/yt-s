## Fact check

- Swimming touchpads register a touch on the wall and stop the timing; times recorded to hundredths of a second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_(sport)
- Touchpad systems introduced for timing swimming at the Olympics (1960s onwards); not stated in the voiceover. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omega_SA
- Pads need a minimum force (script: real force). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_(sport)
