## Fact check

- Modified atmosphere packaging lowers oxygen and raises carbon dioxide and nitrogen to slow respiration in fresh produce; permeable films let gases pass; CO2 above about 10 percent damages fruit and vegetables. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modified_atmosphere
