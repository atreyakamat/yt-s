## Fact check

- An I-beam concentrates material in the flanges, which resist bending, with a web that resists shear. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I-beam
- Bending puts the top in compression and the bottom in tension; stress at the neutral axis is near zero. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bending
- The bar chart is illustrative; no weight saving is claimed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I-beam
