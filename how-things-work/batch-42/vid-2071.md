## Fact check

- Can walls often have rib bulges that stiffen the wall so it resists dents that could split the seams; lids are joined by a double seam of folded rims, a tight dry joint needing no welding or solder. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tin_can
- "Air gets in and food spoils" is general canning reasoning. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tin_can
