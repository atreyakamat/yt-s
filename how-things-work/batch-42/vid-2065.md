## Fact check

- Needles are made from steel wire that is cut, pointed, flattened where the eye goes, punched, hardened and tempered, then polished. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sewing_needle
- Many needles are nickel- or gold-plated. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sewing_needle
- The double-length wire detail (two needles per piece) is a traditional method; script phrased generally. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Needle
