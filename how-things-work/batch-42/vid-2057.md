## Fact check

- 35 mm motion-picture film has sprocket holes, four perforations per frame, runs at 24 fps standard, 16 frames per foot. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/35_mm_movie_film
- Sprocket teeth engage the perforations to advance film frame by frame (a Geneva drive/claw holds each frame still). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_drive
- 35 mm film: 4 perforations per frame, 16 frames per foot, 24 fps: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/35_mm_movie_film
