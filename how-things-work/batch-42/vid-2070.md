## Fact check

- Single serpentine queues feeding multiple servers give first-come first-served ordering and avoid the problem of picking a slow line; they also use space efficiently. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queue_area
- Pooling servers behind one queue reduces average waiting variance (queueing theory). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queueing_theory
