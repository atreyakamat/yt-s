## Fact check

- A radome is a structural, weatherproof enclosure that protects a radar antenna and is as transparent to radio waves as possible. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radome
- Radomes protect the antenna from weather and wind loading and hide the antenna's motion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radome
- Spherical or geodesic radomes give uniform transmission regardless of the antenna's pointing direction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radome
