## Fact check

- Paper caps contain small amounts of a shock-sensitive explosive compound; a hammer strikes the cap to fire it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap_gun
- In strip and ring-cap guns the trigger advances the caps and pulls the hammer back before releasing it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap_gun
