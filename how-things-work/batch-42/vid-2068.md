## Fact check

- Light exposure causes potato tubers to produce chlorophyll (green) and, separately, higher glycoalkaloids such as solanine; greening is a visual indicator of light exposure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potato
- Solanine is a bitter glycoalkaloid that is a natural defence. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solanine
