## Fact check

- The Plimsoll line (load line) marks the maximum depth to which a ship may be safely loaded. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waterline#Plimsoll_line ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Load_line
- Samuel Plimsoll campaigned for load line legislation in the 1870s, leading to the Merchant Shipping Acts. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Plimsoll
- Different marks are used for fresh water, tropical, summer and winter conditions; salt water is denser than fresh water so ships float higher in it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Load_line
- Plimsoll campaigned 1873 onward; Merchant Shipping Act 1876 made load line compulsory: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plimsoll_line
