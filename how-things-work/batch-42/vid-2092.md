## Fact check

- An artificial pacemaker delivers electrical pulses to the heart muscle through leads when the heart's own rhythm is too slow or missing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_cardiac_pacemaker
- Demand pacing: the device senses natural beats and inhibits output. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_cardiac_pacemaker
- Pacing pulse widths are around a fraction of a millisecond up to about a millisecond. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_cardiac_pacemaker
- The heart beats about 100,000 times a day. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart
- Pacemaker batteries last years. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_cardiac_pacemaker
- Pacemaker pulses are about 0.5-1 ms, demand pacing, battery 5-10 years: OK. Source: https://derangedphysiology.com/main/required-reading/intensive-care-procedures/Chapter-9125/sensitivity-and-output-settings-temporary-pacemaker
