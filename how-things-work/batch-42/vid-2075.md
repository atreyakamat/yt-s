## Fact check

- The Bessemer process was patented in 1856 by Henry Bessemer; air is blown through molten pig iron to oxidise carbon and impurities. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessemer_process
- The oxidation is exothermic, keeping the metal molten without added fuel; the process turned a batch around in about half an hour. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessemer_process
- Steel became cheap enough for rails, bridges and ships. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessemer_process
- Bessemer patented 1856; cut cost from about 40 to 6-7 pounds per ton: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bessemer_process
