## Fact check

- Surgical needles are commonly curved (e.g. 1/4, 3/8, 1/2, 5/8 circle) to be driven through tissue by wrist rotation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surgical_suture
- Swaged needles have the suture attached to the end, avoiding an eye. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surgical_suture
- Needle holders grip the needle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Needle_holder
