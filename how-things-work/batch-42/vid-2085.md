## Fact check

- Records are pressed from a heated vinyl (PVC) biscuit between two metal stampers made by electroplating nickel onto a lacquer master. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonograph_record
- Excess material is trimmed and the disc cooled. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonograph_record
- The music is a single continuous spiral groove. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonograph_record
