## Fact check

- Cans are closed by double seaming: a first operation rolls the lid and body flanges into interlocked hooks and a second operation flattens the seam tight. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Can_seamer
- A sealing compound in the lid fills tiny gaps in the seam. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Can_seamer
- Seaming a can takes on the order of a second; script says about a second as a rounded figure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverage_can
