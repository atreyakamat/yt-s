## Fact check

- Meijer and Robbers (2014), Proc. R. Soc. B: wild mice and other animals used wheels placed outdoors, even without food reward; mice made up most of the visits (reports give 72% urban, 91% dune, or 88% overall; softened to "most"). Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4046404
- Wild shrews, rats and frogs were also recorded using the wheel. Source: https://www.sciencenews.org/blog/wild-things/mice-really-do-run-wheels
- Meijer and Robbers 2014: wild mice used wheels, also shrews, rats, slugs, frogs; mice 72% urban and 91% dune visits per one report: softened to 'most visits'. Source: https://phys.org/news/2014-05-animals-wild-wheel-choice-video.amp
