## Fact check

- Nathaniel Bagshaw Ward, a London physician and botanist, first tested the case in July 1833 and published On the Growth of Plants in Closely Glazed Cases in 1842. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wardian_case
- Salty air, lack of light and fresh water killed the great majority of imported plants on long sea journeys. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wardian_case
- The 1833 test shipped two cases of British ferns and grasses to Sydney, arriving in good condition after several months. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wardian_case
