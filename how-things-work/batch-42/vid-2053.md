## Fact check

- Weight-activated feeders close the feeding ports when a heavier animal such as a squirrel stands on the perch, using a spring-loaded mechanism. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bird_feeder
- Many models allow the spring tension to be adjusted (manufacturer feature, qualitative).
