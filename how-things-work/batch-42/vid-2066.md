## Fact check

- Space toilets on the ISS use airflow (suction) instead of gravity and water, with restraints to hold the user. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_toilet
- Solid waste is stored and loaded onto cargo vehicles that burn up on re-entry. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_toilet
- Urine is processed by the station's water recovery system into potable water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_control_and_life_support_system#International_Space_Station
