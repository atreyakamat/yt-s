## Fact check

- The meniscus is the curve at a liquid's surface in a container; water forms a concave meniscus because adhesion to glass exceeds cohesion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meniscus_(liquid)
- Mercury forms a convex meniscus. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meniscus_(liquid)
- Volume readings are taken at the bottom of a concave meniscus at eye level to avoid parallax error. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meniscus_(liquid)
- The word meniscus comes from Greek for crescent or little moon. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meniscus_(liquid)
