## Fact check

- Electric pencil sharpeners use a motor-driven cutter (often a helical or conical cutter) to shave the pencil. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pencil_sharpener
- Many models have a switch activated when a pencil is inserted; some stop automatically. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pencil_sharpener
