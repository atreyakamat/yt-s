## Fact check

- The Space Shuttle reached orbit about 8.5 minutes after launch. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Shuttle
- Low Earth orbit requires an orbital speed of about 7.8 km/s, roughly 28,000 km/h. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low_Earth_orbit
- Orbit is a state of continuous free fall around Earth at high horizontal speed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit
- Most of a launch vehicle's energy goes into gaining horizontal velocity; multi-stage rockets drop empty stages. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multistage_rocket
- Orbital speed about 7.8 km/s (28,000 km/h): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbital_spaceflight
- Shuttle main engines burn about 8.5 minutes to orbital speed: OK per NASA. Source: https://www.nasa.gov/?p=296282
