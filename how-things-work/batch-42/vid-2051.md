## Fact check

- Charles F. Brannock patented a first prototype in 1925 and an improved version in 1927, to improve on the wooden RITZ Stick; it takes three measurements of each foot (length, arch length heel to ball, width); width shown as letters from AAAA to EEEE; it has left and right heel cups and is rotated 180 degrees for the second foot.
