## Fact check

- Most plant roots need oxygen from air spaces in the soil, and waterlogging causes root rot. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_rot
- Gravel layers in pots do not improve drainage because water collects at the soil/gravel boundary (perched water table). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perched_water_table
- Cache-pot practice is standard horticultural advice (qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flowerpot
