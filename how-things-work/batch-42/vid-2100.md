## Fact check

- Electronic article surveillance uses tags that respond to a field from gates at the exit; tags are deactivated or removed at checkout. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_article_surveillance
- Common systems include radio-frequency and acousto-magnetic tags that resonate at a set frequency; no battery needed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_article_surveillance
