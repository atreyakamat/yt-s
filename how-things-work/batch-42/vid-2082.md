## Fact check

- Air is about 78% nitrogen and 21% oxygen. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmosphere_of_Earth
- Oxygen concentrators use pressure swing adsorption with zeolite to adsorb nitrogen. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxygen_concentrator
- Medical concentrators typically deliver about 90-95% oxygen. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxygen_concentrator
- Alternating twin sieve beds give continuous output. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pressure_swing_adsorption
- Oxygen concentrators use zeolite pressure swing adsorption, output over about 90% oxygen: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxygen_concentrator
