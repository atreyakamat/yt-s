## Fact check

- A CD has a single spiral track read from the inside out.
- Track pitch is 1.6 µm, so roughly 40-60 turns fit across a human hair (about 70-100 µm).
- Total track length is about 5-6 km for a standard disc.
- Data is moulded as pits and lands in polycarbonate, coated with aluminium and lacquer; a 780 nm laser reads from below, and pit edges (transitions) represent ones.
- CDs use constant linear velocity: about 500 rpm at the inner edge down to about 200 rpm at the outer edge.
