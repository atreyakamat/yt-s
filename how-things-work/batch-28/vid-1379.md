## Fact check

- Chimney pots terminate individual flues; a stack usually has one pot per flue/fireplace.
- They extend flue height (draught increases with height) and raise the outlet above turbulent air around the roof that causes down-draught.
- Many traditional pots taper, increasing exit velocity.
- Fired clay resists heat and weather and protects the top of the stack; cowls keep out rain and birds.
