## Fact check

- Many rulers have a small margin before the zero mark so wear or damage to the end doesn't affect accuracy.
- Measuring from the printed zero line avoids errors from chipped or worn ends.
- Some rulers do start at the edge, hence 'on many rulers'. The gauge is illustrative.
