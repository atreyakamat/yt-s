## Fact check

- Oil-filled radiators are electric heaters: an element heats a sealed charge of mineral oil that is never consumed or replaced.
- The oil circulates by natural convection inside the fins and has a high boiling point.
- The oil and metal act as a heat reservoir, so the heater keeps emitting heat after the thermostat switches off.
- Heat is passed to the room mainly by convection from the fins.
