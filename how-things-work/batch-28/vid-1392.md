## Fact check

- Candy floss (cotton candy) machines have a heated spinning head that melts sugar and spins it through small holes by centrifugal force.
- The thin streams cool too fast for crystallisation, forming amorphous (glassy) sugar threads, so floss is mostly air.
- The electric machine was invented in 1897 by dentist William Morrison and confectioner John C. Wharton; it was sold as 'Fairy Floss' at the 1904 St Louis World's Fair.
- Amorphous sugar absorbs moisture readily, so floss collapses in humid air.
- Dentist William Morrison and John C. Wharton built the machine in 1897; shown at the 1904 World's Fair. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotton_candy
