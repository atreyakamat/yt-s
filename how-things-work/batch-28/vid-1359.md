## Fact check

- Sleeping bags insulate by trapping air in the fill; the fluffiness is called loft.
- Prolonged compression can reduce the loft of down and especially synthetic fills, lowering warmth.
- Manufacturers recommend storing bags uncompressed, in a large breathable storage sack or hung up, and dry.
- Stuff sacks are intended for transport only.
