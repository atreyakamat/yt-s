## Fact check

- Toilet paper → short cellulose fibres with little wet strength, designed to disperse in water.
- Wipes → nonwoven fabrics, often hydroentangled, many containing polyester or polypropylene fibres, designed to be strong when wet.
- Fatbergs → wipes combine with fats, oils and grease in sewers to form blockages.
- 130 tonnes → the Whitechapel fatberg (2017) was reported as about 130 tonnes and 250 m long.
