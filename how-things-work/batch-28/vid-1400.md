## Fact check

- 'Bascule' is French for seesaw/balance; each leaf pivots on trunnions and is balanced by a counterweight in the pier, so little power is needed to raise it.
- Tower Bridge opened in 1894 with steam engines pumping water into hydraulic accumulators to drive the lifting engines; it was converted to oil and electric hydraulics in the 1970s.
- Each Tower Bridge bascule weighs over 1,000 tonnes, and the bridge still lifts for tall vessels.
- Tower Bridge opened 30 June 1894, steam engines pumped water for hydraulic accumulators, each bascule about 1,070 tons. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_Bridge
