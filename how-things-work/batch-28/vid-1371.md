## Fact check

- White glue is a polyvinyl acetate (PVA) emulsion: polymer particles dispersed in water.
- The particles scatter light, making it look white (like fat droplets in milk); as water leaves, particles coalesce into a continuous transparent film.
- PVA glue is water-based and washable before drying.
