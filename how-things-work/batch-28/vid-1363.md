## Fact check

- Abrasives → hydrated silica and calcium carbonate (chalk) are the most common toothpaste abrasives.
- Softer than enamel → toothpaste abrasives are chosen to clean without significantly abrading enamel (measured by RDA/REA tests).
- Humectants and binders → glycerol/sorbitol keep paste moist; gums (e.g. xanthan, carrageenan) thicken it.
- Detergent → usually sodium lauryl sulfate, which makes foam.
- Fluoride → standard adult toothpastes contain about 1,350-1,500 ppm fluoride; fluoride helps remineralise and strengthen enamel. No advice given.
- Toothpaste fluoride is typically about 1,000-1,450 ppm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluoride_therapy
