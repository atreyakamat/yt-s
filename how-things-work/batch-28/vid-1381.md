## Fact check

- Many smartphones have a secondary microphone on the back (often near the camera) or top edge.
- Dual-microphone noise reduction compares signals: speech is much stronger at the primary (mouth-side) mic, while diffuse ambient noise is similar at both, allowing suppression.
- The rear mic is also used for video recording audio. Mic ports are small and backed by mesh; manufacturers warn against inserting objects into openings.
