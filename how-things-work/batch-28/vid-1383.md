## Fact check

- Windscreens are laminated glass: two glass plies bonded to a PVB (polyvinyl butyral) interlayer that holds fragments together and helps prevent ejection.
- Side and rear windows are usually toughened (tempered) glass: rapid cooling leaves the surface in compression, so when broken it fragments into small, relatively blunt pieces.
- Some newer cars use laminated side glass too, hence 'usually'. Emergency services break tempered side windows for access.
