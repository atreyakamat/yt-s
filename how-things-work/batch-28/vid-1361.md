## Fact check

- Baking (parchment) paper is coated with silicone, giving a non-stick, heat-resistant surface.
- Wax paper is coated with paraffin or soy wax; manufacturers say it is not oven-safe because the wax melts and can smoke or ignite.
- Parchment makers typically rate it to about 215-230 °C (420-450 °F) and warn against contact with grill elements or flames.
