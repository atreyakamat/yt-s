## Fact check

- Dead blow hammers have a hollow head partly filled with steel shot or sand, usually with a polyurethane or plastic covering.
- On impact the loose fill arrives slightly after the shell, opposing rebound, so the blow is 'dead' and more energy is transferred to the workpiece.
- The soft face is non-marring and reduces shock; they are used for assembly, knocking parts loose and automotive work.
