## Fact check

- Lift comes from the wing's shape and angle of attack turning airflow downward (downwash).
- By Newton's third law, deflecting air down produces an upward force on the wing.
- This is equivalent to a pressure difference: lower above, higher below (Bernoulli/pressure and Newton views are consistent).
- The 'equal transit time' explanation is a known myth; air over the top typically reaches the trailing edge before air underneath (NASA and others debunk it).
- Aerobatic planes fly inverted by setting an angle of attack, showing angle matters more than curvature alone.
