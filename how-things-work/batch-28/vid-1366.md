## Fact check

- Smartwatch fall detection uses accelerometer and gyroscope data to identify a characteristic sequence: free fall/drop, high-g impact, and post-impact inactivity.
- Ordinary activities like sitting produce small accelerations (around 1-2 g); falls can produce impacts of several g or more at the wrist (values rounded, illustrative).
- When a hard fall is detected, devices alert the wearer and, if there's no response for about a minute, can contact emergency services (feature behaviour varies by device and settings). This describes how the feature works; it is not medical advice.
