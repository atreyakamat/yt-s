## Fact check

- Classic dress shirts have longer front and back tails and a curved hem rising at the side seams.
- Long tails keep the shirt tucked during movement; the higher sides give hip room: standard shirtmaking rationale.
- Historically, men's shirts served as underwear, with long tails, until separate drawers/underpants became common in the 19th century.
- Shirts designed to be worn untucked are cut shorter with a straighter hem.
