## Fact check

- Smart bulbs contain an LED driver plus a microcontroller with a radio (Wi-Fi, Zigbee or Bluetooth) that receives commands.
- Colour bulbs use red, green and blue LEDs (often plus white LEDs) and mix colours additively; red + green appears yellow, RGB together appears white.
- Brightness of each channel is usually set by pulse-width modulation at frequencies well above visible flicker (hundreds to thousands of Hz).
- If the wall switch cuts power, the bulb's radio is off and it can't respond to the app.
