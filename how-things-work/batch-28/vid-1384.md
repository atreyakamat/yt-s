## Fact check

- Modern flat-bottom rails have a head, web and foot.
- Rails act as beams between sleepers under wheel loads.
- In bending, stress is greatest at the top and bottom fibres; putting material there (I-section) maximises stiffness per unit mass, as with steel I-beams.
- The head is sized to allow wear; the broad foot gives stability and is fastened to sleepers with clips.
