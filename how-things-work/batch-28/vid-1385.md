## Fact check

- Speed cushions are speed humps divided into sections with gaps; their width is set so vehicles with a wide wheel track (buses, fire engines) straddle them while cars must ride over at least partly.
- They are used on emergency and bus routes to reduce delays to those vehicles.
- Cyclists can pass through the gaps.
