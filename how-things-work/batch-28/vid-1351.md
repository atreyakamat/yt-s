## Fact check

- Tests (e.g., by repair and gadget testing groups) found uncooked rice is no better than leaving a phone in open air for drying, and is slower than proper desiccants.
- Apple's support guidance explicitly advises against putting a wet iPhone in rice, as small rice particles can damage it; it recommends tapping it gently with the connector facing down and leaving it in a dry area with airflow.
- Phone makers advise not charging while the port is wet; some phones show a liquid-detection alert.
