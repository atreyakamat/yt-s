## Fact check

- Score lines are grooves moulded into tablets to aid breaking; a groove concentrates stress so the tablet fractures there.
- Regulators (e.g. US FDA guidance on tablet scoring) assess whether scored tablets split into accurate portions; some lines are not intended for dose splitting.
- Splitting coated or modified-release tablets can affect how they work; product information states whether a tablet may be split. Educational only, not medical advice.
