## Fact check

- The 19-inch rack (panel width 482.6 mm) originated with telephone/relay equipment in the early 20th century and was later standardised (EIA-310, IEC 60297).
- One rack unit (1U) = 1.75 in (44.45 mm).
- 42U racks are common; 42 × 44.45 mm ≈ 1.87 m of mounting space, with the cabinet a bit over 2 m tall.
- Standard hole spacing lets equipment from any maker mount in any compliant rack.
- 19-inch rack format set by AT&T around 1922 for telephone equipment; 1U = 1.75 in; 42U standard. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19-inch_rack
