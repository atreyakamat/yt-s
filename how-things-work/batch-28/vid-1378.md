## Fact check

- Soap in standing water softens → bar soap absorbs water at the surface and forms a soft, swollen layer that washes away quickly.
- Ridges/drainage lift the bar → draining soap dishes keep the bar out of standing water and let air circulate.
- Drying between uses makes bars last longer → widely given advice; dissolved mush is lost soap.
