## Fact check

- Most smart/keyless fobs contain a mechanical emergency key blade released by a button or slider.
- Many cars conceal the driver's door lock cylinder under a removable cap on the handle.
- Push-button-start cars typically have a backup antenna (at the start button, steering column or a marked spot) that reads the fob's passive transponder chip by induction, so a fob with a flat battery can still start the car; the handbook shows the exact spot.
