## Fact check

- Airliner cabin windows typically have three panes: outer and middle structural acrylic panes and an inner non-structural scratch pane.
- The small breather (bleed) hole in the middle pane equalises pressure between cabin and the inter-pane gap so the outer pane carries the pressure differential.
- The middle pane is a fail-safe that can carry the load if the outer pane fails.
- The hole also lets moisture escape, reducing fogging and frost.
