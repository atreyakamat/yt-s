## Fact check

- Wireless mouse receivers ('dongles') contain a 2.4 GHz radio and antenna.
- Receivers are factory-paired to their device ID; some can be re-paired or take several devices.
- Report rates: around 125 Hz standard, 1,000 Hz for gaming mice (bar values are typical examples).
- Mice enter low-power sleep modes when idle, giving battery life of months to over a year.
