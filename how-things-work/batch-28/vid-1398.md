## Fact check

- A scissor jack uses a lead screw across a linkage of four arms; turning the screw draws the side pivots together and raises the top.
- Mechanical advantage: the handle moves a long distance per turn while the lift point moves a few millimetres, multiplying force (work in ≈ work out minus friction).
- Jacks lift one corner/side of a car, not the whole weight. Thread friction makes the screw self-locking so it doesn't back-drive under load.
