## Fact check

- Machined metal surfaces have microscopic roughness; contact happens only at high points, leaving air gaps.
- Thermal conductivity: air ≈ 0.026 W/m·K, typical thermal paste ≈ 1-10 W/m·K, copper ≈ 400 W/m·K (bar units W/m·K).
- Thermal interface material is meant to fill gaps, not to separate the surfaces; a thinner bond line conducts better.
- A pea-sized amount spread by mounting pressure is common guidance.
