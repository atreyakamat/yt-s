## Fact check

- GPS was developed by the US Department of Defense; the first Block I satellite launched in 1978 and the system reached full operational capability in 1995.
- Receivers need signals from at least four satellites to solve for position and time.
- GPS satellites carry atomic clocks; a timing error of 1 microsecond equals about 300 m of range error.
- Selective Availability degraded civilian accuracy to roughly 100 m until it was switched off on 2 May 2000; accuracy is now typically within several metres.
