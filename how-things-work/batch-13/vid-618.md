## Fact check

- A 12 V lead-acid battery has six cells in series of about 2.1 V each (about 12.6 V fully charged).
- Cells contain lead and lead dioxide plates with separators in dilute sulphuric acid; discharge converts both plates to lead sulphate, releasing electrons.
- Starter motors draw roughly 100-300 A briefly; headlights draw around 10 A. Both labelled 'roughly'.
- The alternator recharges the battery while the engine runs; cold reduces available capacity and slows the reaction, so battery failures peak in winter.
