## Fact check

- Container and pillar candles 'tunnel' if early burns are too short for the melt pool to reach the edge; later burns follow the same pool.
- Candle makers commonly advise a first burn of roughly one hour per inch of diameter, until the melt pool reaches the edge (never leave a candle unattended).
- Trimming wicks to about 1/4 inch (about 5-6 mm) reduces flame size and soot, per common candle-industry guidance.
- Trim wick to about 1/4 inch (~6 mm): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candle
