## Fact check

- Martin Cooper of Motorola made the first public handheld cellular call on 3 April 1973 in New York, calling Joel Engel of Bell Labs (AT&T), Motorola's rival.
- Mobile car telephone service began in the US in 1946.
- The DynaTAC prototype weighed about 1.1 kg (2.5 lb), offered roughly 30-35 minutes of talk time and took about 10 hours to charge.
- The first commercial handheld cell phone (DynaTAC 8000X) went on sale in 1983.
- 3 April 1973 call, Joel Engel (Bell Labs), 1.1 kg, ~30 min talk, ~10 h charge, 1983 on sale: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Cooper_(inventor)
- Car phone service 1946 (St. Louis, 17 June 1946): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_Telephone_Service
