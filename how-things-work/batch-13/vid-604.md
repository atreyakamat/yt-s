## Fact check

- Theophilus Van Kannel of Philadelphia received a US patent for a revolving 'storm door' in 1888, intended to keep out wind, snow, rain and noise.
- The first installation is commonly given as 1899 at Rector's restaurant in New York's Times Square.
- The stack (chimney) effect in tall buildings drives air in at ground level when it is cold outside.
- Revolving doors maintain a seal via wings and brushes; studies (e.g. at MIT) found they save energy compared with swinging doors when used.
