## Fact check

- Incandescent signal lamps were typically around 135-150 W; LED signal heads use roughly 10-15 W, about 90% less, and last many years.
- LED signal heads contain many LEDs (or a few high-power ones), so a single failure doesn't extinguish the indication.
- Because LEDs produce little heat at the lens, snow and ice can build up and obscure signals; heated lenses and visors are used in some cold regions.
