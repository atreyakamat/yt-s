## Fact check

- Overhead door closers use a spring compressed during opening and a hydraulic oil damper with adjustable valves to control closing speed.
- Fire doors only resist fire and smoke when closed; fire safety guidance says not to wedge them open. Bars are illustrative.
- Electromagnetic hold-open devices release fire doors when the fire alarm activates.
