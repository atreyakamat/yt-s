## Fact check

- Cuticle → hair's outer layer of overlapping scales.
- Negative charge → hair surfaces carry a net negative charge, increased by washing and damage.
- Cationic surfactants → conditioners use positively charged surfactants (e.g. behentrimonium chloride) plus silicones/oils that adsorb onto the negative hair.
- Reduced friction and static → the deposited film lowers combing force and flyaways.
