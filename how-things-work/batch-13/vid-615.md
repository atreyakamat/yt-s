## Fact check

- Bin bags are made by blown film extrusion: molten polyethylene is extruded through an annular die and inflated into a tall bubble (blown film lines are often several storeys high), then collapsed, sealed and perforated.
- Low-density/linear low-density polyethylene bags are soft and stretch before tearing; high-density polyethylene film is crinkly, stiffer and more prone to puncture.
- The green polyethylene garbage bag is credited to Canadians Harry Wasylyk and Larry Hansen in 1950.
- Plastic garbage bag invented in Canada, 1950 (Wasylyk, Hansen, Plomp): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bin_bag
