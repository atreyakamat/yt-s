## Fact check

- Food waste disposers use a motor-driven turntable with blunt, often freely swivelling impellers (lugs); there are no sharp blades.
- Centrifugal force throws waste against a stationary serrated grind ring that shreds it into small particles.
- Running water carries the particles through the ring and into the drain.
