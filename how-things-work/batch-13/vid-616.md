## Fact check

- Phones use MEMS microphones: a silicon diaphragm and a perforated backplate form a capacitor; sound pressure moves the diaphragm and changes capacitance.
- MEMS mic packages are a few millimetres across and include an ASIC that amplifies and often digitises the signal (PDM output).
- Smartphones commonly have three or more microphones for calls, video and noise reduction (grid shows an example).
- Diaphragms are on the order of a micrometre thick, much thinner than a human hair (~50-100 micrometres).
