## Fact check

- Before the modern safety pin, clothes were fastened with straight pins, among other fasteners.
- Ancient fibulae (brooch-like clasps) date back over 3,000 years.
- Walter Hunt, a New York mechanic, patented the safety pin on 10 April 1849, reportedly devising it to pay off a 15 dollar debt.
- The design: one wire with a coil spring at one end and a clasp covering the point.
- Hunt sold the patent rights for 400 dollars.
