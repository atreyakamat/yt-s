## Fact check

- 30-second pulse → many electric toothbrushes have a 'quadpacer' style timer that pauses or changes the buzz every 30 s.
- Two minutes → the standard brushing time recommended by dental bodies such as the NHS and the ADA (stated as a common recommendation, not advice).
- 4 × 30 s = 120 s → arithmetic.
- Many people brush for less → studies of brushing habits commonly find average times under two minutes; worded as 'many'.
