## Fact check

- Two-tone erasers combine a soft pencil eraser with a harder, abrasive side (often blue/grey) intended for ink or typewriter marks.
- Abrasive erasers remove ink by wearing away the paper surface, which can damage or tear thin paper.
- The abrasive side works better on heavier paper.
