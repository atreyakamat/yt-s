## Fact check

- Older recipes routinely used vague measures such as 'butter the size of an egg', 'a teacup of flour' or 'a handful'.
- Fannie Farmer's 'The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book' (1896) promoted standardised, level measurements and is credited with popularising level cups and spoons in the US; she is often called the 'mother of level measurements'.
- Most countries outside the US mainly measure baking ingredients by weight.
