## Fact check

- A brush's parts: tips, belly, heel (where bristles meet the ferrule), ferrule (metal band) and handle.
- Standard painting advice: dip about a third (up to half) of the bristle length, so paint doesn't load the heel, where it dries and splays the bristles and drips down the handle.
- Paint is held between bristles by capillary action.
- Tapping the brush against the inside of the tin keeps the load; scraping on the rim strips paint off and fills the lid groove.
