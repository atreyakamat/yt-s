## Fact check

- Shirting and denim are woven fabrics (warp and weft interlaced at right angles).
- T-shirts are usually single jersey knit: interlocking rows of loops.
- Knit loops deform under tension, giving stretch; woven fabrics stretch little along the grain but give more on the bias (diagonal).
- A broken loop in a knit can unravel down the column (a 'run' or 'ladder'): most familiar in tights, also possible in jersey.
