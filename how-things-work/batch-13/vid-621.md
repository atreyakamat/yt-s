## Fact check

- Ballpoint tips typically use balls about 0.5-1.0 mm in diameter held in a socket.
- Ballpoint ink is viscous, oil-based paste; the rolling ball transfers it and reduces drying by sealing the tip.
- Tungsten carbide is widely used for ballpoint balls because of its hardness.
- Standard ballpoints rely on gravity to feed ink; pressurised cartridges are needed to write upside down.
