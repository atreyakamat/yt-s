## Fact check

- Handbarrows (a tray or frame on two poles carried by two people) were used for site loads before and alongside wheelbarrows.
- The earliest clear depictions of wheelbarrows are in Han-dynasty China, around the 1st-2nd century AD ('nearly 2,000 years ago').
- Wheelbarrows appear in Europe around the late 1100s-1200s (Middle Ages).
- Many traditional Chinese wheelbarrows had a large central wheel carrying the load; the European type has a front wheel and acts as a second-class lever, so the user lifts only part of the weight.
- Wheelbarrow in Han China: Chengdu mural dated 118 AD (nearly 2,000 years ago); Europe 1170-1250; Chinese designs had a central wheel: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheelbarrow
