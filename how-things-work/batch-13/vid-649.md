## Fact check

- Telescoping luggage handles use nested tubes of decreasing size.
- Spring-loaded detent pins or latches engage holes at set heights to lock the handle.
- A button in the grip operates a rod or cable mechanism that retracts the latches together (designs vary).
- Handles are commonly made of aluminium alloy tubes for stiffness and low weight.
