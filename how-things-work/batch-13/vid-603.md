## Fact check

- Mary Anderson received a US patent in 1903 for a hand-operated window cleaning device swung by a lever inside the car.
- Charlotte Bridgwood patented an automatic, electrically driven roller-type wiper in 1917.
- Vacuum-powered wipers (1920s onward) used intake manifold vacuum, which falls under acceleration, so they slowed down; electric wipers replaced them. Intermittent wipers appeared in the 1960s.
- Many modern cars have rain-sensing automatic wipers.
- Anderson patent 1903 (lever-operated); Bridgwood 1917 automatic wiper that used rollers: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windscreen_wiper and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Bridgwood
