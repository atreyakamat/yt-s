## Fact check

- Many button-down shirts have a small loop at the back yoke, below the collar, often above a box pleat.
- It is commonly called a locker loop and is intended for hanging the shirt on a hook (e.g. in a locker or changing room).
- A box pleat adds ease across the back: standard shirt construction.
