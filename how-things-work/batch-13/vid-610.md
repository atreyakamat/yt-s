## Fact check

- Ear trumpets were used from at least the 17th century as passive sound collectors that funnel sound into the ear.
- Beethoven used ear trumpets, some made by Johann Mälzel.
- Concealed hearing devices were built into furniture, fans and other objects in the 19th century.
- Electric hearing aids using carbon microphones appeared around 1900; transistor aids arrived in the early 1950s. Bars show relative size, illustrative only.
