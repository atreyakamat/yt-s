## Fact check

- Fenway Park's Green Monster scoreboard was placed in 1934 and is still updated by hand from behind the wall. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Monster
- The scoreboard displays Morse code initials of former owners Thomas A. Yawkey (TAY) and Jean R. Yawkey (JRY) in vertical stripes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Monster
