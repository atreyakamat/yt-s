## Fact check

- Wire rope is made of steel wires twisted into strands that are laid helically around a core. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wire_rope
- Wire rope is flexible, and the load is shared between many wires, so a single broken wire does not mean failure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wire_rope
- Strands and wires in regular lay rope are twisted in opposite directions to resist unwinding. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wire_rope
