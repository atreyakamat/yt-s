## Fact check

- Automatic number-plate recognition uses cameras (often with infrared illumination), image processing and optical character recognition to read plates; used for car park access. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automatic_number-plate_recognition
- Number plates are often retroreflective. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plate
- 'Blink' is a SOFTENED qualitative claim (processing takes a fraction of a second). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automatic_number-plate_recognition
