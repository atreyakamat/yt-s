## Fact check

- Rumble strips alert inattentive drivers with vibration and audible rumbling transmitted through the wheels. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumble_strip
- Shoulder and centreline strips run along the lane; transverse strips cross it to warn of an intersection or toll booth. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumble_strip
- The audio frequency depends on groove spacing and vehicle speed; musical roads / singing shoulders use this. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumble_strip
- Early use in 1943 on New Jersey Route 6; first implemented on the Garden State Parkway in 1952. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumble_strip
