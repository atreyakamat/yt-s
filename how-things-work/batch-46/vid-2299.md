## Fact check

- A protractor measures angles in degrees; many have two scales (inner and outer) that read in opposite directions. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protractor
- A half-circle protractor spans 180 degrees. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protractor
