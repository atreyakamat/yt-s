## Fact check

- Extracorporeal shock wave lithotripsy uses focused acoustic shock waves to fragment kidney stones. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithotripsy
- Shock waves can be generated by spark-gap (electrohydraulic), electromagnetic or piezoelectric sources and are focused on the stone. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithotripsy
- Waves pass through soft tissue and fragment the stone; fragments are passed in urine. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithotripsy
- A treatment uses a few thousand pulses (script says thousands of pulses). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithotripsy
