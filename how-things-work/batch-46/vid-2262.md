## Fact check

- An audiometer plays pure tones at set frequencies and levels; the threshold is the quietest level heard. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audiometry
- Results are plotted on an audiogram with frequency on one axis and hearing level in decibels on the other. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audiogram
- Bone conduction testing uses a vibrator placed behind the ear. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audiometry
- Tests are done one ear at a time in a quiet room or booth. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audiometry
