## Fact check

- Reading stones (early magnifiers) were used before spectacles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reading_stone
- Eyeglasses developed in northern Italy in the second half of the 13th century (about 1290 at the latest). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasses
- Early glasses had no temples (arms); rigid side arms came in the 18th century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasses
