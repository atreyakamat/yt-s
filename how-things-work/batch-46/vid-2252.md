## Fact check

- The earliest existing record of a telescope is a 1608 patent application to the Dutch government by Middelburg spectacle maker Hans Lipperhey for a refracting telescope. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telescope
- The Hague discussed patent applications first from Lipperhey and then Jacob Metius of Alkmaar; Zacharias Janssen of Middelburg is sometimes associated; the true inventor is not known. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telescope
- 1609: Galileo heard of the invention, built his own and used it for celestial observations. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telescope
