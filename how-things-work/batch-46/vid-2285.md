## Fact check

- Offset lithography relies on the immiscibility of oil and water: image areas accept ink and blank areas accept water and repel ink. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offset_printing
- The image is transferred from the plate to a rubber blanket and then to paper. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offset_printing
- Full colour uses cyan, magenta, yellow and black (CMYK) inks, one plate each. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CMYK_color_model
