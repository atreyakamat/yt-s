## Fact check

- Jewellers use a thermal probe with two battery-powered thermistors in a copper tip; one heats, one measures; a diamond conducts heat away quickly causing a temperature drop; most simulants insulate; the test takes about 2-3 seconds; moissanite has similar conductivity and can fool older probes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_simulant
