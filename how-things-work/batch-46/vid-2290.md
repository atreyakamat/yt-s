## Fact check

- Robert Propst designed the cubicle for Herman Miller: Action Office I in 1964, Action Office II in 1967. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubicle
- Propst's studies found open layouts reduced communication and hindered initiative; the aim was quiet and privacy at lower cost than private offices. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubicle
- Before cubicles, most workers sat at desks in rows in one open room. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubicle
- Bars chart is illustrative (relative cost), no figures claimed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubicle
