## Fact check

- Cement is the binder; concrete is cement, water and aggregate (sand and gravel). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
- Aggregate makes up the bulk of a concrete mixture; the cement share is small. The exact percentages could not be confirmed on Wikipedia, so the script gives no figure and the bars are a rough illustration. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
- Cement paste alone shrinks and cracks more and costs more than concrete. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cement
