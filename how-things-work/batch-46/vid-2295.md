## Fact check

- William Watts patented the shot tower method in 1782 in Bristol, dropping molten lead from a height so drops form spheres. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shot_tower
- Molten lead droplets become spherical due to surface tension and solidify as they fall into water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shot_tower
- Lead is poured through a sieve or colander at the top of the tower. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shot_tower
