## Fact check

- Party blowers (blowouts) are curled paper tubes with a reed mouthpiece; air uncurls them and the curl returns when blowing stops. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_horn
- No timing figure is given; "faster than a blink" is a loose description of the quick unroll.
