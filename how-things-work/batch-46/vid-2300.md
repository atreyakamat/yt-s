## Fact check

- ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 is the standard card size of about 85.6 by 54 mm, used by bank cards, ID cards and many key cards; ROUNDED to 'about 86 by 54'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO/IEC_7810
- Card readers and ATMs are built to the standard card format. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO/IEC_7810
