## Fact check

- A camera obscura (Latin for dark chamber) projects an inverted, reversed image through a small hole; Kepler first used the term in 1604. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camera_obscura
- Mozi described the effect (traditionally 4th century BC); Ibn al-Haytham (965-1040) gave the first experimental and mathematical analysis. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camera_obscura
- Portable box and tent camera obscuras existed from the 1500s-1600s, and portable box models became widely available in the 18th century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camera_obscura
