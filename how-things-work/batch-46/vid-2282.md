## Fact check

- Hypodermic needles have a bevelled tip; the bevel forms a sharp point and cutting edge. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypodermic_needle
- The bevelled tip reduces insertion force and tissue damage compared with a blunt end (design rationale). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypodermic_needle
- Needle points are often ground with multiple facets. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypodermic_needle
