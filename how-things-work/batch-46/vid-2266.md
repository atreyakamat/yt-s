## Fact check

- The ISS Oxygen Generation System makes oxygen by electrolysis of water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_control_and_life_support_system
- Carbon dioxide is removed from cabin air by scrubbers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_control_and_life_support_system
- The station's water recovery system recycles water from humidity condensate and urine. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_control_and_life_support_system
