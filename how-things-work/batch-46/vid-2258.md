## Fact check

- Maple sap is roughly 2-5% sugar; syrup takes about 20 to 50 volumes of sap per volume of syrup. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maple_syrup
- Sap flows on freeze-thaw days in late winter and early spring; syrup is boiled to about 4.1 °C above water's boiling point. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maple_syrup
