## Fact check

- Swim caps are commonly silicone, latex or lycra; purposes include keeping hair out of the face, reducing drag, protecting hair from chlorine or sun, retaining warmth, and indicating skill level in lessons. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swim_cap
- Early 20th century: rubberised fabric; by the 1920s: latex; 1950s: decorated caps fashionable. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swim_cap
- The Soul Cap was banned at the 2020 Summer Olympics because of its size and configuration; FINA approved it on 2 September 2022. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swim_cap
