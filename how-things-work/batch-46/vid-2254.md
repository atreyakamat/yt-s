## Fact check

- A longer snorkel places the lungs deeper where water pressure is higher, making inhalation harder. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snorkel_(swimming)
- Snorkels add respiratory dead space; longer tubes enlarge it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snorkel_(swimming)
- EN 1972 (2015) limits snorkel length to 380 mm for larger lungs (350 mm for smaller). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snorkel_(swimming)
