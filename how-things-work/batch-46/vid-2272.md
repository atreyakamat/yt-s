## Fact check

- Pump-action nasal sprays use a metering pump with a dip tube, valve and spray nozzle, without propellant gas. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasal_spray
- Swirl or mechanical break-up nozzles form droplets. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomizer_nozzle
- Pumps generally need priming to expel air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasal_spray
