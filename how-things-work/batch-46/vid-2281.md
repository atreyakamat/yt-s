## Fact check

- Heron of Alexandria (1st century AD) is the earliest known reference to a vending machine, dispensing wine or holy water for a coin via a pan, lever and valve with a counterweight. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vending_machine
- Percival Everitt invented a postcard vending machine in London in 1883; the Thomas Adams Gum Company gum vending machine on New York train platforms, 1888. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vending_machine
