## Fact check

- Cue marks (cue dots, "cigarette burns") appear in the upper right of a print: a motor cue about 8 seconds before the reel ends and a changeover cue about 1 second before it; each lasts 4 frames (about 0.17 s). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cue_mark
- Most feature reels run about 14 to 20 minutes; two projectors alternated; Fight Club (1999) popularised the term "cigarette burn". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cue_mark
