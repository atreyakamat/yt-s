## Fact check

- Geostationary satellites orbit about 35,786 km above the equator with a period matching Earth's rotation, so they stay over one point. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geostationary_orbit
- From the northern hemisphere a geostationary satellite lies to the south; from the southern hemisphere, to the north. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_dish
- At higher latitudes the satellite appears lower in the sky. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geostationary_orbit
