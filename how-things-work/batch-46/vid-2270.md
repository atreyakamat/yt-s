## Fact check

- Hotel energy-saving systems use a key card holder that switches power to the room on when a card is inserted and off when removed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keycard_lock
- Many systems also set back air conditioning when the card is removed; SOFTENED to 'often'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keycard_lock
