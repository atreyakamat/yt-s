## Fact check

- A tsunami in deep ocean travels at well over 800 km/h. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsunami
- In the open ocean a tsunami is only about a metre or less in amplitude. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsunami
- Wave speed depends on water depth (shallow-water waves); the wave slows and rises as it approaches shore (shoaling). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsunami
