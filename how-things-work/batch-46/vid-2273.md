## Fact check

- The earliest sundials in the archaeological record are shadow clocks from about 1500 BC, Egypt and Babylon. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundial
- The gnomon's style must be parallel to Earth's rotational axis; its angle with the horizontal equals the latitude. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundial
- The equation of time makes solar time drift from clock time by up to about 16 minutes over the year. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundial
