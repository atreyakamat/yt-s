## Fact check

- Single-use (disposable) cameras are simple pre-loaded film cameras with a fixed-focus plastic lens, a simple shutter and a thumbwheel to advance the film. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-use_camera
- After processing, many single-use cameras are collected and reloaded for resale. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-use_camera
