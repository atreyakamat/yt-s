## Fact check

- Inflatable play structures are held up by a continuous blower; air escapes through seams and vents, so pressure is maintained only while the blower runs. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inflatable_castle
- Pressure inside is only slightly higher than the surrounding air (low-pressure inflatable). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inflatable_castle
