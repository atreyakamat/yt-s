## Fact check

- Paint consists of pigment, binder, solvent or carrier, and additives. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paint
- Emulsion (latex) paints are dispersions of polymer particles in water that fuse into a film as water evaporates (coalescence). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latex_paint
- Titanium dioxide is the main white pigment. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titanium_dioxide
