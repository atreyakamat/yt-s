## Fact check

- A paint roller has a reusable frame (with handle) and a cover; the cover is a cylindrical core with pile fabric on it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paint_roller
- The thickness of the fibre is the nap; thinner for smoother surfaces, thicker for rough ones like masonry. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paint_roller
- The cover absorbs paint and transfers it to the surface. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paint_roller
- A cylindrical core can hold paint inside, with the cover absorbing it from inside and filtering it through by wicking. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paint_roller
- Norman James Breakey (Canada) and Richard Croxton Adams (US) each produced a paint roller in 1940. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paint_roller
