## Fact check

- Dry ice (solid CO2) sublimates at about -78.5 C. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_ice
- CO2 gas or dry ice with water vapour makes thick white fog because moisture condenses in the cold; chilled fog dissipates quickly as it rises. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fog_machine
- Gas expanding through a valve cools (Joule-Thomson effect), the principle behind CO2 jets. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joule%E2%80%93Thomson_effect
