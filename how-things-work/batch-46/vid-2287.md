## Fact check

- A polarising filter darkens skies, reduces reflections and cuts glare; rotating it adjusts the effect. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polarizing_filter_(photography)
- Skylight is most polarised about 90 degrees from the sun; non-metallic surfaces like water and glass reflect strongly polarised light. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polarizing_filter_(photography)
