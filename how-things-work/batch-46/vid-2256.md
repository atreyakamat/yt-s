## Fact check

- The first manned balloon flights were in 1783. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balloon_(aeronautics)
- Leonardo da Vinci sketched a pyramidal parachute around 1485. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parachute
- Louis-Sebastien Lenormand made a public parachute descent from a tower in 1783 and coined the word 'parachute'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis-S%C3%A9bastien_Lenormand
- André-Jacques Garnerin made a frameless parachute descent from a balloon on 22 October 1797 at Parc Monceau, Paris; the canopy swung violently and Jérôme Lalande added a vent in 1804. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9-Jacques_Garnerin
