## Fact check

- Steam catapults accelerate an aircraft to flying speed in about two to four seconds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_catapult
- A catapult consists of a piston in a cylinder with a slot, and a shuttle that connects to the aircraft nose gear and is driven by steam from the ship's boilers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_catapult
- Steam catapult stroke lengths range from about 46 to 94 m by model; script gives no figure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_catapult
