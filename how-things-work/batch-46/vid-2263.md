## Fact check

- The trap typically snaps shut in about one-tenth of a second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus_flytrap
- Two trigger hairs must be touched in succession within about 20 seconds (or one hair twice in rapid succession) to close the trap; digestion starts after five more stimuli. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venus_flytrap
