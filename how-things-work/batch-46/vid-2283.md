## Fact check

- A drip emitter restricts flow so a pressure drop occurs; the head loss is achieved by friction and turbulence within the emitter, and early Israeli designs used long, wide passages that clogged less than tiny holes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drip_irrigation
- Delivering water at the roots reduces losses to evaporation and runoff (qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drip_irrigation
