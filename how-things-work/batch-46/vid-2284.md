## Fact check

- The penalty mark is 11 m (12 yards) from the goal line. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_kick_(association_football)

- Keepers study takers' habits (qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penalty_kick_(association_football)
