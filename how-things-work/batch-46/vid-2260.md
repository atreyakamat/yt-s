## Fact check

- Heat sealing joins thermoplastics by applying heat and pressure so the surfaces melt and bond on cooling. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_sealer
- Cup sealing machines apply a film to the rim of cups using heat sealing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_sealer
