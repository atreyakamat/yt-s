## Fact check

- Birdbaths are designed shallow, with gently sloping sides; small songbirds wade rather than swim (design guidance from wildlife and bird organisations, qualitative; no numbers claimed). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birdbath
- Birds bathe to maintain feather condition. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bathing_(birds)
