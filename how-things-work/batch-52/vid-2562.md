## Fact check

- Wood burns by pyrolysis: heat drives off volatile gases which burn as flames. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood_fuel
- Modern stoves use primary and secondary air supplies to burn volatile gases, reducing smoke. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood-burning_stove
- Airwash systems direct air across the inside of the glass. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wood-burning_stove
