## Fact check

- Allicin forms when fresh garlic is crushed or chewed and is the compound most responsible for the hot sensation of raw garlic; ajoene forms when crushed or chopped; allyl methyl sulfide causes garlic breath; cooking removes allicin. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garlic
- Under acid and heat, alliin reacts with amino acids to form pyrroles; two pyrrole rings look red, three blue, four green. A bulb typically has 10 to 20 cloves. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garlic
