## Fact check

- Sun dogs appear about 22 degrees left and right of the Sun, at the same height; made by plate-shaped hexagonal ice crystals with faces nearly horizontal in high cirrus/cirrostratus; most visible with Sun near horizon; reddish side nearest the Sun, blue farther out, colours muted; 20 April 1535 Stockholm halo display lasting about two hours, Vadersolstavlan painting, 1630s copy in Storkyrkan. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_dog
