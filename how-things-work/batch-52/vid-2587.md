## Fact check

- Thermoscope: tube with liquid that rises and falls with temperature, warming the bulb expands the air and lowers the liquid level; Philo of Byzantium credited with the first thermoscope; Galileo may have built one in 1593 (uncited on the page); Ferdinando II de' Medici introduced coloured alcohol replacing gas; Ole Rømer effectively invented the thermometer in 1701 by adding a scale; no scale means no standardised measurements. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermoscope
