## Fact check

- Blushing is reddening of the face caused by psychological factors such as embarrassment, shyness, fear or anger; increased blood flow through capillaries mainly in cheeks and forehead, sometimes ears, neck and upper chest. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blushing
- Facial blood vessels are wider, closer to the surface and more visible than elsewhere; Mellander and colleagues (1982) suggested beta-adrenoceptors dilate facial veins in blushing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blushing
- Salzen (2010) proposed blushing reflects fight-or-flight when neither is possible; Crozier (2010) argues it signals shame and a wish to repair a social misstep and is credible because involuntary. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blushing
- Darwin, in his 1872 book, called blushing 'the most peculiar and most human of all expressions'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blushing
