## Fact check

- Counterfeit detection pens contain an iodine solution that reacts with starch to give a dark stain; ordinary paper often contains starch while banknote paper generally does not. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iodine_test
- The pen only tests the paper, not the printing, so it is used alongside other checks; polymer banknotes are checked differently. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counterfeit_money
- Other checks: watermarks, security threads, raised printing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counterfeit_money
