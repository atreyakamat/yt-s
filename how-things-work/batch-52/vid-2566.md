## Fact check

- A 1.2 mm mesh stops mosquitoes; 0.6 mm also stops biting midges. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquito_net
- The net must be sealed and must not touch the skin, since insects can bite through it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquito_net
- Insecticide-treated nets (pyrethroids such as permethrin) developed and tested in Burkina Faso in the 1980s; estimated about twice as effective as untreated nets. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mosquito_net
