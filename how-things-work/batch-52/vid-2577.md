## Fact check

- Average refraction at the horizon is about 34 arcminutes; apparent radius of the Sun at the horizon is 16 arcminutes (diameter about 32); sunrise is when the upper rim of the Sun appears on the horizon. About 2 minutes early is a derived estimate: Earth turns about 15 arcminutes per minute at the equator, so 34 arcminutes is about 2.3 minutes (script rounds to about two). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunrise
