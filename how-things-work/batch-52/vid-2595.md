## Fact check

- Night modes shift display colour temperature towards warmer tones by reducing blue light. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F.lux
- Daylight contains more blue light than incandescent or candle light. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_temperature
- Evidence for sleep benefits of blue light filters is mixed (script says "debated"). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_effects_on_circadian_rhythm
- Bar values are illustrative.
