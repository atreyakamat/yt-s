## Fact check

- Pins and needles (paresthesia) follows a limb 'falling asleep', usually from keeping it still for a long time; sustained pressure on a nerve restricts its signals; removing the pressure typically brings gradual relief. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paresthesia
- Awkward posture such as prolonged cross-legged sitting is a common trigger. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paresthesia
- Shifting position keeps pressure from building (reasoning from the mechanism; not medical advice).
