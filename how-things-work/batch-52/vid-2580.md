## Fact check

- Each labyrinth contains three semicircular canals roughly at right angles (horizontal, anterior, posterior). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vestibular_system
- Endolymph has roughly the same density and viscosity as water; fluid movement pushes the cupula, which contains hair cells. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vestibular_system
- Cupula relaxation time constant about 20 seconds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vestibular_system
- That the fluid keeps moving after you stop and the sensor misreads it as spinning is the standard explanation (physical reasoning from the above mechanism).
