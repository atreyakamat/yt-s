## Fact check

- The thumb is the only digit opposable to the other four; pulp-to-pulp opposition requires rotation at the carpometacarpal joint. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thumb
- Thumb muscles are described as guy-wires supporting a flagpole, in extrinsic (forearm) and intrinsic (hand) groups. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thumb
- Napier's two main grips: power grip (thumb gives counter pressure) and precision grip; the pad-to-pad pinch depends partly on the index finger's distal phalanx hyperextending. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thumb
- The human thumb's carpometacarpal shape appeared about 5 million years ago; anatomists define opposition in hundreds of ways. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thumb
