## Fact check

- LEDs convert a large fraction of electrical power to light but the rest becomes heat in the chip, which must be removed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LED_lamp
- High junction temperature reduces LED life and light output. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light-emitting_diode
- Heat sinks use extended surface (fins) to dissipate heat. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_sink
- LED beams contain very little infrared compared with incandescent bulbs. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LED_lamp
