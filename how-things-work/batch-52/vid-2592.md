## Fact check

- A scram (reactor trip) rapidly inserts control rods to terminate the fission chain reaction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scram
- Control rods contain neutron-absorbing materials. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_rod
- Many designs use gravity-driven insertion; insertion takes a few seconds (varies by design). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scram
- Decay heat continues after shutdown and must be removed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decay_heat
- The 3 second figure is a rough order of magnitude, not a spec.
