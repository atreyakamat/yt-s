## Fact check

- Bullwhip length, flexibility and taper let part of the whip exceed the speed of sound near the end of the throw, making a small sonic boom. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullwhip
- The cracker flips suddenly around the whip's end and amplifies acceleration; a whip still cracks without one, much more quietly. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullwhip
- Bullwhips range from about 1 m (3 ft) to 6 m (20 ft), with some longer; falls are 25-75 cm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullwhip
