## Fact check

- Bonsai kept small through container confinement with crown and root pruning, not genetic dwarfing; ordinary stock and seedlings. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonsai
- Wiring sets form; wire removed or concealed, must not leave permanent marks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonsai
- Sandai Shogun five-needle pine thought to be at least 500 years old; originated from Chinese penjing, Japanese envoys brought back container plantings from the 6th century onward; 1195 Saigyo Monogatari Emaki earliest known Japanese depiction; first World Bonsai Convention Osaka 1980. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonsai
