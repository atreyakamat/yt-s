## Fact check

- Most premises worldwide get about 220-240 V at 50 Hz; North America usually 120 or 240 V at 60 Hz, 'the biggest exception'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mains_electricity_by_country
- A 1997 IEC amendment replaced older 220/380 V and 240/415 V standards with harmonised 230/400 V. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mains_electricity_by_country
- Japan: 100 V; eastern Japan including Tokyo uses 50 Hz and western Japan including Osaka uses 60 Hz. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mains_electricity_by_country
- Many modern devices accept 100-240 V at 50 or 60 Hz. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AC_power_plugs_and_sockets
