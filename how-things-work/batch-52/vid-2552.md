## Fact check

- Warm air rises and escapes high in a building, drawing cold air in low (stack effect). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stack_effect
- Draught excluders and weatherstripping seal gaps around doors and windows. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weatherstripping
- Buildings with fuel-burning appliances and moist rooms need ventilation; do not block vents. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ventilation_(architecture)
