## Fact check

- Postage meters print postage and keep a locked register of used and remaining credit. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postage_meter
- The first known postage meter was a coin-operated machine installed in Christiania (Oslo), Norway, on 24 August 1900; the Pitney Bowes meter in the US followed in 1920 (corrected from "first in 1920"). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postage_meter
- Modern digital meters print 2D barcodes with cryptographic security. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postage_meter
- Gauge value is illustrative.
