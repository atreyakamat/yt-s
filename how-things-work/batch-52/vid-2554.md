## Fact check

- A magnetic stripe is a strip of magnetic material on a plastic card; swiping past a reading head reads it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_stripe_card
- Hotel key cards generally store their own data rather than the standard banking layout. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_stripe_card
- A moving magnetic pattern induces a current in a coil: Faraday's law. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faraday%27s_law_of_induction
