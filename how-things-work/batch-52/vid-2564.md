## Fact check

- Fire-resistant safes are rated by how long they keep the internal temperature below a set limit, such as 350 F (177 C); paper chars at about 451 F (233 C); ratings from half an hour to four hours; they will not protect indefinitely. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_safe
- Insulation between steel walls slows heat transfer, an 1849 patent described a double-walled chest filled with cement for this purpose. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire_safe
