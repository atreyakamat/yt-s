## Fact check

- A hand truck (sack truck) is an L-shaped frame with two wheels; the load is tilted back to sit over the axle so the wheels carry its weight. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hand_truck
- Technique points (heavy at bottom, tilt to balance) follow from balance of moments. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hand_truck
- Gauge value is illustrative; no safety advice beyond general technique.
