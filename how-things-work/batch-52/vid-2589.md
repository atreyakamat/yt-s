## Fact check

- Bee space about 6-9 mm (1/4-3/8 in); in that gap bees build no comb and do not seal it with propolis. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langstroth_hive
- Rev. Lorenzo Langstroth observed the effect in 1851; US Patent 9300 issued October 5, 1852; frames hang inside boxes and can be removed without cutting comb. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langstroth_hive
