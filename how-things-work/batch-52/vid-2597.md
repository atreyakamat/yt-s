## Fact check

- The band is the glow of unresolved stars in the direction of the galactic plane, as we are inside the disc; the Sun is about 25,000-28,000 light-years from the Galactic Centre, article lead cites roughly 27,000 ly; estimated 100-400 billion stars; Galileo resolved the band into stars with a telescope in 1610; name translates Latin via lactea from Greek galaxias from gala, milk.
