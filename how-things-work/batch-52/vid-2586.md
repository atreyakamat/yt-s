## Fact check

- Reusable coolers usually have interior and exterior plastic shells with hard foam between; disposable ones are polystyrene foam about 2 cm thick. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooler
- Grant Acton applied for a patent for a metal-skin cooler in 1947; the Esky followed in Australia in 1952; Coleman popularised a steel model in the USA in 1954. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooler
- Trapped air as insulator, melting ice absorbs heat, keeping lid shut: standard heat-transfer physics.
