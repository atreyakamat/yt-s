## Fact check

- Exif metadata in image files can include date, camera settings and GPS coordinates. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exif
- Many online services strip location metadata from uploaded images; direct sharing may keep it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exif
- Phones let users disable camera location or strip location when sharing (script says "let you"). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exif
