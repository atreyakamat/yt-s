## Fact check

- Cones regain maximum sensitivity in about 9-10 minutes; full dark adaptation takes roughly 20-30 minutes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptation_(eye)
- Red light helps because rods are much less sensitive to long wavelengths, so it preserves rod sensitivity. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptation_(eye)
- Airlines dim lights for night takeoffs and landings so passengers' eyes are adjusted in an emergency: reason stated by airlines and cabin crew (general airline guidance); no source page fetched.
