## Fact check

- The metal nib has a central slit that works as a capillary channel, same principle as fountain pen nibs; a small hole, indent or pocket holds a drop of ink; dip pens generally have no reservoir; a clip-on reservoir can add several sentences. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dip_pen
- John Mitchell of Birmingham began mass-producing metal-nib pens in 1822; by 1830 Mitchell, Gillott and Mason were major Birmingham makers; quill was most common before. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dip_pen
