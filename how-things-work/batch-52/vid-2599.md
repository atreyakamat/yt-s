## Fact check

- Most small motors use black powder with cardboard bodies and moulded clay nozzles; delay number is seconds before ejection charge fires; during delay a tracking charge produces smoke with essentially no thrust; '0' means no delay/ejection charge. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_rocket
- Each letter class has up to twice the impulse of the previous: A 1.26-2.50 N·s, B 2.51-5.0, C 5.01-10, D 10.01-20 (bars show the class maximum). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_rocket
- Ejection charge pushes the parachute out, often popping off the nose cone. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_rocket
