## Fact check

- 15th century: British cattle hooves were boiled to produce a gel; late 17th century: Denis Papin discovered extraction by boiling bones; 1754: an English gelatin production patent; 1812: Jean-Pierre-Joseph d'Arcet experimented with hydrochloric acid and steam; mid-19th century: Peter Cooper patented a gelatin dessert powder; late 19th century: Charles and Rose Knox set up their company. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelatin
- Gelatin comes from partial hydrolysis of collagen from animal skin, bones and connective tissue. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelatin
