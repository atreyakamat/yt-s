## Fact check

- Red cabbage dye is anthocyanin-based; red, pink or magenta in acid (pH below 7), purple at neutral (about pH 7), and blue then green then yellow in alkaline conditions; the juice can serve as a homemade pH indicator. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_cabbage
- Vinegar is an acid and baking soda is alkaline (ordinary chemistry); colours follow the acid and alkaline ends above.
