## Fact check

- Nitrification: ammonium to nitrite by Nitrosomonas species, nitrite to nitrate by Nitrobacter species; Nitrospira can oxidize ammonia to nitrate in one organism (comammox). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitrogen_cycle
- Fishless cycling: method for maturing aquarium ecosystems (listed on the same page). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitrogen_cycle
- Fish waste produces ammonium/ammonia that is toxic to fish: standard aquarium knowledge (qualitative, no number used).
