## Fact check

- Saliva is about 99% water; production estimated at about 1,500 mL per day (the script says 'over a litre'); output drops significantly during sleep. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saliva
- Amylase begins breaking starch into simpler sugars; about 30% of starch digestion happens in the mouth. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saliva
- Saliva lubricates food, buffers mouth acidity and helps limit bacterial growth and tooth decay; unstimulated, the submandibular gland gives roughly 65-75% and the parotid 20-25%, rising to about 50% for the parotid after stimulation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saliva
