## Fact check

- An electromagnet on the frame holds an armature plate on the door; fail-safe locks release when de-energised. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_lock
- Holding force ranges from about 275 lbf for micro units to 1,200 lbf for standard ones; no moving parts. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_lock
