## Fact check

- Screenshots copy the contents of the display framebuffer or compositor output to an image file. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screenshot
- Apps and DRM systems can prevent capture, producing black images. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screenshot
- Framebuffer holds the image sent to the display. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framebuffer
