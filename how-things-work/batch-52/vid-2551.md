## Fact check

- Internal (poker) vibrators consolidate fresh concrete by removing entrapped air voids; over-vibration can cause segregation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete_placement
- Air voids reduce strength and let water reach reinforcement. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
