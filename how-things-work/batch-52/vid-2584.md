## Fact check

- Peak angular acceleration of 1,600,000 degrees per second squared, reached in 7 milliseconds; an eye blink takes about 150 milliseconds; a snap has the highest angular acceleration of any motion produced by the human body. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finger_snapping
- Georgia Institute of Technology study, 2021, 'The Ultrafast Snap of a Finger Is Mediated by Skin Friction' (J. R. Soc. Interface). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finger_snapping
- Tension builds between the thumb and another finger, which is driven down to hit the palm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finger_snapping
