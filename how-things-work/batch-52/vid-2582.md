## Fact check

- Balanced flues draw combustion air from outside and expel flue gases through a concentric arrangement, sealed from the room. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flue
- Room-sealed appliances reduce the risk of fumes entering the room. Source: https://www.plumbingsuperstore.co.uk/help-and-advice/product-guides/heating-and-ventilation/a-guide-to-boiler-flues/
- Fan-assisted flues allow longer runs. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flue
- The warming gauge is illustrative of heat exchange between the pipes.
