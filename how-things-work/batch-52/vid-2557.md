## Fact check

- Cumulonimbus flat anvil top is caused by wind shear or inversion at the equilibrium level near the tropopause; typical peaks roughly 12,000 m, unusually high ones around 20,000 m; overshooting tops exist; most individual storm cells die after about 20 minutes once precipitation downdrafts overtake the updraft. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumulonimbus_cloud
