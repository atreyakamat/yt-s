## Fact check

- Postal sorting machines use optical character recognition to read addresses and apply barcodes; unreadable items are sent for human keying. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mail_sorting
- OCR output is checked against address databases. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_character_recognition
- 'In a blink' is qualitative; no number claimed.
