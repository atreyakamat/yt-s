# vid-1510 · How the second is defined

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "One second is nine billion vibrations."
- Backup 1: "The second has a very strange definition."
- Backup 2: "What counts a second for the whole planet."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

SI second = 9,192,631,770 periods of the Cs-133 hyperfine transition. Best optical clocks are far better; cesium fountain clocks lose roughly a second per hundreds of millions of years (NIST). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. One second is nine billion vibrations.
2. Atomic clocks count the vibrations of cesium-133 atoms.
3. Exactly 9,192,631,770 of them make one second.
4. Atoms are better timekeepers than the spinning Earth.
5. Good atomic clocks lose about a second in hundreds of millions of years.
6. That precision is why your phone knows the exact time.
