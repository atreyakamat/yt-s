# vid-1503 · Why you hear the firework after you see it

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "You always hear fireworks after you see them."
- Backup 1: "Count the seconds and you can measure the sky."
- Backup 2: "Your ears are slower than your eyes."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Speed of sound in air ~343 m/s at 20C, so ~3 s per km. Same rule as lightning/thunder. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_of_sound

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. You always hear fireworks after you see them.
2. Light travels almost instantly across the sky.
3. Sound is far slower, about 340 metres every second.
4. So the further away you stand, the bigger the gap.
5. Count the seconds between flash and boom.
6. Divide by three and that's roughly how many kilometres away it burst.
