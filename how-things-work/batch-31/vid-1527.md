## Fact check (verify before posting)

Fritz Lang's Frau im Mond (1929) is widely credited with popularising the backward countdown; some historians note earlier uses, so word as 'widely credited'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countdown

3. He wanted a launch scene with suspense.
4. So he had them count backwards: ten, nine, eight.
5. Real rocket teams adopted it, and parties copied them.
6. Now everyone counts down to midnight.
