# vid-1546 · Why the earliest sunset isn't the shortest day

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "The earliest sunset comes before the shortest day."
- Backup 1: "Why your evenings brighten before your mornings."
- Backup 2: "The solstice isn't the earliest sunset."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

At mid-northern latitudes the earliest sunset is about 2 weeks before the winter solstice and the latest sunrise about 2 weeks after (equation of time + Earth's tilt/eccentricity). Exact dates depend on latitude. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equation_of_time

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. The earliest sunset comes before the shortest day.
2. The shortest day is the winter solstice, around December 21.
3. But the earliest sunset arrives in early December.
4. The latest sunrise comes in early January.
5. Solar noon drifts across the year, a gap called the equation of time.
6. So afternoons brighten before mornings do.
