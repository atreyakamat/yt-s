# vid-1509 · Why we have leap years

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Leap years exist because Earth is late."
- Backup 1: "Your calendar has a bug fix every four years."
- Backup 2: "2100 will not be a leap year."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Tropical year ~365.2422 days. Gregorian rule: divisible by 4, except centuries unless divisible by 400. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leap_year

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Leap years exist because Earth is late.
2. Earth takes about 365.24 days to orbit the Sun.
3. A 365-day calendar loses almost six hours every year.
4. Add a day every fourth year and it mostly lines up.
5. Except centuries, which skip it, unless divisible by 400.
6. Without that, the seasons would slowly drift.
