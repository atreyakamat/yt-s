## Fact check (verify before posting)

3-2-1 backup rule popularised by photographer Peter Krogh: 3 copies, 2 different media, 1 offsite.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Lose your phone, keep your photos.
2. Keep three copies of anything important.
3. Store them on two different kinds of storage.
4. Keep one copy somewhere else, like the cloud.
5. A single drive is one fire or spill away from gone.
6. Do it once this January and you're set.
