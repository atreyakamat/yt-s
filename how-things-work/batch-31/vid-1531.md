# vid-1531 · Why time zones were invented

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Trains forced the world to agree on time."
- Backup 1: "Why every town used to have its own time."
- Backup 2: "Railways created time zones."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Great Western Railway adopted GMT 1840; GMT standard across UK railways by mid-1840s; International Meridian Conference 1884 picked Greenwich as prime meridian. Time zones spread after (US railways standardised 1883). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_zone

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Trains forced the world to agree on time.
2. Before railways, every town kept its own local noon.
3. That made timetables a nightmare.
4. Britain's railways adopted a single Greenwich time in the 1840s.
5. In 1884, delegates chose Greenwich as the world's zero meridian.
6. Zones followed, and so did the idea of a global midnight.
