## Fact check (verify before posting)

50/30/20 rule popularised by Elizabeth Warren & Amelia Warren Tyagi (All Your Worth, 2005). A rule of thumb, not advice.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. A budget is permission, not punishment.
2. A budget is just deciding where money goes before it goes.
3. A popular starter split is 50-30-20.
4. Fifty percent for needs like rent and food.
5. Thirty for wants, twenty for savings and debt.
6. Tweak the numbers to fit your life.
