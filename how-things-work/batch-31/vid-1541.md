# vid-1541 · How the Gregorian calendar stays accurate

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Even our calendar slowly drifts off."
- Backup 1: "How accurate is your calendar?"
- Backup 2: "The calendar will drift by a day eventually."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Gregorian mean year 365.2425 d; mean tropical year ~365.2422 d; difference ~0.0003 d = ~26 s/yr; one day per ~3,200 years (often stated ~3,000; ~3,300 by some). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julian_calendar

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Even our calendar slowly drifts off.
2. A Gregorian year averages 365.2425 days.
3. The true tropical year is about 365.2422 days.
4. The gap is about twenty-six seconds a year.
5. That adds up to a day in roughly three thousand years.
6. Good enough for now.
