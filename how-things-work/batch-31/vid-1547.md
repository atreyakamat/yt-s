# vid-1547 · Why snow looks white

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Snow is made of clear ice."
- Backup 1: "Clear ice turns white once it's broken up."
- Backup 2: "The colour snow doesn't actually have."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Snow is white because ice crystals scatter all visible wavelengths roughly equally (diffuse multiple scattering); crushed ice and foam appear white for the same reason.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Snow is made of clear ice.
2. Each snowflake is a tiny transparent ice crystal.
3. Light bounces off the many crystal faces.
4. All the colours scatter back out together.
5. Mixed colours look white to your eyes.
6. Crush ice and it turns white too.
