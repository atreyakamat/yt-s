## Fact check (verify before posting)

Typical sleep cycle ~90 min (range 70-110); AASM/SRS recommend 7+ hours for adults. Mid-cycle grogginess is 'sleep inertia' (stronger after deep sleep) — hedge. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleep_cycle

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Your sleep runs in 90-minute loops.
2. Each night you move through light, deep and REM sleep.
3. One full pass takes about ninety minutes.
4. You repeat it four to six times a night.
5. Adults are advised to get seven hours or more.
6. Waking mid-cycle is why you can feel groggy.
