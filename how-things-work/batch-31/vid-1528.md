# vid-1528 · Why January 3 puts Earth closest to the Sun

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Earth is closest to the Sun in January."
- Backup 1: "Winter happens when Earth is nearest the Sun."
- Backup 2: "Why seasons aren't about distance."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Perihelion ~3-4 Jan, distance ~147.1 million km vs ~152.1 million km at aphelion (about 3% closer; ~7% more sunlight). Axial tilt ~23.4 degrees. 'A few percent' of distance, ~7% of energy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apsis

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Earth is closest to the Sun in January.
2. Around January 3, Earth reaches its closest point to the Sun.
3. Yet it's winter in the Northern Hemisphere.
4. Seasons come from Earth's 23-degree tilt, not distance.
5. The Sun's rays hit the north at a shallow angle.
6. Distance changes our energy by only about seven percent.
