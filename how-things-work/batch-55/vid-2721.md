## Fact check

- The grader's blade (mouldboard) sits between the front wheels and rear tandem axles; many graders have an articulated frame; uses include preparing the base course for paved roads, maintaining gravel roads and snow removal. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motor_grader
- Blade in the middle of the wheelbase halves the influence of a bump on the front or rear wheel: geometric reasoning, no number used.
