## Fact check

- Four forces: thrust, lift, weight, drag; trim adjustments with dihedral, ailerons, elevators, rudders made by bending or cutting the trailing edges. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_plane
- Centre of mass should sit ahead of the centre of area; two ways to shift mass forward: rolling up the leading edge, or using a swept wing/axial folds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_plane
- Joe Ayoob threw a paper airplane 69.14 m (226 ft 10 in) in 2012, designed by John Collins (since broken by later records); earliest known from an American children's book of 1864. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_plane
