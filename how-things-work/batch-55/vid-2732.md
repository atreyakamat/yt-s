## Fact check

- Wind turbine towers are commonly tapered tubular steel, with larger diameter and thicker walls at the base where bending moment is greatest. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_turbine_design
- Bending moment from a force at the top grows toward the base. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bending_moment
- Towers house ladders, cables and electrical equipment. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_turbine
- Gauge is illustrative.
