## Fact check

- Strongrooms were built in bank basements, where ceilings were vaulted, which gave the vault its name. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_vault
- Older vaults had walls at least 1 ft thick and doors typically 3.5 ft thick; time locks were invented in the late 19th century (Sargent) and keep the door shut until a set number of hours has passed; round doors were popular in the early 20th century but fell out of favour due to manufacturing complexity, sagging and cost. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_vault
