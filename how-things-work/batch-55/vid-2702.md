## Fact check

- Cavity walls have two leaves separated by an air gap; insulation can be injected into the cavity. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cavity_wall
- Insulation materials work mainly by trapping air or gas in small cells, preventing convection. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermal_insulation
- Still air has low thermal conductivity. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermal_conductivity
- Bar values are illustrative, not measured.
