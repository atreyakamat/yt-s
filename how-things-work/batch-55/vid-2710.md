## Fact check

- An automatic, reflex-controlled trapdoor-like movement of the epiglottis covers the larynx and closes the upper airway during swallowing; there is a finite period of apnea. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swallowing
- The bolus moves through the pharynx at about 30-40 cm/s and down the esophagus at about 3-5 cm/s, pushed first by striated and then smooth muscle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swallowing
