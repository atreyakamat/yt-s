## Fact check

- Hot water storage tanks lose heat to their surroundings; insulation reduces standing losses. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_water_storage_tank
- Insulation works by trapping air or gas in small cells. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermal_insulation
- Many modern cylinders come factory-insulated with foam. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_water_storage_tank
- Gauge is illustrative.
