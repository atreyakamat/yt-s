## Fact check

- Hydraulic bottle jack: a plunger pumps oil through check valves into a cylinder, raising the ram; best for short vertical lifts. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_(device)
- Hydraulic pressure multiplies force (Pascal's principle). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydraulics
- Holding with a mechanical support (jack stand) instead of hydraulic pressure is standard safety guidance; reasoning: seals can leak. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_(device)
