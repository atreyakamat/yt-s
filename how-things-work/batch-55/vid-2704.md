## Fact check

- Turnstiles often use ratchet mechanisms that let the stile rotate in one direction and block the other. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turnstile
- Three evenly spaced arms are 360 / 3 = 120 degrees apart: geometry. The one-person-per-turn effect is design reasoning.
