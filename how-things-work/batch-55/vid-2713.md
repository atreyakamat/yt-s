## Fact check

- A cone megaphone is an acoustic horn: speak into the narrow end, sound exits the wide end; it matches acoustic impedance so more sound power is radiated, directs sound the way it points and favours higher frequencies. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megaphone
- Samuel Morland's work published 1672 described horn experiments; his largest was over 6 m of copper tube. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megaphone
- Thomas Edison's 1878 device used three funnels. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megaphone
- Electric megaphones have a microphone, battery-powered amplifier and horn loudspeaker, can reach over 90 dB; transistors (1947) made portable units practical. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megaphone
