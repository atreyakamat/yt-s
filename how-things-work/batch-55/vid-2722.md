## Fact check

- AC EV charging uses a control pilot signal (about 1 kHz PWM) to communicate available current between charger and vehicle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAE_J1772
- Type 2 connectors include proximity pilot and control pilot pins alongside power pins. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEC_62196
- Contactors close only after the vehicle signals readiness. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAE_J1772
- Plug is locked on many installations while charging. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEC_62196
