## Fact check

- Angle grinders spin abrasive or cut-off discs; adjustable guard should be set so sparks fly away from the operator; sparks are produced mainly when cutting ferrous metals; if a disc jams the grinder can jerk back toward the user; face shield and cut-resistant gloves recommended. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angle_grinder
- 'Tiny grains glow as sparks' is a standard explanation of sparks from grinding steel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angle_grinder
