## Fact check

- Ancient Greeks cooked flat cakes called obelios between hot metal plates; as they spread through medieval Europe they became wafers; wafer irons (moule a oublies) emerged around the 9th-10th centuries. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waffle
- Waffle comes from Dutch wafel, from Middle Dutch wafele (late 13th century); French walfre dates to 1185; both trace to Frankish wafla meaning honeycomb. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waffle
- The grid motif appears on a French fer a oublie and a Belgian wafelijzer in the 15th century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waffle
