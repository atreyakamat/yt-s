## Fact check

- St. Elmo's fire is luminous plasma from corona discharge on pointed objects in an atmospheric electric field; blue or violet; roughly 100 kV/m needed in moist air, sharp points reduce required voltage; seen mostly during thunderstorms; named for St. Erasmus of Formia (St. Elmo), patron saint of sailors; Antonio Pigafetta recorded it on Magellan's 1519-1522 voyage; Julius Caesar and Pliny the Elder referenced it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Elmo%27s_fire
