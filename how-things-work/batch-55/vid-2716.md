## Fact check

- The word comes from Spanish hamaca, borrowed from Taino and Arawak; Columbus' first-voyage account mentions people bartering 'hamacas, or nets, in which they sleep.' Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammock
- Sailing ships began using hammocks around 1590; the Royal Navy formally adopted them in 1597; sailors preferred them because they move with the ship. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammock
- Most hammocks are best used with a diagonal position, which provides the most room and support; optimal angle of lines to the anchor point is usually about 30 degrees. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammock
- A cloth can only pull, so it sags into a curve: basic physics (catenary).
