## Fact check

- The funny bone is the ulnar nerve, pinched between bone and skin at the medial epicondyle; it passes behind it through the cubital tunnel; striking it gives an electric-shock-like sensation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulnar_nerve
- The name is thought to be a pun on humerus and humorous; sensory supply is the little finger and medial 1.5 digits; nerve roots C8-T1; motor to small hand muscles (lumbricals). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulnar_nerve
