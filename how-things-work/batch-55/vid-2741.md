## Fact check

- Slump test: Abrams cone filled in three layers, each rodded; cone lifted vertically; slump is the vertical drop; a collapse slump usually indicates too wet a mix; indicates workability. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete_slump_test
- Too much water weakens concrete. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
- No numbers used except 'three layers'.
