## Fact check

- The 610 AD story credits an Italian monk, with dough strips crossed like praying hands; the page presents it as a story, not verified fact. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pretzel
- Practical theory: loops could be hung on sticks for handling or storage, shown in a c. 1681 painting; pretzel used as a bakers' guild emblem in southern German areas since at least the 12th century; 1190 Hortus deliciarum illustration may be the earliest depiction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pretzel
- Treatment with washing soda or lye gives the traditional skin and flavour through the Maillard reaction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pretzel
