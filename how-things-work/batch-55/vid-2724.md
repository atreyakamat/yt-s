## Fact check

- An electric strike replaces the fixed strike plate; its ramped surface lets the latch engage on closing, and when powered the keeper pivots out of the way. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_strike
- Fail-secure types remain locked on power failure, while the mechanical lock can usually still open from inside; AC power produces an audible buzz and DC a faint click. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_strike
