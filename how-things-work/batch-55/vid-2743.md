## Fact check

- In 1882 the sailing ship Dunedin carried a cargo of frozen meat from New Zealand to London, considered the start of the frozen meat trade. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dunedin_(ship)
- Ship refrigeration used mechanical cooling machines to chill air circulated in insulated holds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reefer_ship
- Before refrigeration, meat was preserved by salting or canning, or livestock was shipped alive. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refrigerator_ship
- Verified: Dunedin sailed Feb 1882 from Port Chalmers to London with frozen mutton, lamb and more; arrived after 98 days still frozen. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refrigerator_ship
