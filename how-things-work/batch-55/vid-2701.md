## Fact check

- A gambrel is usually a symmetrical roof with two slopes on each side, the upper shallow and the lower steep. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambrel
- It adds headroom in the upper level, shortens what would otherwise be a tall roof, and reduces the span of each set of rafters. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambrel
- Second Harvard Hall, built in 1677, is the oldest known gambrel roof in North America; the c. 1677-78 Peter Tufts House may be the oldest surviving US house with one. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambrel
- Name from Medieval Latin gamba, 'horse's hock or leg'; also a bar butchers used to hang carcasses. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambrel
