## Fact check

- 54 wooden blocks, tower of 18 layers of three; dimensions 1.5 cm x 2.5 cm x 7.5 cm, each block three times as long as wide. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenga
- Blocks have small random variations from these dimensions to create imperfections in stacking, making the game harder; layers alternate direction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenga
- Leslie Scott of Oxford Games launched it at the London Toy Fair in January 1983; name from Swahili kujenga, to build. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenga
