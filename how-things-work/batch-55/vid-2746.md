## Fact check

- Human feet are too small and poorly shaped to produce much thrust; fins extend surface area and larger blades convert effort into thrust more efficiently, perceived as less fatiguing in one study. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimfin
- Louis de Corlieu demonstrated a prototype in 1914 and registered his patent in April 1933. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimfin
- Full-foot (closed-heel) fits like a shoe; open-heel uses a strap or spring and fits a range of foot sizes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimfin
- A 2003 study found no significant performance change when the split was taped over. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimfin
