## Fact check

- Rime is a deposit that forms when supercooled water droplets, such as in fog, freeze on contact; soft rime in calm or light wind is feathery, hard rime in at least moderate wind is denser and comb-like with streaks parallel to the wind; hoar frost forms by direct deposition of vapour to ice; rime builds on the windward side; supercooled droplets can stay unfrozen down to near -40 C. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rime_ice
