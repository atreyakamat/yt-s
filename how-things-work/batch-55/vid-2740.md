## Fact check

- Snoring is partially obstructed, turbulent airflow making upper-airway tissues vibrate; the airway repeatedly closes and reopens about 50 times per second; soft palate, uvula and base of tongue are the main vibrating structures; usually on inhalation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snoring
- Mild snoring 40-50 dB (quiet conversation), moderate 50-60 dB, severe above 60 dB (busy traffic). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snoring
- Snoring affects 2.6-83% of males and 1.5-71% of females depending on population, and about 8-12% of children. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snoring
