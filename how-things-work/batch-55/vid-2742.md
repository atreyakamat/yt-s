## Fact check

- A fan heater uses a fan to blow air over a heat source, usually an electric heating element. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fan_heater
- A tip-over switch shuts the heater off if it is not upright. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fan_heater
- A thermal fuse near the element prevents overheating if the fan fails or the intake is blocked; a bimetal overheat cutout resets once cool; the thermal fuse is a permanent backup if the cutout fails. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fan_heater
- In 220/230 V countries 3 kW is the maximum but 2 kW is common. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fan_heater
