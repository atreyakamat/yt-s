## Fact check

- Vein-pattern recognition images subcutaneous veins with near-infrared light; haemoglobin in blood absorbs it, so veins appear dark against surrounding tissue. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vein_matching
- The vein pattern is inside the body, so it is not left on surfaces the way fingerprints are: reasoning from the technique. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vein_matching
