## Fact check

- Inversion is a layer of warmer air over cooler air so temperature rises with altitude; fog typically present below the inversion cap; the Great Smog of London 1952 is one of the most serious examples, blamed for an estimated 10,000 to 12,000 deaths; radiative inversions commonly occur at night; valleys and basins intensify inversions by retaining cold air. Clean air laws followed in Britain (the 1956 Clean Air Act): general knowledge, no date claimed in script. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inversion_(meteorology)
