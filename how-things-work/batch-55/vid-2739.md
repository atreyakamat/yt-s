## Fact check

- Cat's eye marbles: coloured glass veins injected into a transparent main stream, creating the eye-shaped core. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marble_(toy)
- Small stone balls from about 2500 BCE found near Mohenjo-daro; early 1890s S. C. Dyke of Akron, Ohio made first mass-produced US toy marbles (clay); 1903 Martin Frederick Christensen made first machine-made glass marbles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marble_(toy)
- Marbles typically range from about 0.5 to 3.5 cm; a shooter or taw is larger than most others, used for shooting. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marble_(toy)
