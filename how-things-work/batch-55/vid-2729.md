## Fact check

- Hoops used since at least 500 BC (willow, rattan, grapevines, stiff grasses); 14th-century hooping craze in England recorded by Charles Panati. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hula_hoop
- Wham-O popularized the plastic version in 1958; earliest known ad June 16, 1958, $1.98; hoops of 110 cm (42 in) Marlex plastic; 25 million sold in less than four months, over 100 million within two years. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hula_hoop
