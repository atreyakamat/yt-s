## Fact check

- A party popper is a friction-actuated charge set off by pulling a string; the charge is in the neck of the bottle-shaped body; confetti or streamers are blown out. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_popper
- Some poppers use compressed air instead of an explosive charge. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_popper
- Explosive charges contain less than 0.25 grains (0.016 g). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_popper
- Poppers have caused serious eye trauma when aimed at people; classed as indoor fireworks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_popper
