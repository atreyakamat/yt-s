## Fact check

- Nixtamalisation: kernels are cooked and soaked in an alkaline solution, usually limewater (calcium hydroxide), then washed and hulled; Aztec and Maya used slaked lime and lye. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nixtamalization
- Untreated cornmeal cannot form a dough with water, nixtamalised cornmeal can (masa); calcium from the lime cross-links protein and polysaccharide chains. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nixtamalization
- Treatment makes corn's tryptophan available to be converted to niacin and helps prevent pellagra (later experiments showed niacin alone did not explain the effect, so the script says helps). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nixtamalization
- Earliest evidence: equipment on Guatemala's southern coast dating to 1200-1500 BCE. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nixtamalization
