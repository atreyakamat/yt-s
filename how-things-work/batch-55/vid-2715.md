## Fact check

- Power use in chips falls when clock speed and voltage are reduced (dynamic voltage and frequency scaling). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_frequency_scaling
- Phone battery saver modes lower performance, dim the display and restrict background activity. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smartphone
- Display, processor and radios are major power consumers (script says "uses power"). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smartphone
