## Fact check

- Main ingredients sugar, tapioca, corn syrup and either pectin or starch; smaller amounts of lecithin, an edible wax (carnauba or beeswax), salt and confectioner's glaze; soft candy shells and thick gel interiors. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jelly_bean
- Claimed first sale in 1861 by Boston confectioner William Schrafft; earliest definite reference in USDA records published 1887; linked to Easter in the US in the 1930s, likely because of the egg-like shape. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jelly_bean
- F. Scott Fitzgerald published 'The Jelly-Bean' in 1920; in 1964 American fans pelted the Beatles with jelly beans, copying British fans who threw Jelly Babies. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jelly_bean
