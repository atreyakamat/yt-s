## Fact check

- The Moon's rotation period equals its orbital period (synchronous rotation); tidal bulges raised by Earth slowed its spin; about 59% of the surface can be seen over time thanks to libration (about 6 degrees) and parallax (about 1 degree); Pluto and Charon are mutually tidally locked. The Moon's far side receives sunlight (standard fact). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidal_locking
