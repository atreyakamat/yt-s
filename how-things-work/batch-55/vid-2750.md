## Fact check

- Circadian rhythms repeat roughly every 24 hours; in mammals the primary clock is the suprachiasmatic nucleus in the hypothalamus; special retinal ganglion cells send light information to it; the pineal gland releases melatonin at night. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circadian_rhythm
- A 1999 Harvard study estimated the natural human rhythm at about 24 hours 11 minutes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circadian_rhythm
- Hall, Young and Rosbash won the 2017 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for molecular mechanisms controlling the circadian rhythm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circadian_rhythm
