## Fact check

- The first roadside pillar boxes were brought into use in Jersey (Saint Helier) on 23 November 1852; the first on mainland Britain was Carlisle, 1853. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pillar_box
- Traditional pillar boxes are cast iron columns with a hooded letter slot. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pillar_box
- Hood purpose (keeping rain out) is a common-sense design reading; gauge value is illustrative.
