## Fact check

- Nails are made of alpha-keratin, the same protein found in claws, hooves and horns; the nail plate contains no nerves or blood vessels. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nail_(anatomy)
- The nail matrix produces the cells that harden as they move outward; the lunula is the visible part of the matrix. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nail_(anatomy)
- Fingernails grow about 3.5 mm a month, toenails about 1.6 mm; fingernail regrowth about 3 to 6 months, toenail 12 to 18 months. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nail_(anatomy)
- Nails protect the fingertip, support fine movements and fingertip sensitivity, and act as tools for precision gripping and scraping. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nail_(anatomy)
