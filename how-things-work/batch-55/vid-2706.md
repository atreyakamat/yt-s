## Fact check

- A bulb heats a wax-and-liquid mixture in a glass vessel; heated wax becomes less dense and rises, then cools, sinks and repeats. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lava_lamp
- Common wax is much less dense than water; carbon tetrachloride, about 60% denser than water, was historically added to raise the wax's density; US-market lamps stopped using it in 1970. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lava_lamp
- Edward Craven Walker invented the lava lamp in 1963; Mathmos's Astro celebrated its 50th anniversary in 2013. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lava_lamp
