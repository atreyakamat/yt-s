## Fact check

- NITZ (network identity and time zone) lets mobile networks send local time and time zone to phones. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NITZ
- Network Time Protocol synchronises device clocks over the internet. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_Time_Protocol
- GPS satellites carry atomic clocks and phones can derive time from them. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Positioning_System
- Gauge value is illustrative.
