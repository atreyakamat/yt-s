## Fact check

- Weighbridges use load cells; most load cells contain strain gauges whose resistance changes with deformation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighbridge
- Strain gauge resistance changes as it stretches. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strain_gauge
- Vehicles are weighed full and empty to find the net load. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weighbridge
