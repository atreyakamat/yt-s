## Fact check

- External: microphones, speech processor, transmitter (radio frequency across skin); internal: receiver/stimulator and electrode array in the cochlea; it bypasses the damaged hair-cell pathway and stimulates the cochlear nerve. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cochlear_implant
- 1957 Andre Djourno and Charles Eyries invented the original single-channel cochlear implant; 1961 William House also invented one. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cochlear_implant
- 1985 Cochlear Limited's Nucleus device was the first multi-channel implant to receive FDA approval. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cochlear_implant
- About 600,000 recipients worldwide in 2016 (Ear Foundation estimate). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cochlear_implant
