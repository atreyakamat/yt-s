## Fact check

- Classic puff pastry (Julia Child's method): 729 butter layers and 730 dough layers, 1,459 in total; dough wraps solid fat and is rolled, folded and rested with a 90-degree turn between rounds; kept at about 16 C so the fat stays solid. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puff_pastry
- No yeast: water in the fat turns to steam and pushes the layers apart; melted fat leaves gaps and layers crisp where they touch it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puff_pastry
- 1311: earliest documented flaky pastry in France (charter of Robert, bishop of Amiens); 1651: first explicit folding technique (tourage) by Francois Pierre La Varenne. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puff_pastry
