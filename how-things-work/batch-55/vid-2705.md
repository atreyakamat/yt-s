## Fact check

- AirDrop uses Bluetooth Low Energy for discovery and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi for transfer; Android's Quick Share works similarly. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AirDrop
- Wi-Fi Direct lets devices connect without a router. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wi-Fi_Direct
- Bar values are illustrative of the speed gap, not measurements.
