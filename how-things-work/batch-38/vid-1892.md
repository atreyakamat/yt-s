## Fact check

- Crutches should bear weight through the hands and arms, not the axilla; pressure under the arm can injure nerves (crutch palsy). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crutch
- Axillary crutches have an underarm pad, a handgrip and adjustable height. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crutch
- Rubber ferrule at the foot improves grip. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crutch
