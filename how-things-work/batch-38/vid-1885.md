## Fact check

- Cocoa butter can crystallise in six polymorphic forms (I-VI); form V is the one prized for snap and gloss. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chocolate
- Tempering melts the chocolate, cools it with agitation to form seed crystals, and reheats slightly to melt unstable crystals. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chocolate
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocoa_butter
