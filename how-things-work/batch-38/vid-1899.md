## Fact check

- Cold air is denser than warm air and sinks; convection then circulates air in the container. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convection
- Foam insulation works by trapping air, a poor heat conductor. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermal_insulation
