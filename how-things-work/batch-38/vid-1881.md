## Fact check

- Most US carts have swivel front wheels and fixed rear wheels; Europe more commonly four swivel wheels, correlating with smaller premises. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shopping_cart
- Caster trail: the pivot axis is ahead of the contact point, so the wheel trails and self-aligns. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caster
