## Fact check

- Auto-Tune invented by Andy Hildebrand, released September 1997 by Antares Audio Technologies. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auto-Tune
- It shifts pitch to the nearest semitone; Hildebrand used autocorrelation for pitch detection. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auto-Tune
- "Believe" (1998) producers used extreme settings that removed the natural slide between pitches, the "Cher effect". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auto-Tune
