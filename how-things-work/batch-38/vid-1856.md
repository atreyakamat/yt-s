## Fact check

- Arresting gear can stop an aircraft of about 24,000 kg moving at about 240 km/h within roughly 100 m and about two seconds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arresting_gear
- Carriers use several arresting wires and the aircraft's tailhook engages one; hydraulic damping absorbs the energy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arresting_gear
- Pilots apply full power on touchdown so they can take off again if the hook misses the wire (a bolter). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolter_(aviation)
- Land runways at big airports are often around 3 km long (rounded illustrative figure). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway
