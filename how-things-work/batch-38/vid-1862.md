## Fact check

- Hospital beds have adjustable sections, typically powered by electric motors, plus height adjustment and side rails. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospital_bed
- Raising the knee section with the backrest reduces sliding (design rationale of articulated frames). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospital_bed
- Caster wheels with brakes are standard. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospital_bed
