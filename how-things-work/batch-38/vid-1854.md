## Fact check

- World Athletics rule: reaction time under 0.100 s counts as a false start, measured by pressure sensors in the blocks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False_start
- Speakers behind each block play the starter's gun sound (used in major championships). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starting_blocks
- Since 2010 a single false start disqualifies the athlete. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False_start
