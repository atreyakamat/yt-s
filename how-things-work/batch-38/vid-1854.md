## Fact check

- The paradox: an arrow flies in the direction it points at full draw even though it seems it must pass the side position; the bending of the arrow on release explains it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archer%27s_paradox
- The arrow flexes out of the bow's way and oscillates as it travels; dynamic spine is the needed stiffness; too stiff forces it to one side, too weak sends it to the other; stiffer arrows for more powerful bows. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archer%27s_paradox
- Dynamic spine depends mainly on shaft length, head weight and static spine. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archer%27s_paradox
