## Fact check

- Honey is about 80% sugars and 17-18% water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honey
- Honey granulates (crystallises) as glucose comes out of solution; the rate depends on its glucose-to-water ratio and the type of nectar. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honey
