## Fact check

- Alexander Wood (all-glass syringe 1851) and Charles Pravaz developed the hypodermic needle and syringe independently in the early 1850s; Francis Rynd used a hollow needle in 1844; first disposable plastic syringe 1956. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypodermic_needle
- Earlier syringes existed, used for wounds and fluids. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syringe
- Disposable plastic syringes arrived in the mid-20th century (1950s). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syringe
