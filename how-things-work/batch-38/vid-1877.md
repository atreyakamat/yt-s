## Fact check

- Rolling shutter scans the scene in sequence, so different parts are recorded at different instants; fast motion skews. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_shutter
- Propellers can look bent, thinner on one side, or disconnected at the centre. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_shutter
- Global shutter captures the whole frame at once; most consumer CMOS cameras use rolling shutters. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_shutter
