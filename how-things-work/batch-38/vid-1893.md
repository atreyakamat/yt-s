## Fact check

- Evaporation losses are higher in heat and wind; cooler, calmer periods let more irrigation water reach roots (qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrigation
- Prolonged leaf wetness favours fungal diseases. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant_pathology
- No numbers are claimed.
