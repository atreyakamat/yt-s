## Fact check

- The patellar reflex is a monosynaptic stretch reflex: sensory neuron to spinal cord to motor neuron, without the brain. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patellar_reflex
- Reflex hammer is used to test deep tendon reflexes, checking nerve pathways. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflex_hammer
- Latency from tendon stretch to start of quadriceps contraction is about 18 ms (script shows ~20 ms, "until the muscle fires"; vo says a few hundredths of a second). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patellar_reflex
