## Fact check

- American wire gauge: a larger gauge number means a smaller diameter; the historical origin is the number of drawing operations. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_wire_gauge
- AWG diameters: gauge 10 about 2.59 mm, gauge 20 about 0.81 mm, gauge 30 about 0.255 mm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_wire_gauge
- Each gauge step changes diameter by a factor of about 1.12 (about 12 percent); every 6 gauges roughly halves the diameter. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_wire_gauge
- Thicker wire carries more current. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_wire_gauge
