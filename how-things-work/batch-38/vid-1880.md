## Fact check

- Kegs are pressurised with carbon dioxide or a mix of gases via a coupler, pushing beer through a dip tube (spear) to the tap. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keg
- Gas pressure also keeps carbonation and excludes air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keg
