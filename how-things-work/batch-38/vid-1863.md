## Fact check

- Rainfall of 1 mm equals 1 litre per square metre, so 10 m² of roof gives 10 litres. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_gauge
- Water butts collect roof runoff via a downpipe diverter; overflow sends excess to drain; lids reduce light and algae. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_barrel
