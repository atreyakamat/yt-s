## Fact check

- Edwin Beard Budding of Stroud, Gloucestershire, invented the lawn mower in 1830, with a British patent granted 31 August 1830. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawn_mower
- It was designed as an alternative to the scythe; grazing animals were also used to keep grass short. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawn_mower
- Cylinder (reel) cutting design is described in the same article. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawn_mower
