## Fact check

- IMAX 15/70: 65 mm stock running horizontally, 15 perforations per frame, 70 mm print film; frame 69.6 x 48.5 mm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX
- Three times the theoretical horizontal resolution of a 35 mm frame. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX
- A 2.5-hour feature platter weighs about 250 kg. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX
- Standard IMAX 15/70 screen about 18 x 24 m. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMAX
