## Fact check

- A compound microscope has an objective lens and an eyepiece; total magnification is the product of the two. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_microscope
- A 10x eyepiece with a 40x objective gives 400x total (arithmetic). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_microscope
- Most microscopes have a nosepiece turret holding several objectives. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_microscope
