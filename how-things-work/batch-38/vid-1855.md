## Fact check

- Extruded wirecut bricks are often made with perforations (cores). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brick
- Perforations save clay and reduce weight; the exact saving varies, so the bar chart is illustrative only (no number claimed). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brick
- Firing in a kiln hardens the clay; holes help the bar dry and fire more evenly. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brick
