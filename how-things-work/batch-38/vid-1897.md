## Fact check

- Three points define a plane (geometry), so three feet always touch the ground; a fourth leg may not (reasoning, no number).
- Tripods are needed for slow exposures because camera movement blurs the image. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripod_(photography)
- Most photographic tripods are braced around a centre post. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripod_(photography)
