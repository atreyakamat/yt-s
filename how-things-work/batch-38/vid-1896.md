## Fact check

- Many round parachutes have a vent (hole) at the apex that lets air escape in a controlled way, improving stability and reducing oscillation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parachute
- A parachute slows descent by creating drag. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parachute
