## Fact check

- Runway edge lights are white, threshold lights are green and runway-end lights are red (FAA/ICAO practice). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport_lighting
- Taxiway edge lights are blue and taxiway centreline lights are green. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport_lighting
