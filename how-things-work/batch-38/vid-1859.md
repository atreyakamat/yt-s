## Fact check

- Cloth nappies of cotton or linen were fastened with fastenings and eventually the safety pin; wool and rubber pants covered them. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diaper
- Marion Donovan made a waterproof cover in 1946 based on a shower curtain; it first sold in 1949. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diaper
- Pampers was launched in 1961 (Procter & Gamble project, 1959). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diaper
