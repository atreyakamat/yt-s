## Fact check

- Pop-up sprinklers use water pressure to raise the riser, and an internal spring retracts it when pressure drops. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrigation_sprinkler
- 'In a flash' is loose wording, no figure is claimed.
