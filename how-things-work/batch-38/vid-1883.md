## Fact check

- Hot composting's thermophilic phase runs about 50-60 °C; temperatures of about 50-70 °C kill most pathogens and seeds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compost
- Turning the pile or aerating maintains oxygen and moisture. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compost
- Boiling water is 100 °C; 15 °C is just an illustrative cool day.
