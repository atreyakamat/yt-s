## Fact check

- Gym chalk is magnesium carbonate, used for drying hands in gymnastics, climbing and weightlifting. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnesium_carbonate
- Liquid chalk is chalk suspended in alcohol that evaporates. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climbing_chalk
- Many gyms restrict loose chalk because of dust (script: many). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climbing_chalk
