## Fact check

- A lead line is a weighted line used to measure water depth, marked at intervals. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lead_(sounding_weight)
- A fathom is 6 feet (1.8288 m). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fathom
- The base of the lead was hollowed and filled with tallow ('arming') to pick up a sample of the seabed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lead_(sounding_weight)
