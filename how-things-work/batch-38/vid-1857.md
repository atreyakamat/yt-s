## Fact check

- Most common tilt sensor is a grounded plumb bob hanging inside an electrified metal ring; contact completes a circuit. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinball
- On tilt the lights go out, flippers stop and the ball drains; a separate slam tilt protects the coin mechanism. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinball
