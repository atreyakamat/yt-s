## Fact check

- Wire drawing pulls metal through a die to reduce its diameter; multiple dies are used in sequence. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wire_drawing
- Cold drawing work-hardens the metal and annealing is used between passes; diamond dies are used for fine wire. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wire_drawing
