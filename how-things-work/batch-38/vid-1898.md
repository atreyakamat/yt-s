## Fact check

- Ancient writers (Varro, later Pappus) suspected it; the honeycomb conjecture (hexagonal tiling divides a plane into equal areas with least perimeter) was proved by Thomas Hales in 1999. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honeycomb_conjecture
- Only triangles, squares and hexagons tile the plane as regular polygons. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessellation
