## Fact check

- Modern lever nutcrackers resemble pliers with the pivot at the end beyond the nut, rather than in the middle; hinged lever nutcrackers may date back to Ancient Greece and were documented in England and France by the 14th century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutcracker
- The reel's line that the nut sits close to the pivot where the squeeze is strongest is standard lever physics (not stated on the page).
- Nuts were historically opened with a hammer and anvil, often stone; screw nutcrackers introduced in the 17th century and still used for macadamia nuts; spring-jointed patented by Henry Quackenbush in 1913; ratchet type patented 1947 (Cuthbert Leslie Rimes), raising pressure gradually to avoid damaging the kernel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nutcracker
