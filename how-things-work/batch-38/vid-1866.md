## Fact check

- A bell buoy has a bell struck by clappers moved by wave action, giving a sound signal without power. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_buoy
- Buoys are moored to the seabed by a chain and a sinker and mark channels and hazards. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buoy
- Audible buoys help warn mariners in fog. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buoy
