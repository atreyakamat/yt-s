## Fact check

- Beer head is made of bubbles of carbon dioxide stabilised by proteins; pouring technique affects how much foam forms. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beer_head
- Pouring gently down the side of a tilted glass reduces turbulence and foam; common serving advice. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beer_head
