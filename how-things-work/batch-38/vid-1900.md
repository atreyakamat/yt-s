## Fact check

- Nitro cold brew is cold brew coffee infused with nitrogen and served from a tap. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitro_cold_brew
- Nitrogen is much less soluble in liquids than carbon dioxide and makes smaller bubbles; air is about 78% nitrogen (SOFTENED to 'most of the air'). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitrogen
- Stout/nitro taps use a restrictor plate with small holes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stout
