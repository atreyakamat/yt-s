- Incubation temperature about 37.5 °C (99.5 °F) in forced-air incubators, 21 days (Wikipedia gives 21 days only). Source: https://scienceinsights.org/what-temperature-should-you-incubate-chicken-eggs/ and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incubator_(egg)
