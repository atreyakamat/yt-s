## Fact check

- Food processors have a motor base with a drive shaft into a removable bowl, with an S-shaped chopping blade or slicing/grating discs.
- Safety interlocks: the bowl and lid must be locked; a tab or rod on the lid/bowl actuates a switch in the base so the motor cannot run otherwise.
- Pulse mode runs the motor only while pressed, for coarse chopping.
