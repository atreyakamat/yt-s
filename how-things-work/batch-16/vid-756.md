## Fact check

- Many copy paper reams mark a preferred print side with an arrow on the wrapper.
- Paper formed on a wire mesh has a wire side and a felt/top side, and develops curl in one direction.
- Laser printer fusing adds heat curl; loading paper as recommended helps sheets come out flatter and feed reliably.
- Tray orientation varies by printer model.
