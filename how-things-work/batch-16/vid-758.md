## Fact check

- A sternum strap connects the shoulder straps across the chest.
- It keeps shoulder straps from spreading outward and stabilises the load: standard pack-fitting guidance.
- Fitting advice: adjust height to roughly armpit level and keep it snug, not restrictive.
- Many hiking packs integrate an emergency whistle into the sternum strap buckle.
