## Fact check

- Optical image stabilisation (OIS) suspends the lens (or sensor) on springs/flexures and moves it with voice-coil actuators (magnets + coils).
- A gyroscope senses angular shake at high rates (hundreds to thousands of samples per second); a controller drives the actuators to counter-move.
- Natural hand tremor causes blur in longer exposures; OIS allows slower shutter speeds in low light.
