## Fact check

- Hacksaw blades are fitted with teeth pointing away from the handle (forward), so cutting happens on the push stroke (standard instruction).
- Pressure should be relieved on the return stroke to avoid dulling the teeth.
- The blade must be tensioned with the wing nut; a slack blade wanders and can break.
- Rule of thumb: at least three teeth in contact with the work; otherwise teeth can straddle thin material, catch and strip off.
