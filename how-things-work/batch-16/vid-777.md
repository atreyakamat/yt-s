## Fact check

- Holes in crackers are called docking holes, made with a docking tool/roller before baking.
- During baking, water vaporises; without vents, steam gets trapped between layers and forms large blisters.
- Docking lets steam escape so the cracker bakes flat and evenly; pricking pastry with a fork works the same way.
