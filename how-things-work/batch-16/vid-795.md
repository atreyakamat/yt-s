## Fact check

- Dryer sheets are non-woven polyester sheets coated with a softening agent (cationic surfactants such as quaternary ammonium compounds, often with fatty acids) and fragrance; dryer heat softens it so it transfers to fabric.
- Cationic (positively charged) softeners adsorb onto fibres, whose surfaces tend to be negatively charged, lubricating them.
- The coating reduces friction and makes fibre surfaces less insulating, reducing static build-up.
- Softener build-up reduces towel absorbency, which is why towel makers often advise against it.
