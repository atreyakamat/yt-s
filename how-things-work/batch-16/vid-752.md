## Fact check

- Cc stands for carbon copy, from carbon paper; Bcc is blind carbon copy.
- Bcc recipients are not shown to other recipients.
- Recipients in To/Cc are visible to everyone; mass emails in To/Cc expose addresses and invite reply-all storms.
- A reply-all from a Bcc recipient goes only to the visible sender and To/Cc recipients, not to other Bcc recipients.
