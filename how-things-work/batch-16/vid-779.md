## Fact check

- Settled/wet snow can weigh a few hundred kg per cubic metre, so a typical roof can carry several tonnes under deep snow.
- Steeper pitches shed snow more readily; building codes reduce design snow load for steep roofs.
- Flat roofs are traditional in many hot, dry regions, where they're used as terraces and there is little rain or snow to shed.
- Roof pitch broadly reflects climate: a generalisation, hence 'often'.
