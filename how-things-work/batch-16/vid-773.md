## Fact check

- Natural rubber without treatment becomes sticky when hot and brittle when cold.
- Uncrosslinked polymer chains can slide past each other when warm and stiffen in cold.
- Charles Goodyear discovered vulcanisation (heating rubber with sulfur) in 1839; rubber boots were being made by the 1850s.
- Sulfur cross-links the polymer chains into a network, giving elasticity and temperature stability.
- 'Vulcanisation' is named after Vulcan, Roman god of fire.
