## Fact check

- Rear fog lamps are required on cars in Europe and have a luminous intensity similar to brake lamps.
- UK Highway Code (rules 226 and 236): use fog lights when visibility is seriously reduced, generally when you cannot see for more than 100 metres; do not use them otherwise, as they can dazzle and obscure brake lights.
- The rear fog lamp tell-tale on the dashboard is amber/yellow.
