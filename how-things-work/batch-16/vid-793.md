## Fact check

- Humidistat fans → many extractor fans include a humidity sensor that switches the fan on above an adjustable relative humidity setting.
- Capacitive humidity sensors → a hygroscopic polymer dielectric between electrodes absorbs water vapour, changing capacitance.
- Switches off as humidity falls → usually with a run-on timer.
