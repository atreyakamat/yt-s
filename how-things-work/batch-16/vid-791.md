## Fact check

- Some Dutch ovens and casserole pots have 'self-basting' lids with spikes, dimples or ridges on the underside.
- Steam condenses on the cooler lid; the spikes give droplets low points to collect and drip from, distributing moisture over the food rather than running down the walls.
- Heavy, tight-fitting lids reduce steam loss.
