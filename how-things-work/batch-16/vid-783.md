## Fact check

- Shark-fin modules house multiple antennas (AM/FM or digital radio, GNSS/GPS, cellular/LTE, sometimes satellite radio) under a plastic radome.
- Roof mounting gives an elevated, unobstructed view for satellite and radio signals.
- The low, swept shape reduces wind noise and drag compared with whip antennas and avoids damage in car washes.
