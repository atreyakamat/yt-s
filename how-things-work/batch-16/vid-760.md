## Fact check

- Push-and-turn closures have an outer cap that rotates freely until pressed down, engaging lugs that turn the inner cap.
- Child-resistant packaging is tested with panels of young children (who must mostly fail to open it) and adults (who must mostly succeed). Bars are illustrative of that test logic, not exact results.
- Canadian physician Peter Hreljac is credited with inventing a child-resistant cap in 1967; the US Poison Prevention Packaging Act was passed in 1970.
- They are officially 'child-resistant', not child-proof.
