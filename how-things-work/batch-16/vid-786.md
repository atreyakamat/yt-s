## Fact check

- Embossed card numbers and names were designed for manual imprinters ('knuckle-busters') that transferred them to carbon-copy sales slips.
- With chip, magnetic stripe and contactless readers, embossing is no longer needed and many issuers now print numbers flat, sometimes on the back.
