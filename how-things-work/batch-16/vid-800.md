## Fact check

- Road tunnel lighting standards define a threshold (entrance) zone lit much more brightly in daytime, followed by transition zones stepping down to the interior level, to allow eye adaptation and avoid the 'black hole' effect.
- Entrance lighting is controlled by outdoor luminance sensors and reduced on dull days and at night.
- Bar values are illustrative of relative levels, not measured figures.
