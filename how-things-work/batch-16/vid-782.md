## Fact check

- The common Wi-Fi signal icon shows a point with concentric arcs, representing radio waves radiating from a source.
- Signal strength falls with distance and obstacles; lit arcs represent received signal strength. Bar percentages are illustrative.
- The original IEEE 802.11 standard was released in 1997.
- 'Wi-Fi' was coined in 1999 by the branding firm Interbrand for the Wi-Fi Alliance; it is not an abbreviation of 'wireless fidelity' and was a play on 'hi-fi'.
- Wi-Fi does not officially stand for anything; name coined by Interbrand in 1999: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wi-Fi
