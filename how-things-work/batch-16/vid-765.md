## Fact check

- Incandescent filaments are tungsten, typically coiled-coil, operating at roughly 2,500-3,000 °C; tungsten melts at about 3,422 °C. A candle flame is roughly 1,000 °C.
- Incandescent bulbs convert only about 2-5% of input power to visible light; the rest is mostly infrared/heat.
- Bulbs are filled with inert gas (argon/nitrogen) to slow filament evaporation; oxygen would burn the filament out almost instantly.
- Many countries phased out general incandescent bulbs for efficiency reasons.
