## Fact check

- Bulbous bows are fitted below the waterline on many large ships.
- A ship spends much of its resistance on wave-making at higher speeds.
- The bulb creates a wave system that interferes destructively with the bow wave, reducing wave-making resistance.
- Fuel savings are often quoted around 5-15% at design speed; 'several percent' is conservative. Some ships have had bows refitted after slowing their service speed.
