## Fact check

- Engine bearings run on hydrodynamic oil films of a few micrometres, much thinner than a human hair (about 50-100 µm).
- A pump draws oil from the sump and feeds it under pressure through galleries to bearings, cam and valve gear; oil drains back to the sump.
- Oil also cools, carries away combustion by-products and suspends dirt (detergents), darkening it; oil degrades and is changed periodically along with the filter.
