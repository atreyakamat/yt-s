## Fact check

- Industry estimates put spam at roughly 45-50% of global email traffic.
- Filters use sender authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), link and content analysis, and sender reputation.
- Many filters combine signals into a score with a threshold; modern ones use machine learning trained on large volumes of mail.
- User spam reports feed back into filtering.
- Point values in the bars are illustrative.
