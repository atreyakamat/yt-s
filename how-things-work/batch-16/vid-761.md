## Fact check

- Many modern dishwashers use manual-clean filters (a cylindrical filter and flat mesh in the tub floor) instead of self-cleaning grinders, partly because they run quieter.
- Manufacturers advise removing and rinsing the filter regularly; a clogged filter leads to poor washing, grit on dishes and odours.
- Recommended intervals vary by maker (from weekly to monthly depending on use), hence 'every few weeks' and 'check your manual'.
