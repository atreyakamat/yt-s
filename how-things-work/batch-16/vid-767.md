## Fact check

- HTTP is stateless; cookies are small pieces of data a server asks the browser to store and send back with later requests.
- Cookies typically hold session IDs and have an expiry date.
- They power logins and shopping baskets.
- Third-party cookies set by ad/tracking domains embedded on many sites enable cross-site tracking; browsers increasingly restrict them.
- Consent banners stem from privacy laws such as the EU's ePrivacy rules and GDPR.
