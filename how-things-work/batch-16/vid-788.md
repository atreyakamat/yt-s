## Fact check

- A shank button has a loop or stem on the back instead of holes through the face.
- The shank raises the button so the thickness of the buttonhole layer can sit beneath it without puckering: standard tailoring.
- When sewing flat (sew-through) buttons onto thick fabric, tailors wrap the thread to create a 'thread shank'.
