## Fact check

- Automatic air brakes use a brake pipe along the train; each vehicle has a control (triple) valve, an auxiliary reservoir and a brake cylinder.
- Reducing brake pipe pressure applies the brakes; recharging it releases them.
- If the train parts, the brake pipe vents and both portions brake automatically (fail-safe).
- George Westinghouse patented the straight air brake in 1869 and the automatic (fail-safe) version in 1872. Many trains today use electro-pneumatic or vacuum variants of the principle.
- Westinghouse invented the railway air brake in 1869; automatic air brake 1872: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westinghouse_Air_Brake_Company
