## Fact check

- Open-end spanner heads are typically offset about 15° from the handle axis.
- A hexagonal nut repeats every 60°; flipping a 15°-offset spanner allows re-engagement after about 30° of swing.
- Open jaws contact only two flats and are more prone to slipping or spreading on tight nuts; the ring end of a combination spanner encloses the nut and grips its corners, so it is preferred for breaking nuts loose.
- Open-end wrench heads angled about 15 degrees, flip over in tight spaces; grip two flats: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrench
