## Fact check

- Squeegee blades are soft rubber with a sharp, square-cut edge; the edge contacts the glass in a continuous line, and flexibility lets it follow surface irregularities.
- Nicks or rounded/worn edges leave streaks, so window cleaners replace or flip blades.
- Professional technique includes wiping the blade between strokes so collected water isn't redeposited.
