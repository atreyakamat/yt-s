## Fact check

- Stone has high compressive strength but low tensile strength, so beams crack on their stretched underside while arches work in compression.
- An arch carries load as compression through its voussoirs (wedge stones) to the abutments; the keystone completes it, and arches are built on temporary timber centring that is removed afterwards.
- The Pons Fabricius in Rome, built in 62 BC, is still in use as a footbridge, so Roman bridges have stood for over 2,000 years.
