## Fact check

- Cross-chaining means combining the largest front chainring with the largest rear cog, or smallest with smallest.
- This creates a large chain angle (chainline), causing rub on the front derailleur, noise, extra friction and faster drivetrain wear.
- Mechanics recommend avoiding the extreme combinations and keeping a straighter chainline by using the front shifter.
