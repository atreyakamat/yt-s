## Fact check

- William Painter of Baltimore patented the crown cork (crown cap) in 1892, with a cork liner sealing against the bottle lip; he patented a bottle opener in 1894.
- Earlier closures included corks wired in place and the Hutchinson wire-and-rubber stopper, which were prone to leaks and hygiene issues.
- The original crown had 24 crimps; 21 became the standard during the 20th century.
- King C. Gillette worked as a salesman for Painter's Crown Cork & Seal company before developing the safety razor.
