## Fact check

- Immersion (stick) blenders have a handle motor driving a long shaft to a blade in a bell-shaped guard; blade speeds are typically in the thousands to over ten thousand rpm.
- The spinning blade acts like a pump, expelling contents through the guard's side openings and drawing more in from below; the resulting low pressure can make the guard stick to the pan bottom.
- Manufacturers advise keeping the blade fully submerged to avoid splashing.
