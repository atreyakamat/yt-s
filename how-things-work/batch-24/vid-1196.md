## Fact check

- Many deep fryers (especially commercial ones, and many domestic models) place the element or burner tubes above the tank base, creating a 'cold zone' below.
- Food particles sink into the cooler zone rather than carbonising, which slows oil degradation and off-flavours.
- Typical frying temperatures are about 170-190 °C, thermostatically controlled.
- Surface moisture rapidly turns to steam, drying the surface so a crust forms.
