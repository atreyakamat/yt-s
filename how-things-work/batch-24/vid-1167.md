## Fact check

- Sherbet powder typically contains sugar, a food acid (citric, tartaric or malic) and sodium bicarbonate (baking soda).
- When dissolved in saliva, the acid and bicarbonate react to produce carbon dioxide, creating fizz; excess acid gives sourness.
- Vinegar-and-baking-soda 'volcanoes' are the same type of acid-carbonate reaction.
