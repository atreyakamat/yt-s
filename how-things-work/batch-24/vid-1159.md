## Fact check

- Classic funiculars have two counterbalanced cars attached to opposite ends of one haul cable around a bullwheel at the top station.
- The cars' weights largely balance, so the motor supplies the difference plus friction.
- A passing loop midway lets the cars pass on a mostly single track.
- The Abt switch uses cars with double-flanged wheels on one side and flat, wide wheels on the other, so each car always takes its own side without moving points.
