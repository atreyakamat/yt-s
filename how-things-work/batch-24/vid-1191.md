## Fact check

- Pressure = force / area; by Newton's third law the force at the head and point is (approximately) equal.
- A tack head of ~1 cm² versus a point of well under 0.001 cm² gives a pressure ratio in the thousands.
- Bar values are illustrative (the real ratio is often larger). Gauge is illustrative.
