## Fact check

- Light-up shoes contain a small battery, a controller chip, LEDs and a motion or pressure switch in the sole.
- A common switch is a vibration (spring) switch: a coil spring inside a metal tube that touches it when jolted.
- The chip runs a flash pattern for a short time after each trigger.
- LEDs turn on in nanoseconds to microseconds.
