## Fact check

- Automatic number plate recognition (ANPR) cameras typically use infrared illumination so they work day and night.
- Number plates are retroreflective, so they appear bright in the IR image.
- Software locates the plate, segments characters and applies optical character recognition in a fraction of a second.
- Reads are matched against databases for car parks, road charging/tolls and police alerts.
