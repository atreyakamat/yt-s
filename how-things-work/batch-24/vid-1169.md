## Fact check

- Light switches use an over-centre (toggle) spring mechanism, so contact movement is fast and independent of how fast the rocker is pushed.
- Slowly separating contacts sustain an arc that erodes them; quick make/break minimises arcing.
- Contacts are commonly silver alloy; 'a few thousandths of a second' is the order of snap-action contact travel time.
