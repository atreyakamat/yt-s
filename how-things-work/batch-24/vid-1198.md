## Fact check

- Obsidian mirrors about 8,000 years old → polished obsidian mirrors from Çatalhöyük, Anatolia, dated to about 6000 BC.
- Copper and bronze mirrors → used in ancient Egypt, Mesopotamia and China; they tarnish and needed polishing.
- Venetian tin-mercury amalgam mirrors → Venice (Murano) led mirror-making from the 1500s; mercury is toxic.
- 1835 → Justus von Liebig developed the process of depositing metallic silver on glass.
- Modern mirror build → glass, reflective metal layer (silver or aluminium) and protective backing paint.
- Obsidian mirrors from Catalhoyuk about 6000 BCE (about 8,000 years ago); copper/bronze mirrors; Venice tin-mercury mirrors by the 16th century; Liebig silvering 1835: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirror
