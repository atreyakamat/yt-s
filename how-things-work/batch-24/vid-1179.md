## Fact check

- Bouncing putty is a silicone polymer cross-linked with boron compounds, forming temporary, reversible links between chains.
- It is viscoelastic: under slow stress it flows; under sudden stress it behaves elastically (bounces, snaps).
- A sharp hammer blow can make it shatter, while left at rest it slowly flows into a puddle.
- The behavior depends on the timescale of the force compared with how fast the links break and re-form.
