## Fact check

- Modern tent poles are segmented aluminum or fiberglass tubes threaded on an internal elastic shock cord.
- The tensioned cord keeps sections together and pulls them into alignment.
- Each joint uses a ferrule, a narrower sleeve that slides into the next section.
- Manufacturers advise seating joints by hand rather than letting them snap, which can damage the ends.
- Folding from the middle is common advice to reduce strain on the shock cord.
