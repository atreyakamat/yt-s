## Fact check

- Ratchet tensioners wind webbing onto a slotted mandrel (spindle) fitted with ratchet wheels; spring-loaded pawls engage the teeth to prevent unwinding.
- The handle provides leverage, allowing high tension by hand.
- Manufacturers recommend only about 1.5 to 3 wraps of webbing on the spindle; excess can jam the mechanism.
- Releasing is done by pulling the release catch and opening the handle fully flat, which disengages the pawls.
