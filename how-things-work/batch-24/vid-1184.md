## Fact check

- Lever and knob sets are connected by a square spindle (commonly 8 mm in the UK) through the door and the latch's follower (hub).
- Rotating the follower retracts the latch bolt against the latch spring.
- Latch springs and (on lever handles) return springs restore the bolt and handle position.
