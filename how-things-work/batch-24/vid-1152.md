## Fact check

- Tamper-evident closures have a band connected to the cap by small frangible bridges.
- The band locks under a bead/ledge on the bottle neck (the 'finish'); unscrewing lifts the cap, the band catches, and the bridges break, leaving the band on the bottle.
- A broken band shows the bottle has been opened.
- Caps for carbonated drinks have vent slots across the threads so pressure is released before the cap disengages.
