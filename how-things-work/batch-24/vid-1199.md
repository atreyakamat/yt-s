## Fact check

- Single glazing has a very cold inner surface in winter; water vapour from occupants can freeze on it, forming 'frost ferns' inside windows.
- Heavy curtains, shutters and draught excluders were standard defences.
- Secondary windows (an inner sash or storm window) were used historically.
- Sealed units: first US patent 1865, developed commercially in the 1930s (several patents); the "1970s spread" date was not sourced and was softened to "much later". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insulating_glass
