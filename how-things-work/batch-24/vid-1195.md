## Fact check

- Polycarbonate is used for safety lenses, riot shields and machine guards because of its high impact resistance.
- Its long-chain polymer structure deforms and absorbs energy rather than fracturing like brittle glass.
- Standards such as ANSI Z87.1 and EN 166 include high-velocity steel-ball impact tests; lenses must not fracture or dislodge.
- Polycarbonate inherently blocks almost all UV but is relatively soft, so lenses carry hard coatings.
