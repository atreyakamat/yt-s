## Fact check

- Cotton yarns and fabrics are held under tension during spinning, weaving/knitting and finishing, so they are dimensionally 'stretched' when sold.
- Wetting swells cotton fibres and releases these strains (relaxation shrinkage); heat and mechanical action in tumble drying add further (consolidation) shrinkage.
- Pre-shrunk fabrics have been relaxed/compacted at the mill (e.g. compressive shrinkage), so they shrink much less.
