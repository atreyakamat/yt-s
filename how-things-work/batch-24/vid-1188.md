## Fact check

- Full hybrids combine an internal combustion engine, electric motor(s) and a small traction battery, managed by a control computer.
- They can drive on electric power at low speed, use the engine at higher loads, and charge the battery from the engine and regenerative braking.
- Conventional (self-charging) hybrids are not plugged in; plug-in hybrids have larger batteries charged from the grid.
- Hybrid fuel savings are greatest in stop-start urban driving.
