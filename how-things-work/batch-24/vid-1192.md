## Fact check

- Heat guns use a motor-driven fan blowing air over a resistance heating element; typical output is about 100 to 550 °C, with some models up to about 760 °C (voice and text use a safe 500). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_gun
- Hairdryer temperature: no source found, so the voice only says "warm" (softened); the bar value is illustrative.
- For a given heater power, lower airflow gives a higher outlet temperature (same energy into less air).
- Heat guns are commonly used for paint stripping; nozzles stay hot after use, and manufacturers advise resting them on the stand to cool.
