## Fact check

- Cricket balls have a cork core built up with layers of cork and tightly wound string, inside a leather cover.
- The cover is stitched with a raised seam, typically six rows of stitching.
- A men's cricket ball weighs 155.9-163 g (about 160 g); a baseball is about 142-149 g.
- Bowlers polish one side; with the seam angled, airflow differs on the two sides (boundary layer effects), producing swing.
- Cricket ball 156-163 g (men's): OK; baseball ~145 g is the standard regulation weight (142-149 g), not fetched from a page this pass. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cricket_ball
