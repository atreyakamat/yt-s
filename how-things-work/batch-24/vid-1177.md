## Fact check

- Random orbital sanders mount the pad on an eccentric bearing; the motor drives the eccentric in a small orbit (a few mm diameter) while the pad is free to rotate.
- Typical speeds are about 7,000-12,000 orbits per minute; 12,000 per minute = 200 per second.
- The free rotation randomises the abrasive path, avoiding the repeating swirl patterns of fixed-orbit sanders.
- Excess pressure slows or stops the pad rotation, reducing the random action and leaving swirl marks (standard manufacturer advice).
- 12,000 orbits a minute = 200 a second: arithmetic, OK; 12,000 is a typical figure (not sourced from a page).
