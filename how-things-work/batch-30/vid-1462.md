## Fact check

- Heat shrink tubing (usually cross-linked polyolefin) is cross-linked, heated above its crystalline melting point, expanded, and cooled to lock it in the expanded state.
- Reheating lets the cross-linked network return to its original dimensions (shape memory).
- Common shrink ratios are 2:1 and 3:1 (also 4:1); adhesive-lined (dual-wall) versions seal against moisture.
- Standard advice: heat evenly, working from the centre outward, to avoid trapping air.
