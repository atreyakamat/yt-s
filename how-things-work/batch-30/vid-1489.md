## Fact check

- Most airliners have an auxiliary power unit, a small gas turbine usually in the tail cone, with its exhaust at the rear.
- The APU provides electrical power and bleed air for air conditioning on the ground.
- APU bleed air (or electric power on some types) is used to start the main engines.
- APUs are typically shut down in cruise but can supply backup electrical (and sometimes pneumatic) power in flight.
