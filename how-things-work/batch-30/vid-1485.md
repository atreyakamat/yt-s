## Fact check

- Paper strength comes from hydrogen bonds between cellulose fibres, which water disrupts; toilet tissue is designed to disperse in water.
- Paper towels add wet-strength resins (e.g. polyamide-epichlorohydrin) that form water-resistant cross-links.
- Multi-ply towels are embossed to bond the plies and create bulk and voids that increase absorbency by capillary action.
- Because they don't break down quickly, water companies advise against flushing paper towels.
