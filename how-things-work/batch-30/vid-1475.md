## Fact check

- UK motorway marker posts are spaced every 100 m and show a telephone symbol with an arrow pointing to the nearest emergency roadside telephone.
- Driver location signs (small blue signs) appear about every 500 m and show the road number, a carriageway letter and a location number, so callers can give their exact position.
- Official advice for breakdowns is to get out on the side away from traffic and wait behind the safety barrier where possible.
- Marker posts about every 100 m with an arrow to the nearest emergency phone; driver location signs about every 500 m (England): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Driver_location_sign
