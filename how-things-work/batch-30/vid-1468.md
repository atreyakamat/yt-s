## Fact check

- Lubricating strip → the strip on cartridge razors, typically containing water-soluble polyethylene oxide, releases a slippery layer when wet.
- Colour fades with use → many strips include an indicator colour that fades as the strip dissolves, designed as a replacement cue.
- Edges wear → blade edges dull with use; the fading strip roughly tracks how many shaves the cartridge has done.
