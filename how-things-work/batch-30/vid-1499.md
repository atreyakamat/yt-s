## Fact check

- Eaves are the part of the roof overhanging the walls.
- Overhangs shed rainwater clear of walls and into gutters, reducing damp and protecting foundations.
- Correctly sized overhangs block high-angle summer sun while admitting low-angle winter sun (passive solar design).
- Soffit vents in the underside of the eaves provide roof-space ventilation.
