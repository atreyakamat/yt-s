## Fact check

- Radiant ceramic hobs have ribbon or coil heating elements in insulated dishes under a glass-ceramic top.
- Glass-ceramic has near-zero thermal expansion, so it resists thermal shock; it transmits much of the infrared radiation and has low lateral conductivity, so heat stays in the cooking zone.
- A temperature limiter cycles the element to protect the glass.
- A residual-heat indicator ('H') shows when a zone is still hot.
