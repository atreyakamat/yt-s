## Fact check

- Sump pumps sit in a sump pit in basement floors, collecting groundwater from perimeter drains.
- A float switch starts the pump when water reaches a set level and stops it when the level falls.
- A check valve on the discharge pipe prevents pumped water flowing back into the pit.
