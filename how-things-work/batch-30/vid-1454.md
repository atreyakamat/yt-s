## Fact check

- Pet microchips are passive RFID transponders about the size of a rice grain, implanted under the skin, storing a unique ID.
- They have no battery; the reader's electromagnetic field powers them to transmit their number.
- Microchip cat flaps read the chip in the tunnel and unlock only for registered IDs.
