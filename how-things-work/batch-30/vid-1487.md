## Fact check

- Many password managers use end-to-end ('zero-knowledge') encryption: the vault is encrypted on the device with a key derived from the master password, which is not sent to the provider. Not every product works this way, hence 'many'.
- Password managers generate long random unique passwords.
- Autofill is tied to the saved domain, so look-alike phishing domains don't get autofilled.
