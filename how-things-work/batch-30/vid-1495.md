## Fact check

- Under EN 149, FFP1, FFP2 and FFP3 respirators must filter at least 80%, 94% and 99% of test aerosol respectively.
- Fibrous filters capture particles by impaction and interception (larger particles), diffusion (very small particles) and electrostatic attraction; they are not simple sieves.
- The most penetrating particle size is around 0.1-0.3 micrometres, which is why test aerosols target that range.
- Leakage around the face seal reduces protection, and some models have exhalation valves.
