## Fact check

- Ctrl+Shift+T (Cmd+Shift+T on macOS) reopens the last closed tab in major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
- Repeated presses reopen earlier closed tabs in reverse order.
- If the last thing closed was a window, the shortcut restores that window with its tabs.
- Browsers restore session history, often including scroll position.
