## Fact check

- Thin plastic films resist tearing until a notch exists; once a tear starts, stress concentrates at its tip and it propagates easily.
- Serrated dispenser blades concentrate force at many points, initiating multiple tears that join across the tape.
- Desk dispensers have weighted bases for one-handed use; the cut end rests on the blade/ledge ready for the next pull.
