## Fact check

- Ribbon loops sewn inside garments at the shoulder or waist are hanger loops.
- They keep wide-necked or slippery garments on hangers and let heavy or delicate items hang without stretching or creasing on the hanger shoulders: their stated purpose in garment care guides.
- Tucking them in rather than cutting is common advice.
