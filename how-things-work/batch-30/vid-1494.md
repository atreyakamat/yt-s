## Fact check

- Basketballs have an inner rubber (usually butyl) bladder, wound with nylon or polyester thread, then a rubber layer and a cover of leather, composite or rubber.
- The windings stop the bladder from bulging and keep the ball round and consistent.
- The cover is pebbled for grip; most balls have 8 panels separated by channels.
- NBA rules specify inflation of 7.5-8.5 psi.
- NBA ball inflated to 7.5-8.5 psi: OK. Source: https://official.nba.com/rule-no-1-court-dimensions-equipment/
