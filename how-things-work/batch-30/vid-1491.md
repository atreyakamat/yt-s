## Fact check

- Window envelopes display the address printed on the enclosed letter, avoiding separate addressing and mismatches.
- Windows are covered with transparent film or glassine (a dense, supercalendered translucent paper).
- Window positions follow standards (e.g. DL/C5 window positions) to suit folding/inserting machines and postal OCR.
- Americus F. Callahan received a US patent for the window envelope in 1902.
