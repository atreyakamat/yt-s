## Fact check

- Goggles fog when water vapor from the warm face condenses on lenses cooled by the water.
- Many goggles have a hydrophilic anti-fog coating on the inner lens surface that spreads condensation into a thin transparent film.
- Manufacturers advise not touching or wiping the inside of the lenses because rubbing removes the coating.
- Recommended care is rinsing in clean water and air drying.
