## Fact check

- A 2-litre four-stroke engine at 2,000 rpm draws about 2,000 litres of air per minute (displacement × rpm / 2), so 'thousands' is fair.
- Stoichiometric petrol combustion needs about 14.7 kg of air per kg of fuel; 1 litre of petrol (~0.74 kg) needs ~10.9 kg of air, about 9,000 litres at normal density ('roughly').
- Abrasive dust causes cylinder and ring wear; pleated paper filters maximise surface area and are replaced at service intervals.
- Most modern cars also have a cabin (pollen) filter.
