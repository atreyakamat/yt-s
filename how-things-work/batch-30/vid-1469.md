## Fact check

- Radiators are commonly placed under windows.
- Curtains drawn in front of a radiator trap its convective warm air between curtain and glass, directing heat to the window where much is lost.
- Energy-saving advice recommends tucking curtains behind radiators or using sill-length curtains.
