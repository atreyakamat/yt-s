## Fact check

- The Thames Barrier spans about 520 m with ten steel gates; it protects London from tidal surges driven by North Sea storms.
- Its rising sector gates rest in concrete sills on the riverbed, allowing navigation, and rotate up to close.
- The four main gates are each about 61 m wide and weigh over 3,000 tonnes; a blue whale weighs up to roughly 150 tonnes.
- It became operational in 1982, was officially opened in 1984, and has closed more than 200 times.
