## Fact check

- True left-handed scissors reverse the blade order so the top blade is on the left; a left hand's natural squeeze then presses the blades together.
- With right-handed scissors in the left hand, the squeeze tends to separate the blades and the top blade blocks the view of the cutting line.
- Roughly 10% of people are left-handed.
