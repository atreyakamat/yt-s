## Fact check

- Electronic parking brakes commonly use 'motor-on-caliper' units: an electric motor and gearbox drive a spindle that pushes the caliper piston and pads against the rear discs (some use a central motor pulling cables).
- The spindle is self-locking, so clamping force is held without power.
- Many systems release automatically when the driver pulls away (often requiring the seatbelt fastened/door closed) and enable hill-hold functions.
