## Fact check

- Phones measure received signal power (e.g., RSRP for LTE, roughly -140 to -44 dBm); typical received levels are picowatts to nanowatts, far below a millionth of a watt.
- There is no universal standard mapping signal level to bars; manufacturers and carriers choose thresholds, so different phones can show different bars in the same location.
- Bars reflect strength, not network load or signal quality (SINR); congested cells can be slow at full bars.
