## Fact check

- Materials → traditional baths are enamelled cast iron; most modern baths are acrylic.
- Weight → cast iron baths commonly weigh roughly 100-180 kg empty; acrylic about 20-40 kg (rounded examples shown).
- Heat capacity → a cold, heavy iron tub absorbs a lot of heat from the water initially, then retains heat well.
- Acrylic → low thermal conductivity, so it feels warm to touch and draws little heat from the water.
