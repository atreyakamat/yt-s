## Fact check

- Rinse aids contain surfactants that reduce water's surface tension, so water drains as a thin film ('sheeting') rather than droplets.
- Evaporating droplets leave dissolved minerals as spots; sheeting reduces spotting and speeds drying, notably on plastics, which hold less heat.
- Dishwashers dose rinse aid in the final rinse from a separate reservoir; many have an adjustable dose setting and a refill indicator.
