## Fact check

- Most hearing aid batteries are zinc-air cells that use oxygen from the air as a reactant, entering through small holes.
- A factory tab seals the holes; removing it activates the cell, and manufacturers commonly recommend waiting about a minute before use.
- Once activated, zinc-air cells degrade over weeks even if unused, while sealed ones keep for years.
- Standard tab colours: size 10 yellow, 312 brown, 13 orange, 675 blue. Bars show relative size.
