## Fact check

- Inverted squeeze bottles use a silicone slit valve (often cross-cut) in the closure.
- The valve stays closed under the weight of the product, opens when the bottle is squeezed, and closes when pressure is released, preventing drips.
- As the bottle recovers its shape, air is drawn back in through the valve.
- These bottles are designed to be stored cap-down so product sits at the opening.
