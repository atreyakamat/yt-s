## Fact check

- Flush causes cold pressure drop → a toilet refilling draws cold water, lowering cold supply pressure.
- Pressure-balance valves → a spool or diaphragm between hot and cold supplies moves to keep their pressures balanced, throttling the hot when cold pressure drops.
- Common in North America → widely required by US plumbing codes for showers (anti-scald valves).
