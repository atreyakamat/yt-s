## Fact check

- Silica gel is a porous form of silicon dioxide (the main component of sand/quartz).
- Its internal surface area is very large, typically several hundred square metres per gram (often around 700-800 m²/g); a tennis court is about 260 m².
- Water vapour adsorbs onto the pore surfaces, lowering humidity in a package and helping prevent rust, mould and damp.
- Packets are labelled 'do not eat' because they aren't food and are a choking hazard; they can be reused to keep small spaces dry.
