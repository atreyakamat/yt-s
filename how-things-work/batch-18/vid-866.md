## Fact check

- Digital instant-read thermometers use a thermistor or thermocouple sensor at or near the probe tip; thermistor resistance varies strongly with temperature.
- A small sensor in a thin probe has low thermal mass, so it reaches food temperature in seconds.
- Food-safety guidance says to measure the thickest part of the meat and avoid bone, fat and gristle, which can give misleading readings.
