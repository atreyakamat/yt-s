## Fact check

- Ice or frost on wings disrupts airflow, reducing lift and raising stall speed.
- Ground de-icing uses heated Type I fluid (commonly dyed orange), and anti-icing Type IV fluid (commonly green) to delay re-icing for a holdover time.
- Many jets use engine bleed air to heat wing leading edges.
- Many turboprops use pneumatic de-icing boots that inflate to crack ice.
- The Boeing 787 uses electro-thermal heater mats on its wing leading edges.
