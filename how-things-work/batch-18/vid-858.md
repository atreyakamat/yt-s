## Fact check

- Heated rear windows use a grid of resistive silver-ceramic paste lines screen-printed and fired onto the glass; current heats them.
- They clear condensation and light frost; high current draw (often 10-20 A) is why most systems have an automatic timer of around 10-15 minutes.
- Some cars use the heater grid or additional printed lines as a radio antenna.
- A break in a line opens that circuit, leaving a band unheated.
