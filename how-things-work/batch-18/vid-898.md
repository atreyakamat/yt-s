## Fact check

- Concave → magnifying mirrors are concave spherical mirrors.
- Enlarged upright image close up → an object inside the focal length of a concave mirror gives a virtual, upright, magnified image.
- Flips beyond the focal point → objects beyond the focal point give a real, inverted image.
- 5x and 10x → the most common magnifications for cosmetic mirrors.
- Telescope mirrors → reflecting telescopes use concave primary mirrors.
