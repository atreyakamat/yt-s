## Fact check

- Two-way switching uses two changeover (SPDT) switches with a common terminal and two 'strapper' wires (L1, L2) between them.
- The lamp is lit when both switches select the same strapper, completing the circuit; changing either switch breaks it.
- Called 'two-way' in the UK and '3-way' in North America.
