## Fact check

- Disposable masks include a malleable nose strip (often aluminium or plastic-coated wire) to conform the mask to the nose bridge.
- Airflow follows the path of least resistance, so gaps around the edge leak far more easily than flow through the filter. Bars are illustrative.
- Fogging of glasses is caused by warm, humid exhaled air escaping from the top edge; shaping the nose strip reduces it.
