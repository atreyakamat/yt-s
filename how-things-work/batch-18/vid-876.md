## Fact check

- Scissors are a pair of first-class levers pivoting at the screw; force at the blade is greatest closest to the pivot (mechanical advantage = handle arm / blade arm).
- Bar values are relative and illustrative, not measured.
- Nutcrackers use the same principle (load placed close to the hinge).
