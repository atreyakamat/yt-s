## Fact check

- Clothing fibres trap dust and abrasive particles.
- Dry-wiping drags particles across lens surfaces and coatings, causing micro-scratches that accumulate into haze.
- Opticians and lens makers recommend rinsing with lukewarm water and a drop of mild dish soap, then drying with a clean microfibre cloth, and avoiding tissues and paper towels (wood fibres can scratch).
