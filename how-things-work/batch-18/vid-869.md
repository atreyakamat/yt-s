## Fact check

- Many TRVs use 1-5: 3 about 20 C, 2 about 15-17 C, highest about 26-28 C (setting 1 swapped for setting 2 at 16). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermostatic_radiator_valve
