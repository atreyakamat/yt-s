## Fact check

- Mechanical kitchen timers store energy in a spring wound by turning the dial.
- A gear train and a simple escapement (escape wheel plus oscillating lever/pallet) release the spring in small steps, producing the ticking and controlling the speed.
- At zero, a cam or notch releases a spring-loaded hammer that strikes a bell.
