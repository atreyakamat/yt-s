## Fact check

- Immersion heaters are sheathed electric elements fitted into hot water cylinders, usually from the top or side, with a rod thermostat alongside.
- Sheathed elements use a resistance coil packed in magnesium oxide powder, an electrical insulator that conducts heat well.
- UK domestic immersion heaters are typically 3 kW, similar to a kettle.
- Limescale deposits on elements in hard water areas reduce efficiency and shorten element life.
