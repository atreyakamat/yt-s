## Fact check

- Air is about 1.2 g/L and helium about 0.18 g/L at room conditions, roughly seven times less dense.
- Helium atoms are small enough to diffuse through latex; a standard latex helium balloon typically floats for well under a day (often 8-12 hours).
- Foil balloons are metallized nylon/plastic film, far less permeable, and float for days to weeks.
- Gas volume falls with temperature, so balloons shrink in the cold and recover when warmed.
- Treatments that coat the inside of latex balloons with a gel are widely used to extend float time.
