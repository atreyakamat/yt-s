## Fact check

- Electric vibrator horns: an electromagnet pulls a steel diaphragm, which opens contact points, cutting the current; the diaphragm springs back and recloses them, oscillating at audio frequency.
- Car horn tones are typically a few hundred Hz; dual-horn sets commonly pair roughly 400 Hz and 500 Hz units (labelled 'about').
- 'Snail' horns use a coiled horn-shaped passage to amplify sound like a trumpet.
