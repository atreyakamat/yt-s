## Fact check

- Thermal laminating pouches are typically polyester (PET) film coated with a heat-activated adhesive such as EVA.
- Heated rollers melt the adhesive and press out air; insufficient heat leaves a cloudy finish.
- Cold laminating uses pressure-sensitive adhesive films. Gauge is illustrative.
