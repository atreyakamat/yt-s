## Fact check

- Panel radiators with convector fins transfer the majority of their heat by convection; radiation is the smaller share (no percentage claimed).
- Fins (convector plates) welded between panels greatly increase surface area in contact with air.
- The vertical fin channels promote a chimney-like convective airflow from bottom to top.
