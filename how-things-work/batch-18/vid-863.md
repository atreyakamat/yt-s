## Fact check

- Polyester absorbs very little water (moisture regain under 1%).
- Wicking is capillary flow through the small spaces between fibres, the same effect as water rising in a paper towel.
- Spreading moisture over a larger outer area speeds evaporation, and evaporation removes heat.
- Cotton absorbs a lot of water and dries slowly, so it stays heavy and damp.
