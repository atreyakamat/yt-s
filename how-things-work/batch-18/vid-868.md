## Fact check

- Twist-lock pumps → many lotion and soap pumps lock in the down position by twisting; they're shipped that way to prevent dispensing in transit.
- Spring, one-way valves and dip tube → standard lotion pump construction (piston, spring, ball or flap check valves, dip tube).
- Locking stops accidental pumping → the head can't be pressed while locked; worded as 'stops it pumping', not 'leak-proof'.
