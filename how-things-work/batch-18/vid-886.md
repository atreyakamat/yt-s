## Fact check

- Many true wireless earbuds use optical (infrared) proximity sensors and/or capacitive skin-detect sensors for in-ear detection.
- Reflected IR rises sharply when the sensor is against skin, indicating the bud is worn.
- Earbuds signal the paired device to pause/resume playback and may power down parts of the bud when removed, saving battery.
