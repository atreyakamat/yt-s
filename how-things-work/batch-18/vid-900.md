## Fact check

- Hanger arms slope to approximate shoulder slope so garments hang in their worn shape.
- The open hook lets the hanger be lifted on and off a rail.
- Narrow wire hangers concentrate load and can leave 'shoulder bumps' in knits; wider, contoured hangers spread the weight.
- Many hangers include a trouser bar and notches for straps; a triangle is a rigid shape, which helps thin wire hold its form.
