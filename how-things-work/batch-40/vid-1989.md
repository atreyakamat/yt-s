## Fact check

- Rotating platforms spin on a central bearing; once moving, angular momentum keeps them turning until friction slows them. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angular_momentum
- Riders feel "outward" force because of their inertia in a rotating frame (centrifugal effect). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrifugal_force
