## Fact check

- Lawn stripes are created by bending grass blades in different directions with a roller; the difference is reflected light, not colour. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawn
- Pitch stripes are made by mowing in alternating directions. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawn_mower
