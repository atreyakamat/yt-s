## Fact check

- Heated fog machines push a glycol/glycerin-and-water fluid into a heat exchanger, where it vaporises; it condenses into visible fog on meeting cooler air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fog_machine
- Hazers produce particles under 5 micrometres. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fog_machine
- Chilled fog: water heated to near boiling with dry ice dropped in makes thick fog that stays close to the ground. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fog_machine
- Laser beams are visible when scanned through theatrical smoke or fog. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_lighting_display
