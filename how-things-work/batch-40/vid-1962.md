## Fact check

- Blister packs are formed by thermoforming a plastic cavity, filling it, and sealing with a lidding foil. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blister_pack
- Push-through packs use thin foil; peel-push and child-resistant versions use laminated backings. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blister_pack
- Each cavity is sealed separately, protecting the others from moisture and air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blister_pack
