## Fact check

- Manual wheelchairs typically have large rear wheels with handrims and small swivelling front casters. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheelchair
- Camber (wheels tilted inwards at the top) widens the base and improves lateral stability and manoeuvrability, strongest in sports chairs. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheelchair
- Larger wheels roll more easily over obstacles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheel
