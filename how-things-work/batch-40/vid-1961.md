## Fact check

- Fresh meat is red because heme iron in myoglobin is ferrous and binds an oxygen molecule (oxymyoglobin); oxidised iron (metmyoglobin, ferric) turns meat dark red/brown. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myoglobin
- "Shops often wrap meat in film that lets air in" and "sealed pack looks darker" are general retail practice; no numbers claimed.
