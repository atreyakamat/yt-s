## Fact check

- Lean-to-steer scooters and skateboards turn through a tilted pivot axis that links deck lean to wheel steering. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kick_scooter
- Skateboard trucks turn when the deck is tilted (a rake/kingpin angle). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skateboard_truck
