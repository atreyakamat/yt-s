## Fact check

- A wheat kernel has bran, germ and endosperm; white flour is mostly endosperm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheat
- Roller mills replaced millstones in the 19th century; grain passes through successive roll and sifting stages. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roller_mill
