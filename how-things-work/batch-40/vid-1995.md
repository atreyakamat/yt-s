## Fact check

- Leo Baekeland made Bakelite, the first fully synthetic plastic, in 1907. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakelite
- Bakelite is a thermosetting phenol formaldehyde resin that does not remelt; it was used as an electrical insulator in plugs, radios and telephones. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakelite
- Baekeland was initially seeking a shellac substitute. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Baekeland
- Celluloid (1860s) was semi-synthetic, based on modified cellulose. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celluloid
- Bakelite developed 1907, first synthetic plastic, infusible thermoset: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakelite
