## Fact check

- The 'chlorine smell' at pools is caused mostly by chloramines formed when chlorine reacts with sweat, urine and other body matter. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chloramine
- Chloramines irritate eyes and are produced by reaction with swimmers' contaminants. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_pool_sanitation
- Chlorine is consumed as it disinfects. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swimming_pool_sanitation
