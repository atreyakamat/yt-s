## Fact check

- Clarence Saunders founded Piggly Wiggly on 6 September 1916 in Memphis, widely considered the first self-service grocery; patent for the self-serving store 1917; before that clerks fetched items. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piggly_Wiggly
- King Kullen opened 4 August 1930 in Jamaica, Queens, identified as the first true US supermarket combining self-service, separate food departments, discount pricing and a parking lot. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermarket
