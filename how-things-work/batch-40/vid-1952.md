## Fact check

- Joseph Lister introduced antiseptic surgery using carbolic acid, publishing in 1867 (article has "Antiseptic system 1865-1867"); surgeons of the time took pride in unwashed operating gowns and did not have to wash hands. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Lister
- Infection (sepsis) was a leading cause of surgical deaths before antisepsis. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiseptic
- Lister was inspired by Louis Pasteur's germ theory. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Lister
- Lister reported that infection and death rates in his wards fell. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Lister
