## Fact check

- Patio heaters burn propane or butane to heat an emitter that radiates infrared heat, often under a reflector dome. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patio_heater
- Infrared radiation transfers heat directly to people and objects without needing to heat the air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrared_heater
- Many models include a tilt-over safety shut-off. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patio_heater
