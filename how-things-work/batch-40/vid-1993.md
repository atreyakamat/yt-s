## Fact check

- Francis Beaufort, a Royal Navy hydrographer, devised the scale in 1805; it was officially adopted in the 1830s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaufort_scale
- It originally had 13 levels (0 to 12), based on the effects of wind on a frigate's sails; forces 13 to 17 were added in 1946. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaufort_scale
- Land-based descriptions (smoke, leaves, branches) were added later. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaufort_scale
- Beaufort devised scale 1805, Navy adopted 1830s, 0-12 originally, 13-17 added 1946: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaufort_scale
