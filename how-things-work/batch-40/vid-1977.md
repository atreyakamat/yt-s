## Fact check

- Edison announced the phonograph on 21 November 1877; early machines recorded onto tinfoil wrapped around a grooved cylinder via a diaphragm and stylus. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonograph
- Edison reportedly tested the first machine by reciting "Mary Had a Little Lamb". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonograph
- Berliner began commercial production of disc records in 1892 and switched from hard rubber to shellac in 1895. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonograph
