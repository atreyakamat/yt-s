## Fact check

- Ice rinks use refrigerated pipes beneath the surface (brine or glycol) to freeze water into ice. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_rink
- Rink ice is built up to 19 to 38 mm (about 2 to 4 cm); scripted as "a few centimetres". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_rink
- White paint/lines are put under the ice surface. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_rink
