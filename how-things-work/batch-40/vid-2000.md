## Fact check

- Burj Khalifa has a Y-shaped plan with setbacks; the shape was designed to reduce wind forces on the tower. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burj_Khalifa
- Wind flowing past tall structures sheds alternating vortices (vortex shedding), which can force sway. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vortex_shedding
- Stepping the shape disrupts vortex formation along the height; stated generally. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burj_Khalifa
