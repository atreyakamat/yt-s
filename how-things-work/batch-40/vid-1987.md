## Fact check

- Laser shows use small mirrors on galvanometers, driven by control voltages, in X and Y to aim the beam at any point on a square (vector scanning). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_lighting_display
- Beams are very visible when scanned through theatrical smoke or fog; shows use beamstops and keep beams above audiences, and audience scanning requires keeping the beam moving. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_lighting_display
