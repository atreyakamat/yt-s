## Fact check

- A dumbwaiter is a small freight elevator or lift for carrying food and goods between floors; hand-operated versions use ropes and pulleys, later versions use motors. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumbwaiter_(elevator)
- Name refers to a silent servant (dumb = silent). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumbwaiter_(elevator)
