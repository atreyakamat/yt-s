## Fact check

- Out-of-focus points blur into the polygon formed by the aperture blades; more or curved blades approximate a circle; mirror (catadioptric) lenses give doughnut-shaped bokeh. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bokeh
- Bokeh comes from Japanese boke, meaning blur or haze. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bokeh
