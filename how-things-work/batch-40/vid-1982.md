## Fact check

- Tablet contains a bicarbonate (e.g. sodium bicarbonate) and an acid (e.g. citric or tartaric acid) in powder form; they react once dissolved in water, releasing carbon dioxide. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effervescent_tablet
- Tablets are pressed from powders into a dense mass and packaged in blister packs or hermetically sealed containers, with a desiccant (silica gel) in the cap of some tubes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effervescent_tablet
- Effervescent medicinal beverages date to the late 1800s, used to mask bitter mineral waters. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effervescent_tablet
