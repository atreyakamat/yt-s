## Fact check

- Tablets are made by compressing powder or granules between punches in a die. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tablet_(pharmacy)
- Tablet formulations include active ingredient, fillers (diluents), binders and lubricants. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tablet_(pharmacy)
- Rotary tablet presses have a turret with many die stations. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tablet_press
