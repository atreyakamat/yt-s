## Fact check

- Butter is made by churning cream; agitation ruptures fat globule membranes so fat clumps, leaving buttermilk. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butter
- Butter-making is ancient and churn designs evolved from skin bags to barrels to mechanical churns. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churning_(butter)
