## Fact check

- Acoustic mirrors were concrete structures built on Britain's coast, mainly between about 1915 and the 1930s, to detect aircraft by sound. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustic_mirror
- Examples include the Denge sound mirrors near Dungeness, Kent. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denge_sound_mirrors
- Radar (Chain Home) replaced them in the late 1930s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chain_Home
- Air raids on Britain from Zeppelins began in 1915. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeppelin_raids_in_World_War_I
- Mirrors built 1920s-30s, ended by faster aircraft and radar: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustic_mirror
