## Fact check

- A de Laval nozzle (converging-diverging) accelerates hot gas to supersonic speeds; flow is sonic (Mach 1) at the throat. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Laval_nozzle
- In the diverging section the gas expands, pressure and temperature fall and velocity rises. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocket_engine_nozzle
- Vacuum-optimised engines use larger nozzle expansion ratios. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocket_engine_nozzle
