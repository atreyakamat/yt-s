## Fact check

- A Norman door is one whose design gives the wrong signals about how to use it; named after Don Norman, who wrote on design of everyday things. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_door
- Flat plates signal pushing, handles signal pulling (affordances). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affordance
