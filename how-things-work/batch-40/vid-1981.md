## Fact check

- Reverse vending machines identify containers mainly by scanning the UPC barcode and matching a database; older systems used shape; weight is also analysed; items are crushed and sorted by material for recycling. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_vending_machine
- Australia: 10-cent refund per eligible container. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_vending_machine
