## Fact check

- A cleat is a fitting with two horns used to secure a rope; a cleat hitch uses a turn around the base, figure-eight wraps, and a locking hitch. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleat_(nautical)
- Friction on a rope wrapped around a cylinder increases exponentially with the angle of wrap (capstan equation). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capstan_equation
- A cleat hitch can be released quickly even under load, by freeing the locking hitch. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cleat_hitch
