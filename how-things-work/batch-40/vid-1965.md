## Fact check

- Tissue paper is made on a paper machine, creped and wound onto large parent rolls, then converted: embossed, perforated, rewound on cores and cut into rolls. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toilet_paper
- Creping gives tissue softness and stretch. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creping
