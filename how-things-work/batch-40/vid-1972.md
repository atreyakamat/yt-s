## Fact check

- The metric teaspoon is defined as 5 mL. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaspoon
- Household teaspoons vary in size, so dosing devices are recommended for liquid medicines. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaspoon
- Dosing cups and oral syringes are marked in mL. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oral_syringe
