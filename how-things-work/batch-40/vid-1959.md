## Fact check

- Residue analysis suggests ceramic feeding vessels were used as early as 1200 BCE; pewter, tin and silver feeding pots in the 1700s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_bottle
- 1841: first glass nursing bottle patented in the US (C.M. Windship); 1845: Elijah Pratt patented the first rubber nipple. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_bottle
- Early 1900s rubber could withstand sterilisation heat; 1922 Corning heat-resistant Pyrex bottles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_bottle
