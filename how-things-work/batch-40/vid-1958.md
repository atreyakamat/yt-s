## Fact check

- The Janssen effect: pressure in a silo saturates with depth because wall friction supports part of the stored material's weight. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janssen_effect
- Silos store bulk grain and unload by gravity from the bottom. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silo
