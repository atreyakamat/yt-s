## Fact check

- Cup anemometers rotate because the drag on the concave side is higher than on the convex side; rotation speed is proportional to wind speed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anemometer
- They respond to wind from any direction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anemometer
- Bar values on screen (rpm) are illustrative only and not measurements.
