## Fact check

- Combustion needs oxygen; kettle grills regulate temperature with intake and exhaust vents (qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbecue_grill
- Closing the vents starves the fire of oxygen, which is how coals are extinguished. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combustion
