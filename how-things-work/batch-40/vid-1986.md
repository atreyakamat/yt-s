## Fact check

- Under the international collision regulations (COLREGs) Rule 34, one short blast means altering course to starboard, two to port, three means engines going astern. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Regulations_for_Preventing_Collisions_at_Sea
- A short blast is about one second; a prolonged blast is four to six seconds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Regulations_for_Preventing_Collisions_at_Sea
- In restricted visibility a power-driven vessel sounds one prolonged blast at intervals of not more than two minutes (Rule 35). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Regulations_for_Preventing_Collisions_at_Sea
- Low-frequency sound is absorbed less and travels farther. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound_attenuation
