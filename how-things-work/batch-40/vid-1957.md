## Fact check

- Red-eye: flash bounces off the back of the eye in low light; red comes from the blood in the choroid. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-eye_effect
- Pre-flashes make pupils contract; a flash close to the lens, especially a built-in one, makes red-eye more likely; bouncing flash off a white surface helps. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-eye_effect
