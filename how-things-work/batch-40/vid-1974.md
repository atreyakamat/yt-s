## Fact check

- Frank Zamboni built the first ice resurfacing machine (Model A) in 1949 in California. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamboni
- Resurfacers shave, collect snow, wash and lay a thin layer of water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_resurfacer
- Earlier resurfacing was done by hand with scraping and flooding. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_resurfacer
- First Zamboni resurfacer built 1949 in Paramount, California: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamboni
