## Fact check

- Grit sizes are defined by the mesh of sieves that grains pass through; a higher number means a smaller grain. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandpaper
- Grit numbers of roughly 40 (coarse) to 400 (fine) are common commercial grades; script says "like forty" and "like four hundred". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandpaper
- Sanding through progressively finer grits removes earlier scratches. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandpaper
