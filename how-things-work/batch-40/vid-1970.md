## Fact check

- Taipei 101's tuned mass damper is 660 metric tons, located on floors 87 to 92. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuned_mass_damper
- Tuned mass dampers move in opposition to the building's resonant oscillations. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuned_mass_damper
- Tall buildings sway in wind and the motion can bother occupants. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuned_mass_damper
- Taipei 101 damper pendulum is 660 tonnes (steel pendulum, hung from the 92nd to 88th floors). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei_101
