## Fact check

- In a tipping bucket gauge, rain flows through a funnel into a seesaw-like container; once a preset amount collects it tips, empties and sends an electrical signal (often a reed switch). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_gauge
- Rainfall depth in mm is equivalent to litres per square metre, so 1 mm = 1 L/m². Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_gauge
- Counter value of 10 tips is illustrative.
