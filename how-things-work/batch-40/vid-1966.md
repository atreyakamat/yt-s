## Fact check

- Inflatable liferafts are stored in canisters with a hydrostatic release unit that frees the canister at a certain depth if a vessel sinks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liferaft
- The raft is attached by a painter line; tension on it triggers inflation from a gas cylinder, and a weak link breaks to free the raft. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liferaft
- Hydrostatic releases are typically set to operate a few metres under water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrostatic_release_unit
