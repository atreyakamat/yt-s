## Fact check

- Before containerisation, break-bulk cargo was handled piece by piece by large gangs of longshoremen; the grid illustrates a gang, not an exact count.
- Ships could spend days to weeks in port loading and unloading.
- Malcolm McLean's converted tanker Ideal X carried 58 containers from Newark to Houston in April 1956.
- ISO container size standards were developed in the 1960s, allowing intermodal transfer between trucks, trains and ships.
- McLean estimated loading cost fell from about $5.86 per ton to about $0.16 per ton.
- 1956 Ideal X, 58 containers, Newark to Houston: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Ideal_X
- ISO container standards published 1968-1970 (script says 1960s, fine). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermodal_container
