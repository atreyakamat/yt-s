## Fact check

- Most domestic gas meters are diaphragm (bellows) meters: positive-displacement meters with two (or more) chambers that alternately fill and empty.
- Slide valves switch the chambers; a linkage drives the index, each cycle displacing a fixed volume.
- UK meters read in cubic metres; older imperial meters read in hundreds of cubic feet.
