## Fact check

- On 19 September 1982, Scott Fahlman at Carnegie Mellon proposed :-) to mark jokes and :-( for serious posts on the university's online bulletin board, after a joke post about a mercury spill was taken seriously.
- Emoticons spread through online communities in the 1980s-1990s.
- Emoji sets appeared in Japan in the late 1990s (SoftBank 1997; the best-known 176-icon DoCoMo set by Shigetaka Kurita, 1999); "first emoji set 1999" removed because earlier sets exist. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emoji
- 1982 Fahlman proposal at Carnegie Mellon: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emoticon
