## Fact check

- Ornamental shell buttons from the Indus Valley (Mohenjo-daro) date to around 2000 BCE, over 4,000 years ago.
- Early buttons were mostly decorative; garments were fastened with pins, brooches (fibulae), laces and ties.
- Functional buttonholes appear in 13th-century Europe (often credited to Germany).
- They enabled close-fitting garments, and 14th-century fashion featured rows of many buttons on sleeves and fronts.
