## Fact check

- Before rubber balloons, inflated animal bladders and intestines were used as toys and balls.
- Michael Faraday made the first rubber balloons in 1824 for experiments with hydrogen, pressing two rubber sheets together with the inner surfaces floured so they wouldn't stick.
- Thomas Hancock was selling do-it-yourself rubber balloon kits by 1825.
- Latex balloons made by dipping molds became mass-produced in the 20th century (the "1930s" date could not be confirmed, so the script says "later"). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balloon
