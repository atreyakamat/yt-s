## Fact check

- Night vision relies on rod cells; full dark adaptation takes roughly 20-30 minutes.
- Bright white light bleaches rod photopigment and largely resets dark adaptation.
- Rods are relatively insensitive to long-wavelength (deep red) light, so dim red light preserves dark adaptation; astronomers use red lights for this reason.
- Red light is also less dazzling to others in the dark.
