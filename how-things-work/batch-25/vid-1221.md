## Fact check

- Many postage stamps (e.g. Royal Mail since 1959) carry phosphor bands that glow (luminesce) under UV light.
- Automatic facing machines detect the phosphor to locate the stamp, orient letters, and cancel the stamp; phosphor also helps detect forged or reused stamps.
- Grid count is illustrative.
