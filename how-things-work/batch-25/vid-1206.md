## Fact check

- Before ATMs, cash was obtained from tellers during banking hours, which were short and mostly on weekdays. Bar values are illustrative.
- The first widely recognised cash machine opened at a Barclays branch in Enfield, London, on 27 June 1967.
- It used paper vouchers (impregnated with a mildly radioactive carbon-14 marker) and a personal code, rather than plastic cards.
- Magnetic stripe cards later replaced vouchers.
