## Fact check

- Pod/capsule machines pierce the capsule with blades or needles when closed, then an electric pump forces heated water into it.
- In many espresso capsule systems the foil lid deforms under pressure and ruptures against a pyramid-patterned plate, so extraction happens under pressure (around espresso pressures).
- Capsules are sealed (often nitrogen-flushed) to protect the coffee from oxygen.
- A typical capsule brew takes well under a minute.
