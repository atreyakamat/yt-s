## Fact check

- A classic pogo stick has a large steel coil spring inside its tube, compressed by the rider standing on foot pegs.
- The spring stores energy on landing and releases it to launch the rider.
- Pushing down at the bottom of each bounce adds energy and increases jump height.
- Extreme pogo sticks use compressed air or elastomer (rubber) bands; Xpogo sticks have the potential for about 3 m (10 ft) of height (a potential figure, not a documented record; script says "about 3 meters"). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pogo_stick
