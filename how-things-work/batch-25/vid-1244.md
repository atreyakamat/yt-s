## Fact check

- Water hardness is mainly dissolved calcium and magnesium; it forms limescale when heated.
- Ion-exchange softeners use resin beads charged with sodium ions, which exchange for calcium and magnesium ions.
- When exhausted, the resin is regenerated with brine from the salt reservoir and the hardness ions are flushed to drain.
- 'Chalk' in the hook is a loose everyday word for the calcium in limescale; the VO explains it properly.
