## Fact check

- Link-and-pin coupling required workers to stand between cars and was a major cause of injuries and deaths among railway workers.
- Eli Janney patented the knuckle coupler in 1873.
- The US Safety Appliance Act of 1893 required automatic couplers.
- Knuckles close and lock automatically on impact; uncoupling uses a lever from the side of the car. Europe largely uses buffers and screw couplings, with automatic couplers on many passenger trains.
- Janney knuckle coupler patented 1873; Safety Appliance Act 1893: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janney_coupler
