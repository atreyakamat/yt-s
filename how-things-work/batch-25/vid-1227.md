## Fact check

- Eggshells have thousands of pores (commonly cited as about 7,000-17,000).
- Over time moisture and CO2 escape through the pores and air enters, enlarging the air cell at the blunt end.
- In water, fresh eggs lie flat; older eggs with bigger air cells tilt or stand up; very old eggs may float.
- The test indicates age, not safety; dates and storage guidance on the pack still apply.
