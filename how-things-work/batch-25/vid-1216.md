## Fact check

- Small phone sensors and short focal lengths give deep depth of field, so little natural background blur.
- Portrait modes estimate depth via stereo disparity from two cameras, dual-pixel (split pixel) disparity, and/or machine-learning segmentation.
- Software applies synthetic blur based on the depth map; fine hair and edges are common failure points.
