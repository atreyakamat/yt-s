## Fact check

- Before electric supply, homes were lit by candles, oil lamps and gas.
- Edison's Holborn Viaduct station in London (January 1882) and Pearl Street Station in New York (September 1882) were among the first central power stations.
- Pearl Street distributed low-voltage DC through underground copper conductors to an area of roughly a square mile; low-voltage DC loses too much power to travel much further.
- AC can be stepped up by transformers for efficient long-distance transmission and stepped down for use, which is why it won the 'war of the currents'.
