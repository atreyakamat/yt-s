## Fact check

- Eccrine/apocrine sweat is mostly water with salts, lipids and proteins; it is close to colourless.
- Antiperspirants use aluminium salts that temporarily plug sweat ducts.
- Yellow underarm stains are widely attributed to aluminium compounds combining with sweat lipids/proteins and building up in fabric over washes.
- Common care advice: let antiperspirant dry before dressing and launder promptly.
