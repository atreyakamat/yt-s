## Fact check

- Disposable moisture absorbers use calcium chloride, which is hygroscopic and deliquescent: it absorbs water vapour until it dissolves into a brine.
- The brine drains through a grille or membrane into a lower reservoir.
- Calcium chloride can absorb more than its own weight in water in humid conditions (several times at high humidity).
- These products reduce humidity locally but don't fix causes such as leaks, poor ventilation or condensation.
