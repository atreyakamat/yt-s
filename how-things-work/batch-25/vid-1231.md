## Fact check

- Airplane mode disables cellular radio and, by default, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth (which users can re-enable).
- In poor coverage, phones raise transmit power and scan more for base stations, increasing battery drain.
- With radios off, more of the charger's power goes to the battery, so charging can be slightly faster.
