## Fact check

- Indigo dyes mainly the outer layer of denim yarns (ring dyeing), leaving a white core.
- Abrasion in washing removes surface indigo, causing fading.
- Washing inside out moves abrasion to the inner face and covers hardware: standard denim care advice.
- Cold water and less frequent washing reduce fading.
