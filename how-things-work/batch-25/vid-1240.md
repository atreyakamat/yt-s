## Fact check

- Safety guidance notes that shouting in water is tiring and carries poorly over wind and wave noise; whistles are standard on approved lifejackets.
- High-pitched whistle tones stand out from low-frequency wind and wave noise and carry further for less effort. Bars are illustrative.
- Many marine and rescue whistles are 'pealess', since a cork pea can jam or swell when wet.
- Three blasts is a commonly taught distress signal.
