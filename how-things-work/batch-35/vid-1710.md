## Fact check (verify before posting)

Small bruises fade in about two weeks; red/blue then green (biliverdin), yellow (bilirubin), brown: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruise

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Bruises change colour as they heal.
2. Blood leaks under the skin when vessels break.
3. Fresh bruises look red, then blue or purple.
4. Your body breaks down the blood pigment.
5. That makes green, then yellow shades.
6. Most fade away in two weeks.
