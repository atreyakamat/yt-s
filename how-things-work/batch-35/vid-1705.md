## Fact check (verify before posting)

A/B/AB/O differ by red-cell antigens; O Rh-negative is often called the universal red cell donor (generally true, with caveats). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_type

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Your blood type depends on markers.
2. Red blood cells carry sugar markers called antigens.
3. Type A, B, AB and O differ by these markers.
4. A plus or minus sign adds the Rh factor.
5. Mismatched blood can trigger an immune reaction.
6. Type O negative is the universal red cell donor.
