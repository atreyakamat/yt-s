# vid-1718 · Why eggs change when cooked

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Heat turns runny eggs solid."
- Backup 1: "Why egg white turns white."
- Backup 2: "Heat rewrites protein shapes."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Denaturation and coagulation of egg proteins (ovalbumin sets ~80 °C; yolk ~65-70 °C). Irreversible in normal cooking. Good.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Heat turns runny eggs solid.
2. Egg proteins are folded into tight shapes.
3. Heat unfolds them.
4. They tangle together and trap water.
5. That turns clear white into opaque solid.
6. Cooked proteins can't go back to runny.
