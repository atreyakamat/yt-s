## Fact check

- A moka pot has a bottom water chamber, a funnel-shaped coffee basket and a top collecting chamber with a central spout, sealed by a gasket, with a safety valve on the base.
- Heating raises the pressure of the trapped air and water vapour above the water, pushing water up through the grounds.
- Moka pots brew at roughly 1-2 bar (sources vary; some measure under 1 bar), compared with about 9 bar for espresso machines, giving a different, less concentrated result.
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moka_pot (typical extraction at 1 to 2 bar).
