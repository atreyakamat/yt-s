## Fact check

- Retroreflective materials return light toward its source using glass microbeads with a reflective backing or microprismatic corner-cube elements.
- A corner cube has three mutually perpendicular reflecting faces and returns light parallel to the incoming direction.
- Because a driver's eyes are close to the headlights, retroreflected light appears very bright to them. Bar values are illustrative.
- Apollo missions left retroreflector arrays on the Moon used for laser ranging.
