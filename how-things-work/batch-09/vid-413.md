## Fact check

- Hook-and-eye fasteners are formed from bent wire (or flat metal for trouser hook-and-bar).
- Used at the top of dress zips, on trouser waistbands and on bra bands: true.
- Under tension the hook seats in the eye and resists sliding out; slack is needed to disengage: basic mechanics of a hook.
- Bra bands typically offer multiple columns of eyes for adjustable tightness.
