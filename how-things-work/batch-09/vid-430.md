## Fact check

- Hard capsules have two parts, a body and a slightly larger cap, which allows them to be separated, filled and rejoined by machine.
- Industrial capsule fillers handle many thousands of capsules per hour.
- Many capsule designs have locking rings or dimples so the halves snap shut after filling.
- Capsule shells are commonly gelatin or HPMC (a plant-derived cellulose) and dissolve in the stomach.
