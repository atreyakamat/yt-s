## Fact check

- Most e-scooters use brushless hub motors where electromagnet coils push permanent magnets.
- An electric motor driven by the wheel acts as a generator; the electrical load produces a braking torque.
- Many e-scooters use regenerative braking that returns some energy to the battery.
- Recovered energy is modest, and scooters also have mechanical brakes (disc, drum or fender brakes).
