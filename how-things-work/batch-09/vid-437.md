## Fact check

- Submarine cables carry the vast majority (commonly quoted ~99%) of intercontinental data traffic.
- Deep-sea cable sections are roughly 17-25 mm across, similar to a garden hose; shore ends are armoured and thicker.
- Construction: optical fibres, copper conductor (powers repeaters), steel strength wires, polyethylene insulation.
- Optical repeaters amplify the signal at intervals (tens of km).
- There are several hundred cable systems with a total length over 1 million km.
- Most faults are caused by fishing and anchors; shark bites on cables have been documented but are rare.
