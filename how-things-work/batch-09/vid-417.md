## Fact check

- Joseph Coyle, a newspaper editor in Smithers, British Columbia, invented the egg carton in 1911 after hearing a dispute between a farmer and a hotel owner over broken eggs.
- Separate cells stop eggs knocking into each other.
- Modern cartons are commonly moulded paper pulp, which cushions impacts.
- Raised posts between cells support the lid and stacked loads so weight isn't carried by the eggs.
- Eggs are commonly packed pointed end down, keeping the air cell at the blunt top end.
