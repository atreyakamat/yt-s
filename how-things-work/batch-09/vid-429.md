## Fact check

- In equal temperament each semitone is a frequency ratio of 2^(1/12) ≈ 1.0595, so each fret shortens the vibrating length by about 5.6% of what remains.
- Because the shortening is a fixed fraction of a shrinking length, fret spacing gets smaller up the neck.
- The 12th fret sits at half the scale length, giving the octave.
- On a common 648 mm (25.5 in) scale, the first fret gap is about 36 mm, the 12th about 18 mm, the 24th about 9 mm.
- Evenly spaced frets would not produce equal-tempered pitches.
