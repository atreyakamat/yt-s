## Fact check

- Popping candy (patented by General Foods chemist William Mitchell, 1950s) is made by mixing molten sugar with carbon dioxide under high pressure (about 600 psi, roughly 40 bar).
- Cooling under pressure traps high-pressure CO2 bubbles in the hard candy; releasing the pressure shatters it into pieces.
- Saliva dissolves the sugar, releasing the bubbles with a popping/crackling sound.
