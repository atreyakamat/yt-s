## Fact check

- Classic thermal flashers used a bimetallic/heated strip that bent to open the circuit, cooled and closed again, clicking each time; later flashers used an electronic timer driving an electromechanical relay, which also clicks.
- Many modern cars switch lamps electronically (body control module) and generate the tick through a speaker (or keep a relay for sound) so the driver knows the indicator is on.
- 'Hyper-flash': many cars flash roughly twice as fast when a bulb fails, because the circuit detects the changed current. Rates are labelled 'about'/'roughly'.
