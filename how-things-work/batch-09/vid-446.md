## Fact check

- Flame failure devices (FFDs) use a thermocouple placed in the burner flame; heated, the junction of two dissimilar metals produces a few millivolts (Seebeck effect).
- This current holds a spring-loaded magnetic valve open; when the flame goes out, the thermocouple cools within seconds and the spring closes the valve.
- You hold the knob in while lighting to manually open the valve until the thermocouple is hot enough.
- FFDs are required on new gas hobs in the UK and EU and common elsewhere, though not on every hob worldwide; the description says 'many'.
