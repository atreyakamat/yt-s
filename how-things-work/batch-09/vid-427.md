## Fact check

- 'Set' is the alternate bending of saw teeth to either side; it makes the kerf wider than the blade body so the blade doesn't bind.
- Gullets are the spaces between teeth that carry sawdust out of the cut.
- Rip-saw teeth are filed square like small chisels to cut along the grain; crosscut teeth are filed to knife-like points to sever fibres across the grain.
- Saws for green/wet wood need more set; less set gives a narrower, cleaner kerf.
