## Fact check

- Thermoplastic road marking is applied molten at roughly 180-220 °C.
- Glass beads are dropped onto the hot material and embed partly; others are mixed in so new beads are exposed as the surface wears.
- Partly embedded beads act as retroreflectors: light refracts into the bead, reflects off the pigment behind, and returns toward the source, making markings visible in headlights.
- Thermoplastic road marking is applied molten at about 180 to 200 C, glass beads dropped on straight after and embedded about half way: OK. Source: https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thermoplastic_road_marking_paint
