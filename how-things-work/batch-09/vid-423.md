## Fact check

- Car bodies generate some aerodynamic lift at speed because air over the curved roof speeds up; rear lift reduces rear tyre load.
- A spoiler 'spoils' (disrupts) airflow to reduce lift and sometimes drag; a wing is an inverted aerofoil producing downforce.
- Aerodynamic forces scale with the square of speed, so doubling speed gives about four times the force and tripling gives about nine.
- At low (town) speeds the forces are small, so most road-car spoilers have little effect there.
