## Fact check

- No tank, fed from cold mains → electric showers heat cold mains water instantaneously as it flows through.
- 9-10 kW → typical UK electric showers are rated about 7.5-10.8 kW; 'around nine or ten' covers common models. A UK kettle is about 3 kW, a hair dryer about 2 kW.
- Dedicated circuit and pull-cord/isolator switch → required because of the high current.
- Temperature set by flow rate → electric showers control temperature by restricting flow; colder winter inlet water means a lower flow for the same temperature.
- UK electric showers are commonly rated 7.5 to 10.5 kW (8.5 and 9.5 kW popular), a kettle is about 3 kW: OK. Source: https://www.tritonshowers.co.uk/blog/post/9kW-9.5kW-10.5kW-Electric-Showers-Finding-your-Perfect-Match
