## Fact check

- Memory (viscoelastic polyurethane) foam was developed in 1966 by Charles Yost under a NASA contract to improve aircraft seat cushioning and crash protection.
- It entered mattresses and pillows commercially around the early 1990s.
- It is temperature-sensitive: softer when warm, stiffer when cold, and recovers slowly after compression.
- Moulding to the foot spreads load over a larger area, lowering peak pressure: basic mechanics; no medical claim made.
