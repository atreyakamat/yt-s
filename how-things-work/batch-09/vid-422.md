## Fact check

- IEC 60417 symbols: a line = on (power), a circle = off, a line within a broken circle = standby/power toggle, a line in a full circle = push on/push off.
- The line/circle originate from 1/0 used for on/off on switches; the IEC standardised the symbols in the 1970s (IEC 60417, 1973), and binary on/off use dates back to around WWII.
- Standby means the device may still draw a small amount of power.
- The power symbol (1 and 0) appeared in the 1973 edition of IEC 417 / now IEC 60417; the 1-and-0 reading is the popular interpretation: OK. Source: https://99percentinvisible.org/article/modern-hieroglyphics-binary-logic-behind-universal-power-symbol/
