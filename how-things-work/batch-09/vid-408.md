## Fact check

- Ultrasonic parking sensors use a piezoelectric transducer that both emits and receives ultrasound (typically around 40 kHz, above the ~20 kHz limit of human hearing).
- Distance is found from echo time of flight; the speed of sound in air is about 343 m/s at 20 °C.
- Beep rate increases as distance falls, typically ending in a continuous tone; multiple sensors are combined to locate obstacles.
