## Fact check

- The UK 50p (introduced 1969) and 20p (introduced 1982) are equilateral curve heptagons, a curve of constant width (Reuleaux polygon).
- Each side is an arc centred on the opposite vertex, so the coin's width is the same in every direction, allowing coin mechanisms to gauge it.
- The distinctive shape aids identification by touch.
- UK 50p and 20p coins are seven-sided curves of constant width (Reuleaux heptagons): OK. Source: https://www.scientificamerican.com/blog/roots-of-unity/british-objects-of-constant-width/
