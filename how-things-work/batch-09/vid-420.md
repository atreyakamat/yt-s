## Fact check

- Front-loading drums rotate on a horizontal axis; lifters (paddles/baffles) carry clothes up and let them fall through the wash water, which provides the mechanical cleaning action.
- Top-loaders traditionally move clothes with an agitator or impeller rather than tumbling.
- Drum perforations let water in and out and let water escape during the spin.
- Some machines have perforated lifters that scoop and pour water onto the load.
