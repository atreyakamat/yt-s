## Fact check

- Door shelves are exposed to room air each time the door opens and are generally the warmest, least stable zone; food-safety agencies and appliance makers advise not storing milk there.
- Temperature fluctuation shortens the shelf life of perishable dairy.
- Condiments, jams and drinks tolerate the door well.
- Raw meat is commonly advised for the bottom shelf, which is often coldest and prevents drips onto other food; the script says 'in many fridges' because layouts vary.
