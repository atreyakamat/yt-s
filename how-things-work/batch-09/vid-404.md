## Fact check

- Double-glazed units are two panes separated by a sealed cavity of dry air or gas; still gas conducts heat far less than glass.
- Argon is commonly used; it has lower thermal conductivity than air (it's denser and slower to transfer heat).
- Spacer bars contain desiccant (e.g. molecular sieve) to absorb moisture in the cavity.
- Low-emissivity coatings reflect long-wave infrared heat back into the room.
- Condensation between panes indicates a failed seal.
