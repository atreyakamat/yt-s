## Fact check

- Olbers' paradox: a static, infinitely old, homogeneous universe with infinitely many stars would give a bright night sky; Kepler posed the problem in 1610; Heinrich Olbers described it in 1823; Lord Kelvin gave the standard resolution in 1901; finite age limits visible stars; expansion redshifts distant light; light from last scattering is now about 2.73 K, microwave wavelengths. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olbers%27_paradox
