## Fact check

- Olfactory fatigue is a temporary, normal inability to distinguish an odour after prolonged exposure; the sense returns after leaving. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olfactory_fatigue
- Odorants bind receptor neurons, calcium influx activates a negative feedback loop lowering sensitivity; the process begins immediately. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olfactory_fatigue
- It can reduce awareness of chemical hazard exposure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olfactory_fatigue
