## Fact check

- Swivel casters rotate about 360 degrees; the swivel axis is offset from the axle so the wheel follows behind the direction of movement and self-aligns. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caster
- The shopping cart front casters swing to follow the turn. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caster
- Spinner suitcase wheels are the same swivel-caster principle (page does not discuss luggage specifically).
