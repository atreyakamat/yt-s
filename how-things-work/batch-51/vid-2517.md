## Fact check

- Saros is 223 synodic months, about 18 years, 11 days and 8 hours; earliest known record by Neo-Babylonian (Chaldean) astronomers in the last several centuries BCE; the extra third of a day shifts the visible region about 120 degrees west; Antikythera mechanism inscription records the saros as 223 months, dating around 150-100 BCE. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saros_(astronomy)
