## Fact check

- The Kensington Security Slot is a small, metal-reinforced hole for attaching a lock-and-cable; the original slot is 7 mm x 3 mm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kensington_Security_Slot
- A metal anchor on a cable goes in the slot and is secured with a key or combination lock; the cable's loop end wraps around a fixed object; the lock is usually a tubular pin tumbler. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kensington_Security_Slot
