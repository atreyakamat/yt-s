## Fact check

- Excavator buckets use replaceable ground-engaging teeth that concentrate force and protect the bucket from wear. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excavator
- Pressure = force divided by area, so a smaller contact area gives higher pressure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pressure
- Teeth mount on adapters and are pinned on. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excavator
- Gauge is illustrative; no number spoken.
