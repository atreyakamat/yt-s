## Fact check

- Eccrine glands produce the watery sweat tied to temperature control; evaporation of sweat from the skin has a cooling effect. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perspiration
- Sweat is mostly water with about 0.2-1% solutes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perspiration
- Average-intensity exercise can reach about 2 litres per hour; adult daily volume varies from 100 to 8,000 mL (max up to 2-4 L/h). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perspiration
- Dripping sweat does little cooling (reasoning from the evaporation mechanism).
