## Fact check

- About 15 letter designations A to O in the IEC plug guide. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AC_power_plugs_and_sockets
- Adapters only change plug shape and do not change voltage or frequency; many modern devices accept 100-240 V so only need an adapter. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AC_power_plugs_and_sockets
- Most premises worldwide get about 220-240 V; North America 120/240 V at 60 Hz. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mains_electricity_by_country
