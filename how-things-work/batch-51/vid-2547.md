## Fact check

- Freezing rain forms when snow melts in a warm layer, then the rain falls through a shallow subfreezing layer near the surface and is supercooled; part of each drop freezes on contact forming glaze; sleet freezes fully while falling as ice pellets; roughly 6.4 mm (0.25 in) of ice can cause damage (article figure, uncited). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freezing_rain
