## Fact check

- An English-language set has 100 tiles, 98 with a letter and a value of 1 to 10, plus two blanks; each tile's value based on its frequency in English; vowels, L, N, R, S, T worth 1; Q and Z worth 10. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrabble
- Alfred Mosher Butts invented it in 1931 in Jackson Heights, Queens, as Criss-Crosswords; analysed letter frequencies including from The New York Times; James Brunot bought rights in 1948 and renamed it Scrabble; 15x15 board of 225 squares. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrabble
