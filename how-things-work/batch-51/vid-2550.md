## Fact check

- The cough reflex has three phases: inhalation, forced exhalation against a closed glottis, and sudden release of air when the glottis opens. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cough
- Two classes of afferent nerves (rapidly adapting receptors and C fibres) in the lungs start the reflex. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cough
- A healthy adult coughs about 18.6 times a day on average; healthy children about 10. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cough
- Acute under 3 weeks, subacute 3 to 8 weeks, chronic over 8 weeks; in children half of cases resolve in 10 days. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cough
