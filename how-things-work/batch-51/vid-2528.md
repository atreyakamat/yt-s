## Fact check

- Some people describe the leaves as soapy, linked to variations in OR6A2; the dominant leaf flavourants are 2-decenal and 2-dodecenal; aroma comes from about six substances, most of them aldehydes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coriander
- Dislike rates listed: East Asians 21%, Europeans 17%, African descent 14%, South Asians 7%, Hispanics 4%, Middle Eastern 3%; identical twins shared a preference about 80% of the time, fraternal twins about half. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coriander
