## Fact check

- Dock levelers are adjustable ramps that bridge the gap between a loading dock and a vehicle; hydraulic types raise the deck and extend a lip onto the truck bed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dock_leveller
- Bar values are illustrative relative heights, not measurements.
