## Fact check

- Phases are not caused by Earth's shadow; the angle of the Sun relative to the Moon determines how much is illuminated; synodic month mean 29.53 days; sidereal orbital period about 27.3 days; Moon's orbit tilted about 5 degrees to the ecliptic so eclipses occur only near the nodes, roughly twice a year. Word 'month' from 'moon' is standard etymology (kept softly). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_phase
