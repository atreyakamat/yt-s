## Fact check

- Cai Lun, a Han dynasty court official, is traditionally credited with inventing paper around 105 CE (traditional attribution); traditional materials mulberry, plant bast fibres, fishing nets, cloth scraps, hemp. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papermaking
- Mesh stretched in a wooden frame called a deckle; dilute fibre suspension drained through a screen and water pressed out. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papermaking
- Papermaking reached the Islamic world in the 8th century; in 1844 Charles Fenerty and Friedrich Gottlob Keller developed wood pulp paper, ending nearly 2,000 years of reliance on pulped rags. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papermaking
