## Fact check

- Laminated glass is two or more glass layers bonded around polymer interlayers (commonly PVB); on breaking, the interlayer holds the fragments in place, giving a spider-web crack pattern. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laminated_glass
- The layers are combined under heat and pressure in an autoclave; the interlayer absorbs impact energy; laminated glass is the most common material for automobile windshields. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laminated_glass
