## Fact check

- HDR imaging merges multiple exposures to capture a wider range of brightness than one shot can. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-dynamic-range_imaging
- Phones use computational photography with burst frames and alignment. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_photography
- Moving subjects cause ghosting in merged exposures. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-dynamic-range_imaging
