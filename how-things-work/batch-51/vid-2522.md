## Fact check

- Pumped-storage hydroelectricity moves water between two reservoirs at different heights to store and release energy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumped-storage_hydroelectricity
- Round-trip efficiency is typically about 70 to 80 percent. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumped-storage_hydroelectricity
- Reversible pump-turbines can work both ways. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumped-storage_hydroelectricity
- It is the largest form of grid energy storage by capacity. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumped-storage_hydroelectricity
- Verified: round-trip efficiency 70-80%; pumped storage is by far the largest-capacity grid storage. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumped-storage_hydroelectricity
