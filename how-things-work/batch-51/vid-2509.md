## Fact check

- Quenching hardens steel via martensite transformation; steel must cool rapidly through the eutectoid point (727 C); typical heating 815-900 C; rapid cooling traps carbon atoms. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quenching
- Water is among the most efficient quenchants for maximum hardness but carries risk of distortion and cracking; mineral oil has a much lower cooling rate. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quenching
- Quenched alloys often too hard and brittle; tempering reheats below the critical point to increase toughness. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quenching
