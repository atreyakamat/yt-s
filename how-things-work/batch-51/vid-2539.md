## Fact check

- Bayer and Diaconis (1992): a deck is truly random after seven riffle shuffles, begins to become random after five; Trefethen and Trefethen (2000) argue six suffice under a different definition. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuffling
- A 52-card deck has 52! orderings, about 8.0658 x 10^67 (script: 'eight followed by sixty-seven zeros' is a simplification of 8.07e67). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuffling
- Eight perfect faro shuffles restore a 52-card deck if the original top and bottom cards stay in place. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuffling
