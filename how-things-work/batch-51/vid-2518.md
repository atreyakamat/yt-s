## Fact check

- Robert Matthews published 'Tumbling toast, Murphy's Law and the Fundamental Constants' (European Journal of Physics, 1995) and won the 1996 Ig Nobel Prize for physics. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murphy%27s_law
- Typical tables are 2-6 ft (0.7-1.83 m) tall; toast has time to rotate about half a turn and lands upside down relative to its start; from over about 3 m it rotates a full 360 degrees and lands butter side up. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buttered_toast_phenomenon
- Fall time under a second: from 1.8 m, t = sqrt(2h/g) = about 0.6 s (arithmetic from the table height above).
