## Fact check

- Bags should be about one-half to two-thirds full; overfilled bags don't seal against the ground. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandbag
- Lay bags overlapping, with each higher layer offset by half a bag length, and tamp each one. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandbag
- Sandbag structures do not prevent seepage; they divert floodwater. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandbag
