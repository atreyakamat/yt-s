## Fact check

- ICAO standard atmosphere lapse rate is 6.50 C per km from sea level to 11 km; dry adiabatic lapse rate about 9.8 C per km; rising air expands and cools, doing work without gaining heat; ground heated by sun warms the air above it. 3 km is roughly 19.5 C at the standard rate (script says about 20). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lapse_rate
