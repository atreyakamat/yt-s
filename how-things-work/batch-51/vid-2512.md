## Fact check

- Grid frequency must be kept close to nominal (50 or 60 Hz); loss of generation lowers it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utility_frequency
- Battery storage systems can respond to frequency deviations in a fraction of a second, faster than conventional plant. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battery_storage_power_station
- Gas turbine plants take minutes to ramp to output. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peaking_power_plant
- Battery inverters switch between charge and discharge electronically. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battery_storage_power_station
