## Fact check

- Dump truck beds are raised by a hydraulic ram under the front of the body (front post hoist) or an underbody hoist; tailgates swing open on top hinges. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dump_truck
- Motorised dump trucks appeared in the US around 1910, with hydraulic beds following soon after. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dump_truck
