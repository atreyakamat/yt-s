## Fact check

- Cornstarch thickens soups, sauces and gravies; usually mixed with a cool liquid first to make a slurry; heated above 203 F (95 C) the molecular chains form a mesh that thickens the liquid; continued boiling breaks the molecules down and thins it again. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornstarch
- Adding water turns cornstarch into oobleck, a non-Newtonian fluid; it is starch from the endosperm of the corn kernel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornstarch
