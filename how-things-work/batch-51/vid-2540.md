## Fact check

- Muscle memory is a form of procedural memory formed when a movement is repeated until performed with little conscious effort; the exact storage location is not known. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_memory
- The cerebellum is the main area for motor learning; basal ganglia support habit formation; motor and somatosensory cortex activity decreases once a skill is learned. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_memory
- A myonuclei-based muscle memory was long believed, but current studies show myonuclei are lost during detraining. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_memory
- In an early typing study, relearning showed savings after a 25-year period without practice. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_memory
