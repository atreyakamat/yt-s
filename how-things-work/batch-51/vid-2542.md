## Fact check

- Tidal stream generators extract energy from tidal currents using turbines similar to wind turbines. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidal_stream_generator
- Seawater is about 800 times denser than air (roughly 1,025 kg/m3 vs 1.2 kg/m3). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seawater
- Tidal flows are predictable far in advance. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidal_power
- Bar values are order of magnitude, not exact.
- Verified: water is about 800 times the density of air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tidal_stream_generator
