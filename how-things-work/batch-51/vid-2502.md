## Fact check

- Geothermal power plants use steam produced from hot water reservoirs to drive turbines. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geothermal_power
- Flash steam plants let hot pressurised water flash to steam as pressure drops. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geothermal_power
- Used fluid is typically reinjected into the reservoir. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geothermal_power
