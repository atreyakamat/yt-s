## Fact check

- Rennet is a set of enzymes from the stomachs of ruminants; calf rennet comes from the fourth stomach of a young calf; chymosin cleaves kappa-casein and removes the negatively charged glycomacropeptide so micelles cluster into a curd trapping fats. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rennet
- About 1 g of traditional extract could coagulate 2 to 4 litres of milk (3 litres shown as a midpoint). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rennet
- Fermentation-produced chymosin was the first artificially produced enzyme registered by the US FDA, in 1990; it held 90% of the global rennet market in 2017. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rennet
- Homer's Iliad suggests the Greeks used fig juice to coagulate milk. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rennet
