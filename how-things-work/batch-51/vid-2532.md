## Fact check

- Edmond Becquerel observed the photovoltaic effect in 1839. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photovoltaic_effect
- Selenium photovoltaic cells in the 1880s were very inefficient (around 1 percent). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_cell
- Bell Labs demonstrated the first practical silicon solar cell in 1954, about 6 percent efficient. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_cell
- Early satellites such as Vanguard 1 (1958) used solar cells. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanguard_1
- Gauge is illustrative, not measured.
- Verified: Becquerel 1839; Fritts selenium cell 1883 (~1%); Bell Labs 1954 silicon cell about 6%. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_cell , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Fritts , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daryl_Chapin
