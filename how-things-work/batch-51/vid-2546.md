## Fact check

- Blue lights normally indicate the edge of the taxiway, and green lights are located in its center. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airport_lighting
- Threshold and end lights are green toward approaching aircraft and red from the runway; runway edge lights are white. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway
- Runway lights controlled by towers: not stated in script.
