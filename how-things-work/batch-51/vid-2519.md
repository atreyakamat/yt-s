## Fact check

- Patent application for the modern brick design filed in Denmark on 28 January 1958; 1958 bricks still interlock with current bricks; early Automatic Binding Bricks produced from 1949; company took the name Lego in 1934. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego
- Brick-making machines have tolerances as small as 10 micrometres (0.01 mm). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego
- Six 2x4 bricks can be combined in 915,103,765 ways. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego
