## Fact check

- Searing seals in the juices is a widespread myth; searing causes greater moisture loss than cooking to the same internal temperature without it; 1930s experiments found seared roasts lost the same or more; the idea was proposed by Justus von Liebig around 1850. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Searing
- The surface must exceed 150 C for a brown crust, so it must be free of water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Searing
- Searing destroys more cells, so more liquid is released. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Searing
