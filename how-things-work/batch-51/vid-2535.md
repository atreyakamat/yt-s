## Fact check

- Telephone voice band is about 300 to 3,400 Hz. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voice_frequency
- HD Voice (wideband AMR-WB) widens the audio range to roughly 50 to 7,000 Hz. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HD_Voice
- HD Voice requires support from both handsets and networks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HD_Voice
- Narrow bandwidth blurs high-frequency consonants like s and f. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voice_frequency
