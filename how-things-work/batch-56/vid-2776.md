## Fact check

- A pool noodle is a cylindrical piece of flexible, buoyant polyethylene foam. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pool_noodle
- Typical size about 160 cm long and 7 cm in diameter; uses include swimming aid, rescue reaching, and protecting sharp edges. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pool_noodle
- Generally credited to Steve Hartman of Industrial Thermal Polymers in Toronto in the early 1980s; no patent. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pool_noodle
- Foam floats because it is full of trapped air (low density): standard physics. The article does not explain why there is a central hole, so the script does not claim a reason.
