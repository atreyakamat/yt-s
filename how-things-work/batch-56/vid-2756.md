## Fact check

- Trays must be stowed for takeoff and landing; in most economy seats the tray folds down from the seatback; in most first, business, bulkhead and exit-row seats it folds out from the armrest. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airline_seat
- Reason (clear aisles and exits, loose items in a sudden stop): airline safety guidance, stated generally; no number.
