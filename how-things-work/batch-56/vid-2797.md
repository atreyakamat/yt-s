## Fact check

- A glory is concentric rings of colour around the shadow of the observer's head on cloud or mist, from sunlight interacting with tiny water droplets through wave interference; each ring red outside and bluish inside; inner brightest ring spans about 5 to 20 degrees; centred on the antisolar point, so each person sees the glory around their own head's shadow; seen from aircraft around the plane's shadow; Brocken spectre named for the Brocken in Germany's Harz mountains. Rainbow main bow about 42 degrees per the Rainbow article; glory bar uses a mid value of 12 degrees from the 5-20 range. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glory_(optical_phenomenon)
- Primary rainbow about 42 degrees. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainbow
