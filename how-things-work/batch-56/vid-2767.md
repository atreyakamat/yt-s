## Fact check

- Moon's mean distance about 384,400 km (semi-major axis 384,399 km); distance varies between roughly 356,400 km (perigee) and 406,700 km (apogee); light travel time about 1.28 seconds. Speed of light about 299,792 km/s (standard constant). Round trip about 2.5 s is double 1.28 s. Thirty Earths across: Earth's diameter about 12,742 km, 384,400 / 12,742 is about 30 (derived). Blink lasts about 100-400 ms per the Blinking article. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon
- Blink: 100 to 400 ms (Harvard database). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blinking
