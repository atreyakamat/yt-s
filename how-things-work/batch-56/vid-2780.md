## Fact check

- Lip skin has only three to five cell layers versus up to sixteen on typical facial skin; light-skinned lips have fewer melanocytes so blood vessels show through; darker skin's melanin makes this less visible. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lip
- Lip skin has no hair and no sweat glands, so lips dry out faster and chap more easily; they have many nerve endings and a large area of the sensory cortex. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lip
