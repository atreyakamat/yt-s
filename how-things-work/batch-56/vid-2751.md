## Fact check

- A wheel chock is a wedge placed against a wheel to stop unintentional movement; it supplements the brakes; common on aircraft, rail cars, trucks being loaded; regulators require chocks in some settings (e.g. heavy trucks being loaded, vehicles parked on a grade in mining). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wheel_chock
- Chock on the downhill side: basic geometry of a rolling wheel and a wedge, reasoning only.
