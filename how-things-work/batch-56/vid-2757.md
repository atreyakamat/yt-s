## Fact check

- Stellar classification O, B, A, F, G, K, M from hottest to coolest; class O at least 33,000 K blue; class M 2,300-3,900 K light orangish red; Sun is G2V with surface temperature around 5,800 K; Rigel is B8Ia, a B-class supergiant; Betelgeuse is M1-M2Ia-Iab, an M-class supergiant; hotter stars bluer, cooler stars redder. 'Red stars about 3,000 K' bar is a mid-range example of class M. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellar_classification
