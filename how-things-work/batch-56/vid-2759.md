## Fact check

- Crochet uses one hook; usually one live stitch on the hook (Tunisian crochet excepted) while a knitter keeps a row active; each stitch finished before the next begins. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crochet
- Word from French crochet, diminutive of croche, from Germanic croc, both meaning hook. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crochet
- Aluminium, bamboo and plastic hooks range 2.25 to 30 mm; steel hooks 0.9 to 2.7 mm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crochet
- First published modern instructions in Dutch magazine Penelope, 1823 (source volume dated 1824); crochet lace introduced as famine relief during the Great Irish Famine (1845-1849); first Irish crochet pattern book 1846. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crochet
