## Fact check

- Donald Bailey, a British War Office civil servant, sketched the design around 1940; first wartime use in North Africa in 1942. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bailey_bridge
- Panels are joined with pins and need no special tools or heavy equipment; a 10-foot panel weighs about 570 lb and can be lifted by four men. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bailey_bridge
- Allied forces erected thousands in Sicily and Italy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bailey_bridge
