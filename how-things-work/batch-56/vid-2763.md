## Fact check

- Each key is attached to a typebar with a reversed letter at its tip; striking a key sends the typebar against an inked ribbon, which marks the paper wrapped around the platen. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typewriter
- A small bell rang a few characters before the right margin to warn the typist to use the carriage-return lever. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typewriter
- Christopher Latham Sholes and partners patented the first commercially successful design in 1868; Remington began producing it on March 1, 1873. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typewriter
- The shift key was introduced on the Remington No. 2 in 1878, so each typebar could produce two characters. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typewriter
