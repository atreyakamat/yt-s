## Fact check

- Trench shields (boxes) protect workers if a wall collapses; shoring is designed to prevent collapse. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trench_shoring
- Some shoring must be designed or approved by an engineer and installed by trained personnel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trench_shoring
- Heavy soil can trap a person: widely documented trench-collapse hazard. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trench_shoring
