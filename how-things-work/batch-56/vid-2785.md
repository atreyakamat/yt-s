## Fact check

- Caller ID spoofing is easy because networks traditionally trusted the claimed number. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caller_ID_spoofing
- STIR/SHAKEN uses digital certificates to sign and verify caller identity on calls. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STIR/SHAKEN
- It works only where all carriers in the call path support it (script says every network). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STIR/SHAKEN
