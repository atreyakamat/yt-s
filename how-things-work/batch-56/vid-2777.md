## Fact check

- The Norwegian cyclone model, introduced in the late 1910s, first brought frontal zones onto weather maps; Loomis attempted a similar idea in 1841; the term 'front' came from the military fronts of World War I, since air mass boundaries resembled them; the US did not formally analyse fronts on surface maps until late 1942 when the WBAN Analysis Center opened in Washington, D.C. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_weather_analysis
