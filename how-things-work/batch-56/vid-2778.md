## Fact check

- Sodium bicarbonate reacts with acids to release carbon dioxide; baking powder contains about 30% bicarbonate; it begins to decompose at about 80-100 C. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodium_bicarbonate
- Modern baking powder: sodium bicarbonate plus acids (monocalcium phosphate, sodium aluminium sulfate, sodium acid pyrophosphate or cream of tartar) and a starch desiccant; double-acting powder has a fast-acting and a slow-acting acid. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baking_powder
