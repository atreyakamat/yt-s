## Fact check

- Polar jet streams are typically near 9.1 km altitude (30,000 ft), typically about 180 km/h and speeds of 400 km/h have been measured; westerly flow; a few hundred km wide; formed by temperature differences between air masses with the Earth's rotation (Coriolis) deflecting the flow; Pan Am's first commercial use in 1952 cut Tokyo to Honolulu from 18 to 11.5 hours; in late February 2024 unusually strong winds pushed commercial jets to over 800 mph (1,300 km/h) relative to the ground. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_stream
