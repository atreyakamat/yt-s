## Fact check

- Since the 13th century the Inuit made goggles from flattened walrus or caribou ivory with narrow slits that blocked most harmful reflected sunlight. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunglasses
- Pliny the Elder claimed Nero watched gladiator fights through cut emeralds, which likely worked more like mirrors than filters. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunglasses
- Ai Tai: flat panes of smoky quartz used in China in the 12th century or possibly earlier, no corrective power, reduced glare; judges reportedly used them to conceal expressions. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunglasses
- Sam Foster sold inexpensive celluloid sunglasses on the Atlantic City boardwalk in 1929; Bausch & Lomb introduced the aviator design in 1936 for US military aviators; polarised sunglasses first available in 1936. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunglasses
