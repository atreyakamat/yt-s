## Fact check

- Platelets adhere to damaged vessel lining within seconds and release chemicals attracting more platelets, forming a plug. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemostasis
- Fibrin strands begin forming after approximately sixty seconds; trapped red and white blood cells strengthen the plug into a clot; about a dozen proteins activate in sequence. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemostasis
- A scab is a hard coating on the skin formed during the wound-healing reconstruction phase. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scab
