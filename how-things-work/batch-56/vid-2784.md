## Fact check

- Shock sensors are the most common aftermarket sensor, detecting vibration from bumps or breaking glass; tilt sensors detect towing; sound or glass-break and motion sensors are also used. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Car_alarm
- High sensitivity settings are a common cause of false alarms; alarms may flash the headlights. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Car_alarm
