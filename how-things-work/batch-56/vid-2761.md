## Fact check

- Boom pumps use double-piston hydraulic pumps; while one piston draws concrete from the hopper the other pushes it out; a transfer tube swings between cylinders at the end of each stroke. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete_pump
- SOFTENED from 'began': Max Giese and Fritz Hull came upon the idea of pumping concrete through pipes in 1927; a Dutch patent in 1932. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete_pump
- Pumping saves moving concrete by wheelbarrow or crane bucket. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete_pump
