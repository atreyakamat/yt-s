## Fact check

- IP Code defined by IEC 60529: IPX7 protects against immersion up to 1 m for 30 minutes; IPX8 covers continuous immersion at 1 m or deeper, with depth and conditions set by the manufacturer. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IP_Code
- Standard tests use fresh water; protection against salt water, oils or solvents requires separate testing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IP_Code
- Bag A / Bag B depths in the compare visual are illustrative examples of two different IPX8 claims.
