## Fact check

- Most common bells use a thumb-operated lever that spins two loosely hung metal discs inside the housing, which rattle against the bell; simple ping bells use a spring-mounted external clapper. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_bell
- Patents for bicycle bells appear as early as 1877 (English patent dated June 22, 1877). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_bell
- UK: bells on new bikes were compulsory until 1983 and again since 1 May 2011. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_bell
- New Jersey: an audible device must be heard at least 100 feet away. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_bell
