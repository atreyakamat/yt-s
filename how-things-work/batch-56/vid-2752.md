## Fact check

- An automatic transfer switch senses loss of the normal supply, starts the generator, then transfers load. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transfer_switch
- Emergency power systems commonly aim to supply load within about 10 seconds of a failure (e.g., NFPA 110 Type 10). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergency_power_system
- Transfer switches prevent backfeeding of the grid. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transfer_switch
