## Fact check

- A liquid on a surface much hotter than its boiling point forms a vapour cushion; first described by Johann Gottlob Leidenfrost in 1756; the Leidenfrost point for water is about 193 C. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leidenfrost_effect
- A drop that vaporised almost immediately at 168 C lasted 152 seconds at 202 C (Fairbairn's account); steam conducts heat far worse than metal. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leidenfrost_effect
- Water sprinkled on a pan at or above about 193 C bunches into balls, skitters and evaporates more slowly. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leidenfrost_effect
