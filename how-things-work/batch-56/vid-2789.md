## Fact check

- Warp: longitudinal threads held taut; weft: lateral threads crossing at right angles; heddle lifts and lowers warp threads forming the shed through which the weft passes; three basic weaves: plain, twill, satin. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weaving
- Some indications of weaving about 27,000 years ago (indistinct textile impression at Dolni Vestonice); firmer evidence at Guitarrero Cave, Peru (10,100-9080 BCE). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weaving
- John Kay invented the flying shuttle in 1733; Edmund Cartwright first proposed a weaving machine in 1784. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weaving
