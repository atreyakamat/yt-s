## Fact check

- Aeromarine Airways screened Howdy Chicago in 1921 aboard a Felixstowe F.5 flying boat flying around Chicago. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In-flight_entertainment
- Regularly scheduled in-flight movies began in 1961, when TWA showed By Love Possessed to first-class passengers on 19 July 1961. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In-flight_entertainment
- Projector at front and screen: general description of early cabin movie set-ups; seat-back screens and Wi-Fi streaming later: general history.
