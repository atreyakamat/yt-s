## Fact check

- Lossy compression such as JPEG discards detail that cannot be restored. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lossy_compression
- Repeated recompression causes generation loss. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generation_loss
- Messaging apps commonly resize and recompress photos; sending as a file or document keeps the original. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instant_messaging
- Bar values (5 MB vs 1 MB) are illustrative of the shrink, not measurements.
