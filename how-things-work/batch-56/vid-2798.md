## Fact check

- Hokkien kôe-chiap and kê-chiap meant pickled fish or shellfish brine (Carstairs Douglas, 1873 dictionary). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ketchup
- 1682: earliest print appearance of ketchup; 1690: printed as catchup; British ketchup in the 1600s was often made with mushrooms. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ketchup
- 1812: James Mease published the first known tomato ketchup recipe; 1837: Jonas Yerkes had distributed tomato ketchup nationally; 1909: Katherine Bitting showed more sugar and vinegar would prevent spoilage without preservatives. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ketchup
