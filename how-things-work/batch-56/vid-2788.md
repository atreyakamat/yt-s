## Fact check

- Bromelain is a mixture of enzymes extracted from pineapple, mainly from the stem; its main enzymes are proteolytic and break down proteins; it is one of the most widely used tenderisers alongside papain, sold as a powder mixed into marinades or sprinkled on raw meat. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromelain
- The enzymes are heat-labile; cooked or canned pineapple does not tenderise meat. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromelain
- Meat is mostly protein (general knowledge).
