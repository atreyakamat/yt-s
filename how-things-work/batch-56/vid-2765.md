## Fact check

- Accelerometers measure acceleration including gravity, which lets phones detect orientation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accelerometer
- Screen orientation switches when the sensed gravity direction changes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_rotation
- With the phone flat, gravity is perpendicular to the screen (reasoning, no figure).
