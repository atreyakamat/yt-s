## Fact check

- A safe deposit box is an individually secured container, usually held inside a larger safe or bank vault. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safe_deposit_box
- Dual-key operation: a renter key plus a bank guard key, both needed at once; banks may open boxes without the renter only in limited cases (e.g. court order), so the script says 'alone'. Sources: https://www.pnc.com/insights/personal-finance/protect/what-is-a-safe-deposit-box.html and https://www.locksmithledger.com/locks/article/10222849/opportunities-in-safe-deposit-boxes
