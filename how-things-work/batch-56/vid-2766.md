## Fact check

- DEET developed in 1944 by USDA's Samuel Gertler for the US Army; military use began in 1946 and civilian use in 1957. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DEET
- Thought to work by confusing or inhibiting mosquito odorant receptors from a distance and repelling insects that touch it, partly because it tastes bitter. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DEET
- Concentrations above 50% do not extend protection time; the CDC recommends 30-50% and the NHS 50% for travel to malaria areas. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DEET
