## Fact check

- The General Postal Union was founded by the Treaty of Bern in 1874 and renamed the Universal Postal Union in 1878. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Postal_Union
- Before the UPU, postal rates and rules between countries were set by bilateral treaties, and mail often needed stamps or accounting for each country crossed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Postal_Union
- Nearly all countries are UPU members (192 or so; script says 'almost every'). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Postal_Union
- Verified: Treaty of Bern 9 Oct 1874 created the General Postal Union, renamed UPU in 1878; 192 members. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Postal_Union
