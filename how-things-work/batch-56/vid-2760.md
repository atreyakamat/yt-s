## Fact check

- A cramp is a sudden, painful, involuntary contraction of skeletal muscle; nocturnal cramps often occur in calves and soles and usually end within seconds or minutes; soreness may remain. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_cramp
- The precise cause of nocturnal cramps is unclear; dehydration and low minerals are possible contributors but evidence is mixed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_cramp
- Gentle stretching, massage, putting weight on the leg or a warm bath may help; for calf cramps, flexing the foot toward the shin gives almost immediate relief. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_cramp
