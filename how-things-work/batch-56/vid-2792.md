## Fact check

- Radiators transfer heat by convection and thermal radiation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiator_(heating)
- Reflective surfaces have low emissivity and reflect infrared radiation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiant_barrier
- Reflective foil's benefit is modest and greatest for poorly insulated external walls. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiant_barrier
- Gauge is illustrative.
