## Fact check

- Order: dead battery positive, charged battery positive, charged battery negative, then the dead vehicle's negative to bare metal away from its battery; disconnect in reverse order. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jump_start_(vehicle)
- The final connection goes to bare metal away from the battery to keep sparks away from hydrogen the battery may release. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jump_start_(vehicle)
- If the charged battery's loose end touches bare metal the charged battery is shorted. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jump_start_(vehicle)
- Lead-acid jump starters are rated at 300-1700 amperes; do not connect 6-volt and 12-volt systems together. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jump_start_(vehicle)
