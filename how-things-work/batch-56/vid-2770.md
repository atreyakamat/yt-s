## Fact check

- Heart sounds mostly come from heart valves closing; S1 is the mitral and tricuspid valves closing at the start of ventricular contraction, S2 the aortic and pulmonary valves closing at its end. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_sounds
- The S2 split is most noticeable during inhalation; murmurs are whooshing sounds from turbulent flow, harmless or pathological, graded 1 to 6. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_sounds
