## Fact check

- Modern spinners use a central ball bearing, small metal balls rolling between inner and outer race for low resistance; weighted lobes far from the centre increase the moment of inertia; speed decreases following an exponential decay. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidget_spinner
- Catherine Hettinger filed in May 1993; patent issued January 7, 1997; lapsed in 2005; spinners very popular in 2017. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fidget_spinner
