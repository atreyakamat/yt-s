## Fact check

- Mozi and Lu Ban, 5th-century BC philosophers, credited with flying wooden kites (muyuan); paper kites followed paper's invention in the Han dynasty; by 549 AD a kite reportedly used to send a message. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kite
- Lift from air flow over the wings, drag horizontally in the wind direction; combined force balanced by tension in the line. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kite
- In 1752 Benjamin Franklin published an account of a kite experiment to show lightning is electricity. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kite
- On 23 September 2014 a team led by Robert Moore flew a kite to 16,009 ft (4,880 m) above ground level in New South Wales. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kite
