## Fact check

- Self-adhesive labels consist of a face material, a pressure-sensitive adhesive and a release liner coated with silicone. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Label
- Release liners are coated with silicone so adhesive does not bond strongly to them. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Release_liner
- Pressure-sensitive adhesives bond with light pressure and need no solvent, water or heat. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pressure-sensitive_adhesive
- Gauge value is illustrative.
