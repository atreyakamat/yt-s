## Fact check

- The Archimedes screw lifts water; run in reverse it works as a hydropower turbine for low heads. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedes%27_screw
- Screw turbines suit low head sites (a few metres) and slow flow. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedes%27_screw
- They are marketed as fish-friendly because of slow rotation and wide gaps. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedes%27_screw
