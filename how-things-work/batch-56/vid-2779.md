## Fact check

- Sections are groups of folded bifolia; sewing through the fold (Smyth sewing) is called the gold standard; oversewing uses lockstitches through punched holes and gives narrower margins. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bookbinding
- Codex invented in the Roman Empire in the 1st century AD, described by Martial; largely replaced wax tablets and scrolls by 300 AD; completely replaced in the Western world by the 6th century; Diamond Sutra (868 CE) earliest dated printed book. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bookbinding
