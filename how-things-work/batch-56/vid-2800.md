## Fact check

- The navel is a scar on the abdomen where the umbilical cord was attached; it can be protruding, flat or hollowed; an outie protrudes past surrounding skin, an innie is concave; some are distorted. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navel
- The navel is used to divide the abdomen into quadrants and sits at the T10 dermatome, typically at the junction of the L3 and L4 vertebrae. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navel
