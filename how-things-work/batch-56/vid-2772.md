## Fact check

- Michael Faraday discovered electromagnetic induction in 1831. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faraday%27s_law_of_induction
- Faraday's disc of 1831 was the first electric generator; practical self-excited dynamos followed in the 1860s (Siemens, Wheatstone, Varley). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamo
- Before this, electricity came mainly from electrostatic machines and chemical batteries. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_electricity
- Gauge is illustrative.
- Verified: Faraday's 1831 work showed a magnet moved through a loop of wire drives a current. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Faraday
