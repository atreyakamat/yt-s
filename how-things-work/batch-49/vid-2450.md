## Fact check

- In puberty the larynx enlarges in both sexes; male vocal folds become longer and thicker; male voice deepens by about one octave, female voice by a few semitones. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puberphonia
- Typical adult fundamental frequency about 85-180 Hz for males and 165-255 Hz for females. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puberphonia
- Longer, thicker folds vibrate more slowly (physics reasoning), and a changing instrument is harder to control (reasoning, script says 'can slip'); the crack mechanism itself is not claimed as measured.
