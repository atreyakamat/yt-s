## Fact check

- Impacts work more like explosions than mechanical displacement, so craters are nearly always circular; only very low-angle impacts make significantly elliptical craters. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_crater
- Impact speeds on Earth range from about 11 to 72 km/s, median about 20 km/s; South Pole-Aitken basin about 2,500 km in diameter. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_crater
