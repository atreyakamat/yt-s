## Fact check

- Boeing entered in-flight connectivity in 2000-2001 with Connexion and closed it in 2006; its hardware weighed about 1,000 pounds (450 kg). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In-flight_entertainment
- Satellite-based systems (Row 44, OnAir, AeroMobile) and air-to-ground cellular (Aircell) followed; Starlink and OneWeb low-Earth-orbit satellites emerged in the 2020s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In-flight_entertainment
- An adult person at about 80 kg is a typical illustration figure, not a statistic.
