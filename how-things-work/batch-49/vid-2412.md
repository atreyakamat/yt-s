## Fact check

- Arch dams curve upstream and transfer water load to the valley abutments through compression. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arch_dam
- Gravity dams resist water by their own weight and so need much more material. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity_dam
- Concrete is strong in compression and weak in tension. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
- Arch dams need a suitable narrow site with strong rock walls. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arch_dam
- Bar values are illustrative, not measured.
