## Fact check

- Solar water heaters use a collector to absorb sunlight and heat water or fluid. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_water_heating
- Thermosiphon systems circulate water by natural convection without pumps. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_water_heating
- Evacuated tube collectors use a vacuum to reduce heat loss. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evacuated_tube_collector
