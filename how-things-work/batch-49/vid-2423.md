## Fact check

- Packing peanuts are made of expanded polystyrene or starch-based biodegradable materials; starch ones dissolve in water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packing_peanut
- Expanded polystyrene is mostly air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polystyrene
- Disposal rules vary by area; script says check local rules (no advice beyond that).
- Verified: peanuts are commonly expanded polystyrene foam (about 3 g per litre); starch-based ones exist, e.g. corn or sorghum. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packing_peanut
