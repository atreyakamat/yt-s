## Fact check

- Pneumatic tube systems move capsules by air pressure or vacuum. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumatic_tube
- Paris pneumatic post began in 1866 and closed in 1984. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumatic_post
- New York City's pneumatic mail system opened in 1897 (ran until 1953). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumatic_tube
- Hospitals still use pneumatic tubes to send samples. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumatic_tube
- 'Minutes across a city' is qualitative; gauge value is illustrative.
- Verified: Paris network 1866 to 1984; New York mail tubes began 15 October 1897. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumatic_post and https://about.usps.com/who-we-are/postal-history/pneumatic-tubes.pdf
