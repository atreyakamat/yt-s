## Fact check

- A one-way mirror is a partially reflective, partially transparent pane; it appears as a mirror from the brightly lit side and as a window from the dark side, because the transmitted light from the bright side outweighs the faint reflection on the dark side. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-way_mirror
- Swapping the lighting swaps which side acts as a window. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One-way_mirror
