## Fact check

- Green flash usually lasts no more than about two seconds; atmosphere refracts light by wavelength, blue is scattered, leaving green; needs an unobstructed horizon, stable clear air, mirages enhance it; occurs at sunrise too; Joule 1869 letter described the last glimpse as bluish green; Jules Verne's The Green Ray 1882; first colour photograph D.J.K. O'Connell 1960 at Vatican Observatory. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_flash
