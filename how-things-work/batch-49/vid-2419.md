## Fact check

- A computer-controlled sharp blade moves over the material like a printer's nozzles; blade moves along X and Y axes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinyl_cutter
- Kiss cutting: cutting the vinyl without penetrating the release liner; depth typically does not exceed 1 mm; too shallow vinyl won't separate, too deep cuts the backing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinyl_cutter
- Adhesive vinyl comes attached to a release liner; excess is weeded; transfer tape moves design. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinyl_cutter
