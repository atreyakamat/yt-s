## Fact check

- Sea has greater heat capacity than land so warms slower; sea breeze blows from water to land by day, land breeze reverses at night; sea breezes typically extend 10 to 100 km inland and 1 to 2 km deep; starts after sunrise, dissipates after sunset. 'Strongest mid-afternoon' is from the article's 'afternoon wind'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_breeze
