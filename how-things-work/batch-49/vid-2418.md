## Fact check

- There are 310 specific pasta forms known by over 1,300 documented names. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta
- Dried pasta is semolina and water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasta
- Rigatoni are always grooved; penne are ridged tubes; conchiglie are shell-shaped, usually furrowed; paccheri are often topped with sauce or stuffed; radiatori suit thicker sauces; small pastas are mainly used in soups. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_pasta
