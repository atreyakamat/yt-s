## Fact check

- The pinna funnels sound, with roughly 10 to 15 dB amplification (including the middle ear) in the 1.5 to 7 kHz range. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auricle_(anatomy)
- The pinna creates a notch around 10 kHz (range 6 to 16 kHz), stronger for sounds from above, aiding vertical localisation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auricle_(anatomy)
- It begins forming around the sixth week of gestation from auricular hillocks; pinnae are common in mammals and absent in reptiles, amphibians and birds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auricle_(anatomy)
