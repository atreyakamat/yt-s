## Fact check

- Piezoelectric buzzers use a disc of piezoelectric ceramic that flexes when a changing voltage is applied; the sound is boosted by a resonant cavity. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piezoelectric_speaker
- Personal alarms are small devices that make a loud noise to attract attention. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_alarm
