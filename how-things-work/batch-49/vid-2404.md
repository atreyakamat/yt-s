## Fact check

- Tamper-evident labels and tapes show attempts to open a package; some use a pattern or text that transfers to the surface when peeled. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamper-evident_technology
- Layered construction with a partial release coating is the standard 'void' label design: mechanism description, no figures used.
