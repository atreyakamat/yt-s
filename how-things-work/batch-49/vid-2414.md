## Fact check

- The Egyptian wooden lock had a bolt, a door fixture and a key; the key raised pins out of holes in the bolt, and removing it let the pins drop back in. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lock_and_key
- The pin tumbler lock descends from the ancient Egyptian pin lock. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pin_tumbler_lock
