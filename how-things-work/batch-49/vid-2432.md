## Fact check

- A UPS provides emergency power from a battery when mains fails; standby units switch in a few milliseconds, online units have no transfer time. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uninterruptible_power_supply
- Computer power supplies use capacitors that provide hold-up time of a few tens of milliseconds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_supply_unit_(computer)
- Inverters convert DC from batteries to AC. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_inverter
- Standby UPS switch-over "can be as long as 25 milliseconds"; online units have no transfer time; typical runtime 5-15 minutes (counter changed from ~10 ms to under 25 ms). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uninterruptible_power_supply
