## Fact check

- Storm glass is a sealed glass with liquid in which crystals were believed to form in response to coming weather; Robert FitzRoy popularised it in the 1860s; usual contents distilled water, ethanol, potassium nitrate, ammonium chloride and camphor; little value for weather prediction; Charles Tomlinson in 1863 called it a 'crude kind of thermoscope'; a 2008 Journal of Crystal Growth article concluded temperature change alone drives crystal growth. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storm_glass
- FitzRoy appointed in 1854 to head the department that became the Met Office. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_FitzRoy
