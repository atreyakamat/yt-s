## Fact check

- Two main yoghurt bacteria: Lactobacillus delbrueckii subsp. bulgaricus and Streptococcus thermophilus; they ferment milk sugars into lactic acid, which acts on milk protein to give texture. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogurt
- Fermentation at 30-45 C for 4-12 hours; higher heat risks a lumpy texture or whey separation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogurt
- Dairy processing appears in Neolithic Turkey by 6600 BCE. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogurt
