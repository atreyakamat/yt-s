## Fact check

- Bolt seals are single-use tamper-evident container seals with a bolt and a locking barrel; they must be cut with bolt cutters. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seal_(mechanical)
- High-security seals are tested to ISO 17712. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_seal
- Seals carry unique serial numbers recorded in shipping documents. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seal_(mechanical)
- Gauge value is illustrative, not a measurement.
