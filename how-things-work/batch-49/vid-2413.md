## Fact check

- Dry ice is solid carbon dioxide that sublimates at about -78.5 °C at atmospheric pressure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_ice
- Dry ice should not be kept in airtight containers because the gas builds pressure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_ice
- Expanded polystyrene foam insulates because it is mostly trapped air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polystyrene
- Phase change materials absorb heat as they melt at a roughly constant temperature. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase-change_material
- Verified: dry ice sublimes at -78.5 C. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dry_ice
