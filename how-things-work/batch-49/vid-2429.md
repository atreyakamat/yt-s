## Fact check

- Artificial fly: fur or hair, feathers, synthetic fibres or yarn wound onto a plain hook with thread. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_fly
- Dry flies float or land softly; wet flies sink and imitate larvae, pupae, nymphs, drowned insects, freshwater shrimp and small baitfish. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_fly
- Aelian's Natural History, probably written about 200 AD, mentions a Macedonian fly; Treatyse on Fysshynge with an Angle (1496) includes fly dressings for different times of year; first known illustration in the 1652 edition of Dennys. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_fly
