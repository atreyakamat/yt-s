## Fact check

- Five solids used as dice with 4, 6, 8, 12, 20 faces; 20-sided icosahedron has equilateral triangle faces; icosahedra date to Roman and Ptolemaic times; 20-sided dice date to the 2nd century CE, possibly 2nd-century BCE Ptolemaic Egypt. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dice
- The d10 is a pentagonal trapezohedron with ten kite-shaped faces. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dice
- Opposite faces: d8 usually 9, d12 usually 13, d20 numbered 1-20 sums to 21. Possibly oldest dice at the Burnt City, about 2800-2500 BCE. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dice
