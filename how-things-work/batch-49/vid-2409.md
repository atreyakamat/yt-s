## Fact check

- HO scale 1:87, track gauge 16.5 mm, name derived from 'Half of 0'; N scale 1:160 (standard), gauge 9 mm, name from 'Nine millimeter'; O scale gauge 32 mm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_transport_modelling_scales
- Real standard gauge is 1,435 mm (4 ft 8.5 in). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_transport_modelling_scales
