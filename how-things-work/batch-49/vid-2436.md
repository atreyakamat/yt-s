## Fact check

- Passports with chips carry digital face images, with matching done outside the chip at electronic border control systems; chip data is signed in a country PKI chain. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biometric_passport
- Live face compared to the chip photo; failure routes to an officer: general e-gate practice, no number given.
