## Fact check

- Whipping cream has typically 30-36% butterfat; fat globules form stable air bubbles; partly merged fat particles build a network that traps air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whipped_cream
- Whipped cream has about twice the volume of the original cream; over-whipping makes fat droplets clump together, breaking the foam and producing butter; results are best when equipment and ingredients are chilled. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whipped_cream
