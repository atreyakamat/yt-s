## Fact check

- Wind chill is the sensation of cold from wind on exposed skin and does not change how cold objects get; 2001 index adopted by Canada, the US and the UK developed by JAG/TI; models bare face walking into wind at 1.4 m/s (5.0 km/h); defined only for air at or below 10 C and wind above 4.8 km/h; at -20 C: 5 km/h wind gives -24, 30 km/h gives -33; Siple and Passel developed first formulas in Antarctica before WWII. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_chill
