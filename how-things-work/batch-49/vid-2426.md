## Fact check

- Strain gauge detects changes in electrical resistance as a foil pattern deforms; stretching makes the conductor longer and thinner, raising resistance. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strain_gauge
- Strain gauge invented independently by Edward Simmons and Arthur Ruge in 1938. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strain_gauge
- Digital scales turn the signal into weight with a chip: general description; luggage scales are weighing devices. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luggage_scale
