## Fact check

- Audio fingerprinting converts sound to a spectrogram, selects peaks and hashes pairs of them to match a database. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shazam_(application)
- The method is designed to survive background noise. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustic_fingerprint
- Grid and gauge values are illustrative; no real figures are claimed.
