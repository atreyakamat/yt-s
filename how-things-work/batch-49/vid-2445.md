## Fact check

- Face detection locates faces in images; facial landmark tracking then follows points on the face frame by frame. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_detection
- Augmented reality overlays digital objects aligned to the real scene. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmented_reality
- Tracking works worse in poor lighting (reasoning, no figure).
