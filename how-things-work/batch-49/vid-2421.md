## Fact check

- Concrete hardens by hydration, a chemical reaction with water, not by drying. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
- A lower water-cement ratio gives higher strength and lower permeability; excess water leaves pores. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
