## Fact check

- Wrecking ball: forged steel; dropped onto roofs or swung like a pendulum at walls; relies on kinetic energy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrecking_ball
- Roughly 1,000 lb (450 kg) to about 12,000 lb (5,400 kg). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrecking_ball
- Most common in the 1940s and 1950s; replaced by hydraulic excavators and controlled blasting, which is less collateral damage. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrecking_ball
