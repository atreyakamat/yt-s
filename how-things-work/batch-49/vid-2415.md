## Fact check

- GIF was introduced by CompuServe in 1987; the 89a version adding animation support came in 1989. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GIF
- A GIF frame uses a palette of at most 256 colours. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GIF
- Many websites convert animated GIFs to video formats because the files are far smaller. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GIF
- Bar values (12 MB vs 1 MB) are illustrative of the size gap, not measurements of one file.
