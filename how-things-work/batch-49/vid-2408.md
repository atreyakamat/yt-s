## Fact check

- A starter is a live mixture of flour and water holding wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria; yeast makes CO2 and ethanol; bacteria make lactic acid. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sourdough
- A starter made from scratch stabilises at pH 4.4-4.6 after about 54 hours at 27 C; below pH 4.0 lactobacilli are inhibited and acid-tolerant yeasts are favoured. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sourdough
- One of the oldest known sourdough breads, excavated in Switzerland, dates to 3700 BC. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sourdough
