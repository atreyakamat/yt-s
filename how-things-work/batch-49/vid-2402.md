## Fact check

- Engine-generators use an alternator driven by an engine, with a governor to hold speed and a voltage regulator. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engine-generator
- Output frequency depends on rotation speed and number of poles (two-pole machine at 3,000 rpm = 50 Hz; 3,600 rpm = 60 Hz). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternator
- Mains frequency is 50 or 60 Hz depending on country. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utility_frequency
