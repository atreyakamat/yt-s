## Fact check

- An oscillator sends AC through a coil making an alternating magnetic field; nearby metal produces eddy currents which create their own field, which a second coil can detect. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_detector
- Pulse induction sends brief powerful pulses and measures decay; mostly unaffected by ground mineralization. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_detector
- 1881 Alexander Graham Bell tried to locate a bullet in President Garfield (failed because of the metal bed springs); Gerhard Fischer patent 1925. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_detector
