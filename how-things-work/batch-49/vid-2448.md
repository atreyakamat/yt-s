## Fact check

- Bromelain is a mixture of enzymes from pineapple whose main enzymes break down proteins; it is widely used as a meat tenderiser. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromelain
- The enzymes are heat-labile: cooked or canned pineapple does not tenderise meat. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bromelain
- Gelatin comes from partial hydrolysis of collagen from animal skin and bones; it forms a 3D network that traps water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelatin
- Linking the two: a protein-digesting enzyme in fresh pineapple breaks down gelatin (a protein), so jelly does not set; cooked or tinned pineapple is fine. This follows from the two sources above.
