## Fact check

- The patellar reflex is a classic monosynaptic stretch reflex; the signal reaches the spinal cord (about L3-L4) and goes straight to a motor neuron; quadriceps contracts, hamstring relaxes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patellar_reflex
- About 18 ms passes between tendon stretch and the start of quadriceps contraction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patellar_reflex
- Erb and Westphal independently reported it in 1875; it tests spinal segments L2-L4 and nerves involved. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patellar_reflex
