## Fact check

- Floaters are shadows cast on the retina by tiny protein or cell debris in the vitreous gel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floater
- They drift with eye movements and settle with gravity; most noticeable against plain backgrounds like blue sky. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floater
- Vitreous detachment typically affects people over 50. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floater
