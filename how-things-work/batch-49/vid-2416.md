## Fact check

- Travel pillows are 'U' shaped, fitting around the back of the neck to support the head in a sitting position. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pillow
- U-shaped pillows can push the head forward and cause neck stiffness. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pillow
- Open front leaves room for the throat: reasoning from the design, no number.
