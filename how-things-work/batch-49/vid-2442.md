## Fact check

- A fuel cell converts the chemical energy of hydrogen and oxygen into electricity, with water as the product. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuel_cell
- Proton-exchange membrane cells pass protons through a membrane while electrons flow through an external circuit. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proton-exchange_membrane_fuel_cell
- A fuel cell keeps producing power as long as fuel and oxidant are supplied. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuel_cell
