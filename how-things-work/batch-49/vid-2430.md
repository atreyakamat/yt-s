## Fact check

- Three basic types: basal, reflex and emotional (psychic). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tears
- Tear film has lipid, aqueous (about 4 micrometres) and mucous layers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tears
- Basal secretion about 0.5-2.2 microlitres per minute (about 0.75-1.1 g per day); irritation can raise output roughly 100-fold, to about 300 microlitres per minute. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tears
- Tears drain through the nasolacrimal duct into the nose, so strong crying can make it run; lysozyme fights bacteria. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tears
