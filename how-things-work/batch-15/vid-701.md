## Fact check

- Metals expand when heated; a thin tray partly covered with cold food heats unevenly, so the hot areas are constrained by cooler ones and thermal stress builds.
- Thin sheets relieve the stress by buckling, which can happen suddenly (snap-through) with an audible bang.
- Thicker gauge metal, rolled/encapsulated rims and pressed ridges increase stiffness and reduce warping.
