## Fact check

- Gel inks are water-based with polymer thickeners (e.g. xanthan gum) that make them shear-thinning.
- The ball's rotation shears the ink, lowering viscosity so it flows; it recovers viscosity on the paper.
- Gel pen refills use a 'follower', a clear viscous plug that prevents ink leaking backward.
- Gel inks can suspend heavy pigments, enabling opaque white, metallic and glitter inks.
