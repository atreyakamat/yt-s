## Fact check

- IPv4 addresses are 32 bits, written as four numbers 0-255: 2^32 = 4,294,967,296 addresses; world population is over 8 billion.
- Regional registries exhausted their free IPv4 pools between 2011 and 2019.
- Home routers use NAT to share one public IPv4 address among many devices.
- IPv6 uses 128-bit addresses: about 3.4 × 10^38, vastly more than estimates of Earth's sand grains (~10^18-10^20).
