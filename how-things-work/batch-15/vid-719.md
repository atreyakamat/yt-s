## Fact check

- Door knockers date back to antiquity and were often decorative status symbols.
- 18th and 19th century houses used mechanical bell systems: pulls connected by wires and bell cranks to a bell board in the servants' area, labelled by room.
- Joseph Henry demonstrated an electromagnetic bell, rung over a long wire, in 1831; electric doorbells followed later in the century.
- Joseph Henry made a bell ring at a distance by wire around 1831 (precursor of the electric doorbell): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doorbell
