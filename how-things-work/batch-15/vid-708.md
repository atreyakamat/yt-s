## Fact check

- Electronic fuel injectors are solenoid (or piezo) valves: the ECU energises a coil, lifting a needle so pressurised fuel sprays through small nozzle holes.
- Port injection runs at about 3-4 bar; petrol direct injection at roughly 100-350 bar, i.e. over 100 times a car tyre's ~2.3 bar.
- Injector pulse widths are typically a few milliseconds (about 1-10 ms); the ECU varies pulse length to meter fuel.
- Fine atomisation improves mixing and combustion.
- Direct injection pressure roughly 100-350 bar vs about 2-3 bar tyre: OK, "over a hundred times tyre pressure" holds for the top of the range. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_injection
