## Fact check

- Alcohol in blood equilibrates with alveolar air, so deep-lung (end-expiratory) breath reflects blood alcohol; testers require a sustained breath.
- Fuel-cell breathalysers oxidise ethanol at a platinum electrode, producing a current proportional to the alcohol in the sample.
- Early breath testers used potassium dichromate, which changes from orange to green when it reacts with alcohol. Evidential devices may use infrared instead. Bars illustrative.
