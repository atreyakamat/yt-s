## Fact check

- US DOE HEPA standard: at least 99.97% removal of 0.3 µm particles. HEPA media is a mat of randomly arranged glass fibres, usually pleated.
- Capture mechanisms: inertial impaction and interception for larger particles, diffusion (Brownian motion) for very small ones.
- Efficiency is lowest near the most penetrating particle size, around 0.1-0.3 µm, which is why 0.3 µm is the test size; larger and smaller particles are captured more efficiently.
