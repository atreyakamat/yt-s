## Fact check

- Bridge bearings sit between the superstructure (deck) and the piers/abutments, allowing thermal movement and rotation under load.
- Laminated elastomeric bearings are rubber layers bonded to thin steel plates: stiff in compression but flexible in shear.
- Sliding bearings use PTFE (a slippery plastic) against stainless steel to allow larger movements.
- Restraining that movement would cause large stresses and cracking.
