## Fact check

- Before paper filters, coffee was commonly boiled with grounds and decanted, or brewed in cloth bags or percolators, giving gritty or over-extracted, bitter coffee.
- Melitta Bentz of Dresden used blotting paper from her son's school exercise book in a brass pot she had punched with holes; she was granted a German utility patent (Gebrauchsmuster) for the filter in 1908.
- Cone-shaped paper filters were introduced by her company in the 1930s.
- Melitta Bentz, Dresden, blotting paper from son's notebook, brass pot, German patent June 1908: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melitta_Bentz
