## Fact check

- The Sharp J-SH04, released in Japan in 2000, is widely cited as the first mass-market camera phone; its camera was about 0.11 megapixels (110,000 pixels; roughly 350 x 300 pixels).
- It allowed sending photos electronically via the carrier's mail service.
- Many modern phones have a 12 MP or higher main camera (12 shown as typical), plus several lenses.
- Early camera phone about 0.1 MP: OK. Kyocera VP-210 (1999) had a 110,000-pixel camera; Sharp J-SH04 (2000) was the first mass-market camera phone. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camera_phone
- Title changed from a stamp comparison to the 0.1 MP figure: SOFTEN, stamp size not supportable. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camera_phone
