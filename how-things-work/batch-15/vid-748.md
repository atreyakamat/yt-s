## Fact check

- Colour code myth is false → manufacturers and fact-checkers (e.g. Snopes) confirm the marks don't indicate ingredients.
- Eye marks → printing term for registration marks read by photo sensors on packaging machines.
- Tubes filled from the open end, cap down → standard tube-filling process; the end is then crimped or heat-sealed.
- Colour only needs contrast → the sensor detects a change in reflectance against the background print.
