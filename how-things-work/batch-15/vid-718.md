## Fact check

- Egyptian scented oils and spices → ancient Egyptians used perfumed oils, baths and aromatic spices for body odour.
- Bacteria cause odour → fresh apocrine sweat is nearly odourless; skin bacteria break it down into smelly compounds.
- Dress shields → 19th-century cotton or rubber pads pinned or sewn into garments to absorb sweat.
- 1888 → first commercial deodorant (Philadelphia); 1903 → first commercial antiperspirant.
- Roll-on inspired by the ballpoint pen → introduced in the 1950s.
- Antiperspirants use aluminium salts that temporarily plug sweat ducts; deodorants target odour-causing bacteria and mask smell.
