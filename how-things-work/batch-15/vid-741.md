## Fact check

- Correction fluid is mainly titanium dioxide pigment with a resin binder and a volatile solvent.
- Titanium dioxide is the main white pigment in house paint and is highly reflective and opaque.
- Bette Nesmith Graham invented correction fluid in 1956 while working as a typist.
- Bottles contain a mixing ball to re-suspend settled pigment; water-based versions and correction tape are common today.
- Bette Nesmith Graham painted over typing errors in the 1950s: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bette_Nesmith_Graham
