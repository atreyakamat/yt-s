## Fact check

- Momofuku Ando invented instant ramen, launching 'Chicken Ramen' in 1958 in Japan; he developed it in a shed behind his home over about a year.
- He was inspired by seeing long queues for ramen in post-war Osaka and by watching tempura being fried (flash-frying dehydrates the noodles and leaves porous holes).
- The porous structure lets hot water rehydrate the noodles in about three minutes.
- Cup noodles were launched in 1971. Global consumption is over 100 billion servings a year.
