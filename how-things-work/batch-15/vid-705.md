## Fact check

- Steel wool is made by drawing low-carbon steel wire past serrated cutting tools that shave off thin, curly filaments.
- Grades range from #4 (coarsest) to #0000 (finest); the finest fibres are about 0.025 mm, thinner than a typical human hair (about 0.05-0.1 mm).
- Soap-filled pads combine steel wool with detergent.
- Because of its very high surface area, steel wool rusts quickly when wet and fine grades can ignite (e.g. from a spark or a 9 V battery).
- Steel wool grades 4 (coarse) to 0000 (finest): OK, standard grading scale. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steel_wool
