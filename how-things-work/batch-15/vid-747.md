## Fact check

- UK Food Standards Agency / EU rules: 'best before' (minimum durability) relates to quality; food can be safe after it if stored correctly. 'Use by' relates to safety and is used on highly perishable foods; food shouldn't be eaten after it, even if it looks and smells fine.
- Typical best-before foods: dried, tinned and frozen foods, crisps; use-by foods: fresh meat, fish, ready meals, some dairy.
- Date-label confusion is a recognised cause of household food waste (WRAP, UK). The grid is an illustration, not a statistic.
