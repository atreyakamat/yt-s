## Fact check

- Concrete is cement, fine aggregate (sand), coarse aggregate (gravel) and water; it hardens by hydration, a chemical reaction of cement with water forming calcium silicate hydrate and other crystals that bind the aggregate.
- If concrete dries too early, hydration stops and strength and durability suffer; moist curing is standard practice.
- Concrete typically reaches about 65-70% of its 28-day strength at 7 days; compressive strength is conventionally specified at 28 days, and it continues gaining slowly after.
- Portland cement is hydraulic: it sets and hardens underwater.
