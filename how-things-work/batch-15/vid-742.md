## Fact check

- Consumer pressure washers use an electric motor driving an axial piston pump (a wobble/swash plate pushes several pistons in turn).
- Typical home units are rated roughly 100-150 bar; household mains water pressure is typically about 1-5 bar.
- A small nozzle converts pressure into high jet velocity.
- Typical flow is about 5-8 L/min, less than a typical garden hose (often 10-20 L/min or more).
- An unloader valve diverts flow into a bypass loop when the trigger is released, protecting the pump and hose.
