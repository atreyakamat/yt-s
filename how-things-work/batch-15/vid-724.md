## Fact check

- Latitude can be found from the sun's noon altitude; longitude requires knowing time at a reference meridian.
- The 1707 Scilly naval disaster helped prompt the Longitude Act of 1714, which offered prizes up to £20,000.
- The Earth rotates 360° in 24 hours, 15° per hour.
- John Harrison, a self-taught carpenter and clockmaker, built marine timekeepers; his H4 was tested on a voyage to Jamaica in 1761-62.
- Pendulum clocks are unreliable on moving ships; marine chronometers became standard until radio and satellite navigation.
