## Fact check

- Skateboarding began in California in the late 1940s and 1950s, when surfers attached roller-skate wheels to boards to 'sidewalk surf'.
- Early wheels were metal, then clay composite; they gave poor grip and a rough ride.
- Urethane skateboard wheels were introduced in 1972 (Frank Nasworthy), greatly improving grip and sparking a boom including pool and ramp skating.
- Skateboarding debuted at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics, held in 2021.
