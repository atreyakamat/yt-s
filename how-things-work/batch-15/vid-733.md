## Fact check

- Macerating toilets → a unit with a cutting blade and pump behind/under the toilet.
- Pressure switch → rising water level in the unit triggers the motor.
- Narrow discharge pipe → typically about 22-32 mm, compared with about 100 mm for a standard soil pipe.
- Lift → units can pump several metres vertically, allowing toilets below sewer level or far from the stack.
- Wipes → manufacturers say only toilet paper should go in.
