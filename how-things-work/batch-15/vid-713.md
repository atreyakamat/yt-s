## Fact check

- Dyes appear coloured because their chromophores absorb certain visible wavelengths and the rest is reflected.
- Chromophores are conjugated bond systems; UV and high-energy light can break or alter them (photodegradation), leaving colourless products.
- Fading accumulates with exposure time, so covered areas (under collars or flaps) stay brighter.
- Drying inside out or in shade is standard care advice to reduce fading.
