## Fact check

- Milk fat exists as globules less dense than the watery part, so cream rises when raw milk stands (creaming).
- Homogenisation pumps milk at high pressure through a narrow valve gap, breaking globules into much smaller ones (typically to around 1 micrometre or less).
- Smaller globules rise far more slowly, so the milk stays uniform through its shelf life.
