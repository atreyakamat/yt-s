## Fact check

- Watch straps attach via spring bars: a tube containing a spring (or two) with retractable pins at each end.
- The pins seat in holes drilled in the lugs; a spring-bar tool or quick-release lever compresses them.
- Lug holes and pins are typically around 0.8-1.1 mm in diameter: 'about a millimetre'.
- Quick-release spring bars have a small protruding lever for tool-free removal.
