## Fact check

- Chinese bronze cash coins had square holes and were strung in large strings, making large sums very heavy.
- Merchants in Sichuan used deposit receipts (jiaozi) that circulated as money; the Song government took over issuing them in the 1020s (often dated 1023-1024).
- Marco Polo described Yuan dynasty paper money in the late 1200s.
- Stockholms Banco issued Europe's first banknotes in 1661.
- Most money today exists as bank deposits rather than physical cash. Grid is illustrative.
- First official government jiaozi notes 1024 (office set up 1023), i.e. the 1020s: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiaozi_(currency)
