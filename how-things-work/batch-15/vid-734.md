## Fact check

- Door viewers contain several lenses: a strong diverging (concave) objective on the outside and a converging eyepiece on the inside.
- The diverging lens gathers a wide field (commonly about 160-200 degrees) into a small virtual image that the eyepiece magnifies.
- Looking in from outside, the optics only show a tiny, unfocused bright spot, so it works one way.
