## Fact check

- Formation → pumice forms when gas-rich (often silica-rich) lava is explosively erupted and cools rapidly, trapping gas bubbles in volcanic glass.
- Floats → its high porosity gives it a low bulk density, so many pieces float until waterlogged.
- Abrasive → the glassy bubble walls make it a mild abrasive for skin.
- 2019 Tonga raft → a large pumice raft formed after an undersea eruption near Tonga in August 2019.
- 2021 Okinawa → pumice from the Fukutoku-Okanoba eruption (Aug 2021) reached Okinawa's coasts in late 2021.
