## Fact check

- Swiss-type cheeses such as Emmental use Propionibacterium freudenreichii, which ferments lactate into propionate, acetate and CO2 during warm ripening.
- The CO2 collects in the elastic curd as round 'eyes' (holes).
- In 2015 the Swiss research centre Agroscope reported that microscopic hay particles in milk act as starting points for hole formation, explaining why very hygienic milking led to fewer holes.
