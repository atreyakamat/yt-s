## Fact check

- Capacitive fingerprint sensors use an array of tiny capacitor plates (pixels around 50 micrometres); ridges in contact and valleys with air gaps produce different capacitance.
- Phones extract minutiae (ridge endings, bifurcations) into a template rather than storing a raw image for matching.
- Templates are stored and matched in a secure hardware area (secure enclave / TEE). Optical and ultrasonic under-display sensors also exist ('many sensors').
