## Fact check

- The US MUTCD sizes the pedestrian clearance interval using a walking speed of 3.5 ft/s (about 1.07 m/s); typical adult walking speed is about 1.2-1.4 m/s.
- At ~1 m/s, a 15 m crossing needs about 15 seconds.
- US countdown displays run during the flashing 'don't walk' clearance interval, which is meant for finishing, not starting, a crossing.
- FHWA lists pedestrian countdown signals as a countermeasure that reduces pedestrian crashes in studies.
