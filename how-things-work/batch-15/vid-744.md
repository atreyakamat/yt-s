## Fact check

- Regulation ice hockey pucks are solid vulcanized rubber, 3 inches in diameter and 1 inch thick, weighing about 5.5-6 ounces.
- Elite slap shots exceed 100 mph.
- NHL pucks are frozen before games because cold rubber bounces less and slides more predictably.
- Pucks warm up during play and are routinely replaced with frozen ones at stoppages.
- The puck's edge is textured (knurled) to improve grip on the stick.
