## Fact check

- Water's specific heat capacity (about 4.2 J/g·K) is roughly five times that of stone (about 0.8) and nine times that of iron (about 0.45); bars are scaled to water = 100.
- Hot water bottles are rubber or thermoplastic bags with ribbed surfaces and a screw stopper.
- Manufacturers and safety bodies advise against boiling water and recommend expelling air before sealing.
- Many bottles (e.g. under UK BS 1970) carry a 'daisy wheel' date stamp showing year and month of manufacture, since rubber degrades with age.
