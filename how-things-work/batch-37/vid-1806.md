## Fact check

- Radiosondes measure pressure, temperature and humidity and radio them to the ground; winds come from tracking the balloon. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiosonde
- Launched at hundreds of sites, typically twice a day (00 and 12 UTC). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_balloon
- Balloons expand as air pressure falls and burst at roughly 30 km (range given in the article is about 30-40 km); script says 'roughly 30'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weather_balloon
- A parachute slows the descent of the instrument package. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiosonde
