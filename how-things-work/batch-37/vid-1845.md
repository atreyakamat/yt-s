## Fact check

- The float glass process, developed by Pilkington in the 1950s, pours molten glass onto a bath of molten tin. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Float_glass
- The glass floats because it is less dense than tin; the surfaces become flat and parallel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Float_glass
- Float glass goes through an annealing lehr then is cut. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Float_glass
