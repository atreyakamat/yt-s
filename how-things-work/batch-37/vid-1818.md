## Fact check

- Biological filtration: bacteria oxidise ammonia to nitrite and then nitrate in aquarium filters (the nitrogen cycle). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquarium_filter
- Ammonia is toxic to fish; nitrate is far less toxic. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitrogen_cycle
