## Fact check

- Hotel key cards are magnetic-stripe or RFID cards that the lock reads and checks against a stored code. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keycard_lock
- Passive RFID tags have no battery and are powered by the reader's radio field. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio-frequency_identification
- Many keycard locks are battery powered and offline; SOFTENED to 'many'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keycard_lock
