## Fact check

- Geostationary orbit is about 35,786 km above the equator, and satellites there match Earth's rotation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geostationary_orbit
- A parabolic dish reflects incoming waves to a focus point where the feed (LNB) sits. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite_dish
- An LNB amplifies the weak signal and converts it to a lower frequency that can travel through coaxial cable. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-noise_block_downconverter
