## Fact check

- Sylvan Goldman introduced the shopping cart on 4 June 1937 in Oklahoma, inspired by a folding chair; a frame with two wire baskets he called a folding basket carriage. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shopping_cart
- Orla Watson's nesting cart 1946, patent 1949. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shopping_cart
- Reason shoppers stopped buying when baskets got heavy is general reasoning, not a quoted figure.
