## Fact check

- Rotary mowers cut with a fast-spinning horizontal blade; many blades have a bent-up, sail-like section that creates lift and airflow under the deck. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawn_mower
- Airflow lifts grass and carries clippings to the bag or chute. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawn_mower#Rotary_mowers
- Dull blades tear leaf tips rather than slicing (general turf-care guidance, no numbers used). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawn_mower
