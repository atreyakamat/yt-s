## Fact check

- Spin bikes use a heavy flywheel directly (fixed) coupled to the pedals, no freewheel; resistance via friction pad or magnets. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stationary_bicycle
- Flywheel stores rotational energy (momentum). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flywheel
