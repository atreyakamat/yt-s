## Fact check

- Milking machines use vacuum and a pulsator that alternates vacuum and atmospheric pressure around the liner; typical pulsation is on the order of one cycle per second (roughly 50 to 60 per minute). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milking_machine
- Liners are flexible rubber or silicone sleeves in the teat cup. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milking_machine
