## Fact check

- Typical eye drops are larger than the volume the eye surface can hold, so extra overflows. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_drop
- Some of the drop drains through the tear ducts (lacrimal puncta) to the nose. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasolacrimal_duct
- Drop size depends on the tip opening and the liquid's surface tension and viscosity. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drop_(liquid)
- Avoiding contact between the dropper tip and the eye reduces contamination; general hygiene, not medical advice. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_drop
