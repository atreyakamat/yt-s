## Fact check

- Continuity equation: for an incompressible fluid, flow rate = area x speed, so halving the area doubles the speed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_equation
- Nozzles convert pressure into jet speed (Bernoulli). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nozzle
- Thumb over a hose end narrows the exit and the jet speeds up. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nozzle
