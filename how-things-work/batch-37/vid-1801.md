## Fact check

- Acousto-magnetic tags hold a resonating amorphous metal strip and a semi-hard magnetic strip; detectors pulse at about 58 kHz and listen for the ringing; demagnetising the second strip deactivates the tag. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_article_surveillance
- RF tags are tuned circuits, standard retail frequency 8.2 MHz ("eight megahertz" in script); a deactivation pad destroys the capacitor. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_article_surveillance
