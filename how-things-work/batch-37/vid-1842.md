## Fact check

- A centrifuge spins samples so denser components move outward and separate; measured in relative centrifugal force (g-force). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrifuge
- Laboratory centrifuges reach thousands of g, ultracentrifuges far more. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultracentrifuge
- Rotors must be balanced with matching loads. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrifuge
