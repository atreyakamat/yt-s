## Fact check

- A windsock shows wind direction and relative strength; it points downwind and extends more as the wind strengthens. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windsock
- A fully extended windsock indicates about 15 knots or more (commonly quoted). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windsock
- Windsocks are typically orange and white for visibility. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windsock
