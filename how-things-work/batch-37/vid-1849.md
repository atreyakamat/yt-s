## Fact check

- A sealed bottle develops lower pressure as liquid leaves, and vented bottles let air in to equalise it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_bottle
- Vents are built into the teat, base or an internal tube on many bottles. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_bottle
