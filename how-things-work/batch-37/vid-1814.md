## Fact check

- Treadmills use a safety key/clip: removing it cuts power to the motor (emergency stop). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treadmill
- Dead man's switch concept: operator incapacitation stops the machine. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_man%27s_switch
- Magnetic safety key is a common design (script says magnet key). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treadmill
