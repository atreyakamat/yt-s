## Fact check

- Impulse sprinklers use water pressure to drive a spring-loaded swing arm that deflects into the stream and taps the rotating head; the arm is returned by a spring. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrigation_sprinkler
- No electric motor is used in the standard impulse design. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irrigation_sprinkler
