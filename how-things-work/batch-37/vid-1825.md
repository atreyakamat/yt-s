## Fact check

- Plywood is made of thin veneers glued with adjacent layers' grain rotated up to 90 degrees. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plywood
- Cross-graining reduces splitting and reduces expansion and shrinkage; plywood usually has an odd number of layers, so the sheet is balanced. The 5 on screen is an example (plywood is commonly 3, 5 or more layers); the bars show relative sizes, not measurements. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plywood
