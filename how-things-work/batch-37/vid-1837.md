## Fact check

- The Gramophone Company set 78 rpm as its recording standard in 1912; industry largely standardised on it by 1925. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonograph_record
- Columbia unveiled the LP in June 1948; about 22 minutes per side for a 12-inch 33 1/3 rpm LP; microgroove lets more grooves fit per side. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonograph_record
- RCA Victor released the 7-inch 45 rpm single in February 1949; a 7-inch single holds roughly 4 to 5 minutes a side (the article text says eight but its own table gives about 5.3, and the EP article says about four). Script says "about five". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonograph_record and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EP_record
- The 12-inch LP prevailed in the "War of the Speeds". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonograph_record
