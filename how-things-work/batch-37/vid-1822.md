## Fact check

- An autoclave uses pressurised steam to sterilise; typical cycle 121 °C for 15 minutes or more (article: 15 to 60 minutes depending on load; script says "at least fifteen") (also higher temperatures for shorter times). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoclave
- Water boils at 100 °C at sea-level pressure; higher pressure raises the boiling point. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boiling_point
- Moist heat kills microbes more effectively than dry heat. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterilization_(microbiology)
- Air removal (pre-vacuum or displacement) is required so steam contacts all surfaces. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoclave
