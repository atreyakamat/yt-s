## Fact check

- Polarised 3D: two images projected through orthogonal or opposite-handed filters; each lens passes only its own image. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polarized_3D_system
- Circular polarisation keeps eye separation when the head tilts; linear filters give ghosting when tilted. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polarized_3D_system
- RealD uses a liquid crystal filter that can switch polarity 144 times per second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polarized_3D_system
