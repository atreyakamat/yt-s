## Fact check

- Eggs are asymmetric, so they roll in an arc rather than a straight line; the shell is strong under compression along its long axis. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg
- Egg shape varies across species, linked to nesting habits. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eggshell
