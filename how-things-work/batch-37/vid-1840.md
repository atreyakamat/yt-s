## Fact check

- Automatic doors use motion sensors; common types include microwave radar and active infrared. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automatic_door
- Drive systems commonly use a motor with a belt moving the panels. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automatic_door
- Safety sensors reopen the door if an obstruction is detected. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automatic_door
