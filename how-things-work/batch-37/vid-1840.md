## Fact check

- Venetian blinds are overlapping horizontal slats lowered and drawn together by pulling a cord; the slats move in unison through wires/cords. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Window_blind
- Slats are typically aluminium, plastic or wood; wooden slats come in 25 mm, 35 mm or 50 mm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Window_blind
- 1841 patent for modern-style blinds held at angles and adjustable by cords (diagram credited to John Hampson); widely adopted in offices in the late 19th/early 20th century and used in 1930s Rockefeller Center. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Window_blind
