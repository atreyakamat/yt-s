## Fact check

- Anchors hold either by weight or by embedding in the seabed; most modern anchors work by embedding. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anchor
- Flukes dig into the bottom as horizontal load increases; a low angle of pull (long scope) is needed to keep them set. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anchor
- Chain's weight keeps the pull near horizontal and absorbs shocks (catenary effect). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anchor
