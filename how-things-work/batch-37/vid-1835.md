## Fact check

- Charles Goodyear discovered vulcanisation in 1839 and received a patent in 1844. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Goodyear
- Raw rubber becomes sticky in heat and brittle in cold; vulcanisation with sulphur cross-links polymer chains. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulcanization
- Natural rubber comes from latex of rubber trees; it was used to rub out pencil marks. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubber
