## Fact check

- Perforated cinema screens have small holes so loudspeakers can be placed behind the screen and sound passes through. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projection_screen
- Putting the speakers behind the screen makes dialogue seem to come from the picture (stated as reasoning; the screen is acoustically transparent). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projection_screen
- Holes cost some light reflection (general effect; no number given).
