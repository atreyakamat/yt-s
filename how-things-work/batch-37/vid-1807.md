## Fact check

- Compact Cassette tape is 3.81 mm wide and runs at 4.7625 cm/s (so "under 5 centimetres a second"). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Cassette
- Invented by Lou Ottens and his team at Philips; introduced 28 August 1963 at the Berlin Radio Show. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Cassette
- Cassette uses two pairs of tracks, one pair for each direction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Cassette
- Tape is plastic film coated with magnetic particles and read/written by magnetic heads (general mechanism). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_tape
