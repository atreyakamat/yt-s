## Fact check

- ESLs are digital price tags updated wirelessly by radio, infrared or light; e-paper draws power only during updates and holds its image without power; each tag confirms receipt. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_shelf_label
- Battery life stated only as "years" (a 1997 system claimed over 5 years). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_shelf_label
