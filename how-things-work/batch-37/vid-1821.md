## Fact check

- Coin/token/card deposit unlocks the cart and is returned when the cart is brought back; common in continental Europe and the UK, less common in Canada, rare in the US. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shopping_cart
- Chain linking between trolleys is the common design; no number claimed.
