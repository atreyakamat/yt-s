## Fact check

- Sippy cups use a flexible valve (often silicone) in the lid that opens under suction and closes when it stops. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sippy_cup
- Sucking lowers the pressure at the spout; vents let air replace liquid. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sippy_cup
