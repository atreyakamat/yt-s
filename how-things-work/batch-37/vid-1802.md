## Fact check

- IV gravity sets use a drip chamber, a roller clamp and tubing; the chamber lets the drop rate be seen and counted. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intravenous_therapy
- Microdrip sets deliver 60 drops per mL. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intravenous_therapy
- The chamber also helps stop air travelling down the line (bubbles rise into the air space). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drip_chamber
