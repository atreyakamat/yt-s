## Fact check

- String trimmers cut vegetation with a rotating monofilament line, held out by centrifugal force. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/String_trimmer
- Bump-feed heads release more line when the head is tapped on the ground. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/String_trimmer
- Nylon line is flexible and gives way on hard objects (qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/String_trimmer
