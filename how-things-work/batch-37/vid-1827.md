## Fact check

- In film projectors a Geneva drive pulls film through the gate frame by frame, holding each frame still; typical rate is 24 cycles per second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_drive
- First Geneva drives in film projectors date to 1896. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geneva_drive
- Standard sound film speed is 24 frames per second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frame_rate
- A shutter blocks light while film advances (standard projector design). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movie_projector
