- Many wooden toothpicks are made from birch or similar soft hardwood, peeled on a lathe-style cutter and cut into sticks; wording kept general ("mills pick soft, tight-grained wood like birch"). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toothpick
- Tumbling to shape and polish the points is common in machine production; no numbers claimed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toothpick
