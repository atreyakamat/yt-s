## Fact check

- Treadmills use a motor-driven belt over a deck/rollers; incline raised by a motor. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treadmill
- Decks are commonly lubricated with silicone or wax-type lubricant (script says usually); friction heats belt and strains motor if unlubricated. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treadmill#Design
- Cushioned deck suspension is a standard feature of modern treadmills. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treadmill
