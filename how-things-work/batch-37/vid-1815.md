## Fact check

- Container glass is made from sand, soda ash and limestone (plus recycled glass). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_production
- Glass melting temperatures are well above 1,000 C (script says only "well over a thousand degrees"). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_production
- Gobs are cut and formed in blank and blow moulds (blow-and-blow or press-and-blow); annealing follows. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glass_container_industry
