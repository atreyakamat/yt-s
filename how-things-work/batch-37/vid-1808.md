## Fact check

- Dogs hear up to roughly 45,000 Hz; humans about 20,000 Hz at best, with high-frequency loss with age. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hearing_range
- Galton-style whistles use an adjustable air chamber to set pitch. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dog_whistle
