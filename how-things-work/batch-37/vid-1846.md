## Fact check

- Radar measures range from the round-trip time of reflected radio pulses; radio waves travel at the speed of light, about 300,000 km/s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radar
- Range = speed of light x delay / 2, which is about 150 m per microsecond of round-trip delay (299,792,458 m/s x 0.000001 s / 2 = 149.9 m). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radar
- Radio waves are not blocked by darkness and pass through fog much better than light. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radar
