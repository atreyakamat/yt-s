## Fact check

- Disposable nappies use superabsorbent polymer, usually sodium polyacrylate, with fluff pulp. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diaper
- Sodium polyacrylate can absorb hundreds of times its mass in water (Wikipedia gives 100 to 1000 times its mass; commonly cited about 300 in pure water). Script says "hundreds", counter shows up to about 300. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodium_polyacrylate
