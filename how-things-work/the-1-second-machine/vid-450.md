## Fact check

- 1,400 rpm / 60 = 23.3 revolutions per second.
- Drum radius roughly 0.24-0.25 m: rim speed = 2π × 0.25 × 23.3 ≈ 37 m/s ≈ 130 km/h.
- Centripetal acceleration ω²r ≈ (146.6 rad/s)² × 0.25 m ≈ 5,400 m/s², about 550 g, i.e. hundreds of times gravity.
- Machines run a slow distribution/balance phase before ramping to full spin; higher spin speeds leave lower residual moisture.
