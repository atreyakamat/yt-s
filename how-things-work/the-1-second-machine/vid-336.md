## Fact check

- Electronic coin validators pass coins through electromagnetic coils; eddy currents in the coin change the coil signal according to the coin's diameter, thickness and alloy.
- The measured signature is compared with stored values for valid coins; a gate then routes it to the cash box or the reject/return chute.
- Validation happens in a fraction of a second as the coin rolls or falls past the sensors.
- Gauge is illustrative.
