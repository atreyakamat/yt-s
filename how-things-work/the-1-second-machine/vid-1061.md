## Fact check

- Many jarred foods are hot-filled (or heat-processed) and sealed; cooling contracts the headspace gas and condenses steam, leaving a partial vacuum.
- Lids with a 'safety button' (vacuum indicator) are pulled concave by the lower internal pressure; on opening, air enters and the button pops up.
- Labels commonly say 'do not use if button is raised/up', because it indicates the seal may have been broken.
