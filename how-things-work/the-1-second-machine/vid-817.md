## Fact check

- Impact drivers use a rotary hammer-and-anvil mechanism: under load the spring-loaded hammer cams back, disengages from the anvil lugs, then is driven forward and round to strike them (typically twice per revolution).
- Typical ratings are about 2,800-4,000 impacts per minute, so 'about 3,000' is a fair typical figure.
- Because torque is delivered in short impulses, much less reaction torque reaches the user's wrist than with a drill.
