## Fact check

- Electronic toll collection uses windscreen transponders (RFID/DSRC) that respond to readers on overhead gantries, enabling open-road tolling at highway speed.
- Tag-reader exchanges take milliseconds; 70 mph is about 31 m/s, i.e. about 3 cm per millisecond.
- Vehicles without tags are identified by automatic number plate recognition cameras and billed by plate.
