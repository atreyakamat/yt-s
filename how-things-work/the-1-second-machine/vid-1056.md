## Fact check

- Basic mechanical coin sorters use a sloped track with openings of increasing size; each coin falls through the first opening larger than its diameter.
- Motorised sorters use a rotating disc to feed coins onto the sizing track.
- Hole sizes in the chart are illustrative; real sizes match the local coins.
- Size sorting works because different denominations have different diameters.
