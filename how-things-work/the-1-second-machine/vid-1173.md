## Fact check

- Incandescent brake lamps take time for the filament to heat; LEDs reach full output in well under a millisecond.
- Studies commonly cite LED brake lights illuminating roughly 0.17-0.2 s sooner than filament bulbs ('up to about').
- 70 mph = 31.3 m/s, so 0.2 s ≈ 6.3 m.
