## Fact check

- Electric motors can run as generators; the inverter controls the switch from driving to regenerating within milliseconds of lifting off.
- Regenerative braking converts some kinetic energy into electrical energy stored in the battery, creating a retarding force.
- Many EVs offer strong regen or 'one-pedal' modes that slow the car to near standstill; friction brakes still handle hard stops.
- Friction brakes turn kinetic energy into heat; regen recovers part of it (efficiency varies, so no number is given).
