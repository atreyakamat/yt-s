## Fact check

- Popcorn kernels hold about 13-14% water inside a hard, nearly waterproof hull.
- Heating turns the water to steam; pressure builds until the hull bursts at about 180 °C (studies cite roughly 180 °C and about 9 atmospheres).
- The gelatinised starch expands into a foam and sets as it cools; high-speed video shows the burst happens within milliseconds.
- Hot air poppers use a heater and fan; light popped kernels are blown out of the chute while unpopped ones stay in.
