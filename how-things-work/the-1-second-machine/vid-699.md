## Fact check

- In standard tuning the low E string is about 82.4 Hz, the A string 110 Hz and the high E about 329.6 Hz (two octaves above low E, i.e. four times the frequency).
- Vibrations that fast blur to the eye.
- A plucked string vibrates in its fundamental and harmonics (halves, thirds, ...) simultaneously.
- The relative strength of harmonics (timbre) is why the same note sounds different on different instruments.
- Wavy strings in phone videos are a rolling-shutter artifact: the sensor reads rows at slightly different times.
