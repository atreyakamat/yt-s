## Fact check

- Digital hearing aids use microphones, an analogue-to-digital converter, a DSP chip that applies frequency-specific gain in multiple channels, and a receiver (speaker).
- Age-related hearing loss commonly affects high frequencies first. Bars are illustrative of a typical fitting shape.
- Hearing aids include noise reduction and feedback cancellation to prevent whistling.
- Processing delay is usually kept below about 10 ms because longer delays are noticeable, especially for the wearer's own voice.
