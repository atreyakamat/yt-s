## Fact check

- All objects above absolute zero emit infrared radiation; at skin temperature it peaks around 9-10 micrometres, invisible to the eye.
- Non-contact thermometers focus infrared onto a thermopile (many thermocouple junctions) that produces a voltage related to the received radiation.
- Infrared readings typically take about a second; contact digital thermometers often take from several seconds to around a minute depending on the model. Bars are illustrative.
