## Fact check

- Magnetic bag snaps and jewellery clasps contain small permanent magnets, often under a metal cover.
- Attraction between magnets increases very steeply as separation decreases, so the final gap closes by itself very quickly (well under the ~0.1-0.4 s of a blink).
- Many magnetic snaps have a male post that seats into a female ring for alignment.
- Magnets resist being pulled apart straight more than sliding/tilting (shear), so sliding open is easier: general magnet behaviour.
