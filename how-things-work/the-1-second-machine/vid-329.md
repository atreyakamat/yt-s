## Fact check

- Water hammer: sudden valve closure stops a moving water column, converting momentum into a pressure surge (Joukowsky effect).
- The pressure wave travels at roughly 1,000-1,400 m/s in water-filled pipes, depending on pipe material: 'roughly a kilometre per second'.
- Solenoid valves in washing machines and dishwashers and quarter-turn/quick-shut taps are common causes.
- Water hammer arrestors use an air or gas cushion (often behind a piston) to absorb the surge.
