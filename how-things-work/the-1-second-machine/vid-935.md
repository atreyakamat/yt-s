## Fact check

- Many modern vending machines use an infrared drop-sensor array ('guaranteed vend') across the delivery chute.
- If no product interrupts the beams after a vend, the machine re-rotates the spiral, then refunds or lets the customer choose again.
- Shaking or rocking vending machines is dangerous, which is why the loop says 'no shaking needed'.
