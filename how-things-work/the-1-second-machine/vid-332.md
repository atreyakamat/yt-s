## Fact check

- Loading a page involves DNS resolution (name to IP), a TCP/QUIC connection, a TLS handshake (key agreement), then an HTTP request for the HTML.
- The HTML references more resources; typical pages load dozens of requests (often many more).
- Servers can be far away; data crosses long-haul and undersea cables, though content delivery networks often serve nearby copies.
- Browsers render progressively as resources arrive. Many pages load in about a second on a good connection.
