## Fact check

- UK BS 1363 plugs contain a BS 1362 cartridge fuse in the live connection; common ratings are 3 A, 5 A and 13 A.
- The cartridge is a ceramic tube with a fusible wire, typically packed with silica sand, which quenches the arc as the element melts.
- Under a heavy short-circuit current, the element melts within milliseconds; at modest overloads it takes longer.
- Low-power appliances such as lamps are commonly fitted with 3 A fuses; kettles and heaters 13 A.
