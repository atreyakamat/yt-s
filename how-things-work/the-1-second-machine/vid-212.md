## Fact check

- Desktop hard drives commonly spin at 7,200 rpm = 120 revolutions per second (others 5,400 rpm).
- Platters are aluminium or glass with a magnetic coating.
- Heads fly on an air bearing created by the spinning disk; modern fly heights are a few nanometres, versus a hair of roughly 50,000-100,000 nm.
- Average seek times are several milliseconds.
- Data is stored as tiny magnetised regions; the read head detects changes in magnetisation. 'North = 1, South = 0' is a simplification for the screen.
