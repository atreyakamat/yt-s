## Fact check

- Building materials expand when warmed and contract when cooled (thermal expansion); evenings bring cooling as heating switches off and outdoor temperatures fall.
- Materials expand at different rates (e.g. copper pipe more than timber), so parts move relative to each other.
- Friction causes stick-slip motion: stress builds until static friction is overcome and the joint slips suddenly, making a crack or tick. Changes in humidity also play a part; the script focuses on temperature.
