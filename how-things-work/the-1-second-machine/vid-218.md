## Fact check

- Auto-open umbrellas have a spring in or along the shaft that is compressed when the umbrella is closed.
- The button releases a latch holding the runner (the sliding collar), and the spring pushes it up.
- The runner connects to stretchers, which push the ribs out and tension the canopy; a notch/catch holds it open.
- Opening happens in well under a second: observable.
