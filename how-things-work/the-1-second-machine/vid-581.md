## Fact check

- Thermal shock: uneven expansion or contraction creates internal stress; glass is weak in tension and can crack suddenly; cracks travel through glass extremely fast.
- Borosilicate glass has a thermal expansion coefficient of about 3.3 x 10^-6 /K, versus about 9 x 10^-6 /K for soda-lime glass, roughly one third.
- Much glass bakeware (especially in the US) is now tempered soda-lime glass rather than borosilicate; makers warn against sudden temperature changes such as placing hot dishes on wet or cold surfaces.
