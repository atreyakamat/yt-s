## Fact check

- The white 'smoke' after extinguishing a candle is mostly condensed paraffin vapour from the still-hot wick.
- A flame held in that trail ignites the vapour, and the flame travels down to relight the wick (a classic demonstration described by Faraday).
- It only works for a few seconds, while the wick is hot enough to keep vaporising wax.
