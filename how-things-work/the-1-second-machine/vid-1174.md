## Fact check

- Self-balancing scooters have two halves, each with a hub motor and gyroscope/accelerometer sensors, plus footpad sensors.
- Leaning tilts the platform; the controller drives the motor to bring the platform back level, which moves the board in the lean direction (inverted-pendulum control).
- Control loops run many times per second.
- Different tilt on each side makes the wheels turn at different speeds or directions, steering the board.
