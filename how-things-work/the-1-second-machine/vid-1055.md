## Fact check

- Electronic coin validators roll coins past inductive sensor coils; the coin's alloy (conductivity, magnetic properties), diameter and thickness alter the coils' signals.
- A microcontroller compares the signal with stored profiles for genuine coins within milliseconds.
- A solenoid-driven gate diverts accepted coins to the cash box; rejected coins fall to the return chute.
