## Fact check

- Glucose test strips draw blood into a narrow chamber by capillary action; many modern strips need around 0.3-1 microlitre, while early meters needed much larger drops. Bars are approximate.
- Strips contain an enzyme (glucose oxidase or glucose dehydrogenase) and a mediator; the reaction transfers electrons to electrodes, producing a current proportional to glucose.
- Results typically appear within about 5 seconds. Educational only, not medical advice.
