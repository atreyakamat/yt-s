## Fact check

- Xenon flash units charge a photoflash capacitor to roughly 300 V using a DC-DC converter (oscillator + transformer), which produces the audible high-pitched whine.
- A capacitor stores charge on conductive plates/foils separated by an insulator.
- A high-voltage trigger pulse ionises the xenon gas so the capacitor discharges through the tube.
- Flash durations are typically around 1/1000 s or shorter; a blink lasts roughly 100-400 ms (100 ms shown as the fast end).
