## Fact check

- Discharge times for small portable extinguishers are short: e.g. 1-2 kg powder units often discharge in roughly 6-15 seconds, and 2 kg CO2 units in around 10 seconds; larger units last longer. 'Around ten to twenty seconds' is a fair range for many small units.
- Common sizes: about 1-2 kg for home units, around 6 kg (or 6-9 litres) for offices. Bars are typical sizes.
- PASS training says to aim at the base of the fire; fire services advise evacuating if a fire is spreading. Educational only, not fire safety training.
