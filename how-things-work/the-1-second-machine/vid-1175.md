## Fact check

- Metal-oxide surge arresters contain zinc oxide varistor blocks in a ribbed housing, connected between the line and ground, often beside transformers.
- Zinc oxide is highly non-linear: it draws only a tiny current at normal voltage but conducts heavily above its threshold, responding in nanoseconds.
- Lightning impulses last on the order of tens of microseconds; afterwards the arrester returns to its high-resistance state.
