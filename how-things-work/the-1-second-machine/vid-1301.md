## Fact check

- Most gas hobs use an electronic spark generator that produces pulses of high voltage (typically several to over ten thousand volts) to a ceramic-insulated electrode beside the burner.
- The voltage ionises the small air gap, producing a brief spark (microseconds) hot enough to ignite the gas-air mix; the module repeats several times per second while the knob is held.
- Some appliances and lighters use piezoelectric igniters, where squeezing/striking a crystal generates the high voltage.
