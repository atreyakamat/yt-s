## Fact check

- When milk is heated, whey proteins denature and, with fat, form a skin at the surface.
- Milk proteins (and fat) stabilise bubble films, so bubbles persist and accumulate as foam instead of bursting, and the skin traps rising steam.
- Close to boiling, vapour production rises rapidly and the foam can rise over the rim within seconds; water lacks these stabilisers.
- Stirring, gentle heat and removing it before a full boil prevent boil-overs.
