## Fact check

- Red light cameras are connected to the signal controller and are only active during the red phase.
- They detect vehicles with sensors (often induction loops, or radar/video) placed beyond the stop line.
- Many systems use a short grace period after the change to red before enforcing.
- Systems typically take two images: one as the vehicle crosses the stop line and one showing it continuing through the junction.
