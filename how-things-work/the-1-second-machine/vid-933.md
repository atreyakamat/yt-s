## Fact check

- AEB uses forward radar and/or cameras to track vehicles ahead, estimate time-to-collision, warn the driver, pre-fill the brakes and then brake automatically.
- The 1.5 s countdown is an example value, labelled as such.
- Driver reaction times are typically around 1 s; electronic systems respond much faster.
- IIHS research found front crash prevention with AEB cut rear-end crashes by about 50 %. AEB is required on new cars in the EU (from July 2024) and is being mandated in the US.
