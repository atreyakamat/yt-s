## Fact check

- Automatic garage door openers use photoelectric sensors mounted near the floor on both sides: an infrared emitter and a receiver.
- Infrared has wavelengths just longer than visible red light, so it's invisible to us.
- If the beam is interrupted while closing, the opener stops and reverses the door.
- US rules (UL 325, federal law from 1993) require external entrapment protection such as photo eyes on residential openers; openers also reverse on contact (force sensing).
