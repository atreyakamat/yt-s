## Fact check

- Expulsion-type fuse cutouts on distribution poles hold a fuse link in a tube; overcurrent melts the link quickly (a large fault in a fraction of a second).
- The arc heats the tube's lining, which releases gas that blasts out and de-ionises the arc, often with a loud bang.
- The fuse holder is hinged and drops open when the link blows, giving a visible indication from the ground; line workers re-fuse it using an insulated hot stick.
