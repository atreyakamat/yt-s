## Fact check

- An inflated balloon's latex is under tension, with gas at slightly higher pressure inside.
- Once punctured, a crack propagates across the stretched rubber at high speed and the rubber retracts; high-speed video shows the whole skin gone within a few milliseconds for a typical party balloon.
- Slow-motion footage of popping water balloons shows the water briefly retaining the balloon's shape after the skin has gone.
- The bang is the sudden release of pressurized air creating a pressure (sound) wave.
- A human blink takes roughly 100-400 ms; 300 ms is used as a rough figure.
