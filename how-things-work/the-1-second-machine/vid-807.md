## Fact check

- The cola-and-mint eruption is mainly physical: the mint's rough, pitted surface provides countless nucleation sites for dissolved CO2 to form bubbles (studied by Coffey, American Journal of Physics, 2008).
- The dense mints sink quickly, nucleating bubbles throughout the bottle.
- Ingredients such as gum arabic in the mint, and sweeteners/preservatives in diet cola, lower surface tension and speed bubble growth; diet cola gives the biggest eruptions.
- Fountains of several metres are commonly recorded (bars are illustrative).
