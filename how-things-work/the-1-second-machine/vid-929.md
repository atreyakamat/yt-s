## Fact check

- PIR (passive infrared) sensors detect infrared radiation emitted by warm bodies.
- A segmented Fresnel lens divides the field of view into zones focused onto the sensor.
- Dual-element pyroelectric sensors respond to differences between two halves, so motion across zones triggers them while steady heat sources are ignored.
- Response is a fraction of a second; units typically have timer and light-level (dusk) controls.
