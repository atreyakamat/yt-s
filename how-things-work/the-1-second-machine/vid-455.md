## Fact check

- Near-infrared (NIR) optical sorters identify polymer types (e.g. PET bottles, HDPE milk jugs) by their reflected infrared spectrum on belts running at roughly 2.5-3.5 m/s.
- The controller times compressed-air valves (often hundreds across the belt width) to fire within milliseconds and eject target items over a splitter into a separate stream.
- Throughput of several tonnes per hour corresponds to thousands of bottles per minute.
