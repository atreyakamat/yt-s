## Fact check

- Regulations (e.g. FAA) require slides to be fully inflated within about 6 seconds of deployment.
- 'Doors to arm' means attaching the slide girt bar to the floor so opening the door deploys the slide.
- Slides use a compressed gas cylinder (often CO2/nitrogen mix) with aspirators that draw in large amounts of ambient air.
- The ambient-air share is large but varies by design; the gauge is qualitative ('mostly').
- Certification requires showing full evacuation within 90 seconds using only half the exits.
