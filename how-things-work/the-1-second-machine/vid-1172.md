## Fact check

- In sleep (suspend-to-RAM, ACPI S3, or modern standby), most components power down while RAM stays powered in self-refresh.
- DRAM is volatile and must be refreshed, so sleep draws a small amount of power.
- Resume from sleep typically takes about a second or two.
- Hibernate (suspend-to-disk) writes memory contents to storage and powers off completely; resuming takes longer but uses no power. Bar timings are typical examples.
