## Fact check

- Modern gas boilers pre-purge the combustion chamber with the fan before ignition.
- Ignition is by a high-voltage spark electrode producing rapid sparks (the clicking).
- Flame rectification: a flame conducts a tiny current (microamps) between the sensing rod and burner; the control board monitors it continuously.
- If flame isn't proven within a set safety time (a few seconds), the gas valve closes and the boiler locks out.
