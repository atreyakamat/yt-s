## Fact check

- To a first approximation, contact patch area ≈ wheel load / inflation pressure: a 1,500 kg car puts about 3.7 kN on each wheel; at about 2.3 bar (230 kPa) that is about 0.016 m² = 160 cm², roughly a postcard (about 150 cm²) or an adult hand.
- A typical car tyre circumference is about 2 m; at 70 mph (31 m/s) the wheel turns about 15 times per second.
- A patch about 15 cm long passing at 31 m/s means each bit of tread is in contact for roughly 5 ms.
