## Fact check

- Domestic sewing machines are commonly rated around 750-1,100 stitches per minute; 900 per minute is 15 per second, so 'can make 15' is fair. Industrial machines go much faster.
- A lockstitch interlocks a top (needle) thread with a bobbin thread inside the fabric.
- Machine needles have the eye near the point.
- As the needle begins to rise, the thread forms a loop that the rotary or oscillating hook catches and carries around the bobbin; the take-up lever then pulls the stitch tight.
