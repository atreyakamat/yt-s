## Fact check

- Radar speed cameras use the Doppler effect: the reflected wave's frequency shifts in proportion to the vehicle's speed (the same effect that raises an approaching siren's pitch).
- Many fixed (e.g. older UK wet-film) radar cameras take two photographs a fixed interval apart, and calibrated white lines on the road let the speed be verified from the distance travelled.
- Other camera types exist (average-speed cameras, loop-triggered cameras), hence 'many fixed cameras'.
