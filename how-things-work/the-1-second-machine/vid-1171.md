## Fact check

- Many OLED (and some LCD) displays use pulse-width modulation (PWM) dimming, switching emission on and off rather than lowering current alone.
- Common phone OLED PWM frequencies range from about 240 Hz to over 1,000 Hz ('hundreds of times a second').
- Perceived brightness follows the duty cycle (fraction of time on); the eye integrates fast flicker above its fusion threshold. Bar values are illustrative duty cycles.
- Rolling-shutter cameras can reveal PWM as moving bands.
