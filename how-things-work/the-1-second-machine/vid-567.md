## Fact check

- Shaking a sealed can doesn't meaningfully increase its pressure; it disperses headspace gas into many small bubbles in the liquid.
- When opened, the pressure drops and dissolved CO2 comes out of solution; it diffuses into existing bubbles (nucleation sites), which grow rapidly.
- The rapid bubble growth makes foam that is forced out through the opening.
- Letting the can rest allows the bubbles to rise to the top, so it fizzes less on opening.
