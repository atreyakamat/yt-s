## Fact check

- Magnetic (vibrator) motor clippers → many corded clippers use an AC electromagnet and a spring-loaded armature instead of a rotary motor.
- 50 or 60 Hz → mains frequency is 50 Hz in Europe and much of the world, 60 Hz in North America.
- Pulls twice per cycle → an electromagnet attracts on both positive and negative half-cycles, so 50 Hz gives 100 strokes per second = 6,000 per minute (60 Hz clippers are sold as 7,200 strokes per minute).
- Moving blade over fixed comb blade → standard clipper blade set.
