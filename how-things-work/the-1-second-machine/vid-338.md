## Fact check

- Press studs (snap fasteners) have a stud with a bulbous head and a socket containing a spring element (a wire ring or S-spring) narrower than the head.
- Pushing together spreads the spring; once the head's widest point passes, the spring closes behind it, holding it until enough pull spreads it again.
- The snap takes a small fraction of a second; the click is the spring closing: observable.
