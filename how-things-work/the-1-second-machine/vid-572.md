## Fact check

- 3 GHz = 3 × 10^9 cycles per second; one cycle lasts 0.33 ns.
- Light travels about 30 cm per nanosecond, so about 10 cm in 0.33 ns.
- Clock signals are derived from a quartz crystal oscillator, multiplied up by a PLL.
- Modern CPUs have multiple cores and can complete several instructions per cycle.
