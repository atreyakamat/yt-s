## Fact check

- Smartwatches use accelerometer/gyroscope data processed by a low-power sensor hub or motion coprocessor to detect a wrist-raise gesture.
- Gesture recognition matches the characteristic lift-and-rotate motion and rejects normal arm swing.
- Screens stay off or in a low-power always-on mode most of the time to save battery.
