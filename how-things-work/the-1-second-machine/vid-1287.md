## Fact check

- Home freezers run at about -18 °C, so ice from them is well below 0 °C.
- When placed in a warmer liquid, the surface warms and expands faster than the cold interior, creating thermal stress (thermal shock).
- Ice is brittle and fractures under this stress; cracks propagate very quickly, producing audible crackling.
- Rough ice surfaces also nucleate CO2 bubbles in fizzy drinks.
- Letting ice temper (warm towards 0 °C) reduces the temperature difference and cracking.
