## Fact check

- Dead man's switches (driver's safety devices) require the driver to keep a handle or pedal depressed; releasing it triggers a warning then an emergency brake application.
- Vigilance devices additionally require periodic acknowledgement (often every minute or so) to guard against a driver holding the control while incapacitated.
- Timings vary by system and country, so the script says 'a few seconds' and 'every minute or so'.
