## Fact check

- Wired and wireless intruder alarm door contacts commonly use a magnetic reed switch on the frame and a magnet on the door.
- A reed switch is two ferromagnetic metal reeds sealed in a glass tube; a nearby magnetic field pulls them into contact.
- Reed switches operate and release in roughly a millisecond or less.
- When the alarm is armed, the circuit change triggers the entry delay or the siren; many panels can chime when disarmed.
