## Fact check

- 70 mph = 31.3 m/s; 50 mph = 22.4 m/s; 30 mph = 13.4 m/s, so one second of travel is about 31, 22 and 13 m.
- Driver perception-reaction times in studies typically range from about 0.7 to 1.5 s; 'around a second' is a fair summary.
- Speed is unchanged during the reaction phase; total stopping distance = thinking distance + braking distance, and braking distance rises with the square of speed.
- The 'two-second rule' for following distance is standard advice in UK and other road safety guidance.
