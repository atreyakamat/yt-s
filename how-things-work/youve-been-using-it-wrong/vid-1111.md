## Fact check

- Power bank mAh ratings refer to the internal lithium cells at their nominal ~3.6-3.7 V.
- USB outputs 5 V (or higher with fast-charging protocols), requiring a boost converter.
- Energy conservation: 10,000 mAh x 3.7 V = 37 Wh; at 5 V that is 7,400 mAh before losses.
- Converter, cable and phone charging losses typically reduce delivered energy to around 60-70% of the rated cell energy ('roughly two thirds').
