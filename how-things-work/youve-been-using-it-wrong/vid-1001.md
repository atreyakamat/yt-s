## Fact check

- Many swivel peelers have a pointed tip or loop beside the blade for removing potato eyes and blemishes.
- The swivelling double-edged blade cuts on both push and pull strokes and follows curved surfaces.
- A slot above the blade lets peel pass through; the gap between blade edge and guard limits peel thickness.
