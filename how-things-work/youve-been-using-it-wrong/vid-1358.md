## Fact check

- Forcing a foot into a fastened shoe folds the heel area and its internal heel counter.
- Repeated folding breaks down heel counters, leaving a soft, floppy heel.
- A shoehorn provides a smooth curved surface that keeps the heel upright while the foot slides in: standard footwear care advice.
