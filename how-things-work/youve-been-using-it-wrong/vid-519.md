## Fact check

- Climbing carabiners are marked on the spine with strength ratings in kilonewtons for major axis (gate closed), minor axis (cross-load) and gate open.
- Typical ratings are around 20-25 kN major axis and roughly 7 kN for both minor axis and gate open.
- Cross-loading or loading with the gate open therefore cuts strength to about a third.
- Locking carabiners have a sleeve that prevents accidental gate opening.
- Keychain/accessory carabiners have no such rating; the script refers to climbing carabiners.
