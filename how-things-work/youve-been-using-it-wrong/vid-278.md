## Fact check

- Silica gel is porous amorphous silicon dioxide, the same compound as quartz sand.
- Its pores give it a very large internal surface area (hundreds of square metres per gram), which adsorbs water vapour.
- Packets are added to shoes, leather goods and electronics to prevent moisture damage and mould during shipping.
- Reusing them in bags and toolboxes is a common tip; they are labelled 'do not eat' and should be kept from children and pets.
