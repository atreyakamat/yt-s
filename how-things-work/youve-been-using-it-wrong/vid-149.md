## Fact check

- Many ceiling fans have a direction (reverse) switch on the motor housing.
- Downdraft creates a wind-chill effect by speeding evaporation from skin; fans don't lower air temperature (the motor adds a little heat), hence the 'turn off when you leave' advice.
- Warm air stratifies near the ceiling; running the fan in reverse at low speed draws air up and pushes warm air down the walls without a direct draught. Standard energy-saving advice.
