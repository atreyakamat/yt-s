## Fact check

- Major laptop/monitor makers advise not spraying liquid directly on the screen, because it can run into the bezel and electronics.
- Paper towels and tissues contain wood fibres and are discouraged for coated screens; microfibre is recommended.
- Ammonia- or strong alcohol-based cleaners can damage anti-glare/anti-reflective coatings.
- Recommended routine: power off, dry microfibre, then cloth lightly dampened with water; follow the manufacturer's guidance for anything stronger.
