## Fact check

- Front-loading washers have a folded rubber door gasket that can retain water, lint and detergent residue.
- Damp, dark residue supports mould and bacterial growth, causing black spots and musty odours.
- Manufacturers recommend leaving the door and dispenser drawer ajar after use, wiping the gasket, and running a periodic hot maintenance wash (often monthly).
