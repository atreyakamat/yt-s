## Fact check

- The snowflake symbol on heating controls/boilers denotes frost protection mode.
- Frost protection runs the heating only when the temperature falls to a low threshold, commonly around 5 °C (varies by model).
- Water expands about 9% on freezing, which can burst pipes.
- UK insurers and energy advice commonly recommend leaving heating on low/frost setting rather than fully off when away in winter.
