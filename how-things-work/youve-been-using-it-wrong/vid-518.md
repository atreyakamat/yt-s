## Fact check

- International (GINETEX/ISO 3758) care symbols: washtub (washing), triangle (bleaching), square (drying), iron (ironing), circle (professional cleaning).
- Dots in the washtub: one = 30 °C, two = 40 °C, three = 50 °C, four = 60 °C; dots on the iron indicate low/medium/high heat.
- One bar under the tub = mild (gentle) process, two bars = very mild.
- A circle inside a square = tumble drying; a cross means do not.
- Hand in the tub = hand wash; an empty triangle = any bleach allowed; a crossed triangle = do not bleach.
