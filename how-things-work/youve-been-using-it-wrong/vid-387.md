## Fact check

- Pleated paper portion ('soufflé') cups are made with folded sides that can be pulled outward to widen the cup into a shallow dish.
- Widening the cup makes dipping easier; this is a common tip, not an official instruction, so the script avoids claiming what the designers intended beyond the pleats letting it open.
