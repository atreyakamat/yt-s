## Fact check

- Fabric softeners deposit cationic surfactants that lubricate fibres; the hydrophobic coating builds up and reduces towel absorbency, which is why towel makers advise avoiding or minimising softener.
- Performance/wicking sportswear and microfibre makers advise against softener because it clogs the fabric structure.
- Shaking towels and tumble drying on low helps lift the pile; detergent overdosing also leaves stiff residue.
