## Fact check

- Household chlorine bleach is a dilute solution of sodium hypochlorite in water.
- Hypochlorite plus acids (e.g. some acidic toilet cleaners, vinegar) releases chlorine gas; plus ammonia it forms chloramines. Both are respiratory and eye irritants; public health agencies warn never to mix them.
- Safety advice (one product at a time, rinse, ventilate) follows product labels and public health guidance; this is general safety information, not medical advice.
