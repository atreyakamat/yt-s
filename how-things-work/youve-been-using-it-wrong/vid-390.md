## Fact check

- Washing-up liquids are surfactant solutions; surfactants have hydrophilic heads and hydrophobic tails and lift grease into micelles.
- Foam is air stabilised by surfactant films; foam level is not a measure of cleaning power. Low-foaming detergents (e.g. for dishwashers) clean well.
- Oils and fats destabilise foam, so suds collapsing in a sink is a sign the surfactant is being used up by grease.
