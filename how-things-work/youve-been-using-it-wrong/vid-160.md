## Fact check

- Stored-pressure extinguishers use a propellant gas to expel the agent; loss of pressure means the agent cannot be discharged even if the cylinder is full.
- The gauge shows charged (green), undercharged (left/red) and overcharged (right/red) zones.
- Fire safety guidance (e.g. NFPA 10 in the US) calls for monthly visual inspection and annual maintenance.
- CO2 extinguishers have no gauge and are checked by weight.
