## Fact check

- Lint is made of fibres shed from fabrics by abrasion during washing and tumbling.
- A clogged lint filter restricts airflow, lengthening drying time and raising energy use; manufacturers say to clean it after every load.
- US NFPA data: 'failure to clean' is the leading factor contributing to home clothes dryer fires.
