## Fact check

- Escalators have a small clearance between moving steps and the stationary skirt panels.
- Soft-soled shoes (e.g. rubber clogs), laces and loose clothing have been caught in this gap; safety agencies warn about it.
- Yellow demarcation lines on step edges and the step's back mark these hazard areas.
- Standard safety advice: stand in the middle, face forward, hold the handrail. Skirt deflector brushes are fitted on many escalators.
