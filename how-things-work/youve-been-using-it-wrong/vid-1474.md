## Fact check

- Most bicycle tyres have a recommended (or maximum) pressure range moulded on the sidewall, in psi and/or bar.
- Hand squeezing is unreliable compared with a pump gauge.
- Under-inflation causes pinch flats ('snakebites'), where the tube is pinched between tyre and rim.
- Over-inflation reduces comfort and grip.
- Inner tubes lose pressure gradually through permeation; regular checks (weekly or so) are common advice.
