## Fact check

- The 'memory effect' is associated with nickel-cadmium (and to a lesser extent NiMH) batteries after repeated partial discharges to the same level.
- Lithium-ion batteries do not suffer a practical memory effect; manufacturers say partial charging is fine.
- Deep discharge and time at very high or very low charge increase lithium-ion wear; keeping roughly 20-80% is common general guidance ('roughly').
