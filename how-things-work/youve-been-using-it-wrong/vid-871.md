## Fact check

- Fingerprint oils on a lens cause light scattering, which reduces contrast and creates haze, glare and streaks around bright light sources.
- Phone makers advise cleaning camera lenses with a soft, lint-free cloth.
- Abrasive materials and grit can scratch lens covers over time (paper towels contain wood fibres and can carry grit).
