## Fact check

- Continuous braking on long descents overheats brakes, causing brake fade; road safety guidance (e.g. UK Highway Code) advises selecting a lower gear on steep downhill stretches.
- Engine braking comes from pumping losses and friction when the throttle is closed and the wheels drive the engine; a lower gear raises engine speed and increases the effect.
- Many fuel-injected engines use deceleration fuel cut-off, injecting no fuel while engine braking.
- Automatic gear selectors often provide L, 1/2, or manual minus modes to hold low gears.
