## Fact check

- Many ranges and built-in ovens include a heated warming drawer with its own control, designed to hold cooked food or warm plates.
- Many older (especially US) gas ranges have a broiler (grill) drawer at the bottom, directly under the oven burner.
- On other models the drawer is unheated storage; the owner's manual says which, hence 'many' throughout.
