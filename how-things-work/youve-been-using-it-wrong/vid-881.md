## Fact check

- Many cling film, foil and baking paper boxes have perforated 'press to lock' tabs at each end; pushed in, they hold the roll's core so it doesn't lift out. Often printed on the box; widely shared as a kitchen tip.
- Not all brands include them, hence 'many' and 'not every box'.
