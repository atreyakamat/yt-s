## Fact check

- Cue tips are usually leather; without chalk, off-center strikes tend to slip, causing a miscue.
- Billiard chalk is not calcium carbonate; it is typically silica with an abrasive such as aluminum oxide, plus dye and binder.
- Chalk raises friction between tip and ball, allowing off-center hits that impart spin (draw, follow, side).
- Common advice is to brush or dab chalk onto the tip rather than grinding it, to avoid uneven wear.
