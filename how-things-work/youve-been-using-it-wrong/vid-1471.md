## Fact check

- OLED pixels emit their own light; black pixels are off, so darker content uses less display power.
- LCDs use a constant backlight; dark content blocks light but power draw is nearly unchanged.
- Studies (e.g., Purdue University, 2021) found dark mode savings on OLED phones are small at low/auto brightness (a few percent) and much larger at 100% brightness (around 40% display power or more).
