## Fact check

- Crisper drawer sliders open or close a vent; closed gives high humidity, open gives lower humidity. This is standard appliance-maker guidance.
- High humidity suits leafy greens and herbs that wilt as they lose water; low humidity suits fruit such as apples and pears that tend to rot.
- Many fruits (apples, pears, bananas, etc.) release ethylene, a gas that speeds ripening of nearby produce; venting lets some escape.
