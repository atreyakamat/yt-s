## Fact check

- Inductive (Qi) charging couples a transmitter and receiver coil; efficiency drops as coils move apart laterally or vertically.
- Lost energy becomes heat; phones throttle charging when hot to protect the battery.
- Charger makers warn that metal objects, magnetic/credit cards and thick cases reduce or block charging; foreign-object detection may stop charging.
- Gauge is illustrative of the trend, not a measured value.
