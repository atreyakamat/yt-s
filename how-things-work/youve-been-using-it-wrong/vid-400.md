## Fact check

- Extinguishers have a safety pin through the operating lever to prevent accidental discharge, held by a tamper seal that breaks when the pin is removed.
- A broken or missing seal indicates the extinguisher may have been used or tampered with.
- PASS (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep) is the standard taught method; training says to aim at the base of the fire. Bars are illustrative.
