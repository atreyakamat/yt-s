## Fact check

- Trickle vents are small adjustable vents in window frames that provide background ventilation when windows are closed (required on new windows under UK Building Regulations Part F).
- Everyday activities (breathing, cooking, bathing, drying clothes) release a lot of water vapour indoors.
- Moist air condenses on cold surfaces, leading to condensation and mould growth; background ventilation reduces this. Standard UK guidance.
