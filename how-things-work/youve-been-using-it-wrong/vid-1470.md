## Fact check

- All loads on daisy-chained leads pass through the first lead and the wall socket, which share one rating (13 A, about 3,000 W at 230 V in the UK).
- Typical kettles are 2-3 kW, fan heaters and hair dryers around 2 kW, so combinations can exceed 13 A.
- Each plug/socket connection adds contact resistance and a potential hot spot.
- UK fire services and Electrical Safety First advise against daisy-chaining extension leads and overloading sockets.
