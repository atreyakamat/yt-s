## Fact check

- Single-track vehicles turn by leaning; countersteering initiates the lean.
- Pressing the right handlebar forward momentarily steers the front wheel left, causing the bike to lean right and then turn right.
- Once leaned, the steering settles into the turn direction.
- Countersteering is taught in motorcycle safety courses for quick swerving; cyclists do it subconsciously. It applies above walking speed.
