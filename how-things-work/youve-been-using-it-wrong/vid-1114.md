## Fact check

- Common bike-fit guidance: leg nearly straight (slight bend) at the bottom of the pedal stroke; seated riders usually cannot put both feet flat on the ground.
- A too-low saddle increases knee flexion and makes pedalling less efficient.
- The heel-on-pedal method (leg straight with heel on pedal at the bottom) is a widely used starting point.
- Hip rocking while pedalling is a common sign of a saddle set too high. Not medical advice; it's general bike-fit guidance.
