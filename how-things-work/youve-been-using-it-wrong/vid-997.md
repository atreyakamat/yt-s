## Fact check

- For the same swing time, a grip near the end of the handle gives the head a larger radius and higher speed; standard technique is to grip near the end for driving and choke up only to start nails.
- Kinetic energy = 1/2 m v^2, so doubling head speed quadruples energy.
- Placing a block under the hammer head when pulling nails raises the fulcrum, improving leverage and protecting the surface.
- A relaxed grip reduces fatigue (common trade advice).
