## Fact check

- With the short arm in the socket, the long arm gives maximum leverage (torque); with the long arm in the socket, the short arm can be spun quickly.
- Ball-end hex keys allow angled entry but have reduced contact area; manufacturers advise against using the ball end for final tightening or breaking loose, as it can twist off or round the socket.
- Fully inserting the key maximises contact and reduces cam-out and rounding.
