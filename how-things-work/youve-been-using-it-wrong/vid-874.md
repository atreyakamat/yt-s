## Fact check

- Quick-release skewers use a cam lever; closing the lever generates the clamping force. The adjusting nut sets the tension.
- Bike manufacturers and mechanics warn that rotating the lever like a wingnut does not clamp the wheel properly.
- Common guidance: closing should require firm hand pressure and leave an imprint in the palm.
- Pointing the closed lever rearward/upward reduces the chance of it being snagged open.
