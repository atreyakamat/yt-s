## Fact check

- A single chirp roughly every 30-60 seconds is the standard low-battery warning on most smoke alarms, distinct from the loud alarm pattern.
- Battery voltage drops slightly at lower temperatures; homes are usually coolest in the early morning, so marginal batteries often trigger the warning at night. Fire safety organisations cite this explanation.
- Sealed 10-year alarms chirp to signal end of life.
