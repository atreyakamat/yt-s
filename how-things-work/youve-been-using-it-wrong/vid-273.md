## Fact check

- Manual day/night (prismatic) mirrors use wedge-shaped glass with a reflective coating on the back; the tab tilts the mirror so the driver sees the weak reflection from the uncoated front surface.
- Uncoated glass reflects about 4 % of light at normal incidence; a silvered/aluminised back reflects most of it (about 80 % or more after passing through the glass), labelled 'roughly'.
- Many newer cars use auto-dimming mirrors instead, hence 'many manual mirrors'.
