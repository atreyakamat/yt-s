## Fact check

- During braking, load transfers from the rear to the front wheel; under hard braking on a typical bike most of the possible deceleration comes from the front brake.
- A lightly loaded rear wheel locks and skids easily, giving less stopping force.
- Cycling guidance (e.g. bike instructors) recommends using both brakes, applying the front progressively rather than grabbing it.
- The gauge is qualitative ('most'), not a measured split.
