## Fact check

- The internal stopcock (main shut-off valve) isolates the incoming mains supply; in the UK it's commonly under the kitchen sink or near where the supply enters.
- Most stopcocks close clockwise.
- Water suppliers and plumbers advise checking/operating it periodically, as unused valves can seize, and leaving it slightly backed off from fully open.
