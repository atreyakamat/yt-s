## Fact check

- Many disc brake pads have a mechanical wear indicator, a spring-steel tab that contacts the rotor when the friction material is nearly worn, producing a high-pitched squeal.
- Other cars use electrical wear sensors embedded in the pad that trigger a dashboard warning light.
- Brake noise can also come from surface rust/moisture after damp nights, dust, or glazing, so a squeak isn't always wear.
