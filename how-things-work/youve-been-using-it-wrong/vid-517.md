## Fact check

- The numbered collar on a drill/driver is an adjustable torque clutch; the drill-bit symbol locks it out for drilling.
- Typical designs use springs pressing balls or pins against raised features on a ring gear of the planetary gearbox; when torque exceeds the spring setting the ring gear slips and the clutch ratchets audibly.
- Lower settings release at lower torque, which helps avoid stripping or overdriving small screws; starting low and stepping up is standard advice.
