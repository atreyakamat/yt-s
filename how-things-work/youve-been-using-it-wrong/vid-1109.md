## Fact check

- Split-system indoor units have removable mesh air filters behind the front panel that protect the evaporator coil.
- Clogged filters reduce airflow, cutting capacity and efficiency.
- Low airflow over the evaporator can drop coil temperature below freezing, causing icing.
- Manufacturers typically recommend washing filters every two weeks to a month in regular use, so 'check your manual'.
