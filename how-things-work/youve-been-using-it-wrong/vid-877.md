## Fact check

- Flat washers distribute the clamping load of a nut or bolt head over a larger bearing area, reducing surface pressure and embedding in soft materials.
- They provide a smooth bearing surface for the turning element, protecting finishes; standard practice places the washer under the part being turned.
- Washers bridge oversized or slotted holes so the fastener can't pull through.
