## Fact check

- Many laptops have air intakes on the underside; soft surfaces can block them.
- With restricted airflow, CPU/GPU temperatures rise and the chip throttles its clock speed to protect itself.
- Lithium-ion batteries degrade faster at high temperatures.
- Using a hard flat surface under the laptop keeps vents clear.
