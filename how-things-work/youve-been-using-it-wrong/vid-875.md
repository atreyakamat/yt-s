## Fact check

- Interstate numbering: odd-numbered primary routes run north-south and increase from west to east (I-5 on the Pacific coast, I-95 on the Atlantic); even-numbered routes run east-west and increase from south to north (I-10 in the south, I-90 in the north).
- I-35 runs through the middle of the country (Texas to Minnesota); I-40 crosses the middle east-west.
- Three-digit auxiliary routes: an odd first digit usually marks a spur, an even first digit a loop or bypass; the last two digits give the parent route. There are exceptions, hence 'usually'.
