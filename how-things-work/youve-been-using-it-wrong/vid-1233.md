## Fact check

- The pressure moulded on the sidewall is the tyre's maximum inflation pressure, not the recommended operating pressure.
- Vehicle makers specify recommended pressures on a placard (driver's door jamb, fuel filler flap) and in the owner's manual.
- Over-inflation reduces the contact patch (less grip) and ride comfort.
- Recommended pressures are for cold tyres; driving heats the air and raises pressure.
