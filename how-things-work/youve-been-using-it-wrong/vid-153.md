## Fact check

- Head restraints are fitted to reduce whiplash injury in rear impacts by limiting backward head movement relative to the torso (road safety bodies such as IIHS and Thatcham).
- Safety guidance: the top of the restraint should be at least level with the top of the head (or no lower than eye level) and as close to the back of the head as practical.
- A restraint left in its lowest position can sit below the head's centre of gravity and offers less protection. No medical advice is given; the script describes the restraint's purpose and setup only.
