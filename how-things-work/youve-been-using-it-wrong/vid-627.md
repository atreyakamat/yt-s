## Fact check

- Aluminium foil is produced by repeated rolling between polished rollers.
- In the final pass two sheets are rolled together; the sides in contact with the polished rollers come out shiny, the sides in contact with each other come out matte.
- Manufacturers state the difference in heat reflection is negligible for normal cooking. (The bars show it as 50/50 to make that point, not as a measurement.)
- Non-stick foil has a coating on one side, which should face the food per the packaging.
