## Fact check

- Airline safety briefings instruct passengers to inflate the life vest only after leaving the aircraft.
- Vests inflate from CO2 cartridges when the tab is pulled; a mouthpiece allows topping up.
- An inflated vest in a flooding cabin can push the wearer upward and make it hard to swim to an exit; investigators have cited this in ditching accidents.
- A bulky inflated vest also slows movement through aisles and exits. The grid is an illustration, not a statistic.
