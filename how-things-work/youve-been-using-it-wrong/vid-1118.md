## Fact check

- Wool fibres have overlapping cuticle scales.
- Warmth, moisture and alkaline detergent swell the fibres and raise the scales.
- The directional friction of the scales (easier one way than the other) plus agitation makes fibres migrate and entangle irreversibly: felting.
- Care advice: cool water, wool cycle or hand wash, minimal agitation, dry flat.
