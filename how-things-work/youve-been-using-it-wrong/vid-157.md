## Fact check

- Spirit level vials are very slightly curved (barrel-shaped inside) so the bubble rests at the highest point and centres when level.
- They are filled with alcohol-based spirit (often ethanol), which has a low freezing point and low viscosity, so the bubble moves freely.
- Impacts can shift a vial or warp the frame and make the level inaccurate.
- The reversal (flip) test, reading on the same spot then rotating 180 degrees end to end, is the standard way to check a level's accuracy.
