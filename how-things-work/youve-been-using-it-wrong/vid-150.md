## Fact check

- Washing machines clean largely through mechanical action (lifting and dropping), plus chemistry, heat and time.
- Overdosing creates excess suds that cushion the load and can leave detergent residue that rinsing doesn't remove; manufacturers warn against it, especially in front-loaders.
- Detergent residue can leave fabrics stiff and attract soil and odours.
- Recommended doses on packs vary by water hardness, load size and soil level; soft water needs less.
