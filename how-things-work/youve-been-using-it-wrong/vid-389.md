## Fact check

- Radiators normally have a control valve (manual or thermostatic) on one end and a lockshield valve, usually under a plastic cap, on the other.
- Water follows the path of least resistance, so radiators near the pump/boiler get more flow; balancing partially closes lockshields on those to divert flow to distant radiators.
- The bars show relative flow only, no units, as an illustration.
