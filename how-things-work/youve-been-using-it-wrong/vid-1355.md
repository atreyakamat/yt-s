## Fact check

- Bridge decks lose heat to the air on both their upper and lower surfaces and lack the heat stored in the ground beneath a normal road, so they cool faster and can freeze first.
- Ground-level roads gain heat from the soil below, which delays freezing overnight.
- This is why many road authorities post 'Bridge ices before road' warning signs.
