## Fact check

- Derailleur shifting under heavy pedalling load is harder for the chain to move across cogs and can cause slipping, noisy shifts or dropped chains.
- High-load shifting increases wear on chain and cogs.
- Cycling coaches and mechanics advise shifting to an easier gear before a climb and easing pedal pressure briefly when shifting under load.
