## Fact check

- Rear loading pitches the car nose-up, raising headlamp aim and causing glare for oncoming drivers.
- EU-type cars have headlamp levelling: a dashboard dial (0 for driver only, higher numbers for passengers/luggage) drives small actuators that tilt the lamps down.
- Cars with high-output lamps such as xenon/many LED units typically have automatic self-levelling (required for xenon in the EU).
