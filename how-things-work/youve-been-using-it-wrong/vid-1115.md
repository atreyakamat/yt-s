## Fact check

- Materials recovery facilities sort on fast conveyors; tied bags of recyclables are usually removed unopened as contamination because staff can't safely open them.
- Plastic bags and film wrap around rotating disc ('star') screens that separate paper from containers, forcing shutdowns so workers can cut them free.
- Rules differ between councils and cities, hence 'unless your local rules say otherwise'.
