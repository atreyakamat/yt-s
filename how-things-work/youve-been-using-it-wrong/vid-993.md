## Fact check

- Rear door child safety locks are a lever or key slot on the door's shut face; when engaged, the interior handle is decoupled from the latch while the exterior handle still works.
- Many newer cars use an electronic child lock switch on the driver's door instead.
- A rear door that won't open from inside is very often just the child lock engaged.
