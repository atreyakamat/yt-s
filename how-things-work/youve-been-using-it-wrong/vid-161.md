## Fact check

- Pan handle holes are primarily for hanging cookware on hooks or rails, which saves space and avoids scratches from stacking.
- Resting a spoon through the handle hole is a widely shared kitchen tip; it works on many pans but depends on the hole and spoon size, hence 'on many pans' and 'not every hole fits'.
