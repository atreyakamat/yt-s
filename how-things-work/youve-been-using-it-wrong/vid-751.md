## Fact check

- Multi-camera phones offer optical focal lengths at fixed steps (e.g., 0.5x, 1x, 2x/3x/5x depending on model).
- Zoom levels beyond or between optical lenses use digital zoom (crop and upscale, often with multi-frame processing), reducing true resolution.
- At 2x digital zoom only a quarter of the sensor area (1/2 width x 1/2 height) covers the frame, as the grid shows.
