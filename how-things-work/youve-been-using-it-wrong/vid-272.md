## Fact check

- Routers are embedded computers; reboots clear memory leaks, stale tables and stuck processes.
- Capacitors in power circuits can hold charge briefly after unplugging.
- ISPs commonly advise waiting around 30 seconds before reconnecting so the device fully powers down and the session can reset.
- Full start-up commonly takes one to a few minutes; 120 s is illustrative.
