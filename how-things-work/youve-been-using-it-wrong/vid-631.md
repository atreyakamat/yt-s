## Fact check

- IR remote signals reflect off walls and ceilings; with a sensitive receiver, indirect bounces often operate the TV (depends on room and distance, hence 'often').
- Many modern TV and streaming remotes use RF (Bluetooth or proprietary radio), which doesn't need line of sight.
- Radio at 2.4 GHz passes through soft furnishings and thin wood cabinets.
