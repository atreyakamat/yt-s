## Fact check

- Microfibre is generally defined as fibre finer than 1 denier (roughly under 10 µm); cleaning microfibre is usually split polyester/polyamide. Human hair is roughly 50-100 µm, cotton fibres about 12-20 µm.
- Splitting creates wedge-shaped filaments and capillary spaces that trap dust, oils and grime.
- Manufacturers advise no fabric softener (it coats fibres and clogs the spaces), washing separately from lint-shedding cotton, and avoiding high heat (polyester can melt).
