## Fact check

- Vacuum suction depends on airflow; all intake air exits through filters, so clogged filters or an overfull bin/bag reduce airflow and suction.
- Manufacturers instruct emptying bins at the 'max' line and washing filters where washable, letting them dry completely (often 24 hours) before refitting.
- Damp filters can develop mould and odours.
- Many vacuums have thermal cut-outs that stop the motor when blocked airflow causes overheating.
