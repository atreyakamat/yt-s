## Fact check

- Windscreen fog is condensation of water vapour (largely from occupants' breath) on glass colder than the air's dew point.
- The A/C evaporator cools air below its dew point, condensing moisture out; the heater core then reheats it, giving warm dry air that evaporates fog quickly.
- Many cars' defrost setting engages the A/C compressor and fresh-air intake automatically (compressors may not run in very low temperatures).
