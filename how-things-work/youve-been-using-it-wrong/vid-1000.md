## Fact check

- A thermometer tip approaches body temperature roughly exponentially: fast at first, then slowing. Bars are illustrative.
- Many digital thermometers beep when the rate of rise falls below a threshold (the reading has nearly levelled), rather than at a true final value.
- Predictive thermometers estimate the final temperature from the early rise curve.
- Placement and timing instructions vary by model. Educational only, not medical advice.
