## Fact check

- Aircraft passenger seatbelts use a lift-lever buckle; you lift the flap to release.
- Cabin crew demonstrate fastening and releasing in the safety briefing; passengers unfamiliar with the buckle may try to press like a car belt.
- Lift-lever buckles are quick to release in an emergency.
- Airlines advise wearing the belt low and tight across the hips and keeping it fastened when seated, since clear-air turbulence can occur without warning.
