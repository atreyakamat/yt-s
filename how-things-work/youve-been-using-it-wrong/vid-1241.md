## Fact check

- Starch released by pasta or potatoes stabilises bubble films, so foam builds up and can boil over; plain water bubbles burst at the surface.
- A dry, cooler wooden spoon destabilises bubbles that touch it (and wood is hydrophobic enough when dry), so the foam collapses; this is a widely explained kitchen trick.
- The effect fades as the spoon heats up and gets wet; reducing heat or removing the lid is the reliable fix.
