## Fact check

- Two common types → cup (sink) plungers and flange (toilet) plungers are the standard household kinds.
- Flange sleeve fits the toilet outlet → the fold-out rubber flange seals inside the curved trap opening.
- Plungers work by moving water → a sealed plunger pushes and pulls the water column to loosen a blockage.
- Flat cup leaks on a curved bowl → it can't seal the curved outlet, so pressure escapes around the rim.
