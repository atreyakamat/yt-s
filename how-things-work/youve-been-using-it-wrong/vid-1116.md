## Fact check

- Contactless cards contain an antenna coil (typically running around the card's perimeter) connected to the chip; they have no battery and are powered by the reader's 13.56 MHz field.
- The working range is a few centimetres (NFC/ISO 14443).
- Inductive coupling is strongest when the card's coil is parallel to the reader's antenna.
- Multiple cards in the field can cause collisions or the wrong card to be selected ('card clash').
