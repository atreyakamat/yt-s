## Fact check

- NFPA 291 recommends colouring hydrant bonnets and caps by rated flow: light blue 1,500 gpm or more (Class AA), green 1,000-1,499 (Class A), orange 500-999 (Class B), red below 500 (Class C).
- It is a recommendation; adoption varies by city, hence 'not every city follows it'.
- Available flow depends largely on the size and pressure of the water main feeding the hydrant.
