# vid-1642 · Why you should unsubscribe from marketing emails

## Hook

## Fact check (verify before posting)

CAN-SPAM (US) and GDPR/ePrivacy require easy opt-out for marketing emails; clicking links in spam can confirm an address is active. The '5 a day' number is advice, not a rule.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Clean your inbox with one daily habit.
2. Marketing emails pile up without you noticing.
3. Every unsubscribe removes a daily distraction.
4. Legit senders must include a working unsubscribe link.
5. Don't click unsubscribe on obvious spam.
6. Aim for five a day until the inbox is calm.
