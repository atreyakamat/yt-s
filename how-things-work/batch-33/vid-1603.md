## Fact check (verify before posting)

Depner et al. (2019, Current Biology): weekend recovery sleep did not prevent metabolic dysregulation in a small lab study of ~36 adults; 'social jetlag' linked to health risks. Evidence is mixed; don't state as proven for everyone.

## Voiceover (read-through)

3. Sleeping in helps you feel better for a day.
4. But research finds other effects linger after short sleep.
5. A big weekend shift also confuses your body clock.
6. Steady bedtimes beat catching up.
