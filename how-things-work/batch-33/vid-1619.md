## Fact check (verify before posting)

Quadrantids peak around 3-4 Jan; parent body usually given as asteroid 2003 EH1 (possibly a dormant comet); ZHR roughly 80-120 under ideal conditions; peak is narrow (~6 hours). Visibility depends on location and Moon. Verified: Peak Jan 3-4, ZHR 120 (yearly max 82-146), parent 2003 EH1, above half max only ~8 h: wording changed from about 100 to 100+. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrantids
