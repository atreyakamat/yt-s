# vid-1601 · How your heart rate shows your fitness

## Hook

- Backup 1: "Your pulse is a fitness score."
- Backup 2: "Why athletes' hearts beat slower."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Normal adult resting heart rate 60-100 bpm (AHA); well-trained athletes often 40-60. Lower can reflect fitness but very low with symptoms warrants medical advice. Add 'not medical advice'.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. A lower resting heart rate often means fitter.
2. Most adults rest between sixty and one hundred beats a minute.
3. Regular exercise makes the heart pump more per beat.
4. So it needs fewer beats to move the same blood.
5. Athletes may rest in the forties or fifties.
6. Check it first thing in the morning.
