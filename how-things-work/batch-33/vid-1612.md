## Fact check (verify before posting)

Emmons & McCullough (2003) gratitude journaling over ~10 weeks raised self-reported wellbeing; Seligman et al. (2005) 'Three Good Things' improved happiness for six months in some groups. Replications show small effects; not a clinical treatment. Verified: Seligman 2005 three-good-things, asked for one week, effects up to six months: reworded from ten weeks (not found in sources). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gratitude
