## Fact check (verify before posting)

In the northern hemisphere the winter full Moon is high (like the summer Sun) because it sits opposite the low winter Sun; high altitude means less atmospheric extinction. Snow albedo is ~0.8 so reflects moonlight. Good.

4. Less air in the way makes it look brighter.
5. Snow on the ground reflects it too.
6. That's why winter nights feel bright.
