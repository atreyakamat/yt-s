# vid-1631 · Why trees lose their leaves

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Deciduous trees form an abscission layer at the petiole base in response to shorter days/cooler temps; chlorophyll breaks down revealing carotenoids; anthocyanins (reds) are made fresh in autumn. Tree shed leaves to avoid water loss in freezing soil.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Trees drop leaves to survive winter.
2. Leaves lose water and can't work in freezing soil.
3. Short days tell the tree to stop making chlorophyll.
4. Other pigments show through as autumn colours.
5. A layer of cells seals the leaf stem shut.
6. The tree rests until spring.
