# vid-1604 · How blue light affects sleep

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Screens at night can delay sleep."
- Backup 1: "Why your phone wrecks your bedtime."
- Backup 2: "Blue light is only half the story."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Short-wavelength (~460-480 nm) light most strongly suppresses melatonin via melanopsin-containing ganglion cells; effect size of device screens is debated and content/arousal also matter. Reasonable.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Screens at night can delay sleep.
2. Blue-rich light is the strongest signal to your body clock.
3. It can suppress the sleep hormone melatonin.
4. The content on screens can keep you alert too.
5. Night modes help a little but don't fix it.
6. Dim lights and stop screens an hour before bed.
