## Fact check (verify before posting)

Window frost forms when surface temp is below freezing and below the dew/frost point; water vapour deposits as ice (deposition). Patterns follow imperfections; branching from diffusion-limited growth. Good.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Frost draws feathers on your windows.
2. The glass gets colder than the air inside.
3. Water vapour touches it and freezes directly.
4. That is called deposition, vapour to ice.
5. Tiny scratches and dust give crystals a starting point.
6. Branches grow out as more vapour attaches.
