# vid-1632 · Why your breath looks like smoke in the cold

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Your breath turns visible when it's cold."
- Backup 1: "Why you puff clouds outside."
- Backup 2: "Your breath is a tiny cloud."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Exhaled air is near body temperature and ~100% relative humidity; mixing with cold air cools it below its dew point so vapour condenses into micro-droplets visible via Mie scattering. 'Smoke' is a visual simile.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Your breath turns visible when it's cold.
2. Your breath is warm and full of water vapour.
3. Cold air can't hold as much moisture.
4. The vapour condenses into tiny droplets.
5. Those droplets scatter light, so you see a cloud.
6. It's the same trick as clouds in the sky.
