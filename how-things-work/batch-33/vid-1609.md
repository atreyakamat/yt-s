## Fact check (verify before posting)

Pomodoro Technique by Francesco Cirillo (late 1980s), named for tomato-shaped timer; classic 25/5 with a 15-30 min break after four. Evidence of benefits is anecdotal/limited; timed breaks generally help vigilance. Verified: Cirillo, late 1980s, 25 min work, short break, long break after four, tomato timer: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique
