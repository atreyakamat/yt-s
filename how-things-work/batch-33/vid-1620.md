# vid-1620 · Why the Sun rises in the east

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Earth's spin makes the Sun rise east."
- Backup 1: "Why the Sun always rises in the east."
- Backup 2: "On Venus it rises in the west."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Earth rotates west-to-east; equatorial speed ~1,670 km/h (~465 m/s); Venus rotates retrograde (about 243 Earth days), so the Sun rises in the west there (hidden by thick clouds). Verified: Equatorial speed about 1,674 km/h (1,670 ok): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth%27s_rotation

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Earth's spin makes the Sun rise east.
2. Earth rotates eastward, once a day.
3. So the Sun appears to move west.
4. Rotation speed at the equator is about 1,670 km/h.
5. You don't feel it because you move with it.
6. Venus spins the other way, so its Sun rises in the west.
