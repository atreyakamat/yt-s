## Fact check (verify before posting)

Day length at mid-northern latitudes increases only seconds per day just after the winter solstice, reaching ~2-3 minutes per day near the March equinox (depends on latitude). Verified: Day length gain: a minute or two/day average, hedged: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_solstice
