## Fact check

- Polymer clay is PVC resin plus liquid plasticizer (plastisol), gelling agents, typically no clay minerals. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymer_clay
- Bake 265-275 F (129-135 C) for 15 minutes per 1/4 inch (6.4 mm). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymer_clay
- Remains workable until cured. 1939 formulation brought to Käthe Kruse, daughter Sophie 'Fifi' used it; later sold to Eberhard Faber as FIMO; early formulations were developed as possible replacement for Bakelite. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymer_clay
