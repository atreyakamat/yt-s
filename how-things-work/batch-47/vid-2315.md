## Fact check

- SMS was designed by Friedhelm Hillebrand and colleagues; he found most postcard and telex messages under 160 characters, giving the limit. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMS
- First text message sent in December 1992. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMS
- Messages using non-GSM characters (UCS-2) are limited to 70 characters per segment; concatenated SMS splits long texts. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMS
- Grid (18 of 20) is illustrative; the script only says nearly always under about 160.
- Wikipedia: Hillebrand and Bernard Ghillebaert developed the SMS concept in 1984; he argued about 160 characters sufficed based on his observations and typical postcard and telex lengths. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SMS
