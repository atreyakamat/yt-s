## Fact check

- Louis Camille Maillard described the reaction in 1912; John E. Hodge established its mechanism in 1953. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maillard_reaction
- Browning proceeds rapidly from about 140 to 165 °C and produces melanoidins and hundreds of flavour compounds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maillard_reaction
- Searing browns meat mostly through the Maillard reaction; bread crust, coffee roasting and malted barley also brown this way. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maillard_reaction
- Water boils around 100 degrees C and the surface must be free of water, above 150 °C, for a brown crust. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Searing
