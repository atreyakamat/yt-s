## Fact check

- Panoramic photos are made by stitching overlapping images, matching features and blending seams. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_stitching
- Moving subjects between frames cause ghosting or cut-off objects in stitched panoramas. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_stitching
- Phones use motion sensors to help estimate camera rotation (reasoning; no figure given). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panoramic_photography
