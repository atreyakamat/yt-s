## Fact check

- Hair colour comes from two melanins, black-brown eumelanin and reddish pheomelanin, made by melanocytes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_hair_color
- Gray or white hair is not caused by a true gray or white pigment but by a lack of melanin; the clear hairs look gray or white because of how light is reflected. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_hair_color
- Greying normally begins in the early to mid-twenties in men and late twenties in women; over 60 percent of Americans have some gray hair by 40; the age it starts seems almost entirely due to genetics. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_hair_color
- Usual order of graying: nose hair, hair on the head, beard, body hair, eyebrows. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_hair_color
