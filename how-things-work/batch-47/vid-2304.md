## Fact check

- The chain's end knob rides in a slot and is released through a wider opening: standard slide-chain design, general mechanism, no figures used.
- Chains limit how far the door opens and act as a visitor check; the weak point is the fixing screws in the frame. Reasoning only, no numbers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Door_chain
- Wikipedia confirms door chains are weak to force: they usually ship with very short screws that rip out, and a chain should not let the door open far enough for a hand to reach through. It does not describe the slider-release mechanism, which is standard slide-chain design. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Door_chain
