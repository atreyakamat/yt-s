## Fact check

- There are five types of primary tooth with four of each, so 20 baby teeth. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deciduous_teeth
- Primary tooth development starts at the sixth week of tooth development; they erupt during infancy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deciduous_teeth
- Erupting permanent teeth push on the roots of primary teeth, which are dissolved by odontoclasts (root resorption). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deciduous_teeth
- Shedding is called exfoliation and may last from six to twelve years of age. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deciduous_teeth
- Primary teeth maintain arch length, guide the eruption path of permanent teeth, and jaw muscles and bones depend on them. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deciduous_teeth
