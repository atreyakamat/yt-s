## Fact check

- Pavement is usually grooved so standing water drains into the grooves while tyre contact patches stay on the surface, reducing hydroplaning. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway
- Dynamic aquaplaning needs a water film of at least 2.5 mm (1/10 inch). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroplaning
- In 1965 a US delegation visited the Royal Aircraft Establishment at Farnborough to see its grooved runway, prompting an FAA/NASA study; most major airports have since adopted grooving. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroplaning
