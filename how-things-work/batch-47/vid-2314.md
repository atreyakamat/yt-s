## Fact check

- U-locks resist high-leverage tools such as bolt cutters better than most other lock types. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_lock
- A U-lock is a rigid metal ring in the shape of the letter U (also called a D-lock), attached to a crossbar. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_lock
- Wikipedia confirms U-locks resist bolt cutters better than most other types; it does not mention the gap size, so the script says 'less room' (design reasoning, softened). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_lock
- Many models lock both ends of the shackle: model-dependent, so the script says many.
- Advice to fit the lock snugly around frame, wheel and fixed object is general guidance.
