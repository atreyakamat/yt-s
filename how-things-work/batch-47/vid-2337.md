## Fact check

- Dust devil forms from hot ground heating air, rising updraft that starts to rotate and intensify as it stretches; most under 0.9 m wide, largest about 90 m; most dissipate in under a minute, rare ones over 20 minutes; forms under fair weather skies unlike tornadoes. The ice-skater analogy is standard angular momentum conservation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dust_devil
