## Fact check

- The adult Eustachian tube is about 35 mm long and 3 mm in diameter. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eustachian_tube
- It normally stays closed and opens briefly to equalise middle ear pressure; swallowing and yawning open it; chewing gum also works. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eustachian_tube
- On ascent air in the middle ear expands and escapes; on descent it shrinks and the tube must be actively opened, often with a pop. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eustachian_tube
