## Fact check

- Halo has about 22 degree radius, roughly a hand at arm's length; made by refraction in hexagonal ice crystals in thin cirrus or cirrostratus; inner edge reddish, outer bluish; darker sky inside; weather folklore is unreliable. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/22%C2%B0_halo
