## Fact check

- Saccade is French for 'jerk'; the word appears to have been coined in the 1870s by Émile Javal, who observed eye movement in silent reading. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saccade
- Saccades last about 20-200 ms depending on size; 20-30 ms is typical in language reading. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saccade
- Peak angular speed reaches up to 700 degrees per second in humans for great saccades; among the fastest movements of the human eye. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saccade
- The fovea gives the high-resolution part of vision and covers only about 1-2 degrees; saccades let the brain assemble a coherent continuous mental representation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saccade
- Velocity-based algorithms are a common way to detect saccades in eye tracking. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saccade
