## Fact check

- Excavators use hydraulic cylinders for boom, stick and bucket, and hydraulic motors for swing and travel, powered by engine-driven pumps. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excavator
- Hydraulic fluid is effectively incompressible and pressure transmits through it (Pascal's principle). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydraulics
- No numbers used.
