## Fact check

- Beating unfolds (denatures) egg white proteins, which bond into a network that traps air; whites expand six to eight times their size. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meringue
- Cream of tartar lowers pH and helps the proteins form a stronger network; sugar draws water out and strengthens the foam; add it slowly because all at once can collapse the network. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meringue
- The name meringue first appeared in print in 1691 (Massialot); earliest recognisable recipe 1604 (Elinor Fettiplace). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meringue
