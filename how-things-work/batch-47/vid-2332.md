## Fact check

- Three-bladed rotors are the common modern design as a balance of efficiency, loads and cost. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_turbine_design
- Blade tip speed is much higher than root speed, so blades are twisted. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_turbine_aerodynamics
- Fewer blades mean cheaper rotors but higher rotation speed and noise. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_turbine_design
- Bar values are illustrative, not measured.
- Verified: three blades give smoother yaw loads than two; efficiency gains diminish beyond three. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wind_turbine_design
