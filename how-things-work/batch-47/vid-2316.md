## Fact check

- BCBP standard used by more than 200 airlines, published by IATA in 2005. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boarding_pass
- All boarding passes barcoded by 2010 (IATA program). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boarding_pass
- Paper passes use PDF417; mobile passes can use Aztec, Data Matrix or QR. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boarding_pass
- Phone apps can reveal name, date of birth, airports and the 6-character PNR booking code. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boarding_pass
- 2020 case: a posted photo exposed enough information to access a Qantas account. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boarding_pass
- Cropping and shredding advice is general privacy practice, no numeric claim.
