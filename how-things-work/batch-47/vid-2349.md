## Fact check

- Named for Japanese astrophysicist Kōryō Miura; unfolds with a single pull on two opposite ends, folds by pushing together; rigid origami, continuous motion, parallelograms (example angles 84 and 96 degrees). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miura_fold
- Space Flyer Unit 2D Array deployed from a Miura-folded state (article dates it 1996); Ian Bain's 1980 New Scientist piece 'The Miura-ori map'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miura_fold
