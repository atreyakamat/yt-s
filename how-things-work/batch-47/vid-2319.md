## Fact check

- Knit stitch loop passes through previous loop from below, purl from above; knit seen from one side looks like purl from the other; knit = stacked V's, purl = wavy horizontal lines. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knitting
- Each new loop passes through an existing one to secure it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knitting
- William Lee of Nottinghamshire invented the first stocking frame in 1589; Paris knitters' organization recorded from 1268. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knitting
