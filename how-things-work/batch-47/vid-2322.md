## Fact check

- Home batteries store surplus solar for later use and are managed by battery-management electronics. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grid_energy_storage
- Most home batteries use lithium-ion chemistries such as lithium iron phosphate. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate_battery
- Time-of-use tariffs make off-peak charging worthwhile. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-of-use_pricing
- Bar values are illustrative, not measured.
