## Fact check

- Passive RFID tags have no battery and are powered by the reader's radio or magnetic field; they reply by changing how they load that field. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio-frequency_identification
- Access control compares the tag's ID against a list held by a controller. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Access_control
