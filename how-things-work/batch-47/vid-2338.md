## Fact check

- Scoring lets the dough expand without tearing the skin, lets moisture escape and gas out, and lets the baker control where the loaf opens (blooms). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bread_lame
- A lame is a double-sided blade, often slightly concave. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bread_lame
- A baguette's usual length is about 65 cm (can reach 1 m). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baguette
