## Fact check

- Runway number is roughly magnetic heading divided by 10; 09 east, 18 south, 27 west, 36 north (north is 360, not 0). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway
- The two ends of a runway differ by 18 (15 becomes 33). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway
- Parallel runways get Left, Center, Right suffixes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway
- Magnetic drift forces renumbering; Fairbanks renames its runways roughly every 24 years. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runway
