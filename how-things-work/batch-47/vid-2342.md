## Fact check

- Hydroelectric plants convert the potential energy of stored water into electricity via turbines and generators. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroelectricity
- Penstocks carry water from reservoir to turbines. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penstock
- Power depends on flow rate and head (height). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroelectricity
