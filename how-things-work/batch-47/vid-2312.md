## Fact check

- Many devices draw a small power in standby even when seemingly off. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standby_power
- 1 W continuously for 8,760 hours (24 x 365) = 8.76 kWh. Arithmetic.
- Typical standby draw is around a watt or a few watts for modern devices (varies; older ones draw more). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standby_power
- Verified: each watt run continuously uses about 9 kWh a year (1 W x 8760 h = 8.76 kWh). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standby_power
