## Fact check

- Truck mixers carry a rotating drum with helical (spiral) blades; one direction mixes, the opposite discharges the concrete. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete_mixer
- Cement hardens through a chemical reaction with water (hydration), so wet concrete must be placed within a limited time. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concrete
