## Fact check

- Beam melts, burns, vaporizes or is blown away by gas; thermal stress cracking used for glass. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_cutting
- Narrowest focused spot generally under 0.32 mm (script: under a third of a millimetre); kerf as small as 0.10 mm. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_cutting
- First production laser cutting machine in 1965 drilled holes in diamond dies (Western Electric); 1967 British laser-assisted oxygen jet cutting; early 1970s titanium for aerospace. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laser_cutting
