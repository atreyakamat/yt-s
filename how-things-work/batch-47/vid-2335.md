## Fact check

- Front cameras commonly show a mirrored preview, and many phones offer a setting to save selfies mirrored or not. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selfie
- People tend to prefer their mirror image because they see it most (mere-exposure effect). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mere-exposure_effect
- Human faces are not perfectly symmetrical. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facial_symmetry
