## Fact check

- Counterbalance forklifts use a heavy rear counterweight to offset the load carried ahead of the front axle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forklift
- Rated capacity depends on load centre distance and is shown on the data plate. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forklift
- Many forklifts have a pivoting rear axle giving a three-point support known as the stability triangle. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forklift
- Bar values are illustrative percentages of rated capacity, not measurements.
