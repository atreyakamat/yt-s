## Fact check

- Fructose caramelises at 105 °C, glucose at 150 °C, sucrose at 170 °C. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caramelization
- Caramelisation is a non-enzymatic, pyrolytic process that needs no amino acids, unlike the Maillard reaction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caramelization
- Sucrose is a disaccharide that breaks into fructose and glucose; volatile products include diacetyl (butter-like). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caramelization
- Brown colour comes from three polymer groups: caramelans, caramelens and caramelins. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caramelization
- Above the Maillard range, caramelisation and then pyrolysis become more pronounced. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maillard_reaction
