## Fact check

- US postal zones for large cities were introduced in 1943 and ZIP codes launched in 1963; ZIP stands for Zone Improvement Plan; ZIP+4 came in 1983. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZIP_Code
- The first ZIP digit identifies a broad group of states, later digits narrow it. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZIP_Code
- The UK postcode system was trialled in Norwich from 1959. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postcodes_in_the_United_Kingdom
- Verified: postal zones for 124 large cities in May 1943; five-digit ZIP Codes from 1 July 1963; ZIP = Zone Improvement Plan; ZIP+4 in 1983; first digit = region. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZIP_Code
- Verified: UK postcode trial in Norwich announced 28 July 1959. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postcodes_in_the_United_Kingdom
