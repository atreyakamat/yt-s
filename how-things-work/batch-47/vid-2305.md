## Fact check

- Apple and Google each run a push service (APNs and Firebase Cloud Messaging) that apps use to reach devices. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Push_Notification_service
- Devices keep a persistent connection to the push service so apps do not poll individually. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Push_technology
- Last scene runs into the hook: '...It's waiting.'
