## Fact check

- Steel drums are formed from sheet steel into a tube and welded along the side seam. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_(container)
- Drums have top and bottom chimes (rims); most steel drums have reinforcing rolling hoops of thickened metal or plastic that strengthen them so they can be turned on their sides and rolled when filled. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_(container)
- Many drums are just under 880 mm tall and just under 610 mm in diameter, nominal volume 208 L (55 US gal); 25 US gal (95 L) drums share the same height, so mixed pallets stack easily (script says 'nearly 88 centimetres'). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_(container)
- Henry Wehrhahn received two patents in December 1905 leading to the modern 55-gallon steel drum; use of 200-litre drums became widespread in World War II. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_(container)
