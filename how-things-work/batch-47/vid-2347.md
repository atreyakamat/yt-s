## Fact check

- Dew forms when a surface cools below the dew point and vapour condenses; calm clear nights favour it; non-metallic insulated surfaces such as grass collect most, shiny metal less; theoretical maximum about 0.8 mm per night, measured rarely above 0.5 mm. Bar chart values for grass vs metal are illustrative only. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dew
