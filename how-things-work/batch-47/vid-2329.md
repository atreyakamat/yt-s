## Fact check

- Jacquard patented the machine in 1804; earlier work by Bouchon (1725), Falcon (1728), Vaucanson (1740). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacquard_machine
- One card per row of the design; rods pass through holes unmoved, where there is no hole the rod is pushed aside and its hook displaced; undisplaced hooks are raised and lift the warp threads. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacquard_machine
- Woven silk portrait of Jacquard (1839) needed 24,000 punched cards; Babbage owned one and it inspired his use of cards in the Analytical Engine. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacquard_machine
