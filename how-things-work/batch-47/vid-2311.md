## Fact check

- Modern rebar is rolled with ribs (deformations) that anchor it in concrete; plain round bars bond far worse. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebar
- Concrete is strong in compression and weak in tension, so steel carries the tension. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforced_concrete
- Steel and concrete have similar coefficients of thermal expansion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforced_concrete
