## Fact check

- George Borwick launched the first commercial baking powder in England in 1842; Alfred Bird developed his own in 1843; Horsford patented monocalcium phosphate in 1856. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baking_powder
- Pre-powder leavening: yeast or emptins (brewer's leftovers); pearlash (purified potash, potassium carbonate) used by European bakers by the mid-1600s, first written use of the term 1703. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baking_powder
- Modern powder: baking soda plus acid, with starch as a desiccant; double-acting (fast and slow acid) developed by Horsford in the 1860s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baking_powder
