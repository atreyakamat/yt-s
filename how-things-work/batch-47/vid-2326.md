## Fact check

- First operational jet bridge ('Aero-Gangplank') installed by United at Chicago O'Hare in 1958; prototype 'Air Dock' tested 1954. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_bridge
- Terminal end is anchored at a pivot (rotunda) and swings; cab raises/lowers, extends/retracts and pivots; operator controls from the cab. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_bridge
- Accordion-style canopy docks with differently shaped aircraft and makes a nearly weatherproof seal. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_bridge
- Telescoping sections and drive wheels: standard jet bridge construction (caption mentions outboard gantry and driving wheels). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jet_bridge
