## Fact check

- Solar cells produce direct current; most homes use alternating current. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_inverter
- Inverters use fast switching to synthesise an AC waveform. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_inverter
- Maximum power point tracking adjusts the operating voltage as light changes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_power_point_tracking
- Grid-tied inverters must stop feeding the grid in an outage (anti-islanding). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islanding
