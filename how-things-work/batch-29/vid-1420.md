## Fact check

- Self-contained emergency luminaires have an internal battery charged from the mains and a circuit that switches to battery when supply fails.
- Standards require rapid illumination of escape routes, within seconds (e.g. UK BS 5266 requires a large share of output within 5 seconds). The gauge is illustrative.
- Typical rated durations are 3 hours in the UK and 90 minutes in the US (NFPA 101).
- Maintained fittings are always lit; non-maintained only in a mains failure. Monthly function tests often use a key switch to simulate failure.
