## Fact check

- Before aerosol paint, painting was by brush or by spray gun driven by compressed air.
- The aerosol 'bug bomb' was developed in the early 1940s (Goodhue and Sullivan) and used by US forces in WWII.
- Edward Seymour of Sycamore, Illinois, created aerosol spray paint in 1949; his wife Bonnie suggested the aerosol can to demonstrate their aluminium paint.
- Spray cans hold paint with a liquefied gas propellant; a mixing ball (pea) disperses settled pigment when shaken.
- Edward Seymour, 1949, wife Bonnie's suggestion; aerosols developed by the military for insecticide in the 1940s: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spray_paint
