## Fact check

- Washing soda is sodium carbonate (Na2CO3); baking soda is sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3), a much weaker base. A washing soda solution has a pH of about 11.
- It softens water by precipitating calcium and magnesium as insoluble carbonates, so detergent and soap work better.
- As a strong alkali it helps remove grease; it can damage wool, silk and aluminium, and makers advise gloves.
