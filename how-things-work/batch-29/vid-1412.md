## Fact check

- Spell checkers look words up in dictionaries of often around 100,000 or more entries using fast structures (hash tables, tries), taking microseconds.
- Suggestions use edit distance: insertions, deletions, substitutions and transpositions.
- Damerau's 1964 study found about 80% of misspellings were a single such error, matching the 8-in-10 grid.
- Word list "a hundred thousand or more": SOFTENED from "a few hundred thousand"; Wikipedia cites about 90,000 entries as a practical English size, larger lists exist. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spell_checker
