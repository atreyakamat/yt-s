## Fact check

- Scissors cut by shearing: two edges pass closely, stressing material between them past its shear strength.
- Blades have a slight curve/twist so they touch at a single point that moves along the blades as they close, maintaining contact pressure.
- The pivot screw keeps the blades pressed together; looseness lets material slip between the blades and fold.
- Gauge is illustrative.
