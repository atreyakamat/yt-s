## Fact check

- Captive portals block or redirect traffic until the user authenticates or accepts terms.
- Operating systems detect portals by requesting a known URL (e.g. a 'generate_204' or success page); an unexpected response triggers the sign-in window.
- The portal intercepts HTTP requests and redirects them to its login page.
- Access is commonly granted per device MAC address and may time out. Note: some devices randomise MAC addresses, which can also trigger re-prompts.
