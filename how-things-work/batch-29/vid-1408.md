## Fact check

- Rotating tweezer drum → epilators use a rotating head of metal discs/tweezers (typically about 20-40) that close on hairs and pull them out.
- Pulls from the root → epilation removes the whole hair rather than cutting it at the skin.
- Regrowth → shaved hair is visible again within a day or few; epilated hair typically takes about 2-4 weeks (bars show rounded examples: 2 and 21 days).
