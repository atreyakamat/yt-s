## Fact check

- Vacuum flasks (invented by James Dewar, 1892) have double walls with the air evacuated between them, stopping conduction and convection through the gap.
- Silvered or polished surfaces reduce heat transfer by radiation.
- The walls join at the neck, the main heat-loss path along with the stopper.
- Insulation works both ways, keeping cold contents cold.
