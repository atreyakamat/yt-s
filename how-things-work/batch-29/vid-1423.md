## Fact check

- Cushion (pneumatic) brushes → bristles mounted in a rubber pad over an enclosed air space.
- Vent hole → lets air escape and return so the pad compresses and recovers; without it the trapped air would make the pad stiff.
- Flex → the cushion lets bristles conform to the scalp, reducing pressure and pulling.
