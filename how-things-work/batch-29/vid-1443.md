## Fact check

- In August 1888 Bertha Benz drove the Benz Patent-Motorwagen about 106 km from Mannheim to Pforzheim, reportedly without telling her husband Karl.
- The car ran on ligroin, a petroleum solvent sold by pharmacies; she bought it at the town pharmacy in Wiesloch, often called the first filling station.
- Early motorists bought fuel in cans from shops and garages; purpose-built filling stations appeared from about 1905 and spread in the 1910s.
- Bertha Benz 1888, about 106 km, bought ligroin at a pharmacy in Wiesloch, did not tell her husband: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertha_Benz
