## Fact check

- Rack-and-pinion steering, used on most modern cars, has a pinion gear on the steering shaft meshing with a linear toothed rack; tie rods connect the rack ends to the steering arms.
- Lock-to-lock is typically about 2.5-3.5 turns on cars ('roughly 3').
- The overall steering ratio (commonly around 12-20:1) turns large steering-wheel rotations into small road-wheel angles.
