## Fact check

- Personal writing slates were common in schools in the 1800s because paper was expensive.
- Large classroom blackboards spread in the early 1800s.
- Blackboard chalk is calcium carbonate (limestone) or calcium sulfate (gypsum).
- Dry-erase whiteboards appeared in the 1960s and became common in classrooms in the 1990s, partly because they produce no chalk dust.
- Grid is illustrative.
- First attested chalk blackboard use in the US 1801; whiteboards commercial early 1960s, widespread in the 1990s: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackboard and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whiteboard
