## Fact check

- Spring (flex) hinges contain a small coil spring in the temple behind the hinge.
- They let the temple flex outward beyond the normal open position; the spring returns it quickly.
- Standard hinges have a hard stop, so over-extension stresses the screw or frame.
- Spring tension gives a light grip on the head and reduces breakage: standard optical retailer description.
