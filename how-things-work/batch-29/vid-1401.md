## Fact check

- Self-seal envelopes use cohesive latex adhesive on both flap and body; cohesives bond strongly to themselves but very weakly to other surfaces.
- The bond forms by inter-diffusion/entanglement of rubber polymer chains on contact under pressure.
- Peel-and-seal envelopes instead use a pressure-sensitive adhesive protected by a release liner strip.
