## Fact check

- Thermoelectric coolers use Peltier modules: arrays of n- and p-type semiconductor pellets connected electrically in series between ceramic plates.
- A DC current pumps heat from one plate to the other (Peltier effect); reversing the current reverses the direction, so many coolers can also warm.
- A heat sink and fan remove heat from the hot side.
- They are less efficient than compressor fridges and typically cool only to a limited difference (often roughly 15-20 °C) below ambient.
