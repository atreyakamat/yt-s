## Fact check

- Pilling: abrasion draws fibre ends out of the yarn surface, which entangle into small balls anchored by fibres.
- Pills form fastest in high-friction areas (underarms, cuffs, where straps rub).
- Weaker natural fibres often let pills break away; strong synthetic fibres (polyester, acrylic, nylon) anchor them so they accumulate.
- Washing inside out on gentle cycles reduces abrasion; fabric shavers remove pills.
