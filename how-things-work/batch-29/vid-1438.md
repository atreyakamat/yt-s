## Fact check

- Strigil → Romans and Greeks oiled the skin and scraped off oil, sweat and dirt with a curved metal strigil.
- Babylonian soap about 2800 BC → clay cylinders from ancient Babylon record a soap-like substance of fats boiled with ashes; about 4,800 years ago.
- Saponification → fat plus an alkali (from ashes, lye) makes soap; its molecules have an oil-loving and a water-loving end.
- Early soap used for textiles → early soap was largely used to clean wool and cloth and for medicine.
- 1853 → Britain abolished its soap tax in 1853.
- Babylonian soap about 2800 BC (nearly 5,000 years), soap tax repealed in 1853, Romans used oil and a strigil: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soap
