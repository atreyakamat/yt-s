## Fact check

- Medieval Europeans carried belongings in purses/pouches hung from belts.
- 'Cutpurse' refers to thieves who cut the strings of such purses.
- Pockets sewn into men's garments became common in the 17th century.
- Women wore separate tie-on pockets under skirts, accessed through slits, from the 17th into the 19th century (V&A and other museum collections).
- The rhyme 'Lucy Locket lost her pocket' is commonly linked to tie-on pockets.
- By the 17th century pockets were built into men's clothing; women used separate tie-on pouches: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pocket
