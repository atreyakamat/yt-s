## Fact check

- Chewing gum consists of an insoluble gum base (elastomers/polymers, resins, waxes) plus sweeteners and flavours.
- Bubble gum bases are formulated with more/higher-molecular-weight elastomers for greater elasticity and film strength, allowing thin films.
- Stretching aligns polymer chains; bubbles burst when the film thins too far.
- Walter Diemer created a commercially successful bubble gum in 1928; it was pink reportedly because pink was the only food colouring at hand.
