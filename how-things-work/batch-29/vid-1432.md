## Fact check

- Stepladders are two frames hinged at the top with spreader braces that lock the frames open.
- Locked spreaders form a triangulated frame; a triangle is rigid because its shape can't change without changing a side length.
- Stability requires the centre of gravity to remain within the base of the feet.
- Stepladders carry labels with duty/load rating and which top steps/cap must not be stood on (standard safety labelling).
