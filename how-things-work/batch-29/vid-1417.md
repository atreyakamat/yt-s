## Fact check

- Blind (pop) rivets consist of a hollow rivet body and a mandrel with a head; they are installed from one side.
- The rivet tool pulls the mandrel, drawing its head into the body and expanding the blind end to clamp the sheets.
- The mandrel has a designed break point (neck) and snaps at a set load, with an audible pop.
