## Fact check

- Drying rate rises with airflow because wind removes the humid boundary layer around wet fabric.
- Sunlight's UV has a bleaching effect on many stains and on yellowing.
- Ceiling-hung pulley airers above kitchen ranges were common in 19th/20th-century homes.
- The rotary clothesline was popularised in Australia in the mid-1940s (Hills Hoist, 1945); home tumble dryers became common from the 1960s onward.
- Hills Hoist production began 1945, but earlier rotary lines were patented in Australia from 1895: FIXED/SOFTENED wording to name the Hills Hoist, 1945. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hills_Hoist
