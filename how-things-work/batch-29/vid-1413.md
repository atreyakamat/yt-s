## Fact check

- Traction control uses ABS wheel-speed sensors to detect a driven wheel turning faster than the others (wheelspin).
- It reduces engine torque (throttle, ignition or fuel) and/or brakes the spinning wheel, which through the differential sends torque to the wheel with grip.
- Interventions happen within fractions of a second; a flashing dashboard light often shows it's working.
