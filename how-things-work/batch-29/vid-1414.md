## Fact check

- Traction elevators have an overspeed governor: a sheave driven by a governor rope attached to the car.
- Centrifugal flyweights (restrained by springs) move outward as speed increases.
- At a set overspeed (typically around 115-125% of rated speed), the governor first cuts power, then grips the governor rope.
- The held rope pulls the safety linkage on the car, forcing wedges/rollers against the guide rails to stop it.
