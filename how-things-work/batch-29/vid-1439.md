## Fact check

- Thatch (straw, water reed, sedge, grasses) was the dominant roofing in much of the world for millennia.
- Thatch is laid thick at a steep pitch; water runs along the stems and off, so only the outer layer gets wet.
- Dry thatch and trapped air give good insulation.
- Thatch is highly flammable; after fires, a London regulation of 1212 restricted thatched roofs in favour of tile or other materials.
- London banned new thatched roofs after the 1212 fire: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thatching
