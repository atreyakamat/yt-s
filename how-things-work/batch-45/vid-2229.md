## Fact check

- An abacus is a counting frame; each rod represents a place value and beads are moved to represent numbers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abacus
- Chinese suanpan and Japanese soroban use upper beads of value five and lower beads of value one. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soroban
