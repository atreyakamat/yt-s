## Fact check

- A suspension is a heterogeneous mixture where solid particles are dispersed in a liquid and settle over time. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suspension_(chemistry)
- Pharmaceutical suspensions need shaking to redisperse settled particles so the dose is uniform. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suspension_(chemistry)
- Settling happens because particles are denser than the liquid. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedimentation
