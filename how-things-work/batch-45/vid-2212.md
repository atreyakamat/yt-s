## Fact check

- PCR uses repeated cycles of denaturation (about 94-98 °C), annealing and extension to amplify DNA; thermal cyclers heat and cool samples. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymerase_chain_reaction
- The product doubles each cycle, so 30 cycles give about 2^30, roughly a billion, copies. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymerase_chain_reaction
- Taq polymerase comes from the thermophilic bacterium Thermus aquaticus, found in hot springs. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taq_polymerase
