## Fact check

- Harry Brearley, working in Sheffield in 1913, is credited with discovering stainless steel while looking for erosion-resistant steel for gun barrels. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Brearley
- Stainless steel contains at least 10.5 percent chromium, which forms a self-repairing passive oxide layer. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stainless_steel
- Iron rusts through contact with oxygen and water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rust
