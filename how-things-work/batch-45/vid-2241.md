## Fact check

- Heron of Alexandria (1st century AD) described a temple door powered by heat from a fire pressurising water to pull ropes and pulleys; Horace Raymond and Sheldon Roby of Stanley Works designed an optically triggered door in 1931 at a Connecticut restaurant; Dee Horton and Lew Hewitt invented the first sliding automatic door in 1954 using a mat actuator, commercial version 1960; microwave detectors common in the 1970s; first infrared-sensor door in 1980. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automatic_door
