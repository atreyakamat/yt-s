## Fact check

- Scissors are paired levers; force at the blades is greater closer to the pivot for a given handle force. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scissors
- Lever mechanical advantage depends on the ratio of distances from the fulcrum. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lever
