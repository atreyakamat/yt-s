## Fact check

- Bypass pruners work like scissors and make a clean cut without crushing, preferable for live wood; anvil pruners close a blade onto a flat anvil and crush the stem, best for dead wood. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pruning_shears
- Pruning shears can cut hard branches sometimes up to two centimetres thick; thicker branches need loppers; an anvil technique for thick branches is to bite partway, swing the handle and bite again from another direction. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pruning_shears
