## Fact check

- Silent films were almost always shown with live music: pianists in small towns, organists or ensembles in big theatres from the mid-1910s; theatre organs imitated horse hooves and thunder. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silent_film
- At the height of the silent era, movies were the largest source of employment for instrumental musicians in the US. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silent_film
- Before sound films standardised at 24 fps (1926 to 1930), silent films were shot at 12 to 40 frames per second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silent_film
- The Jazz Singer (1927) was the first commercially successful sound film; 1929 is treated as the start of the modern sound era; talkies and the Great Depression hurt musicians. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silent_film
