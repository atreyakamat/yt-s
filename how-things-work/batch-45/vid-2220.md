## Fact check

- Building codes and accessibility standards commonly require handrails to extend beyond the top and bottom of stairs and to have returned (non-snagging) ends. SOFTENED to 'many building rules'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handrail
- Extensions give a continuous grip and help people with low vision locate the stairs. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handrail
