## Fact check

- Expanded polystyrene is made from small beads containing a blowing agent; steam pre-expands them and then fuses them in a mould. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polystyrene
- The foam is mostly air by volume, which makes it light and a good insulator. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polystyrene
- Beads remain visible in the finished foam. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polystyrene
