## Fact check

- The Canadair CL-415 scoops about 6,137 litres of water in about 12 seconds, over a run of roughly 410 m at 70 knots (130 km/h). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardier_415
- The scoops are lowered into the water as the aircraft skims the surface. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardier_415
- Water is dropped over the fire by opening doors under the hull. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombardier_415
