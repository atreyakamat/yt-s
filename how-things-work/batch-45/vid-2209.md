## Fact check

- Coloured pencil cores are made of pigment in a wax or oil binder with fillers, not graphite. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colored_pencil
- Marks form as the paper's texture abrades the core and deposits pigment. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colored_pencil
