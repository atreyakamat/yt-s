## Fact check

- Wind blowing across a microphone causes low-frequency turbulence noise; furry and foam windshields reduce it by breaking up airflow around the capsule (general acoustics). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_filter
- Windshields on microphones are commonly nicknamed "dead cats" or windjammers. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microphone
