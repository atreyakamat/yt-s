## Fact check

- Beakers are cylindrical containers with a flat bottom and a lip (spout) for pouring. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaker_(glassware)
- Beaker graduations are approximate; graduated cylinders are used for accurate volumes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beaker_(glassware)
- Borosilicate glass beakers can be heated. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borosilicate_glass
