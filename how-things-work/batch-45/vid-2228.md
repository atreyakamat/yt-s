## Fact check

- McCormick claimed his reaper was invented in 1831 (claim, not confirmed demonstration); patent granted 1834 (US patent X8277). Several blacksmiths built reapers in the 1830s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McCormick_reaper
- The combine harvester later combined reaping with threshing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combine_harvester
