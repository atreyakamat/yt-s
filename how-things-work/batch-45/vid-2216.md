## Fact check

- A taller light is visible from farther away because the horizon distance increases with height. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizon#Distance_to_the_horizon
- Many lighthouses are round tapered towers to resist wind and waves. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lighthouse
- A wide base lowers the centre of gravity and improves stability. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lighthouse
