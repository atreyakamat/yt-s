## Fact check

- Tablet presses compress powder between an upper and lower punch in a die; binders help powder particles stick. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tablet_(pharmacy)
- Rotary tablet presses mount many dies on a turret and can produce well over a hundred thousand tablets per hour (script: hundreds of thousands, shown as 100,000+). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tablet_press
- Each compression is a fraction of a second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tablet_press
