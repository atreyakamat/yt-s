## Fact check

- Stranded conductors are more flexible and resist work hardening and fatigue breakage better than a solid wire of the same size. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_cable
- Copper wire is made by drawing rod through dies; insulation is extruded over the conductor. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_cable
- Green and yellow striped wire is the protective earth conductor in many countries. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_wiring
