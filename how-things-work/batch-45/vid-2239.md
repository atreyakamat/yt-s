## Fact check

- An object is stable while the vertical line through its centre of mass falls within its base of support; a wider base increases the tilt angle before tipping. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_of_mass
- A wider support polygon improves stability. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stability_(mechanics)
