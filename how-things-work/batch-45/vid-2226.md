## Fact check

- Channel 16 (156.8 MHz) is the international maritime VHF distress, safety and calling channel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_VHF_radio
- Vessels typically make first contact on channel 16 and then switch to a working channel. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_VHF_radio
- The distress call is 'Mayday', repeated three times. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayday
