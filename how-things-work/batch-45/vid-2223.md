## Fact check

- Six's thermometer was invented by James Six in 1780; a U-shaped tube with alcohol and a section of mercury in the bend, and two small steel markers that record the highest and lowest temperatures since the last reset. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Six%27s_thermometer
- Early models reset with a small magnet that slides the markers back to the mercury. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Six%27s_thermometer
