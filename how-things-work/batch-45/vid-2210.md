## Fact check

- Commercial ice makers freeze water on a refrigerated evaporator surface, then briefly heat it so the ice harvests and falls into a bin. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_maker
- Flowing-water freezing tends to push out dissolved air and impurities, giving clearer ice; SOFTENED to 'can come out clearer'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice
