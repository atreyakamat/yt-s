## Fact check

- A tuning fork used to check vibration sense is C128, i.e. 128 Hz; hearing-test forks are higher (C512). Prongs move in opposite directions and the base is a node. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuning_fork
- The two prongs vibrate in antiphase, so little vibration reaches the handle and the tone is nearly pure and sustained. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuning_fork
- Concert pitch A is 440 Hz. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A440_(pitch_standard)
- Pitch depends on prong length and thickness (stiffness and mass). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuning_fork
