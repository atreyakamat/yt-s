## Fact check

- Leaf blowers use an impeller driven by an engine or motor to produce a high-speed air stream through a nozzle; some models can convert to vacuum/mulcher mode. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leaf_blower
- Narrowing a duct speeds up the airflow (continuity). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuity_equation
