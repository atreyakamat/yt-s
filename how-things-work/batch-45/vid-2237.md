## Fact check

- Humans hear roughly 20 Hz to 20 kHz; Nyquist-Shannon needs a sampling rate over twice the highest frequency; at 44.1 kHz the filter transition band is about 2.05 kHz. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/44,100_Hz
- Early digital audio used video-tape PCM adaptors at no more than 3 samples per video line per channel; PAL: 294 lines x 50 fields/s x 3 = 44,100 Hz. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/44,100_Hz
- Sony's implementation prevailed and became the de facto standard. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/44,100_Hz
