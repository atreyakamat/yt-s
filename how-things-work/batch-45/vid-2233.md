## Fact check

- Horticultural fleece protects plants from frost by trapping air and reducing heat loss, not by generating heat (qualitative). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Row_cover
- On clear nights, surfaces cool by radiating heat to the sky. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frost
