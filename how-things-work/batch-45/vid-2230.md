## Fact check

- Baffle grease filters in commercial kitchen hoods force air to change direction; grease particles, being heavier, impact the baffles and drain into a collection cup. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Range_hood
- Metal baffles can be washed and are less prone to clogging than mesh; SOFTENED wording. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Range_hood
