## Fact check

- Garrett Brown's Steadicam was introduced by Cinema Products in 1975; Bound for Glory (1976) was the first film to use it, and Rocky (1976) was its third. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steadicam
- The operator wears a vest attached to an iso-elastic arm; a low-friction multi-axis gimbal connects the arm to a sled with the camera at one end and counterweight (monitor, batteries) at the other; the sled's bottom is slightly heavier than the top, and the operator feathers the gimbal. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steadicam
