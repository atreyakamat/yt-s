## Fact check

- A teleprompter displays script on a screen near the lens, with clear glass (a beam splitter) reflecting the text toward the presenter while the camera sees through; the text is mirror-reversed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teleprompter
- Hubert Schlafly built the first one in 1950, a mechanical paper scroll first used 4 December 1950 on a CBS soap; Herbert Hoover used one in 1952; glass teleprompters were first used at the 1956 Democratic convention. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teleprompter
