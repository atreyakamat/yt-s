## Fact check

- A magnifying glass is a convex lens, usually mounted in a frame with a handle; lens power is measured in dioptres; a typical 25 cm focal length (4 dioptres) is sold as a 2x magnifier. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnifying_glass
- Simple magnifiers typically reach 2x to 6x; triplet loupes up to about 25x; beyond about 25x a microscope is needed. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnifying_glass
- 424 BC: Aristophanes' The Clouds contains the earliest explicit reference (lenses sold in a pharmacy to ignite tinder); 13th century: Roger Bacon described magnifying glasses. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnifying_glass
