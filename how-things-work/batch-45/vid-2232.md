## Fact check

- Progressive lenses have a gradual change in power from distance at top to near at bottom, with no visible dividing line. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_lens
- Presbyopia is age-related loss of near focusing as the eye's lens stiffens. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbyopia
- Progressives have areas of peripheral distortion beside the corridor. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_lens
- Bifocals have a visible dividing line. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bifocals
