## Fact check

- Evangelista Torricelli is usually credited with inventing the barometer in 1643 (some historians say 1644). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barometer
- Standard atmospheric pressure supports a mercury column about 760 mm tall. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barometer
- A rapid drop in pressure suggests an approaching low and a higher chance of rain; rises usually come with improving conditions. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barometer
