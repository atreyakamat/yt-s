## Fact check

- Rice milling removes the hull to give brown rice, and further milling removes bran and germ to give white rice. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice
- Brown rice has a shorter shelf life because its bran contains oils that can go rancid. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_rice
