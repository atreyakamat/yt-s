## Fact check

- Spacesuits provide breathable oxygen, pressure, thermal control and micrometeoroid protection. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_suit
- NASA's EMU includes a liquid cooling and ventilation garment of water-filled tubes worn against the skin and a backpack life-support system. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extravehicular_Mobility_Unit
- Suits operate at a low pressure, well below sea level, in pure oxygen. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_suit
- Helmet visors carry a thin gold coating to reflect solar radiation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extravehicular_Mobility_Unit
