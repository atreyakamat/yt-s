## Fact check

- A slush machine freezes sugary liquid against a cylinder wrapped in freezer coils; a rotating scraper (dasher) shaves ice off and keeps the mix uniform; sugar acts as an antifreeze. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slush_(beverage)
- Omar Knedlik, a Kansas Dairy Queen franchise owner, around 1958 found sodas turned slushy in a freezer; patent 1962; 7-Eleven sold Slurpee from 1965. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slush_(beverage)
