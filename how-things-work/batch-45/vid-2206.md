## Fact check

- Rockets carry both fuel and oxidiser because there is no atmospheric oxygen in space. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocket_propellant
- Liquid hydrogen (boils at about -253 C) and liquid oxygen (about -183 C) are a common cryogenic propellant pair. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid_hydrogen ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid_oxygen
- Burning hydrogen with oxygen produces water vapour. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid_hydrogen
- Turbopumps feed propellants to the combustion chamber. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turbopump
