## Fact check

- Hard tags are larger reusable tags removed at checkout with a detacher; most detachers are magnet-based; in many hard tags a pin is held by bearing balls under spring tension and a strong magnet compresses the spring so the balls release the pin. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_article_surveillance
