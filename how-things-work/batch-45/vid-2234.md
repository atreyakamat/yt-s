## Fact check

- Treadmills for punishment were introduced in 1818 by English engineer Sir William Cubitt. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treadmill
- Punishment treadmills remained in use until the second half of the 19th century; Prison Act 1865 required certain prisoners to use them. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treadmill
- Treadwheels were used to power mills and pumps. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treadwheel
