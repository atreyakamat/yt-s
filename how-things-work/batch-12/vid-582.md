## Fact check

- Vinegar is a dilute solution of acetic acid (table vinegar is commonly around 5%).
- Most food spoilage bacteria grow best near neutral pH; acidic conditions inhibit them.
- Vinegar pickling acidifies the vegetables, greatly extending shelf life.
- Fermented pickles are made in brine, where lactic acid bacteria produce the acid.
