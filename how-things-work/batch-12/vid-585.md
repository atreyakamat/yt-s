## Fact check

- Pod films are polyvinyl alcohol (PVOH/PVA), a water-soluble polymer.
- Pod fillings are concentrated detergents with low water content so they don't dissolve the film from inside.
- Multi-chamber pods separate ingredients until release.
- Manufacturers instruct placing pods in the empty drum before clothes, not the dispenser drawer.
- Many pods use a bitter-tasting agent and child-resistant packaging to deter accidental ingestion.
