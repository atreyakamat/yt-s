## Fact check

- Bluetooth audio is compressed with codecs (SBC, AAC, etc.), packetised, transmitted, buffered against packet loss, decoded and played.
- Typical end-to-end Bluetooth audio latency is around 100-300 ms for common codecs (150 ms shown as 'often'); wired analog is effectively near zero.
- Video players compensate using reported audio latency to delay video for lip sync; interactive games cannot buffer this way.
- Bluetooth Classic audio latency about 100-200 ms: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluetooth
