## Fact check

- Calculator solar cells are commonly amorphous silicon thin-film cells, which perform relatively well under indoor lighting.
- Photovoltaic cells free electrons with light; the p-n (or p-i-n) junction's built-in field drives them as current.
- Basic calculators with LCDs need microwatts, far below a phone.
- Many solar calculators include a backup button cell; solar-only models dim or blank when the cell is covered in low light.
