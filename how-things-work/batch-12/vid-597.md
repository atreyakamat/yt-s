## Fact check

- Slow freezing forms large ice crystals that damage cell walls; on thawing, juices leak and texture goes mushy. Quick freezing forms small crystals.
- Clarence Birdseye worked in Labrador in the 1910s and saw fish caught by Inuit freeze almost immediately in very cold air (around -40 °C), staying good when cooked later.
- He developed quick-freezing methods, including pressing packaged food between refrigerated metal plates, and founded a company in the mid-1920s (1924).
- Quick-frozen foods went on retail sale in 1930 (Springfield, Massachusetts). Birdseye is named in the loop as a person, not a brand.
- Birdseye in Labrador 1912-1915; General Seafood Corp 1924; Springfield retail tests 1930: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarence_Birdseye
