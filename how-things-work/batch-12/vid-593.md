## Fact check

- UV light has shorter wavelengths than visible violet light (roughly 100-400 nm) and is invisible.
- Lens tint reduces visible light; UV blocking comes from UV absorbers in the lens material or coatings.
- 'UV400' means blocking wavelengths up to 400 nm, covering UVA and UVB.
- Eye-health bodies warn that dark lenses without UV protection cause pupils to dilate and can let more UV reach the eye.
- Polycarbonate and many clear lenses block essentially all UV.
- UV400 = blocks UV up to 400 nm; tint does not mean UV protection: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunglasses
