## Fact check

- Typical corded circular saws (184 mm / 7-1/4 inch blade) run about 4,500-6,000 rpm no-load; 5,000 rpm used.
- Rim speed: pi x 0.184 m x (5,000/60) per second is about 48 m/s, about 173 km/h.
- A 24-tooth blade at 5,000 rpm passes 24 x 83.3 = about 2,000 teeth per second.
- Circular saws have a spring-loaded retractable lower guard; many models have an electric brake that stops the blade in about 2 seconds or less.
