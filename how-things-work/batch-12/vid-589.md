## Fact check

- Pitot tubes face into the airflow and measure total (ram) pressure.
- Static ports measure ambient static pressure; airspeed is derived from the difference (dynamic pressure).
- Pitot probes are electrically heated to prevent icing, since blockages cause erroneous airspeed (as in several accidents).
- Airliners carry multiple pitot probes for redundancy.
