## Fact check

- Storage heaters contain high-density ceramic or clay bricks (often magnetite/feolite) with electric elements between them, inside thermal insulation.
- They charge overnight on off-peak tariffs (e.g. Economy 7 in the UK), 'on some tariffs'.
- A damper/flap controls convective release; the input control sets charge level and the output control sets release rate.
