## Fact check

- Liquefied propellant → most deodorant aerosols use liquefied hydrocarbon gases (butane, isobutane, propane) stored as a liquid under pressure.
- Propane boils at about -42°C → standard boiling point at atmospheric pressure (isobutane about -12°C, butane about -1°C).
- Evaporation absorbs heat → latent heat of vaporisation is taken from the surroundings, so the spray and skin cool.
- Can cools during long sprays → continued boiling inside the can as pressure is released.
- Fridges use the same effect → refrigerators cool by evaporating a refrigerant.
- Propane boils at about -42 C: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propane
