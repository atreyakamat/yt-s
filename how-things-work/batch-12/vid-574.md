## Fact check

- Modern elevator doors commonly use infrared light curtains: an emitter strip and a receiver strip on the door edges.
- Light curtains use dozens of beams (often scanned in a criss-cross pattern) covering most of the door height; the grid shows 'dozens', not an exact count.
- When a beam is interrupted, the controller stops and reopens the doors within a fraction of a second.
- Older elevators used mechanical safety edges that reopened the door on contact.
