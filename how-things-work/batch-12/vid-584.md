## Fact check

- Mineral wool (glass or rock wool) loft insulation is made of spun fibres.
- Its insulating value comes from trapped still air; conduction through still air is low and convection is suppressed.
- UK guidance (e.g. Energy Saving Trust) recommends about 270 mm of mineral wool; a standard ruler is 300 mm.
- Compressing insulation reduces its thickness and thermal resistance.
- In an uninsulated home roughly a quarter of heat can be lost through the roof: 'a big share'.
