## Fact check

- Traditional jams are typically about 60-65% sugar.
- Sugar binds water and lowers water activity, so microbes can't grow easily.
- Pectin, with sugar and acid, forms a gel network when boiled, which is why jam sets.
- Boiling concentrates the jam and kills microbes; jars are filled hot and sealed.
- Reduced-sugar jams have less preservative effect and are labelled to refrigerate after opening.
- Jam needs about 60 percent soluble solids/sugar: OK. Source: https://www.fao.org/3/v5030e/v5030e0m.htm
