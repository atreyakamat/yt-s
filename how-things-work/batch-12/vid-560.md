## Fact check

- Recycling plants remove steel (ferrous) cans with overhead or drum magnets.
- Eddy current separators use a rotor of alternating-polarity magnets spinning fast inside the head pulley; the changing field induces eddy currents in non-ferrous metals like aluminium, whose own field opposes the rotor, throwing them forward over a splitter.
- Non-metals such as plastic and paper are unaffected and drop off normally.
