## Fact check

- Ordinary dice have drilled or molded pip recesses, so faces with more pips have slightly less material; the six face is lightest, opposite the one.
- The lighter face tends to land up slightly more often; the effect is small, hence 'very slightly'.
- Casino (precision) dice have pips drilled and filled with paint of the same density as the removed material, so the die stays balanced.
- Casino dice are transparent (to show no hidden weights), have sharp edges and corners, and usually carry a serial number; tolerances are tiny, around a thousandth of an inch or better.
