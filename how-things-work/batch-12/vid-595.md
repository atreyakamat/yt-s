## Fact check

- Most domestic fire blankets are woven glass fibre (sometimes coated), which is non-combustible.
- They extinguish small fires by smothering, excluding oxygen from the fire triangle.
- Water on burning cooking oil vaporises explosively and spreads burning oil, which is why fire services warn against it. Bars are illustrative.
- Blankets are kept in wall cases with pull tapes, and guidance says leave the blanket in place until the fire has cooled. Educational only, not safety training.
