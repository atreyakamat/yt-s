## Fact check

- Webcams use CMOS image sensors with millions of photosites that measure light intensity only.
- A Bayer colour filter array covers them: 50% green, 25% red, 25% blue, reflecting the eye's greater sensitivity to green.
- Demosaicing interpolates the two missing colour values for each pixel from neighbours, so two of three colour values per pixel are estimated.
- In low light, gain amplifies the signal and the noise, producing grain.
- Bayer filter: 50 percent green, 25 red, 25 blue, mimicking eye sensitivity: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayer_filter
