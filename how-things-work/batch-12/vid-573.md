## Fact check

- ESC uses steering-angle, yaw-rate, lateral-acceleration and wheel-speed sensors to compare intended path with actual motion.
- Oversteer correction: brake the outer front wheel; understeer correction: brake the inner rear wheel; ESC can also reduce engine torque.
- First production ESC systems appeared in 1995. The US required ESC on all new light vehicles from the 2012 model year; the EU required it on all new cars from November 2014.
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_stability_control (US from 2012 model year; EU all new cars 1 Nov 2014)
