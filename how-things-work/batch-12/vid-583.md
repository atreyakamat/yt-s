## Fact check

- Rotary dampers → soft-close hinges contain a rotor in a sealed chamber of viscous (usually silicone) fluid.
- Viscous damping → resistance rises with speed, so the seat descends slowly.
- One-way → most soft-close dampers resist only the closing direction (via a one-way clutch or valve).
- Soft-close drawers and cabinet hinges use similar dampers.
