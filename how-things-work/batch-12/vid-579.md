## Fact check

- Photo finish cameras are line-scan (slit) cameras aimed exactly along the finish line.
- They record a one-pixel-wide strip thousands of times per second and place the strips side by side, so the horizontal axis is time.
- Stationary backgrounds become streaks and athletes can look distorted depending on their speed.
- Each athlete's crossing time is read from the image's time scale.
- In athletics, finish is judged when the torso (not head, neck, arms, legs, hands or feet) reaches the finish line.
