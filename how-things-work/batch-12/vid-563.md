## Fact check

- Safety footwear uses a steel or composite toe cap under the upper.
- A domed shape distributes load to its edges, like an arch.
- EN ISO 20345 requires toe caps to withstand a 200 J impact (a 20 kg striker dropped about 1 m) and a 15 kN compression (about 1.5 tonnes-force).
- After testing, a minimum internal clearance must remain: part of the same standard.
- EN ISO 20345 toecap: 200 J impact and 15 kN compression (about 1.5 tonnes); 20 kg from 1 m = about 200 J: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steel-toe_boot
