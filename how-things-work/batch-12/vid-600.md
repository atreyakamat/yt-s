## Fact check

- Hubert Cecil Booth patented a powered suction vacuum cleaner in Britain in 1901; earlier machines blew dust rather than sucking it.
- Booth's machine ('Puffing Billy') was a large engine-driven pump on a horse-drawn cart, with long hoses fed through windows.
- It was used to clean carpets at Westminster Abbey for Edward VII's coronation in 1902.
- The handkerchief test (sucking through cloth on a chair) is a widely told anecdote, so the script says 'legend says'.
- James Murray Spangler invented a portable electric upright with a rotating brush and cloth bag in 1907 (patented 1908).
- Booth powered vacuum 1901, horse-drawn "Puffing Billy"; Spangler portable electric with brush and bag 1907: OK. Coronation 1902 (Edward VII crowned 1902; Wikipedia page says 1901, likely typo). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Cecil_Booth and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacuum_cleaner
