## Fact check

- Car alternators are belt-driven from the crankshaft; a rotating electromagnet (rotor) induces alternating current in stationary copper windings (stator).
- A diode rectifier converts AC to DC; a voltage regulator holds output around 13.5-14.5 V to charge the 12 V battery.
- With the engine running, the alternator supplies the car's electrical load; the battery's main job is starting.
- Alternator regulator holds about 14 V: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternator_(automotive)
