## Fact check

- Robot vacuums have several downward-facing infrared cliff sensors (emitter plus detector) around the front of the base.
- If the reflected IR signal drops, the robot treats it as a drop-off, stops and backs away; the check runs continuously while driving.
- Very dark or black carpets absorb IR and are a known cause of false cliff detection in many models.
