## Fact check

- Phones have an ambient light sensor (a photodiode-based chip) usually near the top bezel or under the display.
- Photodiodes convert light to current; output scales with illuminance.
- Typical illuminance: home lighting about 50-150 lux, office around 300-500 lux, direct sunlight around 30,000-100,000 lux (upper value shown).
- Adaptive brightness on major phone OSes learns user adjustments; some phones have colour (RGB) light sensors for white-balance features.
