## Fact check

- In 1902 Willis Carrier designed a system for the Sackett-Wilhelms printing company in Brooklyn, New York, to control humidity so paper didn't swell and misalign colour printing.
- It passed air over chilled coils, condensing out moisture and cooling the air as a side effect.
- Traditional passive cooling included thick walls, high ceilings, courtyards and Persian windcatchers (badgir).
- Air-conditioned cinemas became popular in the 1920s; room/window units spread into homes after WWII, especially the 1950s.
