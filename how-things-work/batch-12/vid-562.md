## Fact check

- Soft-close cabinet hinges integrate a spring that pulls the door closed over the last part of travel and a small hydraulic damper (piston in an oil-filled cylinder).
- Damping comes from forcing fluid through a small orifice; flow rate is limited, so speed is limited.
- On opening, a return spring resets the damper piston (often with a one-way valve letting the fluid flow back easily).
