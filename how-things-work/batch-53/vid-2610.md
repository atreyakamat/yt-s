## Fact check

- Donald Unger cracked the knuckles of his left hand for more than fifty years and not his right; neither hand developed arthritis; he received the 2009 Ig Nobel Prize in Medicine. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cracking_joints
- A 2011 study of 215 people aged 50 to 89 found no link to hand osteoarthritis. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cracking_joints
- Gases dissolved in synovial fluid form cavitation bubbles when pressure drops; the exact origin of the sound is still debated; about 20 minutes refractory period. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cracking_joints
