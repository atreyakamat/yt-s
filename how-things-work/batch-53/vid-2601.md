## Fact check

- Rivets were heated to about 900 C (cherry red) and the cooling rivet contracted, pulling the joined parts together; a bucking bar held the head while the tail was hammered into a second head. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivet
- Riveting held structures such as the Eiffel Tower and Sydney Harbour Bridge; high-strength bolts and welding have largely replaced structural rivets. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivet
- Eiffel Tower completed 1889. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eiffel_Tower
- Sydney Harbour Bridge opened 1932. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Harbour_Bridge
- Boiling water 100 C; household oven about 220 C is a typical setting.
