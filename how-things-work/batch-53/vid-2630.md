## Fact check

- A greater intensity of light causes the pupil to constrict; the example shows about 3 mm in bright conditions and 9 mm in dim. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pupillary_light_reflex
- Light shone into one eye makes both pupils constrict (consensual reflex); constriction is about 3 times faster than redilation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pupillary_light_reflex
- Rhazes of Baghdad first described the reflex in the ninth century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pupillary_light_reflex
- The iris controlling pupil size is standard anatomy (regulates how much light enters the eye). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pupillary_light_reflex
