## Fact check

- Consumer ovens work at 2.45 GHz, wavelength 12.2 cm; hot and cold spots can be mapped with damp thermal paper. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microwave_oven
- The turntable turns the food so it moves through the uneven energy pattern; the centre may still be uneven. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microwave_oven
- A mode stirrer is a fan-like device with microwave-reflective blades, usually at the top of the chamber. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microwave_oven
- Running the oven empty can overload the magnetron (standing waves). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microwave_oven
