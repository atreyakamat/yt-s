## Fact check

- Evaporation happens at the surface at any temperature; higher temperature, lower humidity, moving air and larger surface area all speed it; vapour carried away by wind; boiling occurs when vapour pressure reaches ambient pressure. Puddles also soak into ground in some places; script says 'a lot'. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evaporation
