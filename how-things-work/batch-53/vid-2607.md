## Fact check

- Sun's diameter is about 400 times the Moon's and about 400 times as far away, so both appear about 0.5 degrees wide; totality can currently last no more than 7 min 32 s; central eclipse track typically 100-160 km wide; Moon's orbit moves about 3.8 cm farther each year. 'Typical totality 3 min' bar is illustrative of typical eclipses (kept approximate). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_eclipse
