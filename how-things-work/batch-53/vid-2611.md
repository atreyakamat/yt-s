## Fact check

- Skid-steer loaders have no steering mechanism; the sides are driven at different speeds and the machine turns by skidding the wheels; counter-rotating sides give a zero-radius turn. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skid-steer_loader
- Attachments such as buckets, forks and sweepers are swappable. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skid-steer_loader
