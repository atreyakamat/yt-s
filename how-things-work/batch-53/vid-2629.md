## Fact check

- Stratification helps dense materials, like gold, sink to the bottom of the pan; lighter material rises and is washed out; dense material is usually black sand. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_panning
- Common pan diameters 10 to 17 inches (25 to 43 cm), 14 inches (36 cm) most used; sides generally angled between 30 and 45 degrees. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_panning
- Gold being much denser than sand: general physics (no number used).
