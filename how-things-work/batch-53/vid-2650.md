## Fact check

- The Adam's apple is the protrusion formed by the angle of the thyroid cartilage; everyone has the laryngeal prominence but it is usually larger in adult men. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laryngeal_prominence
- During puberty the thyroid cartilage and larynx grow and the cartilage shifts forward, linked mainly to testosterone; the larger resonating space deepens the voice; the cartilage helps protect the larynx front. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laryngeal_prominence
- In females the larynx is usually smaller so the bump is less visible; some women still have a noticeable one. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laryngeal_prominence
