## Fact check

- Civil twilight Sun 0-6 degrees below the horizon, brightest stars and planets such as Venus visible; nautical 6-12 degrees, horizon still usable for sailors; astronomical 12-18 degrees, dark enough for stars; civil twilight can be as short as about 24 minutes at the equator, 33-48 minutes at Greenwich depending on season; white nights near summer solstice at high latitudes.
