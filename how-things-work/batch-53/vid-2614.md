## Fact check

- A wax seal on a folded letter showed the letter had not been opened and could replace an envelope; breaking it revealed opening. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seal_(emblem)
- Medieval sealing wax was about two-thirds beeswax and one-third resin; signet-ring stones such as agate, carnelian and sardonyx do not stick to wax. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seal_(emblem)
