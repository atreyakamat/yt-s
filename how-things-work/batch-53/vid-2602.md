## Fact check

- Heat recovery ventilators transfer heat between outgoing and incoming air streams through a heat exchanger without mixing them. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_recovery_ventilation
- Typical efficiencies are roughly 60 to 95 percent depending on design. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_recovery_ventilation
- Units include filters on the supply air. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_recovery_ventilation
- Verified: heat recovery typically 60-95% of exhaust heat; "70% or more" within range. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_recovery_ventilation
