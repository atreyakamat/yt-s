## Fact check

- A fifth-wheel coupling consists of a kingpin on the trailer and a plate with locking jaws on the tractor; jaws lock automatically when the kingpin is pushed in. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifth-wheel_coupling
- The coupling allows pivoting between tractor and trailer. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fifth-wheel_coupling
- 'In seconds' is qualitative; no number claimed.
