## Fact check

- Tail lifts (lift gates) are hydraulic platforms on lorries; many use parallel linkages to keep the platform level and some fold up to form a rear door. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tail_lift
- Hydraulic rams extend when pumped with oil. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydraulic_cylinder
