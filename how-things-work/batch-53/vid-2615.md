## Fact check

- GPS receivers only receive satellite signals and need no data connection. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Positioning_System
- Web maps are split into tiles that can be stored locally. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiled_web_map
- Live traffic needs a data connection (script states this). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Maps
