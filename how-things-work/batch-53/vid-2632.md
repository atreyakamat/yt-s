## Fact check

- Solar cells in a module are connected in series; a shaded cell limits current and can overheat. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photovoltaic_system
- Bypass diodes allow current to skip shaded cell strings. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_cell
- A typical module has three bypass diodes, each covering about a third of the cells. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photovoltaic_module
- Bar values are illustrative, not measured.
- Verified: bypass diodes limit hot spots from shaded cells. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photovoltaic_system
