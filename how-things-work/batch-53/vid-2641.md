## Fact check

- Detonating cord: plastic tube filled with PETN, started by a blasting cap, detonation velocity about 6,400 m/s (range roughly 6,000 to 7,000). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detonating_cord
- Used in demolition to time charges so structural elements come down in a set order, and to set off several charges at nearly the same moment. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detonating_cord
- Faster than any car or plane: 6,400 m/s is far above any plane's speed (typical fighter jets are under 1,000 m/s).
