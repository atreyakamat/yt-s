## Fact check

- The hazard identification number (Kemler code) appears in the top half of an orange plate and the four-digit UN number in the bottom half; a doubled digit indicates intensification. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kemler_code
- UN 1203 is gasoline (petrol); Kemler 33 is a highly flammable liquid. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UN_numbers
- Blank orange plates are used for multi-load vehicles under ADR (rules applying in Europe). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ADR_(treaty)
- Verified: UN 1203 is gasoline (petrol), class 3 flammable liquid; 30/33 are flammable-liquid danger codes. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_UN_numbers_1201_to_1300 , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kemler_code
