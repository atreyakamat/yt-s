## Fact check

- Clothing and bedding can be stored in bags emptied with a household vacuum cleaner; this greatly reduces the bulk of non-food items. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacuum_packing
- Vacuum packing removes air and reduces the volume of the contents and package. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacuum_packing
- Fibres trap air; squashed fill may lose loft: general packing guidance, no number.
