## Fact check

- Cane species are thought to have come from India and New Guinea. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar
- Mid-1400s: sugar introduced to Madeira and the Canary Islands and mass-produced; 1520s: sugar industries in Cuba and Jamaica. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar
- 1747: Marggraf published experiments on extracting sugar from plants; 1801: beet sugar first produced industrially, in Cunern, Prussia; 19th century: sugar became a household item. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar
- From 1650 to the early 1900s British consumption rose from about 2 lb to about 90 lb per person per year (about 45 times). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar
