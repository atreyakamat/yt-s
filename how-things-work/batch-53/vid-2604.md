## Fact check

- Luggage locks recognised by airport screening can be opened by screeners using master keys, and locks not recognised may be cut. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TSA_lock
- A master-key design shared across many locks is only a deterrent against casual tampering: reasoning, no figures used.
- Travel Sentry's red diamond marks locks that TSA and other screeners can open with master keys; researchers have shown copies of those keys are easy to make, which is why the script calls it only a casual deterrent. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travel_Sentry
