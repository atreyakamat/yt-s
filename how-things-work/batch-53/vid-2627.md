## Fact check

- Harvest moon is the full moon nearest the autumnal equinox; Moon normally rises about 50 minutes later each day (50.47); near the equinox the orbit, tilted about 5 degrees to the ecliptic, runs nearly parallel to the horizon at mid-northern latitudes so the delay shrinks; about 30 minutes in 2007 at about 40 degrees; OED cites 1706 for harvest moon and 1710 for hunter's moon; golden colour from atmospheric scattering near the horizon, not unique. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvest_moon
