## Fact check

- Latency is the time for data to make a trip; bandwidth is how much data per second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latency_(engineering)
- Ping measures round-trip time in milliseconds. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ping_(networking_utility)
- Light travels in optical fibre at roughly two thirds of its vacuum speed (refractive index about 1.5). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_fiber
- Bar values (10 vs 100 ms) are illustrative of the gap, not measurements.
