## Fact check

- Bags are made of filter paper, food-grade plastic, or occasionally silk. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea_bag
- Tea bags typically use fannings, the leftovers after larger leaf pieces are gathered for sale as loose tea. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea_bag
- Some paper bags contain a heat-sealable thermoplastic such as PVC or polypropylene, so they are not fully biodegradable. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea_bag
- Roberta Lawson and Mary McLaren of Milwaukee: US patent 723287 'Tea Leaf Holder', filed 1901, granted 1903; Thomas Sullivan, a New York importer, marketed silk tea bags around 1908; pyramid (tetrahedral) bags introduced 1997. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea_bag
- A tea bag performs the same function as a tea infuser. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tea_bag
