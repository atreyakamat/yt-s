## Fact check

- Speed tests measure data transfer over a short period against a test server. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bandwidth_(computing)
- There are eight bits in a byte, so megabits per second divide by eight for megabytes per second. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byte
- Wi-Fi performance depends on distance, walls and interference. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wi-Fi
- Bars example: 80 megabits equals 10 megabytes per second (80 divided by 8).
