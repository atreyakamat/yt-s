## Fact check

- Sauerkraut is lactic acid fermentation of finely shredded raw cabbage layered with salt. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauerkraut
- Three phases: anaerobic bacteria start fermentation and acidify; Leuconostoc mesenteroides dominates once acidity is high; Lactobacillus species (L. brevis, L. plantarum) ferment remaining sugars and lower pH further. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauerkraut
- Cured sauerkraut pH is about 3.6; keeps for several months in an airtight container at 15 C or below; the Great Wall origin is presented only as a claim. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sauerkraut
