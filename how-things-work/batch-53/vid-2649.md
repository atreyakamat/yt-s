## Fact check

- Hoops or frames stretch the material to ensure even stitching tension that prevents pattern distortion; common types are scroll frames and artist's stretcher bars. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embroidery
- Common stitches: chain, buttonhole/blanket, running, satin, cross. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embroidery
- The Bayeux Tapestry is an embroidered cloth nearly 70 metres (230 ft) long and 50 cm tall, thought to date to the 11th century. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embroidery
