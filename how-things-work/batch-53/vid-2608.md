## Fact check

- Penicillium roqueforti causes the blue veins in Roquefort; Penicillium glaucum is used in Gorgonzola; both moulds need oxygen; loaves are punctured to allow air in. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_cheese
- Ripening usually takes 60-90 days at 8-10 C; Hallstatt miners (800-400 BC) ate blue cheese and beer, based on palaeofaeces analysis. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_cheese
- The page describes blue cheese as made with edible moulds.
