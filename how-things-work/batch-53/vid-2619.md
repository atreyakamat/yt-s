## Fact check

- A pin or blade extends from the bottom of the car into the slot (guide flag, swivels); power comes from metal strips beside the slot picked up by contacts on the guide flag; low-voltage motor inside. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slot_car
- Controller varies voltage with a resistor (rheostat); power pack steps household current down to roughly 12 to 20 V; common scales 1:24, 1:32, HO. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slot_car
- 1954 Southport Model Engineering Society six-lane layout; 1957 Minimodels converted Scalex clockwork racers to electric creating Scalextric; Russkit pistol-grip controller 1965. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slot_car
