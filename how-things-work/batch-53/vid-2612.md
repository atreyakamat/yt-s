## Fact check

- Vitruvius described watermills in De Architectura (c. 25 BC). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watermill
- The Domesday Book of 1086 recorded over 5,000 watermills in England. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watermill
- Water wheels drove mills, forges, bellows and sawmills. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_wheel
- Verified: Vitruvius gives the first technical description of a watermill (dated 40/10 BC); Domesday Book records 5,624 mills. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watermill , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domesday_Book
