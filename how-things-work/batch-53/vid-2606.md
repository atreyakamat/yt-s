## Fact check

- Self-inflation is possible due to the open-cell foam that fills the internal cavity; the design is protected by patents held by Cascade Designs. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_pad
- Sleeping pad warmth is characterized by R-value, with pad values from 1 to more than 10. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_pad
- Foam springs back and draws air through the open valve; trapped air insulates: general mechanism (page does not detail air intake).
