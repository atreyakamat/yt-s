## Fact check

- Intermodal containers have standardised corner castings that take twist-lock fittings for stacking, lifting and securing. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermodal_container
- Twist-locks engage by rotating a cone-shaped head within the casting aperture. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermodal_container
