## Fact check

- Anaerobic digestion is bacterial breakdown of organic matter in the absence of oxygen, producing biogas and digestate. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anaerobic_digestion
- Biogas is mainly methane and carbon dioxide. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biogas
- Digestate can be used as fertiliser. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digestate
- Biogas can be burned for cooking, heat or electricity. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biogas
- Verified: biogas is primarily methane with carbon dioxide (50-80% CH4, 15-50% CO2). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biogas
