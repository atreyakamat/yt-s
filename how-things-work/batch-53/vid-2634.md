## Fact check

- A keyway is the profile of the keyhole; keys must have the matching grooved profile to enter, and different keyways separate different key families. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pin_tumbler_lock
- Restricted keyways are used so that blanks are supplied only by authorised sources: general locksmithing practice. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_keying
