## Fact check

- Sprites occur typically 50-90 km up in the mesosphere above thunderclouds; reddish-orange upper region with bluish tendrils below; jellyfish sprites can reach about 50 by 50 km; usually triggered by positive cloud-to-ground lightning; first photographed accidentally in 1989 by University of Minnesota scientists with a low-light video camera; named for mythological sprites. Duration is a few milliseconds to tens of milliseconds (script says a fraction of a second; no exact number). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sprite_(lightning)
