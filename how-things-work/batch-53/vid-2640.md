## Fact check

- Escaped red blood cells break down; haemoglobin goes to biliverdin (green), then bilirubin (yellow), then hemosiderin (golden-brown); hemoglobin itself is red-blue. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruise
- Small, low-impact bruises normally heal within two weeks; the underlying damage is often repaired before the colour fades; a black eye healed over nine days in one example. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruise
- Petechiae are under 3 mm, purpura 3 to 10 mm, ecchymosis 1 cm or larger. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruise
