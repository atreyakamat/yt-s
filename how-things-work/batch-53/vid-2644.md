## Fact check

- Security cameras commonly use infrared LEDs for illumination and image sensors that respond to near-infrared; a day/night infrared-cut filter is moved out of the way at low light. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrared_cut-off_filter
- Infrared light is invisible to human eyes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrared
- Night-vision images are monochrome because the sensor records infrared intensity, not colour: general explanation. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed-circuit_television
