## Fact check

- Freckles are clusters of concentrated melanin-containing cells; they don't have more melanocytes, the melanocytes overproduce melanin granules. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freckle
- UV-B stimulates melanocytes to make more melanin so freckles darken; MC1R variants are linked but not absolutely; freckles are most common in Fitzpatrick types 1 and 2 but occur across all skin tones; they can fade with age; liver spots look like large freckles after years of sun exposure. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freckle
