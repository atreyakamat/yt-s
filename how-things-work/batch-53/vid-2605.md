## Fact check

- Optical image stabilisation moves a lens or sensor to counteract camera shake, driven by gyroscopes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_stabilization
- Electronic image stabilisation shifts a cropped window within a larger captured frame. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_stabilization
- Electronic stabilisation reduces the field of view (crop). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_stabilization
