## Fact check

- Aircraft can reverse with reverse thrust ('powerback'), but noise, jet blast, prop wash and debris can injure staff and damage equipment; pushback is preferred. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushback_(aviation)
- A typical tractor for large aircraft weighs up to 54 tonnes with drawbar pull of 334 kN. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushback_(aviation)
- Conventional tugs use a tow bar to the nose gear with shear pins; towbarless tugs lift the nose gear. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushback_(aviation)
- Pilots cannot see behind the aircraft, so the tug driver does the steering, talking by headset. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushback_(aviation)
- Family car about 1.5 tonnes: typical figure for illustration.
