## Fact check

- Treadle wheel: foot turns drive wheel via crankshaft and connecting rod leaving both hands free; flyer-and-bobbin system twists yarn and winds it simultaneously. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinning_wheel
- Double drive: bobbin whorl smaller radius than flyer whorl, so bobbin turns slightly faster; this speed difference winds yarn on when tension is released; taut yarn means bobbin and flyer turn together and add twist. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinning_wheel
- Origin disputed (India 500-1000 CE, China, Middle East by 1030); earliest clear Baghdad illustration 1237; European illustration c. 1280; a citizen of Brunswick is said to have added a treadle in 1533. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinning_wheel
