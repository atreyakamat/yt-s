## Fact check

- A heavily spring-loaded bar is released by a trip; bait goes on the trip; the latching mechanism varies by model. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mousetrap
- Larger plastic trips have more leverage, so less force is needed to trigger them. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mousetrap
- William C. Hooker got US patent 528671 for the spring-bar design in 1894; James Henry Atkinson patented the Little Nipper in 1898. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mousetrap
- Some commercial snap traps deliver striking forces 6-8 times greater than others. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mousetrap
