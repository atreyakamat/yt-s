## Fact check

- Sacrificial anodes of magnesium, aluminium or zinc corrode in preference to the protected steel (galvanic protection). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacrificial_anode
- Anode rods are used in water heater tanks and need periodic replacement. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_heating
- Magnesium is more electrochemically active than iron. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galvanic_series
- Bar values are illustrative.
