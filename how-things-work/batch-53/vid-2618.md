## Fact check

- Temperatures below 4 C (39 F) convert a potato's starch into sugar, changing taste and cooking qualities; short-term storage is 7-10 C (45-50 F). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potato
- Potatoes were domesticated in southern Peru and north-western Bolivia, around Lake Titicaca, about 7,000-10,000 years ago; earliest verified remains from Ancon, Peru, date to 2500 BC; about 5,000 varieties, roughly 3,000 in the Andes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potato
- A domestic fridge typically runs at about 1-5 C; the script says near or below four degrees, which is general knowledge and not a quoted figure.
