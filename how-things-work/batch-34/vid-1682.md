# vid-1682 · Why dogs tilt their heads

## Hook

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Hypotheses: improving sound localisation, seeing face past the muzzle (Coren), or learned attention-getting; a 2021 Animal Cognition study (Dror et al.) found 'gifted' word-learner dogs tilted more when hearing toy names. Mechanism not settled.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Dogs tilt their heads when you talk.
2. It may help them hear where a sound comes from.
3. It may also help them see your face better.
4. Their muzzles block part of their view.
5. Some dogs tilt more when they hear familiar words.
6. Owners reward it, which makes it a habit.
