# vid-1689 · Why octopuses have three hearts

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Octopuses have two branchial hearts and one systemic heart; haemocyanin (copper-based) makes blood blue; the systemic heart stops during swimming (making it tiring; they prefer crawling). Well documented. Verified: Octopus has three hearts (one systemic, two branchial): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octopus

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood.
2. Two hearts pump blood through the gills.
3. One heart pumps blood to the rest of the body.
4. The blood is blue because it uses copper, not iron.
5. Copper carries oxygen well in cold, low-oxygen water.
6. The main heart stops when they swim.
