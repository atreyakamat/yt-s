## Fact check (verify before posting)

Dionysius Exiguus (525) devised Anno Domini counting without zero; Bede later used it; astronomers use astronomical year numbering with year 0 = 1 BC; 21st century and third millennium technically began 1 Jan 2001. Strictly correct. Verified: No year zero, Dionysius Exiguus 525; 21st century began 2001 (arithmetic): OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Year_zero

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. There is no year zero.
2. Dionysius Exiguus set up the AD count in 525.
3. Europeans then had no zero as a number.
4. So 1 BC is followed straight by 1 AD.
5. Astronomers add year zero to simplify maths.
6. That's why the 21st century began in 2001.
