## Fact check (verify before posting)

Influenza vaccine protection takes ~2 weeks to develop (CDC); strains drift so formulas are updated annually; effectiveness varies by season (often ~40-60% against illness). Timing differs by hemisphere. Not medical advice.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Flu shots take time to work.
2. Your body needs about two weeks to build protection.
3. Flu season peaks in winter in many places.
4. The virus changes, so the shot is updated yearly.
5. It lowers risk and often lessens severity.
6. Ask a doctor or pharmacist what's right for you.
