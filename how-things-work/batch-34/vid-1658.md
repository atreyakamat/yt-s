## Fact check (verify before posting)

Unix time stored as signed 32-bit integer overflows at 03:14:07 UTC on 19 Jan 2038; 64-bit time_t lasts ~292 billion years. Many systems already migrated; legacy and embedded devices remain at risk. Verified: 32-bit overflow 19 Jan 2038 03:14:07 UTC; 64-bit lasts 292 billion years: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Year_2038_problem

6. Modern 64-bit systems fix it for billions of years.
