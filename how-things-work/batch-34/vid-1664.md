# vid-1664 · Why your first hour of work should be your best

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Protect your first work hour."
- Backup 1: "Your best hour belongs to your best task."
- Backup 2: "Don't open email first."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Chronotype varies; for many people alertness peaks late morning (~2-4 hours after waking). 'Eat the frog' and batching are popular productivity heuristics; evidence is mostly anecdotal/applied (e.g. email batching study by Kushlev & Dunn 2015 found reduced stress).

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Protect your first work hour.
2. Many people feel most alert a few hours after waking.
3. Email and chats pull you into other people's tasks.
4. Start with your most important task.
5. Silence notifications for the hour.
6. Then check messages in a batch.
