# vid-1653 · Why July and August are named after emperors

## Hook

- Backup 1: "Caesar and Augustus left their names."
- Backup 2: "The myth of August's stolen day."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Quintilis renamed Julius (44 BCE) after Caesar; Sextilis renamed Augustus (8 BCE). The story that Augustus stole a day from February to make August as long as July is a medieval myth (Sacrobosco); August already had 31 days under Caesar's reform.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Two months are named after Roman rulers.
2. July honours Julius Caesar.
3. August honours Augustus, the first emperor.
4. The month was originally called Sextilis.
5. Caesar's reform gave us the Julian calendar.
6. The old story says August got extra days; it's a myth.
