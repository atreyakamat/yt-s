# vid-1672 · Why echoes happen

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "An echo is sound bouncing back."
- Backup 1: "Why a canyon answers back."
- Backup 2: "The 17 metre rule."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Human ear perceives echo as distinct if delay ≥ ~50-100 ms (~0.1 s); round-trip 0.1 s × 343 m/s ≈ 34 m ⇒ ~17 m one-way. Good.

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. An echo is sound bouncing back.
2. Sound waves reflect off hard, flat surfaces.
3. Your ear needs a gap to hear it separately.
4. The gap must be about a tenth of a second.
5. So the wall must be at least seventeen metres away.
6. Closer walls blur into reverb instead.
