## Fact check (verify before posting)

Mpemba effect named after Erasto Mpemba (1963 with Denis Osborne); reproducibility is contested (e.g. Burridge & Linden 2016 failed to see it under controlled conditions; other recent studies claim it under specific conditions). Present as debated. Verified: Mpemba described it in 1963 as a Tanzanian student: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mpemba_effect

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Hot water sometimes freezes faster.
2. It's called the Mpemba effect.
3. It was named after a Tanzanian student in 1963.
4. Scientists still argue about whether it's real.
5. Evaporation, convection and dissolved gases may all play a part.
6. Results change with container and setup.
