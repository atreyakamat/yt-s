# vid-1671 · How to estimate distance with thunder and fireworks

## Hook

- **Chosen (0-3s):** "Count seconds to measure distance."
- Backup 1: "How far away is that firework?"
- Backup 2: "The three second rule."

## Fact check (verify before posting)

Speed of sound in air ~343 m/s at 20 °C (slower in cold). Rule: seconds / 3 = km; seconds / 5 = miles (approx). Works for thunder and fireworks (ignoring wind). Verified: Sound ~343 m/s at 20 C, so about 3 s per km: OK. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_of_sound

## Voiceover (read-through)

1. Count seconds to measure distance.
2. Sound travels about 343 metres each second.
3. That is about one kilometre in three seconds.
4. See the flash, then start counting.
5. Stop when you hear the bang.
6. Divide by three to get kilometres.
